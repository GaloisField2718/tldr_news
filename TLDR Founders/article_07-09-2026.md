HEADLINES & TRENDS

 CALIFORNIA IS TAXING SAAS AND AI TOOLS AND IS ABOUT TO MAKE YOUR
SOFTWARE 8-10% MORE EXPENSIVE. HERE'S WHAT SB 122 DOES TO BUYERS AND
VENDORS (13 MINUTE READ) [6]

 California signed SB 122 on June 29, which will apply sales tax to
SaaS and AI tools starting on January 1. The rate is 7.25% at the
state level plus local district taxes, so the total lands between
7.25% and 10.75% depending on where the buyer sits. Sacramento
projects about $2B a year. Remote sellers cross into collection duty
at $500,000 in California sales. Every California customer's invoice
grows by up to 10.75% that day, and the vendor collects it, so pricing
pages, billing systems, and renewal conversations all change before
year-end.

 GOOGLE AI MODE SHOWS THE SAME PRODUCTS 21.6% MORE EXPENSIVE THAN
TRADITIONAL SEARCH (13 MINUTE READ) [7]

 A small e-commerce analytics company compared over 2 million product
listings across more than 100,000 search result pages and AI Mode
answers over 23 days in August, covering the US and UK. Products that
appeared in both surfaces were 21.6% more expensive in AI Mode, with a
median price of $149 against $100 in traditional search. Only 1.28% of
products in traditional results also showed up in AI Mode for the same
query, so a rank in traditional search says almost nothing about
whether a product is in the answer box. The main seller differed on
49.6% of matched products.

 THE INCUMBENTS ARE COMING (9 MINUTE READ) [8]

 AI will make systems of record more important as customers will soon
be able to pipe the system's data into an AI agent and skip the
AI-native app. An incumbent system of record working with a general
agent will be enough to get the work done. The incumbents have an
incentive to control and charge for access to their data, and also to
push their own agents forward. The vertical AI-native company has to
perform a specific cross-system job better than an incumbent's
purpose-built agent or a general-purpose agent that can reconstruct it
from the underlying tools.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 YOU GET WHAT YOU REWARD (4 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Announcing the behavior you want does approximately nothing. You get
the behavior you reward. This is true anywhere people watch each other
for signals about what counts. It matters most when trying to fix
something in the culture.

 HOW TO GET YOUR FIRST CUSTOMER (THEN YOUR FIRST 3) (11 MINUTE READ)
[10]

 Your first customers are most likely already in your network. Start
with ten warm prospects, make a direct offer with a price and
deadline, deliver manually, and use ten real sales conversations to
validate or reject the idea quickly.

 HOW TO BUILD A ONE-PERSON COMPANY THAT RUNS FROM SLACK (17 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 A one-person company can run recurring work through Slack by giving
one AI assistant shared business context, connected tools, scheduled
briefs, and strict approval gates. The setup works best when workflows
are added gradually and corrections become standing rules.

TOOLS & RESOURCES

 ANNOUNCING PROJECT ZENITH: THE READY-TO-CODE WINDOWS EXPERIENCE ON
DEVELOPER-CLASS DEVICES (6 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Zenith is a ready-to-code, distraction-free experience on Windows on
developer-class devices. It will first become available with AMD's
Ryzen AI Halo. Zenith devices will come with a preconfigured Windows
setup for development and a set of tools curated for what developers
need. They will be capable of running 30+ parameter models locally and
unmetered.

 PROJECT HYDRAFUSION (14 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Project HydraFusion delivers frontier intelligence through runtime
orchestration. It can create a full execution plan, choose from models
across multiple providers to draft, critique, and revise, or cascade
to more powerful models to complete tasks. Developers just select
HydraFusion like any other model, and it chooses a workflow that
balances performance, cost, and latency for each task. HydraFusion is
now available to users on all GitHub Copilot plans as a research
preview.

MISCELLANEOUS

 CHINESE BUSINESSES ARE GIVING AWAY AI TOKENS WITH COFFEE, CREDIT
CARDS, AND DUMPLINGS (6 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Tokens are getting so cheap in China that businesses give them away
as loyalty rewards. Daily token use there reached 500 trillion this
year, up from 100 billion in early 2024. Chinese models cost 60% to
90% less than American ones. China Telecom sells 10 million tokens a
month for about $1.40. A Beijing dumpling restaurant hands out more
than 100 compute vouchers a day, and a Shanghai bank bundles up to 3
billion Qwen tokens with a credit card, in the slot where airline
miles used to go.

 ON THE YC S26 BATCH — RETURN OF THE ATOMS (5 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Industrials made up 23% of the 245 companies launched so far in YC
S26. The previous five-year average for industrials was just 6.6%.
SaaS has fallen to 12% vs a 28% five-year average. B2B software
overall is at its smallest share since S22 at 52%. AI appears to be on
a downward trend, but this may just be due to companies no longer
tagging themselves as such as it has become a default.
