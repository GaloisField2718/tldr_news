HEADLINES & TRENDS

 HOLDING THE DOOR FOR AGENTS (8 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Developers are handing routine deployment work to AI agents. Cloud
host Fly.io says at least 55% of its deployments now come from agents.
Users want agents to work without dashboard interruptions, so Fly.io
provides auth.md instructions for signup with human email confirmation
and a code. The resulting credentials expire after 24 hours and
exclude billing or organization membership changes. Agents can also
request fixed credit purchases that a human approves in Stripe's Link
app after reading the agent's stated reason.

 YOUR NEXT CUSTOMER WILL SEND AN AGENT (15 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Shopping has had four mega waves in 110 years. Self-service arrived
in 1916, the card in 1950, the internet in 1994, and the phone in
2007. Each one took one more person or step out of the purchase. The
next wave will take out the customer.

 THE BAR TO GET FUNDED IS FAR HIGHER THAN MOST FOUNDERS REALIZE: 900%
MEDIAN GROWTH UNDER $10M, 115% AT $100M+, AND $655K IN REVENUE PER
EMPLOYEE (6 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Tripling from $3M to $9M ARR used to be a sure path to a term sheet.
Now it trails the 900% median growth of the sub-$10M AI companies that
top growth funds are backing, and those same companies get funded with
only 55% gross margins and 105% net revenue retention. About 10% of
VCs still back a triple-triple-double plan, so founders growing 2 to
3x should build a much longer investor list and price their round
modestly.

 99 FOR FOUNDERS IS BACK (31 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Smart people learn from others, often, and as broadly and as deeply
as they can. This post is part of a series of lessons for founders and
investors collected from founders. The advice covers fundraising,
product, governance, competition, operations, exiting, and much more.
Each piece of advice was chosen to help founders think in a way they
may not have thought in before.

 WHERE AI ANSWERS GET THEIR SOURCES: 4,912 ANSWERS ACROSS SIX SOFTWARE
CATEGORIES (6 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Software buyers now ask AI assistants to compare tools like auth
providers and Postgres hosting. Notra tested buyer questions across
software categories to compare which sources assistants cite. ChatGPT
never cited Reddit or YouTube within the reports' top-ten domain
comparisons, while Google's AI Overviews often did. The cited pages
differ by engine, so teams should check which comparison pages show up
for their buyers' questions. Brands named in at least half the answers
had their own sites cited in a median 12% of answers.

MISCELLANEOUS

 WHY 2027 IS THE YEAR OF USER (10 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Everything that only deals with 'things' and not with 'people' is
quickly losing value. AI will soon solve all tasks with verifiable
rewards. They can build almost anything very easily. The new metrics
that still work are how many resources you have that other people
cannot get, and how much your work affects people. Both require real
people to spend time or money, as nobody can generate them from
nothing.

 STARTUPS ARE PICKING NAMES THAT CAN WORK EVEN IF THE ORIGINAL
BUSINESS MODEL DOESN'T (4 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Recently funded startups are favoring names that are distinctive but
vague, like Also, Sunday, or Flapping Airplanes, so they can pivot
without a rebrand. Many companies make one or two major pivots within
seven or eight years.

 THE FALL FUNDRAISING WINDOW IS NOW CLOSED (6 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Fundraising processes launched after Thanksgiving run into the
December holiday break. Pushing to close before year-end for founders
who already hold a signed term sheet. Founders with soft-circled
angels and no lead can close those commitments now so they do not lose
them. Founders without real investor traction should consider pausing
to improve the business and restart in January. Casual intro calls in
December can still build relationships for January.
