HEADLINES & TRENDS

 QM IS TRYING TO SOLVE THE COMPANY AGENT PROBLEM (6 MINUTE READ) [4]

 QM is a multiplayer agent harness for companies. It remembers enough
for people to work together without treating the whole company as one
person with one permission set. The agent recognized personal,
channel, group, team, and organization scopes, allowing people to
collaborate through the same agent without becoming one
undifferentiated user. QM is open-source and available for Slack and
the web.

 THE FRONTIER AI PRICE WARS CONTINUE (6 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Model pricing can now go stale before a startup ships. OpenAI cut
Luna's API price 80% only three weeks after launch and dropped Terra's
price 20%. Anthropic released Opus 5 at half the price of Fable 5,
while Google priced Gemini 3.6 Flash below Kimi K3 per task. Cheaper
models are already forcing frontier labs to move before most
enterprises have even started switching. If model spend matters to
your margins, keep switching costs low.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 THE RULES I TRY TO DO BUSINESS BY (49 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Service is what the customer experiences when trying to get something
done. For people to trust you, you need to make their journey easy,
respect their time, take ownership, ensure that advisable details
inspire confidence, be competent and decent when something goes wrong,
and build a system that learns. Trust takes time to earn but can be
lost surprisingly quickly. It is one of the most valuable things a
service provider can possess.

 FORWARD DEPLOYED EXECUTIVES: THE NEXT BILLION-DOLLAR AI UNBLOCK (13
MINUTE READ) [7]

 Forward-deployed engineers brought proximity, speed, and ownership of
the outcome, but that isn't enough. Engineers are still often too far
away from the key decision-makers who are critical for decisions and
culture. Companies can't just rapidly hire and replace the entire
C-suite in a month - they need outside help. Forward-deployed
executives can help close the gap by bringing both technical and
leadership experience to help businesses navigate the chaos and
complexity of AI adoption.

TOOLS & RESOURCES

 QM (GITHUB REPO) [8]

 QM is an agent designed for startups. Each employee gets their own
isolated workspace and can work independently without affecting each
other. They can also collaborate with the agent in channels, group
messages, and projects. Skills are scope-owned and shareable by grant.
QM can be used to search internal knowledge, build internal apps, work
on existing repositories, and track projects in a shared channel with
updates and follow-ups.

 CHATGPT ADS BUSINESS AGENT CONVERSATION CAMPAIGN TYPE (2 MINUTE READ)
[9]

 OpenAI's new campaign type, Agent, allows marketers to send ChatGPT
searchers and users to a Business Agent conversation. The agents will
be created through a combination of scraped data from the business
website and instructions from advertisers. They will have access to
product feeds and MCP tools, and can capture leads through custom
forms. They can also launch ad campaigns that point to the agent
rather than a website URL.

 THE ONLY MOAT THAT SURVIVES AI (11 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Most of what founders currently call a moat is a lease. Routing,
distribution, marketplace liquidity, and the app-layer feature set all
lose value as the models get better. What appreciates is proprietary
data, expert decision-making, and a pipeline that turns those
decisions into the next training run.

 EXERCISE WINDOWS SHOULD BE LONGER (7 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Startup options can turn into a bill the day an employee leaves. The
usual 90-day window asks someone to pay the strike price and possibly
taxes before they know whether the stock will ever be worth anything.
That made more sense when startups exited in four to seven years, not
today's 10 to 20 years. Backplanes gives people their tenure minus one
year to exercise, while Craftwork gives them a full decade. If you use
equity to recruit great people, make sure they can actually keep it.
