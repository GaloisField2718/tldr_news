 THE INFINITE SAAS FACTORY (4 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Microsoft has announced more than 30 new Copilot skills across
Dynamics 365 Sales, Service, and Customer Insights, allowing users to
use Copilot across Chat, Cowork, Autopilot, and Code to complete even
more end-to-end workflows. Microsoft has also introduced Microsoft
Copilot Managed Runtime, which provides hosting infrastructure that
lets code run safely inside a company's environment, governed by IT.
Together, these new tools start to form what the company envisions as
an infinite SaaS factory.

 YOU NEED A NEW CFO (12 MINUTE READ) [6]

 AI-driven tools are transforming the CFO role, shifting focus from
data management to strategic business architecture. Finance teams, now
leaner and equipped with AI-native software, are evolving toward
continuous forecasting and automated workflows. This change allows
CFOs to prioritize real-time decision-making, enhancing their
strategic influence within companies.

 PRODUCT DEFINITION (11 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Product definition is the ability to define requirements,
functionality, and context in a clear, actionable form that enables
humans and agents to deliver the right product. It involves getting
the right product built well, understanding the people you build for,
choosing the outcomes that matter, and rallying people around the
organization to make things happen. The foundation of successful
product management is the ability to work with a cross-functional team
to define, build, and launch well-designed, stable products. The best
product builders strive to execute flawlessly.

 REFERENCES ARE THE MOST UNDERRATED TOOL IN HIRING (15 MINUTE READ)
[9]

 By the time you're calling references on a candidate you want to
hire, it can be hard to change your mind. This hiring guide starts the
calls after the second interview, with the candidate's permission, and
spreads them through the process. Build ten questions about what you
still need to know for this particular role. Ask for stories and
comparisons with other colleagues instead of adjectives like smart or
hardworking. The answers can shape the next interview, including which
claims to test. If a reference suggests another person to call, get
the candidate's permission first.

 INTRODUCING THE GEMINI AGENT (39 MINUTE READ) [11]

 The Gemini agent is a single, universal agent for work. It can gather
all of a user's business context to complete tasks from knowledge work
to answering questions, and from content creation to coding, all from
a single prompt box. The agent plans the work, uses skills and tools,
connects to systems, and returns something finished. It chooses the
best model for the job and has built-in cost controls. The Gemini
agent is able to follow security, administration, and governance
policies set by the company.

 AI VERSUS VENDOR LOCK-IN (12 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Lock-in persisted for a long time because leaving was too expensive
and risky. AI changed the cost of migrations. It's now possible to
build a purpose-made converter, reimplement the behavior the business
depends on, and use agents to move both code and data across.
Businesses now have the ability to leave when the cost of staying gets
too high.

 COMPUTE CAPITAL MARKETS (17 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Compute is the fastest-growing asset class in the world. The demand
for compute will likely continue to increase. Supply will remain
constrained across chips, memory, power, and data center
infrastructure. A new universe of productive financial assets is
emerging, which means there will inevitably be robust markets to
price, finance, and hedge exposures around the underlying. There is an
immense opportunity in building a marketplace with embedded financial
primitives to allow producers and consumers of compute to rigorously
express risk preferences along the price and availability curve. These
new capital markets are uniquely suited for crypto rails.
