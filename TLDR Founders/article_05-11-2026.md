HEADLINES & TRENDS

 THE FINANCIAL ENGINEERING ESSENTIAL FOR AI IN 2027 (4 MINUTE READ)
[5]

 AI labs are in a price war over tokens while the GPUs behind those
tokens cost more to rent. Labs try to secure GPUs on multiyear
contracts at $2 to $4 an hour, then pay up to $13 once that capacity
runs out. An investor's analysis estimates that Anthropic's average
GPU cost rose 82% in two quarters. Anthropic's $5 billion short-term
compute bill in Q3 helped turn an operating profit back into a loss.
The analysis predicts labs will push enterprise buyers into multiyear
minimum commitments so their customer contracts run as long as their
GPU leases.

 THE TOP 1% OF AI SPENDERS STALLED IN Q3. SO DID ANTHROPIC'S DOLLAR
GROWTH (6 MINUTE READ) [6]

 AI spend peaked in July. The top 1% of firms on Ramp spent less on AI
per employee in September than in July. Anthropic added $5.4 billion
in revenue in the third quarter, down from $6.8 billion in Q2.
Adoption across all other firms is still rising.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 AI COMPANIES SHOULD OWN THEIR METERING, NOT THE ENTIRE BILLING STACK
(11 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Billing involves several distinct jobs. Teams without billing
expertise risk making bad choices with lasting consequences. AI
companies should own usage measurement, pricing logic, and customer
balances, but use specialist software for invoicing and revenue
recognition. The back office should own a shared model of customer
agreements.

 YOUR CUSTOMERS DON'T JUST WANT AN AGENT. THEY WANT AGENTS (6 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Calling a product a team of agents can make it sound more useful even
when the features haven't changed. Irrational Labs showed 1,014 US
software buyers the same product page with different agent labels and
asked whether they'd buy it and what they'd pay. The team label raised
stated purchase interest by 6.3 percentage points over a single agent,
but it didn't significantly beat wording with no agent and didn't
raise willingness to pay. Buyers also rated the team version harder to
use, so the researchers recommend making each agent's separate job
visible and keeping setup clear.

 HOW WE BUILT ALLOY, FIRECRAWL'S INTERNAL DESIGN HARNESS FOR AI AGENTS
(17 MINUTE READ) [9]

 A simple sales sheet could sit in Firecrawl's design queue for three
or four days because the designer had other priorities. One of the
company's product marketers built Alloy, an internal workspace that
gives AI agents Firecrawl's brand rules and past approved work for
each new asset. Editable product demos have been the biggest win,
since changing wording or timing before launch no longer means
recording the whole sequence again. The team has 63 reusable animation
packages and says Alloy has saved days of work. Experiments stay on a
canvas until someone deliberately publishes them for teammates.

TOOLS & RESOURCES

 ENTERPRISE MANAGED AUTH FOR CONNECTORS (15 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Claude Enterprise Managed Auth lets organizations connect their users
to MCP servers without sending each other through an OAuth consent
screen. Claude presents authorization servers with an identity
assertion and receives an access token after the assertion is
validated. Existing access tokens remain valid until their specified
lifetimes, even if a user is removed from the organization.

 NEXT GEN ZAPS FIX THEMSELVES WHEN THEY BREAK (1 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Zapier Zaps now fix themselves when they break. An agent reads the
error messages, works out a fix, tests it, and publishes a new version
of the Zap. Changes are emailed to users. Small fixes will go through
on their own - the agent will ask users before any big changes.

 COPYING COPE (7 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Competitors used to wait until your product was obviously winning
before they copied it. SuperMe's CEO watched rivals copy Grubhub and
Pinterest years ago and says four venture-backed startups copied
SuperMe in recent months, down to its navigation and custom icons.
Copying is now cheap enough that rivals imitate a product before they
know whether it's worth copying, so the CEO puts more weight on
distribution and networks that keep growing once the products look
alike. Grub Canada once copied Grubhub's HTML outright but couldn't
copy the restaurant network behind the site.

 THE NEXT ERA OF NOTION (4 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Agents are starting to use the same software your team does, and
their needs can pull a product in a new direction. Notion's CEO says
agents now create roughly half of Notion's pages, and MCP traffic from
connected agents has grown about 60 times this year. He is steering
Notion toward keeping company context up to date for other companies'
agents as well as its own. Notion also moved finance reviews from
monthly to several times a week to keep up with model releases.

 RETIRE THE WORD “CONTEXT” (2 MINUTE READ) [15]

 An agent with access to every Slack thread and call transcript at
your company can still miss what you need done today. Linear's
cofounder argues that storing more information still leaves someone to
decide what the work is for and what a good result looks like. A
useful briefing is short because someone chose what to leave out, so
he recommends explaining why decisions were made and what the agent
shouldn't change. Those answers need to stay current, since a
six-month-old explanation can still look clear and credible after the
team has changed direction.
