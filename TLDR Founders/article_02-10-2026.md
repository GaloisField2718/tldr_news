HEADLINES & TRENDS

 FINTECH IS DEAD (12 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Fintech has been brutally effective at stripping costs out of
traditional banking. It has made entire customer segments profitable
for the first time. However, cloud and mobile are no longer the
driving forces of disruption in financial services - intelligence
tokens and value tokens are. The first trillion-dollar finance company
will be an AI-first, token-first company.

 STATE OF MARKETS II (7 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Andreessen Horowitz's "State of Markets II" report highlights tech's
dominance in capital markets, contributing 76% of the SP500's earnings
growth in 2026. A pivot from software to hardware sees high demand for
AI-driven semiconductors, funded by tech giants' profits and debt.
Despite fears of GPU obsolescence, demand-driven pricing remains
strong, with SaaS companies facing a revaluation as they shift from
growth to profitability.

 Writing helps you process ideas, analyze problems, and explore

 HIRING BREAKPOINTS (7 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Hiring decisions should consider that incremental hires are not
linearly useful and can even be detrimental. Key team size
breakpoints, such as 0-1 and 7-10 employees, significantly impact
hiring effectiveness. Crossing the Dunbar number (~150 employees)
necessitates robust coordination structures, as productivity per hire
generally decreases beyond this point.

 “THIS IS A PRETTY FANCY DINNER. THAT PROBABLY MEANS I'M
OVERPAYING.” A DREAMFORCE LESSON FROM A TOP 5 CUSTOMER (6 MINUTE
READ) [17]

 A Groupon executive commented on a fancy vendor dinner, suggesting it
signaled overpayment, leading to strategic adjustments. Companies must
ensure customers perceive value continuously, not just at renewal, by
avoiding mediocre support and unnecessary upcharges and focusing on
customer success. Especially crucial in AI, value perception affects
fast audits and contract renewals.

 NOW IS NOT THE TIME TO INCREASE YOUR BURN RATE (5 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Founders misinterpret the current VC market as abundant in capital,
leading to increased burn rates without securing additional funding.
Investors increasingly prioritize fast-growing startups, leaving many
others, including those needing bridge rounds, unable to secure
additional capital. To avoid running out of runway, founders must
engage in open dialogue with investors and heed their guidance on
funding realities.
