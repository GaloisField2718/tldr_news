 CEOs truly rebuilding for AI rather than just adding features face

 US STARTUPS ARE QUIETLY REPLACING OPENAI AND ANTHROPIC WITH CHINESE
AI (5 MINUTE READ) [6]

 US startups are increasingly switching to Chinese AI models due to
significantly lower costs. Chinese models dominate OpenRouter traffic,
largely displacing US-developed systems, as cost differences are vast
- DeepSeek offers services at $0.14 per million tokens, compared to
OpenAI's $5.00. However, this shift raises concerns about data privacy
and security risks, with US lawmakers investigating potential
cybersecurity threats from these models.

 HOW LONG SHOULD AN AI AGENT LIVE? (4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 How long your AI agent stays alive between resets is a product
decision, and the data here says the long-lived ones quietly forget
their instructions. This essay proposes a coordinator agent that lives
a day, specialist agents that live about 30 seconds, and state written
to ordinary files rather than held in the agent's head. It cites
research where squeezing a conversation down to fit dropped standing
rules in 30 to 59% of runs. Memory quality falls as it grows across
every frontier model tested. Memory carried between sessions is also
somewhere an attacker can plant text.

 ON WHY YOU SHOULD INVEST IN THE MOST EXPENSIVE YC STARTUPS (5 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 A fund that buys into the top slice of YC batches just published what
it now reads into your Demo Day valuation cap. If you raised at one,
an investor somewhere is running this math on you. The fund looked at
642 companies from the 2021 through 2024 batches. Companies priced
above their batch median reached a Series A or beyond 25% of the time,
against 8% for those priced below, and shut down at 6% versus 16%. The
pattern holds inside each batch and among companies the fund passed
on. Median caps went from $15M to $40M between W21 and P26.

 AI AND THE CITY (10 MINUTE READ) [12]

 AI-era businesses are far more distributed than ever before. They are
increasingly forming in the outer suburbs rather than in city centers
and high-density areas. The companies that are most responsible for AI
development itself continue to cluster in major metro areas. AI-era
business formation appears to be broadly distributed across the US,
but major metro areas are seeing below-average business formation
relative to their sizes.
