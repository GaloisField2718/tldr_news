HEADLINES & TRENDS

 YOUR MARKETING TEAM WILL BE ON GITHUB WITHIN A YEAR (7 MINUTE READ)
[6]

 Marketing teams need a different kind of shared folder now that AI is
part of everyday marketing work. For many teams, this will mean using
GitHub. AI systems need a history of every change and why it was made,
a safe way to revert changes, and independence from any one AI vendor.
GitHub solves these issues and can be used by people without any
technical skills or support.

 WHY SERVICE BUSINESSES WIN THE NEXT DECADE (7 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Services have always been expensive because they involve human labor.
Scaling service businesses meant scaling headcount, which doesn't
create the outsized leveraged return required for startups. AI unlocks
a hybrid business model that looks like a service from the outside but
runs mostly like a product. Customers just want something that works
for them, and someone accountable when it doesn't.

 There's no speed limit to how fast you can grow. Startups need to

 KEEP THE SAAS ADVANTAGE. KILL THE SAAS PLAYBOOK (5 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Air had added AI features and more than 50 generative models, and its
business had accelerated only a little. So the team changed how it
sold the product and what customer success was asked to do. Every new
contract and renewal included AI credits, with overage rates
negotiated into the deal. Customer-success quotas shifted to adoption,
giving the team a reason to help customers use what they'd bought.
Usage figures arrived every morning. Air reports that AI action-credit
usage rose 15-fold in five months.

 INTRODUCING STRIPE TREASURY FOR AGENTS (3 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Stripe Treasury for agents gives startups a way to manage financial
operations with agents. It allows business finances to be executed by
agents from inside the tools businesses use to run the rest of their
operations. Agents can check balances, pay invoices, transfer funds
between accounts, and convert currencies. Startups using Stripe
Treasury for agents can choose which accounts and data agents can
access, whether they can read information or take action, and when
money movement requires human approval.

US GET THERE (3 MINUTE READ) [13]

 This post looks at ideas for companies that could change the future.
An AI app server on which agent apps are deployed as configurations
would commoditize the app layer and let them compete on the runtime
and underlying infrastructure. A community-driven ecosystem for open
apps could be an open-source ecosystem around which an infrastructure
and hosting platform could be created. There are opportunities in
agent memory, an intelligence router, new methods to finance compute,
and new types of infrastructure.
