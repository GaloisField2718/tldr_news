HEADLINES & TRENDS

 THE OLD B2B PRICING MODEL IS DEAD. THESE ARE THE NEW KINGS (12 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 AI will fundamentally alter software pricing. Seat-based pricing, the
traditional B2B pricing model, is slowly fading away. New B2B pricing
models charge for work done. This post looks at three emerging pricing
models that look promising.

 KEITH RABOIS' NEW RULES OF VERTICAL AI (17 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Frontier models will continue to improve. Vertical AI that does the
job end-to-end gains from improving models. Use model routing to
optimize workflows - long term, model mixes and open-weights can
recapture margin. Let labs supply intelligence, but own the data
loops, private data, corner cases, and last-mile liability. Earn data
by having a primary product that customers pay for, and learn what
labs can't by doing work previously done by humans.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 NOBODY LEARNS IN THE CLEAN HALF (7 MINUTE READ) [9]

 AI is taking over the routine work that used to teach new hires their
job, like reconciling ledgers or fixing small bugs. ATMs took over
cash handling and left bank tellers with customer problems and loan
conversations. The worry is who learns the messy half now, since a
500-person compliance team might shrink to 30 and the cuts land on
people who learned on the easy tasks. One startup studio hired its AI
engineer by asking them to fix a real SEO problem and demo the fix, so
new hires hit judgment calls in their first months.

 INSIDE PM AT ANTHROPIC: WHO MAKES IT, AND WHO DOESN'T (9 MINUTE READ)
[10]

 A bigger title at a bigger company counts for less than it used to
when Anthropic hires product managers. The head of product for
Anthropic's research teams walks through how she hires and ramps PMs
on the team that incubated Claude Code. Her favorite candidate is a
startup's first PM who also did customer success and business
development, since people who've worked without structure go find
answers themselves. The team also dropped annual planning for
one-month plans, because yearly OKRs went out of date within two
weeks.

 INTRODUCING MINTLIFY BASE (3 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Mintlify Base is a workspace where people and agents work from the
same specs, runbooks, and onboarding pages. It can help draft,
develop, and refine pages. Teammates can contribute to the work or
hand the work to an agent. Agents can follow style guides, review
checklists, and page templates. Base has a built-in MCP server that
other agents can read from.

 INTRODUCING VIRTUAL MODELS ON AI GATEWAY (3 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Vercel's AI Gateway now lets teams point an app at a virtual model ID
such as vmc/coding-agent, after 125 new models from more than 10 labs
landed on the gateway in 90 days. The company says the underlying
model, fallbacks, and data-retention settings can be swapped without a
redeploy.

 WHAT ONE PERSON CAN ACTUALLY OPERATE (14 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Stelae is a small hosting service run by one person. Most of the
technical decisions made stem from the fact that there is no team, no
on-call rotation, and nobody to escalate to. The system assumes nobody
will be there when something breaks, and tries to be boring enough for
that to be fine. This post discusses what that looks like in the code.


 PERSISTENCE IS A NUMBERS GAME: 150 STARTUPS, ONE COLLAPSE, AND THE
ONE THAT WORKED (4 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Startup studios look like a machine for making companies until the
studio itself runs out of money. A former studio CTO who helped build
more than 150 startups walks through why theirs collapsed. It carried
50 staff on a model where partners had to fund every accelerator
round, so when the partners slowed down, the payroll didn't. The CTO
lost their stake in the collapse, and their next company runs with a
skeleton crew.
