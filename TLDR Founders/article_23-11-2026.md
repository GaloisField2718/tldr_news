HEADLINES & TRENDS

 ALWAYS ON: WHAT IT'S LIKE BEING A TWENTY-SOMETHING FOUNDER IN THE AGE
OF AI (21 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Young AI startups often try to win over big enterprise buyers through
sheer speed. First Round interviewed young leaders behind Gumloop and
Reducto about running companies in this wave. A buyer asked Gumloop
for new features at 10 AM and got a walkthrough of them live by 5 PM,
which Gumloop says changed how the buyer saw the team even though the
features went unused. Enterprise deals still brought growing pains,
with Reducto mishandling early procurement and Gumloop eventually
hiring experienced enterprise sellers. The leaders also separate their
own hours from what they ask of staff.

 THE AGENTIC STORY ON THE EVE OF THE 2026 HOLIDAY SEASON (2 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 Agentic payments continue to grow fast. Airfare and retail are
currently the dominant spend categories. More than half of consumers
who make one agentic purchase come back to make at least a second,
typically in the same spending category.

 CRAZY AGENT SCALE: COMPANIES ALL-IN ON AI (15 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Experts demonstrated what pushing agents to their limits looks like
today at the Crazy Agent Scale event at Sutter Hill Ventures. Clement
Pang, co-founder of Wavefront, shared lessons from building an
open-source system for large-scale agent orchestration. David Cramer,
co-founder of Sentry, discussed using one agent for the whole company.
Shawn Wang, an AI engineer, discussed tiny teams and internal tools.
Quinn Slack, CEO of Amp and Co-founder of Sourcegraph, shared how to
use agents to scale. John Hayes talked about running a software
factory in production.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 A TRANSPARENT PRICING MODEL FOR AI (8 MINUTE READ) [9]

 AI software companies keep redoing their pricing because model costs
have plunged and customers are moving to cheaper open models. The
automation startup Gumloop switched to passing token and compute costs
through to customers plus an 8% orchestration fee, with a separate
enterprise platform fee. Gumloop reports that its Pro trial conversion
rate rose 42% after the switch, while open-weight models grew from
nothing to a real share of usage. Existing contracts stay in place
until renewal, and Gumloop gives up pricing control since token
revenue now moves with outside model prices.

 HOW TO BUILD A KNOWLEDGE FACTORY (11 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Making AI work for a company requires better knowledge, not just more
context. Agents need knowledge of how the company works, what
standards for operating it has in place, and where data is stored and
how it's accessed. This knowledge is often tacit, implicit, and
scattered. Companies need to collect, curate, and maintain a
representation of that knowledge to pass to agents.

 EXPANSION ABM PLAYBOOK (10 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Selling into other divisions of an existing customer is often easier
than winning a cold account. A marketing firm ran an expansion pilot
for TestRail that reportedly built $2.4 million in qualified sales
pipeline over six months. The playbook starts with customer champions
who help build private case studies under NDA and co-host educational
events. Marketing owns the content while sales reps map each unit's
buyers. Reps use webinar responses and content-hub activity to choose
follow-up.
