HEADLINES & TRENDS

 WHEN THE MODELS GET BETTER, YOUR TAM SHOULD GO UP (9 MINUTE READ) [5]


 The first thing every AI native business needs to ensure is that
their TAM goes up when models get better. Most AI businesses operate
in the gap between what models can do and what the customer needs.
Every model release eats a layer of that, and the market shrinks with
it. If a better model is bad news for your business, you're
arbitraging a capability gap on a timer.

 WE'RE GOING TO NEED DEFAULT HARD BUDGET CAPS ON PRETTY MUCH
EVERYTHING (3 MINUTE READ) [6]

 The world is going to need more default hard budget caps in the
coming months and years. Agents greatly reduce the friction of
spinning up code, but that costs money. Nobody wants to wake up to an
over-budget bill generated by an agent overnight. Businesses don't
want applications to throw errors because some budget was exceeded,
but most will likely prefer errors to a surprise $10,000+ bill. Hard
budget caps should be the default - people who want to live
dangerously should be able to do that, but it needs to be on an opt-in
basis.

 SALESFORCE, ATLASSIAN, AND HUBSPOT ARE STARTING TO CHARGE FOR AGENT
API ACCESS (7 MINUTE READ) [7]

 SaaStr received an estimate of up to $240,000 a year for agent API
access that had previously come with its software subscriptions. Its
revenue agent makes 35,000 to 40,000 API calls a day across its tools.
The agent proposed copying the data into a $5 Postgres database to
reduce those calls, though SaaStr hasn't built that workaround yet.
Salesforce is introducing access charges, and Atlassian already
charges. HubSpot's fees appear limited to its own agents so far, with
third-party agents still uncharged in SaaStr's account.

 PREDICTION: THE NEXT MASSIVE GOOGLE UPDATE IS AROUND THE CORNER (27
MINUTE READ) [8]

 Google updates its documentation with clues before launching major
updates. The company rewrote its guidance on AI content this week. It
is now 'critical' that sites manually fact-check and review all
AI-generated content before publishing. Fake authors are now
categorized as a form of deception. Google has now announced what it
considers low quality - now is the time to fix any issues before the
update officially rolls out.

 IS BUYING YOUR PRODUCT OPTIONAL FOR THE CUSTOMERS YOU ARE TARGETING?
(5 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Your best customer loves the product. Why won't another company just
like them buy it? Having the same problem doesn't mean having the same
reason to act now. Take AI search. A marketer might urgently need a
dashboard because their boss wants an answer about visibility, while
feeling little pressure to buy a tool that actually improves it. Look
back at what was happening when customers bought. What could they no
longer postpone, and why couldn't their existing tools or another
available option solve it?

 WHEN ARE NEGATIVE GROSS MARGINS ACCEPTABLE FOR AN AI STARTUP? (8
MINUTE READ) [10]

 Your AI product gets better, customers use it more, and each account
becomes less profitable. Harvey reportedly hit negative 50% gross
margins in June as usage grew under flat seat pricing. Subsidizing
usage can help acquire customers or learn what they'll pay for, but it
needs a way out. The comparison with Legora lays out three options.
Charge for usage, make each request cheaper, or cap what's included.
One proposed test is whether customers would stay when the product
keeps 40 cents of each dollar after covering the cost of serving them.

 THE COGNITION PLAYBOOK: FROM DEBUNKED DEMO TO $1B ARR (3 MINUTE READ)
[13]

 Cognition crossed $1 billion in annualized revenue on September 25.
It was at $1 million just 24 months earlier. The company had no
customers and no revenue when its launch video went viral in March
2024. Since then, the company has raised three rounds in the last
twelve months, the last one at $48 billion, and bought Windsurf, a
company five times its size. The company's own agent now writes about
90% of its code.
