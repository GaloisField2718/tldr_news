HEADLINES & TRENDS

 HOW AI APPLICATIONS EARN THEIR PREMIUM (12 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Execution, context, proof, and pricing together strengthen the case
for buying. Reliable work and trusted context create an advantage.
Clear evidence makes value visible, and pricing lets customers share
in efficiency gains while rewarding the vendor for improving delivery.
Advantages must widen fast for applications to keep earning their
premiums.

 THE MCDONALDIZATION OF VENTURE CAPITAL (27 MINUTE READ) [6]

 A bigger venture fund can change which version of your company an
investor wants to back. Venture is splitting between firms seeking
exceptional returns and asset managers built to deploy enormous pools
of money. Large asset managers can be satisfied doubling or tripling
their money, so they favor companies that can absorb billions of
dollars in familiar markets. The same incentives push founders toward
businesses that fit the next large investor's expectations.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 WHEN THE MODELS CATCH UP (14 MINUTE READ) [7]

 If the promised outcome depends on future model improvements, start
with the work customers can finish today. Products that only
demonstrate what might be possible can generate excitement, but back
it up with evidence about what customers can finish today, as people
pay for what they can accomplish. A useful partial solution can help
you gain valuable feedback for the next model iteration. Keep in mind
what the business can still offer when competitors can buy access to
the same model.

 BUILDING A GROWTH ENGINE WITH AI AGENTS (8 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Growth is becoming an engineering problem. This post presents a case
study of agent-led growth for growth marketing teams experimenting
with AI agents. It covers the experiments the team shipped, what
worked, and what went wrong. Not everything needs to scale, and not
every problem needs AI.

 PEOPLE DATA FOR AI AGENTS (WEBSITE) [10]

 Firecrawl provides people data for AI agents. It helps agents find,
enrich, or verify professional contacts and high-intent audiences. The
service connects to 12 people providers, each callable through the

 INTRODUCING MONITORS V2 (5 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Exa Monitors watches the web and delivers only what's new to agents
at a chosen interval. It covers Exa's entire index of 211 million
businesses, 3.2 million podcast episodes, 1.27 billion product
listings, and over 130 million web pages, and only calls agents when
something actually changes. Results are published to a webhook, so
agents never have to poll. Monitors v2 drops the price of monitors 7x
to $2 per 1,000 runs.

 WHAT CHANNELS GROW WHEN ORGANIC SEARCH SHRINKS? (10 MINUTE READ) [12]


 An email audience is becoming more valuable as organic search sends
fewer visitors. An analysis of Similarweb data puts US email traffic
up 16.3% in the first eight months of 2026 against the same period
last year. Email added more visits than paid search after excluding
Google's ad-routing domain, and the median website's email traffic
grew 14.7%. The gains include receipts and notifications, so they
measure visits rather than new customers. As search becomes less
dependable, give potential buyers a reason to keep reading your emails
and return directly to your site.

 OUR ORGANIZATIONS ARE BEING DDOSED FROM THE INSIDE (6 MINUTE READ)
[13]

 Organizations are losing attention as everyone within is doing
something they think is useful, but all that work draws on the same
limited capacity to understand, review, and decide. Just because work
exists doesn't mean it's the right thing to do. Output is not proof of
value. A working implementation only means that something can be
built, not that it should be - some changes have negative value.
