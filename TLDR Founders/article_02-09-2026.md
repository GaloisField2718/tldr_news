HEADLINES & TRENDS

 THE NEW VALLEY (8 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Investment committees now pause weekly on AI companies with great
revenue curves to ask what is truly unique, since every viral product
has copycats. As one founder-investor put it, AI made it 10x easier to
build a $10M ARR company but did not make it 10x easier to build a $1B
one. A seed and Series A investor at FPV Ventures calls the stall zone
the new valley, $5M to $25M in ARR. Carta found 15.4% of Q1 2022 seed
companies reached a Series A within two years, down from 30.6% for
2018. The four ways out are talent M&A and seed-strapping, raising
small, getting profitable fast, and keeping 80% of the company.

 THE HARNESS, THE HORSE, OR THE HAY (34 MINUTE READ) [9]

 There are three ways to make money in the AI industry: models, the
harness, and everything else the model and harness need to stay
healthy at scale. Founders picking a category need to make sure
they're using the right playbook. The categories are obvious, but the
strategies are close to inverted. This article looks at each category
to see what works.

 THE NEW HIRING FILTER (15 MINUTE READ) [10]

 AI is changing hiring faster than many organizations can adapt.
Companies are changing how they think about who they hire, when they
hire, and why. Competitive advantage is increasingly shifting toward
judgment as AI lowers the cost of execution. Traditional markers of
pedigree still matter, but they are becoming less predictive than
evidence that someone can learn, adapt, and operate in unfamiliar
requirements.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 THE COMPANY DOES NOT LIVE IN THE LLM (3 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Deterministic systems should be used to establish context. Large
language models should be used to reason over that context. Models
shouldn't be used to establish whether something has happened or what
the current state of a system is. Systems should be used to assemble
that information into context so that models can interpret, connect,
reason, and synthesize.

 CITATION SHARE IS NOT A METRIC YET (6 MINUTE READ) [12]

 The discovery layer and the infrastructure layer have never been
equally measurable. The industry puts all of its attention and spend
into the layer where the numbers are contested, produced by interested
parties, and unstable across instruments. The layer that determines
whether agents can transact with them at all returns a clean pass or
fail on every check. Both citation and infrastructure matter, but
infrastructure is what gets you transacted with.

 "I'M CHURNING" | 10 BIGGEST THREATS TO SAAS (8 MINUTE READ) [13]

 This is the person on the other side of your renewal itemizing how he
decides to cancel. A working CFO lists what he is cutting and why,
including a workflow tool that quoted him double what he pays for
Claude and a $ 50K-a-year point solution whose function a larger
vendor now bundles for free. His own playbook is opening renewal
conversations four or more months early, and he reckons 30 to 60
minutes of demoing alternatives buys enough negotiating power.
Backdrop is the Bessemer cloud index up 55% in six months, its highest
since early 2022.

 THE "PLAYER-COACH" ROLE IS BECOMING INCREASINGLY HARDER (7 MINUTE
READ) [14]

 Teams using coding agents open 6x more pull requests than traditional
teams and 3x more than they did in 2024, according to Linear's report
on AI usage in software teams. Review load is climbing faster than
anyone's model of the job, which is the likeliest way your first
engineering manager hire burns out. Managers already see only about
40% of what a report does in a day. The squeeze lands hardest on
engineering managers and tech leads: the people expected to write code
and manage at once.

TOOLS & RESOURCES

 KEEPING CREDENTIALS OUT OF AN AI AGENT'S CONTEXT WITH RELAY (8 MINUTE
READ) [15]

 WorkOS Relay proxies third-party agent API calls and injects the
credentials at the boundary so prompt injection attacks are unable to
steal tokens. It lets apps call third-party APIs on behalf of users
without directly handling secrets. Requests are proxied through
WorkOS, where credentials are swapped in based on the user and
organization context. Relay is still in early access, and access
requires contacting support.

 BEHIND THE BUILD: GENERATIVE PLUGINS AND SHADERS AT FIGMA (11 MINUTE
READ) [16]

 Figma has introduced generative plugins and shaders to the canvas to
give users a way to design the tools they need, when they need them.
Users can publish plugins and shaders to the Figma community or
privately within organizations on Organization or Enterprise plans.
The agent can build motion and interactivity into any effect, and
users can view and download the code behind any shader or plugin. The
Figma MCP server can be used to view, change, and export generative
plugins and shaders.

 INTRODUCING ADAPTIVE INTELLIGENCE: UNDERMINING THE ECONOMICS OF EVERY
BOT ATTACK (12 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Adaptive Intelligence is a new bot detection engine from Cloudflare
that makes getting through so slow and costly that attacks stop being
worth running. Not every attack is obvious to spot, and the economics
of deterministic detection are always in the attacker's favor.
Adaptive Intelligence makes sure that nothing attackers learn stays
true, so each new attempt costs more than the last. It recognizes bots
without visibly reacting to them and treats detection as a statistical
judgment, weighing many signals at once so there's no single piece of
logic for an attacker to isolate and beat.

MISCELLANEOUS

 WHY ADDING MORE AI AGENTS MAKES YOUR TEAM SLOWER (19 MINUTE READ)
[18]

 Agent scaling is about how cleanly a system allows agents to work in
isolation without stepping on each other all the time. This requires
expertise in the problem domain, a keen eye for good architectural
decisions, and good measurement. Coding is the easy part of writing
software - it's everything else that takes most of the time. There's
only so much you can streamline before you start losing a significant
amount of quality.

 “ENSHITTIFICATION” ISN'T REAL (11 MINUTE READ) [19]

 "Enshittification" isn't real. Social media platforms change to meet
evolving user needs, not to worsen user experiences. Despite
complaints, user satisfaction and engagement with platforms like
Instagram continue to rise. Critics mistake platform evolution and
user dislocation for deterioration, but the data shows that people use
these platforms more, not less.
