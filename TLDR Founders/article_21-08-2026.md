HEADLINES & TRENDS

 RISE OF THE BORDERLESS FOUNDER (13 MINUTE READ) [7]

 International founders that make it in Silicon Valley tend to find
communities of similar people who have done the same. When these
founders get together, they have each other's backs. The emergence of
these international tech mafias has changed the playing field for
international founders. These borderless founders who have a foot in
their home country and in Silicon Valley know the strongest engineers
in their home market before the rest of the market catches on and can
get early customers from large companies at home that give them big
proof points early.

 APPLE ANNOUNCES CHANGES FOR APPS IN THE EUROPEAN UNION (5 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Apple set new EU App Store terms that take effect October 1, and
agreements can be signed now. The standard in-app purchase commission
moves to 26%, or 15% for qualifying developers. Alternative payment
processing runs 20%, or 10% for eligible program members, and apps
distributed outside the App Store pay a 5% Core Technology Commission
on digital transactions and require notarization. For the first time,
a single app can offer both Apple in-app purchase and alternative
payments. Once an option is picked, it stays for 12 months.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 FIRSTHAND: HOW I BUILT A BRAND WITH HEART AT LOVABLE (22 MINUTE READ)
[9]

 Lovable launched in late 2024 with a visual identity that was
prototyped in 30 machines. The brand quickly picked up $10 million in
annual recurring revenue just 60 days after launch. Lovable's brand
was designed and redesigned over two and a half years by an insanely
talented design team. This post tells the story of how Lovable's first
designer translated a feeling of genuine infectiousness into a design
brief and brand identity. The company's growth shows what is possible
when people with innate curiosity and genuine care for what they do
are given complete trust: they produce exceptional work, remarkably
fast.

 THE ONE SALES MOVE THAT DOESN'T ASK YOUR PROSPECT FOR ANYTHING (13
MINUTE READ) [10]

 Commenting on your prospects' posts can create investment while
adding value. Comments create a direct touchpoint with your prospects,
and it works as a distribution to many more similar prospects. They
allow you to get free reach into a network of people just like your
buyer. Commenting lets you stay visible in a way nothing else in
outbound can.

 RAISING YOUR NEXT ROUND IS HARDER THAN YOUR LAST ONE. HERE'S WHY (4
MINUTE READ) [11]

 This post lays out the arithmetic behind why a company that raised a
seed easily gets Series A nos without changing anything about itself.
An accelerator writing checks at $330K to $2.5M post-money only needs
a $33M to $250M exit to work. Your seed at $5-10M post requires belief
in a $500M to $1B outcome, and a Series A investor underwrites a path
to roughly $100M a year, which is why $1M ARR is not enough. Dilution
eats 50%+ by exit. Three escape valves get named, including
customer-funded growth and capital that does not need 100x.

 SLACK CODE (3 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Slack Code creates temporary channels where teams and agents can work
together on a specific task or project. The channel is archived
automatically when the work is done, but the context remains
searchable. Code channels follow Slack's enterprise-grade security and
permissions model. They make it easy for anyone, even non-technical
employees, to see what an agent is building, review previews, and
share feedback directly in Slack.

 TECHNICAL INGREDIENTS, REVENUE INGREDIENTS (3 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Shipping code is not the same as shipping revenue. Confusing the two
is an unforced error. If you haven't prepared great market-readiness
ingredients, you just have another unsellable software. CEOs need to
identify the next revenue to bottle next when code development is
faster than selling.

 AI acquisitions require code-level diligence because ARR and growth
cannot reveal whether proprietary technology is defensible or merely a
model wrapper. Buyers should score model dependency, data moat, talent
concentration, and substitution risk before signing or assigning
premium valuations.
