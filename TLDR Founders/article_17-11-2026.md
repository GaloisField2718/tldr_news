 INTRODUCING THE DATABRICKS AI INDEX: THE STATE OF ENTERPRISE AI
TOKENOMICS (12 MINUTE READ) [5]

 The Databricks AI Index is a public resource that brings the
company's insights into real-world adoption and spending into one
place. It makes the company's internal evaluation suite permanently
public. The index shows how new models compare and enterprise AI
adoption rates. It is updated weekly as Databricks evaluates new
proprietary and open-weight model releases.

 THE FRONTIER BLINKS (2 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Anthropic's newest AI models, released last week as Fable 5.2, cost
20% less to use than the top models before them. Until then, only the
smaller AI models were getting cheaper. The new models also run about
30% faster, so the same chip turns out more tokens for the same hourly
cost. The price cut roughly matches those savings, which Anthropic
could have kept as margin. Expect more cuts like it as models keep
getting cheaper to run.

 IF YOU'RE REFOUNDING YOUR B2B COMPANY FOR AI, MAKE YOUR PRODUCT
CHEAPER. PROBABLY MUCH CHEAPER (6 MINUTE READ) [7]

 HubSpot is rebuilding its products around AI agents, and plenty of
other business software companies are doing the same. Most of their
prices were set for people, who stop paying attention once they sign.
Agents check the cost of every call and call less when a product costs
too much. SaaStr's own agents cut their calls to one vendor by about
90% after its bill rose 40%. Price agent usage apart from human seats,
and watch calls per customer per day for the first sign of trouble.

 STATE OF APP MONETIZATION 2026: HOW APPS CLIMB FROM $500 TO $1M A
MONTH (15 MINUTE READ) [8]

 One in four apps climbs revenue tiers within a year, with paths from
$500 to $1 million achievable through strategic adjustments. Key
factors include optimizing paywall conversion rates from 2.9% to 6.4%,
reducing checkout abandonment from 73% to 57%, and enhancing
trial-to-paid conversions from 20% to 31%. Apps that experiment more
with features like multi-page flows, pricing strategies, and
personalized onboarding see higher growth, emphasizing the importance
of targeted testing and adjustments.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 WHY YOUR STARTUP SHOULDN'T HIRE LIKE GOOGLE (2 MINUTE READ) [9]

 A startup's hiring advantage is that it can find talented people
before their resumes make them obvious to everyone else. AI will
change how many people a successful company needs, as headcount is not
the same as impact. Build a team whose potential you believe in, give
them room to grow, and be honest about whether it is working.

 CODE REVIEW AT LINEAR (12 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Linear expects engineers to continue reviewing code in the near term,
but it has started building tooling to group related changes, explain
their intent, and make meaningful edits easier to distinguish. It
seems likely the amount of manual code review will decrease over time
as models and review agents improve. The next evolution of review will
make it easier for humans to review specific code when needed, make
more PRs mergeable without human review, and elevate the focus of
review from mechanical implementation to a higher altitude.

 AI SEARCH META ANALYSIS - Q4 2026 (18 MINUTE READ) [11]

 AI is changing the path from discovery to decision-making. AI answers
reduce clicks, but recommendations can introduce buyers to brands and
shape their shortlist before a website visit. Brands need to show up
while buyers are researching to give them the information they need to
choose. Measure the journey, make decisions easier, and test your
visibility.

 THE GAMMA MODEL INDEX: OPENAI TAKES THE LEAD FOR THE FIRST TIME (10
MINUTE READ) [12]

 Anthropic has dominated the Gamma Model Index for knowledge work for
most of the year, but OpenAI has provided some real competition in the
last few months. The new GPT-6.1 Terra replaced Claude Haiku 5.5 and
Sonnet 5.5 for several key jobs in Gamma in October. Gamma's agent
handles millions of sessions each week, and the company A/B tests
every new model on real users. This post looks at how it makes model
decisions to serve millions of knowledge workers around the world.

 WORKOS ATLAS: A SECURE HOME FOR THE INTERNAL APPS YOUR TEAM BUILDS
WITH AI (TOOL) [13]

 WorkOS Atlas hosts the internal tools that teammates build with
coding agents like Claude Code or Cursor. Each app gets company
sign-in, and an admin connects tools like Slack and Snowflake once so
credentials stay out of app code. Admins see every running app and its
owner, and they can limit sensitive apps to certain groups. Atlas is
in early access and is onboarding teams one at a time.

MISCELLANEOUS

 WHAT ARE YOUR AGENTS DOING ALL NIGHT? (16 MINUTE READ) [16]

 A question on X drew more than 300,000 views last week by asking what
everyone's overnight agents do. Most of the hundred-plus replies
described short scheduled checks that finish in minutes. Long runs
work when a test or score decides which changes to keep. Coding is
harder to score, and one team's overnight builds usually need rework
in the morning. That team saves the setup for features that take
several days, since it costs too much to prepare for a bug fix.

 BUMBITION (11 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Two of the founders behind TypeSafe AI, recently valued at $7.5
billion, spent years out of work before they started it. One made her
own clothes and won hackathons, and the other became a top-ranked
Hearthstone player. Bumbition is a name for that path, waiting for
work that's worth doing instead of always chasing the next goal. The
time off brings in ideas from other fields and people from outside
your industry. The cost is real too, since skills can fade and the
right opportunity may come late or never.

 THE INTERFACE IS THE ORG CHART (5 MINUTE READ) [18]

 AI is reshaping company structures by enhancing customer
interactions, as exemplified by AI voice agents at Banco Inter, which
streamline customer service. AI's ability to access information and
expertise across departments can eliminate traditional organizational
limitations. This shift suggests potential changes in company
divisions, highlighting a need for executives to reconsider existing
departmental constraints.
