HEADLINES & TRENDS

 GROWTH IS BECOMING AN ENGINEERING PROBLEM (12 MINUTE READ) [8]

 The most AI-pilled teams are rebilling their entire GTM stacks. Small
and medium businesses are actively trying to use agents to recreate
software as some of their old tools aren't making the cut. Larger
companies have contracts, integrations, and years of operational
habits to work through. This post looks at the growth teams rebuilding
their GTM stacks around agents to see what they're building, buying,
or replacing.

 THE END OF FEATURE DISCOVERY AS WE KNOW IT (8 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Growth teams used to spend months getting customers to try a feature
they'd just shipped. Lovable's growth lead now flags it in Slack when
the agent itself doesn't know about a feature, because customers
describe a job and leave it to the agent to pick the tools. The next
sign of adoption is a customer handing the agent a whole workflow
after trusting it with a small task. That customer might spend less
time in the app, so the product has to show what the agent decided and
give them a way to undo it.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 STARTUP MARKETING ADVICE IN THE AGE OF AI (4 MINUTE READ) [10]

 AI can turn out a finished launch video before anyone has checked
whether it could pass for a competitor's. One startup marketer
recommends covering your name and logo with a competitor's and
watching or reading the whole thing through. Start with a rushed
tagline or the stock footage between demos. Rewrite anything the
competitor could still use as is, and keep the lines and shots that
stop making sense under their logo.

 FIND HIGH-VALUE MEMBERS WITH A CLAUDE AGENT (1 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Selling to wealthy consumers can look a lot like selling enterprise
software. Atlas' founder says the typical member spends about $1
million a year on cards, so he started treating members like key
accounts. He used a Claude agent to find existing members the company
could do more for and called them himself. A few of those
conversations ended with six-figure trips booked through the company's
concierge.

 NOW AVAILABLE: RETENTION MESSAGES FOR SUBSCRIPTIONS (1 MINUTE READ)
[13]

 Apple's Retention Messaging API is a server-to-server service that
enables developers to select messages to display to customers when
they view a subscription details page and might cancel. Retention
messages can include text, images, or a special offer. The API allows
developers to choose which message to show subscribers based on their
subscription details. API access is available by request.

 AGENTIC BANKING IS HERE TO STAY. HERE'S HOW MERCURY KEEPS YOU IN
CONTROL (TOOL) [14]

 Mercury lets AI agents prepare payments or transfers that a person
still has to approve under the team's existing rules. The agent can
only use permissions its owner already has, so an employee who can't
see the payroll balance can't get it through an agent either. Admins
can review each agent's activity and see who connected it. They can
also revoke access from Mercury on the web.

 INTRODUCING VERCEL SCHEDULES (3 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Vercel Schedules allows agents and applications to create, change, or
cancel one-time and recurring schedules through code without
redeploying. They are designed to let users and agents decide when
work should happen while applications are still running. A schedule
tells Vercel when to trigger a task and where to send it. Applications
provide the code that carries out the work. Schedules can be used for
both static and dynamic jobs.

 IS PRIVATE EQUITY COOKED? (12 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Private equity firms could borrow to buy mature software companies
because lenders could count on their customers renewing. AI is making
those renewals harder to bank on, just as higher rates make the next
buyout harder to finance. It is estimated that a typical buyout now
needs 10% to 12% annual EBITDA growth to match what a 2015 deal could
earn with about 5%. That's a higher bar for the next buyer at a time
when revenue growth at Golub Capital's software borrowers has slowed
from 9% to under 3% in two years.

 AX IS NOT THE NEW DX, IT'S THE NEW UX (20 MINUTE READ) [17]

 The Agent Experience has become one of the most talked-about shifts
in infrastructure over the past year. The focus has been on rebuilding
developer tools for coding agents, but that's just because coding
agents got good first. Every product is becoming something agents use.
As time goes on, more and more users will be agents.
