 STRIPE'S NEW INDEX SHOWS NON-AI B2B REVENUE UP 30%+ YEAR OVER YEAR
THROUGH THE SAASPOCALYPSE (7 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Software stocks lost an estimated $2 trillion early this year on
fears that AI would replace business apps. Stripe's new weekly index
of non-AI software companies that bill through it shows revenue up
more than 30% from a year ago. Most of those companies sell self-serve
products paid by card, and few of them are the enterprise names whose
stocks fell. A company that shuts down also drops out of the index
instead of counting as a loss. The index says little about enterprise
seat pricing, so watch public companies' net revenue retention for
that.

 ON WHY YC SERIES A ROUNDS FELL OFF A CLIFF (7 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Series A rounds for Y Combinator startups have drastically declined
since 2023, linked to a broader market downturn caused by rising
interest rates. The downturn affected all YC batches, regardless of
founder quality or admission year, as global early-stage funding fell
sharply. Recent signs show a recovery, but raising Series A may become
less necessary due to advancements in AI and increased focus on
capital efficiency.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 EVEN THE BEST TECHNOLOGY WON'T ACHIEVE WIDESPREAD ADOPTION IF ITS
VALUE ISN'T LEGIBLE TO BOTH TECHNOLOGISTS AND BUYERS. (7 MINUTE READ)
[8]

 Learn how to sell your product before hiring an executive to scale
sales. Founders need to understand how customers make purchasing
decisions and translate technical capabilities into value that both
technologists and buyers recognize. Selling has become monotonous and
repeatable, with more opportunities than the founder can handle alone.


 BRAND AS INFRASTRUCTURE (17 MINUTE READ) [9]

 In-house designers keep getting requests to remake the same decks and
one-pagers for other teams. Ramp's brand team handed that work to a
Slack agent that builds them from approved examples the designers make
in Figma. Automated tests catch broken layouts and missing fonts
before anything goes back to the person who asked. One contractor
automated about 90% of the company's whitepaper work in her second
week. The designers now spend their time on work nobody has made yet,
and each new hand-made deck goes back into the system as an example.

MISCELLANEOUS

 ROI ON IRL EVENTS? (5 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Events are a marketing and brand motion that educates people on your
brand before they decide to do business with you. They are a great way
to engage with existing customers and clients. Engaging socially with
prospects is a much warmer experience for them, and it allows you to
establish an initial dialogue. Events will increase your odds of
setting up follow-up meetings and ultimately get new customers. To
measure ROI, do a survey of attendees and track which customers
attended the event.

 LESSONS LEARNED FROM COMMERCIALIZING DEV INFRASTRUCTURE (9 MINUTE
READ) [18]

 Plenty of developer tools give away the core product and plan to
charge for a faster managed version. Earthly did that with its build
tool, and developers adopted it while few of them paid. Teams could
get most of the speed by keeping their own build machines running, so
the paid runners had little left to sell. Earthly shut that business
down in 2025 and now sells automated checks for engineering standards
to leaders who control the budget. Check whether your free version
already delivers the value you plan to charge for.
