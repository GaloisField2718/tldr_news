HEADLINES & TRENDS

 HOW FAIRE DEVELOPED PRODUCT BUILDERS (9 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Faire accelerated product development by transforming engineers into
"product builders," combining coding with product and design thinking.
This shift allowed engineers to manage features end-to-end, reducing
dependencies on PMs and designers, freeing them to focus on complex
customer problems. Early results showed significant improvement in
engineers' contributions to customer outcomes, with a notable increase
in product and design thinking capabilities.

 RISING HEALTH INSURANCE PREMIUMS SQUEEZE PAYCHECKS IN 2026 (4 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Employers are paying more for health coverage while employees are
feeling the squeeze. Total family premiums rose 9.8% from 2025 to
2026, while gross pay for the same employees grew 6.4%. Employers
still pay most of the premium. More employees are on high-deductible
plans, with the share of family enrollees on HSA-eligible plans rising
from 20.9% in 2021 to 33.9% in 2026.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 INSIDE CLAY'S GTM MACHINE (9 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Build your account scoring around what your highest-paying customers
have in common. Clay's head of GTM engineering found that its top
customers by ARR had at least 10% of their employees in sales or
go-to-market roles. That pattern became one input in its fit score for
other accounts. The fit score feeds a weekly ranking that gives each
rep five accounts from their own book to focus on.

 THE SECRET BEHIND VIRAL PRODUCT LAUNCHES (14 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Startups have the unique opportunity to capture millions of eyeballs
and attention worth millions of dollars on X. This post looks at how
to actually win the timeline for a day. It provides clear steps to
follow to grab attention, even against AI, which has industrialized
content production and saturated the space. It also covers the
different things a company must do to coordinate a launch with over a
million views.

 SHOULD YOU INCORPORATE AI IN YOUR POSITIONING? (13 MINUTE READ) [11]

 A homepage that sounds like your closest competitor's feels like a
positioning failure. A positioning consultant argues that this can be
the right call for AI CRMs such as Lightfield and Day AI, which have
both used "AI-native CRM." A buyer using Salesforce or HubSpot first
needs a reason to consider switching to an AI CRM. The differences
between the startups can wait for a sales conversation once that buyer
is ready to try one.

MISCELLANEOUS

 THE THING I TALK TO MOST (4 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Picnic's founder had an agent read customer replies and come back
with a feature he hadn't thought to build. One person wanted to stop
approving each inbox search, and another needed to keep a client's
Social Security numbers out of the results. The agent proposed a
permission that stays on until revoked and lets the customer block
specific terms. It suggested showing the idea first to the person with
the privacy request and drafted a reply.

 THE GREAT SOFTWARE IP WRITEDOWN (7 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Software companies count on years of engineering work continuing to
pay off after the product ships. Faster copying is turning that
investment into a recurring expense. Agents can already start
reproducing apps from screenshots. Switching is now easier too because
an agent can pull customer records out of the old web app even when
the vendor offers no clean export.
