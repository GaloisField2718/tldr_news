HEADLINES & TRENDS

 WHAT PEOPLE ARE PROMPTING IN POSTHOG AI (9 MINUTE READ) [6]

 PostHog AI's usage trends show founders often prompt late hours, with
a quarter of prompts after 6 PM, while marketers focus more on revenue
queries. Tuesday sees the highest prompt volume, peaking at 11 AM.
Users become more informal over time, with increased swearing and only
a minority using polite language consistently. Prompts frequently
relate to dashboards, session replay, and database queries, reflecting
user roles' specific interests, such as engineering's focus on
debugging and marketers' interest in web traffic analytics.

 THE RETURN OF THE FULL-STACK APP (7 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Emerging AI app startups resemble infrastructure companies by
training their own models and managing complex workflows, enhancing
technical depth. Four key changes favor these startups: owning models,
handling complex and costly agent operations, prioritizing security
and governance, and building customer trust. Successful AI app
companies focus on delivering outcomes, specialize in high-stakes
verticals, integrate organization-wide workflows, and incorporate deep
technical infrastructure.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 PRINCIPLES FOR BUILDING EFFECTIVE VERTICAL AI PRODUCTS (7 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Vertical AI takes the workflows of the best practitioners and
codifies them into agentic systems that deliver capability, quality,
and efficiency a third-party frontier model can't match. The challenge
is finding workflows and translating them into products, context
layers, agentic harnesses, data systems, and feedback loops. Seamless
transition requires an organization and culture that brings
world-class GTM and engineering to the same table every day. The
organization's people need to speak a common language and rapidly
build based on that shared vernacular and best practices.

 HOW TO GIVE AI COMPANY CONTEXT AND BUILD A COMPANY BRAIN (8 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 Ramp recommends a one-to-two-page context document paired with live
connectors to the tools where work happens, arguing AI gives confident
but wrong answers because it cannot guess internal definitions,
policies, and workflows. The guide compares five ways to supply
context, from custom instructions to fine-tuning, and advises setting
permissions first, testing with 10 to 20 known-answer questions, and
assigning owners. Ramp says its most AI-forward staff kept personal
setups few colleagues saw, which led to its Glass workspace.

 EDIT ENTIRE PRESENTATIONS WITH GEMINI IN GOOGLE SLIDES (2 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 Gemini in Google Slides can now be used to edit multiple slides or an
entire presentation with a single prompt. It can update content
throughout a deck, refresh visual styles in bulk, or match the style
of another presentation. The AI determines which slides need to change
and applies edits directly. Users stay in full control and can make
further adjustments. Gemini displays a summary of the changes in the
side panel after it finishes editing.

 INTRODUCING DEEL SECURITY: A NEW WAY TO DETECT AND PREVENT IDENTITY
FRAUD (3 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Deel Security connects key identity signals and flags risks before
bad actors can gain access to a business. It assesses identity and
looks for signs of fraud, bringing the findings together in one place.
Teams are able to see how risks develop across the hiring process and
spot patterns that individual checks might miss. It can spot fake
applications, detect deepfakes and proxy candidates in live
interviews, and verify government ID.

 CLOUDFLARE SERVICES FOR PRIVATE ORIGINS IS NOW GENERALLY AVAILABLE (5
MINUTE READ) [13]

 Cloudflare services for private origins connects internal services to
the Internet without exposing a public IP, opening a firewall port, or
running cloudflared alongside the origin. It gives private
applications the same protections and controls used for public
applications. Argo Smart Routing and Dedicated CDN Egress IPs aren't
supported, and origin health checks require Load Balancing.

MISCELLANEOUS

 OUR CORE VENDOR WAS DOWN FOR 24 HOURS. OUR AI AGENT REPLACED THEM
BEFORE THEY CAME BACK UP (5 MINUTE READ) [14]

 AI has made outages even more risky for businesses as replacements
are now easier to source. Humans may wait for a vendor to fix their
services, but agents making less emotional and more calculating
decisions may choose to switch vendors or build in-house solutions
much sooner. Vendors that communicate poorly during incidents will
lose the most customers. Their customers' agents will build or suggest
replacements, and there will be nothing to save by the time renewal
comes up.

 THE SOFTWARE FACTORY AS IDENTITY DEFENSE (AND REINVENTION) (5 MINUTE
READ) [15]

 Software factories are becoming more and more appealing to engineers
trying to preserve their role as AI makes coding cheaper. The cloud
put operations teams in a similar position in 2012 when Netflix
described needing few of them and an Etsy employee called “NoOps”
“the dumbest marketing term ever coined in my field.” Someone
still had to build the platforms that let developers serve themselves.
Engineers building agent factories are making a similar move, taking
control of the quality standards and deployment systems that determine
which code reaches customers.
