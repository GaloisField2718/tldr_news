HEADLINES & TRENDS

 AI OVERVIEWS HAVE STEADILY CLIMBED AS A SHARE OF SEARCHES (1 MINUTE
READ) [4]

 AI Overviews have become a core part of how people search. AI Mode
visits have grown fast, showing how Google is actively pulling users
toward a more conversational search experience. ChatGPT's share is
declining as Gemini and Claude gain real ground. Referrals, citations,
and ad placements are rewriting the rules of visibility month to
month.

 LIGHTHOUSE OR LANDGRAB? HOW TO PICK YOUR AI SALES STRATEGY (13 MINUTE
READ) [5]

 AI founders need to choose between two sales strategies: Lighthouse
focuses on securing prestigious clients for social proof, essential
for new categories. Landgrab emphasizes speed and broad customer
acquisition when the market already understands the product. Each
strategy caters to different buyer needs and risks, so choosing the
right approach can significantly impact success.

 CODE REVIEW (AS WE KNOW IT) MUST DIE (11 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Code review was built for a world where humans wrote code slowly and
reading the lines was how you earned confidence in a change. The
companies that rebuild around how software actually gets made now will
win because of the operating speed it gives them. AI gives companies
more shots to iterate toward the right answer before competitors can
plan their way there. It also frees engineers to do the creative,
high-leverage work they signed up for.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 THE BEST PRIORITIZATION IS NO PRIORITIZATION (8 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Prioritization is a trap that wastes time and effort and delivers
worse results than just executing faster. Spend more time focusing on
building faster. Shift everyone into durable teams that don't have to
do cross-business prioritization at all. Manage 'prioritization'
through resource decisions to grow, downsize, or split teams.

 REJECT OSSIFYING YOURSELF (3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 CEOs are responsible for building an enduring company. For the
company to grow, the CEO must grow across all dimensions. Instead of
identifying your known strengths, chip away at the ossified shell
you've been carrying for protection. This will uncover new areas of
strength you never knew you could have.

 IF YOU DON'T TAKE THE WHEEL, YOUR BOARD WILL (9 MINUTE READ) [9]

 CEOs must take ownership of decisions rather than deferring to their
boards, as doing otherwise invites external control. Founders should
articulate clear strategies on people, product, and financial velocity
to maintain autonomy and build board trust. Successful management
demands informed decision-making, a strong organizational vision, and
a proactive approach to addressing challenges.

 Codex Security CLI has been open-sourced. It allows users to scan
repositories, track findings across runs, verify fixes, and add

 MCP 2026-07-28 is the largest update to the protocol since launch. It

 BENDING SPOON S-1 BREAKDOWN (16 MINUTE READ) [12]

 After their first startup failed, the founders of Bending Spoons were
left with $40,000 and a lesson. They thought finding product-market
fit involved a lot of luck, while running a software business well was
mostly skill. So they spent $10,000 buying an app that already had
users. Bending Spoons has now done this more than 50 times, including
Evernote, Vimeo, and WeTransfer. Every product gets the same pricing,
data, and experimentation system. Remini alone ran more than 1,000
monetization tests. The system behind the apps is really what they
have been building.

 THE BEST CHARITY OF THE NEXT 50 YEARS MIGHT BE A COMPANY (6 MINUTE
READ) [13]

 AI-driven wealth may shift philanthropy as tech founders choose to
integrate profit with societal good, blurring lines between companies
and nonprofits. Some mission-driven companies already tie growth to
positive outcomes, such as health improvements or environmental
benefits. This trend suggests impactful future social institutions
will be profit-aligned businesses rather than traditional nonprofits.
