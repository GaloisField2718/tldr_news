HEADLINES & TRENDS

 INTRODUCING THE STRIPE AI INDEX (4 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Founders have been guessing how much real revenue sits behind the AI
boom. Stripe launched a weekly AI Index tracking payments to about
20,000 AI businesses on its platform. Aggregate AI revenue rose 103%
over the past year and easily beat the SaaS Index. Median AI growth
trailed aggregate growth by a wide margin, so larger AI companies
appear to be doing better than typical ones. The index only counts
payments through Stripe and tracks weekly shifts as overall growth
cools.

 THE NEW APP STORE NOBODY'S OPTIMIZING FOR (4 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Apps can now integrate with ChatGPT, allowing interactions without
leaving the chat, as seen with partners like Spotify and Expedia.
Developers can seize this low-friction distribution channel, ensuring
app functions complete quickly and drive users back to their main
platform. However, reliance on ChatGPT means limited control over
distribution, possible cuts of transactions by OpenAI, and user
relationships residing in the chat interface.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 HOW DID LOVABLE GROW SO FAST? (14 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Most founders look for a secret growth playbook behind breakout
startups. Lovable turned two this week with over $600 million in
annual recurring revenue and no growth hack. The AI app builder rides
an exploding category, so tactics that failed at older software
companies now work like a raft on a fast river. The lean team ships
big updates nearly every week to keep word of mouth moving. New hires
are expected to automate the repetitive parts of their own jobs with
AI from the start.

 YOUR NEXT GTM HIRE SHOULD BE A CREATIVE MARKETER. (8 MINUTE READ) [8]


 Cold email volume has exploded now that anyone can launch a massive
campaign in minutes with Claude Code. About 91% of cold emails get no
replies. A B2B outbound agency reports lifting its reply rate to 15%
by targeting signals like a fresh funding round or a new sales leader.
Everyone can automate the sending now, so the next hire after a GTM
engineer should be a creative marketer who owns the angle and the
copy. Teams should score that marketer on replies and message tests.

 EMPLOYEE-LED CONTENT PROGRAMS ARE A DANGEROUSLY GOOD DISTRIBUTION
STRATEGY (2 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Employee-led content programs create a ripple effect that can bring
any company exponential upside. This post presents a guide on how
anyone can get started. What kills these programs are copy-paste
captions, forced posting quotas, and posts that only promote the
company. People can smell a mandate.

 HOW I USED AI TO GET 25 MILLION VIEWS IN 7 WEEKS (11 MINUTE READ)
[15]

 Founders often let their social accounts go quiet once daily work
takes over. A dormant Threads account reached 25 million views across
platforms after an AI loop began drafting new posts from old hit
ideas. Raw views converted into far fewer followers on Threads than on
Instagram, so select posts offered a free guide and collected 1,745
email addresses. The system scores core ideas and rests each winning
idea for at least a month before reposting it.

 THE M&A PROCESS | WHO, WHAT, AND WHEN (12 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Most founders only see the public announcement of an acquisition and
rarely hear how messy the private process gets. Company sales start
with only top executives and the board in the loop because leaks
distract employees and weaken the seller's hand. Choosing an
investment bank is a common leak point since banks that lose the pitch
often talk. Many deals die early in the process, so keeping the circle
tight avoids spooking customers unnecessarily. Bankers and lawyers
also cost more than most founders expect.
