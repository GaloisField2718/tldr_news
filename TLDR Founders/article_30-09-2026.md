HEADLINES & TRENDS

 DEPLOYMENT GAP (5 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Robotics is finally having a moment. Models are getting better, costs
are coming down, and robots are logging real hours in customer
facilities. However, full deployment will still take some time - even
companies that publicly claim they have deployed robots often do not
have them working on actual production lines. Until the technology
improves, the industry will need patient capital.

 THE GREAT AI CAPITAL MISALLOCATION (7 MINUTE READ) [6]

 The flood of capital into AI infrastructure has left many promising
startups underfunded, with investors chasing companies that can absorb
huge sums quickly. AI's rapid growth benchmarks and the belief in a
winner-take-all scenario drive this trend, yet hinder more traditional
but solidly growing tech firms. As capital focuses on AI
infrastructure, there's potential in identifying application companies
ready to leverage cheaper AI tech for broader market success.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 WHAT TYPESAFE GOT RIGHT WITH THE JEV LAUNCH (5 MINUTE READ) [7]

 TypeSafe's Jev launch achieved rapid adoption among Vercel's paid AI
teams due to its clear value and easy integration into existing
workflows. A compelling demo showcased Jev's speed in making bounded
decisions, outperforming competitors and highlighting its utility. The
product's early success stemmed from allowing users to quickly
demonstrate and share tangible outcomes, supported by strategic
distribution to influential tech figures.

 THE GUERRILLA HIRING PLAYBOOK (12 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Leaders face challenges hiring talent in the AI-focused market, where
usual tactics are less effective. Guerrilla hiring involves frontier,
potential-based, and asymmetric hiring to attract trajectory-changing
talent without relying on brand power. Utilizing "talent magnets" as
domino hires can jumpstart recruitment by attracting follow-on
candidates who define and drive expected roles and career paths.

 MARKET TO THE MIDDLE (3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Ever read a thread about your product and wondered if these people
are even your customers? Tracksuit's CEO argues that online discussion
overrepresents enthusiastic fans and angry critics, while ordinary
buyers rarely post at all. AI recommendations can pick up those same
opinions, making a vocal minority seem more representative than it is.
Tracksuit compares consumer surveys with AI responses to see where
perceptions differ. Before changing your positioning around a loud
complaint, find out whether the customers who aren't posting share it.

MISCELLANEOUS

 MODELING SOFTWARE'S BUSINESS IMPACT (14 MINUTE READ) [13]

 There's no guaranteed relationship between software cost and value.
While cost improvements are typically marginal and difficult, value
improvements are game-changing. AI can produce a lot of work, but that
doesn't mean that customers can absorb it. Businesses need to treat
customer attention as the scarce resource it is and only ship the
things that are truly valuable.

 THE ONLY REASON TO RAISE VENTURE CAPITAL (4 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Founders should raise venture capital only when money is the limiting
factor on growth. Treating fundraising as success can distort
incentives, encourage unnecessary spending, and leave companies
trapped at valuations their businesses may never justify.
