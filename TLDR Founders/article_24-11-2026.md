HEADLINES & TRENDS

 HOW TO WIN BOTS AND INFLUENCE PURCHASES (11 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Shoppers are starting to let AI agents pick products and software for
them. a16z and The Prompting Company report over 11,000 controlled
agent tests to see how they choose. Stores with better return terms
won 86% of equal-price comparisons in those tests. Agents also
finished guest checkout forms most of the time, while every checkout
requiring a new account stopped early. Mandatory signups stopped those
agents, so keep guest checkout open and make return terms easy to
compare.

 IS SAAS DEAD? THE 8 BUSINESS MODEL WAVES BEHIND THE DEBATE (7 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 One way to compete with a leader is to pick a problem people already
pay to solve and ride a new wave that the leader would lose money
copying. This is called counter-positioning. This post looks at the
eight waves of counter-positioning that have appeared since 1994. It
covers how monetized open source helped Red Hat become the first
open-source company to reach $1 billion in yearly revenue, how
Amazon's hourly rentals cut into Sun Microsystems' server sales, and
much more.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 DOGFOODMAXXING (3 MINUTE READ) [10]

 The best content shows how companies use their products themselves.
This is probably the most convincing way to market a product. Show
people how a team can use a product for a real job, and customers will
see what they could do with it as well. If your team can't use your
own product, use it for a dream customer and send them the result.

 THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT AEO AGENT YOU NEED TO BE RUNNING RIGHT NOW
(7 MINUTE READ) [11]

 AI search engines already cite outside websites when answering buying
questions, giving outreach teams specific pages to approach. A
marketing agency linked an AI agent to a spreadsheet and team inbox to
find cited pages and contact their editors. The agency reports sending
206 emails and landing 28 live mentions in its initial client test,
while tracked AI citations also climbed. Start with a small batch of
pages and review every draft before enabling autonomous sending, since
a human should still handle any payment or commercial terms.

MISCELLANEOUS

 STOP ASKING USERS WHAT THEY WANT (9 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Users will only tell you what they think they want. They don't know
what they actually need because they haven't spent as much time
thinking about it. Product engineers need to spend more time talking
to users to understand their real problems so they can solve them. Ask
questions that get real, useful intel on what to build.

 WATCHING USERS IS HOW YOU MAKE PRODUCTS BETTER (5 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Few companies watch real people use their products anymore. Several
people getting stuck in the same place marks a real problem, so repeat
the sessions after a fix. Go where each person normally uses the
product and let them use their own device. Give them a short script of
basic tasks and ask them to think out loud while you say as little as
possible.
