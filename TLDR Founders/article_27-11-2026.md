HEADLINES & TRENDS

 IS JEVONS PARADOX HOLDING? (2 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Cheaper AI models are often expected to push total spending higher as
usage grows. OpenRouter's paid token volume grew 44 times this year
while total spend grew 7.6 times. Usage kept climbing after early
October while spend barely moved, so buyers were getting more output
without substantially increasing budgets. Enterprises appeared more
cost-conscious than AI-native scale-ups in this paid-usage dataset
excluding free models and reseller traffic. Similar slowdowns late in
earlier years reversed after new model launches.

 AUDITING ENTERPRISE NETWORK EFFECTS (6 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Software founders often rely on network effects to protect B2B
products from AI rivals. Those defenses depend on whether customers
contribute proprietary data or features that competitors can recreate
cheaply. Equifax's proprietary credit data is harder to reproduce than
ZoomInfo's publicly available contacts. AI makes thin marketplace
features easier to build, so an app ecosystem like Salesforce
AppExchange provides less protection. Networks connecting business
partners combine proprietary data with distribution because each
customer brings counterparties onto the platform, as Nominal does with
hardware test data.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 AI AND MARKET RESEARCH: HOW I USE IT AND WHERE IT STILL FAILS (6
MINUTE READ) [7]

 Founders are testing AI tools to speed up market research and cut
routine admin work. Marketing agency Parvi records every client
meeting so an AI agent can write CRM notes and follow-up tasks from
actual conversations. Parvi reports that an AI-assisted audit found a
B2B account spending roughly 50% more on LinkedIn than competitors
while showing the same people ads about 50 times a quarter. The agency
rejects synthetic respondents as a replacement for customer interviews
and builds its research around real source material.

 HOW TO RUN YOUR GTM IN 2027 (DO THESE 9 THINGS FIRST) (8 MINUTE READ)
[8]

 Outbound teams are tempted to point AI agents at thousands of
accounts on day one. The founder of an outbound agency first tests
each account list and email sequence by hand on 25 similar accounts.
Each week he picks about 20 high-signal accounts per client and
contacts several people at each one personally. Repeatable steps run
as fixed n8n workflows, since sending the same job through an AI model
on every run costs far more, by his estimate.

TOOLS & RESOURCES

 INTRODUCING SEARCH FOR AGENTS (5 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Cloudflare's Search for Agents helps websites set up an AI search
without having to manually create and configure it initially. It
completes all of the integration work required to get a search
endpoint available from the site domain, and it makes sure agents know
where to find the index and how to use it. The tool automatically
creates an index and exposes APIs that any AI agent can discover and
use to search your website, under your domain, all done at the network
level. Search for Agents can be enabled using the Cloudflare API or
through the zone page dashboard.

 INTRODUCING EFFECT UI (WEBSITE) [10]

 Effect UI is a frontend library built on Effect and the Elm
Architecture. It provides Components with explicit state transitions
and side effects as values. Components are self-contained and can
provide their own services and load in their own chunk. If a Component
fails, the rest of the app keeps running. Effect UI is currently in
public preview.

MISCELLANEOUS

 STATISTA GAVE UP MOST OF ITS 35 MILLION MONTHLY SEARCH USERS TO KEEP
ITS DATA AWAY FROM AI (4 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Publishing free pages for Google search used to bring data companies
steady traffic. Statista says it drew about 35 million monthly search
users before AI crawlers pulled its data into chatbot answers.
Customers questioned paying for access, so Statista put almost
everything behind a registration wall and saw search users fall to
about 8 million. Some smaller customers left because chatbot answers
were sufficient, while its enterprise business is growing. Statista
now sells licensed data access through an API and MCP server that
connect to customers' AI tools.

 THE 3 FINANCIAL PLANS YOU NEED FOR THE YEAR: C-90, C-60 AND C-10 (6
MINUTE READ) [14]

 A C-60 Plan is one where you have a 60% confidence rate you can hit.
This is your base plan. A C-10 plan is a variant of the C-60 plan that
you think you can maybe hit - this is the stretch plan. A C-90 plan is
used to figure out how long a business' cash can last if the next year
is harder than planned. Businesses need to make sure they have enough
cash to support the C-90 plan, especially during a tougher economy.
