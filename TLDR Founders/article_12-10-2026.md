 SOFTWARE'S CENTAUR AGE MAY LAST DECADES (7 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Human engineers paired with AI coding systems are more effective than
either by themselves. This fact is likely to remain true for the next
decade or two. Software engineers have plenty of time to consider
their next move. There's no need to panic and jump ship early when
there's still time to live out an entire normal-length career.

 THE DEVELOPER HIRING REBOUND FAVORS SENIORS (7 MINUTE READ) [7]

 CNBC reports that software-development job postings rose even as
overall US postings declined. Senior jobs supplied most of one
measured increase, while employment among developers aged 22 to 25
remained under pressure. Founders should distinguish demand for
experienced AI-capable builders from a broad recovery across the
engineering talent market.

 PART 1/3 OWNING INTELLIGENCE SERIES: HOW COINBASE BUILT, EVALUATED,
AND POST-TRAINED A FRAUD AGENT (9 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Coinbase built an LLM risk agent onto Onramp's existing ML model and
rules. Online A/B experiments showed a 30% reduction in fraudulent
transactions and 22% less fraud value. Coinbase then post-trained
Qwen3.5-9B on its proprietary Onramp fraud dataset. The resulting
model outperformed Opus 4.5 on all fraud detection metrics while
delivering 55% lower end-to-end online serving latency.

 “YOU DIDN'T DO IT RIGHT” (6 MINUTE READ) [9]

 When OKRs don't work, how many times are you supposed to try again?
WP Engine gave them four attempts, including paid training and
coaching, and kept hearing that it wasn't doing them properly. What
eventually worked was much smaller. One team picked a metric it cared
about, set a loose goal, and followed it for a quarter before adding
anything else. The metric also had to measure something the team could
actually control, so people weren't being judged on a number their own
work couldn't fix.

 YOUR CHURN PROBLEM STARTED IN THE COMP PLAN (4 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Your reps are hitting their targets, but a year later the customers
are gone. One founder in this piece lost 30% of the previous year's
new customers and wanted to know what was wrong. The answer starts
with what reps get paid to sell. In a separate Airtable example, a
birthday-calendar user counted as much toward the new-logo target as a
team running a critical workflow. Airtable began scoring prospects
against its best customers, giving reps smaller account lists and
paying more for customers with room to grow.

 MODELS AS INSIDER RISKS IN THE SUPER INTELLIGENCE ERA (7 MINUTE READ)
[14]

 We need to separate the supply of intelligence from the authority
over it. This means surrounding non-deterministic models with strong,
deterministic system design, human controls, and reliable operating
procedures, and establishing industry standards where existing ones
are insufficient. Any sufficiently capable actor with access to
important systems can make mistakes or be compromised. The
architecture of containment and control must account for that.

 HOW CHINA'S VENTURE ECOSYSTEM WORKS (8 MINUTE READ) [15]

 China's venture ecosystem pushes startups toward early IPOs through
investor repayment clauses and a weak acquisition market.
Government-backed funds, domestic USD investors, financial advisors,
and relationship networks shape fundraising, while industrial policy
directs capital toward strategic sectors.
