HEADLINES & TRENDS

 TACTICAL TEAM CHANGES FROM AI (7 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Small teams can now run orders of magnitude further thanks to AI.
Many teams need fewer designers than before, but that doesn't mean
designers aren't needed. People who are good at building products can
now build way more. Companies are being more nimble with resourcing.
AI has catalyzed the presence of A+ teams: small teams where there are
no weak links and everyone is a 100xer.

 A GREAT B2B AGENT DOUBLES YOUR ACV. MOST AI FEATURES DON'T MOVE IT AT
ALL (9 MINUTE READ) [9]

 A well-designed B2B AI agent can double ACV by performing tasks
previously done by humans, tapping into labor budgets rather than
limited software budgets. In contrast, most AI features manage only to
improve existing workflows without significantly increasing ACV,
leading vendors to charge more by adding per-action fees. Companies
like Podium exemplify successful agents by automating substantial
tasks, offering clear value, and supporting higher pricing.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 SETTING THE PACE (4 MINUTE READ) [10]

 The single most significant predictor of the success of an
early-stage company is how quickly they sprint out of the gate, as
this demonstrates its ability to execute at scale with speed.
Fast-moving companies can adapt to changing market conditions, deliver
innovative products or services, and make efficient decisions.
Founders need to create a vision, set lofty goals, and inspire others
to join them. After that, it's about setting an uncomfortably fast
pace.

 WHAT IT TAKES TO OPERATE A SAAS WHEN AI AGENTS WRITE ALL THE CODE (10
MINUTE READ) [11]

 DocSpring's founder runs 49 Claude Code sessions, each assigned to
one task or one part of the product, and spends most days reading
their updates and answering their questions. One session does nothing
but chase the errors Sentry reports, another works through batches of
small bugs, and a third hunts slow pages. Agents also found a vendor
that will issue a trusted PDF-signing certificate for a few thousand
dollars a year, a job whose contracts once started at $50,000. The
constant switching between sessions left the founder exhausted at the
end of every day at first.

 LINKEDIN OUTREACH: HOW WE BOOKED 321 CALLS IN 2 MONTHS (5 MINUTE
READ) [12]

 Needle booked 321 LinkedIn calls in two months by writing to people
who had just commented on posts about the problem it solves. Those
commenters replied 34.2% of the time in a two-week test across 2,253
new prospects, against 9.9% for people who followed Needle's own
company page. AI finds the comments and drafts each note, and a person
approves every message. The team tried to remove that person with
hundreds of prompts and eval loops and kept the approval step.

MISCELLANEOUS

 REBUNDLING YOUR DATA STACK IS INEVITABLE. PICK YOUR BUNDLE CAREFULLY.
(6 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Fivetran and dbt Labs have merged, creating a $600M revenue
powerhouse, signifying a shift from unbundling to rebundling data
stacks. This strategic move by key players like Fivetran and Snowflake
offers fewer integrations but risks higher customer lock-in and
surprise billing. To navigate this, tech professionals should maintain
open formats, anticipate pricing changes, and ensure an easy exit
strategy to retain flexibility and control over their data
infrastructure.

 WHY I STOPPED SELLING A PROFITABLE $5M SAAS (5 MINUTE READ) [19]

 This founder stopped selling Groove, a $5M profitable SaaS, due to
stagnation and AI-driven revenue declines, and shifted focus to
Helply. Groove faced an "NRR zombie" phase with stagnant customer
growth and revenue loss from AI shrinking support teams, leading to
more seat downgrades than cancellations. Despite attempts to integrate
AI into Groove, they reallocated resources to Helply, closing Groove
signups, funded by its cash flow, to innovate with a new
product-driven strategy.
