NEWS & TRENDS

 SCHEDULING THE REMOVAL OF THE KUBE-PROXY IPVS BACKEND (7 MINUTE READ)
[7]

 Kubernetes plans to remove the kube-proxy IPVS backend in 2026,
ending support for that load balancing mode. The removal simplifies
the codebase and reduces maintenance overhead for the SIG Network
team.

 KITESURF IS NOW OPEN SOURCE (4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Cloudflare released Kitesurf, its experimental browser engine for AI
agents, under Apache 2.0. It parses HTML, applies CSS, executes
JavaScript, and renders screenshots on Workers without a browser
container, with Chrome DevTools Protocol support for clients such as
Puppeteer.

OPINIONS & TUTORIALS

 TECHNICAL DEBT IS SOCIAL; IT IS THE PRICE OF LEARNING BY DOING (3
MINUTE READ) [9]

 Technical debt is largely social, a price of learning by doing. AI
can generate software faster than teams can build shared understanding
of it, making repayment a sociotechnical task.

 HOW SALESFORCE EFFICIENTLY RIGHT-SIZES KUBERNETES WORKLOADS AT SCALE
ON AMAZON EKS (12 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Salesforce combined vertical and horizontal autoscaling across its
EKS fleet, reporting more than $5 million in cumulative infrastructure
savings. For Istio, VPA adjusts CPU and memory while HPA scales
replicas by proxy connection count, keeping the two controllers from
reacting to the same signal.

 IS YOUR OPENTELEMETRY PIPELINE BECOMING A PERFORMANCE BOTTLENECK? (10
MINUTE READ) [11]

 Telemetry collection consumes resources too: excessive attributes,
unbounded queues, and expensive processing can turn observability into
a bottleneck. This guide explains memory-limiter placement, sampling
trade-offs, and why tail sampling needs trace-aware routing when
Collectors scale horizontally.

 Claude-BugHunter is a skill bundle for bug hunting and external

 FOR PLATFORM TEAMS, THE CHALLENGE IS THE WAY (4 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Octopus Deploy's Steve Fenton uses Future of Platform Engineering
survey data to separate obstacles from challenges. A lack of clear
strategy is the top obstacle, while tooling sprawl is the top
challenge for high-performing teams.

 UBER'S SECURE AI FRAMEWORK GOVERNS AUTONOMOUS AI AGENTS ACROSS THEIR
LIFECYCLE (3 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Uber built a lifecycle framework that judges whether an AI agent's
proposed action fits the user's request, purpose, permissions, and
context. It combines pre-deployment registration, runtime behavioral
authorization, and post-deployment replay. Per-agent intents worked
better than generic thresholds.
