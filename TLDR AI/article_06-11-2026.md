 INTRODUCING INKLING 2 SMALL (37 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Inkling 2 Small is a Mixture-of-Experts model with 594B total
parameters, 17B active, that supports image inputs and variable
effort. The model's vision and agentic capabilities enable it to
incorporate visual feedback to iteratively build and refine websites,
games, and 3D designs with interactive elements. It will be initially
available through Tinker with closed weights. Full weights will be
available under an Apache 2.0 license next week after the completion
of external safety testing and red-teaming.

 THE BENCHMARK WHERE AIS HAVE TO LIE TO WIN (23 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Blood on the Clocktower is a social deception game. Watching AI play
the game unveils a lot about how AI is progressing on a set of AI
capabilities that no other benchmark gives clear visibility into. The
game requires AI to execute schemes with creativity, on-the-spot
bluffing, and collective reasoning. This post looks at what happens
when you pit AI models against each other in the game.

 HOW PRIMARY COMPUTE MARKETS WORK (7 MINUTE READ) [16]

 GPU providers sell capacity through reservations, on-demand rentals,
and interruptible spot contracts. Reservations help secure financing
through predictable revenue, on-demand offers flexibility at higher
rates, and spot monetizes idle capacity. Ornn's compute price indices
track executed on-demand transactions, excluding reservations and spot
rentals.
