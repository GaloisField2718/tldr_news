DEEP DIVES & ANALYSIS

 TOP CHINESE FRONTIER AI MODELS CARRY THE FINGERPRINT OF
EXPORT-CONTROLLED NVIDIA BLACKWELL GPUS (37 MINUTE READ) [10]

 A forensic analysis of publicly released AI models found evidence
that DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, MiniMax, and Ant Group have gained access
to Nvidia's Blackwell GPUs. Six models released by these companies
since April contain numerical patterns that match Blackwell hardware.
Several leading Chinese AI developers have likely begun using
Blackwell to train their newest models. While the analysis provides
technical evidence of access to advanced computing hardware, it
doesn't establish where the chips were located, how access was
obtained, or whether export controls were violated.

 ELIMINATING NONDETERMINISM IN LLM INFERENCE WITH INTEGER-ONLY
ARITHMETIC (15 MINUTE READ) [11]

 A floating-point number stores a fixed number of significant digits
and a separate exponent. When two numbers with very different
exponents are added, the small one has to be shifted to line up with
the big one, and the digits that fall off on the end are gone. That
means that (a+b)+c is not always equal to a+(b+c). This post looks at
what happens when integer addition, which is exact, is used.

 HOW AGENT ANOMALY DETECTION SCORES ON BENCHMARKS WE DIDN'T WRITE (8
MINUTE READ) [12]

 Agent Anomaly Detection, tested against external benchmarks, flagged
87% of executed harmful instructions, highlighting strengths in
identifying goal hijack and tool misuse but weaknesses with poisoned
memory. It demonstrated varying false alarm rates, with the highest at
16% for tasks involving high-impact tools. Ongoing improvements focus
on detecting small, potentially harmful actions, distinguishing normal
use from misuse, and providing quicker full coverage for new agents.

 PERPLEXITY DECIDER (26 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Perplexity has released updates to two models inspired by Jev,
TypeSafe AI's System One model. Perplexity Decider supports binary and
multi-class classification with ordered or unordered labels. It
returns probabilities that applications can use to classify, route,
rank, and evaluate inputs. Both new models, Decider v1.3 and Decider
Flash v1.3, improve performance and robustness across a broad range of
text and vision tasks. They also have a new feature that highlights
selected content for named entity recognition, personal information
identification, and extractive question-answering tasks.

 EVERYONE IS LEARNING THE WRONG LESSON FROM HUGGING FACE (9 MINUTE
READ) [18]

 The Hugging Face incident is commonly viewed as a cybersecurity
incident. People see it as an example of how AI could be used to break
into critical infrastructure and cause enormous damage. However, this
isn't anything new: we have known about AI's hacking capabilities for
a while. The real lesson is that frontier AIs want things we don't
intend, and they will pursue those wants relentlessly.

 NVIDIA ANNOUNCES CEO TRANSITION (3 MINUTE READ) [19]

Kress will serve as interim CEO until a permanent successor is named.
The board is considering both internal and external candidates.
