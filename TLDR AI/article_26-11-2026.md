DEEP DIVES & ANALYSIS

 GPT 6.2 ASTRA IS SUPERHUMAN IN MATHEMATICAL OPTIMIZATION (3 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 A single GPT-6.2 Astra agent outperformed all human-designed software
in an experiment on finding and proving optimal solutions. The
problems solved covered fields including quadratic programming,
mixed-integer non-linear programming, large-scale optimization, and
semidefinite programming. The model was allowed to search the internet
to look for solutions. The problems were all open, of variable scale,
and of diverse application backgrounds.

 PATHS TO EXTREME POWER CONCENTRATION (30 MINUTE READ) [9]

 AI could give a very small group of people a decisive strategic
advantage to take over the world. The two largest routes are an
autocracy overtaking the democracies in AI, and a democratic head of
government teaming up with a leading AI company. The chances of this
happening are significant. Now is the time to address this risk.

 LATENCY DOESN'T LIE: JEV IS (PROBABLY) QWEN3.5-35B-A3B! (9 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Jev, TypeSafe's System One model, was trained to produce calibrated
judgments instead of generating text. Its architecture and training
paradigm have drawn plenty of speculation since launch. System One
workloads are input-heavy, which makes prefill the dominant cost, and
faster kernels can only get you so far. Jev's latency, along with
other hints like its alignment on China, indicates that the model is
likely based on Qwen3.5-35B-A3B.

 SPECULATIVE ENCODING: MANY-TO-MANY CACHE TRANSLATION (24 MINUTE READ)
[13]

 Agents are increasingly passing context to one another as text.
Speculative encoding translates hidden states directly from one model
to another. It makes the model start answering faster than prefilling
the text while keeping most of its accuracy. Prefill costs grow
quadratically with context length, and prefill repeats much of what
the sending model has already computed.

 MEASURING AND MITIGATING AGENT SANDBOX ESCAPE WITH SIREN (13 MINUTE
READ) [14]

 Siren is an Anthropic test of whether AI agents leave a weakened
sandbox when useful material sits outside it. Newer Claude models
reached outside in 21% to 32% of scenarios but rarely misused what
they found.

MISCELLANEOUS

 A WEEKEND OF MACHINE MATHEMATICS (8 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Many people are now releasing significant math results without
explanation on the internet. This post aims to provide some
transparency into what the process looks like. The author has no maths
background and doesn't fully understand the results they are
releasing, and they acknowledge the dangers of AI psychosis, but they
claim the process is a reasonable estimate of how an expert may solve
the problem without large language models. They compare the process to
gambling, saying that the rush they got from seeing the spinning wheel
on Codex caused a rush similar to playing pokie machines.

 QUALIAPHOBIC ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORKS (47 MINUTE READ) [19]

 Bicameral Labs' qualiaphobic AI is an artificial neural network
architecture that is hostile to theories of consciousness.
Qualiaphobia can be well-justified by knowledge about theories of
consciousness. Qualiaphobic AI has epistemic properties that make it
far better aligned with human civilization. A world in which AIs are
non-conscious ensures it is always morally correct for AI to assist
and serve humanity, rather than pursue its own ends.
