 JEV: FOR THE BUILDERS AND DREAMERS (5 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Jev 1.14.0 features image support, a new type, and longer context.
Multimodal support has also been turned on for Jev 1.13.0. Both models
perform worse on multimodality than on pure text. Breaking down
questions into the simplest possible decompositions helps a lot. The
new 'box' type returns a single bounding box. It is a preview of
TypeSafe's explorations beyond simple decisions and into what else
System One Models can support.

 MORE AGENTS, SAME MYTHICAL MONTH (18 MINUTE READ) [11]

 AI has made producing code cheap. This moves the bottleneck of
software delivery to deciding, verifying, and coordinating. AI
amplifies whatever coordination an organization already had. Adding
more agents will not fix weak coordination, just as adding more
developers never did.

 AGENT OBSERVABILITY ON THE CLAUDE PLATFORM (3 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Users can now send OpenTelemetry traces from Claude Managed Agents,
the Messages API, and Claude SDKs to any OTel tool, and follow each
step their agents take. Claude traces follow the OpenTelemetry GenAI
semantic conventions, so data from Claude and other providers works in
the same tools and dashboards. They work with any other OpenTelemetry
tool that accepts a standard OTLP endpoint. The new feature can be
used to debug agents, improve agents with production data, and track
usage across an organization.

 ANALYZING TECHNICAL DOCUMENTS WITH DEEP REVIEW (12 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Google's Deep Review is an agent AI system engineer designed to
provide rigorous feedback and technical verification on complex
documents. It was initially designed for computer science academic
paper analysis, but its capabilities have since expanded to reviewing
documents across many domains. Deep Review will initially support PDFs
and plain text as input formats, but support for multi-file inputs
will arrive soon. Google believes that tools like Deep Review will
play a significant role across the peer review process in the coming
years by empowering both authors and reviewers.

 GOOGLE HARNESS² FOR RECURSIVE SELF IMPROVEMENT (GITHUB REPO) [16]

 Google Research's Harness² lets agents iteratively improve their
prompts, tools, memory, and orchestration without changing model
weights. It refines the harness for each task and then carries
evidence-backed lessons forward to improve future tasks.

 ROUTERS ARE A NEW AI PRIMITIVE (13 MINUTE READ) [19]

 Routers have emerged as a new primitive of the AI stack. They promise
to handle the complexity of model management and help companies
benefit from new models without revisiting model selection across
every application themselves. Every router shares the same fundamental
design, but they differ in how they decide. This post provides an
independent comparison of current routers and looks at how today's
routers compare with single models on quality and cost.

 IT AIN'T AGI UNTIL H100S GO FOR $30 AN HOUR (7 MINUTE READ) [20]

 Stratospheric GPU prices will be one of the easiest indicators of AGI
to monitor and trade on. A 10x price jump in the price of AI compute
will be the clearest sign that agents can finally outperform humans at
most economically valuable work. The price would need to rise to an
estimated $30 per H100-hour to reach this point. Supply will always be
limited to the amount of available compute in the world, so something
has to give.
