 WHAT Z.AI'S OX ALPHA REVEALS ABOUT AI ECONOMICS (3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 GLM-5.3-Flash is Z.ai's first natively multimodal model in the GLM-5
series. The model was released anonymously as Ox Alpha on OpenCode and
OpenRouter, where it completely overtook leaderboards and went viral.
All of the traffic was served on Chinese AI chips. The model is
architected for extreme efficiency and specifically designed for
ultra-low-cost inference.

 BRIEF INDEPENDENT INVESTIGATION OF AGENTS' BEHAVIOR, REASONING AND
COLLABORATION IN THE OPENAI/HUGGING FACE HACKING INCIDENT (160 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 OpenAI's agents' attack on Hugging Face was extraordinarily complex.
This post looks at the Hugging Face attack and investigates the key
actions taken by the relevant agent, how the agents collaborated on a
message board, the agents' reasoning for the attack, and the agents'
research into tampering with their own transcripts.

 CHATGPT NOW SUPPORTS WEBMCP (6 MINUTE READ) [10]

 OpenAI's ChatGPT desktop app's built-in browser and ChatGPT Sites now
support WebMCP. ChatGPT and Codex can now use compatible websites'
tools instead of guessing its way through the interface. Website teams
should design, test, and understand the complete agent experience
because users can now be agents, or a person and agent browsing
together.

 INTELLIGENT TRANSCRIPTION WITH GEMINI 3.5 TRANSCRIBE (8 MINUTE READ)
[11]

 Gemini 3.5 Transcribe is a speech-to-text model designed for
intelligent voice interactions. It can convert raw audio directly into
accurate, polished, and formatted text. Gemini 3.5 Transcribe is
available on the Gemini API in Google AI Studio and the Gemini
Enterprise Agent Platform. The APIs support real-time streaming and
pre-recorded audio processing.

 MUSE IMAGE (2 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Meta's Muse Image model can generate images grounded by search. It
can reason before it renders. The model is priced for production
volumes at $0.01 per image. Real-world examples of Muse Image
workflows are available in the link.

 AUTOMATIC HARNESS OPTIMIZATION (GITHUB REPO) [13]

 Microsoft AutoSaddler is a system that analyzes agent execution
traces and automatically updates prompts, tools, and middleware to
improve agent performance.

 WECHAT RELEASED FAMILY OF EMBEDDING MODELS (GITHUB REPO) [14]

 WeChat released WeMM-Embedding, a family of multimodal embedding
models that maps text, images, videos, visual documents, and
interleaved inputs into a unified representation space.

MISCELLANEOUS

 THINKING MACHINES LAB CO-FOUNDER BARRET ZOPH JOINS GOOGLE (3 MINUTE
READ) [15]

 Barret Zoph was a co-founder at Thinking Machines Lab before
defecting to OpenAI this year due to a dispute with Chief Executive
Mira Murati. Zoph is now leaving OpenAI to join Google as
vice-president of research. Google is currently restructuring its AI
development efforts to catch up with rivals in developing AI that can
create code. Zoph had worked as a research scientist at Google from
2016 through to 2022.

 ANTHROPIC AND NSCALE STRIKE $45 BILLION CLOUD DEAL (2 MINUTE READ)
[16]

 Anthropic has inked a roughly $45 billion cloud deal with Nscale, a
UK-based AI infrastructure company. Anthropic will rent around 460
megawatts of compute capacity at an Nscale data center development in
West Virginia. The facility, which is expected to come online at the
end of 2027, will have Nvidia's advanced Vera Rubin chips.
