HEADLINES & LAUNCHES

 OPENAI NEARS UP TO $30 BILLION FUNDING ROUND AT $1.25 TRILLION
VALUATION LED BY UAE INVESTORS (3 MINUTE READ) [6]

 OpenAI is closing in on a funding round that could value it at more
than $1 trillion. Investors from the United Arab Emirates are leading
the round. The round is being positioned as an opportunistic bridge
ahead of a potential IPO. OpenAI is aiming to secure signatures by the
end of next week, but the final size of the round is still being
determined.

 INTRODUCING 1PASSWORD FOR AGENTS (7 MINUTE READ) [7]

 1Password introduced a platform that lets AI agents authenticate to
websites without accessing users' passwords directly. Agents can
request credentials from a user's vault, and once approved, 1Password
autofills them into the destination site while keeping the credentials
hidden from the agent.

DEEP DIVES & ANALYSIS

 WORLDTUI: I TRAINED A 360M MODEL TO PRETEND TO BE A TERMINAL (18
MINUTE READ) [8]

 WorldTUI is a 360-million-parameter model fine-tuned to act as a
terminal. The model predicts how the screen will change for every key
press, and there is no shell or file system behind it. WorldTUI can
create files, run vim and tmux, play snake, and usually remember
commands. It can repair mistakes and decode screen rows in parallel. A
browser demo is available.

 HOW RESEARCHERS TESTED OPENAI'S INTERNAL AGENT MONITORING (6 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 METR and Redwood Research conducted a three-week security assessment
with access to OpenAI's internal infrastructure, identifying
vulnerabilities that could allow AI agents to evade monitoring, bypass
sandbox restrictions, or conceal actions.

 DOES FLAPPING AIRPLANES MEAN SHORT TIMELINES AND HARD TREATY
ENFORCEMENT? (7 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Flapping Airplanes' potential valuation jump from $1.5B to $5B may
reflect investor hype rather than verified technical progress.
Breakthroughs in sample-efficient AI could accelerate
superintelligence timelines and make compute-based treaty enforcement
harder.

 CLAUDE'S TEXT OUTPUT NOW CARRIES AN INVISIBLE WATERMARK (9 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 Anthropic's latest Claude models embed a SynthID-Text-based watermark
in all text output to comply with the EU AI Act. It works by nudging
choices among near-equivalent words, adds no hidden characters or
tokens, and carries no information about the user.

 HOW THE US AND CHINA COULD VERIFIABLY SLOW AI (29 MINUTE READ) [12]

 A unilateral US pause will fail because rivals would catch up within
about a year, so any slowdown needs a treaty that catches defection
early. This post surveys how one could govern compute through remote
sensing, on-site inspections, and hardware mechanisms like chip
licensing and location verification.

 HOW WE DEPLOY TRILLION-PARAMETER FRONTIER MODELS ON CEREBRAS (6
MINUTE READ) [14]

 A single Cerebras WSE-3T wafer has 44 GB of on-chip memory.
Trillion-parameter frontier models need terabytes of memory for their
weights alone. This post explains how the company splits a large
frontier model across multiple WSE-3T wafers. It covers how models are
filled into on-chip memory, how models are mapped across multiple
chips, and how Cerebras handles a high number of concurrent requests.

 A NOVEL SELF-DISTILLATION METHOD (7 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Perplexity's JOLT is a self-distillation method that jointly trains a
model as both teacher and student, adapting feedback to the student's
capabilities. It improved performance and training efficiency across
mathematical reasoning, coding, and agentic tasks.

 NVIDIA'S VIDEO GENERATION WITH MATCHING 3D MOTION (4 MINUTE READ)
[16]

 NVIDIA has introduced Joints, Camera, Action! (JCA), a system that
generates videos and synchronized 3D motion for arbitrary character
rigs from text prompts. It supports scene-aware motion, keyframe-based
control, and bidirectional conversion between video and rig animation.

MISCELLANEOUS

 AN INTERVIEW WITH OPENAI'S RICHARD HO ON WHY JALAPEÑO IS A
GROUNDBREAKING AI CHIP (22 MINUTE READ) [17]

 OpenAI's Jalapeño AI inference chip outperforms its rivals in
performance per watt. It is likely the most promising chip program in
the latest wave of non-GPU AI chips. This post contains highlights
from a conversation with one of the top engineers in the AI and chip
industry. The interview is packed with technical details and insights
on where chip design and memory technology are going.

 WHAT HAPPENED TO THE MISSING GEMINI 3.5 PRO MODEL THAT NEVER SHIPPED?
(1 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Google had both Flash and Pro versions of the 3.5 series. However,
there were gaps in the model's agentic capabilities, particularly
around coding. The team felt the fastest way to climb that hill was to
use Flash, as the smaller model allowed for more iterative loops. By
the time the team had improved Flash's abilities, Argon pre-training
was done.
