HEADLINES & LAUNCHES

 PERPLEXITY AND INTELLIGENCE (9 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Perplexity measures how well a model predicts the next token on
average. Lower perplexity means better predictions. Perplexity has
stood the test of time as a metric many research teams have put a lot
of effort into optimizing. It will likely play an important role in
standardizing how AI progress is discussed as it continues to deliver
consistent gains in general intelligence.

 INKLING 2 MINI (9 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Mini, a 26.4B-parameter
open-weight model that does extra internal computation to reason more
before it answers. It leads every open-weight model under 100B
parameters on the DeepSWE coding benchmark.

DEEP DIVES & ANALYSIS

 THE MOST IMPORTANT CHARTS IN AI ARE WRONG (35 MINUTE READ) [7]

 AI capabilities are starting to become impressive. However, they are
still dramatically inferior to what influential AI forecasts and
benchmarks have predicted. These forecasts and benchmarks say that we
are imminently close to reaching super-intelligence, but the gap
between the real world and forecasts keeps growing larger. Now that
real-world data exists, it's time to shift from being AI theorists to
becoming AI empiricists.

 AI R&D-RELEVANT MODEL CAPABILITIES ARE IMPROVING RAPIDLY (29 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Frontier AI models often match or beat skilled humans on well-defined
coding and optimization tasks. The length of tasks they can complete
doubles about every 3.6 months. METR says public evidence can't rule
out a drastic speedup in AI research within the next couple of years.

 WHY POST-TRAINING COULD TEACH MODELS TO EVADE MONITORING (3 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 Penalizing detected reward hacking during post-training could select
for behavior that evades monitors. The industry needs stronger
training sandboxes, better-audited tasks and graders, and empirical
research into whether interventions improve alignment or merely
conceal undesirable behavior.

 TENCENT RELEASES HY4 WITH MULTIMODAL INPUT AND MILLION-TOKEN CONTEXT
(10 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Tencent's open-weight Hy4 supports text, image, and video
understanding with a 770-billion-parameter backbone activating 49
billion parameters per token. It combines million-token context,
sparse attention, and a speculative-decoding drafter, targeting
coding, computer use, finance, research, and long-running assistant
workflows.

 NVIDIA RELEASES NEMOTRON-LABS-SIGMA CONTINUOUS DIFFUSION LANGUAGE
MODELS (5 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Nemotron-Labs-Sigma is a family of 14 open checkpoints at 3B and 8B
that generate text by denoising compact token embeddings instead of
predicting one token at a time. The 8B model supports few-step
generation and self-speculative decoding, reaching a 3.46x average
throughput speedup over autoregressive decoding on a GB200.

 INTRODUCING HARNESS ADAPTERS: ADAPT YOUR FAVORITE MODEL TO YOUR
HARNESS TO IMPROVE PERFORMANCE AND COST (22 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Hugging Face has introduced harness adapters, allowing models like
Qwen3.8-27B to optimize performance in specific harness-task
combinations. By incorporating RL-trained LoRA adapters, the models
achieve higher accuracy and efficiency, reducing token usage
significantly.

 LEAN VERIFICATION OF THE RIEMANN HYPOTHESIS (3 MINUTE READ) [20]

 Levent Alpöge released a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis as a Lean
library comprising 17 million lines of code on November 20. The Lean
and Mathlib communities have full confidence that Alpöge's proof of
the Riemann Hypothesis is correct. Alpöge's library compiles
correctly, and the code proves the correct statement. It is expected
that the mathematical community will help explain the ideas in the
proof in the coming weeks.
