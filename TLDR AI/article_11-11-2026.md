HEADLINES & LAUNCHES

 GEMINI 4 CARBON IS NOW AVAILABLE (12 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Google's strongest model is now available to all paying developers
and enterprises through the API in AI Studio and Gemini for business,
and is rolling out to Google AI Ultra subscribers in Antigravity, the
Gemini app, and AI Mode in Search. Google says Carbon ranks first on
CursorBench 4.0, Perplexity's WANDR, and Zapier's AutomationBench at
more than 2x lower cost per task than the next best external model, at
the same $2/$10 per million input/output token price as Gemini 4
Argon. The release adds frontier cybersecurity capabilities through
the Fairwind Program and previews /swarm, a multi-agent mode in
Antigravity.

 WORKING WITH CLAUDE SHEETS, WHITEBOARDS, AND INTERACTIVE CONNECTORS
(5 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Anthropic introduced Claude Sheets, Whiteboards, and Data, new
artifact types that let teams build spreadsheets, organize ideas, and
create dashboards with Claude without switching apps. Interactive
connectors now embed partner apps like Adobe and Atlassian directly in
Claude, and plugins from Salesforce, NetSuite, and Workday (in beta)
ship with built-in skills for sales, finance, and HR work. Claude
Meetings, which will bring Claude into calls to take notes and update
documents, is coming soon.

 BUSINESSES AND BUSINESS AGENTS IN CHATGPT (3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 OpenAI introduced Businesses in ChatGPT, allowing companies to claim
their profiles, update business information, provide product feeds
with pricing and availability, and track customer engagement. Eligible
e-commerce and online service businesses in the US can also launch
Business Agents that answer questions and help customers explore their
offerings inside ChatGPT. The launch is in beta.

DEEP DIVES & ANALYSIS

 Q3.5 TIMELINES UPDATE: LOOKS LIKE THE INTELLIGENCE EXPLOSION WILL
HAPPEN IN 2027 AFTER ALL (125 MINUTE READ) [9]

 A Superhuman AI Researcher (SAR) is predicted to arrive at some point
in 2027, assuming there is no substantial deliberate slowdown in
development. AIs that can automate all white-collar work are predicted
to arrive before 2028. Companies are currently not on track to align
an SAR, and using a misaligned SAR to do recursive self-improvement
will have negative consequences. China will probably be able to steal
the training recipe and maybe even the model weights if an SAR is
trained in the near future.

 NEURONS AS AN INTERPRETABLE BASIS (15 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Goodfire argues that individual language-model neurons are more
interpretable than commonly assumed. Its Neuron Scientist agents
generate and test descriptions of neuron functions, suggesting
researchers can study model computations directly without always
relying on sparse autoencoders to extract interpretable features.

 SUPPRESSING REWARD HACKING THROUGH MODEL WEIGHT ARITHMETIC (3 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 Researchers subtract code models trained with loose and strict
rewards to identify a direction controlling inefficient solutions.
Extrapolating beyond the strict model reduced resource-limit failures
from 14.5% to 8% without additional training, though excessive
extrapolation breaks outputs and stricter rewards can hurt
correctness.

 WHY INSTINCT FEELS LIKE A PERSONAL ASSISTANT (20 MINUTE READ) [12]

 A twelve-week analysis of one Instinct conversation attributes its
appeal to short messages, quick acknowledgments, proactive follow-ups,
and approval checks before purchases or outreach. The teardown
suggests coordinated task agents and persistent memory underpin the
experience, while exposing retention and delivery-verification
failures.

 MAI-TRANSCRIBE-2 (125 MINUTE READ) [14]

 MAI-Transcribe-2 is the fastest, most accurate, and cheapest speech
recognition model in the world. It has new features like diarization,
configurable transcription styles, and word-level timestamps.
MAI-Transcribe-2 can handle a broader range of real-world audio than
leading models like Gemini 3.5 Transcribe, GPT-Transcribe, Whisper
V3-Large, and ScribeV2. The model costs just $0.10 per hour of audio.

 SWARM (/SWARM) (10 MINUTE READ) [15]

 The /swarm command in Google Antigravity runs a team of named agents
that split tasks, work on the pieces at the same time, and coordinate
on one shared message board. It can be used for tasks that divide
cleanly across separate files, packages, or questions, or when a
second agent is needed to review and test the changes before a human
sees it. The /swarm command is available on Google AI Pro and Google
AI Ultra plans in the Google Antigravity App and Antigravity CLI.
Every worker is its own agent, so swarms use quotas much faster than
single conversations.

 META RELEASES FASTER K-MEANS ALTERNATIVE (GITHUB REPO) [16]

 Meta FAIR released e-means, a drop-in replacement for k-means that
converges to lower losses with up to 10x fewer epochs and faster
runtimes on CPUs and GPUs.

 OPENAI ACQUIRES ODESSIA, TRAVEL STARTUP CREATED BY SONDER FOUNDER (3
MINUTE READ) [18]

 OpenAI has acquired an AI travel agent called Odessia. The deal
appears to be an acqui-hire. OpenAI has not announced new
travel-specific products or plans, but it has several travel-related
companies as customers or partners. Odessia's founder previously
founded a travel startup called Sonder, focused on short-term leasing
of upscale apartments, as a college student in 2014.
