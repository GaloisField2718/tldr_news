HEADLINES & LAUNCHES

 SIRI AI IS NOW OPEN TO ALL DEVELOPERS (4 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Developers can now integrate Siri AI into their apps with the iOS
27.3 beta. This update allows apps to leverage Siri's advanced
features, like natural language processing and personal context
understanding, enhancing user interaction. Siri AI is currently
unavailable in the EU and for users under 13. It supports multiple
languages, with more to come.

 BASETEN INTRODUCES GRID, SANDBOXES, AND SERVERLESS LOOPS (5 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 Baseten announced Grid for model routing and governance, resumable
VM-isolated Sandboxes for agent execution, and general availability of
Loops for serverless supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement
learning. The products connect training, inference, and execution,
with customers retaining ownership of trained weights.

 FIREWORKS LAUNCHES FASTER AI INFERENCE AND ENTERPRISE TOOLS (5 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Fireworks AI unveiled new products at Forge, including Ultra-fast
inference reaching up to 1,500 tokens per second, lower-cost
background processing, and Nexus for accessing multiple AI models
within existing tools. It also introduced capabilities for model
evaluation, fine-tuning deployment, and enterprise-controlled
infrastructure.

DEEP DIVES & ANALYSIS

 HOW MUCH DOES REASONING EFFORT AFFECT SCORE AND COST? (8 MINUTE READ)
[9]

 Lower reasoning effort reduces cost much more than it lowers the
quality of results. Tests show that models on low reasoning can match
models on high reasoning at lower cost. The impact of reasoning effort
depends on the task.

 TRAINING A TEXT-TO-IMAGE MODEL FOR $21K (13 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Linium is a two-person research lab that is trying to train its own
generative video models entirely from scratch, aiming to create tools
to democratize access to animation. Pyramid-JiT (P-JiT) is a
pixel-space architecture that achieves aggressive token reduction
through a noise schedule that helps pixel-space models better learn
fine-grained detail. This study looks at how models trained on the
architecture perform when trained for longer as a pressure test along
the way towards larger models and video. Examples of images generated
by the resulting model are available.

 LEARNINGS FROM AUDITING PUBLIC EVALUATIONS (8 MINUTE READ) [11]

 The article outlines issues faced during public evaluations of LLMs
and details improvements made to benchmarks such as AUTOMATIONBENCH
and OFFICEQA PRO V2. They replaced ambiguous prose descriptions with
JSON schemas for better performance evaluations and introduced new
grading systems to improve accuracy. A multi-layered monitoring system
is suggested to prevent manipulation and ensure accurate measurement
of model capabilities.

 GEMINI NOTEBOOK API (1 MINUTE READ) [13]

 The Gemini Notebook API is a REST interface for programmatically
creating and managing notebooks and sources in Gemini Notebook. It can
turn web pages, YouTube videos, plain text, and Markdown into
persistent notebooks. The API and MCP server are in an early access
Public Preview. Only personal Google Accounts are supported during the
preview - Google Workspace accounts are not yet supported.

 PROGRESS ON PROVABLE INFERENCE FOR LARGE LANGUAGE MODELS (30 MINUTE
READ) [14]

 It is almost impossible to verify which model produced a specific
output. Cryptographic proofs of inference allow model providers to
prove that preach suppose was produced which a specific model. These
proofs could enable new applications and contribute to a toolkit for
verifying compliance with AI pacing requirements. This post details
the progress made on a powerful type of cryptographic proof uniquely
fit for provable inference called zKSNARK.

 ACCELERATING ENGINEERING FOR THE PHYSICAL WORLD (6 MINUTE READ) [15]

 OpenAI is leveraging its AI models to enhance mechanical and
electrical engineering processes, from design to simulation and
production, speeding up internal workflows and improving industry
practices. Engineers utilize models like GPT‑6 Astra for tasks such
as device design, CAD model creation, and performance simulations,
while collaborating with companies like Mazda to explore applications
in vehicle aerodynamics.

 DEEPSEEK PLANS TO TRAIN ITS NEXT-GENERATION MODEL ON HUAWEI ASCEND
CHIPS (3 MINUTE READ) [16]

 DeepSeek plans to use Huawei's Ascend AI chips to pretrain DeepSeek
V5. Ascend chips have previously been used primarily for inference
rather than pretraining. This will be an important test of whether
Chinese AI accelerators can support the full pre-training of a
frontier large language model. A successful run could reduce Chinese
AI developers' dependence on Nvidia hardware and strengthen Huawei's
position as an alternative for advanced AI computing.

 OPENAI: CHATGPT SEARCH IS ABOUT 20% OF GOOGLE'S, 250 MILLION PEOPLE
SPEAK WITH CHATGPT, AND CHAT IS MERGING WITH WORK (16 MINUTE READ)
[17]

 ChatGPT's search volume has reached 20% of Google's, with ChatGPT
rapidly gaining 200 million users in the past two months, leading to
1.2 billion weekly active users. The voice mode of ChatGPT now engages
250 million weekly active users, emphasizing new interaction methods
through GPT Live updates that enable natural bidirectional
conversations. Plans to merge Chat and Work modes aim to simplify user
interaction, while Dots highlights innovations in AI task management
despite high compute costs.
