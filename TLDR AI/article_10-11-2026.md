 TWO KINDS OF TEST-TIME SCALING (28 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Test-time scaling trades compute for performance at test time. There
are two types of test-time scaling: one allows researchers to trade
more compute for more performance per task, but only up to the maximum
quantity of per-task compute used during training; and the other lets
researchers trade more compute for more performance per task, and lets
them do so up to quantities of compute and performance that never
occurred during training. Scaling methods that permit the latter
change will have a larger impact than test-time scaling considered
simply. This essay describes how this may impact the world.

 WHAT OBJECTIVE IS REASONING SOLVING? (12 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Reasoning is pathfinding in token space. There are many paths from
the problem to a correct answer, and the model traverses each of them
with each sample. A path is not guaranteed to converge to the correct
answer - it's only in aggregate, over the distribution of paths, do we
get a high probability of success. RL training directly raises the
success probability. The gains credited to 'thinking longer' come from
the search paid for during training rather than at inference.

 NVIDIA INTRODUCES HARDWARE-AWARE LLM COMPRESSION (24 MINUTE READ)

 NVIDIA's AutoCompress framework optimizes LLM pruning for
hardware-specific latency, throughput, and memory constraints rather
than parameter counts alone. It compressed Nemotron 3.5 Super from
120B to 74B parameters while retaining its performance.
