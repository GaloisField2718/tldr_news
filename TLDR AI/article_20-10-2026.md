HEADLINES & LAUNCHES

 MINIMAX M3.1 FLASH: REDEFINING AGENT PERFORMANCE AND COST (4 MINUTE
READ) [4]

 MiniMax M3.1 Flash delivers a generational leap in agentic tasks,
coding, Computer Use, and cybersecurity. It makes online inference
nearly three times faster and reduces usage costs by 70%. The model is
now available through the MiniMax API, M Plan, and MiniMax Code.
MiniMax plans to release the model weights at a later date.

 MERCURY 3 (4 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Inception Labs' Mercury 3 is a diffusion-based language model that
reportedly outperformed GPT-6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5 on multiple
benchmarks while generating over 1,000 tokens per second.

 INTRODUCING CLAIRIC, A NEW SYSTEM ONE CLASS OF EXTRACTION MODELS (16
MINUTE READ) [6]

 Clairic can extract, compute, estimate, and forecast values from
unstructured inputs. It is an 'extraction model', a new class of
System One-style model that returns values directly extracted from the
input, computed from it, or predicted beyond it. The model doesn't
generate any tokens, so it can't produce a mistyped answer or
hallucinate in the same way LLMs can. It can still make mistakes, but
its outputs come with confidence scores to help users evaluate whether
it is appropriate to trust the model or to take a second look.

 INSIDE META'S GIGAWATT-SCALE AI SUPERCLUSTER (14 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Meta details the engineering behind Prometheus, its gigawatt-scale AI
supercluster supporting the Muse model family. It explores its
infrastructure design, plans to scale toward roughly one million GPUs,
and closed-loop liquid cooling technology.

 AI BENCHMARKS: CLIMBING THE WRONG HILLS (24 MINUTE READ) [8]

 AI benchmarks are now shaping how models are evaluated in training
and the capabilities they are designed to have. Poorly constructed
benchmarks could push the industry in the wrong direction. Creating
benchmarks impervious to benchmaxxing requires expertise in measuring
what we actually want from AI systems. As benchmaxxing continues,
researchers must decide which hills they want to climb and whether
today's benchmarks actually reward progress in that direction.

 IMPORT AI 477: IN WHICH I TELL THE IMF THE SINGULARITY HAS BEGUN (22
MINUTE READ) [9]

 AI's impact on national security and economic trajectories marks the
onset of the singularity, accelerating scientific and technological
progress. AI systems like Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini are already
solving complex problems in disciplines like mathematics, biology, and
robotics, indicating potential for widespread innovation. To
capitalize on these advancements, there needs to be significant
infrastructure development and careful governance to navigate the
associated risks and ensure equitable benefits.

ENGINEERING & RESEARCH

 INTRODUCING EXA 3.0 (3 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Exa 3.0 leads on evaluations spanning coding, finance, deep research,
and company intelligence. The release includes Exa Lite, a new search
type priced at $4 per 1,000 requests. Exa runs its own crawler and an
independent index, tracking over 2 trillion URLs, serving 100 billion
pages, and growing around what its customers search for. The index
includes 1.27B ecommerce listings with prices and availability, over
211 million local businesses with hours and ratings, real-time news,
sports, weather, and market data, and information on flights, local
events, transit, and podcasts.

 INTRODUCING TORCHNEURON: NATIVE PYTORCH BACKEND FOR AWS TRAINIUM (27
MINUTE READ) [11]

 TorchNeuron is an open-source native PyTorch backend for Trainium. It
uses the model code, the libraries, and the tools developers already
use on Trainium. TorchNeuron supports eager execution for research and
debugging. It has been released in public Beta under the Apache 2.0
open source licence.

 OFFICE INTELLIGENCE FOR LONG-RUNNING AI WORK (3 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Office Intelligence explores how AI agents can use evidence, create
useful work artifacts, and carry knowledge across tasks. The project
focuses on environments, datasets, and learning methods for sustained
knowledge work.

 Anthropic and Nscale may be on the verge of announcing another $45

 AI LAB OFFERS FREE PLUMBING AND HVAC REPAIRS ACROSS THREE CITIES TO
TRAIN AI ROBOTS (2 MINUTE READ) [14]

 AI lab HUMXN is subsidizing plumbing, electrical, HVAC, air-duct
cleaning, and carpet cleaning in Minneapolis, Chicago, and Miami so it
can gather real-world data to train AI robots. The laborers doing the
jobs will wear cameras to record their work inside real homes. People
living in the three cities the program is running in can check whether
they qualify on the HUMXN Free Home Service webpage.
