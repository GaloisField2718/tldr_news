 GOOGLE INTRODUCES GENIE 4 (5 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Genie 4 is a world model that generates photorealistic, interactive
environments with synchronized audio and real-time prompt-based
editing. It can maintain consistent simulations for up to two minutes,
doubling Genie 3's duration, while improving physics and character
realism.

 LIQUID AI LAUNCHES LFM3 MODELS FOR ON DEVICE AI (27 MINUTE READ) [9]

 LFM3 is a family of open weight text, vision, audio, omni, and
decision models built on a shared tri-hybrid backbone, starting in the
2B parameter range. The 1.7B text model beats its 2.6B predecessor
with 35% fewer parameters, fits in about 1 GB at 4 bit precision, and
supports a 256K context window for agents running on device.
LFM3-VL-2B adds video input and matches Gemma 4 12B across 62 vision
benchmarks, and every model in the family fine-tunes on a single GPU.

 FORMALIZING THE ENORMOUS THEOREM (27 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Axiom Math has released the first complete computer-checked proof of
the Classification of Finite Simple Groups (CFSG). The original CFSG
proof is considered to be the longest mathematical proof ever written
by humans. It spans roughly 15,000 pages in more than 500 articles
written by about 100 mathematicians. Engineers and mathematicians at
Axiom Math directed swarms of dozens of AI agents over 24 days in a
project that produced approximately 43.1 million lines of code in the
Lean proof assistant.

 WHERE ARE THE WORLD'S AI CHIPS? (1 MINUTE READ) [11]

 The AI Chip Imports explorer estimates AI compute arrivals by country
since 2022, enhancing the mapping of the global AI chip supply chain.
It complements existing data on AI chip components, sales, and data
centers. These resources collectively provide a detailed overview of
AI compute allocation and control worldwide.

 ANTHROPIC VERSUS ANTHROPIC (17 MINUTE READ) [12]

of a conventional business approach, sparking debate about their
commitment to AI safety and global responsibilities.

 THE SLEEP FRAMEWORK: A NEW WAY FOR LLMS TO RETAIN KNOWLEDGE IN
CONTINUAL LEARNING (11 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Researchers have developed a new way to enable large language models
to integrate new facts and enhance reasoning without catastrophic
forgetting or static knowledge cutoffs. The sleep framework equips
large language models with a continuous periodic lifecycle, enabling
them to continually learn and consolidate fragile short-term context
into stable long-term parameters. Alternating between active
interaction and offline memory consolidation allows LLMs to
continuously acquire new facts, retain long-context knowledge up to
10M tokens, and improve complex reasoning.

 FRONTIERCODE 2.0 (13 MINUTE READ) [15]

 FrontierCode 2.0 provides broader coverage, higher mergeability
standards, and a larger task scope while largely retaining the
methodology of FrontierCode 1.0. The benchmark now accommodates
surprising but acceptable behavior. Tasks are drawn from more
repositories and span more categories, and grading criteria now impose
more extensive correctness and design requirements. Reference
solutions now modify 67% more files and 89% more lines of code on
average compared to FrontierCode 1.1, in codebases that are 25%
larger.

 ANTHROPIC'S CLAUDE REFUTES THE EXPONENTIAL TIME HYPOTHESIS, WITH A
LEAN PROOF (GITHUB REPO) [16]

 Anthropic says an internal research Claude model found, with no human
input, a deterministic algorithm that counts the satisfying
assignments of 3-SAT formulas in 2^O(n log log n/√log n) time, which
would make 3-SAT subexponential and refute ETH. This repository holds
the paper and a Lean 4 formalization written by AI models that builds
in about 8 minutes, though Anthropic notes no machine checks that the
Lean statement matches the paper and the result says nothing about
practical SAT solving. It landed hours after OpenAI's own ETH
refutation.

 PROACTIVE VIDEO AGENTS (22 MINUTE READ) [17]

 StreamVibe-VL is a vision-language model that infers implicit user

 META'S INTERACTIVE SIMULATOR FOR WEARABLE AI (GITHUB REPO) [18]

 InteractiveWorld is a simulation framework for training and
evaluating wearable AI assistants. It models interactions where an
assistant observes through a user's eyes, communicates with them, and
receives updated first-person views.
