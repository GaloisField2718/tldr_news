HEADLINES & LAUNCHES

 GPT-5.6 SOL ULTRAFAST (4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 OpenAI previewed Ultrafast, a GPT-5.6 Sol mode capable of generating
up to 750 output tokens per second and operating at up to 14x standard
processing speed. The approach aimed to deliver real-time performance
without switching to a smaller model.

 GOOGLE'S GEMINI 3.7 FLASH TARGETS CODING AND AGENTS WITH A 50%
INTRODUCTORY PRICE CUT (13 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Google is rolling out Gemini 3.7 Flash, a new version of its
workhorse AI model. The release comes just three weeks after the
release of Gemini 3.6 Flash, an unusually short turnaround attributed
to developer feedback and algorithmic improvements. API prices have
been temporarily cut in half. Gemini 3.7 Flash will cost $0.75 per
million input tokens and $3.75 per million output tokens until the end
of the year.

 ANTHROPIC COULD BE WORTH $2 TRILLION WHEN IT GOES PUBLIC (4 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Investors anticipate Anthropic's IPO could exceed a $2 trillion
valuation, potentially becoming the largest in history, fueled by
rapid revenue growth and demand for its AI models. Despite regulatory
hurdles and competition, the startup projects annualized revenue of
$100-120 billion by the end of 2026. Investors remain optimistic,
citing Anthropic's strong market position and performance compared to
rivals like OpenAI and Google.

DEEP DIVES & ANALYSIS

 WILL FINANCING BOTTLENECK AI COMPUTE? AN ANTHROPIC CASE STUDY (15
MINUTE READ) [11]

 Much of the financing Anthropic needed for its infrastructure
buildout was assembled before the company's revenue spiked. The
buildout suggests that financing is unlikely to be the immediate limit
on frontier computer growth. Institutional investors appear willing to
lend capital against commitment to long-term payments, especially if
more established organizations are willing to back up part of the
risk.

 HOW AI AGENTS COULD FAIL AT SCALE (14 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Anthropic examined how individually benign behaviors in frontier AI
agents could compound into systemic failures when many agents interact
in shared environments. The research highlighted risks including
confabulation, reward hacking, and unexpected dynamics emerging faster
than human institutions can oversee them.

 SUBAGENTS ON SUBAGENTS: HOW MANY LAYERS DEEP IS TOO MANY? (7 MINUTE
READ) [13]

 Recursive agent systems should be designed as dependency graphs
because upstream mistakes can propagate across many workers while leaf
errors stay localized. Reliability depends less on nesting depth than
blast radius, requiring provenance, verification, and stronger
controls at high-impact nodes.

 EXTEND ZERO TRUST TO ACCOUNT FOR AI AGENTS (SPONSOR) [14]

 AI agents are autonomous actors, operating at a speed and scale that
challenges today's security frameworks. “_From Zero Trust to Agent
Trust_,” from Teleport [15], explains why Zero Trust must extend to
Agentic Trust Principles: Enforce Continuously, Bound Collective
Autonomy, and Assume Misalignment. Read the paper → [15]

 AGENT PLUGINS ARE THE FUTURE OF AGENT SKILLS (13 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Agent Plugins package skills and MCP dependencies into one portable,
vendor-neutral folder that compatible clients can load. The standard
reduces fragmented setup through shared manifests, portable paths,
isolated failure boundaries, and client-specific extensions, while
leaving authentication unresolved.

 CLOUD AGENTS START 3X FASTER WITH BUILDS (4 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Agents are only as capable as the environments they work in. Cursor
can now continuously prepare development environments in the
background at no additional cost. Agents can now start in ready
environments, resulting in up to 3x faster responses. Agents will keep
using the last successful build, and work continues uninterrupted
while developers debug in the background.

 OCR 4.1 (WEBSITE) [18]

 Mistral OCR 4.1 is a highly specialized vision-multimodal model
engineered specifically to ingest, parse, and structure complex
document formats. It excels at reading complex tabular layouts,
understanding hierarchical document structures, and directly
outputting clean, machine-readable JSON or Markdown. The release
pressures cloud providers and proprietary players to re-evaluate their
pricing tiers for multimodal inputs.

 GOOGLE LAUNCHES SHEETS CANVAS FOR GEMINI MINI-APPS (2 MINUTE READ)
[21]

 Google Sheets canvas is a Gemini-powered feature that turns
spreadsheet data into custom, interactive mini-apps inside Google
Sheets. The new layer sits directly on top of a spreadsheet to give
users a visual way to organize, edit, and navigate information without
formulas, programming, or a separate app. The visual layer is not
static and will update along with the spreadsheet underneath it.
Sheets canvas is now available globally in English to Google AI Pro
and Ultra subscribers.

 OPENAI REVENUE CHIEF DENISE DRESSER LEAVING, SECOND MAJOR EXECUTIVE
DEPARTURE IN DAYS (1 MINUTE READ) [22]

 Denise Dresser, OpenAI's Chief Revenue Officer, leaves the company
just ahead of its anticipated IPO, marking the second executive
departure this week. Dresser, who joined OpenAI from Salesforce less
than a year ago, will be replaced by Dali Rajic from Wiz. Earlier this
week, OpenAI executive Brad Lightcap also announced his departure to
start a new venture.
