HEADLINES & LAUNCHES

 MUSE SPARK OPEN (4 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Muse Spark Open is an 816B-parameter multimodal open-weight model
with 42B active parameters and a 1M-token context window. It was
designed for coding and long-horizon agentic tasks and can run locally
across two Apple M5 Ultra Mac Studios using MLX.

 INSIDE MICROSOFT'S FRONTIER PRE-TRAINING STACK (18 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Microsoft detailed the training of MAI-Base-2, a 2.8T-parameter
mixture-of-experts model with 80B active parameters that it said
surpassed every open-weight model in its held-out pre-training
evaluations. The deep dive covers its data strategy, compute
efficiency, full-stack infrastructure, and Microsoft's plans to turn
internally trained frontier models into enterprise offerings through
Foundry.

 D1-ULTRA: INTRODUCING THE WORLD'S BEST DECISION MODEL (3 MINUTE READ)
[8]

 Liquid AI's d1-ultra is a multimodal decision model designed for
complex real-world tasks like detecting defects on circuit boards. The
model processes text and images and provides better-calibrated answers
and higher accuracy than previous models like Jev 1.14. Available on
the Liquid API, d1-ultra is priced at $0.042 per million input tokens
and will soon be accessible via Vercel and OpenRouter.

DEEP DIVES & ANALYSIS

 NEURAL NETWORKS ARE ALREADY CLEAN (65 MINUTE READ) [9]

 The cause of polysemanticity in early model organisms was thought to
be superposition. This convinced much of the field that networks pack
concepts arbitrarily, but what looked like arbitrary packing was
mostly recursive generalization, which is less concerning. This means
networks can be studied directly, instead of needing to be untangled
first. The outlook for mechanistic interpretability may be far better
than we thought.

 THE GRADIENT IS A COVARIANCE (8 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Reinforcement learning, on-policy distillation, and supervised
fine-tuning on near-policy data change only a small fraction of
parameters. Small and sparse are different claims. The expected
gradient is a covariance, the Fisher is a variance, and the Fisher
caps the gradient for any reward. At one position, the Fisher is the
variance of logit sensitivities: whatever moves every candidate
together counts for nothing. None of this needs on-policy sampling -
it needs a score centered under the sampling distribution.

 MO' TURNS, MO' PROBLEMS (6 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Doing reinforcement learning for agent use-cases is conceptually not
at all that complicated. For each problem, you set up a VM with a
harness and a fresh model context, send the prompt, let the model make
tool calls, score the whole thing at the end, and update the weights.
Agentic post-training means there's more than the model to consider.
Even when the harness is consistent, you can still go off-policy with
the trajectories themselves. Agents spawn subagents, make parallel
tool calls, and compact large sessions, which adds complexity.

 KREA 3 (WEBSITE) [13]

 Krea 3 is an image editing model built for production. It can finish
an edit in under eight seconds, costing only $0.06 per image. The
model was built for production and is four times faster than Nano
Banana Pro. It can combine up to eight references into one picture.

 NVIDIA RELEASED NEMOTRON 3.5 SUPER VL (5 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Nvidia released Nemotron 3.5 Super VL, a multimodal reasoning model
that scored 26.5 on Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index while
using substantially fewer output tokens than its predecessor. The
company also introduced the smaller Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B and
near-lossless NVFP4 quantized weights.

and dots. It is rolling out now to all users and coming soon to the
