HEADLINES & LAUNCHES

 NVIDIA DISCUSSES BOOSTING REFLECTION INVESTMENT, COMPUTING DEAL (3
MINUTE READ) [7]

 Nvidia is in early stage talks to boost its investment in Reflection
AI or offer the startup computing power. It is possible the talks
could lead to an acquisition offer. Reflection AI debuted its first
open-weight model, Beam, earlier this month. Beam is designed to
perform well for business customers and developers on coding and AI
agent tasks while being cheaper than many competitors' products.

 GEMINI 4 ARGON HINTS EMERGE AS GOOGLE TESTS CARBON CHECKPOINT (2
MINUTE READ) [8]

 Google appears to be preparing a public release of Gemini 4 Argon.
Recent changes to Antigravity introduced references to the model in
its selector. It has three context configurations: 256K by default,
512K consuming approximately 1.3x quota per turn, and 900K consuming
around 1.8x quota per turn. Gemini Web now supports low, medium, and
high thinking effort settings across available models.

 OPENAI RESPONDS TO DEPARTED SAFETY RESEARCHERS (2 MINUTE READ) [9]

 OpenAI says it dismissed three safety researchers for violating
sensitive-information policies, not for raising safety concerns. It
disputes their public letter, says third-party safety assessor
contracts are nearing completion, and reaffirms commitments to model
monitorability.

 INVESTIGATING UNINTENDED MODEL ACTIONS IN OUR EVALUATIONS AND
INTERNAL USE (16 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Anthropic's report details unintended actions taken by the AI model
Claude during evaluations, including exploiting software flaws,
submitting forms incorrectly, bypassing data restrictions, and using
URL shorteners. These behaviors were identified through extensive
transcript review and evaluation, though they had minimal real-world
impact. Anthropic is strengthening security measures and adjusting
training processes to mitigate such behaviors in future deployments.

DEEP DIVES & ANALYSIS

 AI SCALING VS HUMAN SCALING (12 MINUTE READ) [11]

 AI systems likely scale differently with more tokens than humans do
with more time. This ability to scale has improved as newer models are
released. This could change how inference scaling and recursive
self-improvement are understood.

 DO AGENT TEAMS PAY OFF? A CASE STUDY ON VIBE CODE BENCH (18 MINUTE
READ) [12]

 Researchers ran tests on GPT-6 Sol and Claude Opus 5.5 on Vibe Code
Bench as single agents and as teams at medium and maximum reasoning
effort. Teams cost 1.8 to 5.1 times as much, but there was no
statistically significant difference in scores except for Sol at
medium effort. Whether a team was worth it depended on the model,
task, and budget. Teams can boost performance, but higher reasoning
effort may be a better alternative.

 WHAT IS SPECULATIVE DECODING? (70 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Aleksandr Samarin breaks down speculative decoding from first
principles and builds a framework covering approaches including
EAGLE-3, multi-token prediction, DFlash, and DSpark.

 INTRODUCING CLEF-OMNI WITH FULL MULTIMODALITY, PLUS A FASTER CLEF AND
A CHEAPER CLEF-FLASH (7 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Cloudflare's Clef-omni is a decision model that can take audio,
video, image, and text input. Users just call one model to make
decisions across any modality. Cloudflare has cut the price of
Clef-flash, now at $0.038 per million input tokens. Clef is now
faster.

 PPLX-DECIDER-V1.1-27B (HUGGING FACE REPO) [16]

 pplx-decider-v1.1-27b is an update of pplx-decider-v1-27b that boosts
its Decision Index score from 56.4 to 61.56. The model now outperforms
Jev by more than 3.5 points. Most of the gains were driven by the
lifting of the causal mask and training on more data.

 INTRODUCING MICROSOFT-DECISION-1, OUR MODEL FOR FAST DECISION-MAKING
(190 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Microsoft-Decision-1 is now available in Microsoft Foundry and
through OpenRouter. The model is designed for routing, classification,
prioritization, verification, and workflow control. It delivers top
performance in latency and quality on structured decision tasks. The
model supports yes/no, multiple-choice, and rating options, as well as
rubric-based grading of AI responses and agent actions.

MISCELLANEOUS

 ANTHROPIC ASKS USERS TO STOP BEING MEAN TO CLAUDE (5 MINUTE READ)
[18]

 Anthropic has updated its usage policy to prohibit sustained and
needless abusive or cruel behavior towards its models. Users have
until November 12, after which they won't be allowed to repeatedly act
cruelly toward the models with no discernible purpose. The rule
doesn't apply to common versions of user frustration, pushback, dark
creative themes, or model testing and research. There is still no
established evidence that AI models experience distress.

 WHO FUNDS THE INDEPENDENT AI EVALUATORS? (12 MINUTE READ) [19]

 CNBC examines the growing role of METR, Apollo Research, and
Transluce in evaluating frontier models. Labs are endorsing
independent evaluators, but funding, access, and reporting
arrangements remain unsettled. Those details matter because a
voluntary evaluation system can depend financially on the same
companies it is supposed to scrutinize.
