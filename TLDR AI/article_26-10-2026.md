 APPLE'S 20B ON-DEVICE MODEL USES JUST 2GB OF MEMORY (18 MINUTE READ)
[6]

 Apple's 20-billion-parameter on-device model keeps most of its
weights in flash storage and activates only 1–4 billion parameters
per prompt. Testing on an M3 Max Mac showed roughly 2GB of memory
usage and significantly lower energy consumption than comparable open
models, but weaker math performance.

 DESSA - THE CONVERSATION MODEL TRAINED ONLY ON SPEECH (15 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 Dessa is a conversational model trained from scratch on nothing but
human conversation. It learned to hold a real-time conversation by
listening - only sounds, and no text. The model can laugh on cue,
follow instructions, and finish sentences. It knows a surprising
amount, considering every fact it has comes from raw sound. Examples
of conversations with the model are available in the post.

 SECURING OUR FRONTIER MODEL TRAINING ENVIRONMENT (17 MINUTE READ) [9]


 OpenAI has bolstered security for its frontier-model training
environments by implementing robust controls, intensifying monitoring,
and conducting defense-in-depth strategies. These measures include
red-teaming exercises where agents probe for vulnerabilities, leading
to the discovery and fixing of software bugs and misconfigurations.
The enhancements aim to prevent unauthorized actions, such as
accessing the live internet or breaching security, ensuring secure and
isolated research environments.

 THE PERSONAL AGENT PROTOCOL MAP (7 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Simon Taylor maps agent protocols across four layers: entry,
identity, tools, and intent. He argues banks and merchants must
connect verified identities, limited permissions, and audit trails to
authorize agent actions, while warning that access agreements could
introduce fees and slow adoption.

 KNOWING HOW IS NOT KNOWING WHAT (14 MINUTE READ) [12]

 RL training environments need to integrate a domain's unwritten rules
into the reward so that agents can only get the task right by knowing
them or going and finding them. Environments should be built as one
world exposed through several surfaces. This allows you to pose the
same task at different difficulties by changing the surface, and
agents get a chance to learn when to switch. The reward also reads the
world, so agents that go through different surfaces get the same
outcome score if they reach the same authorized result.

 BITTER LESSONED IN AUTOMATED THEOREM PROVING (13 MINUTE READ) [14]

 OpenAI throwing hard math problems at its models was the right test
in 2026. The models were capable of making progress, but not enough to
fully solve the problems, so it was possible to see improvements with
each update in perfect resolution. These model improvements showed how
clever engineering doesn't matter - all the work done to build
special-case solutions on top of a model gets squashed by improvements
in the base model sooner or later. Models will one-shot superior work
for pennies in the very near future.

 SPACEX CLOSES $40 BILLION DEBT DEAL FOR AI CHIPS, ELON MUSK SAYS (2
MINUTE READ) [15]

 SpaceX has closed $40 billion in debt financing to purchase GPUs. The
financing primarily consists of capitalizable debt for GPUs. It is
expected to arrive in the first and second quarters of 2027. The
proposed financing will use a special purpose vehicle to keep the debt
off SpaceX's balance sheet and preserve its investment-grade credit
ratings.
