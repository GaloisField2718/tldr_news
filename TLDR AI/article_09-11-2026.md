DEEP DIVES & ANALYSIS

 A YEAR AS A RESEARCH ENGINEER WORKING ON RSI AT META (8 MINUTE READ)
[8]

 This post details the experience of a research engineer working on
RSI at Meta teaching agents to solve the world's hardest problems with
tokens and compute. Their role is to teach AI agents how to train the
next generation of models and self-evolve. The post details how to get
into AI research, what skills matter in research engineering, and
more.

 WHAT THE HECK IS GOING ON WITH OPENAI INTERNAL AI R&D TIME HORIZONS
(7 MINUTE READ) [9]

 OpenAI's report on its own research automation shows that models
struggle to do its R&D work. Recursive self-improvement is supposed to
be one of the most consequential AI developments in the next few
years. Agents are increasingly solving more complex tasks for
researchers, but reliability is still low. This post looks at some
possible explanations for why.

 HOW ANTHROPIC BUILDS AGENT MEMORY (14 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Anthropic explored how AI agents can build effective long-term memory
by capturing experiences during interactions and reorganizing them
during idle periods through a process called “dreaming.” The post
examines how different memory components contribute to organizing and
retaining useful information.

 ACE (WEBSITE) [12]

 Ace is a tool for building software together, locally. It allows
developers to collaborate with agents and their teams, all from their
own machine. Agents run with the privileges of the user running the
host and can execute shell commands and change files beyond the
project. Teams can work together without exposing anything to the
public internet.

 INTRODUCING THE NEW AUTO AND AUTO CODE ROUTERS (11 MINUTE READ) [13]

 OpenRouter has launched the next generation of Auto Router, which
outperforms the old router by up to 4x. The launch includes Auto Code,
a new router specialized for coding work. The new Auto Router and Auto
Code choose models by weighing eleven factors about each request and
combining them with live pricing, provider health, and cache status.
Users can apply any governance policies, and Auto Router makes
efficient choices from the models available.

 A PROOF OF THE AARONSON-AMBAINIS CONJECTURE (9 MINUTE READ) [14]

 The Aaronson-Ambainis conjecture, a key open problem in quantum
complexity theory, has been proven by a research system developed at
PSI using an Anthropic model. This proof shows quantum computers are
not exponentially faster on random inputs compared to classical ones,
resolving a significant question about quantum speedups.

 THE END OF AI FRONTIERS (4 MINUTE READ) [15]

 AI Frontiers was a sub-organization within Microsoft Research that
actively tried to organize coherent research efforts. It succeeded in
pioneering things, with much of the work focused on how to make agents
work. AI Frontiers was reorganized into Microsoft AI in early

 WHAT DOES A POSITIVE AI FUTURE LOOK LIKE? (3 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Patrick Collison questions how work, resource allocation, and human
control would function in a positive AI future. He also argues that
granting moral worth to vastly numerous AIs could overwhelm human
interests under utilitarianism, raising unresolved questions about
AI's societal vision and public support.
