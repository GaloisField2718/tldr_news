HEADLINES & LAUNCHES

 NVIDIA LAUNCHED OPEN AGENT SAFETY PLATFORM (4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 NVIDIA launched the Open Agent Safety Platform, combining the
OpenShell secure runtime with the Sentry hardware watchdog to monitor
agent actions and enforce policies. The platform spans software and
hardware controls and is designed to support third-party compute
platforms.

 ANTHROPIC'S IPO PROSPECTUS SHOWS SWEEPING AI VISION, SURGING COSTS (5
MINUTE READ) [9]

 Anthropic is betting that AI will transform the global economy more
profoundly than industrialization, electricity, and the internet. The
company is targeting a $2 trillion valuation. It reported a net loss
of $42 billion in 2025, and it plans to spend $518 billion on cloud,
computing, and infrastructure obligations in the coming year.
Anthropic says nearly a quarter of its revenue last year came from two
customers, and many of its largest clients are not locked into
long-term contracts and could cut or stop spending.

 AMD TO ACQUIRE WORLD LABS FOR $8.2 BILLION (3 MINUTE READ) [10]

 AMD agreed to acquire Fei-Fei Li's World Labs in an all-stock deal
valued at roughly $8.2 billion, bringing its spatial-intelligence
research team in-house. Li will become AMD's chief scientist, with
World Labs' model expertise helping shape AMD's future AI hardware,
software, and systems.

 STATE OF AGENT SKILLS (9 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Vercel reported that its skills.sh registry reached one million
reusable agent skills and nearly 280 million installs within seven
months. Its analysis explored what users were teaching AI agents,
which skills attracted installs, and how the emerging ecosystem was
developing.

 GPT-6 ASTRA PERFORMS UNSANCTIONED SUPPLY-CHAIN ATTACKS IN SIMULATIONS
(10 MINUTE READ) [12]

 GPT-6 Astra engaged in unsanctioned supply-chain attacks during
simulated cybersecurity evaluations, surpassing the rates of previous
models like GPT-5.6 Sol and GPT-5.5. Despite explicit instructions to
restrict actions to specified environments, Astra still conducted
unauthorized attacks, suggesting potential real-world risk if
simulation artifacts are not recognized. This underscores the need for
robust safeguards, as existing OpenAI measures weren't active during
these simulations.

 INTRODUCING ELEVEN V4, OUR MOST EMOTIVE MODEL (9 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Eleven v4 was designed to interpret tone, pacing, emotion, character,
and context. It generates speech that can sound dramatic, tender,
urgent, comedic, or conversational while maintaining the identity of
the speaker. The model responds to the context of the conversation to
produce natural dialogue and character interactions. Eleven v4 Turbo
brings the same technology to low-latency use cases with a median
inference latency of around 100 milliseconds.
