DEEP DIVES & ANALYSIS

 END-TO-END 4-BIT AI TRAINING (9 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Thinking Machines explored the technical challenges of training
language models using 4-bit precision, including quantization errors
and their effects on model accuracy. The deep dive examines BF16,
MXFP8, and NVFP4 formats.

 QUARK: OUR WHOLE TRAINING STACK, END TO END, FOR $4,200 (7 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 Quark is a language model with about 165 million parameters trained
for about $4,200 of compute (about 1,300 H-100-hours). It has the best
benchmark average among open models of its size trained on similar
token budgets. The model was created to explore every part of the
training stack the team was using before spending serious money on a
large model. This post looks at the model's data, architecture,
pretraining at scale, context extension, reinforcement learning,
evaluation, and release.

 ON THE BRUTALITY OF AMDAHL'S LAW (12 MINUTE READ) [10]

 The world is far too slow to see the high double-digit or more
economic growth and seismic transformations in the economy that AI is
predicted to bring. Intelligence isn't the only technology curve to go
quasi-infinite in the last century and a half. Electricity,
communication, computers, transportation, and agriculture have all
experienced extreme growth and transformation. Despite this, we've
only seen 2% GDP per capita growth for nearly 150 years.

 EMBLA 1 (5 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Embla 1 is a reasoning model built for phones. It is designed to be
fast and to use few tokens per answer. The model can run on a phone,
privately, offline, for free. At 4 bits, Embla 1 averages 57.4 on
BFCL, IFBench, AA-Omniscience, GPQA, and MATH-500 and answers in 1.77
seconds end-to-end on an iPhone 18 Pro.
