DEEP DIVES & ANALYSIS

 CLAUDE WAS TRAINED ON PROTEIN SEQUENCES AT SCALE (26 MINUTE READ) [8]


 Tests show that the latest Claude models were trained on protein
sequences at scale. The models can predict the next amino acid if
given the start of little-studied bacterial proteins. No other model
tested can do this, and the models fail to predict the sequence if the
protein is shuffled. Fable 5.2 beats every open protein language model
tested.

 WORLD 2030 (8 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Predicting how AI will affect the economy requires looking at all of
the different components of that economy and figuring out how they
might affect each other. World 2030 is a study that sets up nine
groups (AI labs, big employers, banks and insurers, utilities,
startups, universities, workers, governments, and the Fed) and runs
the US economy forward in six-month turns to 2030. It aimed to
identify both the invariants and the boundaries of what can change as
these powerful forces interact with each other. The study predicts
that AI will break per-seat software pricing and cut US junior desk
hiring.

 MEASURING ALIGNMENT EVAL TELLS (16 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Investigator agents can increasingly distinguish alignment evals from
capabilities evals, identifying them as artificial or "honeypot"
environments designed to test bad behavior. Opus 5.5 and Astra agents
differentiate these by noticing unrealistic scenarios and specific
task-level cues. The study's findings suggest that while current
models can identify common alignment evaluations, making them less
detectable can refine their efficacy.

 D1.2: STATE-OF-THE-ART OMNIMODAL DECISION MODELS (5 MINUTE READ) [12]


 d1.2 and d1.2-ultra are decision models that support audio input.
They can also read a value from the input, find every passage of a
kind in a text, locate things in a picture, estimate a number, and
pick every option that applies. d1.2-ultra ranks first among decision
models on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2.1. Both models are
available on the Liquid API at $0.02 per million input tokens for d1.2
and $0.042 per million input tokens for d1.2-ultra.

 HUGGING FACE AND OPENROUTER JOIN FORCES (6 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Hugging Face now routes language-model requests from its Inference

for each call. A single Hugging Face access token works for any
open-weight model a provider serves.

 UNLOCKING STABLE ON-POLICY RL ON TPU WITH BITWISE-ZERO
TRAIN–INFERENCE MISMATCH (7 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Google's ZERO-TIM implementation, verified across 64 TPU v5p chips,
achieves bitwise-identical logprobs for sampler and trainer, ensuring
true on-policy stability in RL training by eliminating numerical
discrepancies. This solution addresses floating-point addition errors
in separate serving and training paths, using consistent arithmetic
operations and standardized reduction orders across TPU meshes.

 OPENAI'S FIRST PHASE OF EMBEDDED ASSESSMENTS (11 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Embedded assessments give independent experts deep access to a lab's
internal models and systems so they can assess model alignment and the
effectiveness of safeguards over an extended period. The access gives
assessors the evidence and context to pursue independent evaluations
and uncover unanticipated failures. Public reporting from independent
assessments can give the public a clearer view of how labs are
evaluating and managing risks. OpenAI has signed agreements with four
independent assessors for its first phase of embedded assessments.

 THE FRONTIER IS STILL OPEN (16 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Perplexity Frontier Research is a new initiative at Perplexity that
aims to build new algorithms, models, and evaluations in the open,
with the mission of building an automated scientist. Applications are
now open until December 5. The research effort will be supported by
10,000 GPUs. Results from the initiative will ship as papers, blog
posts, model weights, and open repositories.
