HEADLINES AND LAUNCHES

 CHINA CURBS HUMANOID IPOS AFTER UNITREE'S VOLATILE DEBUT (3 MINUTE
READ) [4]

 Chinese regulators are tightening approval for humanoid robotics
startups seeking to go public, following a volatile trading debut from
sector leader Unitree Robotics, as they try to cool a sector described
as sizzling but rife with copycats and little real technological
breakthrough. The China Securities Regulatory Commission's tighter
posture is expected to affect other humanoid IPO hopefuls, reportedly
including names such as AGIBOT and Galbot.

 CALL FOR OPEN-SOURCE ROBOTICS BENCHMARKS (3 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Robocurve, a public benefit company backed by YC, has opened a
community bounty program to build reproducible open-source evaluation
benchmarks for physical AI and autonomous robot systems. To make these
benchmarks happen, it is giving away $20,000 and a pair of I2RT ABC
Box robot arms to 25 teams who propose the best research benchmarks.
Robocurve hopes to accelerate the development of Physical AI by
increasing the number and diversity of dexterous robotics benchmarks.

ENGINEERING AND APPLICATIONS

 BENCHMARKING LIDAR SLAM ON LIVOX MID-360 DATASETS (3 MINUTE READ) [6]


 This open-source repository presents a comparative benchmark
evaluating four popular LiDAR odometry and SLAM algorithms—KISS-ICP,
DLIO, FAST-LIO2, and GLIM—on live aerial drone flight data collected
with a Livox Mid-360 LiDAR and Pixhawk flight controller. Running
directly on a Jetson Orin NX embedded computing platform, the project
compares trajectory accuracy and map reconstruction quality against
millimeter-grade VICON ground-truth data. These empirical findings
provide developers and perception engineers with actionable
performance data to choose and tune state estimation algorithms for
compute-constrained autonomous platforms.

 XIAOMI 18 FOLD BEATS APPLE'S IPHONE FOLD TO MARKET BY A DAY, PACKING
A TELEPHOTO LENS APPLE REPORTEDLY SKIPS (3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Xiaomi's 18 Fold launched in China a full 24 hours ahead of Apple's
rumored iPhone Fold, packing a wide-style, passport-sized chassis with
a 5.38-inch outer OLED and 7.58-inch inner display, both hitting 4,000
nits peak brightness with a 1-120Hz LTPO refresh rate and, notably, an
RGB sub-pixel structure that's a first for any foldable. Xiaomi
prioritized battery over thinness, fitting a 6,000mAh cell (with 67W
wired charging) into a 5.02mm-unfolded body that's still slightly
thicker than Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold8.

RESEARCH AND PROTOTYPES

 ROBOTOK: AN INTERNET-SCALE DATA ENGINE FOR HUMAN DEMONSTRATION VIDEO
RETRIEVAL AND DEXTEROUS MANIPULATION LEARNING (5 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Data collection for Physical AI is an expensive problem blocking the
development of more advanced models. To get around this bottleneck,
researchers at Rice University have developed RoboTok, an automated
data engine that indexes, filters, and processes large-scale human
demonstration videos from the internet to train manipulation policies.
By extracting hand trajectories and object interactions directly from
uncurated web video, the system learns complex, multi-stage
manipulation skills without requiring physical teleoperation. This
approach showcases how massive online video datasets can be converted
into scalable training data to overcome physical data collection
bottlenecks.

 ORION: A DIY 12-DOF QUADRUPED ROBOT DOG (3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Orion is an open-source, 12-degree-of-freedom quadruped robot
designed, fabricated, and programmed from scratch using ROS 2, custom
3D-printed components, and serial bus servos. The repository provides
complete mechanical CAD models, kinematic calculations for dynamic
walking gaits, and step-by-step firmware integration guides. This
project provides engineering teams and builders with an accessible
reference architecture for prototyping low-cost legged locomotion
platforms.

MISCELLANEOUS

 MAGIC TRICK PERFORMING ROBOT (3 MINUTE READ) [10]

 They say any sufficiently advanced technology is indistinguishable
from magic. Sean Tsai, an engineer at Foxconn, has built an
interactive robot that performs classic sleight-of-hand magic tricks
for human audiences. It integrates computer vision to track spectator
choices and low-latency motor control to execute precise, synchronized
mechanical deceptions in real time. It illustrates how combining
high-speed visual tracking with fine motion planning can significantly
enhance interactive entertainment and human-machine collaboration.

 NASA SPACE ROBOTICIST CHALLENGE (4 MINUTE READ) [11]

 NASA is sending a seven-axis robot into low-Earth orbit, and they are
looking for researchers who would like to test their code in the
challenging environment of microgravity. The NASA Fly Foundational
Robots (FFR) Mission is a competition where teams develop autonomous
software to show off the potential benefits and challenges of space
robotics. Do you think you have what it takes? The last day to submit
an application is September 23.

 IONQ PRICES A BITCOIN-CURVE ATTACK DOWN TO 19,397 IONS AND 26 DAYS
PER ATTEMPT (5 MINUTE READ) [13]

 IonQ published a 70-page resource estimate concluding that a
trapped-ion quantum computer with 19,397 physical qubits, built on its
“Walking Cat” architecture, could compute a 256-bit elliptic-curve
discrete logarithm on secp256k1, the curve securing Bitcoin and
Ethereum, in about 25.7 days per attempt.

LENSES (5 MINUTE READ) [14]

domestic lithography machines.

 QUANTINUUM GETS $100M CHIPS ACT FUNDING TO BRING 300MM WAFER
MANUFACTURING TO TRAPPED-ION HARDWARE (3 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Quantinuum secured $100 million in CHIPS and Science Act funding, the
only trapped-ion architecture selected in this funding round, to
tackle a core scaling problem: the specialized ion traps, control
electronics, lasers, and optics behind its quantum processors get
harder to manufacture consistently as systems grow larger.
