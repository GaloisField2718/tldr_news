HEADLINES AND LAUNCHES

 TRUMP SAYS THE US WILL TAX AMERICAN COMPANIES THAT BUILD DATA CENTERS
ABROAD, CITING CHINA'S ACCESS TO RENTED COMPUTE (2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 At a White House press conference, Trump promised a "tremendous tax"
on US firms building data centers overseas, specifically naming a
proposed Google facility in Finland. Chinese firms rent restricted
GPUs from data centers in countries that can legally import them,
which is the route Commerce's new licensing rule targets. Operators
like Equinix and Digital Realty run facilities abroad partly to cut
latency for time-sensitive workloads, so a penalty could complicate
global buildout.

 US INTELLIGENCE REPORTEDLY TRACKED ALICE HUANG'S CONTACTS WITH
CHINA'S SPY AGENCY FOR OVER A YEAR, WHILE MEGASPEED SAT INSIDE
NVIDIA'S CLOUD PARTNER NETWORK (2 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Alice Huang, a Megaspeed executive, was in touch with a Ministry of
State Security officer from Spring 2025 until this fall, overlapping
with her relationship with Jensen Huang. Her business partner, Kang
Wei, worked with the same agency. Trump was briefed at least twice.
Megaspeed, the Singapore operator suspected of diverting restricted
Nvidia chips, is now a focus of the investigations, which are
continuing.

ENGINEERING AND APPLICATIONS

 TESLA ROLLS OUT FSD V15 WITH A VISION ENCODER SHARED ACROSS FSD,
ROBOTAXI, AND OPTIMUS, CLAIMING 25X BETTER CRITICAL INTERVENTION RATE
(3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 The encoder is trained on millions of internet videos and runs on AI4
(HW4) hardware. A new Reality View replaces separate model signals
with one end-to-end visualization that uses less compute. v15 also
targets known weak spots, including sign reading, potholes, standing
water, school zones, and speed cameras, but Tesla published only the
25x multiplier, not the underlying intervention rate. It also loosens
driver monitoring at higher-speed profiles.

 BOOM SUPERSONIC LIGHTS ITS IN-HOUSE ENGINE CORE FOR THE FIRST TIME, A
SIX-STAGE COMPRESSOR AND SINGLE-STAGE TURBINE SHARED BY A DATA CENTER
TURBINE AND OVERTURE'S ENGINE (2 MINUTE READ) [10]

 The core reached 6,500 rpm with nearly 2,000 sensors on its second
light-off attempt, on a Colorado rig that uses a vacuum pump to
simulate the bypass duct and a variable nozzle to simulate the
low-pressure turbine. The same core underpins the 42 MW Superpower gas
turbine for AI data centers and the 40,000 lb Symphony turbofan for
the Mach 1.7 Overture, with over 75% commonality. Boom is also
building a tooling-free foundry for single-crystal turbine blades.

 GOOGLE'S QUANTUM PROCESSOR WINS A CONTEXTUALITY GAME 98.3% OF THE
TIME AGAINST AN 88.9% CLASSICAL LIMIT, ACROSS TASKS UP TO 105 QUBITS
(2 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Contextuality means a qubit's measured value depends on which
compatible measurements are made alongside it. New research shows it
survives at scale instead of only in few-particle systems. The same
game doubles as a sensitive test for subtle hardware errors, which
helps validate processors for chemistry and materials science. The
qubits also beat constrained classical strategies on tasks spanning up
to 105 qubits.

 A ONE-CRYSTAL-THICK FILM OF MIXED NANOCRYSTALS CAN BOOST OR BLOCK
INFRARED LIGHT, AND A NEW MODEL SHOWS HOW TO TUNE IT (3 MINUTE READ)
[13]

 Researchers mixed two kinds of tiny crystals, differing in how much
tin they contain, into a film just one crystal thick. When infrared
light hits it, each crystal's electrons ripple at a slightly different
rate, and those ripples overlap in the tiny gaps between neighbors.
The film is made in solution, so it avoids expensive etching, and it
could be used in thermal camouflage or self-cooling surfaces.

 SPACEX LENDERS SOUGHT COVENANTS BARRING NVIDIA CHIPS SECURING A $43B
DEBT PACKAGE FROM BEING LAUNCHED INTO ORBIT (2 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Lenders fear that if SpaceX defaults, repossessing and reselling GPUs
installed in a satellite in orbit would be effectively impossible.
SpaceX says the chips are for US data centers it owns and operates,
but Musk's orbital compute plans depend on launching silicon aboard
Starship. Whether the restriction made it into the final documents,
led by Apollo and Goldman Sachs, remains unclear.

 SILICON PHOTONICS IS THE NEXT STEP FOR AI DATA CENTERS (3 MINUTE
READ) [15]

 With construction spending above a $75 billion annual pace and about
3,000 buildings planned or underway, phase-one architecture choices
will carry into capacity arriving at the end of the decade. The
interconnect between chips sets power per token, and moving data as
infrared light through silicon waveguides removes the reach limit.
Photonics-SOI, PD-SOI, and RF-SOI platforms each optimize for
different metrics and aren't interchangeable.
