HEADLINES AND LAUNCHES

 OPENAI'S MODELS REWRITE RTL FOR UP TO 14% PPA GAINS ON AMD'S FLAGSHIP
GPU IP AND 10 TO 13% SMALLER BLOCKS ON ITS JALAPEÑO CHIP (3 MINUTE
READ) [6]

 The models rewrote SystemVerilog for AMD's already-optimized GPU
blocks, with some changes in a design sent for manufacturing and
single-digit gains verified on its next flagship CPU. On Jalapeño,
the matrix and SIMD blocks shrank 10% to 13%, confirmed in silicon,
partly by rewriting adders and multipliers, including a multiplier cut
from two cycles to one. OpenAI is also training GPT-Synopsys with
Synopsys to drive EDA tools directly.

 GOOGLE BOOKS A DEDICATED NEW GLENN LAUNCH IN Q1 2029 FOR PROJECT
SUNCATCHER, ITS ORBITAL TPU COMPUTE CONSTELLATION (1 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Each Suncatcher satellite carries roughly a data center rack's worth
of computing and runs on solar power in sun-synchronous orbit, forming
an interconnected network of TPUs for machine learning compute. The
mission is Google's first dedicated heavy-lift launch for the project,
using New Glenn's large payload volume. The booking is for 2029 even
though New Glenn's hotfire anomaly investigation is still ongoing.

ENGINEERING AND APPLICATIONS

 RELATIVITY FINISHES TERRAN R SECOND-STAGE TESTING AND INTERSTAGE
STRUCTURAL QUALIFICATION AS STAGE ONE INTEGRATION NEARS COMPLETION (3
MINUTE READ) [8]

 The second stage hot-fired at NASA Stennis's A2 stand, and the
interstage passed structural testing on Long Beach's “Tender Loving
Crusher” rig. The thrust structure, housing all 13 Aeon R engines,
fluid lines, and avionics, is being mated to stage one. At LC-16, the
launch table framing is done and a second pad lift cylinder has
arrived. Relativity still targets a late 2026 debut, though observers
expect 2027.

 DETACHABLE FIBER CONNECTORS ARE EMERGING FOR CPO AND NPO BECAUSE
STANDARD FIBERS CAN'T BE REWORKED, TRAY-HANDLED, OR TESTED AT VOLUME
(15 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Fibers bonded to photonic chips can't be reworked if one breaks,
dangle during pick-and-place, and eat die-edge shoreline, since 125
µm cladding adds up across 16 fibers. Designs now bring light out at
the edge or through a 45° mirror, with lenses, snap-fit alignment, or
magnets replacing active alignment. Senko and Advantest demonstrated a
test connector surviving 10,000 matings with little loss variation,
but there's still no standard interface.

RESEARCH AND PROTOTYPES

 A 3D-PRINTED, ELECTRONICS-FREE SOFT HEXAPOD HITS 4.72 M/S BY REUSING
COMPRESSED AIR LIKE A COMPOUND STEAM ENGINE (3 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Higher air pressure speeds up a pneumatic robot but wastes energy, so
the team routes air through small high-pressure chambers that power
the valves, then through larger low-pressure chambers that move the
legs, as in a compound steam engine. The six-legged robot is printed
in one piece from one soft material and runs about 100 times faster
than the 2022 version, over sand, mulch, and concrete, and in water.
It's still tethered to an air compressor.

 SHAPE TRAINS A VLA ON EGOCENTRIC HUMAN VIDEO WITH DISCRETE ACTION
TOKENS AND TWO PARALLEL ACTION STREAMS FOR A BIMANUAL ROBOT WITH
22-DOF HANDS (3 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Human video only gives retargeted end-effector actions, not the joint
commands robots need. SHAPE's Qwen3-VL-4B backbone predicts discrete
EEF action tokens as an auxiliary task, while a shared flow-matching
expert outputs separate EEF and joint streams. Joint readout scores
0.80 across six tasks versus 0.63 for π0.5, and EEF readout via IK
transfers better from humans. After human-data pretraining, it learns
new tasks from human demos with no task-specific robot data.

MISCELLANEOUS

 META REPORTEDLY PRICES ITS POCKET-SIZED MUSE CHARM AT $300 WITH A
FREE YEAR OF VERIZON 5G, DELAYING ITS SMARTWATCH TO 2027 (2 MINUTE
READ) [12]

 Charm has a roughly 2-inch screen navigated with a thumb and a
customizable Muse character at its center, and it carries the Muse AI
agent into the real world. The bundled 5G data, up to 12 months from
Verizon, is meant to speed Muse's responses away from Wi-Fi. Meta
plans a December launch and pushed its smartwatch to 2027 to make
room, and it didn't deny the pricing report.

 CALIFORNIA'S ATTORNEY GENERAL SUBPOENAS NVIDIA OVER WHETHER ADVANCED
CHIPS OR CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION REACHED CHINA (2 MINUTE READ) [13]

 AG Rob Bonta served an investigative subpoena asking whether Nvidia's
chips or confidential data ended up in China, and whether Nvidia
misled investors about that risk under California securities law. The
probe follows Jensen Huang's resignation and centers on Megaspeed, the
Singapore data center operator suspected of diverting restricted
chips, plus how Nvidia reports overseas revenue. Nvidia's market value
has dropped over $1 trillion in two weeks, and it hasn't commented
yet.
