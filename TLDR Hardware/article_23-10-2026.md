HEADLINES AND LAUNCHES

 AGILITY'S DIGIT 5 HUMANOID ROBOT WILL RUN ON NVIDIA AS IT HEADS FOR
NASDAQ (4 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Agility Robotics will use Nvidia's IGX Thor modules as the exclusive
onboard computer for the general-sale version of Digit 5, with early
access starting in the first half of 2027. The robot also adopts parts
of Nvidia's Halos safety platform, whose inspection lab will check
Digit's safety software before outside certification. Agility reports
more than $300 million in multi-year Digit 5 orders, four new
customers, and a new Pittsburgh site, as it prepares to list on Nasdaq
via a merger with Churchill Capital Corp XI.

 INTEL'S CTO SAYS TOOLS CAN ONLY ROUGHLY DOUBLE LEADING-EDGE CHIP
OUTPUT, WHILE AI DEMAND GROWS 3X A YEAR (50 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Dwarkesh Patel toured Intel's fabs and interviewed its leaders. CTO
Pushkar Ranade said that there are enough tools to roughly double
leading-edge output, not 10x. Backside power lets Intel's 14A print
with a single High-NA pass, where TSMC would still need
double-patterned EUV. Other topics discussed included ruthenium wiring
below roughly 20nm pitch, CFET aspect-ratio and heat challenges,
hybrid bonding pitches shrinking toward 0.45 microns, and the Terafab
partnership.

ENGINEERING AND APPLICATIONS

 ELECTRIC SEAGLIDERS PROMISE FASTER COASTAL TRAVEL BY 2028 (8 MINUTE
READ) [6]

 Regent's Viceroy Seaglider, the largest electric
wing-in-ground-effect craft to fly with people aboard, made its maiden
flight on September 2, covering 596 meters over 30 seconds at about 10
meters above the water. The 16.8-meter craft carries 12 passengers at
up to 290 kph with a 290-kilometer range, and is classified as a
maritime vessel under US law, sidestepping the FAA certification limbo
facing eVTOLs. Regent has raised more than $340 million, claims a $10
billion order backlog, and plans commercial production at the end of
2027.

 NEW SUPERCONDUCTING CHIP AIMS TO HELP TAME FUSION PLASMA (5 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 Brookhaven National Laboratory debuted a superconducting chip built
with other US Department of Energy facilities and AI hardware startup
Great Sky to monitor fusion plasma in real time. The chip runs a
neuromorphic neural network meant to spot signs that superhot plasma
is about to become unstable. Its backers hope the hardware is fast
enough to help control plasma before disruptions form, a prerequisite
for practical fusion power.

RESEARCH AND PROTOTYPES

 INTERACTIVE WORLD SIMULATION WITH COSMOS FOR PHYSICAL AI (3 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 NVIDIA's Cosmos 3 enables real-time, interactive world simulations
through models like Cosmos3-Nano-Sim-Bimanual, -Transfer, and -Auto.
These models support action-conditioned dynamics, spatial control for
high-quality visual outputs, and synchronized multi-view video for
tasks like autonomous driving. Leveraging advanced spatial and
language controls, the models surpass performance benchmarks in depth,
edge, and video quality metrics.

 COMMERCE PLANS $389M FOR POWER SEMICONDUCTORS, WITH THE GOVERNMENT
TAKING EQUITY STAKES (2 MINUTE READ) [9]

 The Commerce Department plans four awards: up to $175 million for
Helion's high-voltage power semiconductors and modules for fusion, $85
million for Commonwealth Fusion Systems' plasma-heating and
radiation-hardened electronics, and $73 million for Vertical
Semiconductor's vertical gallium nitride devices for AI racks. Sibelco
gets $56 million for ultra-high-purity quartz purification.

MISCELLANEOUS

 SPACEX BOOKS A $561M UNREALIZED GAIN ON ITS BORING CO. STAKE (2
MINUTE READ) [10]

 SpaceX recorded a $561 million non-cash gain in Q3 from its minority
stake in The Boring Co., more than half of its "other income," lifting
net income to just over $4 billion. The mark-up follows Boring Co.'s
$3 billion raise last month at a $23 billion valuation, backed by UAE
investors. Boring Co. started inside SpaceX in 2017 and was spun out a
year later.

 APPLE'S $1,999 IPHONE DUO FOLDABLE REACHES ITS FIRST CUSTOMERS WITH
STOCK ALREADY TIGHT (2 MINUTE READ) [11]

 The $1,999 iPhone Duo has reached its first customers in Australia
and New Zealand, with rollout moving through Asia, the Middle East,
Europe, then North America. Early owners praise the matte 7.6-inch
inner display and a nearly invisible crease, and note the under-screen
camera is occasionally visible, with Touch ID in place of Face ID.
Apple expects stock to sell out on launch day, and online shipping
estimates have slipped to January 25.

 KAIST researchers developed a method to predict blue OLED lifetimes

 OURA SUES ULTRAHUMAN AGAIN OVER THE REDESIGNED RING PRO'S INTERNALS

 Oura sued Ultrahuman again, alleging the redesigned Ring Pro
infringes two patents on the ring's design and internals, including a
