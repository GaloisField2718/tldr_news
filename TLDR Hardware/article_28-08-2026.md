HEADLINES AND LAUNCHES

 ANTHROPIC PREVIEWS A UNIVERSAL DRIVER LETTING CLAUDE CONTROL ANY LAB
OR FACTORY HARDWARE (5 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Anthropic opened a research preview of the Model Hardware Standard
(MHS), a shared driver spec that lets AI agents discover and operate
lab and manufacturing instruments, microscopes, liquid handlers,
robotic arms, and laser systems through a single interface instead of
the bespoke, vendor-specific integrations that normally take weeks or
months to build. The driver uses simple read/write primitives any
device can implement, plus natural-language tags that capture physical
characteristics (like a robot arm's weight) that used to live only in
paper manuals or an engineer's head, giving the agent everything it
needs.

 PERPLEXITY AND NVIDIA UNVEIL "PORTABLE COMPUTER" FOR OFFLINE LOCAL AI
EXECUTION (3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Perplexity has partnered with Nvidia to launch Portable Computer, an
agentic Computer platform designed to execute advanced AI agent
workflows entirely on-device without continuous cloud API dependency
or recurring token costs. Powered by Nvidia's compact edge silicon
architecture, the system runs quantized local inference models to
process multimodal search, complex reasoning, and persistent agentic
tasks directly at the edge.

ENGINEERING AND APPLICATIONS

 NVME 2.4 SPECIFICATION ADDS POST-QUANTUM CRYPTOGRAPHY AND GRANULAR
POWER MANAGEMENT (3 MINUTE READ) [10]

 The NVM Express organization has released the NVMe 2.4 specification,
integrating quantum-resistant encryption algorithms and advanced
telemetry to address future-proofed enterprise security and datacenter
energy constraints. These enhancements formalize a critical
hardware-level bridge between next-generation flash controllers and
high-efficiency datacenter storage architectures.

 ADVANCED PACKAGING AND THERMAL BOTTLENECKS COMPLICATE HIGH-LAYER HBM
SCALING (3 MINUTE READ) [11]

 As high-bandwidth memory (HBM) architectures scale to 12-high and
16-high vertical DRAM stacks, semiconductor packaging faces severe
physical limits across thermal dissipation, mechanical stress, and die
warpage. Thinner DRAM silicon substrates required to maintain JEDEC
package height profiles exacerbate through-silicon via (TSV)
structural fatigue and increase susceptibility to micro-cracking
during thermal cycling. The escalating complexity and defect density
risks underscore that packaging and thermal reliability—rather than
pure lithography—now dictate the economic scaling and memory
bandwidth roadmap for frontier AI hardware accelerators.

RESEARCH AND PROTOTYPES

 ROS2SMOLVLA: DEPLOYING VLA MODELS ON AN INDUSTRIAL CO-BOT (10 MINUTE
READ) [12]

 ROS2SmolVLA is an open-source ROS 2 interface that deploys Hugging
Face's lightweight SmolVLA model directly onto an industrial Universal
Robots UR10e arm for local, vision-guided pick-and-place. If you have
been playing with an SO-101 arm and would like to graduate up to
controlling an industrial co-bots, this looks like a great starter
solution.

 NVIDIA COMPASS: CROSS-EMBODIMENT ROBOT NAVIGATION POLICY (5 MINUTE
READ) [13]

 NVIDIA just dropped a new tutorial on COMPASS, an agent-assisted
residual reinforcement learning framework designed to adapt pretrained
X-Mobility locomotion policies to different robotic platforms (such as
Boston Dynamics' Spot). It offers developers a powerful methodology to
reuse general navigation and locomotion skills across varied physical
platforms, vastly reducing the time and cost required to train new
hardware embodiments from scratch.

MISCELLANEOUS

 SIFIVE DEBUTS BIGSKY 2U SERVER PLATFORM WITH 32 P870-D RISC-V CORES
(3 MINUTE READ) [14]

 BigSky SF-2U870 is an enterprise-grade 2U rackmount development
platform designed to accelerate RISC-V datacenter software validation
and workload porting. Built around 32 high-performance P870-D cores
clocked at 2.0 GHz, the system features full compliance with the RVA23
profile standard alongside support for 256 GB of DDR5-5600 memory, 64
PCIe Gen 5 lanes across four x16 slots, and dual U.2 NVMe storage. In
addition to running enterprise distributions like Ubuntu 26.04 LTS and
RHEL 10 out of the box, the platform can act as an accelerated host
node, running Nvidia CUDA workloads directly on RISC-V hardware ahead
of future NVLink Fusion integration.

 ROS 2 PACKAGES FOR ROBOTIQ ADAPTIVE GRIPPERS (3 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Robotiq's official, first-party ROS 2 driver release for its 2F-85
and 2F-140 adaptive grippers brings optimized C++ SDK communication,
Modbus-cycle grouping for 200 Hz control, and integrated hardware
simulation. This release is critical for commercial integrators, as
transitioning from community drivers to official manufacturer support
guarantees long-term driver stability, API safety, and reliable
end-effector control.
