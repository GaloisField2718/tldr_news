HEADLINES AND LAUNCHES

 DYNA-2.1: A ROBOT/MODEL FOR UNSUPERVISED LONG-HORIZON WORKFLOWS (5
MINUTE READ) [4]

 Dyna-2.1 introduces a semi-humanoid robot named Taku that is designed
to autonomously complete long-horizon, complex physical workflows. The
system utilizes a dual-model software architecture where a
vision-language model monitors the environment while a separate model
coordinates whole-body manipulation, allowing the robot to dynamically
switch tasks. It highlights a major shift toward utilizing integrated
foundation models and highly flexible physical platforms to automate
complex, multi-step commercial and industrial service roles.

 IKEA AND KODIAK PREPARE FOR AUTONOMOUS FREIGHT OPERATIONS (4 MINUTE
READ) [5]

 Kodiak AI is preparing to launch a driverless commercial shipping
route for IKEA Supply along a 219-mile section of Interstate 45 in
Texas. The milestone follows four years of collaborative testing
during which they successfully completed over 1,300 runs to resolve
operational complexities like scheduling, predictive maintenance, and
dock assignment. It serves as a prime case study of how custom
autonomous technology can successfully transition from controlled,
simulated environments into the real-world logistics and shipping
infrastructure.

 TRANSPORTER-18 TESTS THE BUILDING BLOCKS OF ORBITAL DATA CENTERS (5
MINUTE READ) [6]

 SpaceX's Transporter-18 launched three payloads testing technologies
for orbital data centers. Cowboy Space's Reason-1 tests beaming solar
power via laser, Google's Project Suncatcher runs AI chips on solar in
orbit to evaluate durability, and Star Catcher's Protostar aims to
validate power-beaming to satellites. These tests are crucial for
building infrastructure to support future AI computing and data
centers in space.

 FRAMEWORK IS SELLING ONE BATCH OF ITS 192GB DESKTOP, BUILT FROM
MEMORY IT ALREADY HAS (3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 The new Framework Desktop pairs AMD's Ryzen AI Max PRO 495+ with
192GB of LPDDR5X and 273GB/s of memory bandwidth, and it ships in
November as a DIY kit, a Fedora prebuilt, or a bare Mini-ITX board.
Its PCIe x4 slot is now open-ended, so full-size x8 or x16 cards fit
without a riser, letting owners add 50Gbps networking to cluster
several machines for faster inference. Framework expects memory prices
to keep rising for six months and tells buyers to pick their capacity
now.

ENGINEERING AND APPLICATIONS

 CROSSING CHIPLET BOUNDARIES WITH PCIE OVER UCIE (5 MINUTE READ) [8]

 PCIe 8.0 traffic can cross chiplet boundaries through UCIe 3.0, but
engineers must coordinate both protocols' power, reset, and recovery
states. Timing mismatches can cause completion timeouts, dropped
traffic, and deadlocks. Siemens' verification tools trace transactions
across both layers and inject faults before silicon bring-up.

 MEMORY AT THE EDGE: NON-VOLATILE MEMORY CHALLENGES AND REQUIREMENTS
FOR HUMANOID ROBOTS (8 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Humanoid robots need different memory architectures for centralized
AI compute and distributed joint control. Flash and EEPROM can delay
writes by 5-10 milliseconds, which risks lost position data during
power failures. The article maps F-RAM to continuous telemetry,
safety-capable NOR Flash to boot storage, and high-speed NOR Flash to
time-sensitive networking.

RESEARCH AND PROTOTYPES

 OPENARM 2.0: AN OPEN-SOURCE COMPLIANT ROBOTIC ARM (4 MINUTE READ)
[10]

 OpenArm 2.0 is an open-source, 7-degree-of-freedom robotic arm
engineered by Tokyo-based Enactic for contact-rich manipulation and
physical artificial intelligence research. The arm features
backdrivable, quasi-direct drive joints for highly compliant physical
interactions, a gripper with an integrated camera, and a motorless
replica arm for bilateral force-feedback teleoperation. It delivers a
highly customizable, low-cost platform that facilitates the training
of robust manipulation models using modern imitation learning and
simulation-to-reality pipelines.

 ORION: DESIGNING AND PROGRAMMING A CUSTOM QUADRUPED FROM SCRATCH (3
MINUTE READ) [11]

 Orion is a custom-built, 12-degree-of-freedom quadruped robot
designed and programmed entirely from scratch. The system combines an
NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano for advanced stereo vision depth perception
and LiDAR-based SLAM with an STM32-based microcontroller running
FreeRTOS for low-level kinematic gaits and real-time stabilization. It
provides a complete, open-source blueprint for constructing complex,
dynamically stable legged systems using advanced embedded processors
and modern spatial computing toolchains.

 BUILDING A LEGO-COMPATIBLE ROS 2 DRIVE CONTROLLER (2 MINUTE READ)
[12]

 This open-source repository provides a ROS 2 hardware interface and
differential drive controller designed specifically for the Raspberry
Pi Build HAT. The package enables direct control of Lego Spike Prime
motors and sensors, allowing developers to run modern robot
communication middleware on lightweight educational hardware. It
offers an accessible entry point for testing and prototyping mobile
bases using popular, modular consumer components.

 FEDEX IS BUYING 2,000 ELECTRIC TRUCKS FOR $300M, AND INVESTING IN THE
COMPANY BUILDING THEM (3 MINUTE READ) [13]

 FedEx ordered 2,000 Harbinger electric delivery trucks for $300
million, with delivery due before the end of 2027. FedEx also led the
startup's $160 million Series C last year. Harbinger projects $20,000
in annual savings per truck against diesel, but those figures are
estimates rather than measured fleet results.
