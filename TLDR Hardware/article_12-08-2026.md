HEADLINES AND LAUNCHES

 NVIDIA'S $500B BET TO MAKE AI COMPUTE WALL STREET'S NEXT ASSET CLASS
(5 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Nvidia announced financing platforms with Apollo, BlackRock,
Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, and KKR, aiming to raise over
$500 billion in outside capital for AI "factories," the data centers
that train and run AI models, with Jensen Huang pitching this compute
as a durable, reusable "investable asset class." The core problem is
that these are non-binding MOUs, not signed deals, and the entire
pitch hinges on residual value, what the hardware is actually worth if
the paying customer walks away, since Huang's own evidence for
durability (rising H100 rental rates) proves current earnings, not
resale value.

 CHINA AI CHIP DESIGNER MOORE THREADS PLANS HONG KONG LISTING (3
MINUTE READ) [5]

 Chinese AI accelerator firm Moore Threads, founded by former NVIDIA
executives, filed for a Hong Kong listing to raise capital for global
R&D. As Nvidia faces strict US export bans, Moore Threads is
positioning itself alongside Huawei and Cambricon to capture China's
domestic AI datacenter hardware market.

ENGINEERING AND APPLICATIONS

 AGILITY'S DIGIT V5 TARGETS UNFENCED HUMAN-ROBOT COLLABORATION AND
20-HOUR SHIFTS (5 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Agility Robotics says Digit v5 will work alongside humans without
traditional safety fencing, using upgraded perception to detect people
and cycloidal actuators to react while maintaining balance and payload
control. Better battery life and fast charging target 20-hour
operating days, while an ISO flange will support standard industrial
end-effectors and eventually autonomous tool swapping. Early customer
deployments are planned for December.

 SANDIA AND ELEMENTAL NUCLEAR TO BUILD SCO2 GENERATORS FOR ON-SITE
DATA CENTER POWER (3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Sandia National Laboratories and Elemental Nuclear Energy are
building supercritical carbon dioxide (sCO2) Brayton Cycle generators,
which use CO2 held in a state with properties of both a liquid and a
gas as the working fluid instead of steam, letting the closed-loop
system convert heat to power more compactly than a conventional steam
turbine. The first target is a 1-megawatt system running on natural
gas and waste heat aimed at small modular data centers and remote
military sites, with commercial deliveries planned for 2028.

RESEARCH AND PROTOTYPES

 DYNA-2 ESTABLISHES A MILLION-HOUR SCALING LAW FOR ROBOT LEARNING (31
MINUTE READ) [8]

 Dyna-2 is a video-diffusion world-action model pre-trained on more
than 1 million hours of egocentric human-manipulation video, with no
robot data included during pre-training. Dyna reports that prediction
performance improved monotonically across a 1,000-to-1-million-hour
training ladder, including zero-shot evaluation on 39 robot tasks from
embodiments excluded from training. After limited robot post-training,
normalized physical performance across 14 tasks rose from 20% to 53%
as human-video pre-training scaled, with the largest model leading on
nine tasks. Roughly 10 minutes of robot demonstrations were sufficient
to post-train bottle-cap opening with two five-fingered robot hands.

 HYBRID COMPUTING FRAMEWORK LOOKS BEYOND PEAK TOPS/W FOR AI EFFICIENCY
(4 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Researchers propose a hybrid computing framework that moves beyond
traditional peak TOPS/W efficiency metrics. The paper demonstrates
that combining digital orchestration with analogue, photonic, and
neuromorphic accelerators delivers a scalable path toward
energy-efficient AI systems. Rather than forcing all workloads onto
pure digital logic, the proposed system dynamically routes tasks so
physical substrates earn an expanding role only where they offer a
measurable, system-level efficiency advantage while digital software
manages noise, integration, and fallbacks.

MISCELLANEOUS

 CHINA'S TOP AI STILL TRAINED ON NVIDIA CHIPS AS SWITCH TO LOCAL TECH
LAGS (3 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Leading Chinese artificial intelligence firms continue to rely on
Nvidia GPUs for training frontier AI models, delaying a full
transition to domestic silicon alternatives. Despite Beijing's
aggressive drive for semiconductor self-reliance and heavy investments
in local accelerator developers like Huawei, technical hurdles
persist. Domestic AI chipmakers face ongoing challenges with software
ecosystem maturity, inter-chip interconnect bandwidth, and cluster
stability during massive-scale training runs.

 BATTERIES MADE FROM SALT ARE FINALLY HERE (4 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Sodium-ion batteries made from abundant salt reserves are emerging as
a viable commercial alternative to lithium-ion energy storage systems.
While sodium-ion cells possess lower energy density than lithium,
their low raw material costs and rapid charging capabilities make them
ideal for stationary grid storage and short-range urban electric
vehicles worldwide.
