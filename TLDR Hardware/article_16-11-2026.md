 SPACEX CATCHES SHIP 42 ON FLIGHT 15, BUT BOOSTER 22 ABORTS ITS CATCH
ON A LOW-FUEL-MASS ALARM DURING BOOSTBACK (4 MINUTE READ) [5]

 After 14 orbits, Ship 42 made a 10-second single-engine deorbit burn
and was caught by the tower's chopsticks 22.5 hours after launch, the
first upper-stage catch. It deployed 26 Starlink V3 satellites and had
its heat shield checked using satellite cameras. The V3 booster's
boostback ended early on a low-fuel-mass alarm, so guidance detected
it was off-position and diverted to a Gulf splashdown, with the root
cause still under investigation.

interim CEO. Nvidia fell 2% on Friday.

 RESEARCHERS MATCHED THE RANDOM STARTING WEIGHTS OF CHINESE AI MODELS
TO GPU THREAD LAYOUTS TO SHOW SIX TRAINED ON BLACKWELL (6 MINUTE READ)
[8]

 PyTorch seeds a model's weights with random numbers split across
2,048 threads per SM, so each chip lays them out differently: 132 SMs
for Hopper, 148 for B200/B300, 152 for GB200/GB300. Rows untouched by
training still hold those starting values, so the team searched seed
and layout combinations, checked hits on held-out weights, and
reproduced them on real chips. DeepSeek-V4, Kimi K3, MiniMax-M3, and
Ling-3.1-flash matched Blackwell, while earlier models matched Hopper.

 REINS HARNESS LETS FRONTIER MODELS RUN A DUAL-ARM ROBOT ZERO-SHOT (3
MINUTE READ) [10]

 Reins wraps a frontier model in a harness that controls a dual-arm
robot. The model gets 3D perception tools and atomic actions that
report contact and grip, and chains several per call, each gated on a
condition that halts the chain at the first failure. A second
“narrator” instance watches the cameras and reports what changed.
With no task-specific code, GPT-6.1 Sol succeeded in 88% of real
episodes, but without the harness it failed every shell game and took
8x longer.

 DEPLOYMENT SPEED, NOT UNIT ENERGY COST, DECIDES THE BEST WAY TO POWER
AI DATA CENTERS (3 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Researchers compared grid power, AC- and DC-coupled
solar-plus-storage, and islanded natural gas for AI data centers,
adding a speed-adjusted cost of energy that prices the compute lost to
slow construction. DC coupling cuts lifetime energy costs by 18% by
eliminating inverters. DC microgrids are rarely the cheapest per kWh,
but they build fastest, so they come out lowest-cost in 85% of
locations, or 55% if gas turbine timelines improve.
