 AST SPACEMOBILE IS BUILDING ITS OWN REUSABLE ROCKET, BLACKLIFT, TO
LAUNCH ITS BLUEBIRD SATELLITES (2 MINUTE READ) [4]

 AST is building Blacklift, a 20-ton, three-stage rocket with two
reusable lower stages, to launch up to five stacked BlueBirds per
flight from late 2028. It sized the vehicle around its own
constellation after launch delays, including a New Glenn failure,
slowed deployment, though it keeps outside launch deals for 100+
satellites. The engines come from Firefly: 27 Miranda and 9 Vira for
the first three rockets, plus a license to build them itself, in a
deal valued above $1.3 billion.

 PHYSICISTS USED ELECTRICAL NOISE TO SHOW ELECTRONS IN A
HIGH-TEMPERATURE SUPERCONDUCTOR PAIR UP EARLIER THAN EXPECTED (4
MINUTE READ) [9]

 Superconductors carry current when electrons team up in pairs, but in
cuprates (compounds containing copper in an anionic state), scientists
couldn't tell if the pairs form early or just don't exist until the
material gets cold enough. The team measured electrical noise, which
gets louder when charge moves in pairs, and found the extra noise
appearing early. That suggests the pairs are already there and are
just failing to move together, which could be what limits how warm
cuprates can superconduct.

 BOSTON DYNAMICS' STRETCH GEN 2 CUTS INTERNAL CABLING 81% AND SWAPS A
SWERVE DRIVE IN ABOUT AN HOUR (3 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Using data from 100,000+ operating hours, Boston Dynamics redesigned
its trailer-unloading robot around downtime. It cut internal cabling
81% (about half a mile of wire), switched to automotive-grade
connectors, and added continuous-rotation joints that no longer hit
hard stops. A full swerve drive swap drops from a day to about an
hour. It unloads up to 1,000 cases per hour (varies with weight and
stacking) and fast-charges to 90% in about 75 minutes.

 TSMC DENIES REPORTS THAT IT IS PLANNING 3D DRAM FOR NVIDIA (2 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 The Information reported that TSMC began planning 3D DRAM production,
possibly by converting an existing fab, to give Nvidia a fourth memory
source, but TSMC says it stays a pure-play logic foundry with no plans
to make commodity memory. The engineering case against it is that 3D
DRAM, which stacks memory cells vertically, is years out even for
Micron, whose roadmap has at least four more planar nodes first, and
TSMC has no competitive planar DRAM process to build on.

 GROQ SEEKS A SERIES B AT AN $8B VALUATION, MORE THAN DOUBLE AUGUST'S,
AS IT SHIFTS FROM CHIPS TO INFERENCE DATA CENTERS (2 MINUTE READ) [12]


 Groq, which used to build inference chips and now operates data
centers selling inference capacity, is pitching investors a Series B
at $8 billion before new money, versus $3.15 billion in August.
Executive chairman Alex Davis criticizes rivals' reliance on debt and
projects revenue up eightfold next year and $2 billion in EBITDA by
2028. The pitch comes as listed neoclouds like CoreWeave and Nebius
sell off on debt concerns.
