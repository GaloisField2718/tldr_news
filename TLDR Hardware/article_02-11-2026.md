HEADLINES AND LAUNCHES

 SAMSUNG FOUNDRY CONFIRMS HYPERSCALER 2NM ORDERS AS FIRST GAA CHIPS
ENTER PRODUCTION (16 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Samsung confirmed new 2nm GAA chip production contracts with major
cloud hyperscalers and AI companies during its Q3 2026 earnings call,
marking a significant step in its foundry recovery. This move comes
amid competition from TSMC, with Samsung emphasizing its
first-to-market GAA transistor technology and improved yield rates.
Samsung's foundry division remains unprofitable but expects future
gains as it ramps up production and opens its US fab, aiming to
capture market demand amidst supply constraints.

 BOOM OPENS A $150M COLORADO FACTORY TO BUILD 42 MW GAS TURBINES FOR
AI DATA CENTERS (3 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Boom opened a roughly 200,000-square-foot factory in Centennial,
Colorado, to build Superpower, a 42 MW natural gas turbine for AI data
centers, with deliveries targeted for mid-2027 and output rising to 25
turbines a year in 2028 and 50 in 2029. Superpower and the Overture
airliner's Symphony engine are meant to share a core, and a prototype
called Sprint Core is due for first firing before Thanksgiving. Launch
customer Crusoe has since backed out of its 29-turbine order.

ENGINEERING AND APPLICATIONS

 GOOGLE'S PROJECT SUNCATCHER GENERATES FIRST AI RESPONSE IN ORBIT (1
MINUTE READ) [6]

 Project Suncatcher is a research effort by Google that aims to power
AI compute in space. Its prototype satellite, launched on October 1,
has now returned the first AI output from a data center-class TPU in
orbit. A ground station uplinked a prompt to the onboard Gemma 4
model, and a haiku came back during a pass over Svalbard, Norway.
Thermal stress and radiation tests come next, with two interconnected
satellites planned for 2027.

 META REPORTEDLY BUILT A 106 LB HOUSEHOLD HUMANOID PROTOTYPE WITH
FOURIER, RUNNING ON NVIDIA JETSON THOR (2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Meta built a household humanoid prototype, codenamed Max, in about
three months with Chinese robotics firm Fourier. It's about 1.5 m
tall, runs on Nvidia Jetson Thor, and has a face display, speaker, and
hands for small objects. Max 2, in work since September, redesigns the
torso and hands and adds a projector and extra sensors for stairs. No
launch date has been set.

 SATLYT TAKES ANOTHER STEP TOWARD TURNING SATELLITES INTO SPACE DATA
CENTERS (4 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Satlyt's software successfully tested to distribute computing tasks
among orbiting satellites on a NASA-funded project with the University
of Houston. The software, already on three satellites, uses available
compute resources and communication capacity to manage tasks, aiming
to enhance data processing efficiency in space. Future plans include
broader validation and integration, ultimately enabling developers to
create space applications without rebuilding infrastructure for every
mission.

 SLAC CONFIRMS ALTERMAGNETISM IN MANGANESE TELLURIDE WITH POLARIZED
X-RAYS, A MAGNET TYPE AIMED AT SPINTRONIC MEMORY (4 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Altermagnets are a third kind of magnet alongside ferromagnets and
antiferromagnets. Like antiferromagnets, they have no net
magnetization, so stray fields can't flip them, but they can still
carry spin-polarized currents, so their data can be read out. A
SLAC-lead team shot polarized X-rays at maganese telluride and saw a
directional signal that a conventional antiferromagnet can't produce.
This work, published in Science, targets a usecase within spintronic
memory.

MISCELLANEOUS

 INTEL'S FAB 28 EXPLOSION COSTS ABOUT $600M IN REVENUE AND SQUEEZES
10NM XEON SUPPLY INTO 2027 (4 MINUTE READ) [11]

 On October 14, an explosion at Intel's Fab 28 in Kiryat Gat, Israel,
killed two workers and halted the plant for about two weeks. The fab
makes 10nm-class server chips such as Xeon 6 I/O dies, and Intel
expects about $600 million in lost revenue across Q4 and Q1. Wafers
take roughly six months to finish, so scrapped work delays supply into
2027 on top of an already tight server market.

 CHINA'S EMBODIED AI STARTUPS RAISED $13B IN H1 2026, FIVE TIMES LAST
YEAR, AS ROBOT MAKERS SHIFT FROM MOVES TO BRAINS (3 MINUTE READ) [12]

 After years of building humanoids that run, dance, and backflip,
Chinese companies are pouring money into the AI that would make them
useful across more jobs, meaning software that lets robots understand
their surroundings and learn new tasks without per-job programming.
Chinese embodied AI companies raised 93.5 billion yuan across 322
deals in the first half of 2026, five times a year earlier, with
funding increasingly going to specialist robot-intelligence developers
as well as manufacturers.

BETTER USE OF IT (3 MINUTE READ) [13]
