HEADLINES AND LAUNCHES

 MUSK SAYS SPACEX CLOSED $40B OF GPU DEBT FOR CHIPS ARRIVING IN Q1 AND
Q2 (2 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Elon Musk says SpaceX closed its $40 billion of callable debt for
GPUs, with the chips arriving in Q1 and Q2 and "not a major factor
this quarter." He credits investment-grade status and Starlink and
launch revenue for keeping credit open. SpaceX guided Q4 capex to $35
to $40 billion. It is bringing roughly 200,000 GB300s online monthly
through year-end before shifting to Vera Rubin. The company reported
1.7 GW of installed GPU nameplate capacity at September 30.

 GTC BERLIN: VERA RUBIN IN A SHIPPING CONTAINER, WHILE NEOCLOUD
FINANCING WORRIES DOMINATE THE FLOOR (6 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Nvidia's GTC Berlin centered on the DSX POD, a factory-built Vera
Rubin NVL72 system in a shipping container that cuts delivery to
deployment from months to days, plus OpenShell agent sandboxing and
the Agile ONE SN humanoid on Jetson Thor. Nvidia said it has invested
nearly $10 billion in Europe.

ENGINEERING AND APPLICATIONS

 NASA REWORKS ARTEMIS AROUND ORION'S LIMITED PROPULSION, WITH STARSHIP
PUSHING IT TOWARD THE MOON (4 MINUTE READ) [6]

 NASA turned Artemis III into a roughly two-week Earth-orbit test in
summer 2027, where Orion docks with test versions of Blue Moon and
Starship. For the 2028 landing on Artemis IV, Starship would dock with
Orion in high Earth orbit and perform the trans-lunar injection burn,
while Blue Moon meets Orion in low lunar orbit, which cuts its
propellant needs. NASA will pick the Artemis IV lander based on
Artemis III results.

 WARHOUND RAISES $200M AT A $1.2B VALUATION TO ENGINEER MILITARY DOGS
AND OUTFIT THEM WITH ANDURIL SENSOR GEAR (4 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Warhound is a startup founded by Colossal's Ben Lamm and Anduril's
Palmer Luckey that plans to gene-edit Belgian Malinois and German
Shepherd cell lines into working dogs with better health, speed, and
smell. Anduril will supply the hardware: protective vests with data
sensors that feed handlers, plus goggles. Viagen will clone the first
batch, with births expected early next year and service within two
years. The dogs don't exist yet, and sale prices are undecided.

 MIT'S LIGHT-STEERED SWIMMING ROBOT RUNS ON A HAIR-THIN LAYER OF
LIVING MUSCLE (3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 MIT built a swimming robot from a gum-stick-sized gel frame with two
fins, each coated in a muscle layer thinner than a hair and engineered
to contract under light. Lighting one fin or both steers it through a
maze at about four body lengths per minute. The key fixes were stiff
GelMA gel about half a millimeter thick with square-bottomed grooves
that align the cells, and light-pulse training to strengthen the
muscle. It's the first thin 2D biohybrid swimmer.

 PT-W ALLOY TESTED AS A THINNER LOW-N ABSORBER FOR HIGH-NA EUV
PHOTOMASKS (1 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Researchers at the Chinese Academy of Sciences, Jilin University, and
partner labs evaluated a platinum-tungsten alloy as a low-n absorber
for EUV photomasks, studying its film properties and imaging
performance. Thinner absorbers matter for High-NA EUV because mask 3D
effects degrade imaging as features shrink. Thickness and contrast
figures are in the iScience paper published in August.

MISCELLANEOUS

 IFIXIT'S IPHONE DUO TEARDOWN GIVES THE BEST FOLDABLE REPAIR SCORE SO
FAR, BUT THE INNER SCREEN IS THE CATCH (8 MINUTE READ) [11]

 iFixit gives the iPhone Duo a provisional 5/10, the best score for a
foldable. Both batteries (19.1 Wh total) release with electrically
debonded adhesive inside metal shells, the outer display comes off
intact, and the logic board is a single layer. The weak spot is the
inner screen: a $999 out-of-warranty replacement and a hinge that
still crunches with dust.

 TESLA'S $10B TEXAS SOLAR FACTORY COULD GET 35 YEARS OF TAX BREAKS (7
MINUTE READ) [12]

 Fort Bend County approved four deals that could give Tesla's proposed
$10 billion "Project Crystal Sun" solar plant up to 35 years of tax
breaks, though Tesla hasn't committed to building in Texas. The plant
would be vertically integrated, making silicon ingots through finished
modules near Richmond, with $8.6 billion of the spend in equipment,
which gets 10 years of full abatement and 20 years of 90% rebates.
Commercial cell production is due by the end of 2030.

 NVIDIA FLOATS A $2,000-PER-GPU ANNUAL SOFTWARE FEE FOR RUNNING AGENTS
(3 MINUTE READ) [13]

 At GTC Berlin, Jensen Huang said Nvidia's software for running agents
on AI factories would cost about $2,000 per GPU per year, less than
half the $4,500 per GPU list price of AI Enterprise.

 NVIDIA SAYS KAZAKHSTAN GETS 45,000 GRACE BLACKWELL GPUS, WITH VERA

 Firebird secured a US export license for 45,000 Grace Blackwell GPUs
for Kazakhstan.

 AMAZON'S TORCHNEURON RUNS PYTORCH ON TRAINIUM CHIPS WITH A SINGLE
CONFIG CHANGE (2 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Amazon's TorchNeuron runs unmodified PyTorch code on its Trainium
chips with a single configuration change, aiming at the CUDA lock-in
Nvidia built over more than a decade.

 DARPA'S RACER-DRIVEN ROBOT VEHICLES FIND A TARGET AT FORT KNOX WITH A
HUMAN APPROVING THE STRIKE (2 MINUTE READ) [16]

 DARPA ran four autonomous ground vehicles at Fort Knox on October 15,
using its RACER off-road autonomy software on sensor-equipped,
drive-by-wire platforms.
