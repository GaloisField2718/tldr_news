HEADLINES AND LAUNCHES

 FCC AUTHORIZES SPACEX TO DEPLOY UP TO 1 MILLION SOLAR-POWERED ORBITAL
DATA CENTER SATELLITES, WITH 100,000 DUE BY NOVEMBER 2035 (3 MINUTE
READ) [4]

 SpaceX has been authorized to launch a constellation of up to 1
million AI satellites that would link to each other optically and
reach Earth through Starlink, with the 1 million count acting as a
regulatory ceiling rather than a promised fleet. SpaceX plans to equip
them with Nvidia Rubin GPUs and Vera CPUs, relying on Starship's
falling launch costs to compete with terrestrial datacenter prices

 TSMC'S A14 IS READY FOR DESIGN-IN WITH PDK 0.9 (3 MINUTE READ) [5]

 A14 uses second-generation nanosheet transistors with flexible cell
types, so designers put fast Boost Cells on critical paths and
power-saving Low-Power Cells everywhere else. TSMC claims 50% better
energy efficiency and 23% higher logic density than N2P, and a later
move to A13 shrinks die area about 6% without changing the design
flow.

ENGINEERING AND APPLICATIONS

 QUICK TAKE: AMD'S HELIOS AS A PLUG-AND-PLAY AI APPLIANCE STANDARD (4
MINUTE READ) [6]

 AMD's Helios combines AMD CPU, GPU, and Ethernet with the ROCm
framework in a plug-and-play AI solution using OCP's ORW form factor.
Positioned as a competitive alternative to NVIDIA's offerings, AMD
focuses on integration and easier software porting from CUDA to ROCm.
Though larger cloud providers depend on NVIDIA or custom AI ASICs,
smaller providers may leverage Helios's integration for
straightforward deployment and operation.

 NERC'S FIRST STANDARDS REQUIRE DATA CENTERS OF 50 MW OR MORE TO SHARE
POWER MODELS AND TRIP SETTINGS, AFTER 3.8 GW OF VIRGINIA LOAD DROPPED
OFF THE GRID AT ONCE (4 MINUTE READ) [7]

 On July 22, Northern Virginia facilities drawing 3,800 MW switched to
backup after a routine line fault, spiking frequency and voltage, and
at least eight such events have shed 200 to 1,800 MW since 2022. AI
training makes it worse, since synchronized GPUs pulse between full
power and near idle, and a 100 MW facility swung 10 to 15 MW every 2.5
seconds. Simulations show those pulses can resonate with grid natural
frequencies, so NERC also plans ride-through and oscillation criteria
next year.

RESEARCH AND PROTOTYPES

 SK HYNIX TO START 3D DRAM PRODUCTION IN 2031 (2 MINUTE READ) [8]

 SK hynix plans to start mass production of 3D DRAM by 2031,
potentially utilizing 100-layer designs based on its current roadmap.
The production requires advanced techniques like multi-deck stacking
and hybrid bonding, drawing from technology licensed from YMTC. This
shift involves completely overhauling SK hynix's DRAM architecture and
manufacturing process.

 XPENG SAYS ITS IRON HUMANOID WILL SELL AT ROUGHLY THE MEDIAN PRICE OF
ITS EVS (3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Xpeng's EVs run from 119,800 to 309,800 yuan (about $18,000 to
$44,000), and an executive gave that as the benchmark for the future
cost of their humanoid, IRON. IRON carries over 1,500 tactile sensing
points, with 76 degrees of freedom and three in-house Turing AI chips.
Mass production starts in January. Store trials follow in Q1 2027, and
public sales are targeted for Q2. Xpeng wants to sell tens of
thousands next year, funded by a $900 million robotics round at a $6.3
billion valuation, while its stock is down 55% this year.

MISCELLANEOUS

 2 CALIFORNIA COMPANIES ARE BUYING NVIDIA'S MOST ADVANCED CHIPS. THOSE
COMPANIES ARE ACTUALLY CHINESE-OWNED, A CBS NEWS INVESTIGATION REVEALS
(9 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Nvidia's advanced chips are being sold to US-based companies that are
actually Chinese-owned, raising concerns about evading export
controls. California companies RiCloud AI and Aivres, linked to
Chinese firms, have been involved in questionable dealings, including
sales to Southeast Asian firms tied to Chinese tech companies.
Nvidia's accounting changes may obscure customer locations, prompting
potential Senate investigations into export violations and espionage.

 EU DELAYS ITS VOTE ON BLOC-WIDE APPROVAL OF TESLA'S "HANDS-OFF,
EYES-ON" DRIVER ASSISTANCE (2 MINUTE READ) [13]

 The Technical Committee for Motor Vehicles had been expected to vote
after skipping two earlier meetings, following the Netherlands'
approval in April. Tesla Assisted Driving, renamed from FSD after
regulator concerns, can steer, accelerate, and brake, but needs
constant driver supervision. About 12 countries have approved it, with
Italy the largest, but EU-wide approval takes 15 of 27 states
representing 65% of the population, and a rejection would invalidate
the national approvals already granted.
