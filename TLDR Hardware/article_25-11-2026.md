 NVIDIA'S BOARD UNANIMOUSLY REINSTATES JENSEN HUANG AS CEO (2 MINUTE
READ) [4]

 Nvidia's board says Jensen Huang's candor and the input of US
officials left them confident his return poses no national security
risk, despite the pending independent director investigation into the
foreign intelligence effort targeting him. The company is searching
for a new Chief Compliance Officer, who will report to the Nominating
and Corporate Governance Committee, alongside the independent National
Security and Export Control Committee formed last week.

 TESLA AND SPACEX REPORTEDLY AIM TO SIGN AN ALL-STOCK MERGER BY
YEAR-END, CREATING A ROUGHLY $3.5 TRILLION COMPANY (3 MINUTE READ) [5]


 The FT says talks are moving toward a December 31 target, just before
a new Congress where Democrats have vowed to probe Musk's companies,
even though people familiar say an agreement isn't close. It would
need more than 50% of all outstanding Tesla votes, where Musk holds
about 20% of voting power, while he controls about 84% of SpaceX.
Regulators in the US, China, Europe, and the UK could scrutinize it.

 ARIANE 64 LAUNCHES 36 AMAZON LEO SATELLITES TO ABOUT 460 KM ON ITS
SECOND FLIGHT WITH LONGER P160C BOOSTERS, ARIANE 6'S 10TH STRAIGHT
SUCCESS (2 MINUTE READ) [6]

 The mission ran 2 hours and 4 minutes from liftoff to final satellite
separation, bringing Arianespace's total to 136 Amazon Leo satellites
this year. Amazon has ordered six more Ariane 64 launches, for 24
contracted overall.

 UBER AND NURO BEGIN DRIVERLESS TESTING OF LUCID GRAVITY ROBOTAXIS IN
THE BAY AREA AHEAD OF A Q1 2027 COMMERCIAL LAUNCH (2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 The Gravity robotaxi uses high-resolution cameras, solid-state lidar,
and radar running Nuro's driver, and testing has moved to no human at
the wheel more than six months after California issued the driverless
permit. Lucid is starting to build the first production versions at
its Arizona factory, expanding the combined test fleet that stood at
100 vehicles earlier this year. Uber will own and operate the fleet
and has committed to at least 20,000 Gravity SUVs over six years.

 PSIQUANTUM CEO VICTOR PENG SAYS QUANTUM IS A NEW FORM OF COMPUTING
THAT WILL CREATE WHOLE NEW INDUSTRIES, WHILE GOVERNMENTS ARE STILL
CATCHING UP (3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Peng became CEO in February, more than a year after the Australian
federal and Queensland governments signed an AU$900 million deal with
PsiQuantum to build a first-of-its-kind quantum computer near
Brisbane. He echoes co-founder Mark Thompson's claim that quantum will
dwarf AI in impact, and says many in government find that hard to
grasp, though some parts have physicists and deeply technical staff.

 INSECT-SIZE FLYING ROBOTS COULD LEAVE THE LAB WITHIN TWO YEARS, BUT
ONBOARD POWER, SENSING, AND CONTROL ARE THE BOTTLENECK (4 MINUTE READ)
[10]

 University of Washington's Sawyer Fuller predicts a fully autonomous
insect-size flyer could fly free soon, though techniques from larger
drones break down at this scale and onboard controllers and sensors
add weight. MIT's wing-halved design hovered for about 1,000 seconds,
100 times longer than prior bots, and leaves room for batteries and
sensors. Other designs, like FVW-Fly, control altitude but still lack
directional control.

 LLMS HANDLE VERILOG BUT NOT LAYOUTS OR WAVEFORMS, AND AGENTS MULTIPLY
LOAD ON DESIGN DATA SYSTEMS BY 10 TO 20X (14 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Public RTL is plentiful, but models have never seen layouts, and a
terabyte waveform database must be converted to text, burning tokens,
so panelists want queryable abstractions. Each engineer directing 10
to 20 agents scales version control, NFS, and license demand the same
way, and IP governance is a worry. Customers are drifting back from
air-gapped open models toward frontier ones, while hybrid specialized
models may handle things like temporal assertions.

 BAMBU LAB'S P3C BRINGS A SEVEN-TOOL CHANGER TO A $1,149 PRINTER,
ABOUT HALF THE PRICE OF THE H3C (2 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Instead of purging one nozzle on every color change, the Swaptek
system swaps between separate nozzles in about six seconds, with five
0.4 mm tools included and two open slots that cost $99 each to fill.
The build volume is 256 x 256 x 260 mm versus 330 x 320 x 325 mm on
the H3C, and the bundled AMS 3P loads filament manually, which is the
main compromise. It went on sale on November 24, with the seven-tool
kit at $1,299.
