HEADLINES AND LAUNCHES

 JENSEN HUANG SAYS HE WOULD BE “HONORED” TO RETURN AS NVIDIA CEO
IF THE BOARD DECIDES IT IS IN THE COMPANY'S BEST INTEREST (2 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 In his first statement since resigning, Jensen Huang said he made
serious mistakes, will cooperate with the board's independent
investigation, and would return if the board decides it serves
shareholders and the company. Nvidia has formed an executive committee
to oversee export control compliance, and Huang will testify before
Congress on December 1. A source on Nvidia's board told the Washington
Post that there are no plans to reinstate him, and one senator called
the idea among the worst he'd heard.

 NVIDIA GUIDES Q4 TO $130B WITH NO CHINA DATA CENTER REVENUE AS VERA
RUBIN RAMPS, AND THE BOARD RULES OUT HUANG'S RETURN (3 MINUTE READ)
[9]

 Nvidia's Q3 revenue hit $115.8 billion, with data center up 110% to
$107.6 billion and Vera Rubin NVL72 racks shipping to CoreWeave,
Google Cloud, Azure, Oracle, and Nebius. Q4 guidance of $130 billion,
plus or minus 3%, assumes no China data center compute revenue, and
gross margin dips to 71% to 72%. On the call, board chair Harvey Jones
said the board does not intend to reinstate Jensen Huang, and a search
firm is leading the CEO hunt.

ENGINEERING AND APPLICATIONS

 TESLA'S POWERCORE PACKS TRANSFORMER, INVERTER, AND BREAKER INTO ONE
7.4 MW SOLID-STATE UNIT, TAKING ON HERON LINK (3 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Powercore uses silicon carbide power electronics to replace the
low-voltage inverters and oil-filled iron-core transformer in a solar
or battery site. It's rated 7.4 MW (9 MVA) continuous at 34.5 kV with
grid-forming support up to 10 MVA peak, made in the US, operating in
2027. That's about 50% more than the 5 MW Heron Link from ex-Tesla
exec Drew Baglino's startup, which claims 98.6% efficiency, a Morgan
Hill factory, and customers like Vantage and Intersect. Tesla hasn't
shared efficiency or pricing.

 BACKPLANE IS AN ALPHA, OPEN-SOURCE KICAD FORK WHERE AI AGENTS DESIGN
BOARDS THROUGH KICAD'S IPC AND CLI (1 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Agents drive KiCad directly through kicad_ipc and kicad-cli instead
of rewriting files, so every board stays human-editable. Persistent
bots run in threads with memory, routines, and webhooks. The built-in
KiStack adds 11 auto-updating skills for schematics, placement,
layout, panelization, BOMs, and fab outputs, with the export skill
running ERC and DRC and generating gerbers, drill, pick-and-place,
STEP, and renders. The demo is an STM32N657 SoM, and it ships with a
199-footprint library.

 A 125-QUBIT CHIP TELEPORTS AN ERROR-CORRECTED QUBIT USING WEAKER
ENTANGLEMENT THAN EXPECTED (3 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Teleporting a protected qubit between quantum processors needs a
good-enough entanglement link, and below a certain quality it fails.
By balancing the two kinds of errors in the code, researchers made
their 125-qubit chip tolerate a much weaker link, raising the
threshold from 0.122π to 0.175π. Theory suggests even very weak
entanglement could eventually work, which would make linking
error-corrected quantum computers easier.

 ISOMORPHIC LABS RAISES $5B TO SCALE ITS ISODDE DRUG-DESIGN ENGINE AND
FOUNDATIONAL BIOLOGY MODELS TOWARD CLINICAL TRIALS (2 MINUTE READ)
[14]

 The Alphabet-backed company raised a Series C co-led by Greenoaks and
Sequoia, six months after a $2.1 billion Series B, and Bloomberg
earlier reported a roughly $50 billion valuation. The money funds
IsoDDE, its proprietary design engine, plus new foundational biology
models and its first therapeutic programs moving toward clinical
trials. Isomorphic reports positive preclinical results in oncology
and immunology, with partners including Eli Lilly, Novartis, and
Johnson & Johnson.

MISCELLANEOUS

 WAYMO HALTS FREEWAY RIDES IN ALL 15 CITIES AFTER CALIFORNIA DMV CITES
RECURRING CONSTRUCTION-ZONE INCURSIONS (3 MINUTE READ) [15]

 California's DMV restricted Waymo's freeway operations on November 11
after three robotaxis entered an active construction closure on US-101
near Burlingame and a separate incident involved CHP vehicles on
Highway 280. The DMV calls it an imminent hazard, since similar
incidents on May 18 led to a prior restriction and fixes. Waymo has
halted freeway service in every market while it evaluates performance,
and surface-street rides, over half a million trips a week, continue.
The restriction will stay until the DMV sees effective corrective
action.

 MARVELL AND PENGUIN'S PHOTONIC MEMORY APPLIANCE POOLS 16 TB OF DDR5
AND 1 TB OF HBM ACROSS 16 SERVERS OVER OPTICAL LINKS (3 MINUTE READ)
[16]

 Marvell's module, from its $3.5B Celestial AI acquisition, stacks 72
GB of HBM3e and eight DDR5 channels on a silicon photonic interposer
with 900 GB/s bidirectional bandwidth. Penguin's box holds 16 modules
behind a 57.6 Tbps optical shuffle, serving up to 16 servers over NICs
or CPO and NPO optics, bypassing CXL for the optical modes. An HBM
cache-through mode gives HBM speed with DDR5 capacity, aimed at KV
cache offload, with systems due in the second half of next year.
