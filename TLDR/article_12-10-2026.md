BIG TECH & STARTUPS

 INSIDE MARK ZUCKERBERG'S DECISION TO PULL THE TRIGGER ON META'S AI
AGENT (8 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Meta had delayed the release of Muse for months due to safety
concerns. However, the success of an AI agent created by a startup
called Instinct led Mark Zuckerberg to push for a launch due to fear
of losing a competitive edge. The launch was successful and has given
Meta an early lead with agents. The agent has only had a few safety
hiccups, like when it gave out a user's address to a seller on
Facebook Marketplace without permission.

 APPLE IS ABOUT TO CLOSE THE BIGGEST HOLE IN ITS ECOSYSTEM WITH
HOMEVIEW (17 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Apple is set to unveil new smart home products tomorrow. The
centerpiece will be a smart home hub called the HomeView that
essentially serves as the Siri command center for an Apple-powered
household. There will be a countertop model with a metal base and a
wall-mounted version with a flat, square base that sits against the
wall beneath the display. The HomeView will only support a limited
selection of Apple applications, and there will be no App Store for
downloading additional software.

SCIENCE & FUTURISTIC TECHNOLOGY

 PROBLEMS IN 22 SCIENTIFIC FIELDS HAD SOLUTIONS HIDING IN PLAIN SIGHT.
AN AI HAS FOUND THEM (7 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Borrowing an idea from one research discipline to solve a problem in
another is an age-old scientific trick. However, such clever leaps are
rare because researchers don't tend to spend their time researching
fields outside of their expertise. AI can easily hop between fields.
BootLoops is a harness that can spot and solve problems in seemingly
unrelated fields that share the same mathematical structures at
massive scale. Researchers recently released 36 preprints produced
with BootLoops covering a wide range of topics, including figuring out
the age of sunspots, analyzing government health policies, and
attempting to decipher a mysterious medieval manuscript.

 CHINA'S DELIVERY ROBOTS ARE LEARNING TO TACKLE THE NIGHT SHIFT (11
MINUTE READ) [9]

 Shenzhen opened access for nighttime deliveries by driverless
vehicles in March. Night operations make otherwise idle vehicles and
infrastructure productive. It also means quieter roads, reduced
pedestrian traffic, and lower congestion. Driverless vehicles
eliminate the cost of a human: they don't need night shift wages, they
don't get tired, and they don't need to be paid extra for driving in
the rain.

 SO LONG, SPOKES: GITHUB REWRITES STORAGE TO RESTORE RELIABILITY, JUST
IN TIME FOR AGENTIC HORDES (5 MINUTE READ) [11]

 GitHub is rebuilding its Git storage architecture from scratch. Early
internal testing shows a promising 35x write improvement. The plan
involves decoupling writes from reads, offloading maintenance, and
letting object storage manage redundancy. The change won't alter
developer workflows, review processes, or security controls.

 THE AI COMPUTING CRUNCH IS MAKING RIVALS INTO PARTNERS (8 MINUTE
READ) [13]

 Anthropic entered into a deal worth up to $45 billion earlier this
year to lease computing power from SpaceX. The unlikely alliance
illustrates the industry's increasingly desperate scramble for
compute. Demand is outstripping supply, and prices are rising, causing
tech companies to strike deals with rivals. Venture-capital firms are
helping startups obtain computing capacity.
