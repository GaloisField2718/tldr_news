BIG TECH & STARTUPS

 SPACEXAI DEBUTS GROK 4.6, OVERTAKING KIMI K3'S PERFORMANCE AND
MATCHING GPT-5.6 SOL FOR WORLD'S THIRD BEST ON ARTIFICIAL ANALYSIS (15
MINUTE READ) [8]

 SpaceXAI has released Grok 4.6, a model focused on long-running
agents, coding, and knowledge work. The model surpasses Kimi K3 and
ties with GPT-5.6 Sol Max on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence
Index. It posts sizable gains over its predecessor across coding,
terminal, knowledge-work, and agent benchmarks. The API starts at $2
per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens, less than
half of what GPT-5.6 Sol costs in standard mode.

 GOOGLE REVEALS 2026 HARDWARE LINEUP: PIXEL 11, PIXEL WATCH 5, AND
PIXEL TAG (15 MINUTE READ) [9]

 The Pixel 11 series looks a lot like last year's lineup, but with
some minor tweaks, a few new hardware extras, and even more
Gemini-powered AI. The phones are joined by an updated Pixel Watch 5
and the Pixel Tag, Google's first in-house tracker device. The most
notable hardware innovation for the new Pixel lineup is the return of
a notification LED for the Pro phones. The ring of RGB LEDs is housed
inside the flash assembly on the back of the phone and lights up only
when the user is using Gemini or when there is an incoming call from
specific contacts for both standard phone calls and voice calls in
WhatsApp.

 WORKERS ARE TEACHING AI-POWERED ROBOTS TO TAKE OVER THEIR JOBS (15
MINUTE READ) [10]

 Tens of thousands of workers in India are being recruited to record
their work in first-person. The footage is being fed into AI systems
being built to teach robots how to do things. Robotics companies
currently lack the data needed to train machines that interact with
the physical world. Real footage of humans doing mundane tasks is in
short supply because no one cared about it, until now.

 NORTHROP'S ROBOT SPACE MECHANIC IS A NEW WAY TO KEEP SATELLITES AT
WORK LONGER (5 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Northrop Grumman launched a Mission Robotic Vehicle (MRV) and three
Mission Extension Pods (MEPs) into orbit on a SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket
in July. The MRV is a powerful satellite equipped with two advanced
robotic arms. MEPs are simpler satellites that are essentially modular
propulsion systems. In 2027, the MRV will use its robotic arms to
attach one of the MEP pods to a telecommunications satellite, which
should keep it in orbit for years to come.

 WHY TINY JPEGS LOOK DIFFERENT IN CHROME (4 MINUTE READ) [14]

 What looks like a rendering bug is actually a clever JPEG decoding
optimization in Chrome. Chrome delegates image decoding and rendering
to Skia, which does not always decompress the full image and scale it
afterward. It computes the closest fraction with a denominator of 8
and decodes the image at that scale, then scales the image further
using a more traditional downsampling algorithm until it reaches the
desired size. This is why small images can look thicker on some
machines.

 WHY YOU CAN'T COPY PALANTIR (38 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Palantir is a vertically integrated outcome-based software solution
provider. It offers tech consulting and software services with a
unique outcome-based model that lets it bypass the traditional
trade-offs of both traditional consulting and software companies. It
is vertically integrated because it has an extreme elitist attitude
towards using its own software and services, unlike the rest of the
market, which is much more polyamorous. There's little hope of any
company being able to catch up to Palantir as investors allow the
company to do R&D over an extremely long and unspecified time horizon.

Adoption of OpenAI and, to a lesser extent, Anthropic, has slowed in
recent months. Their growth has to come from existing businesses
spending on AI, and those businesses are increasingly spending on open
source.
