 APPLE ANNOUNCES OCT. 28 EVENT FOR TOUCH-SCREEN MACBOOK PRO (2 MINUTE

 Apple will be holding a product launch event on October 28 to debut
its first touch-screen MacBook. The company will also introduce a new
low-end MacBook Pro with an M6 chip, a refreshed iMac, and a revamped

begin selling new products on November 6. The new touch MacBooks,

cities in the US.

SCIENCE & FUTURISTIC TECHNOLOGY

 AI PROVED THE QUASI-RIEMANN HYPOTHESIS. WHAT HAPPENS NOW? (6 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 The quasi-Riemann hypothesis is a weaker version of the Riemann
hypothesis, arguably the biggest open question in math. Resolving the
Riemann hypothesis would enable mathematicians to better understand
the nature of prime numbers. OpenAI's recently released proof for the
quasi-Riemann hypothesis is the most significant AI-for-math
achievement so far, by quite a margin. The vast majority of the
consequences of resolving it already follow from solving the
quasi-Riemann hypothesis.

 STARTUP TAPS UNDERWATER VOLCANO AS ENERGY SOURCE IN “PROOF POINT”
TEST (11 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Endurance Energy, a Seattle-based startup, recently demonstrated the
technical feasibility of using an active underwater volcano to
generate electricity. The startup deployed a 3-ton prototype power
plant at the Axial Seamount in the northeast Pacific Ocean in
September and generated less than 10 seconds of net-positive
electricity. The company's next trial run, planned for the winter of
2026 to 2027, will aim to sustain higher power generation. It is
currently developing a 2-megawatt commercial generator for its first
planned commercial deployment with the Kingdom of Tonga in 2027.

 INTRODUCING CLAIRIC, A NEW SYSTEM ONE CLASS OF EXTRACTION MODELS (16
MINUTE READ) [11]

 Clairic is an extraction model. Extraction models are a new class of
System One-style models that can extract, compute, estimate, and
forecast values from unstructured inputs, all at significantly higher
accuracy, faster latency, and lower cost than traditional LLMs. It
answers with values directly extracted from the input, computed from
it, or predicted beyond it. The model can't produce a mistyped answer
or hallucinate in the same way an LLM can, but it can still make
mistakes.

 CUTTING OUR INFERENCE BILL BY 68% BY BEING OBSESSIVE ABOUT
OPTIMIZATION (15 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Cloudflare lowered its average review cost from $2.43 to $0.77. The
biggest single saving came from checking what actually reached the
provider on each session's first request, and finding a way to order
those messages in a way that was as consistent as possible across
commits, merge requests, and repositories. Check whether each
session's first request actually reads from the cache and whether
reviewers still find real problems at the effort level being paid for.

 AI WILL NOT AUTOMATE RADIOLOGISTS LIKE YOU THINK (6 MINUTE READ) [13]


 Some worry about radiology being totally automated, but the more
likely outcome is a future where there is more demand for services as
predictive power increases. This is similar to how programming is
thriving despite drastic change in the field. It may be possible one
day to use radiology images to predict disease probabilities far into
the future. Any domain where demand can be elastic and quality can
scale with increased intelligence will similarly flourish with the
development of AI.

 SAUDIS AIDED FAMILY OF ENGINEER ACCUSED OF BUGGING OPENAI,
PROSECUTORS SAY (6 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Former OpenAI engineer Abdulrahman Alfozan was recently arrested and
charged after a hidden recording device in one of OpenAI's offices was
traced back to him. The engineer was in frequent contact with a member
of the Saudi royal family, who was trying to recruit him to work in
Saudi Arabia, but Alfozan had refused, saying he wanted to collect the
company stock he had been promised. The Saudi government has offered
to pay his bail, and a member of the Saudi military paid for his wife
and infant son to fly out of the US the day after his arrest.
