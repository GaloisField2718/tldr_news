 HOW TO FORMALIZE ALL OF MATHEMATICS (16 MINUTE READ) [8]

 It would be a terrible waste of resources to spend millions of
dollars to formalize mathematics results in libraries nobody uses.

AI, disrupt the social norms and practices research depends on, and
set the field back decades. Several projects are now working to ensure
formal libraries fulfill the community's mathematical goals. Within
the next few years, most of mathematics will be able to be formalized
in such a way that mathematicians can explore, build on, and make use
of formal libraries. This will significantly extend their ability to
develop and discover new mathematics.

 DECOHERENCE (48 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Decoherence is the accumulation of locally valid but mutually
incompatible or redundant models within a system. It causes the cost
of change to be dominated by translating among those models and
preventing unintended side-effects. Decoherence grows relative to the
rate of change of a system, and it increases faster the more
decoherent a system is, resulting in runaway exponential growth. It
rules out the specter of runaway recursive self-improvement, as
decoherence both prevents AI from improving in a bubble and limits the
rate at which it can improve outside of it. However, it also
introduces a new kind of disaster called Decoherence Collapse.

 Good products carry something of the people who make them. The

same technology and still make very different things. The difference
is how each team understands what they see and the style they go about
building it. The founder background or company DNA shapes this a lot.
