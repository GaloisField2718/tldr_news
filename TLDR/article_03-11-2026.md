 SPACEX IS REPORTEDLY LOOKING AT BUYING BOOST MOBILE OR USING ITS CELL
NETWORK (2 MINUTE READ) [5]

 SpaceX has held talks with EchoStar to potentially acquire Boost
Mobile outright or gain access to its cell network, which is leased
from AT&T and T-Mobile. Starlink recently confirmed plans to become a
major mobile carrier in the US. The deal will need approval from AT&T
and T-Mobile, both of which have already turned down SpaceX for access
to their cell towers. SpaceX last year acquired $2.6 billion worth of
spectrum licenses from EchoStar to improve its Starlink network's 5G
connections.

 COMING IN 2027: APPLE'S $1,000 HOME ROBOT FOR FACETIME AND SWITCHING
ON LIGHTS (2 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Apple is reportedly working on an AI-driven home robot potentially
named the HomeView Ultra. It plans to launch the robotic product with
a possible price tag of $1,000 or more by 2027. The earliest
prototypes of the home robot first came out in 2018. The robot, which
will support FaceTime calls, will be able to autonomously roam around
and switch lights on whenever a person enters the room.

SCIENCE & FUTURISTIC TECHNOLOGY

 CHINA PUBLICLY DISCLOSES PLAN FOR A LUNAR LANDING WITHIN THREE YEARS
(3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Chinese space officials have confirmed the country's plans to land
astronauts on the Moon within three years. The landings will involve a
lunar-orbit rendezvous similar to what NASA did with the Apollo
program. China and the US are racing to land astronauts on the lunar
surface, as it would be a major geopolitical win. Both countries are
also building capabilities to establish a long-term presence at the
Moon's South Pole.

 WHAT IS QUANTUM ADVANTAGE ACTUALLY WORTH? (9 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Nearly all roadmaps point to a utility-scale quantum computer being
developed around 2030. Despite this, there is little confidence or
clarity on how these machines will actually be useful. The consensus
is now that they'll first be used for breaking cryptography. The
second most concrete answer is to use them to generate simulation data
for AI labs' science models. This post looks at how else quantum
computing could be applied and where all the revenue will come from.

 WHY AGENTS ARE SOON ONLY GOING TO HAVE 1 TOOL (9 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Agents are collapsing their entire tool catalog into one 'exec' tool.
This allows models to write short scripts that chain, combine, and
await actions inside isolated sandboxes. Models can now reliably
combine and chain dependent actions in a single pass. In the previous
standard tool-calling model, agents could only perform single actions,
but the exec tool allows them to perform multiple actions.

 SCALING GIT AT OPENAI (31 MINUTE READ) [12]

 OpenAI's monorepo's size and demands increased at a staggering pace
over the last year, necessitating a revamp of the AI lab's source
control system. Its technical staff built Pando, a new backend for Git
repositories optimized for concurrent pushes to many branches and to
maximize read throughput. The team implemented the backend without
interrupting any engineering work or changing how the team uses Git.
Pando cut median push latency in production by nearly 5x, and it now
delivers nearly a petabyte of Git data per day.

 OUR ORGANIZATIONS ARE BEING DDOSED FROM THE INSIDE (6 MINUTE READ)
[13]

 Organizations are losing attention as everyone within is doing
something they think is useful, but all that work draws on the same
limited capacity to understand, review, and decide. Just because work
exists doesn't mean it's the right thing to do. Output is not proof of
value. A working implementation only means that something can be
built, not that it should be - some changes have negative value.

 THE VALUE OF OPEN SOURCE (16 MINUTE READ) [14]

 People open source projects for various reasons. Some do it to build
a company or product, others do it performatively, and some ship code
just for the love of the game. Open source was never free to copy, and
the code was never the moat. AI can remake a product, but it can't
provide the rest of the value a company generates. The technology is
helping maintainers ship and fix problems faster.
