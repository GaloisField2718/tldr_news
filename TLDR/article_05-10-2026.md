BIG TECH & STARTUPS

 APPLE'S JOHN TERNUS HAS FOUND A NEW DESIGN CHIEF: HIMSELF (11 MINUTE
READ) [6]

 Apple CEO John Ternus is spending a lot of time overseeing the
company's hardware and software design work. He is largely leaving the
company's financial operations to CFO Kevan Parekh and the supply
chain to COO Sabih Khan. Ternus is deeply involved in product
engineering and design. One of his first priorities was to restore the
company's design studio standing. The company is not looking to
recruit an outside chief design officer to oversee the entire
organization.

 PRETRAINING ON 50,000 HOURS OF UNLABELED BRAIN DATA (10 MINUTE READ)
[7]

 Neuralink's decoders are machine learning models trained to convert
neural activity into intended action. These decoders are unique to
each user - all users have to perform calibration prior to using a
Neuralink device. Neuralink has collected over 50,000 hours of
unlabeled, freeform neural data since its clinical trials began. This
post details how the company used this data to improve its models,
making its decoders more accurate and long-lived.

SCIENCE & FUTURISTIC TECHNOLOGY

 SUPERPOWERS RACE TO PUT NUCLEAR REACTORS ON THE MOON (14 MINUTE READ)
[8]

 NASA wants contractors to prepare to build a nuclear reactor that
could survive a space voyage and run without maintenance near the
south pole by December 2030. The agency is racing for the future of
space exploration. The US and China are both racing to establish a
moon base to search for resources and to launch missions deeper into
the solar system. Controlling access to the Moon's assets is vital to
this mission. Both superpowers believe a nuclear reactor is the
centerpiece of this ambition.

 THINGS THAT APPARENTLY CAUSE CANCER (12 MINUTE READ) [9]

 The series of Harvard studies that showed that nuclear power plants
were associated with higher rates of cancer and cancer mortality are
nonsense. The methodology used in the studies will always show an
increased cancer risk and mortality no matter what landmark is used.
The studies simply do not prove anything about cancer incidence.
Sophisticated statistics can produce extremely precise nonsense when
the design fails to identify the causal mechanism.

 E2E (WEBSITE) [11]

 e2e is an open-source AI testing framework. It provides customizable
testing for web, mobile apps, and more. e2e remembers agents' actions
and replays them when possible without calling the model again. Less
repeated reasoning results in fewer tokens and lower costs. Developers
can choose their preferred AI model and customize the testing agent.

 WHY DON'T MORE DEVELOPERS “USE THE PLATFORM”? (11 MINUTE READ)
[12]

 Advocates for web standards, performance, and accessibility have
implored web developers for years to 'use the platform', but despite
how obvious the advice seems to be, many people seem to still need
convincing. One reason for this is that, for the longest time,
browsers were playing catch-up with the ecosystem on top of them.
Another is due to the familiarity with tools they've used in the past.
Often, even with a robust standard, libraries fill a useful gap
between framework ergonomics and the platform underneath it.

 I QUIT OPENAI BECAUSE ITS CULTURE IS BROKEN (7 MINUTE READ) [13]

 David Robinson resigned from OpenAI this week, joining a parade of
former colleagues who have decided that the company's current path is
unacceptable. Robinson headed the writing of the safety reports that
OpenAI publishes with each major launch. OpenAI has thrived by trial
and error, but this approach guarantees periodic failures, and the
scale of those failures is growing. Achieving something much closer to
perfect the first time is becoming more essential because iteration
after a mistake may not be possible.
