BIG TECH & STARTUPS

 DOORDASH TO BUY INSTACART FOR $17 BILLION, UNITING 2 DELIVERY GIANTS
(7 MINUTE READ) [6]

 DoorDash will acquire Instacart for $17 billion. Instacart
shareholders will receive $65 per share, 26% over the company's price
on October 22. Combining the companies' technology and infrastructure
will result in faster deliveries and more affordable prices. The deal
is expected to close late next year or in 2028.

 REDDIT IS MOVING TO “A LOGGED-IN-ONLY EXPERIENCE,” CEO SAYS (2
MINUTE READ) [7]

 Reddit will start testing a logged-in-only experience in select
non-US markets in Q4. The company's CEO says the login requirement
will help Reddit retain new users. It will also provide more signals
for ads targeting and help protect access to the site's data. The
changes will make it significantly easier for Reddit to understand who
uses Reddit and why, which will help the site manage things like AI
scraping and evolving search habits.

 ELON SAYS BLINDSIGHT IS READY 2 YEARS AFTER FDA BREAKTHROUGH (2
MINUTE READ) [8]

 Neuralink's Blindsight implant is now ready from an engineering
standpoint. The device is designed to help blind people see. It is
currently waiting on regulatory approval before it can be sold to
customers. It has already received Breakthrough Device Designation
from the US FDA, which will help speed review of the device.

 A GOLDEN AGE OF SCIENCE IS COMING (16 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Progress has started to accelerate in science in the last two years.

 A LITTLE DETERMINISM (16 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Adding constraints to AI agents usually makes them less capable.
Sometimes the solution is to create a template or a complete
multi-stage workflow. The challenge is in creating the deterministic
behavior while retaining the agent's freedom to use the best tools for
the job. This post discusses a solution that uses a man-in-the-middle
egress proxy that forces behavior by denying actions and forces
reactions via automated messages.

 MAKING AGENTS FAST - WHAT I'VE LEARNT OVER THE LAST 12 MONTHS (12
MINUTE READ) [12]

 Knowing what part of inference a project relies on for speed is

the tools they want rather than what you think they need. Faster

parallelization helps, and classic software performance optimization

 BINANCE SUSPENDS TRANSACTIONS AFTER IT IS TARGETED IN BIGGEST-EVER
CYBER HEIST (5 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Binance has suspended transactions after a hack that the exchange
said totaled around $6.3 billion. Stablecoin providers Tether and
Circle moved quickly to freeze some of the stolen assets. The heist
appears to have been conducted by a North Korean hacking group.
Binance's founder said in a post on social media that users' funds are
safe.

 WE WON'T “JUST HANG OUT” (3 MINUTE READ) [14]

 The standard answer for what humans will do after the Singularity is
that people 'will just hang out'. However, AI will destroy the

education, or accomplishments, so they'll likely not be worth talking
to, and you probably wouldn't want to hang out with them.
