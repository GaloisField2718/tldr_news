BIG TECH & STARTUPS

 META LAUNCHES LIGHTWEIGHT $1,299 VR HEADSET THAT LOOKS LIKE GLASSES
(5 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Meta has launched the Meta VR Glasses at $1,299. The device has many
of the same features as Meta's existing Quest headsets. Its processor,
cooling fan, battery, and other computing components are housed in an
external pack. At about 100 grams, the device is roughly one-sixth the
weight of Apple's Vision Pro.

 CLAUDE DISCOVERS A NOVEL ENZYME SYSTEM WITH CRISPR-LIKE REPEATS (11
MINUTE READ) [6]

 Claude autonomously discovered a novel enzyme system that is
associated with an array of DNA repeats. The system's function is
still unknown, but it is programmable and can perform operations like
cutting, copying, and pasting DNA. It is based on a reverse
transcriptase previously known to science, but Claude appears to be
the first to notice the system's defining features - an associated
array of non-coding DNA sequences and an additional accessory protein
of unknown function.

SCIENCE & FUTURISTIC TECHNOLOGY

 GRAIL'S MULTI-CANCER BLOOD TEST GETS FDA COMMITTEE VOTE FOR PREMARKET
APPROVAL (2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Grail has received a favorable vote from an FDA panel regarding
premarket approval for its multi-cancer early detection test. The test
is designed to detect cancer-specific methylation patterns and, if
detected, predict the cancer signal origin with high accuracy to help
guide diagnostic evaluation. While the FDA isn't bound to the
committee's decision, it takes them into consideration when making the
final regulatory decision. It is expected to make its final ruling on
the test's premarket approval in the coming months.

 CHINA PUTS AI COMPUTE INTO ORBIT WITH SUPERCOMPUTING-1 SATELLITE —
ONBOARD PROCESSING AIMS TO CUT EARTH-OBSERVATION DATA PROCESSING FROM
HOURS TO MINUTES (2 MINUTE READ) [8]

 China has launched nine satellites into orbit as part of a push to
put AI computing in space. The Supercomputing-1 satellite that
launched on the mission is designed to capture and process
Earth-observation data in orbit instead of sending data back to
terrestrial data centers. It features both a high-res optical payload
and an image-processing AI computer. Local processing will cut
cross-regional data processing times from hours to minutes.

 A REDISTRIBUTION OF CODE OWNERSHIP (7 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Product managers and designers used to have to think through a great
deal ahead of time because the build was a larger investment, and the
people paying for it wanted to know what they were getting before they
committed. Agent coding changes the process so that nobody has to hold
the whole vision in their head and transfer it losslessly to someone
else. Engineers have historically owned code out of necessity, but AI
enables a model of ownership and collaboration that is distributed a
little more evenly.

 HOW WE MADE CLAUDE.AI 3X FASTER IN TWO WEEKS (15 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Anthropic made the core user experience of claude.ai and the Claude
desktop app about 3x faster during a two-week sprint in August. Time
to a typeable page on a fresh load of claude.ai went from 3.1 seconds
to 0.55 seconds, starting a new Claude Code session went from 0.8
seconds to 0.3 seconds, and loading a Claude Cowork cloud session went
from 2.6 seconds to 0.73 seconds. The optimizations will likely save
an estimated tens of thousands of user-hours of waiting every day.
This post covers what the Claude team shipped, how they measured it,
and the loop they built with Claude to do it safely.

 META MUSE IS PEOPLE (4 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Meta has been testing a human concierge for its Muse AI personal
assistant. Human contractors are quietly handling some of the phone
calls placed via the digital agent. The Muse agent is designed to
autonomously perform tasks like sending emails, shopping, and booking
travel on a person's behalf. The phone-calling feature allows users to
tell the agent to dial phone numbers for businesses in the US and talk
with people to handle errands like booking haircuts, checking whether
a store has an item in stock, or getting quotes from different
contractors.
