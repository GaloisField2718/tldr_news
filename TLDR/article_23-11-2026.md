 ANTHROPIC MATHEMATICIAN SAYS AI HAS SOLVED THE MOST FAMOUS PROBLEM IN
MATH (5 MINUTE READ) [7]

 An Anthropic researcher claims he worked with AI to discover a proof
of the Riemann Hypothesis. The conjecture in number theory predicts
how primes are distributed. Mathematicians have been trying to crack
it for 167 years, without success. The researcher's proof passed
verification with proof-checking software.

 TESLA LAUNCHES FSD V15, TARGETS ITS BIGGEST WEAKNESSES, FROM SIGNS TO
POTHOLES (10 MINUTE READ) [8]

 The release notes for Tesla's Full Self-Driving v15 read like a list
of the feature's biggest current weaknesses. The update includes
improvements to sign detection, potholes, and speed handling in school
zones. It also adds an impressive-looking live visualization. FSD can
now slow down for speed cameras. The update is currently being rolled
out.

 AI X BIO IS NOT _JUST_ A DATA PROBLEM (14 MINUTE READ) [10]

 How far AI gets in biology will depend largely on how much data we
have. It will also depend on what is measured and the quality of that
data. An abundance of systematically incomplete measurements is
unlikely to lead to a comprehensive understanding of many of the
complex systems that occur in biology. AI will likely become a
powerful tool that will contribute to the study of biology, but there
is still no clear path to documenting a comprehensive account of how a
whole cell or tissue works.

 I DON'T EXPECT SUPERINTELLIGENCE IN 2027 (12 MINUTE READ) [19]

 The industry seems to have a preference towards believing in
extremely short timelines. We are probably about 60% of the way
towards superintelligence relative to where we were in 2022.
Superintelligence will likely arrive in 2029 rather than in 2027.
While this seems like a tiny difference, it changes what the industry
should prioritize. The current push towards deprioritizing
'long-timeline' work is not a good idea.

 THE ALICE HUANG ASSOCIATE ACCUSED OF FUNNELING NVIDIA CHIPS TO CHINA
(6 MINUTE READ) [20]

 Li Ming, a 52-year-old Chinese national, was reportedly a central

currently out on bail in Singapore, living in a luxury villa down the

2025 in a case tied to the alleged transshipment of Nvidia chips. His
