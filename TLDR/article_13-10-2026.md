BIG TECH & STARTUPS

 META LAUNCHES MUSE WONDER AND WONDER FULL FOR AI AGENTS (2 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Muse Wonder is now available through the Meta Model API and Muse
Code. Muse Wonder Full is available only to Muse Code subscribers. The
new models combine Active Memory, which carries important personal
context across conversations and retrieves relevant details when a
task calls for them, with Active Focus, a feature designed to keep the
model working through difficult goals by trying alternative
approaches, handling blockers, adapting to new information, requesting
clarification when needed, and checking before important actions. Meta
says Muse Wonder is its most aligned model yet.

 MICROSOFT PREPARES TO PUT GIGAWATTS OF ITS CHIPS IN DATA CENTERS (3
MINUTE READ) [9]

 Microsoft's newest iteration of its Maia chips will start rolling out
next year. The Maia 300 is designed to handle inference. Microsoft
expects to stock multiple gigawatts of the chips at its server farms.
The Maia 300 delivers 40% more tokens per megawatt than leading
GPU-based systems. Microsoft isn't currently planning to rent the
underlying computing power of Maia chips directly to its cloud
customers.

SCIENCE & FUTURISTIC TECHNOLOGY

 AMAZON HAS OVER 1 MILLION ROBOT WORKERS. WHAT'S NEXT FOR HUMAN
EMPLOYEES? (23 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Amazon already has more than a million robots working in its
warehouses, and it is poised to significantly grow that robotic fleet
in the coming years. Amazon is also the second largest corporate
employer in the world. The company's executives say the growing robot
fleet has eased the burden on its human workforce, but labor advocacy
groups and independent researchers say the warehouse automation is not
all upside for the humans working alongside robots. It can get
complicated when humans are expected to keep pace with their robotic
counterparts.

 IS ‘ATTENTION' THE ESSENCE OF INTELLIGENCE? (14 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Research suggests that human thought is not purely linguistic. More
brain regions light up than just the areas associated with language
when we are thinking. Language may be how we communicate about the
world, but our thoughts seem to draw on many other areas outside of
the brain's language networks. AI's ability to translate effectively
means that it can understand, in some sense.

 WHY WEBMCP MATTERS (12 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Co-browsing needs to work really well for users for the web to
succeed in the agentic age. It is the only model where the developer
and the user maintain control over the experience, and where the web
remains the web. WebMCP enables sites to provide agents with direct
JavaScript tools they can call to accomplish tasks, which makes direct
interaction significantly faster, better, and cheaper. It circumvents
the slowness and awkwardness of AI agents trying to figure out how to
interact with sites.

 MUSE WONDER: ZUCKERBERG AND WANG'S $100B AI (130 MINUTE READ) [14]

 This post features a transcript of an interview with Mark Zuckerberg
and Alexandr Wang where they discuss the launch of Muse Wonder and the
future of personal AI agents. They also talk about the creation of
Meta Superintelligence Labs, the decision to execute a complete talent
reboot, and what it feels like to make an over $100 billion CapEx
commitment before knowing if the model will work. The interview
explores the architecture of Muse Wonder and lays out Meta's vision
for agentic commerce, hardware form factors like smart glasses, and
why superintelligence belongs in the hands of billions.

 EPIC GAMES REPORTEDLY PIVOTING TO “AI FIRST” GAME DEVELOPMENT
MODEL (3 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Epic is shifting to an AI-first development strategy for its
collaboration with the Walt Disney Company. Human developers will
manage and guide code and art assets generated by AI models for an
upcoming shooter. The company says it will aim to use AI to build more
and better stuff rather than try to build the same stuff with fewer
people. Epic laid off over 1,000 employees in March, citing a downturn
in Fortnite engagement as the main reason.
