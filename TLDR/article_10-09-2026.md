 APPLE'S IPHONE DUO, NEW WATCHES, IPHONE 18 PRO, AND AIRPODS 5:
EVERYTHING TO KNOW (12 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Apple's first foldable iPhone, the iPhone Duo, starts at $1,999. The
device expands to a 7.6-inch screen when unfolded, and a 5.4-inch
screen when used as a conventional handset. It comes in two colors:
Star White and Night Sky. More details on the device, as well as
Apple's other recent releases, are available in the article.

 APPLE TAKES ON AI WEARABLES WITH ALWAYS-LISTENING WATCH FEATURES (4
MINUTE READ) [9]

 The Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra models have new Audio
Intelligence tools that use ambient listening to take high-level notes
of wearers' conversations throughout the day. The devices will also
have a Live Recap function that can show a transcription of what was
said in a live conversation in the last 15 seconds and be able to
listen to important sounds, such as a crying baby, doorbells, or
sirens. These features will not create or store recordings, and Apple
will not be able to access the raw audio. They will be available to
test in English later this year. The improved Siri is being rolled out
on watchOS 27 in beta on September 14.

SCIENCE & FUTURISTIC TECHNOLOGY

 GENOME LANGUAGE MODELS CAN DESIGN CANCER VACCINES (32 MINUTE READ)
[10]

 Modern cancer-vaccine design involves identifying mutations,
predicting presentation, predicting immunogenicity, ranking targets,
and designing RNA. However, biology is complicated, and whether a
target makes a good vaccine candidate depends on all kinds of
information. This makes cancer vaccines an unusually direct test of
whether AI models can take what it has learned from biology and apply
that knowledge to design a medicine for one particular human being.

 SCENARIOS FOR OUR ECONOMIC FUTURE (12 MINUTE READ) [11]

 It is unknown yet whether AI will lead to unprecedented growth or
widespread unemployment. Anthropic's Economics team built a model to
understand how AI might affect jobs, growth, and unemployment in the
US in the coming years. The model found that AI will lead to adverse
impacts on wages and job prospects for knowledge workers in scenarios
where growth is faster than anything in economic history, and in those
scenarios, the challenge will be making sure wealth is broadly shared.
The answer will depend on how AI affects all the tasks that make up
the economy, the new tasks it creates, and how fast AI takes on this
work.

 MULTIMODAL MODELS NEED VIDEO. WE FROZE TIME TO GIVE IT TO THEM (8
MINUTE READ) [14]

 PostHog's Replay Vision lets models view session recordings. This
post looks at how PostHog built the tool and how it implemented a
method for the model to actually see replays. It covers how PostHog
built a custom rasterizer that runs hundreds of concurrent jobs across
many pods. The custom rasterizer has so far rasterized more than 370
years of recordings into roughly 3.5 million videos since it was
deployed in production in March.

 TAILWIND LABS IS JOINING SHOPIFY (3 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Tailwind is joining Shopify, one of the first companies operating at
scale to see the potential of Tailwind CSS and start building with it.
Shopify has invested in making sure Tailwind is actively maintained
and will continue to provide a stable long-term home for the project.
Nothing will change with Tailwind CSS or any of Tailwind's other open
source projects, but the project will no longer try to grow its
business. All existing customers will maintain access to their
products, but Tailwind has closed signups for new customers.

 AUTOMATTIC'S BOARD FORCES CEO MATT MULLENWEG INTO LEAVE OF ABSENCE (5
MINUTE READ) [16]

 Automattic's board has put founder and CEO Matt Mullenweg on a leave
of absence against his will. The company's Chief Financial Officer,
Mark Davies, had conspired with other board members to put Mullenweg
on a paid leave of absence. Davies is now acting as interim CEO.
Mullenweg remains the leader of the WordPress project, and the
company's teams, priorities, and work will continue as planned. It is
unclear why the board took this action. Mullenweg remains on the board
of directors.

 CRACKS IN THE AI THESIS PART 2 (4 MINUTE READ) [17]

 The price of AI is coming down. However, volume growth may not be
large enough to counteract the effective decline in prices. The models
driving volume increases are relatively cheap, standard models. These
trends do not appear to be driven by the adoption of open-source
models or Chinese models.
