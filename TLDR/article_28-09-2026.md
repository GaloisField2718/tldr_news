 OPENAI PAUSES TRAINING MOST CAPABLE MODELS AFTER SANDBOX ESCAPE (3
MINUTE READ) [5]

 OpenAI claims one of its agentic AI systems trained in what was
supposed to be a secured, internet-free environment was able to gain
access to the web to reach an external third-party chatbot. The agent
apparently exploited a gap to reach the public internet to send 20
queries to an unnamed, third-party chatbot service. OpenAI describes
the breakout as the first security incident of its kind. The company
has stopped training the model, but said it provided an important
signal about where to focus the next phase of work.

 SPACEX'S STARSHIP IS SET TO MAKE ITS FIRST ORBITAL FLIGHT (8 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 SpaceX is aiming to reach orbit on Starship's 14th test flight. The
test flight is scheduled for Monday at around 8 AM ET, with SpaceX
providing live coverage of the event on its website. Achieving orbital
flight will allow Starship to deploy satellites and travel to more
distant destinations. SpaceX will not try to catch the two rocket
stages during the test - the booster will make a simulated landing in
the Gulf of Mexico, and the upper stage will simulate a landing in the
Pacific Ocean to the west of Chile.

 WAYMO'S LATEST SAFETY NUMBERS SURE MAKE HUMAN DRIVERS LOOK BAD (3
MINUTE READ) [8]

 Waymo reports 82% fewer injury-causing crashes than human drivers.
The company has released data collected from over 270 million miles of
fully autonomous driving. Waymo cars were involved in 95% fewer
collisions that caused serious injury or worse, and there were 93%
fewer pedestrian incidents that resulted in injuries compared to human
drivers. While the company's data only measure the five metropolitan
areas where it operated during the study period, it shows that the
company's driverless robotaxis crash far less frequently than humans.

 S3 IS THE FUTURE, S3 IS THE PAST (5 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Since S3's release 20 years ago, SSD prices have dropped steadily,
and the gap between SSD and disk has narrowed to roughly 3x. S3-based
infrastructure has now become the industry standard. However, the
hardware constraints that motivated it are fading. The industry is
codifying architectural patterns that exist to work around limitations
that SSD storage doesn't have. Cloud providers have no incentive to
disrupt a model that serves them well, so developers need to build the
infrastructure primitives themselves.

 TESLA WORKERS BALK AT TRAINING OPTIMUS HUMANOID ROBOTS AS
REPLACEMENTS (3 MINUTE READ) [15]

 The complexity of Optimus' hands has translated to more production
headaches for Tesla, as human workers must manually assemble the hands
and forearms together using more than 100 small components. The heavy
reliance on imitation learning prompted Tesla to have factory workers
wear special suits designed to record their physical movements while
working. Some workers complained as they knew they were training
robots to eventually replace them. Tesla has since shifted data
collection to dedicated teams.
