BIG TECH & STARTUPS

 STARLINK 'COMMUNITY SITE' PROGRAM TEASES HOURLY, WEEKLY INTERNET
PASSES (2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Starlink plans to offer hourly, daily, and weekly passes for its
satellite internet service. The company is recruiting 'hosts' to sell
passes to interested consumers. Pricing details have yet to be
released. Starlink will take care of payments, user access, and
connectivity while the hosts focus on setting up their sites and
earning from connections.

 WHY MUSE MAY NEVER NEED ADS (13 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Meta is one of the very few companies that can monetize a personal
agent through advertising without ever showing an ad inside the agent
itself. The company can keep ads out of the Muse app and still get
paid for it on Facebook and Instagram. Every Muse errand can be an
incremental signal. Meta can indirectly monetize Muse until enough
demand has been aggregated for it to start with direct monetization.

SCIENCE & FUTURISTIC TECHNOLOGY

 WORLD'S FIRST ENHANCED GEOTHERMAL POWER PLANT COMPLETED IN JUST 23
MONTHS (2 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Fervo Energy has started selling electricity from its Cape Station
geothermal power plant. It is the first enhanced geothermal company to
reach a key commercial milestone. The entire site has the potential to
generate as much as 4 gigawatts of electricity. The first block at the
site took 23 months to complete - Fervo aims to complete future blocks
in as little as 18 months.

 WITH PRIMA, NASA WILL TRY TO BUILD A BILLION-DOLLAR SPACE TELESCOPE
IN RECORD TIME (6 MINUTE READ) [10]

 NASA's Probe Explorers are a new line of observatories intended to do
more science for less money. The project aims to seek a better balance
between the smaller Explorer missions and multibillion-dollar
flagships. NASA announced on Wednesday it was moving forward with the
first of the Explorers, PRIMA (Probe far-Infrared Mission for
Astrophysics). PRIMA will observe the universe in faint, far-infrared
light in a range of wavelengths between 24 and 235 micrometers. NASA
intends to launch the telescope in 2033.

PROGRAMMING, DESIGN & DATA SCIENCE

 ROR CREATOR SPARKS NEW “DEATH OF CODING BY HAND” DEBATE (15
MINUTE READ) [11]

 David Heinemeier Hansson, the creator of Ruby on Rails, recently
announced that 37signals, a company known for its software craft, was
done with writing code by hand. The maturation of AI tools has made it
possible for the company to embrace a future where coding by hand is
almost entirely absent. AI creates new business models and revenue
streams and disrupts traditional software. Companies are now investing
in innovation and are willing to pay top-of-market for software
engineers who can help them build AI products or become AI-native.

 PI DURABLE (33 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Pi Durable is an experimental package built specifically for
long-running, durable, and malleable agents that can run anywhere. It
can run anywhere, can be reached from different surfaces, supports
infinitely long conversations, survives catastrophic internal and
external failures, and lets multiple humans steer the same agents. It
is a framework for building any agentic application, including coding
agents.

 XBOX'S MILLENNIAL CEO ISN'T PLAYING AROUND (13 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Asha Sharma, the 38-year-old chief executive of Microsoft's Xbox
division, wants Xbox properties to entertain a billion people. This
means opening untapped markets in Africa, Latin America, and South
Asia by using cloud computing technology to run games on computers and
smartphones. Another pillar of her strategy is to reinvest in
Minecraft as a competitor to Roblox. Leaders at the company describe
the future of its consoles as embodying that idea.

 THE DOT AND THE SWARM (11 MINUTE READ) [15]

 AI is still too limited to substitute for large amounts of human
work. However, organizing agents is no longer the hard part. Agents
may be easier to integrate into firms than expected, as long as humans
are guiding them in the right direction. This could mean more work for
people, not less.
