 ‘IF ANYTHING HAPPENS TO THIS GUY … EVERYONE IS SCREWED' (13
MINUTE READ) [17]

 Ondřej Surý is one of the volunteers who helps maintain and
automate patches for PHP. He alone is responsible for most of the
world's distribution of PHP. His work, along with just a handful of
others, has allowed countless websites and organizations to fix the
recently revealed PHP vulnerabilities quickly. He's become arguably
the unsung hero of the entire internet, despite the fact that the only
compensation he receives for his work is from donations. This article
features an interview with Surý where he discusses the unfolding
AI-cybersecurity crisis, how the internet's infrastructure is
maintained by small crews of volunteers, and how it feels to save the
web.

 AN ARTIST'S TAKE ON AI IN MATH (9 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Mathematics seems to be a field where the outputs are celebrated less

knowledge and understanding. A mathematician's work may be informed by

by humans or sea sponges or computer programs.
