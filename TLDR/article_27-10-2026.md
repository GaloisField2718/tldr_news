 THE BIGGEST TAKEAWAYS FROM ANTHROPIC'S IPO PROSPECTUS (8 MINUTE READ)
[8]

 Anthropic unveiled its IPO paperwork yesterday, putting it on track
to go public as soon as the week before Thanksgiving. The 400-page
prospectus tells a story of how the company could usher in a new era
of progress that will transform how people code, conduct research,
build financial models, and automate routine tasks. It also warned
that AI could lead to the development of new bioweapons, destabilize
the geopolitical order, and evolve beyond the ability of humans to
understand and control. Anthropic plans to trade on the NASDAQ under
the ticker 'ANT', with a valuation expected to land between $1.5
trillion and $2 trillion.

 DOORDASH IS IN ADVANCED TALKS TO ACQUIRE DELIVERY RIVAL INSTACART (2
MINUTE READ) [9]

 DoorDash is looking to acquire rival Instacart in a deal that would
value Instacart at around $17 billion. The deal could be finalized as
soon as this week, but there is no guarantee it will come together.
Instacart's core business involves online grocery shopping and
convenience home delivery from retailers. The acquisition will add to
DoorDash's food delivery services from retailers such as Kroger,
Safeway, and Aldi.

 'IT'S OFFENSIVELY CHEAP': HOW SOLAR POWER IS QUIETLY BECOMING BIGGER
THAN NUCLEAR (9 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Solar power will likely provide 10% of the world's power by the end
of the year. Solar electricity is the fastest-growing source of new
electricity in history as it has become unbelievably cheap. China is
still the world's biggest solar power producer by far, but the
installations in the US are skyrocketing. Even with tariffs, solar is
still one of the cheapest sources of new power in the US, and it is
getting built regardless of political lines.

 WORKING WITH ACADEMIC COMMUNITIES TO BUILD SCIENTIFIC UNDERSTANDING
(12 MINUTE READ) [11]

 OpenAI has announced three new discoveries in mathematics, computer
science, and physics. For the math result, which is about harmonic
analysis, the AI lab will release the AI output, so researchers are
free to organize their own efforts to explain the result. OpenAI will
form an external group of collaborators to explain the computer
science result, which is about complexity theory, and will make the AI
output publicly available. The lab will also form an external group of
collaborators for the physics result, which is about quantum field
theory, but defer to them on the release of the materials.

 HOW THE CLAUDE CODE TEAM DOES CODE REVIEW (18 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Claude writes about 90% of the code in Claude Code. This post
explains how the team reviews the code at the volume its agents
produce. The team moved most of the reading in the review stage to
machines, optimized for human time and attention, and invested heavily
in making it fast and easy to undo mistakes. This resulted in merged
pull requests to Claude Code's codebase more than doubling while the
share that had to be reverted fell by more than 40% between June and
September.

 THE COUNTING HOUSE (49 MINUTE READ) [15]

 The Counting House pattern adds AI to systems that handle large
volumes of human work, notably customer service departments. It can be
used to control and improve costs, quality, and human ramp-up time
using comparatively cheap AI models. A Counting House uses classic
data science and engineering to put lower-tier models on hardened
rails to support human workflows. It allows teams to measure and
report how well models do against their human counterparts.

 THE END OF THE INTERNET (18 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Blogging began to die when Google started aggregating the internet.
Social media made it much easier to create and share content, but this
caused the amount of content to increase by orders of magnitude.
Algorithms started to matter, and then these sites started becoming
personal entertainment sites, tuned for engagement. The latest series
of security vulnerabilities unveiled by AI will further shrink the
public internet.

 MICROSOFT WILL OFFER BUYOUTS TO 60,000 EMPLOYEES (3 MINUTE READ) [17]


 Microsoft is extending voluntary buyout offers to 60,000 employees in
Canada, India, the UK, and the US. The program is time-limited. It
provides eligible employees with financial support and career planning
services. Microsoft expects less than 30% of employees will accept the
latest buyouts. The voluntary retirement program is part of a broader
shift by Microsoft to alter its performance system and how it awards
bonuses and stock options.
