 RESEARCHERS TRACK WHOLE BODY MOVEMENT WITH JUST A SMARTWATCH (6
MINUTE READ) [10]

 EchoMotion is a technology that can track the movement of a person's
entire body using ultrasonic sound waves with just an off-the-shelf
smartwatch and a lightweight AI model. The system uses the built-in
speaker and microphone on a commercial smartwatch to determine a
person's body position using a sonar-like sensing system. The
technology could potentially be used for tracking workouts, daily
activities, and progress on physical rehabilitation. The EchoMotion
team is currently developing the technology for use in fitness
tracking and exploring the use of the technology in combination with
first-person vision on smart glasses to efficiently track the objects
that a person handles.

 WHEN WILL HUMANS ACTUALLY FLY ON STARSHIP? (3 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Starship is now rapidly moving toward rapid reusability, but all
estimates of human spaceflight on the vehicle should be taken with a
gigantic grain of salt. There will be different timelines for humans
flying aboard Starship and landing on Earth. The first people to fly
on Starship probably won't launch from or land on Earth, as they will
probably be NASA's lunar astronauts. Humans will likely not land on
Earth in Starship before 2030.

 US MOVES TO CUT CHINA'S REMOTE ACCESS TO AI CHIPS (9 MINUTE READ)
[19]

 The US Commerce Department is closing a loophole that previously
allowed foreign rivals to bypass export restrictions on advanced
computer chips by renting access to them from data centers in
Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Brazil. Companies
headquartered in countries deemed to present a higher-than-normal risk
of diversion to China, Russia, and Iran must now apply for a license
with strict know-your-customer controls when transferring high volumes
of advanced chips. Companies with a license must submit monthly
reports on transactions that thoroughly detail all parties involved.
The rule change will take effect 90 days after it's posted to the
Federal Register.
