 APPLE CEO PRESSES SOFTWARE CHIEF TO PURSUE MORE AMBITIOUS AI STRATEGY
(17 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Apple's software chief Craig Federighi is being pressed to pursue a
more ambitious approach to AI as the company aims to transform Siri
into an autonomous agent. Federighi has a reputation for being an AI
skeptic. His leadership style has sometimes complicated product
development and led to delays or less ambitious initial versions of
devices, but he has demonstrated an ability to execute when others
could not. Siri AI finally fulfills Apple's vision of a working voice
assistant, but it is still far from the advanced tools offered by
rivals.

 WHAT TO KNOW ABOUT NVIDIA'S INVESTIGATION OF ITS CEO (5 MINUTE READ)
[7]

 Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang had a romantic relationship with Alice Huang,
a Chinese businesswoman whom US intelligence agencies have concluded
was a spy for Beijing. The company has opened an investigation into
Jensen Huang after the disclosure of the relationship. The board says
that it has no reason to believe that either national security or
Nvidia secrets were compromised. Alice Huang was likely recruited to
gather information on Jensen Huang and potentially influence Nvidia to
push for its most advanced chips to be made available to China.

 LLMS ARE TERRIBLE AT DESIGNING AI SYSTEMS (6 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Large language models don't know how to design software that uses
LLMs. This could be because most training code is pre-LLMs, or RLVR
may just not be a good environment to write code that calls LLMs.
Also, current AI agents might just not be good enough at software
design for the task. LLMs are good and bad seemingly at random, but if
you can predict where they will do well or badly ahead of time, you
can get enormous value from them.

 PROGRAMMING AND FATIGUE (4 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Programming is much more tiring with AI. This is a sign that you are
diligently working on things that require human brainpower and
judgment. AI is taking over the repetitive and obvious parts of the
job, leaving developers to deal with higher-judgment tasks. It is no
surprise that these would be more tiring.

MISCELLANEOUS

 AI IS CAUSING SIGNAL COLLAPSE EVERYWHERE ALL AT ONCE (20 MINUTE READ)
[13]

 AI is making it increasingly hard to tell good work from bad work and
fakes. Humans rely on shortcuts based on signals to make decisions all
the time. AI widely undermines these signals and lowers the incentive
to produce good work. The speed and scale of the current signal
collapse is unprecedented. We need signals to survive in the modern
world, and the rapid collapse of many previously dependable signals
will create large shifts in society.

 THE JEVONS PARADOX HAS A SECOND HALF (13 MINUTE READ) [14]

 The amount of software being created is going to go up much faster
than the number of people paid to make it. AI will enable far more
software of a kind nobody used to make. The developer jobs that grow
will be the ones that the machines made faster. The demand for
software can be bottomless and the work can still move somewhere else.
