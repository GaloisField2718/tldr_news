READ) [8]

 GOOGLE EMBRACES GEOTHERMAL TECHNOLOGY TO VAPORIZE ROCK FOR CLEAN
POWER (3 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Houston-based geothermal startup Quaise Energy plans to use its
technology to vaporize stone, drill at superhot depths, and produce
clean power. The company has signed a deal with Google valued at more
than $500 million for 50 megawatts of power. Quaise is building a site
in central Oregon. The electricity produced at the site will support
Google's existing data center operations in the state.

 UBER AND ZIPLINE'S DRONE DELIVERY SERVICE TAKES OFF IN TEXAS, WITH
DOZENS OF CITIES TO FOLLOW (3 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Zipline and Uber Eats have launched a commercial drone food delivery
service in Spring, Texas. Drone delivery will be limited to
participating restaurants at launch. The drones can carry orders that
weigh up to eight pounds. They can fly at about 300 feet above the
ground at speeds of up to 70 miles per hour. The companies plan to
expand drone delivery to dozens of additional cities in the US by the
end of 2029.
