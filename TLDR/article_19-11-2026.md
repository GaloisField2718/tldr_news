SCIENCE & FUTURISTIC TECHNOLOGY

 HARDWARE IS SOFTWARE (13 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Most hardware engineering is actually software engineering in
disguise. Everything is a calculation, and calculation is software.
The industry is currently doing these calculations essentially by
hand, as if it were doing arithmetic without having yet discovered
algebra. Hardware engineers need to design algorithms for designing
their hardware. This allows small teams to build big things.

 UNBLOCKABLE COMMUNICATION WITH GRAVITY (32 MINUTE READ) [10]

 All modern wireless communication technologies are based on
electromagnetism, which is readily susceptible to screening and
blocking. Gravity is a viable platform for practically unblockable
communication. This study proposes and implements a wireless
communication protocol where a broadcaster encodes a binary message by
moving a mass, and a receiver detects the resulting gravitational
signal with an off-the-shelf, hand-held gravimeter.

 AWS ANNOUNCES EXTENDDB 1.0 (1 MINUTE READ) [12]

 ExtendDB is a production-ready Amazon DynamoDB-compatible adapter. It
is fully compatible with the DynamoDB API and can run on PostgreSQL,
SQLite, Cassandra, and MongoDB. ExtendDB allows developers and
infrastructure teams to run DynamoDB workloads in on-premise data
centers, in private or public clouds, and at the edge, without
rewriting application code. It is open source under the Apache 2.0
license.

 FOUNDATION SOFTWARE (5 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Open source projects are being flooded with PRs thanks to AI. Instead
of submitting these PRs, developers should publish forks with no
expectation of merging. Maintainers could then search through the
forks and see what other people are building, then choose which
changes to make upstream. This search could be done with agents
hunting for patterns.

MISCELLANEOUS

 ON WAYMOS AND THEIR SAFETY RECORD (6 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Waymo has little incentive to prioritize road safety beyond a
self-determined limit. Once the company captures a significant market
share, a board will decide what an acceptable level of road fatalities
is. The company can eat any amount of fine payments. Waymo cannot
solve road safety - it can only shift the locus of policy around road
safety away from the government into an unaccountable corporation.

 NVIDIA INTERIM CEO KRESS TELLS EMPLOYEES ‘LET'S MOVE FORWARD' AT
FIRST ALL-HANDS MEETING SINCE JENSEN HUANG EXIT (4 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Nvidia recently held its first all-hands meeting since its founder,
Jensen Huang, resigned. The meeting opened with a message about moving
forward. Executives announced that pay rises and the yearly review
process will be moved up from March to December. There appears to be
division in the company about whether to reinstate Huang, but the
company's newly appointed board chair has said that the board does not
intend to do so.
