 ALZHEIMER'S IS NO LONGER AN UNTREATABLE DISEASE, MAJOR REPORT
CONCLUDES. HERE'S WHY (7 MINUTE READ) [9]

 There are currently hundreds of clinical trials worldwide
investigating possible medicines, new diagnostic options, and
lifestyle interventions to identify, treat, or delay cognitive
decline. The possibility of a cure is still far away, but it now seems
more possible than ever before. Alzheimer's disease is being
increasingly viewed as a condition for which earlier diagnosis, better
understanding of disease biology, and a wider range of interventions
may collectively improve outcomes. There is now a genuine case for
optimism.

 BUG BLINDNESS (35 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Some bugs give normal users such a bad experience that they tell
their friends about it. Developers and advanced users can be blind to
these bugs because they're used to performing the non-intuitive
workarounds required to get around them. Humans have a high ability to
ignore the negatives in things they like. Being open to criticism can
help developers notice more flaws in their work.
