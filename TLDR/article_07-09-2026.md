 THE CURIOUSLY PLAYABLE UNIVERSE (41 MINUTE READ) [8]

 A domain becomes playable when enough of its complexity can be
compressed into a state where there is some reasonably stable
repertoire of actions, interactions can be repeated, outcomes provide
feedback about better and worse play, and the environment is
stationary enough that lessons learned yesterday remain useful
tomorrow. None of these conditions need to be perfect, as playability
is a matter of degree. Far more of reality than we have previously
expected can be turned into a game. Once something can be turned into
a game, then it's possible to create a system that can beat it.

 THE LOOP CLOSES (5 MINUTE READ) [15]

 AI models are now capable of taking a request from Slack and then
