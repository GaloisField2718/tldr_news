BIG TECH & STARTUPS

 JENSEN HUANG HAS BEEN OUSTED AS NVIDIA CEO (8 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Jensen Huang has resigned as Nvidia's CEO after the revelation of two
extramarital affairs. Finance chief Colette Kress will serve as
interim CEO until a permanent successor is named. The board is
considering both internal and external candidates. Nvidia's board has
appointed long-time director Harvey C. Jones as its first ever chair.
Huang has previously said that he doesn't believe in succession
planning and that leaders should continuously pass on knowledge and
information as they have it.

 SPACEX AND TESLA TALKS ON RECORD MERGER ARE SAID TO PROGRESS (4
MINUTE READ) [9]

 SpaceX and Tesla could announce a merger deal as soon as December.
The companies' boards and advisers are working out the terms of the
deal and the governance structure of the combined entity. The merger
will be a major test case for political sentiment following the US
midterm elections. The aggressive timeline reflects Elon Musk's
tendency to move quickly when it comes to corporate maneuvers.

SCIENCE & FUTURISTIC TECHNOLOGY

 A DEEPSEEK MOMENT FOR BIOTECH (12 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Chinese drugmakers account for at least a third of the world's
pipeline of innovative drugs in development, but China-originated
novel treatments have yet to reach overseas markets at scale. The
country runs more clinical trials than any place else. Chinese
drugmakers with global ambitions currently pursue them primarily
through partnerships with Western companies, who usually take charge
of late-stage development and eventual sales of the drugs outside of
China. Some lawmakers in the US are calling for restrictions on
partnerships with Chinese companies, but industry analysts say the
business momentum is too strong to break.

 THE NEXT CHAPTER OF MRNA (28 MINUTE READ) [11]

 mRNA technology has the potential to generate hundreds of new
treatments for a range of diseases. It is quickly becoming the
preferred technology for its speed - a vaccine can be created against
any pathogen within six to eight weeks, less time than it takes to
conduct the studies required to establish a therapy's safety and
efficacy. The technology could redesign treatments in infectious
diseases, rare diseases, and cancer.

 SLOP IS MOVING UP THE STACK (18 MINUTE READ) [13]

 AI is writing significantly less slop, but slop has not been
eliminated. Slop has now moved up the stack to infest design
decisions. Frontier agents lack foresight into how their code will be
used. They display poor abstraction design and complete disregard for
reusing library-provided code.

MISCELLANEOUS

 AI COMPANY EMPLOYEES SHOULD UNIONIZE (10 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Safety-conscious employees at AI companies should unionize, as this
would give them the power to keep their employers in check if they
have concerns about their work. There are so few skilled AI
researchers that companies will want to negotiate if a dozen top AI
researchers threaten to walk. A union would uphold normal protections
around discipline and firing, demand transparency, and act as an
independent voice to hold companies to their commitments. RSI could
make AI employees obsolete as soon as next year, so they need to take
action now, as they may never have this amount of leverage again.

 GOOGLE PUSHES PRIVACY ANGLE AS GEMINI-POWERED SMARTGLASSES DEBUT
STARTING AT $395 (5 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Google has partnered with Samsung, Gentle Monster, and Warby Parker
to create Gemini-powered smartglasses. The frames, which all weigh
around 41 grams, will have a camera that Gemini can use for tasks. The
assistant will have access to user data and preferences to produce
personalized answers. Google claims it has implemented a number of
features and limitations to ensure that people don't do creepy things
with the device, and that it doesn't do creepy things to users.
