BIG TECH & STARTUPS

 AMAZON'S RING UNVEILS FLYING DRONE AND ROBOT TO PATROL HOMES AND
BUSINESSES (3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 The Ring Drone Cam is a security camera that can fly from room to
room to investigate suspicious activity. For $250, the drone can fly
up to eight minutes at a time before autonomously returning to its
charging dock. The Ring Rover Cam is a $1,000 device with a display
and an extendable periscope camera mounted on a two-wheeled base. The
Ring Drone Cam will ship in the US on January 27 (pending approval
from the FCC), and the Ring Rover Cam will start shipping on December
16.

 APPLE'S CLARIS TESTING 'HARBOR' APP FOR LOCAL AI ON MACS (2 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 Harbor is an app currently being tested by Apple's Claris subsidiary
that turns Apple Silicon Macs into private AI servers. It supports
connecting multiple Mac computers over Thunderbolt 5 to pool unified
memory and enable larger models with higher concurrency. Harbor is in
early access and not publicly available. There was an App Store
listing, but it has now been removed, so it isn't clear whether the
app will be free. The sign-up page for the app asks for a company
name, suggesting a business focus.

SCIENCE & FUTURISTIC TECHNOLOGY

 ‘FLYING CARS' COULD LAUNCH IN CHINA NEXT SUMMER, MAKER OF TWO-SEAT
MODEL SAYS (4 MINUTE READ) [10]

 XPeng's $300,000 low-altitude flying car will be ready for use in the
second half of 2027. About the size of a Fiat 500, the vehicle can fly
using six drone-like battery-powered propellers. It can be steered
using a joystick or fly autonomously to a programmed destination. The
price tag includes a six-wheeled van for transporting the vehicle. The
flying car has a flight range of 30 kilometers, but XPeng says this
will soon be doubled.

 STARLINK MINI 2 HAS A SWAPPABLE BATTERY, BUT IT COSTS WAY MORE THAN
EXPECTED (7 MINUTE READ) [11]

 The Starlink Mini 2 is the first Starlink dish to support the
company's swappable battery, which lasts up to 4.5 hours. SpaceX is
asking early adopters to pay $999 to own it. That fee includes a full
year of Starlink Roam Unlimited, which is usually valued at $2,100.
Shipments are estimated to begin in mid-December.

 MODERN WEB GUIDANCE 1.0: THE WEB PLATFORM EXPERTISE YOUR CODING AGENT
IS MISSING (15 MINUTE READ) [14]

 The Modern Web Guidance is a set of skills that embed web platform
expertise, best practices, and browser compatibility data directly
into coding agents. The stable release of the guidance includes a new
modernization workflow, an expanded guide database, deeper human
expert review for every guide, and full evaluation coverage across all
of the guides. Firefox and Edge have joined Chrome to support Modern
Web Guidance to improve cross-browser compatibility and contribute to
the project. The project aims to ensure that anyone coding with agents
can take full advantage of the power and capabilities of the modern
web platform.

 Q3.5 TIMELINES UPDATE: LOOKS LIKE THE INTELLIGENCE EXPLOSION WILL
HAPPEN IN 2027 AFTER ALL (126 MINUTE READ) [15]

 A Superhuman AI Researcher (SAR) is predicted to arrive at some point
in 2027, assuming there is no substantial deliberate slowdown in
development. AIs that can automate all white-collar work are predicted
to arrive before 2028. Companies are currently not on track to align
an SAR, and using a misaligned SAR to do recursive self-improvement
will have negative consequences. China will probably be able to steal
the training recipe and maybe even the model weights if an SAR is
trained in the near future.

 ANTHROPIC TO SEEK $1.3 TRILLION VALUATION IN IPO (5 MINUTE READ) [16]


 Anthropic plans to sell 825 million shares at a range of $70 to $78 a
share in its initial public offering, which is expected to start
trading on Thursday of next week. The company expects to raise an
additional $26 billion from big investors, including Google and AMD.
Some Anthropic executives and founding members plan to sell around
23.6 million shares in the offering. At the planned valuation,
Anthropic's IPO won't top SpaceX's record-breaking offering in June.
