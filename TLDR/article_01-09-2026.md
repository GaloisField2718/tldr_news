 APPLE'S COOK SAYS HE ‘WILL MISS THIS WORK' IN FINAL MEMO AS CEO (5
MINUTE READ) [5]

 Despite saying he'll miss the role of chief executive officer, Tim
Cook has enormous comfort in the leadership of John Ternus, his
replacement. Cook took over as Apple's CEO in August 2011. His 15-year
tenure included the launch of the Apple Watch, AirPods, and major
expansions to the iPhone, iPad, and Mac lines. Cook will remain as
executive chairman, where his role will include working with
governments around the world.

 FTC SUES AMAZON, ALLEGING IT DECEIVED ADVERTISERS AND DROVE UP PRICES
(6 MINUTE READ) [6]

 The FTC has filed a lawsuit against Amazon alleging that it
manipulated prices that businesses paid to advertise on its retail
platform. Amazon allegedly deceived advertisers by secretly raising
the minimum price advertisers had to pay. Amazon's digital-ad platform
is the third-largest in the world behind Google's and Meta's
platforms. It earned $68 billion from ads in 2025.

SCIENCE & FUTURISTIC TECHNOLOGY

 LACHY GROOM BACKS INDIAN STARTUP AIMING TO KEEP AIRCRAFT ALOFT FOR A
YEAR (5 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Alteon, a Bengaluru startup, aims to keep an aircraft in the sky for
more than a year by harvesting energy from ocean winds. Its small,
fixed-wing autonomous aircraft can extract energy from wind shear
above the ocean through dynamic soaring. While the startup has yet to
demonstrate an aircraft that can actually sustain flight using energy
harvested through dynamic soaring, its 20-person team is building four
to five aircraft a week for testing and has conducted more than 200
test flights in the past 30 days.

 HOW MATIC GOT ROBOTS INTO 10,000 HOMES (12 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Matic didn't grow by taking shortcuts. The company took six to seven
years to ship, picked a category with very little new innovation, and
spent most of that time on problems that don't demo well. It took on a
market that already existed, put a different spin on it, and is now
shipping American-made consumer home robots at real scale. This post
looks at seven core lessons learned from Matic's journey so far.

 THE FUTURE TECH ROLE IS THE PRODUCT ARCHITECT (9 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Coding is moving from humans to agents. The human role is moving
towards what should be built, defining how systems should work, and
verifying results. The person deciding what should exist also needs to
understand the system in which it will run. The product architect role
owns the whole loop - it involves understanding the problem, deciding
on a solution, designing the system, setting constraints, directing
agents, and verifying what gets built.

 GOOGLE HAS REMOVED MANIFEST V2 EXTENSIONS FROM THE CHROME WEB STORE,
INCLUDING UBLOCK ORIGIN (3 MINUTE READ) [15]

 All remaining Manifest V2 extensions have been removed from the
Chrome Web Store. Manifest V2 extensions installed on Chrome 138 or
earlier will remain installed. However, they will be unable to receive
any updates and cannot be reinstalled from the Chrome Web Store once
removed from Chrome. Users will no longer be able to find or install
Manifest V2 extensions through the Chrome Web Store even if the
Chromium-based browser they use continues to support Manifest V2.

 INVISIBLE COMPANIES (22 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Invisible companies remain invisible primarily because no one is
paying attention to them. There are several reasons this could happen:
they could be unknown, data about their existence or profitability is
private, missing, or obscure, they are misunderstood, or they are
disdained. Over time, the market should make these invisible companies
visible, but that's not what happens. Thinking about invisible
companies means thinking about strategy in a new way.
