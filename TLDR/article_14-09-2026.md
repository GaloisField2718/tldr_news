BIG TECH & STARTUPS

 TOP AI LEADERS CALL FOR SLOWING DOWN AI DEVELOPMENT (5 MINUTE READ)
[6]

 Anthropic's CEO recently released a 3,800-word essay calling for a
global slowdown of AI development. He said that while the technology
offers many benefits, it is advancing too quickly for researchers to
continue safely. AI leaders are preparing for a day when an AI system
can grow more advanced without the aid of human researchers. Some
researchers believe that achieving recursive self-improvement will
lead to AI systems spinning out of control.

 OPENAI'S SAM ALTMAN SAYS IT WOULD BE ‘ILL-ADVISED' TO GO PUBLIC IN
2026 (1 MINUTE READ) [7]

 OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has confirmed that the company will not be
going public this year. He said it would be an ill-advised moment to
go public due to current safety concerns. OpenAI had hired bankers and
lawyers with the goal of going public this year. The company is now
leaning toward 2027 due to the volatility of tech stocks and its own
financial challenges.

SCIENCE & FUTURISTIC TECHNOLOGY

 WHERE HAS CONSTRUCTION AUTOMATION BEEN SUCCESSFUL? (20 MINUTE READ)
[8]

 Construction is labor-intensive. There has, unsurprisingly, long been
interest in automating the process to reduce the amount of labor
required. However, automation has only been successful in tasks that
can be done off-site in factories and on-site tasks that are extremely
factory-like. Almost nothing outside of these two categories has
succeeded.

 A SEVERE MISALIGNMENT OF AI IN MATHEMATICS (6 MINUTE READ) [9]

 The push by AI companies to solve mathematical problems as a
benchmark is detrimental to the science of mathematics. The goals of
AI companies and the mathematical community are severely misaligned.
This is a symptom of broader alignment issues that impact other
scientific and creative professions, as well as the whole of society.

 THE RISE OF THE FORWARD DEPLOYED ENGINEER — AND HOW TO DO THE JOB
RIGHT (21 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Forward-deployed engineers have the hottest job in AI right now.
While labs, startups, and PE firms are all hiring engineers to sit
inside their customers' operations and solve their problems, almost
none of them agree on what those engineers are supposed to accomplish.
This article looks at what works and what fails and tries to predict
where the industry is heading. It covers misunderstandings about the
role, its history, what the goal of the role is, and more.

 IF CODING IS SOLVED, WHAT NOW?: MEASURING THE SLOPPINESS OF CODE (7
MINUTE READ) [12]

 Large language models are able to write almost perfectly correct
code. It is pretty straightforward to get a model to generate code and
then let that code be checked by hidden tests. Checking the sloppiness
of this code often requires human intuition and time, and is an
extremely difficult task in general. This post illustrates why this is
by going through possible ways of measuring slop.

MISCELLANEOUS

 WE MUST PACE THE FRONTIER (23 MINUTE READ) [13]

 While AI has the potential to produce a long line of technological
miracles, like many technologies before it, it brings risk. These
risks include losing control of AI systems, the misuse of the
technology for cyberattacks and bioterrorism, and serious economic
disruption. A race to the bottom spurred by commercial incentives
makes these risks more acute. Anthropic's CEO has called for the
industry to prioritize caution over speed and prudence over profit.
Addressing these risks will require pacing the rate of capabilities
advancement so that risk prevention has time to keep up.

 ROBLOX IS MAKING IT EASIER TO BUILD GAMES WITH AI — AND PLAY THEM
OUTSIDE ROBLOX (4 MINUTE READ) [14]

 The company behind Roblox announced several new features at its
annual Roblox Developer Conference, including new game-creation tools,
expanded NPC capabilities, and the ability to make games available
across platforms. The company is also launching a dedicated card and
wallet for creators. Roblox is rolling out its generative AI game
creation feature more broadly to Serbia and Singapore. It is also
gaining desktop access, and later this year, it will get a new
prompt-based scene-generating feature.
