 GOOGLE ANNOUNCES GEMINI 4 ARGON AI MODEL, BUT YOU CAN'T USE IT YET (3
MINUTE READ) [7]

 Google's Gemini 4 Argon model offers industry-leading performance in
coding, knowledge work, and cybersecurity. Google's engineers are
already using the model extensively, and Argon-powered agents have
been working to migrate C/C++ codebases to Rust across Google. The
model supports an output limit of 1 million tokens. It will be priced
at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens for a
limited time. The model is still in limited testing, and there is no
timeline for when it will be available to enterprise and consumer
customers.

manufacturers on devices designed for the Siri AI home platform.

SCIENCE & FUTURISTIC TECHNOLOGY

 GOOGLE FIGURES OUT HOW TO WATERMARK AI-DESIGNED PROTEINS (11 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 The DeepMind team at Google has developed a technique that can create
watermarks on protein sequences without compromising the protein's
function. The watermark is impossible to remove and can survive basic
exporting, resizing, and other editing. The system could be used to
give DNA synthesizers greater confidence in their sources.

 MOST POWERFUL OBESITY DRUG YET: PEOPLE LOST UP TO 25% OF WEIGHT IN
TRIAL (5 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Late-stage clinical trial data on the obesity drug retatrutide showed
an average loss of 25% of body weight after 90 weeks. More than a
third of the participants taking the drug in the trial lost 30% or
more of their weight. The drug also helped reduce knee pain and sleep
apnea events, and it improved cardiometabolic measurements across the
board. At the start of the trial, more than a third of trial
participants had prediabetes - the condition had resolved in more than
90% of those participants by the end.

 TECH CEOS PRIVATELY QUESTIONED AMODEI FOR SOUNDING AI ALARM BELLS (7
MINUTE READ) [14]

 AI chief executives gathered with Trump at the White House on Tuesday
to discuss a statement of principles to keep AI models safe. Behind
the scenes, the executives questioned Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei about
his warnings about the dangers of AI, asking why he was being so
extreme in public about the capabilities of AI models and the risks
they pose. Amodei told them it was important to be honest with the
public about model capabilities and not play down risks. He believes
that safety is possible if the industry and administration work
together.
