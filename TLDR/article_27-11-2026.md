BIG TECH & STARTUPS

 NVIDIA CHIP BUYERS TOLD TO RULE OUT CHINESE RENTERS TO PURCHASE GPUS
(3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 A new rule that restricts remote access to AI semiconductors was
announced by the Trump administration on November 16. The new rule
seeks to close an export controls loophole that allowed Chinese
companies to rent access to Nvidia's most advanced chips when hosted
in data centers overseas. Chip sellers have communicated the message
to neoclouds, telling them they won't be able to purchase Nvidia chips
if any of the computing capacity will be rented by Chinese companies.
Neocloud stocks have come under renewed pressure lately as investors
question the sustainability of the AI boom.

 OPENAI MAY BE IN COST CONTROL MODE (2 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Astra 6.2 and Sol 6.2 have likely been tuned for cost savings. Output
tokens per task and reasoning tokens per task have dropped
significantly. The models seem to be reasoning a lot less compared to
opponents. Charts showing the change in token usage across models are
available in the article.

SCIENCE & FUTURISTIC TECHNOLOGY

 IT'S SO OVER FOR QUANTUM GRAVITY? (6 MINUTE READ) [9]

 A recent paper released by an OpenAI employee claimed to have
quantized gravity. The paper quickly gained viral status on X. The
employee, who is also currently a postdoc at Stanford, spent 400
million tokens while discussing and proving his results with ChatGPT
Astra. However, for now, it appears that AI has not solved quantum
gravity.

 CHINA'S SECOND-LARGEST HOG PRODUCER TURNS TO BARN ROBOTS (2 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Wens Foodstuff Group, China's second-largest hog producer, has
invested in Lasaite Robot, a maker of rail-riding barn robots.
Lasaite's robots log body temperatures, feed intake, and the activity
of the pigs in each pen to keep livestock healthy. Wens sells tens of
millions of hogs every year. The automation provides early warning
signals if disease or other health problems are detected.

 WHY I'M BULLISH ON MCP FOR 2027 (10 MINUTE READ) [12]

 MULTIAGENT CODING IS THE FUTURE (23 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Multiagent coding will have a seismic impact on the software
development industry, and also the general way people do knowledge
work. Early adopters have shipped a variety of experiments showing how
multiagent collaboration could work. Labs have still not made good
products around multiagent coding, possibly because they are waiting
for approaches in the community to settle. There are also dangers with
highly capable agent swarms autonomously interacting with
infrastructure.

MISCELLANEOUS

 BULLISH ON HUMAN INTELLIGENCE (5 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Some people believe that human intelligence is obsolete and that all
there is left is to build RSI and prepare for an AI takeover. However,
despite their recent successes, frontier AIs are still bottlenecked by
human talent in getting better. Many of the properties of human
intelligence are the moonshot goals of frontier AI labs. People should
not give up so easily because of the hype.

 IS JEVONS PARADOX HOLDING? (3 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Jevons Paradox has been the defining law of inference so far in the
AI boom. Usage has exploded enough to push total spend higher as token
prices have fallen. That dynamic has weakened in recent months. Token
consumption shows no signs of slowing, but spend growth has slowed
significantly.
