BIG TECH & STARTUPS

 META LAUNCHES ENTERPRISE AI PLATFORM, HIRES MONGODB CEO TO LEAD NEW
INITIATIVE (2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Meta's new enterprise AI platform is an initiative aimed at expanding
the company's AI offerings to businesses and corporate customers.
Chirantan 'CJ' Desai, former CEO of MongoDB, is leading the
initiative. The Meta Enterprise Platform will focus on turning the
company's AI stack into products and services that companies can
deploy for their own businesses. MongoDB has appointed Dev Ittycheria
as interim chief executive while the board searches for a permanent
replacement.

 OPENAI SCRAPS RELEASE OF NEW AI MODEL OVER SAFETY CONCERNS (6 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 OpenAI is scrapping the release of GPT-6.1 Astra due to safety
concerns. The company had aimed to launch the model in October, but
the model performed poorly on tests measuring alignment compared to
its predecessor. GPT-6.1 Astra showed higher levels of deception, and
it would push ahead on tasks without asking users for permission. It
would also reach for external tools and services even if it might be
unsafe. OpenAI says it is working to investigate a range of agent
security incidents and address the safety issues underneath them.

SCIENCE & FUTURISTIC TECHNOLOGY

 SPACEX'S STARSHIP MAKES IT TO ORBIT FOR THE FIRST TIME, BUT RETURNS
TO EARTH EARLY (8 MINUTE READ) [9]

 SpaceX's Starship circled Earth on Monday. The spacecraft had to
return to Earth after less than two orbits instead of the planned six
because of engine problems that occurred during liftoff. The Starship
spacecraft splashed down with a fiery explosion in the North Pacific
near Hawaii before noon. Reaching orbit is a key milestone towards
SpaceX's goal of eventually sending people to Mars as well as NASA's
plans to send astronauts back to the moon.

 AIRBOUND: AS WE MAY MOVE (130 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Airbound's first drone, the TRT, weighs in at 1.5 kilograms. The
eVTOL can carry a 1-kilogram payload and can land and launch from
anywhere, making logistics possible where infrastructure doesn't
exist. The company's goal is to move mass more cheaply than anyone
else. Its main focus is cost per kilogram-kilometer, and everything is
secondary to that.

 THE CODE NOBODY READS (24 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Know what's worth reading, and on the code you don't read, build
checks you actually trust. Good code is a love letter to the next
developer. The next developer is now an agent, so the letter looks
different now. You need to pass on code that agents can change without
breaking something it can't see. The craft has now moved to the parts
that decide whether the code deserves to be trusted.

 ANTHROPIC HAS LAUNCHED CLAUDE SONNET 5.5 (4 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Claude Sonnet 5.5 scores just 2 points behind Opus 5.5 (max) on the
Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. However, it uses around 60%
more tokens to reach that performance. The model is priced at $2/$10
per million tokens of input/output, matching GPT-6 Sol. Sonnet 5.5
still lags on factual knowledge as it is a smaller class model than
Opus 5.5, but it has a lower hallucination rate.

 APPS, AGENTS, AND AGGREGATION (17 MINUTE READ) [14]

 The new prize in technology is to be the only interface users need
for everything, with agents using computers on users' behalf and
generating whatever UI is needed. For people who are using agents,
this is obvious. Apps are just a vehicle for people to accomplish
something or entertain themselves. Once people can get straight to the
job to be done, it will seem odd that they did it any other way.

 OPENAI UNDERSTANDS SOMETHING IMPORTANT AND RARE (17 MINUTE READ) [15]

customers. It has a well-established track record of being able to
find the simple, obvious-in-hindsight breakthroughs that unlock new
kinds of consumption behavior. This, along with its durable
distribution strategy, will help it become the winner in the AI race.
