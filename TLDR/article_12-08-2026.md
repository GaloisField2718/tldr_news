BIG TECH & STARTUPS

 SPACEXAI UNVEILS GROK BOT TO WORK LIKE A TEAM OF AI AGENTS (2 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 SpaceXAI's Grok Bot is designed to act like a team of AI agents
capable of handling a wide range of professional work. The agent can
sign into various apps and websites, retain information from prior
tasks, and share details and context with other agents. SpaceXAI has
been using the product internally across its engineering, growth, and
marketing teams. It has helped its sales team find new accounts and
draft emails and aided its finance team by pulling receipts from
emails.

 GEMINI BECOMES GOOGLE'S FASTEST-GROWING PRODUCT EVER AS IT HITS 1B
USERS (5 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Gemini has reached 1 billion monthly active users (MAU). It is the
fastest Google product to have managed that feat. Gemini is now in
virtually every Google product and service and is core to Google's
flagship search experience. The 1 billion MAU metric only refers to
users who have opened the Gemini app or visited the Gemini web
interface.

SCIENCE & FUTURISTIC TECHNOLOGY

 AI IS FINDING SPERM WHERE DOCTORS COULDN'T (2 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Scientists from Columbia have created a system that uses AI to locate
difficult-to-find sperm. Men with extraordinarily low sperm counts
often still have several hidden sperm present. Analyzing samples under
a microscope with the human eye often misses these individual sperm
cells. The Sperm Tracking and Recovery system may bring hope to the
10% of infertile men affected by azoospermia.

 INDIA'S FIRST PRIVATE ROCKET REACHED ORBIT — FROM A COMPANY THAT
EXISTED BEFORE THE LAW DID (11 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Skyroot's Vikram-1 became the first privately built Indian rocket to
reach orbit on July 18. It reached success on the first attempt and
delivered a payload to about 450 km. India has no space statute, and
its entire private space industry runs on executive policy. Skyroot is
targeting roughly one rocket launch a month from 2027.

 HIS START-UP'S GOAL: AI THAT IS TRAINABLE AND NOT CONTROLLED BY A BIG
COMPANY (2 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Igor Babuschkin, who founded xAI with Elon Musk, left a year ago to
build a new startup, River AI. The startup wants to create a new type
of home computer that allows people to own and use AI without
oversight from anyone else, including River AI. It plans to share its
AI technologies as open source software. Babuschkin envisions future
AI systems as trained to benefit individuals rather than centralized
and controlled by one big corporation.

 ELON MUSK'S NEW TEXAS FACTORY WILL BE THE LARGEST BUILDING ON EARTH,
IF ALL GOES ACCORDING TO PLAN (3 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Elon Musk's Terafab in Grimes County, Texas, is slated to top 100
million square feet. The factory will function as a joint supply chain
for SpaceX and Tesla. It will combine logic, memory, and advanced
packaging under one roof. Construction is projected to cost $16.8
billion. Eight tax incentive applications have been filed on behalf of
the project, asking for tax breaks so the project has a more favorable
after-tax return.
