SCIENCE & FUTURISTIC TECHNOLOGY

 OPENAI'S ‘SHEPHERDS' AFFIRM BLOCKBUSTER PHYSICS RESULT (3 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 Experts appointed by OpenAI have verified OpenAI's proof of the
existence of the quantum Yang-Mills theory, a vital foundation for the
Standard Model of particle physics. OpenAI created a committee to
supervise the proof's release after backlash it received when it
released a flood of more than 700 AI-generated proofs on a single day
in October. This shepherding approach has riled some mathematicians,
who see it as an act of needless gatekeeping. The experts do not claim
to fully understand the proof yet, as it is too lengthy and
complicated for human minds to immediately grasp.

 HANDING OVER HUMANITY (22 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Some AI experts believe that once we achieve superintelligence,
humans will be permanently disempowered, and we shall all be ruled by
an AI god. This might not be such a desirable outcome, as humans have
a natural desire for freedom. The arrangement will probably not work,
as keeping power responsive to human needs has required checks
developed over centuries, sustained by repeated feedback from the real
world and correction, and there's no reason to keep that equilibrium
stable once humans have surrendered all control. Humans have the
ability now to steer the technology in a way that will not lead to a
complete replacement of human judgment.

 SUBAGENTS CONSIDERED HARMFUL? (11 MINUTE READ) [11]

 There are several ways to delegate to subagents, and they all fail in
different ways. This post covers the different approaches and why
teaching agents to choose between them is difficult. Forking beats
summarization in a lot of cases, but it can get expensive as context
grows. The post presents a solution where a fork keeps the parts of a
conversation it needs and drops the rest.
