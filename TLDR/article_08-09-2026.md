 EARLY DATA INDICATES AN AI-GENERATED DRUG COULD SLOW AGING (2 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Rentosertib is a drug created with the assistance of AI. The AI
originally indicated that the drug could help treat patients suffering
from a chronic lung disease, but data from clinical trials show it
could also slow the aging process. The drug reduced the biological
markers of age as measured by six 'aging clocks', a different kind of
AI technology designed to predict a person's morbidity and mortality.
While the drug shows promise, it is still years away from regulatory
approval, even for use in sick patients.

 HOW WELL DO AGENTS USE TEST/VERIFICATION TECHNIQUES? (68 MINUTE READ)
[14]

 It's easier than ever to hit a particular quality bar by having
coding agents use effective test techniques. However, software quality
seems to be getting worse. This indicates that whatever defaults
developers are using might not work very well. This study tests
whether simple instructions to agents to use particular techniques or
libraries improve implementation correctness. It found that agents
tend to either just write the tests they would normally write, but
inside a framework for a different type of test technique, or they'll
use a technique superficially but not really do the things that get
the value out of the technique.

 THE YEAR FINDING AND EXPLOITING BUGS BECAME CHEAP, AND WHAT TO DO
ABOUT IT (13 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Over the next few years, offensive capability will become abundant.
More people will be able to inspect every valuable project and exploit
vulnerabilities. Handling critical bugs in a timely manner will become
the most challenging and important task for defenders. Having good
security infrastructure and integration will be a must. AI-assisted
formalization will lower some of the cost, but writing the right
specification and connecting proofs to production code will remain
challenging.

 FOUR QUESTIONS ABOUT AGI (28 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), as the term is commonly used,
points to nothing really tangible or concrete and points us at the
wrong thing. Recent announcements about AGI are just companies saying
they've nearly arrived at a destination they defined themselves.
Instead of trying to define AGI, the industry should focus on making a
long series of specific things reliable enough to trust, and let the
destination take care of itself.
