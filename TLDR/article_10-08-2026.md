BIG TECH & STARTUPS

 APPLE EXPLORES SHAKING UP ITS SMARTWATCH FOR A NEW ERA (13 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 The Apple Watch continues to attract new buyers and remains the
top-selling smartwatch. However, the company's industrial design team
has been exploring a broad rethink of Apple's smartwatches and is
evaluating several different directions. Apple needs to adapt to the
rise of screenless fitness bands and smart rings, which are cheaper,
lighter, and more fun and convenient to use for many people. The
company aims to inject new life into its Wearables, Home, and
Accessories segment, which has struggled to generate meaningful growth
in recent years.

SCIENCE & FUTURISTIC TECHNOLOGY

 THE FIRST SELF-DRIVING VEHICLE ON MARS HAS PROVEN TO BE A SMASHING
SUCCESS (4 MINUTE READ) [9]

 NASA's Perseverance Mars rover will set a record for the most
distance driven by any vehicle on another world sometime next week.
The rover's auto navigation system has enabled about 90% of the
distance driven by the vehicle to be autonomous. Not having to wait
for navigation commands from drivers on Earth has allowed the rover to
maximize the amount of scientific return. The vehicle will likely be
able to keep driving itself across Mars for many years to come.

 CHINA-FREE BATTERIES MADE FROM SALT ARE FINALLY HERE (8 MINUTE READ)
[10]

 Peak Energy, a US-based startup, has raised tens of millions of
dollars to build a factory for its sodium-ion battery packs. It
already has more than $1.1 billion in announced deals with customers.
Sodium batteries have less-intensive cooling needs than lithium-ion
packs, and they are less likely to catch fire. Another US-based
startup, Inlyte Energy, is working on another type of sodium-based
battery that can't catch fire at all.

PROGRAMMING, DESIGN & DATA SCIENCE

 AGENTIC CODE QUALITY (11 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Software quality now depends on the constraints set around the agents
that wrote it. Constraints define what a system is allowed to do.
Setting and maintaining these constraints allows developers to build
loops that reliably deliver high-quality production software. Agents
can propose anything - constraints decide whether a proposal is safe
enough, correct, scoped, and useful.

 GOOGLE'S WESTINGHOUSE BET (9 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Google's recent reshuffling of its AI operations has many thinking
that the company has failed in the AI race. However, the company may
just be refocusing its resources on its strengths. Google Cloud may be
a much larger economic opportunity than pursuing its rivals in the
frontier AI race. The company's enormous distribution advantage means
it doesn't have to compete in the expensive and unprofitable frontier
race.

MISCELLANEOUS

 AMAZON CRACKS DOWN ON 'CPU WASTE' AMONG ENGINEERS AS AGENTIC AI
CRUNCH INTENSIFIES (5 MINUTE READ) [13]

 AWS is cracking down on internal use of EC2 instances among its
engineers. Engineers have been told to reduce CPU waste to ensure the
company has enough CPU capacity to meet customer demand. The increased
demand for CPU is due to the rise of AI agentic workloads. Other cloud
companies are also seeing high demand for CPUs.

 CHANGING DEVTOOLS IS CHEAP. OWNING THEM ISN'T (8 MINUTE READ) [14]

 The future of software will be powered by high-quality,
well-documented building blocks that users can build specialized
artifacts on top of. Agents are good at composing high-quality
components. Maintainers can provide these components alongside a focus
application, and users can choose how to modify their experiences
themselves. This allows software to be personalized while still being
developed by maintainers that care.
