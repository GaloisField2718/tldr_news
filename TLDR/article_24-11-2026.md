 TRUMP SEALED DEAL FOR NVIDIA TO REHIRE JENSEN HUANG AS CEO (4 MINUTE
READ) [4]

 President Trump made a personal promise that Jensen Huang had not
broken national security laws. The sharp stock price drop after his
resignation and questions about the leadership of the interim CEO also
spurred the board's decision. Nvidia's newly appointed board chair had
previously stated that the board did not intend to reinstate Huang as
CEO. Trump believes his administration's close working relationship
with Huang gives the US its best opportunity to protect national
security and prevent cutting-edge AI chips from reaching banned
customers.

 OPENAI STAFFER LIKELY CAPTURED SECRETS ON HIDDEN BUG, US SAYS (3
MINUTE READ) [5]

 Abdulrahman Alfozan, a former engineer at OpenAI, has pleaded not
guilty to an eavesdropping charge for bugging an office conference
room, likely to intercept trade secrets. He has requested bail, but
there are concerns that he may try to flee before trial given his
relatively weak ties to the US and the considerable resources and
connections he has to Saudi Arabia. The government has so far located
hundreds of audio files on Alfozan's computer, many of which involve
discussions of what may be trade secrets. He appears to have uploaded
at least some of the recordings to Dropbox.

 TESLA FSD V15 IS AI4'S LAST SHOT AS MUSK REVEALS HOW LITTLE COMPUTE
IS LEFT (5 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Tesla's FSD v15 is the company's last real shot at delivering
unsupervised self-driving on the AI4 computer. The next-gen A15 chip
will not be coming to cars, and the mid-cycle AI4.5 upgrade had its
RAM cut from 32GB to 24GB. The mid-cycle refresh exists mainly because
Tesla needs a new memory type it can actually buy. The cost of the AI4
chip is now twice as much due to memory and storage price hikes.

 THE INCREDIBLE, ELEGANT DNA REPAIR SYSTEM MAKES GENE EDITING WORK (12
MINUTE READ) [7]

 Cells have a native repair mechanism for DNA that recognizes and
replaces faulty DNA before lasting damage or dysfunction can set in.
This ability evolved essentially at the same time as DNA, as it is
impossible to have stable genetic information without DNA repair. The
mechanisms are essential to the evolutionary lineage of all known
life. Scientists and doctors experimenting with gene editing use these
natural self-editing and repair tools to insert, delete, or mutate
genes to study and treat disease.

 LADYBIRD WEB BROWSER/ENGINE REACHES ALPHA (2 MINUTE READ) [15]

 The Ladybird open source desktop web browser was built from scratch
with its own independent web engine. After four years of dedicated
development, it has now entered its alpha phase. Usability is still
modest, but the browser is functional. Some websites will still crash.
The alpha build is available for macOS and Linux.
