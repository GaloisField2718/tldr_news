BIG TECH & STARTUPS

 GOOGLE'S AI RESHUFFLE: CHIEF SCIENTIST JEFF DEAN EXITS AND DEMIS
HASSABIS STEPS DOWN AS DEEPMIND CEO (4 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Google's chief scientist, Jeff Dean, is leaving the company after 27
years. Demis Hassabis, the CEO of Google DeepMind, is moving into a
chairman role of that unit and also assuming the title of chief
scientist at parent company Alphabet. Dean is starting his own
company. The departure is on friendly terms, and Google will invest in
his startup, which will aim to accelerate discoveries in machine
learning, science, and engineering.

 FOUR TOP GOOGLE AI RESEARCHERS FORM NEW START-UP (7 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Jeff Dean, Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le, and Oriol Vinyals have left
Google to start a company called Discovery Loop. The team aims to
build AI that can improve itself with little or no help from humans.
Google plans to collaborate with the new startup and has agreed to
provide the computing power it needs to build its AI technologies for
at least the next year. Discovery Loop will operate as a public
benefit corporation.

 WHY I'M LEAVING OPENAI TO BUILD TELEPATHY (15 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Conduit is a startup building thought-to-text models trained on
non-invasive neural data. Developing thought-to-text technology would
allow humans to talk to AI with their thoughts, allowing AI to become
an extension of them. To do this, the startup needs to scale up its
data collection and develop special hardware.

PROGRAMMING, DESIGN & DATA SCIENCE

 CLOUDFLARE OS (GITHUB REPO) [8]

 Cloudflare OS is an operating system for AI productivity. It features
an agent chat UI where users can ask agents to do tasks, sandboxed
application development, and a security framework that applies
guardrails to both agents and apps. Developed to be used internally at
Cloudflare, Cloudflare OS is now open source so others can copy it and
customize it for their own companies. The basic user experience of
Cloudflare OS is something like an online office suite, but each file
is potentially its own custom application written by AI to serve
exactly your needs.

 WHAT ARE CODE REVIEWS EVEN FOR? (11 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Code review is how teams build a shared understanding of a system.
Automating the process removes that shared understanding. AI
accelerates cognitive and intent debt - debts that don't appear on a
dashboard. By the time anyone realizes, months later, recovering that
understanding will be far more expensive than preserving it would have
been.
