 HUANG APOLOGIZES TO NVIDIA STAFF, SAYS HE'S IN DAILY THERAPY (3
MINUTE READ) [5]

 Jensen Huang said to staff during an all-hands meeting that he has
been in daily therapy to address the behavior that led to his brief
ouster as CEO of Nvidia. He insists that he wasn't reinstated because
he is a 'tech bro' who's above the law. Nvidia's board will hold him
to account going forward. He acknowledges violating Nvidia's code of
conduct and says he is grateful to have a second chance to lead the
company he co-founded three decades ago.

 XAI FOUNDERS UNVEIL NEW AI STARTUP TO PREDICT FUTURE EVENTS (2 MINUTE
READ) [6]

 Epochal, a startup founded by three former xAI founders, has
developed an initial product that lets AI agents browse an archived
version of the internet as it existed on specific dates. It is
designed to simulate the past to teach AI to predict the future. The
company's goal is to create an AI model that can help with
decision-making through superhuman prediction.

SCIENCE & FUTURISTIC TECHNOLOGY

 I'VE BEEN DRINKING WINE IN MY KITCHEN (3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 AI has been solving long-standing problems in mathematics at an
increasing rate. These proofs come with formal certifications that
prove their correctness to a level beyond what would normally be
demanded for a math paper published in a journal. Many people will try
to deny that these proofs are valid, but experts agree there aren't
any real technical issues with the proofs. The phenomenon is causing
some mathematicians to rethink their lives.

 THE MOST FAMOUS PROBLEM IN MATH WAS JUST SOLVED BY AI—AND THIS
HUMAN (10 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Levent Alpöge graduated from Harvard with the highest GPA in his
class, won the prize for America's top young math researcher, and
earned his Ph.D. in math from Princeton before he took a job at
Anthropic. He recently stepped into the public limelight due to his
claim of having solved the Riemann Hypothesis with the help of AI. His
17-million-line-long proof has already been verified by two
independent groups. If it holds up to further scrutiny, Alpöge will
become one of the most prominent mathematicians in history.

PROGRAMMING, DESIGN & DATA SCIENCE

 AGENTIC CODING ON AN OPEN-SOURCE PROJECT (35 MINUTE READ) [9]

 LLM usage in the developer community has evolved drastically over the
past year. Models' progress has been so impressive that developers are
now delegating all of the coding to LLM agents. This article discusses
how to deal with all of the aspects of agentic coding: prompting,
context management, skills, reviews, and orchestration. It also shares
a process for performing large QA operations, running data migration,
and writing entirely new software.

 NIGHTMARE MODE: SIMULATING DISK FAILURES (20 MINUTE READ) [10]

 This post looks at the disk faults that a 'nightmare mode' fault
injector needs to be able to emulate. Disk fault injection can uncover
a number of bugs in customer software. Antithesis' disk fault injector
covers power failures, torn writes and torn sequences, disk I/O
errors, and the latency faults that shake out concurrency bugs. It is
customizable, so users don't have to turn everything on.
