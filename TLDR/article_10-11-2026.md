 EXPERIMENTAL MRNA THERAPY PROMOTES REPAIR AFTER HEART ATTACK DAMAGE
(3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Researchers have developed a treatment that uses mRNA therapy to
reawaken heart muscle cells so that they start to divide and help
repair damaged muscle. The treatment helped mice produce stronger
heartbeats following a heart attack. While not a full cure for heart
disease, even small improvements to heart function have been linked to
lower mortality. The treatment is short-lived and localized, and is
not expected to cause any permanent modification to the heart cells.
Further trials in larger animal models are required before human
testing can begin.
