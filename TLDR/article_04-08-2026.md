BIG TECH & STARTUPS

 SPACEX'S BUMPY RIDE ON THE STOCK MARKET MAY GET BUMPIER (6 MINUTE
READ) [5]

 The first lockup on SpaceX shares is set to expire today. Employees
and other company insiders who were prevented from trading stock after
SpaceX's IPO will now be able to do so. Lockups prevent shares from
flooding the market and driving down their price after an IPO. More
than double the current supply that can be traded will be made
available, which may further depress the price.

 SPACEX IS SET TO ACQUIRE 130,000 ACRES OF MARSHLAND IN SOUTHERN
LOUISIANA (4 MINUTE READ) [6]

 SpaceX is close to finalizing a deal to acquire about 130,000 acres
along the northern coast of the Gulf of Mexico. The deal is expected
to be officially announced later this month. The site, known as Pecan
Island, is an 18-mile stretch of marshland. It became available as
part of a legal settlement that resolved dozens of lawsuits blaming
ExxonMobil for pollution and coastal land loss.

SCIENCE & FUTURISTIC TECHNOLOGY

 BASE POWER RAISES $1B TO ROLL OUT ITS GIANT NEW HOME BATTERY (3
MINUTE READ) [7]

 Base Power's Core is a 39.2-kWh home battery that can keep a house
running for up to 36 hours. Core, which can be installed in under an
hour, can be connected to solar and has a built-in port for recharging
from a portable generator. The lithium iron phosphate battery is
designed to operate between -22F and 122F and has an IP67 rating for
submersion in up to 3 feet of water. Core will only be available as
part of a subscription service, and the company will retain ownership
of the battery.

 DRUG DISCOVERY HAS NO MAGIC WANDS (18 MINUTE READ) [8]

 AI is set to transform human health. However, it will not be a magic
wand: we don't understand human biology well enough for a clever
enough reasoner to find the cures hidden in what we know. Aimed at
biology we barely understand, AI will mostly help us generate failures
faster. The path to real cures is to measure biology in the right way
and use AI to derive novel insights from those measurements.

 THE ENDGAME OF VERTICAL INTEGRATION (8 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Model labs and agent labs are converging. Model labs continue to
develop first-party applications that compete with their customers,
while agent labs are starting to train models. The gains from
co-designing models with harnesses is why agent labs need to move into
model training. The challenge is for agent labs to offer the best
intelligence per dollar at margins that the model labs can't match.

 WHAT ARE COMPANIES GETTING FOR ALL THAT AI SPENDING? (9 MINUTE READ)
[13]

 The cost and benefits of AI are difficult to weigh. There are now
thousands of models, and there is no consensus around how much energy
a token requires, which model is best to perform a given task, or how
many tokens it should take. The Linux Foundation established the
Tokenomics Foundation in June to create common parameters for AI
providers to disclose. Being able to measure the costs and benefits of
AI will help researchers understand how AI is going to change
everything.
