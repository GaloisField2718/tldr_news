 MAJOR INVESTORS ARE SKEPTICAL ANTHROPIC IPO CAN ACHIEVE $1.5 TRILLION
VALUATION (7 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Major investors planning to participate in Anthropic's IPO, expected
to launch the week before Thanksgiving, say the expected $1.5 trillion
valuation will likely need to come down. The AI lab was expected to
raise up to $100 billion, but is now predicted to raise around $80
billion, leaving it with a valuation closer to $1 trillion. Investors
have revolted against AI bets this week as the midterm elections have
put several AI skeptics in power, potentially slowing data center
development in the US. Anthropic has avoided committing to specific
plans for its IPO to maintain optionality.

 BLUE ORIGIN SAYS IT IS BUILDING A HIGHER-PERFORMING ROCKET ENGINE AT
1/4 THE COST (2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Blue Origin has announced two significant upgrades to its New Glenn
rocket that reduce the cost of manufacturing the vehicle and expand
its capacity for payload delivery. The company aims to eventually
launch the vehicle more than 1,000 times a year. It has started test
firing a new engine that will eventually replace the BE-4 engine that
powers the first stage of New Glenn. The company is also developing a
hydrogen-oxygen third stage for the rocket to give it an extra kick.

 THEY CREATED A CELL THAT'S ALMOST ALIVE. WHAT TO DO WITH IT NOW? (8
MINUTE READ) [8]

 A SpudCell is an artificial cell created from lifeless ingredients
that can grow, copy its DNA, divide in two, and start its cell cycle
all over again. It is still far from a self-sustaining cell that any
scientist could manufacture from prepared ingredients, but the
technology could eventually make it easier to produce medicines,
foods, and other items that could not otherwise be produced with
ordinary biology. The researchers working on SpudCell hope that it
will eventually be a platform for biotechnology that will allow a
simple cell to be engineered in scores of useful ways. SpudCell could
potentially be misused, so its researchers are taking security
seriously from the start - its DNA and proteins are engineered so that
it can only survive on an exotic diet of chemicals not found in
nature.

 INSIDE GITHUB'S NEW GIT STORAGE ARCHITECTURE (21 MINUTE READ) [10]

 GitHub's new Git storage architecture is capable of handling

 MEET *SSHOLE, THE VULNERABILITY THAT LETS HACKERS SSH INTO LINUX
DEVICES (3 MINUTE READ) [11]

 *sshole is a vulnerability in Linux that allows attackers to log in
via SSH without knowing the password. The vulnerability exists in
systems using OpenSSH with musl without pluggable authentication
modules. Despite the prerequisites, the vulnerabilities likely
threaten a large number of systems. It is still unclear where the
vulnerability resides and what other packages may be vulnerable.

 THE HACKINTOSH REBORN... WITH AI (3 MINUTE READ) [13]

 A 'Hackintosh' is a non-Apple computer running macOS. The Hackintosh
community started fading away after Apple released its first ARM-based
Mac and discontinued support for x86 versions of macOS. However, the
rise of AI has revived activity in the space, and there has been an
explosion in development, activity, and success in the past few weeks.
This post lists a few recent developments, including macOS 27 running
on a Snapdragon laptop, iOS 17 running on the Nintendo Switch OLED,
and macOS 27.2 Beta on an ARM SBC RK3588 with Mali drivers.
