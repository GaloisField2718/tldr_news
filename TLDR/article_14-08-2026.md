[6]

 OpenAI's UltraOpenAI's Ultrafast API service tier can run GPT-5.6 Sol
at 750 output tokens per second, 14 times the speed of Standard. The
service is designed for products and workflows in which delays can
determine whether an answer remains useful. Access is initially

wider rollout is planned as capacity grows.

 X OPEN SOURCES ITS RANKING ALGORITHM, LETTING USERS SEE IF THEY'VE
BEEN ‘SHADOWBANNED' (5 MINUTE READ) [7]

 X has open-sourced its 'For You' algorithm and its core ranking
engine under the Apache v2 license. The company has also expanded its
previous efforts to open source parts of its codebase to add more
detail, making it roughly 10 to 15 times larger than it was before. X
is providing tools that will let users see for themselves if and how
its ranking systems have impacted their account or posts. The tool
will be available to a test group of accounts for at least a year as a
pilot before rolling out more broadly.

 DRIVER SETS RECORD FOR HYDROGEN-POWERED VEHICLE AT MORE THAN 406 MPH
(4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Andy Green set a record for the fastest land speed driving a JCB
Hydromax on Tuesday at 406.320 miles per hour. The hydrogen-fueled
internal-combustion vehicle took about 72 seconds to get from 50 miles
per hour to 400. At 64, Green is the only person to break the sound
barrier on land, driving a jet-powered car at an average speed of
763.035 miles per hour in 1997. The JCB Hydromax was not built to
rival that record, but to set a new fastest speed for a hydrogen
vehicle - the previous record for a hydrogen combustion vehicle was
185.5 miles per hour, set in 2004 by the BMW H2R.

 FOREMAN (GITHUB REPO) [13]

 Foreman is an eve software factory that puts AI agents on every stage
of the development loop and keeps people on the judgment calls. It
takes tasks from GitHub and Linear, moves each one through four
stations - Classifier, Analyst, Implementer, and Reviewer - and
delivers a reviewed draft pull request on the repository. The
Classifier triages the task, the Analyst turns it into a plan with
acceptance criteria, the Implementer executes the plan in its own
sandbox, and the Reviewer independently judges everything against the
real diff. Developers just review, mark ready, and merge.
