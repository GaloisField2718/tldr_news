 THE ANTITHESIS PRINCIPLE (3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Every insight about human behavior has two sides: a tactic for
influencing others and a bias to resist yourself. Smart people see the
tactic, while wise people also overcome the bias.

 STRIPE AND SIERRA BUILT THEIR OWN CODING AGENT SYSTEMS. YOU PROBABLY
DON'T NEED TO (4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Most companies should buy coding-agent infrastructure and focus their
resources on the context and workflows that make it uniquely valuable.
Building internally is worthwhile only when the technology is
strategically essential, and the organization can support it as a
permanent product.

 OVER A QUARTER OF IT LEADERS FACE AN AI DATA BOTTLENECK (SPONSOR)
[11]

 Right now, 25% of companies leverage barely 10% of their data for AI
initiatives. But a major shift is on the horizon. The State of Search
& AI survey [11] of 330+ IT leaders reveals that only 5% expect to
stay at this low threshold in six months. As organizations prepare to
unlock their broader datasets, the race for smarter AI is officially
on. See how your company compares [11]

 HOW TO RUN CUSTOMER INTERVIEWS FOR A CAPABILITY YOUR USERS HAVE NEVER
EXPERIENCED (4 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Customer research for novel AI products should focus on familiar
needs rather than hypothetical interest. Study current workarounds,
create a realistic experience, and examine the decisions and outcomes
the capability could improve.

 CLAUDE OPUS 5 REVIEW + BROWSER USE IN CODEX + HOW CURSOR AND A
RASPBERRY PI MAKES AI FUN (27 MINUTE VIDEO) [13]

 Browser and computer-use agents can handle tedious digital work, from
exhaustive QA and persona testing to inbox management, shopping, and
remote device setup. They perform best when given room to plan,
matched to the right model and effort level, and paired with brief
human oversight for verification and final decisions.

 DESIGN DOESN'T HAVE A FINISH LINE (4 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Design should continue throughout development because the hardest
decisions often emerge only after building begins. Keeping designers
in the delivery loop helps teams catch mistakes early and avoid
efficiently shipping the wrong product.

 SUBSTACK ASKED FOR VOLUME, THEN SHIPPED A SCANNER (9 MINUTE READ)
[15]

 Substack incentivized frequent posting, then introduced an AI
detector to police the resulting content. This shifts the burden of
verification onto users rather than addressing the platform's growth
mechanics.
