 BUY VS. BUILD IS NOW ALSO A UX DECISION, ESPECIALLY IN ENTERPRISE (12
MINUTE READ) [7]

 In enterprise product development, choosing between buying tools and
building them internally has become a user experience decision driven
by AI. While coding agents reduce initial build costs, ongoing
maintenance, data silos, model retirement, and user context switches
introduce hidden complexities that product teams must manage.

OPINIONS & TUTORIALS

 PRODUCT QUALITY (10 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Product quality involves setting and evaluating against a quality bar
across a product's lifecycle. Often overlooked in favor of new
features, poor quality risks sudden or gradual customer churn.
Maintaining quality is even harder in AI products due to model drift
and non-deterministic interfaces.

 DON'T ASK AI TO WRITE THE USER STORY YET (4 MINUTE READ) [9]

 AI can make vague requirements look complete by quietly inventing
business rules. Instead, use it to identify assumptions and unanswered
questions and resolve key decisions with stakeholders, and only then
draft acceptance criteria.

 THREE WAYS TO INCREASE FEATURE ADOPTION (11 MINUTE PODCAST) [12]

 Product teams can boost feature adoption by applying behavioral
science strategies: requiring features through paternalism, adding
costs to declining, and reducing perceived setup friction. Timing
feature requests to match user intent within a workflow significantly
increases success.

MISCELLANEOUS

 THE SAME OLD BS (6 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Product teams frequently trap themselves in featurism and outdated
backlogs by focusing on speed rather than a clear direction. Teams
should start with a clear destination, delete stale backlog items
untouched for over 90 days, and prioritize what is necessary over what
is easy.

 DAVID EPSTEIN ON WHY CONSTRAINTS BEAT GOALS (6 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Setting tight constraints is more effective than focusing on broad,
outcome-based goals when the path forward is unclear. Constraints
reduce overwhelming choices, prevent scope creep, block default
thinking, and help teams solve complex problems.
