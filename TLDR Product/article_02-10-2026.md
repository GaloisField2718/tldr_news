TLDR PRODUCT MANAGEMENT 2026-10-02

 STATE OF PRODUCT REVEALS WHAT AI STILL HASN'T SOLVED (SPONSOR) [4]

 86% of product professionals say deciding what to build matters more
as AI makes shipping easier. Customer attention is still limited, and
every new feature brings maintenance, making prioritization more
consequential. Atlassian's _State of Product 2027_ [5] explores how
expanding capabilities are changing product roles and putting greater
emphasis on judgment and direction.

 AI makes products easier to build, but not easier to validate. Lean
LaunchPad is shifting its focus from building MVPs to deeply
understanding customers, testing assumptions, and proving demand with
real design partners.

 BUILDING ALONE, FASTER: A PANEL ON WHAT AI IS DOING TO THE PRODUCT
TRIO (12 MINUTE READ) [9]

 AI helps product, design, and engineering move faster, but solo AI
work can create debt, burnout, and fragmented teams. The real
opportunity is using AI with shared guardrails so specialists can move
faster together.

 PRODUCT MANAGEMENT IS NOT OPTIONAL (8 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Reports of the death of product management are exaggerated. While AI
is shifting the role away from administrative work, strategy,
judgment, and customer understanding remain essential.

 65% OF TEAMS HAVE SHIPPED THE WRONG FIX (SPONSOR) [11]

 You ship the fix, the problem sticks around, and it turns out the
root cause was somewhere else entirely. New FULLSTORY [11] research
finds 65% of teams have been there, and 52% still rely on customer
complaints to tell them something's broken. See how teams are tracing
friction to the source and fixing the right thing the first time.

 HOW TO PERSONALIZE USER ONBOARDING WITHOUT INTRODUCING FRICTION (9
MINUTE READ) [13]

 SaaS onboarding surveys often add friction without personalizing the
experience. To succeed, keep surveys short, deliver immediate value,
infer user data, separate attribution questions, and avoid closed
survey loops.

 USER SATISFACTION DEPENDS ON DELIVERED QUALITY RELATIVE TO
EXPECTATIONS (20 MINUTE READ) [14]

 User satisfaction depends on delivered quality relative to prior
expectations. Managing the expectation gap is a key design challenge.
