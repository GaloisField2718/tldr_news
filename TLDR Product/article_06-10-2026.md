NEWS & TRENDS

 BUILDING IS CHEAP. OWNING IT IS NOT (10 MINUTE READ) [7]

 While modern tools and AI make building software products cheaper,
the cost of ownership and making strategic choices remains high.
Dedicated PMs add value by focusing on business viability, ranking
roadmap bets, tracking clear predictions, and acting as communication
bridges and quality gatekeepers for their teams.

 THE FUTURE OF KNOWLEDGE INSIDE THE ENTERPRISE (10 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Enterprise knowledge management creates business value by connecting
organizational experience to AI agents acting under human
accountability. Establishing a context layer over existing systems
extends expertise, improves efficiency, enhances verification, and
strengthens continuity through a structured operating model.

OPINIONS & TUTORIALS

 PRODUCT ETHICS IN THE AGE OF AI (13 MINUTE PODCAST) [9]

 Product ethics in the age of artificial intelligence must become a
core success factor alongside desirability, feasibility, and
viability, rather than remaining an afterthought. This podcast episode
explains how to integrate ethical considerations into the innovation
process, factor them into strategy updates, and measure a product's
ethical impact.

 BEFORE YOU BUILD, ASK YOURSELF: WHO IS YOUR BULLSEYE CUSTOMER? (4
MINUTE READ) [10]

 Early-stage product teams should define a one-sentence Bullseye
Customer hypothesis before building. Specifying the target customer,
trigger, and outcome surfaces hidden team disagreements and improves
user research.

RESOURCES & TOOLS

 A NEW KIND OF AI MODEL: JEV AND QUICK WINS FOR PMS (6 MINUTE READ)
[11]

 TypeSafe AI's Jev is a decision model that outputs probabilities
rather than text. Operating at low cost and high speed, it supports
binary questions, multiple-choice selections, and scoring.

 A MANIFESTO ON HOW A MODERN RESEARCH TEAM SHOULD WORK (16 MINUTE
READ) [12]

 Modern UX research teams fail when researchers work in isolation. To
fix this, teams should adopt shared goals, conduct weekly peer
critiques, maintain a central user research record, and enforce clear
rules for study requests.

MISCELLANEOUS

 USERS DON'T HATE CHANGE. THEY HATE YOU (6 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Users do not inherently hate change - they resent updates that
destroy their familiarity and control without clear benefit. Citing
psychological research on loss aversion, the article explains that
users weigh losses in comfort far more than potential gains,
criticizing tech companies for forcing redesigns without explaining
their value.

 THE FAKE DOUBLE DIAMOND IS DEAD. THE REAL DOUBLE DIAMOND IS IMMORTAL
(6 MINUTE READ) [14]

 The product development dichotomy between upfront definition and
continuous iteration is a false choice. True innovation requires
balancing upfront discovery with ongoing observation within a cohesive
system. Relying on artificial shortcuts or AI-driven alignment
ultimately degrades software quality and prevents teams from solving
complex problems.

 A co-founder of Socratic reflects on their startup's acquisition by

 10 THINGS OPENAI'S TIBO THINKS BUILDERS ARE UNDERESTIMATING ABOUT AI

 Tibo Sottiaux highlights upcoming shifts in AI, emphasizing that
product teams must build for faster, cheaper, and more capable models
while focusing on user understanding and taste.
