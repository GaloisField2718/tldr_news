 THE COMING PRODUCT RETENTION CRISIS (4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 AI excels at routine work but struggles to hold consumer attention,
which depends on novelty and authenticity. As apps become cheaper to
build, they will resemble disposable content, shifting success from
retaining users to producing fresh hits and building lasting brands.

 PRODUCT STRATEGY STARTS WITH WHAT YOU WILL NOT BUILD (15 MINUTE READ)
[15]

 Product strategy is defined as much by what you refuse to build as by
what you pursue. Make a few clear bets, name the sacrifices behind
them, and use those choices to determine what earns a place on the
roadmap.

treat distribution and willingness to pay as seriously as the product
itself.
