 This article explores multi-step paywalls for subscription apps,

single-screen options in conversion, outlines key design patterns, and

 BLAST OPEN THE BOTTLENECK BETWEEN YOUR AI IDEAS AND PRODUCTION (9
MINUTE READ) [8]

 While AI has lowered the cost of generating ideas, expert judgment to
safely validate and deploy them has become the main bottleneck.
Enterprises must shift focus to trust engineering and validation
frameworks. Otherwise, they will realize minimal value from their
agentic AI investments.

 STOP ASKING USERS WHAT THEY WANT (7 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Product engineers should avoid asking users what features they want,
focusing instead on uncovering underlying problems through targeted
interviews. Drawing on user research, this article details questioning
techniques to understand user context, actual behavior, core
motivations, and the true value of features.

 EVALS FOR PRODUCT MANAGERS: WRITE THE BAR, THEN THE PRODUCT (20
MINUTE READ) [12]

 For AI products, product managers should use structured evals as
primary specifications instead of vague requirements. This approach
involves defining quality standards, establishing clear pass-or-fail
criteria, setting numerical launch bars before reviewing scores, and
maintaining a human-in-the-loop evaluation process.

 FROM PROBLEM TO FEATURE: SCOPING WORK (10 MINUTE READ) [13]

 This guide outlines a practical approach to scoping software
development by breaking problems into independent, valuable features
rather than monolithic tasks. It explains how to define business
problems, write implementation-independent acceptance criteria, and
handle edge cases like technical debt and estimation.
