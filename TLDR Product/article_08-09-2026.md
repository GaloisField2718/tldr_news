 SLOP-CREEP: WHEN BUILDING GETS CHEAPER THAN THINKING (7 MINUTE READ)
[9]

 AI agents have created "slop-creep" - the accumulation of unnecessary
features, abstractions, and infrastructure built simply because they
are cheap to produce. Without implementation friction, weak ideas
survive longer, especially in large companies where software persists.
