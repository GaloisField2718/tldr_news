AI is not valuable enough to justify what it costs to deliver. Usage
grows, costs scale, and margins start disappearing.

work with teams shipping AI at Atlassian, Bolt, Salesforce, Cursor,

 A GOOD MOVE IS GROUNDED (4 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Effective product work connects internal changes directly to external
user activity. Validate initiatives like MVPs against specific
hypotheses and behavioral goals. If a clear link is missing, use small
probes or targeted adjustments to bridge the gap.

 20 UNFILTERED OPERATING TAKES (7 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Great product teams win through strong habits, small iterations, and
continuous learning, not rigid frameworks. AI will amplify whatever
operating system a team already has, good or bad.
