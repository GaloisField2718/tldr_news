NEWS & TRENDS

 WHY PMS ARE SUDDENLY VALUABLE AGAIN (58 MINUTE VIDEO) [6]

 Gemini Head of Product Tulsee Doshi explains that model development
is unpredictable, comparing it to potion-making where PMs guide models
by understanding their "vibes." She also outlines Google's AI
strategy: winning frontier conversations with models like Gemini 4
Argon while serving diverse cost and quality needs across a Pareto
curve.

 THE MVP WAS A HACK AROUND A CONSTRAINT THAT NO LONGER EXISTS (6
MINUTE READ) [7]

 The traditional Minimum Viable Product approach is obsolete because
modern engineering tools have drastically lowered software development
costs. Teams should instead ship a maximum viable product that solves
a customer's complete problem, shifting the primary growth bottleneck
from engineering capacity to customer understanding and adoption.

OPINIONS & TUTORIALS

 AI PRODUCT MANAGEMENT AT SPOTIFY: A VIEW FROM THREE ANGLES (12 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Spotify engineering leaders examine how AI product management varies
across technical research, foundational models, and user-facing
experiences. They highlight how evaluation metrics, feedback loops,
and customer definitions shift depending on the problem space.

 PRIORITIZE PRODUCT FEEDBACK BY REVENUE AT RISK (18 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Bellwether is a revenue-weighted feedback engine for B2B SaaS that
combines support, sales, and NPS signals with CRM account revenue.
Weighting themes by ARR and renewal proximity rather than raw count
inverts roadmaps to protect major enterprise accounts.

RESOURCES & TOOLS

 THE GREATEST NOTETAKING APP IS A PEN (SPONSOR) [10]

 Neuroscience has proven that handwriting beats typing for creativity,
critical thinking, and memory. Goodnotes [10] is the notetaking app
for product managers that prefer a tablet to a Google Doc - create
diagrams, plan release timelines, or mockup UI. Write and draw by
hand, then search it like typed text. Try Goodnotes for free. [10]

 HOW TO TURN 1000 PULL REQUESTS A DAY INTO THE FEW YOUR CUSTOMERS NEED
(5 MINUTE READ) [11]

 AI-driven development tools have dramatically accelerated feature
production, overwhelming teams with pull requests that may lack
customer value. To manage this volume, leaders should democratize
strategic clarity, set evidence-based deployment gates, mandate clear
hypotheses, and measure outcomes over raw output.

 HOW TO TALK TO EVERY ONE OF YOUR CUSTOMERS WITH AI (36 MINUTE READ)
[12]

 Product managers can use AI tools like Claude Code for proactive,
automated email customer research at scale. Integrating customer data
with automated outreach allows teams to run thousands of continuous
conversations, capturing insights and boosting conversion rates.

MISCELLANEOUS

 ACCELERATING PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT IN THE AI AGE: HOW FAIRE DEVELOPED
PRODUCT BUILDERS (7 MINUTE READ) [13]

 To resolve bottlenecks from increased AI coding output, software
company Faire created a product builder program to transition
engineers into end-to-end product owners. Expanding engineers' product
and design skills reduced handoffs, leading to faster feature delivery
and improved customer outcomes.

 ENTER THE GAME (5 MINUTE READ) [14]

 This article presents a framework for product strategy based on three
structures (the field, gameplay, and players) and their binding
relationships: operations, orientation, and traction. Maintaining
balance across these interlocking elements prevents common strategic
breakdowns like stagnation.
