NEWS & TRENDS

 WHAT WOULD A SERIOUS AI PRODUCT LOOK LIKE? (15 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Current AI tools often act as misleading software. Serious AI
products must feature error checking, robust citations, no
first-person language, and non-natural language interfaces when
appropriate.

 EXPERTS LEAD EXPERTS (3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 AI is pushing leaders back into hands-on work, but that shift may be
temporary. As building gets cheaper, expert leadership becomes more
important.

OPINIONS & TUTORIALS

 STOP MANAGING AI AGENTS LIKE SOFTWARE. START MANAGING THEM LIKE NEW
HIRES (3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Organizations fail to deploy enterprise AI agents because they treat
them like software rollouts rather than new hires needing onboarding
and data prep. Clean data, workflow integration, and governance
present far greater bottlenecks than model performance.

 GOOGLE HAS THE BEST AI MONEY CAN BUY. ITS PRODUCTS STILL FEEL BROKEN
(3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Google's search quality failures stem from product and organizational
flaws rather than model limitations. By layering probabilistic AI
features onto a deterministic search interface without updating UI
promises or user feedback loops, companies risk eroding user trust
through unverified AI outputs.

 RAISE THE CEILING: HOW TO SCALE INTENT, QUALITY, AND ARTISTRY WITH AI
(12 MINUTE READ) [11]

 The AI building boom risks producing generic "zombie UI" if teams
ignore intent and artistry. To counter this, teams must maintain a
strong design POV, encode standards into production, refuse to settle
for standard builds, and invent new interfaces with AI.

 EVERYONE SHOULD PUBLISH THE DEEPEST, MOST DIRECT COMPETITIVE EVALS
THEY CAN (5 MINUTE READ) [12]

 B2B AI vendors should publish deep, direct, and continuous
competitive evaluations against named competitors to build buyer
trust, as demonstrated by Gorgias open-sourcing a live evaluation
harness across 18 vendors.

 WE'RE BUILDING MULTIPLAYER AI. HERE'S WHAT WE'VE LEARNED SO FAR (9
MINUTE READ) [13]

 Traditional chat interfaces like Slack create an information
bottleneck. It's essential to establish a shared context system to
maintain aligned results and prevent compounding divergences when
multiple users and AI agents collaborate.

 VALUE ACCRUAL FLYWHEEL (5 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Software accrues long-term value by creating dependencies that
compound over time and increase switching costs. This process involves
dependency formation across multiple categories, internal and external
compounding such as network effects, and critical workflow multipliers
that dictate overall switching risk and revenue capture.
