NEWS & TRENDS

 THE AI COMPETENCE/JUDGEMENT GAP (8 MINUTE READ) [4]

 AI is expanding our ability to produce work faster than our ability
to judge it, creating a growing competence gap around quality,
expertise, and accountability.

 NOBODY'S ACTUALLY PRIORITISING ‘VALUE' (8 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Product teams cannot meaningfully prioritize on “value” without
first defining what matters through strategy and outcomes. Since
expected value is ultimately a hypothesis, better prioritization comes
from comparing bets based on evidence and confidence in their ability
to move the desired outcome.

🚀

OPINIONS & TUTORIALS

 ROADMAP DECISIONS RATHER THAN DATES (7 MINUTE READ) [6]

 As AI reduces implementation bottlenecks, decision speed becomes the
real constraint. Teams should roadmap around unresolved decisions, not
dates.

 LANDING THE PLANE (8 MINUTE READ) [7]

 The last 10% of a project is often the hardest. Strong leaders
protect scope, increase focus, and deliberately guide teams across the
finish line.

 KEEP CALM AND CARRY ON… WITH YOUR DEMO (6 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Successful software demos rely on a clear narrative and flexible
recovery strategies rather than avoiding surprises. By organizing
sprints around user journeys, maintaining lightweight run-of-show
notes, preparing recorded demos, troubleshooting safely, and assigning
a support partner, presenters can handle unexpected bugs, data issues,
or timing gaps smoothly.

 SPEC-DRIVEN DEVELOPMENT: WHEN THE SPEC BECOMES THE PRODUCT (12 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 As AI lowers code generation costs, software development's bottleneck
shifts from implementation to intent, coordination, and review. Treat
a living, versioned product specification as the source of truth,
defining each feature as a delta that systems propagate across product
surfaces before generating and verifying code.

🎁

MISCELLANEOUS

 CUTTING THE NOISE (4 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Product teams should simplify before they add. Removing stale
processes, meetings, tools, and backlog items creates more space for
meaningful work and better decisions.

 THIS ESSAY IS 10% AI GENERATED (8 MINUTE READ) [13]

 As AI writing becomes increasingly convincing, the debate is shifting
from writing quality to authorship. People still want to know who
shaped and stands behind an idea because authorship provides context,
meaning, and a way to evaluate what we read.

 The competitive focus of generative media is shifting from individual
