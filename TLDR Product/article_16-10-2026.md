 AI has transformed workflows into personalized context layers while

OPINIONS & TUTORIALS

 SHOULD YOU START WITH A PRD OR A PROTOTYPE? (11 MINUTE READ) [8]

 This article examines whether product teams should start with a PRD
or a prototype, arguing they should evolve together in a learning loop
focused on the core unknown. Although AI tools can quickly generate
both, teams must maintain intentionality, aggressively curate
features, and provide careful human oversight to match actual product
intent.

 HUMAN IN THE LOOP MEANS MAKING SURE THE HUMAN IS NOT OUT OF THE LOOP
(10 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Human-in-the-loop systems often fail when oversight is reduced to
automated confirm clicks or post-hoc approvals, turning human
reviewers into moral crumple zones. Effective agency design requires
clear mandates, permissions-based constraints, and keeping users
involved at points where they can genuinely stop runs or reverse
irreversible actions.
