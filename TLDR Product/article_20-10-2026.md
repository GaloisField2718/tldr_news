 WHAT IS A FORWARD DEPLOYED PRODUCT MANAGER, ANYWAY? (10 MINUTE READ)
[7]

 FDPMs are embedded directly within customer organizations to bridge
the gap between vendors and users, especially for complex AI
deployments. Working alongside Forward Deployed Engineers, they
prototype tailored solutions on-site and feed successful iterations
back into the core platform.

 WHAT ON EARTH IS GOING ON WITH AI PRICING AND PACKAGING? (8 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 AI pricing is confusing because credits and tokens obscure value,
while usage costs are unpredictable. To reach mainstream customers,
companies need clear, predictable pricing that reflects customer value
without sacrificing profitability.

 BUILDING APPS WITH HEART: WHAT NOOM, NUMO, AND OPAL GET RIGHT (13
MINUTE READ) [9]

 B2C mobile apps have become generic due to funnel over-optimization.
Building with "heart" means understanding specific users, tailoring
onboarding, delivering early delight before paywalls, and balancing
authentic user resonance with strong monetization engines.

 YOUR PRODUCT IS NOT THE CENTER OF THE UNIVERSE (4 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Established products relying on a product-centric model will face
diminishing relevance as multi-tool AI agents become common. To adapt,
products must adopt a person-centric view by integrating directly with
users' preferred agents, exposing UI components, expertise, and data
moats.

 MENTAL MODELS IN THE AGENTIC ERA (14 MINUTE READ) [13]

 This piece adapts fifteen established product and economic mental
models to the agentic era. While classic principles still apply, they
yield drastically different outcomes in probabilistic environments.
Changes in unit economics, compounding errors, the jagged frontier,
and shifting bottlenecks require product managers to adopt new
evaluation frameworks, autonomy dials, and editing skill sets.

 While AI makes building applications remarkably easy, achieving basic
