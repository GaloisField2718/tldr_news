NEWS & TRENDS

 AI PRODUCT MANAGER SALARY IN 2026 (5 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Compensation varies widely based on employer and seniority, with top
employers like OpenAI paying median packages up to $750,000. Total
compensation is heavily influenced by stock equity. A candidate's job
level dictates their salary band rather than negotiation.

 PREDICTABILITY VS OUTCOMES (4 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Product development fails to achieve meaningful outcomes because
companies prioritize corporate predictability over innovation.
Prioritizing predictability leads teams to adopt rigid project models
that sacrifice discovery. To achieve better results, organizations
must acknowledge uncertainty, embrace humility in product discovery,
and reward outcomes over fixed plans.

OPINIONS & TUTORIALS

 YOU DIDN'T DO IT RIGHT (6 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Frameworks like OKRs can fail because they don't fit a team's needs,
even after careful implementation. Start with one team, learn what
works, and adapt or replace the framework based on results.

 THE DEATH OF THE INTERFACE (7 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Autonomous AI agents will replace traditional user interfaces like
apps, websites, and checkout buttons. Consequently, payment rails must
evolve into "headless money" powered by stablecoins and fast
settlement networks to support seamless agent-to-agent transactions.

 DESIGNING AN AGENT COLLABORATOR (6 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Ambient is a macOS companion that uses voice, screen sharing, and
drawing to capture context. One week post-launch, Ambient
conversations show deeper engagement than text sessions, though text
remains dominant. The company is testing onboarding tours to boost
discovery and adoption.

 IS YOUR SOFTWARE PRODUCT READY FOR THE CYBER RESILIENCE ACT? (5
MINUTE READ) [12]

 The EU's Cyber Resilience Act enforces cybersecurity standards for
software and hardware, with reporting starting in September 2026 and
full enforcement in December 2027. It mandates 13 security guidelines,
including a clean software bill of materials, though free open-source
projects are exempt.

 You build agency by taking small actions that show you can change

 HOW APPLE PUTS INDIGENOUS LANGUAGES ON THE IPHONE KEYBOARD (5 MINUTE
READ) [14]

 Apple added iOS support for indigenous languages. The process
requires registering characters with Unicode, designing custom fonts,
and collaborating with native communities. Community leaders note that
digital accessibility is crucial for language preservation and data
sovereignty.
