NEWS & TRENDS

 WHY GOOD PRODUCT IDEAS DON'T SURVIVE (12 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Traditional product development models use a cumulative funnel of
hurdles (prioritization, specification, implementation, and
post-launch iteration) that drastically reduces the survival rate of
high-impact ideas. Based on estimated survival rates across these
phases, only about 5% of genuinely high-value product ideas launch and
achieve their full potential.

 5 TIPS FOR BUILDING AN AI TOOL TO ADDRESS WORKER SHORTAGES (3 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 Here are five practical strategies for building AI agents to address
worker shortages in high-stakes industries like construction,
manufacturing, and heavy equipment. The approach emphasizes defining
specific problems, unifying fragmented data, using specialized
multi-agent architectures, maintaining high accuracy, and clearly
communicating user benefits.

 THE FASTEST GUN IN UX: WHY YOUR TEAM IS TELLING THE WRONG STORY (7
MINUTE READ) [8]

 Design teams in a post-ZIRP economy face intense pressure to deliver
rapid outputs, leading them to skip crucial steps like user research.
This dynamic plays into the hands of stakeholders dubbed "Fastest Guns
in the West," who use speed and deliberate ambiguity to control
product narratives and secure early commitment to ideas before proper
evaluation can occur.

 HAS YOUR PRODUCT OUTGROWN ITS FLOOR PLAN? (6 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Successful digital products often outgrow their initial structural
simplicity as features accumulate, requiring a clear spatial "floor
plan" rather than just consistent design system components. Mismatched
spatial movement and flat UI cues make products harder for users to
navigate and understand.

 AI, THE LOSS OF POSITIVE FRICTION, AND WHAT TO DO ABOUT IT (8 MINUTE
READ) [16]

 Generative AI tools risk eliminating "positive friction". By
automating these transition points, knowledge workers may produce
overwhelming volumes of unverified output that outpaces customer
absorption. To maintain quality and intentionality, teams should
preserve original feedback and keep deliberate human loops.
