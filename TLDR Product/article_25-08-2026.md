TLDR PRODUCT MANAGEMENT 2026-08-25

 SHIPPING ON TIME ISN'T ENOUGH (SPONSOR) [4]

 Your IT teams deliver projects on time. But does the work solve the
right problems? A project mindset focuses on scope, deadlines, and
delivery. A product mindset starts with customer problems and business
outcomes.

This guide shows how IT teams can work more like product
teams—prioritize the right problems, learn before they build, and
connect each sprint to a business result.

NEWS & TRENDS

 AUTONOMY AND INNOVATION (10 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Incumbents will adopt AI cautiously because they have more to lose
from mistakes. Startups can embrace autonomy more aggressively, giving
them a natural advantage in turning AI into disruption.

 INSIDE PM AT STRIPE: WHAT COMES AFTER "BECOME A BUILDER?" (61 MINUTE
VIDEO) [7]

 AI is pushing every product-building role upstream rather than simply
eliminating functions. PMs will increasingly create leverage through
shared context, judgment, strategy, persuasion, and domain expertise
while AI and adjacent functions absorb more execution.

🚀

OPINIONS & TUTORIALS

 THE DEADLINE THAT WAS ALREADY MISSED (8 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Bad roadmaps often fail from missing dependencies, not bad estimates.
Working backwards exposes what must be true before execution begins.

 A GOOD MOVE IS ENABLING (4 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Good product moves create value now while opening better options for
what comes next. Teams should avoid short-term wins that lead to
strategic dead ends.

 PRODUCT OWNER INTERVIEW QUESTIONS FOR THE AGE OF AI (16 MINUTE READ)
[13]

 As AI lowers the cost of building software, product judgment matters
more than tool fluency. Great Product Owners know what to build, what
to stop, and where AI should assist rather than decide.

 TO INVENT WAYMO, THEY HAD TO REINVENT PM (60 MINUTE VIDEO) [14]

 Waymo shows how PM must evolve for problems without clear metrics or
playbooks. The key is defining what good looks like, setting concrete
checkpoints, and building teams capable of learning their way forward.

 BIG-BANG REDESIGNS VS. LEGACY MODERNIZATION (6 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Big-bang legacy redesigns often fail because critical dependencies
are easy to miss. Modernizing gradually, with users involved and
workflows replaced in small slices, reduces risk and delivers value
sooner.

 THE ORACLE PROBLEM: AN INVISIBLE BOTTLENECK TO AI AND MEDICINE (8
MINUTE READ) [16]

 Healthcare AI benchmarks can measure test performance without proving
clinical effectiveness. Better evaluations must measure whether AI
improves real-world health outcomes.
