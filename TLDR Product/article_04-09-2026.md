 HOW TO TELL WHEN YOUR USERS ARE LOSING INTEREST, BEFORE IT'S TOO LATE
(SPONSOR) [4]

 Most UX problems don't show up as obvious failures. They surface as
hesitation, repeated clicks, and drop-off, yet by the time the signals
are clear, the moment to act has passed. 

This Datadog eBook [5] shows product managers how to pick up on signs
that are normally spread across analytics, performance, and support
tools. Topics include:

	* 3 questions you should be able to answer about your users
	* The Pulse, Pain, and Proof framework [6] for organizing UX signals
	* How session-level visibility lets you diagnose problems with
limited metrics
