TLDR PRODUCT MANAGEMENT 2026-11-03

 54% OF TEAMS HAD AN AI AGENT MAKE A MISTAKE LAST QUARTER. APPROVAL
GATES DIDN'T LOWER IT. (SPONSOR) [4]

 Leadership wants agents doing more on their own, but nearly half of
teams can't fully trace what agents did. The State of Agentic AI: H2
2026 surveyed 1,030 people who build, buy, or influence AI systems:

	* THE PRESSURE IS RISING: 25.3% adopt agents to meet leadership
expectations, nearly double December's 13.5%. Teams letting agents act
without approval rose from 4.2% to 7.5%.
	* APPROVALS AREN'T THE FIX: Teams that approve every action reported
mistakes at 58%, vs. 52% with no approval.
	* VISIBILITY IS THE GAP: Only 53% can fully trace which agent did
what last week.
