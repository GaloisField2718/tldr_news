NEWS & TRENDS

 STOP USING EPICS & USER STORIES (4 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Debating epics and user stories distracts teams from meaningful
outcomes. Framing work as outcomes, opportunities, and measurable
experiments creates clarity and reinforces an outcome-oriented
culture.

 WEIGHING THE BUSINESS CASE FOR QUALITY (5 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Great craftsmanship is sustainable when quality directly drives
business success. The key is choosing products and markets where
customers reward it.

🚀

OPINIONS & TUTORIALS

 THIS IS WHY WE CAN'T HAVE NICE THINGS (9 MINUTE READ) [6]

 AI models are easy to copy and replace, making their competitive
advantages short-lived. Durable value may come from specialized,
deeply embedded systems that learn from each company's data and
workflows.

 CANVA IS ONE PERCENT OF THE WAY THERE (5 MINUTE READ) [7]

 As AI makes software easier to build, good judgment becomes the new
bottleneck. Canva's advantage is an operating system that moves
founder insight into the product quickly without creating chaos.

RESOURCES & TOOLS

 WHAT I'VE LEARNED WRITING FOR BILLIONS OF USERS (5 MINUTE READ) [8]

 At a massive scale, every word matters. Great UX writing builds
trust, reduces confusion, and helps teams uncover and solve deeper
product problems.

 THIS IS HOW TO GET WHAT YOU REALLY WANT: 6 SECRETS FROM RESEARCH (8
MINUTE READ) [9]

 Our desires are often shaped by comparison with others. By
recognizing those influences, learning from envy, and prioritizing
meaningful goals over status, we can pursue what we truly value.

 WHY THE PRODUCT MANAGER TO CEO PIPELINE IS THE UNDERRATED CRASH
COURSE FOR LEADERSHIP IN TECH (3 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Product management prepares future CEOs to set direction, make
difficult decisions, and own measurable outcomes. Leadership skills
matter more than following a traditional sequence of job titles.

 HOW MAPS CAN HIDE PROBLEMS (3 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Maps and AI can help organizations navigate complexity, but they
cannot resolve conflicting goals, structures, and incentives. Use maps
to uncover and fix misalignment, not simply make it easier to manage.
