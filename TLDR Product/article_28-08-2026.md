 FROM AI DEMO TO AI PRODUCT: WHAT SITS BETWEEN A PROMPT AND PRODUCTION
REALITY (12 MINUTE READ) [7]

 While vibe coding quickly generates impressive AI prototypes, it
often masks the rigorous engineering needed for production. To prepare
these applications for real users, teams must implement robust
controls for output contracts, cost management, security, and
observability.

 YOU'RE 5 DECISIONS AWAY FROM 1M+ USERS (9 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Strong PLG starts with understanding where customers find value and
where growth is leaking. Then prioritize the biggest revenue
opportunities, balance sales with self-service, and make ownership
explicit.

OPINIONS & TUTORIALS

 JUST ANOTHER TRANSFORMATION (5 MINUTE READ) [9]

 AI amplifies the organization it enters, including its dysfunction.
Companies should fix strategy, culture, collaboration, and data
foundations before expecting AI transformation to deliver meaningful
results.

 AGILITY MIGHT HAVE BEEN WAITING FOR AI (3 MINUTE READ) [10]

 AI is shifting bottlenecks, not eliminating them. Organizations need
to focus on outcomes and redesign how work flows around the new
constraints rather than measuring AI activity.

 STRATEGY PROMPTS ACTIVITY (30 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Effective strategy involves challenging conventional assumptions. For
best results, conduct this activity in multiplayer mode to foster
productive team discussions.

 USER TESTING ON A SHOESTRING (8 MINUTE READ) [12]

 User testing does not need to be expensive or complicated. Watch a
few real users complete realistic tasks, then fix the friction their
behavior reveals.

 FAKING A BRAND IS EASY. MAKING IT STOP IS HARD (7 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Brand impersonation is a network problem, not a takedown problem.
Defenders need to continuously identify and disrupt the infrastructure
behind recurring attacks.

 FOCUS AND FOLLOW-THROUGH ARE THE MOAT (4 MINUTE READ) [14]

 For overloaded engineering teams, focus and follow-through are vital,
as every new task displaces another. This team manages capacity by
maintaining prioritized backlogs, stack-ranking sprint plans, removing
lower-priority work when urgent items arise, and regularly updating
stakeholders on progress.
