NEWS & TRENDS

 THE END OF FEATURE DISCOVERY AS WE KNOW IT (8 MINUTE READ) [8]

 AI agents will eliminate traditional software feature discovery by
operating products on users' behalf. Instead of navigating menus,
users can invoke functionality directly using natural language,
forcing product teams to focus on trust and minimal AI interfaces.

 VOICE OF THE CUSTOMER (11 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Effective product building requires mastering the Voice of the
Customer to understand underlying needs rather than surface-level
metrics. AI accelerates this process, turning research loops into
quick conversations and creating accessible organizational knowledge
bases.

OPINIONS & TUTORIALS

 THE HARNESS IS A PRODUCT (9 MINUTE READ) [10]

 As agentic AI systems evolve, the underlying harness (instructions,
tools, and sandboxes) must be managed as a discrete product. Product
teams must apply rigorous testing and proper rollback paths to ensure
reliability and prevent configuration debt.

 THE BOTTLENECK HAS MOVED (6 MINUTE READ) [11]

 With AI agents coding rapidly, the main constraint in software
development has shifted away from writing code. Enterprises must
reorganize around end-to-end value delivery, measuring time-to-learn
rather than raw velocity.
