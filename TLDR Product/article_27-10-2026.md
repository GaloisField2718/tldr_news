NEWS & TRENDS

 ARE WE LIVING IN THE GOLDEN AGE OF AGILE? (5 MINUTE READ) [5]

 While Scrum has declined, Agile is currently in its golden age due to
modern capabilities that allow teams to rapidly build, validate, and
ship prototypes. This article examines alternative frameworks like
Shape Up, concluding that rigid upstream decision-making and fixed
six-week cycles do not suit early-stage companies that need to pivot
quickly.

 EXPLORING THE GAME (5 MINUTE READ) [6]

 This article examines product strategy through three aspects: the
game, gameplay, and players. Product strategy involves scanning for
coherence, identifying key internal and external relationships, and
anticipating shifting landscape forces - providing product leaders
with a framework to assess strategic health and align teams.

OPINIONS & TUTORIALS

 LET CHAOS REIGN, AND THEN REIN IN CHAOS (6 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Chaotic AI adoption can signal that employees have found valuable new
ways to work. Companies should support that initiative with oversight
and access controls, preserving experimentation while keeping
agent-driven work aligned with business goals.

 HOW TO MANAGE THE INEVITABLE MESS OF RUNNING A COMPANY WHERE EVERYONE
IS A BUILDER (5 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Combining product and UX roles into AI-driven "builder" positions
causes internal chaos and low-quality features. Without traditional
quality filters or feature removal processes, organizations face
higher support costs and degraded user experiences. Strategies to help
restore control include separating building from shipping rights,
requiring feature hypotheses, and conducting regular retirement
reviews.

RESOURCES & TOOLS

 OVER HALF OF WEB TRAFFIC IS NO LONGER HUMAN (SPONSOR) [9]

 Automated traffic accounted for 57.5% of HTTP requests in 2025 [9],
compared with 42.5% from humans, according to Cloudflare. As AI agents
increasingly browse, research, compare, and act on behalf of people,
the “human vs. bot” distinction is becoming the wrong one.

Are you ready? [9]

 THE EARLY ACCESS PLAYBOOK (10 MINUTE READ) [10]

 As AI accelerates development, early access helps teams test whether
their products genuinely help users. Observing real tasks, retesting
improvements, and checking results across groups provides stronger
evidence than enthusiasm alone.

 THE POWER OF THE STRATEGIC INITIATIVE POSTMORTEM (12 MINUTE READ)
[11]

 Strategic postmortems should examine assumptions, ownership, and
resources rather than focus solely on execution. Teams should learn
from both successes and failures and use those lessons to guide future
decisions.

 WHY REDDIT BUILT ITS OWN AI PROTOTYPING TOOL (30 MINUTE VIDEO) [12]

 Reddit built an in-house AI prototyping harness called Reddit Proto
to bridge the gap between product design and engineering. The tool
maps the company's design system to production code, allowing
designers and product managers to generate interactive prototypes
using real Reddit user data and deploy them internally via Kubernetes.


 TRUST AS A FEATURE (4 MINUTE READ) [13]

 As software development accelerates, product managers must prioritize
ethics, privacy, and societal impact. The four pillars of ethical
shipping are solving the right problem, making evaluations acceptance
criteria, treating trust as a feature, and orchestrating shifts in
software lifecycles.
