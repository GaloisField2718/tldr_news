NEWS & TRENDS

 ENTERPRISE SSD PRICES RUN AT 6.5% LAST YEAR (4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Enterprise SSD prices rose another 5% in July and remain about 6.5
times higher than in Q3 2025. VDURA prices a 30TB TLC drive at $22,600
and argues that a mixed architecture using flash for
performance-sensitive workloads and HDDs for capacity could save
approximately $38.74 million over three years in a modeled 25PB AI
storage deployment.

 AI WAS MEANT TO SIMPLIFY IT SERVICE MANAGEMENT – NEW RESEARCH SHOWS
IT'S CREATING BIGGER WORKLOADS FOR TEAMS (3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 SolarWinds found that 84% of respondents say AI has met or exceeded
ROI expectations in ITSM, yet 52% say their overall workload has
increased. Teams are saving time on ticket triage and issue detection
but spending more effort maintaining integrations, validating outputs,
and managing the operational overhead of AI itself.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 ADOPTION OF WIFI 7 (2 MINUTE READ) [10]

 WiFi 7 adoption has reached 7.2% in the US about a year and a half
after routers entered the market, with ISPs such as Charter, Comcast,
Frontier, and CenturyLink driving deployment. The technology improves
throughput, interference handling, device capacity, and in some cases
upload speeds, while WiFi 8 and WiFi 9 are already being planned for
future releases.

 THE ENTERPRISE AI BOTTLENECK IS ABOUT CONTEXT, NOT CAPABILITY (9
MINUTE READ) [11]

 Enterprise AI deployments are being constrained less by model
capability than by the quality of organizational context available to
AI systems. Atlan cofounder describes context as knowledge, expertise,
and norms, and outlines Atlan's approach of mining verified context,
integrating it into agent development, and learning from human-agent
interaction traces.

 LAYERED DATA ARCHITECTURE TURNS ENTERPRISE DATA INTO A SYSTEM OF
INTELLIGENCE (4 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Enterprise AI needs more than a vector database or lakehouse:
organizations need metadata, knowledge graphs, and semantic layers
that connect data with business context so agents can return
trustworthy answers. The bigger challenge may be governance, as access
policies also need to move into this semantic layer rather than remain
scattered across individual databases.

LAUNCHES & PARTNERSHIPS

 COMCAST GIVES ITS WI-FI MOTION DETECTOR A SECURITY MAKEOVER (4 MINUTE
READ) [13]

 Comcast has incorporated its WiFi Motion radio-wave intrusion
detection feature into Xfinity Shield, a bundle combining physical and
cybersecurity protections. The feature uses stationary Xfinity
equipment and compatible devices to detect disruptions in wireless
signals, but its privacy assurances are qualified by terms allowing
Comcast to disclose generated information in certain investigations,
disputes, or court proceedings.

 USE GEMINI TO HELP MANAGE GOOGLE WORKSPACE FOR YOUR ORGANIZATION (2
MINUTE READ) [14]

 Google added Admin Assist directly to the Workspace Admin Console,
giving super admins a Gemini side panel for troubleshooting,
administrative guidance, and configuration help. Search Overviews can
also synthesize Workspace documentation into answers and actionable
next steps without admins bouncing between the console and support
articles.

 CEREBRAS REIMAGINES AI CLUSTER DESIGN WITH SWITCHLESS CS-4
ARCHITECTURE (5 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Cerebras unveiled its CS-4 AI system, which can connect wafer-scale
processors directly across racks without network switches, potentially
reducing latency, power use and networking hardware in large AI
clusters. The system still relies on Ethernet and RoCE v2 for storage,
data ingestion and external connectivity, while its proprietary fabric
and management stack could leave enterprises operating a separate
infrastructure domain alongside existing GPU environments.

 AN IT LEADER'S GUIDE TO DREAMFORCE 2026 (5 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Salesforce previews the major IT themes for Dreamforce 2026,
including Headless 360, governing AI agents, securing agentic
workloads, and building AI applications on top of existing enterprise
data and business logic. The September 15-17 event will feature more
than 1,600 sessions, with dedicated tracks covering IT, security,
data, Agentforce and AI development.

 TRUST AND TALENT: THE REAL AI LESSONS FROM A DAY AT THE MCLAREN
TECHNOLOGY CENTRE (4 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Enterprise AI success depends as much on trust, training, and
organizational readiness as on the underlying technology. Companies
need to build AI fluency and governance alongside adoption, especially
as many employees are already using AI without formal training.
