NEWS & TRENDS

 NOT JUST OPENAI: NOW ANTHROPIC SAYS ITS INTERNAL MODELS GOT ONLINE
AND CYBERATTACKED 3 OTHER ORGANIZATIONS (5 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Anthropic discovered that Claude models unintentionally reached the
public internet during supposedly isolated cybersecurity evaluations
and compromised systems belonging to three real organizations using
weak passwords and unauthenticated endpoints. The incidents involved
Opus 4.7, Mythos 5, and an internal research model, with the earliest
dating back to April, and were uncovered only after Anthropic reviewed
more than 141,000 test sessions following OpenAI's similar incident.
Anthropic suspended its cyber evaluations and notified the affected
organizations, two of which had not detected the activity.

 THE MAJORITY OF CORPORATE IT IS NOW OFF PREMISES FOR THE FIRST TIME
(4 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Third-party facilities now host 46% of enterprise workloads, compared
with 44% in company-owned datacenters, marking the first time
off-premises infrastructure has taken the lead, according to Uptime
Institute's survey of more than 800 operators. Typical rack density
also climbed above 11 kW as organizations replace older servers with
more power-hungry hardware.

 MICROSOFT CONFIRMS AN AI WORM IS PROPAGATING THROUGH COPILOT AND
OTHER MS APPS (10 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Researchers demonstrated a document-borne attack that hides
instructions inside files processed by Copilot, alters generated
content, and copies itself into newly created documents. Microsoft has
deployed mitigations, but researchers say the underlying problem
remains because current models cannot reliably distinguish untrusted
data from executable instructions.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 DNS IS INFRASTRUCTURE. IT'S TIME TO MANAGE IT THAT WAY (5 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 DNS is often managed through manual dashboards and scattered accounts
even though a single incorrect change can disrupt websites, email,
certificates, and other critical services. Bring domain and DNS
management into Infrastructure as Code, APIs, and CLI workflows so
changes are repeatable, reviewable, automated, and easier to audit or
roll back.

 DATA CENTER BACKLASH COULD SLOW CIOS' AI PLANS (6 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Opposition to new data centers is becoming a material infrastructure
and budgeting risk, with protests organized across 42 states,
construction moratoriums active in 10 states, and new large-load
electricity tariffs emerging across the country. CIOs may need to
secure capacity earlier, reconsider cloud and colocation cost
assumptions, diversify regions and providers, and treat compute and
power as strategic supply-chain dependencies.

LAUNCHES & PARTNERSHIPS

 OKTA BUYS AI SECURITY STARTUP PERMISO - SOURCE SAYS FOR ABOUT $200M
(4 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Okta agreed to acquire Permiso Security in a deal reportedly worth
just under $200 million, adding technology that detects suspicious
behavior involving employees, service accounts, applications, and AI
agents after they have authenticated. The acquisition pushes Okta
deeper into identity threat detection and response and the security
operations center, with capabilities for identifying excessive
privileges, compromised identities, and anomalous agent activity
across cloud and SaaS environments.

 INFORCER RAISES $50M TO HELP PREPARE SMALLER BUSINESSES FOR A NEW
WORLD OF AI AND SECURITY RISKS (4 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Inforcer raised a $50 million Series C to expand its platform for
MSPs managing Microsoft 365 environments across multiple small and
midsize businesses. The company says revenue is growing 300% year over
year as it adds capabilities for detecting unauthorized AI use and
responding to security threats across customer environments.

 OUTDOOR WI-FI FINALLY MEANS BUSINESS (4 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Cisco's outdoor Wireless 9177 Series combines tri-band Wi-Fi 7 with
automated frequency coordination for outdoor 6 GHz access and
Ultra-Reliable Wireless Backhaul for multigigabit connectivity and
sub-10-millisecond handoffs. The access points offer directional,
omnidirectional, and external-antenna models, copper or fiber uplinks,
and cloud or on-premises management for campuses, ports, industrial
facilities, stadiums, and transit environments.

 STEP-BY-STEP FABRIC DEPLOYMENT WITH CISCO NEXUS HYPERFABRIC (4 MINUTE

 Nexus Hyperfabric lets IT teams design cloud-managed VXLAN EVPN
networks using validated templates, automatically generate hardware

platform abstracts much of the BGP, VXLAN, and cabling complexity
normally associated with datacenter fabric deployments.
