NEWS & TRENDS

 MICROSOFT RESTORES SERVICES AFTER PROLONGED GLOBAL OUTAGE (4 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 Microsoft says it restored service to all customers after an outage
disrupted Exchange Online, Teams, Copilot, and other connected
services from Thursday into Saturday. Servers failing automated health
checks contributed to the disruption, and initial recovery attempts
failed to provide the expected relief.

 MICROSOFT RAISES CLOUD PRICES IN INDIA AND NEW ZEALAND (4 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Microsoft will increase commercial cloud prices by 15.7% in India and
10% in New Zealand starting January 1, 2027, citing currency
adjustments. Prices will fall in China, Australia, and Norway, while
some on-premises products in India face increases of up to 20%.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 AI RECOVERY NEEDS MORE THAN AN UNDO BUTTON (8 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Restoring an AI agent, reversing its changes to internal systems, and
undoing its business consequences are three different problems.
Commvault's recovery tools address parts of the first two, but actions
such as sending customer emails or supplier orders still require
controls before execution.

 MICROSOFT'S OUTAGE EXPOSES THE COST OF VENDOR DEPENDENCE (3 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Microsoft's outage highlights how consolidating email, collaboration,
and productivity tools under one provider can amplify disruption. This
article examines concerns that this concentration leaves organizations
unable to switch providers or keep critical work running when services
fail.

LAUNCHES & PARTNERSHIPS

 CLAUDE JOINS MEETINGS WITH GRANOLA AND OTTER INTEGRATIONS PLANNED (5
MINUTE READ) [11]

 Anthropic is rolling out Claude Meetings in beta for Team and
Enterprise customers, letting Claude join Google Meet calls to answer
questions, draft documents, and capture action items. Upcoming
integrations with Granola, Gong, Attention, Fireflies.ai, Otter, and
Plaud will let teams send meeting transcripts to their existing tools.


 AWS SECRETS MANAGER NOW SUPPORTS CROSS-ACCOUNT REPLICATION (2 MINUTE
READ) [12]

 AWS Secrets Manager now supports cross-account replication, allowing
teams to maintain synchronized copies of primary secrets across AWS
accounts for backup, disaster recovery, and cross-account access. The
feature extends multi-Region replication across account boundaries,
enabling centralized ownership and rotation without shared KMS keys or
cross-account policies.

 NVIDIA, AMD, GOOGLE, AND META JOIN OPEN INFERENCE CONSORTIUM (4
MINUTE READ) [13]

 LMSYS launched the SGLang Consortium to coordinate engineering work
on its open-source AI inference engine, with founding members
including Nvidia, AMD, Intel, Google, and Meta. Members will
contribute deployment feedback, testing resources, and development
work, while SGLang's maintainers retain control over technical
decisions.

 BUILDING ALWAYS-ON AGENTS (14 MINUTE READ) [14]

 The architecture for always-on agents relies on five primary
primitives: an event inbox, durable timers, persistent memory, a
permission system, and an outbound communication outbox. The framework
is designed to enable AI agents to operate autonomously in the
background while maintaining stateful and proactive behaviour.

 MARVELL TARGETS AI'S MEMORY AND NETWORKING BOTTLENECKS (3 MINUTE
READ) [15]

 Marvell is showcasing optical interconnects, CXL memory expansion,
and networking technology at Supercomputing 2026. The demonstrations
focus on scaling memory and compute across racks to address bandwidth
constraints and infrastructure underutilization.
