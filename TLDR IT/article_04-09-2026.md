NEWS & TRENDS

 MICROSOFT TEAMS, OUTLOOK FAIL TO LAUNCH ON ARM-BASED WINDOWS PCS (3
MINUTE READ) [4]

 Teams and New Outlook can crash or fail to launch on ARM-based
Windows 11 devices after recent security updates, particularly on
newly imaged machines missing Microsoft Store updates. Microsoft
recommends updating the Auto Super Resolution Package as a temporary
workaround while it prepares a permanent fix.

 SALESFORCE SIMPLIFIES ITS EDITIONS BY BUNDLING SLACK, SECURITY, AI,
AND ANALYTICS (5 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Salesforce has introduced new Core, Advanced, and Max editions that
package Agentforce, Slack, Tableau Next, security capabilities, and
support into fewer SKUs. The move reflects a broader push by SaaS
vendors to consolidate products into larger platform bundles rather
than selling each capability separately.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 DEFYING GRAVITY: THE SYSTEM OF RECORD IN THE AGE OF AI (10 MINUTE
READ) [6]

 AI is creating a new opportunity for startups to build systems of
record rather than merely lightweight systems of action. AI agents
require integrated, high-quality domain data, so businesses may
replace entrenched software systems, increasing market turnover and
expanding software vendors' potential from seat-based revenue to
automated labor and outcomes.

 STARTUP ARR IS LESS SECURE THAN EVER, NEW RESEARCH SHOWS (5 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 Enterprise buyers are treating AI software very differently from
traditional SaaS: 77% of companies surveyed by Madrona re-evaluate AI
vendors every six months or continuously. That creates a “fast in,
fast out” buying model where even landing an enterprise contract may
no longer translate into durable recurring revenue.

 WHY DATA SOVEREIGNTY HAS BECOME A STRATEGIC IT PRIORITY (7 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Data sovereignty is becoming less about simply knowing which region
hosts your data and more about retaining control when vendors,
regulations, infrastructure, or geopolitical conditions change.
Portability, operational independence, and avoiding concentration risk
should increasingly factor into architecture and vendor decisions.

LAUNCHES & PARTNERSHIPS

 OKTA LAUNCHES AUTH0 TOOLS FOR AI AGENTS & B2B LOGINS (3 MINUTE READ)
[9]

 Okta has introduced new Auth0 capabilities for authenticating AI
agents, agent-led commerce, and enterprise B2B identity. The launch
also includes B2B Connect, which lets SaaS providers add enterprise
SSO, federation, and multi-tenant identity capabilities without
replacing their existing identity infrastructure.

 THE EVOLUTION OF JFROG AI CATALOG: YOUR AI CONTROL PLANE FOR AGENTIC
DEVELOPMENT (8 MINUTE READ) [10]

 JFrog says its AI Catalog has evolved from a secure model registry
into an AI control plane for governing models, MCP servers, skills,
plugins, and agent packages. The company presents semantic scanning,
shadow AI detection, governed registries, and runtime Agent Guard
enforcement as measures to prevent agents from consuming unapproved or
malicious assets across the Agentic Development Lifecycle.

MISCELLANEOUS

 CHATGPT, CLAUDE, AND GROK ALL WENT DOWN AT ONCE; ENTERPRISES NEED A
BACKUP PLAN (6 MINUTE READ) [11]

 ChatGPT, Claude, and Grok experienced overlapping multi-hour outages
on Thursday, exposing a growing business-continuity problem as
companies automate more workflows around AI services. IT teams may
need to treat AI providers like other critical infrastructure, with
fallback models, documented manual workflows, and outage planning.

 HOW NASA IS USING GRAPH TECHNOLOGY AND LLMS TO BUILD A PEOPLE
KNOWLEDGE GRAPH (8 MINUTE READ) [12]

 NASA's People Analytics team built a People Knowledge Graph with
Memgraph, large language models, and secure AWS infrastructure to
connect employees, skills, projects, and organizational data. The
system supports expert discovery, project similarity analysis,
workforce insights, and a GraphRAG chatbot, while remaining under
development as NASA works to improve data quality, automation, and
scale.

 WHEN AI'S HUMAN IN THE LOOP REALLY ISN'T (6 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Many organizations claim to have human oversight of AI when reviewers
can observe or flag decisions but cannot actually stop or override
them. Effective human-in-the-loop controls require the right
expertise, context, and authority, and need to be tested like any
other operational control.
