NEWS & TRENDS

 MICROSOFT TESTS NEW MAI REALTIME VOICE MODEL (2 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Microsoft appears to be testing MAI Realtime, its first native
real-time voice model, through a hidden early-access listing in MAI
Playground. The bidirectional system supports simultaneous listening
and speaking, multilingual conversations, configurable turn-taking,
two voices, and low-latency interruptions, but it is not yet announced
for broad release. The model could eventually reduce Microsoft's
reliance on OpenAI's GPT-Realtime for voice experiences.

 DATA CENTER APPROVALS IN TEXAS HALTED UNTIL AUDITS COMPLETED, GOV.
GREG ABBOTT SAYS (6 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Texas has paused approvals for new data centers seeking connections
to the state power grid while regulators audit their electricity and
water use, tax incentives, cooling systems, ownership and community
impact. ERCOT has more than 1,800 large projects requesting 474
gigawatts of power, with data centers accounting for roughly 90% of
the new demand.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 AI GOVERNANCE IS REPEATING CYBERSECURITY'S MISTAKES (10 MINUTE READ)
[10]

 AI governance is repeating cybersecurity's early mistakes by relying
on vendor-controlled evaluations, weak incident detection, and
voluntary disclosure. Disclosures involving OpenAI and Anthropic in
July allegedly showed models escaping evaluation environments and
accessing external systems, underscoring the need for independent
evaluator oversight, continuously verified containment, monitoring,
and reporting requirements.

 PALANTIR EARNINGS WILL TEST THE REAL SHAPE OF ENTERPRISE AI (8 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 Palantir's upcoming earnings will reveal whether enterprise AI is
becoming operational infrastructure rather than remaining trapped in
pilots. Its rapid growth is driven largely by existing customers
expanding usage and by forward-deployed engineers embedded in
organizations, but that model also creates a sovereignty paradox:
Palantir warns against dependence on AI providers while deepening
customers' dependence on Palantir.

LAUNCHES & PARTNERSHIPS

 OPENAI PRESENCE (3 MINUTE READ) [12]

 OpenAI Presence is an enterprise offering intended to make AI agents
reliable for production use in customer service and internal
workflows. Forward Deployed Engineers help customers select workflows,
connect systems, establish guidelines, test deployments, and launch
them. Presence is limited to qualifying enterprises and its compliance
approach remains unclear.

 SENTINELONE MAKES THE AUTONOMOUS SOC TRUSTWORTHY WITH GOVERNED,
CLOSED-LOOP RESPONSE (4 MINUTE READ) [13]

 SentinelOne launched governed, closed-loop response across its
Singularity Platform, allowing Purple AI to investigate alerts, reach
verdicts and automatically execute response actions within limits set
by security teams. Every action is traceable, reversible, and can
require human approval, while the underlying agentic investigation
system is already processing more than 8,500 critical investigations
per day. The response capabilities are expected to become generally
available later this quarter.

MISCELLANEOUS

 YOUR JSON IS LYING TO YOU (23 MINUTE READ) [14]

 JavaScript's familiar JSON serialization round trip can silently
alter data rather than preserve it. Unsafe integers lose precision,
undefined properties disappear, dates become strings, and values such
as NaN become null. Instead, one should define explicit wire
representations (such as decimal strings for exact integers) at
application boundaries.

 ASANA'S AI AGENTS SHARE MEMORY ACROSS YOUR COMPANY, BUT NOT YOUR
SECRETS (5 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Asana's Agentic Work Management system gives AI agents shared
organizational context through its Work Graph, allowing them to retain
knowledge about tasks, projects, and company processes across
interactions. Memories remain tied to the permissions of their source
data, while model routing automatically selects different AI models
based on a task's complexity.
