ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 GARTNER: WHY THE CLOUD IS HOLDING BACK AGENTIC AI (5 MINUTE READ)
[11]

 According to Gartner, traditional centralized cloud architectures are
holding back the agentic AI revolution by failing to provide the
continuous, high-frequency inference needed for real-time enterprise
workflows. Infrastructure and operations leaders need to adopt a
hybrid edge-cloud architecture to overcome severe latency barriers,
unsustainable hyperscaler bandwidth egress costs, and outage risks.

 MICROSOFT SAYS AI ADOPTION ALONE WON'T TRANSFORM ENTERPRISES (6
MINUTE READ) [12]

 Microsoft says its own AI rollout showed that deploying tools broadly
is not enough. Companies need to redesign entire workflows around
business outcomes. Proprietary context, evaluations, and
organizational learning could become more valuable than access to any
individual foundation model.

 AI AGENTS ARE TURNING RETRIEVAL INTO A MAJOR INFRASTRUCTURE PROBLEM
(5 MINUTE READ) [13]

 As agents perform longer tasks, they increasingly need reliable
access to enterprise data, past decisions, and memory across many
retrieval steps. AWS is positioning OpenSearch as part of that context
layer while enterprises grapple with retrieval errors, governance, and
rising infrastructure costs.

 Dataminr Advanced for Corporate Security introduces agentic AI
capabilities designed to automatically corroborate, contextualize, and

 INTRODUCING AGENTIC BATCH CHANGES (4 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Sourcegraph has launched Agentic Batch Changes, a frontier coding
agent designed to execute codebase-wide modifications across hundreds
or thousands of repositories. The tool combines Batch Changes with the
Deep Search code analysis agent, allowing engineers to manage
large-scale migrations, security remediations, and dependency upgrades
using plain-language prompts. Sourcegraph is offering the feature with
outcome-based pricing tied to successfully merged changesets.

toward continuously operating development environments.

MISCELLANEOUS

 THE TWO AI GATEWAY PATTERNS IN PRODUCTION INFERENCE (10 MINUTE READ)
[19]

 There are two dominant architectural patterns for AI gateways in
production inference: access gateways that route outbound requests
from applications to multiple model providers, and serving gateways
that manage inbound customer traffic for organizations operating their
own models. Serving gateways act as an essential control layer for
identity, routing, protection, metering, and auditing when exposing
proprietary models commercially.

 GOOGLE CLOUD AND SALESFORCE CONNECT GEMINI ENTERPRISE WITH AGENTFORCE
(3 MINUTE READ) [20]

 Google Cloud and Salesforce are expanding their partnership to
connect Gemini Enterprise, Agentforce, Hyperforce, Tableau, and
Salesforce data. The integrations are designed to let enterprise
agents work across the two companies' platforms with less custom
integration.
