NEWS & TRENDS

 GOOGLE MEET HARDWARE DROPPING CHROMEOS FOR ANDROID (3 MINUTE READ)
[4]

 Google is transitioning new Google Meet room hardware from ChromeOS
to Android, opening the door to more device types and hardware that
can potentially support multiple conferencing platforms. Existing
ChromeOS-based Meet hardware will continue receiving support, with
newer devices covered through September 2030.

 PALO ALTO NETWORKS BUYS CONSOLE TO BOOST AGENTIC SECURITY (2 MINUTE
READ) [5]

 Palo Alto Networks acquired AI-native ITSM and automation startup
Console to expand the agentic capabilities of its Cortex platform.
Console connects to identity systems, SaaS apps, and security tools,
letting AI agents gather context, execute workflows, and document
actions - another sign that IT automation and security operations are
increasingly converging.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 J-SPACE: THE LOGS AI SECURITY HAS NEVER HAD (7 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Anthropic researchers reportedly found a small internal workspace in
Claude, called the J-space, whose activity can reveal concepts related
to the model's reasoning before they appear in its output. Exposing
this telemetry would give security teams a third detection layer for
AI agents, while acknowledging that the J-lens used to read it is
imperfect and currently unavailable to enterprises.

 Q&A: SERVAL CEO DESCRIBES BUILDING AN ‘AI-NATIVE' ALTERNATIVE TO
SERVICENOW (10 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Serval argues that AI-native ITSM can make automating a repetitive
support task faster than handling the ticket manually, using natural
language to generate code-based workflows. The bigger argument is
architectural: legacy ITSM vendors can add agents, but still have to
contend with years of accumulated workflows, custom tables, and
configuration that newer platforms were built without.

 WHO GETS TO DECIDE? THE CIO AND THE NEW ARCHITECTURE OF ENTERPRISE
AUTHORITY (9 MINUTE READ) [8]

 As AI shifts from recommending actions to actually executing them,
traditional technology governance based on security, reliability, and
architecture is no longer enough. CIOs increasingly need to define
what authority automated systems can exercise, under what conditions,
and with what accountability.

 INTRODUCING AZURE MULTICLOUD INTERCONNECT FOR AWS (3 MINUTE READ) [9]


 Microsoft has announced a collaboration with Amazon Web Services to
simplify private networking between Microsoft Azure and AWS. The
solution combines Azure Multicloud Interconnect with AWS Interconnect,
enabling customers to provision high-performance connectivity through
an integrated, cloud-native experience built on open API
specifications.

 MICROSOFT INTUNE NOW LETS ADMINS TARGET DEVICES BASED ON SPECIFIC OS
VERSIONS (3 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Microsoft's Intune 2608 release adds generally available OS-version
assignment filters, giving admins finer control over staged app and
policy deployments. It also introduces unattended Remote Help for
Windows devices and a redesigned device-management experience.

MISCELLANEOUS

 COMMISSION DESIGNATES CHATGPT, REDDIT, ROBLOX UNDER DIGITAL SERVICES
ACT (2 MINUTE READ) [11]

 The European Commission designated ChatGPT as a Very Large Online
Search Engine under the Digital Services Act, while Reddit and Roblox
were classified as Very Large Online Platforms. The companies now have
four months to comply with additional obligations around systemic risk
assessments, mitigation, transparency, and independent audits.

 CITRIX ACQUIRES NUMECENT TO MODERNIZE WINDOWS APPLICATION DELIVERY AT
SCALE (3 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Citrix has acquired Numecent, whose Cloudpaging technology packages
Windows apps into isolated containers that can be streamed to
endpoints without modifying the underlying OS image. Citrix says the
approach can simplify app packaging, reduce image sprawl, and make
legacy applications easier to manage across physical desktops, DaaS,
Windows 365, and Azure Virtual Desktop.
