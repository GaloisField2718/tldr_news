NEWS & TRENDS

 CITRIX URGES ADMINS TO PATCH NEW NETSCALER FLAWS ASAP (2 MINUTE READ)
[5]

 Citrix has warned customers to patch two NetScaler vulnerabilities
that could enable remote authentication bypass or denial-of-service
attacks under specific configurations. The company recommends
upgrading affected NetScaler ADC and NetScaler Gateway appliances to
specified builds, although it says neither flaw has been observed
being exploited. Administrators can inspect configuration strings to
determine exposure.

 SLACK WANTS TO DRAG AI CODING OUT OF THE TERMINAL AND INTO THE GROUP
CHAT (8 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Slack Code brings coding agents, including Claude Code, Devin, GitHub
Copilot, and Vercel, directly into shared channels where teams can
prompt, review, and steer their work together. Agents operate with the
invoking user's permissions, while code channels preserve diffs,
previews and activity as a searchable record of the work.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 THE SLEEP-PROMOTING RESILIENCE OF BORING INFRASTRUCTURE (6 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 Conservative, “boring” infrastructure can improve resilience
while reducing operational complexity. Migrating CGI scripts to
FastCGI and primary order databases from MySQL to SQLite reported
better performance and simpler backups.

 FOR ENTERPRISES, THE CAUTIOUS AI ERA HAS BEGUN (11 MINUTE READ) [8]

 The enterprise AI conversation is shifting from broad experimentation
toward tighter scrutiny of cost, governance, workforce impact and
actual business value. Companies are increasingly narrowing
deployments around measurable use cases as token consumption and
usage-based pricing make unrestricted AI adoption harder to justify.

LAUNCHES & PARTNERSHIPS

 NANOCLAW COMES TO SLACK, LETTING YOU CREATE PERSISTENT AI AGENT TEAMS
AND COLLEAGUES FROM A SINGLE MESSAGE (11 MINUTE READ) [9]

 NanoClaw's new Slack integration lets users create entire teams of
persistent AI agents from a single message, with each agent getting
its own identity, memory, permissions, tools, and Slack presence. The
agents remain self-hosted on customer infrastructure and can
collaborate through Slack channels and Canvases, positioning Slack
itself as a workspace where employees can dynamically create and
manage specialized digital coworkers.

 SERVAL'S SUPER AGENT CATALYST CREATES ROVING BACKGROUND AGENTS TO
IDENTIFY AND FIX IT ISSUES BEFORE THEY'RE TICKETED (10 MINUTE READ)
[10]

 Serval has made Catalyst generally available, an admin-facing AI
agent that analyzes ticket history, SOPs, and connected systems to
identify repetitive IT work and build the workflows needed to automate
it. It can also create background agents that proactively detect
issues across systems and propose fixes before employees submit
tickets, with generated automations remaining subject to admin
permissions and approval.

MISCELLANEOUS

 DELTA FLIGHT DISRUPTED WITH WI-FI HACK (8 MINUTE READ) [11]

 A Delta flight from Las Vegas to Atlanta was disrupted after someone
allegedly interfered with the plane's Wi-Fi and created a rogue
network called “Delta WiFi Fast” that led passengers to a
phishing-style login page. Federal authorities are investigating. The
incident happened shortly after Black Hat and DEF CON. No perpetrator
has been identified.

 REMOTE MCP, OAUTH, AND ENTERPRISE AUTH (14 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Model Context Protocol (MCP) is transitioning from local standard I/O
to multi-tenant cloud environments using HTTP and standard
authentication. Moving MCP to the cloud replaces local subprocesses
with streamable HTTP and Server-Sent Events while securing access via
OAuth 2.1 and PKCE bearer tokens.

 AS WORKPLACE SURVEILLANCE GROWS, EXPERTS SAY IT'S GOOD TO KNOW THE
WAYS YOUR EMPLOYER IS WATCHING (7 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Employee monitoring has expanded beyond traditional endpoint
management into location, communications, application activity, and
productivity data. The trend is creating a growing governance
challenge around how workplace telemetry is collected, interpreted,
and disclosed to employees.
