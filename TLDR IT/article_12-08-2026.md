NEWS & TRENDS

 RANSOMWARE ATTACKS ARE TARGETING MANAGERS AND OTHER BUSINESS LEADERS
(4 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Zscaler ThreatLabz research suggests ransomware operators are
increasingly targeting managers and other business leaders whose
business authority, relationships, and access can expose sensitive
information and processes.

 MISTRAL WANTS TO BUILD 1 GW OF EUROPEAN AI COMPUTE BY 2030 (5 MINUTE
READ) [5]

 Mistral plans to build up to 1 GW of AI compute capacity in Europe by
2030 as it pushes for more sovereign infrastructure. The company is
already seeking long-term customer commitments to support the
expansion.

 GOOGLE CLOUD LAYS OUT ITS POST-QUANTUM CRYPTOGRAPHY ROADMAP (5 MINUTE
READ) [6]

 Google Cloud details how it plans to transition its infrastructure
and services toward post-quantum cryptography. The roadmap gives
enterprises a clearer picture of how cloud providers are preparing for
future quantum-era security requirements.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 A WINNER IN EVERY CATEGORY (3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Despite a broad SaaS multiple collapse, market leaders in most
software categories continue to command substantial premiums because
investors see credible AI-driven growth and durability. CrowdStrike,
Cloudflare, Shopify, Samsara, ServiceNow, Figma, Twilio, and
Salesforce are presented as category leaders whose products provide
security, infrastructure, proprietary data, governance, design,
communications, or customer records for enterprise AI adoption.

 WHY ENTERPRISE AI PROJECTS FAIL AT THE AGENT LAYER (5 MINUTE READ)
[8]

 Enterprise AI failures increasingly stem from how agents are governed
and orchestrated rather than from the models themselves. Companies
risk losing control when they outsource too much of that reasoning
layer to vendors.

 JETBRAINS TACKLES RAPIDLY GROWING AI COSTS (2 MINUTE READ) [9]

 JetBrains detailed its early approach to measuring and controlling
AI-related infrastructure spending as usage scales. The effort
highlights AI cost management becoming a distinct operational problem
for engineering teams.

 SECURE CLAUDE ENTERPRISE WITH CISCO AI DEFENSE (5 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Cisco says its AI Defense service now integrates with Claude
Enterprise through Anthropic's inference hooks, inspecting governed
prompts and conversation transcripts before inference to block prompt
injections, jailbreaks, tool exploitation, and sensitive-data risks.
The integration works with Claude, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork,
providing inline allow-or-deny enforcement without changing users'
workflows, although inference hooks remain in beta and currently
enforce only before model execution.

 GOOGLE BRINGS GEMINI TO POSTGRESQL MIGRATIONS (4 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Google Cloud added Gemini-powered code conversion to Database
Migration Service to help migrate databases to PostgreSQL. Teams can
review AI-generated PL/pgSQL conversions alongside the original source
code before deploying them.

MISCELLANEOUS

 WHAT IS ENTERPRISE WORKFLOW AUTOMATION? (9 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Enterprise workflow automation connects systems and departments to
execute repeatable, multi-step business processes without manual
handoffs. It uses a trigger-logic-integration structure and delivers
benefits such as scalability, compliance, consistency, and citizen
development. Zapier-based examples show how teams can automate
processes across sales, marketing, customer service, and IT.

 CANVA REDESIGNS SESSION REVOCATION FOR 100M ACTIVE SESSIONS (4 MINUTE
READ) [16]

 Canva moved durable session-revocation records to Amazon S3 and
distributes compact indexes to application gateways, reducing database
dependencies at massive scale. The redesign also cut its revocation
cache memory footprint by 87.5%.
