NEWS & TRENDS

 GOOGLE IS WORKING ON PLUGINS FOR GEMINI ENTERPRISE (2 MINUTE READ)
[7]

 Google appears to be developing Plugins and a dedicated Notifications
area for Gemini Enterprise, according to unfinished interface
elements. Plugins may package reusable Skills and Connectors into
multi-step workplace workflows, while Notifications would collect
completed results from background tasks. Google has not announced
either feature or provided a rollout timeline.

 FEDS GET 3 DAYS TO PATCH N-ABLE GOD MODE FLAW UNDER ACTIVE EXPLOIT (4
MINUTE READ) [8]

 CISA has ordered federal agencies to patch an actively exploited
N-able N-central vulnerability that can give attackers remote
administrative access to an MSP's management console. Successful
attacks have allowed access to managed customer systems and the
creation of persistent Cloudflare tunnels. All versions before
N-central 2026.3.1.7 are affected.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 AWS IS FAILING FAST. CAN ITS CUSTOMERS KEEP UP? (6 MINUTE READ) [9]

 AWS has placed Amazon Q Business, Amazon Kendra, Bedrock Agents
Classic, and nine SageMaker AI capabilities into maintenance mode,
blocking new customers while continuing access and support for
existing users. This is a rational portfolio correction driven by
changing technology and adoption, but AWS can reallocate resources
faster than enterprise customers can migrate from services in which
they have invested.

 THE ENTERPRISE AI STRATEGY THAT OUTLASTS ANY SINGLE MODEL (5 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Enterprises should avoid tying their AI strategy to a single provider
as models, capabilities, and pricing continue to leapfrog one another.
A model-agnostic architecture that can swap underlying models and
compound improvements at the system level would allow organizations to
benefit from advances across OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and others.

 YOUR TEAM ISN'T IGNORING SECURITY. THEY'RE JUST UNDERWATER (5 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 Cloud security tools can generate more findings than teams can
realistically address, leaving vulnerabilities and misconfigurations
open despite plenty of visibility. The article recommends a recurring
triage process with clear ownership and business context to prioritize
meaningful risks and keep remediation moving.

LAUNCHES & PARTNERSHIPS

 SIXB: THE OPERATING LAYER FOR ENTERPRISE AI (5 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Sixb introduces an operational framework for enterprise AI that
connects business data, models, rules, permissions, and workflows in a
shared environment for teams and AI agents. The framework is intended
to let agents act within controlled business processes rather than
merely answer prompted questions. Version 0.1.0 includes data
connectors, automation, agent tooling, security controls, storage
options, and custom application support.

 SNAPLOGIC INTRODUCES AGENTIC ASSISTANT FOR DATA INTEGRATION (2 MINUTE
READ) [13]

 SnapLogic has upgraded SnapGPT from an integration copilot into an
agentic assistant that can plan, build, explain, validate, and
troubleshoot enterprise data integrations through natural language.
The assistant also provides administrators with activity logs and
production monitoring insights for AI-assisted integration work.

MISCELLANEOUS

 REMOVING THE SECURITY BARRIER TO AGENTFORCE ADOPTION (6 MINUTE READ)
[14]

 Salesforce's Private Connect platform removes a security and
connectivity barrier to enterprise adoption of Agentforce and other
services by providing audited, encrypted private paths that avoid the
public internet. Architectural redesigns and automation reduced
provisioning from weeks to under 30 minutes. The platform now handles
approximately 120 TB of data and 683 million requests monthly across
15 AWS regions.

 AI AGENTS GET BETTER AT IT OPS, BUT ONLY WITH HUMANS IN THE LOOP (6
MINUTE READ) [15]

 A study of nearly 150,000 agent actions across 40 companies found AI
now performs about one-third of IT workflow actions, primarily routine
and reversible tasks, while humans retain control over higher-risk
decisions. Agent performance improved through repeated human feedback,
but identity, onboarding, and offboarding tasks failed most often
because of outdated data, missing accounts, and unreliable
integrations.
