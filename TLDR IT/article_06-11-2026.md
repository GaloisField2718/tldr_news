NEWS & TRENDS

 WHAT'S NEW ACROSS GOOGLE'S AGENTIC DATA CLOUD (8 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Google Cloud has announced major updates to its Agentic Data Cloud,
introducing features like a borderless Lakehouse powered by Apache
Iceberg, enhanced Knowledge Catalog context curation, and specialized
infrastructure for scaling AI agents. These capabilities aim to give
agents secure, real-time access to distributed enterprise data while
lowering token costs and improving query performance. The
announcements also include new development kits and integrated Gemini
tools designed to assist both data practitioners and business users.

 MICROSOFT EXPANDS UNPUBLISHED COPILOT CHAT LIMITS (3 MINUTE READ) [8]


 Microsoft is expanding usage caps for commercial Copilot Chat users
without a paid Microsoft 365 Copilot license, but won't disclose the
limits or let administrators adjust them. The rollout began November 2
for smaller organizations and reaches those with more than 2,000 seats
November 9.

 APPLE REPORTEDLY CUTS EMPLOYEE AI BUDGETS (3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Apple is reportedly reducing employee allowances for third-party AI
tools, with proposed daily caps of $100 for engineers and $25 for
other staff. Some teams are also being encouraged to use cheaper or
locally hosted models, although Apple hasn't confirmed the changes.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 AI JUST RAISED THE INTEREST RATE ON YOUR TECHNICAL DEBT (4 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 AI-assisted vulnerability discovery effectively raises the interest
rate on technical debt by making exploits much cheaper and faster to
develop. While finding bugs has become vastly accelerated, repairing
them still moves at human speed, creating a dangerous asymmetry for
organizations. Leadership teams should look into auditing deferred
security tickets, sunsetting unused internet-facing systems, and
reviewing accepted-risk registers on a regular schedule.

 WHO CHECKS THE BORING LAYER (9 MINUTE READ) [11]

 While high-profile security incidents often involve complex exploits,
the underlying entry points frequently rely on basic vulnerabilities
like missing access controls, unpatched firmware, and exposed
credentials. Coding tools and internal agents can exacerbate risks if
the surrounding access layer and operational security are not strictly
managed and reviewed.

LAUNCHES & PARTNERSHIPS

 INTRODUCING MARIADB ENTERPRISE PLATFORM 2027: ONE FOUNDATION FOR THE
AGENTIC ENTERPRISE (9 MINUTE READ) [12]

 MariaDB has introduced its Enterprise Platform 2027, a unified data
foundation designed for AI agents and enterprise workloads. The
platform combines transactions, real-time analytics, vector search,
and AI tooling such as Agent Memory and Semantic Cache to reduce
latency and token costs. Additionally, it delivers a 56 percent
increase in peak throughput compared to version 11.8 and introduces
multi-region active-active scalability via MariaDB Raft Cluster.

 CONFLUENT BRINGS LIVE SQL QUERIES TO STREAMING DATA (5 MINUTE READ)
[13]

 Confluent made Lightning Tables generally available on AWS, letting
applications query continuously updated Kafka data through SQL and
APIs without maintaining a separate serving database. The launch
supports its Streamhouse architecture, which combines streaming,
processing, governance, and querying to give applications and AI
agents current business context.

MISCELLANEOUS

 HOW TO USE TEST-DRIVEN DEVELOPMENT WITH AI AGENTS (WITHOUT LOWERING
CODE QUALITY) (13 MINUTE READ) [14]

 A new empirical workflow called EXACT combines Test-Driven
Development with agentic AI tools while maintaining code quality. The
approach utilizes example mapping for collaborative human modeling,
natural language test lists, and predictive TDD. Experiments show that
this method significantly improves correctness and code quality
compared to unguided vibe coding or plain TDD prompts.

 GOOGLE EXPLAINS WHY ITS LONDON BACKUP GENERATORS FAILED (3 MINUTE
READ) [15]

 Google's newly detailed postmortem attributes its October London
outage to a voltage-sensing flaw and controller misconfiguration that
prevented backup generators from starting. One power feed was already
undergoing repairs when the other failed, leaving battery backups to
run out before power could be restored.
