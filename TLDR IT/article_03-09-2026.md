NEWS & TRENDS

 META IS SWITCHING TO SLACK BECAUSE IT SAYS IT'S BETTER FOR AI AGENTS
(3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Meta is moving its internal communications from Google Chat to Slack,
with AI chief Alexandr Wang citing Slack's conversational interface,
developer tooling, and third-party integrations as better suited to AI
agents. The move is notable because collaboration platforms are
increasingly becoming the control layer through which employees and
agents interact with enterprise systems.

 ATLASSIAN'S USAGE-BASED PRICING: AI VALUE WITH PREDICTABILITY AND
CONTROL (4 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Atlassian is expanding usage-based pricing across Rovo credits,
automation steps, and autonomous AI-agent resolutions, with billing
beginning December 3. Admins will get centralized usage visibility,
alerts, and spending limits, making AI and automation consumption
another part of SaaS cost management alongside licenses and seats.

 DROPBOX ACCOUNTS BREACHED THROUGH LENOVO EMAIL VERIFICATION FLAW (4
MINUTE READ) [10]

 Attackers accessed Dropbox accounts by exploiting a flaw in Lenovo's
email verification process to create fraudulent Lenovo IDs tied to
victims' email addresses, allowing them to authenticate without the
Dropbox password. Roughly 5,000 accounts were reportedly affected,
making the incident a useful reminder that federated identity risk can
extend well beyond your primary IdP.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 WHY CISCO IS REDEFINING ITS CIO ROLE (6 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Cisco says the profile of a successful CIO is changing from someone
focused primarily on applications, integrations, and UX to someone
capable of redesigning how the company operates. Its EVP of operations
argues that process transformation, AI governance, and deciding where
agents should execute work are becoming increasingly central to the
job.

 REZOLVE AI SHOWS WHY ENTERPRISE SALES STILL NEEDS MIDDLEMEN (8 MINUTE
READ) [12]

 Enterprise AI companies increasingly need cloud providers,
distributors, consultancies, and systems integrators to reach large
customers because those partners already have contracts, approvals,
and delivery teams. Rezolve Ai illustrates the trade-off: its
first-half revenue rose to $130.8 million from $6.3 million after
acquisitions and expansion, while gross margin fell to 48.9%, and the
company has not disclosed how much partner relationships contribute.

LAUNCHES & PARTNERSHIPS

 DEVELOPING ENTERPRISE FRONTIER SAFEGUARDS WITH OUR CUSTOMERS (9
MINUTE READ) [13]

 Anthropic's Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS) is a solution
designed to combine zero data retention with automated detection of
serious misuse of frontier models. EFS stores monitoring data in
customer-controlled cloud infrastructure and sends detected signals to
customer teams, with phased rollout planned for later this fall and
interim ZDR for eligible customers using Fable 5 and Fable 5.1.

 ADOBE IS MAKING ITS TOOLS AVAILABLE IN SLACK (3 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Adobe is bringing more than 70 tools from products including
Photoshop, Acrobat, Premiere, Illustrator, Express, and Firefly into
Slack through an MCP integration with Slackbot. Business+ and
Enterprise+ users can use Slack conversations and files as context
while creating or modifying content without leaving Slack.

 AUTOMATE DRIVE, GMAIL, AND GOOGLE CHAT ACTIONS WITH NEW STEPS IN
WORKSPACE STUDIO (3 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Google Workspace Studio is adding automation steps that can copy or
move Drive files, reply to Gmail threads, and post replies in Google
Chat. Importantly for IT, admins can disable individual actions and
require user approval when workflows could share data outside the
organization.

 ANALYZING CROSSRAT (17 MINUTE READ) [16]

 This is a technical analysis of CrossRAT, a Java-based multiplatform
surveillance implant associated with the Dark Caracal cyber-espionage
campaign. It examines the malware's package structure,
operating-system detection, intended persistence mechanisms,
command-and-control communications, and capabilities, including
file-system manipulation, screenshots, and arbitrary DLL execution on
Windows.

 MICROSOFT DEFENDER FLAGS LEGITIMATE GOOGLE SEARCH LINKS AS MALICIOUS
(2 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Microsoft is investigating a Defender for Office 365 Safe Links issue
incorrectly identifying legitimate Google search URLs as malicious.
The bug can block users from opening links and generate related
incidents in Defender and Sentinel, potentially creating both helpdesk
noise and false-positive alert volume for IT teams.
