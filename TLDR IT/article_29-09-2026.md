NEWS & TRENDS

 OVER 16,000 SUPABASE DATABASES EXPOSE PII, PASSWORDS, AUTH TOKENS (3
MINUTE READ) [5]

 UpGuard identified more than 16,000 Supabase databases exposing
readable tables. Analysis of their schemas indicated that more than
half could expose personal information, with smaller subsets
potentially exposing passwords or authentication tokens. The issue is
misconfigured access controls rather than a newly discovered platform
vulnerability, reinforcing the need for database authorization,
exposure testing, and clear ownership of internally built tools.

 MICROSOFT PAUSES KB5002907 UPDATE AFTER OFFICE LICENSE DEACTIVATIONS
(4 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Microsoft has paused optional update KB5002907 after reports that it
left some Office 2016 and 2019 installations unlicensed or, rarely,
removed. Its recovery guidance is to reactivate or reinstall the
affected product. Importantly, the update makes no changes where
Microsoft 365 Apps updates are managed through Group Policy or Cloud
Update. Support teams should check update history before treating
unexpected licensing tickets as subscription or account problems.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 7 REASONS IT MANAGERS FAIL TO EXCEED YOUR EXPECTATIONS (9 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 Promoting a strong technologist does not automatically prepare them
to manage people, business relationships, or competing operational
priorities. This piece examines unclear expectations, insufficient
leadership development, and the tendency to reward technical output
without developing management skills. It is a useful operating-model
check for CIOs overseeing support, infrastructure, SaaS, and business
systems: define what managers own, coach them on business outcomes,
and provide advancement paths for specialists who should remain
individual contributors.

 THE SAASPOCALYPSE THAT WASN'T, WITH ATLASSIAN CEO MIKE CANNON-BROOKES
(40 MINUTE READ) [8]

 In this Decoder interview, Mike Cannon-Brookes explains why Atlassian
combined internal IT and AI enablement under its people leadership:
employee adoption and changes to business systems are tightly
connected. He also argues that AI will change SaaS workflows without
eliminating the need for structured systems and human judgment. For
CIOs, the interesting question is who owns the combined work of
employee enablement, system integration, and AI cost management,
rather than treating each as a separate rollout.

LAUNCHES & PARTNERSHIPS

 ANTHROPIC TURNS CLAUDE INTO AN AI MARKETPLACE WITH 2,000+ PLUGINS AND
CONNECTORS (3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Anthropic's marketplace brings together more than 2,000 plugins and
connectors, partner-built agents, and services, with eligible partner
software purchasable using a portion of committed Anthropic spending.
For an internal AI platform team, the significant change is the
growing catalog of connections between assistants and business
systems, along with another procurement channel to manage. Marketplace
availability should not replace reviewing permissions, data access,
integration ownership, and how access will be removed when someone
leaves.

 WORKDAY LAUNCHES TOTAL BENEFITS, BRINGING HEALTH, WEALTH, AND
WELLBEING SUPPORT TOGETHER IN ONE PLACE (5 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Workday has announced Total Benefits, combining benefits-provider
integrations, administration services, and employee guidance in one
offering. Workday Wellness is now available to US customers, while
benefits guidance through its Self-Service Agent is expected to become
generally available in October. Evaluation should cover provider
compatibility, sensitive employee-data sharing, and whether the new
workflows can replace existing portals and manual administration
rather than simply adding another layer.

MISCELLANEOUS

 HOW TO DISABLE BITLOCKER IN WINDOWS 11 (AND WHEN YOU SHOULDN'T) (6
MINUTE READ) [11]

 Disabling BitLocker starts decrypting the drive, while suspending
protection leaves the data encrypted but temporarily makes the
encryption key accessible. This walkthrough explains the distinction
and why short-term firmware maintenance or troubleshooting often calls
for suspension rather than removing encryption entirely. The
operational takeaway is to make resuming and verifying protection part
of the endpoint maintenance runbook, not optional cleanup after the
support ticket closes.

 MICROSOFT ADDS PAY-AS-YOU-GO PRICING FOR EXTRA ONEDRIVE STORAGE (2
MINUTE READ) [12]

 Microsoft 365 customers can now use consumption-based billing when
users exceed their licensed OneDrive storage instead of purchasing
additional capacity upfront. Admins choose which users are eligible
and can set budgets and alerts. Additional storage costs
$0.20/GB/month. Enterprise SaaS is shifting toward usage-based
pricing, giving IT more flexibility but also making cost monitoring
part of day-to-day platform management.
