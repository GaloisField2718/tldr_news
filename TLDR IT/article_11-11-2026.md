NEWS & TRENDS

 CHATGPT ENTERPRISE NOW AVAILABLE IN AWS MARKETPLACE (2 MINUTE READ)
[5]

 OpenAI has made ChatGPT Enterprise available through the AWS
Marketplace, allowing organizations to purchase and manage the AI
product using their existing AWS accounts and billing relationships.
Customers gain access to the same models and features available
directly from OpenAI while being able to apply eligible spend toward
AWS commitments. Additionally, eligible customers can access
specialized offerings such as ChatGPT for Healthcare and ChatGPT for
Financial Services.

 MICROSOFT PRESSES TRUMP TO SCUTTLE BAN ON CHINESE DATA CENTER TECH (4
MINUTE READ) [6]

 Microsoft and industry groups are lobbying against a proposed US ban
on new Chinese optical transceivers used in data centers. They warn
that alternative suppliers lack capacity to replace Chinese vendors,
potentially delaying construction and leaving purchased AI chips idle.
The restrictions are still under consideration.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 PANIC OVER CLOUD CAPACITY IS OUT OF HAND (6 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Claims that AWS and other cloud providers have sold out of AI
capacity through 2027 exaggerate infrastructure shortages and create
unnecessary concern for enterprises. While GPU, power, and
infrastructure constraints affect large-scale AI training, standard
inference and fine-tuning workloads remain feasible. Enterprises
should distinguish between workload types, verify capacity directly
with cloud providers, and build flexible architectures rather than
react to market hype.

 IT LEADERS SEEK ALTERNATIVES FOR SAP, VMWARE, AND JAVA (9 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Licensing increases and support deadlines are prompting IT leaders to
explore replacements for SAP, VMware, and Oracle Java. Executives warn
that integrations, training, disruption, and parallel operations can
outweigh license savings. They recommend pricing a realistic exit
before negotiations and distinguishing dissatisfaction from a workable
migration plan.

 CISCO AI DEFENSE TO ADD ANTHROPIC'S CLAUDE MYTHOS FOR AI RED TEAMING
(4 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Cisco is integrating Anthropic's Claude Mythos model into its AI
Defense platform to enhance adaptive red teaming for enterprise AI
agents. The integration is designed to help enterprises identify
security vulnerabilities and behavioral risks in agentic AI
applications through controlled, authorized testing.

 EXPANDING GEMINI IN GMAIL TO MORE GOOGLE WORKSPACE USERS (2 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 Google is expanding email-thread summaries and non-personalized Help
me write to all Workspace users aged 18 or older with Gmail smart
features enabled. These features are on by default, with user and
admin controls to disable them. Rollout began on November 10 and could
take up to 15 days.

 APPTIO EXPANDS PORTFOLIO TO HELP ENTERPRISES CONNECT AI SPENDING TO
BUSINESS VALUE (4 MINUTE READ) [12]

 IBM expanded Apptio's AI spending tools and Kubecost's Kubernetes
network-cost reporting. Available capabilities include AI usage
connectors, financial planning for AI services, and more detailed AWS
NAT Gateway, cross-zone, and cross-region cost visibility. The
announcement also covers planned MCP servers and scenario-planning
features, so availability varies across the portfolio.

MISCELLANEOUS

 USE DOCKER SANDBOXES AS YOUR DEVELOPMENT ENVIRONMENT (12 MINUTE READ)
[13]

 Docker Sandboxes provide isolated microVM-based development
environments for building software projects, such as books using
Pandoc and XeLaTeX, without cluttering the host system. Developers can
use the sbx CLI to create sandboxes, configure network policies, and
install necessary toolchains and dependencies. They can also preserve
and reproduce these environments through snapshots or declarative
Dockerfiles.

 ROYAL AUSTRALASIAN COLLEGE OF SURGEONS TARGETS AU$2.1 MILLION IN
SAVINGS WITH HPE PRIVATE CLOUD (4 MINUTE READ) [14]

 HPE says RACS lowered its virtualization licensing bill by as much as
70% and reduced its fleet from eight servers to four. The migration
followed a renewal quote that was roughly 300% higher. RACS projects a
total AU$2.1 million benefit over five years, including planned
infrastructure and disaster recovery improvements.
