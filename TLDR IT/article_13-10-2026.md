NEWS & TRENDS

 ØRSTED BUILDS COMPLIANCE INTO AUTOMATION, SPEEDS FIREWALL CHANGES (7
MINUTE READ) [8]

 Energy company Ørsted automated firewall change management using
Nautobot, reducing some requests from weeks to around 10 minutes. The
system incorporates policy validation, approvals, and audit trails,
demonstrating how network automation can improve speed without
sacrificing compliance.

 CYBERATTACK SHUTS DOWN REFRIGERATORS AT FRENCH SUPERMARKETS (4 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 Attackers reportedly manipulated refrigeration controllers across
hundreds of French supermarkets, triggering defrost cycles and causing
food spoilage. The incident highlights how compromises of operational
technology and third-party managed systems can create immediate
physical-world disruptions.

 MICROSOFT BLAMES A CONFIGURATION CHANGE FOR EXCHANGE ADMIN CENTER
TUMBLE (3 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Microsoft rolled back a configuration change after administrators
were unable to access Exchange Admin Center on Monday. Access has been
restored, with Microsoft reviewing the change and its monitoring after
another deployment-related disruption.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 RELEASING AI-GENERATED SOFTWARE (7 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Moving toward fully AI-generated software without human code review
is feasible if supported by extreme constraints and automated testing,
similar to arguments made for self-driving cars. Existing legal
frameworks and liability models, which often target human drivers or
developers when things go wrong, are poorly equipped to handle
automated systems where no one actually wrote the code.

 BELOW THE FRONTIER: THE BATTLE FOR GOOD-ENOUGH AI (10 MINUTE READ)
[12]

 While consumer AI agents and frontier coding models capture current
headlines and capital, the most significant future revenue battle lies
below the frontier in mainstream enterprise knowledge work. As
open-weight and cheaper cloud or on-device models become capable of
handling bounded tasks, a massive market will emerge for everyday
business applications. Winning this "Switzerland" layer requires
navigating complex trade-offs in data sovereignty, local versus cloud
inference, and seamless software integration.

 12 STANDOUT CIO SKILLS EVERY COMPANY WANTS (12 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Technology expertise is only part of the job: IT leaders also need
financial judgment, operational understanding, clear communication,
and the ability to build effective teams. This roundup emphasizes
connecting investments to measurable business results and making
deliberate trade-offs instead of allowing every request to become
another backlog item.

LAUNCHES & PARTNERSHIPS

 INTRODUCING THE ANTHROPIC CYBER MISSION (7 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Anthropic has launched the Critical Infrastructure Defense Program to
help protect operational technology environments in sectors like water
systems, power grids, and transportation. The initiative partners
Anthropic with security firms and manufacturers including CrowdShield,
Dragos, Nozomi Networks, and Rockwell Automation to deploy its Claude
AI models and threat research for vulnerability assessment and
patching. Additionally, the company introduced the OSS Scanner service
to provide automated vulnerability reports to open-source software
maintainers.

 RASPBERRY PI DESKTOP CATCHES UP WITH DEBIAN 13 AFTER 4-YEAR WAIT (2
MINUTE READ) [15]

 Raspberry Pi's lightweight desktop operating system for PCs and Intel
Macs has returned with a Debian 13-based release. It includes a
Wayland desktop and Raspberry Pi Connect remote access, but requires a
fresh installation and supports only 64-bit x86 hardware, not Apple
Silicon Macs.

 WHY WEBMCP MATTERS (8 MINUTE READ) [16]

 WebMCP is a crucial primitive for enabling AI agents to interact with
the web through co-browsing models. By allowing sites to provide
direct JavaScript tools with explicit schemas, WebMCP offers
significantly faster and cheaper agentic interactions compared to
traditional DOM or screenshot parsing. Furthermore, imperative WebMCP
tools are necessary to complement declarative HTML semantics and
effectively handle multi-step user tasks.

 CELEBRATING THE BRILLIANT PRAGMATISM OF TCP/IP, THE WIRING OF THE
WORLD (5 MINUTE READ) [17]

 A look at why TCP/IP has survived decades of extraordinary growth
while so many competing technologies disappeared. The argument centers
on open standards, interoperability, and practical incremental
improvements rather than requiring everyone to replace everything at
once.
