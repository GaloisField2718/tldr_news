 NVIDIA IS REPORTEDLY BUYING HUGGING FACE FOR $12.9B (4 MINUTE READ)

 Nvidia has reportedly agreed to acquire Hugging Face for $12.9
billion, though conflicting reports say a deal may not yet be signed.
The acquisition would give Nvidia control of one of the largest
open-source AI ecosystems and another route into cloud compute.

 AMAZON TRIPLES ITS NVIDIA GPU ORDER WITH 2 MILLION MORE CHIPS (4
MINUTE READ) [5]

 AWS plans to add another 2 million Nvidia GPUs, including Blackwell
Ultra and Rubin systems, after demand exceeded its earlier million-GPU
commitment. Nvidia's models and broader software stack will also
expand across Bedrock and SageMaker.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 THE COORDINATION TAX IN YOUR SOFTWARE ESTATE (6 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Large companies with proprietary software should reconsider polyrepo
defaults because repository boundaries create recurring coordination
costs across ownership, backlogs, releases, and controls. A monorepo
can make related software cheaper to change, improve reuse and enable
broader AI productivity, but the business case should be based on
measured coordination work and must account for migration, tooling,
ownership, and access-control costs.

 NVIDIA'S AI DEMAND IS STARTING TO LOOK SUPPLY-CONSTRAINED (5 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 Nvidia expects demand to continue outstripping supply as AI
infrastructure spending expands across hyperscalers, enterprises,
governments, and AI labs. Persistent constraints could increasingly
shape enterprise pricing, procurement timelines, and architecture
decisions.

 DUN & BRADSTREET BRINGS COMMERCIAL GRAPH TO PERPLEXITY (3 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Dun & Bradstreet is partnering with Perplexity to integrate its D&B
Commercial Graph and risk analytics into Perplexity and the Perplexity
Computer agent platform through Model Context Protocol servers. The
integration gives business users access to verified company
identities, ownership relationships, risk intelligence, and auditable
data for workflows including KYC/KYB, procurement, finance,
compliance, and sales.

 ANTHROPIC SIGNS $45B AI COMPUTE DEAL WITH NSCALE (2 MINUTE READ) [11]


 Anthropic will reportedly lease 460MW of AI capacity from Nscale's
West Virginia campus in a six-year, $45 billion agreement. The
deployment will use Nvidia Vera Rubin systems and is expected to come
online in late 2027.
