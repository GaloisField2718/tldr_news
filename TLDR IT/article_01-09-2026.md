NEWS & TRENDS

 ATLASSIAN BETS BIG ON INDIA AS AI CHANGES SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT (4
MINUTE READ) [4]

 Atlassian is expanding its India development centre into a strategic
R&D hub, with more than 2,700 employees and continued hiring planned
across engineering and sales. AI agents are making software creation
faster while shifting engineers' work toward directing agents,
evaluating results, and applying human judgment to architecture,
design, and product decisions.

 MICROSOFT EXCHANGE ONLINE OUTAGE CAUSES EMAIL FAILURES, AUTH ISSUES
(2 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Microsoft spent Monday battling a widespread Microsoft 365 outage
that started with Exchange Online and expanded to Teams, OneDrive,
SharePoint, Purview, and Defender XDR. Microsoft traced the disruption
to a core authentication configuration problem and was rolling out
mitigation across affected infrastructure, with users seeing gradual
recovery by late Monday.

 CURSOR CUSTOMERS WILL LOSE ACCESS TO OPENAI CODING MODELS IN NOVEMBER
(4 MINUTE READ) [6]

 OpenAI says it will stop providing its coding models to Cursor on
November 12, giving customers roughly 10 weeks to switch models or
platforms. Beyond Cursor, the bigger enterprise lesson is vendor
dependency: model availability inside AI tools can change because of
acquisitions, contracts, competition, or regulation, making
multi-model support increasingly important.

 HACKERS CLAIM MILLIONS OF PATIENT RECORDS STOLEN DURING DATA BREACH
AT HEALTHCARE GIANT MCKESSON (3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 McKesson confirmed hackers breached several cloud-hosted accounts and
stole data tied to its oncology, multispecialty, and medical-surgical
businesses. ShinyHunters claims it used phishing and social
engineering to gain access, stealing millions of rows from Snowflake
and Salesforce containing personal and highly sensitive health
information. The group reportedly demanded a $55 million ransom.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 THE BALKANIZATION OF VIRTUALIZATION WILL DE-THRONE VMWARE, WHICH
DOESN'T MIND A BIT (6 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Broadcom's strategy will gradually end VMware's dominance of server
virtualization as customers move workloads to alternatives, while
VMware focuses on a smaller base of lucrative users adopting VMware
Cloud Foundation. Three deadlines (November 22, 2026, March 31, 2027,
and October 11, 2027) are expected to accelerate exits, although
rivals generally acknowledge that VMware remains technically difficult
to match.

 WHY IT PROJECTS STILL FAIL (13 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Even conventional IT initiatives continue to miss their goals: PMI
says 31% of complex projects fail to deliver the full benefits
originally intended. The article points to familiar culprits, unclear
success metrics, weak business ownership, poor prioritization and
under-resourcing, while arguing that AI can actually amplify these
problems by accelerating output faster than organizations can review
or absorb it.

LAUNCHES & PARTNERSHIPS

 TAILSCALE EXPANDS FROM VPN INTO A FULL CONNECTIVITY PLATFORM (5
MINUTE READ) [10]

 Tailscale announced a broader connectivity platform at its Tailscale
Up conference, extending its identity-based networking beyond VPN
access into DNS filtering, privileged access management, AI agent
controls, browser-based access, open-source networking, and developer
APIs. Aperture, its AI gateway, reached general availability with
guardrails, logging, model access, and integrations for provisioning
and connecting to tailnet nodes.

 INTRODUCING DATA IMPORT FOR MICROSOFT ONEDRIVE: AN EASIER, FASTER,
AND HIGHER-FIDELITY MIGRATION TO GOOGLE WORKSPACE (3 MINUTE READ) [11]


 Google has made its advanced OneDrive migration tooling generally
available directly in the Workspace Admin console at no additional
cost. Admins can migrate multiple batches concurrently while
preserving permissions, and a planning utility estimates source-data
size and migration timelines before the move begins.

MISCELLANEOUS

 NEXT GENERATION SALESFORCE DEVOPS CENTER: GET HANDS-ON (27 MINUTE
READ) [12]

 Salesforce's Next Generation DevOps Center is now available natively
in production instances, replacing the need for its managed package
and adding AI-powered actions. This guide explains how to enable the
app and move changes through its five-step workflow: creating a Work
Item, committing changes, reviewing them, marking them ready, and
promoting them through pipeline stages.

 OPENTELEMETRY HAS GRADUATED… NOW WHAT? (5 MINUTE READ) [13]

 OpenTelemetry achieved CNCF graduated status in May, reflecting its
production adoption, governance, security, API stability,
documentation, and community health. Graduation confirms OpenTelemetry
is production-ready and urges organizations that have not adopted it
to do so, while highlighting continued work on AI workflows, browser
and mobile observability, telemetry governance, packaging, and
zero-code instrumentation.

 COMING AUG 31ST: AI-POWERED SUPPORT CASES IN CLIENT VPN AND SWITCHES
(2 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Meraki admins can now start Client VPN and switching support cases
directly from the Dashboard's AI Assistant. The assistant analyzes the
environment, suggests troubleshooting steps, and can generate a case
summary for approval before sending it to Meraki support.
