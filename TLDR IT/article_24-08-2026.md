NEWS & TRENDS

 ALIBABA LAUNCHES $10B SHARE SALE TO FUND AI EXPANSION (3 MINUTE READ)
[8]

 Alibaba plans to raise roughly $10 billion to expand its AI
infrastructure, chips, data centers, and model development. The raise
comes days after the company reported a 75% jump in capex tied largely
to AI.

 AI DATA CENTERS COULD BE FIRST TO LOSE POWER WHEN THE GRID IS
STRAINED (5 MINUTE READ) [9]

 PJM is proposing rules that would make some new large-load customers,
including AI data centers, interruptible when electricity supply gets
tight. The proposal highlights how power availability is becoming a
limiting factor for AI infrastructure growth.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 SAM ALTMAN ON BUILDING OPENAI AND BETTING ON THE IMPOSSIBLE (78
MINUTE PODCAST) [10]

 Sam Altman discusses OpenAI's early non-consensus bets, scaling
compute, AI adoption, and why society may absorb AI more slowly than
model progress suggests. He also argues AI could enable a boom in
small businesses while warning against concentrating control of the
technology among too few players.

 THE AI-NATIVE SDLC PLAYBOOK (8 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Anthropic lays out how AI changes software development beyond simply
adding coding assistants, including planning, implementation, review,
and operations. It argues teams need to redesign development workflows
around agents rather than bolt them onto existing processes.

LAUNCHES & PARTNERSHIPS

 47% OF DEVELOPERS HAVE SEEN AI AGENTS FOLLOW UNTRUSTED INSTRUCTIONS
(SPONSOR) [12]

 1Password's recent survey [12] found that 47% of developers have seen
their AI agents follow instructions embedded in untrusted content, and
74% have witnessed unintended consequences from agents.

Learn more [12] about the security risks of agentic AI, and what
developers can do to use agents securely.

 GITLAB 19.3 HELPS ENTERPRISES SCALE AGENTIC DEVELOPMENT SECURELY (4
MINUTE READ) [13]

 The GitLab 19.3 update is aimed at helping enterprises scale agentic
software development while retaining control over data, permissions,
and spending. The release adds single-tenant deployment for the Duo
Agent Platform, Secrets Manager support beyond pipelines, bulk SAST
remediation, plain-language custom flow creation, and usage caps for
agentic AI.

 RAMP LAUNCHES AN AI MODEL ROUTER (5 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Ramp has opened the internal model-routing infrastructure it uses
across more than 100 AI use cases as a standalone product. Router can
automatically choose models based on cost, quality, latency, and
availability. Ramp says it cut its own LLM costs by roughly 30%.

 NVIDIA BACKS CLOVERLEAF TO EXPAND AI DATA CENTER INFRASTRUCTURE (2
MINUTE READ) [15]

 Nvidia made a minority investment in Cloverleaf Infrastructure. The
companies have partnered to develop AI factories across the US.
Cloverleaf says its pipeline already includes multiple gigawatt-scale
infrastructure projects.

MISCELLANEOUS

 THE GPU BILL IS BECOMING THE NEW AWS BILL (6 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Enterprises risk repeating the cloud cost-management mistakes of the
last decade as expensive GPU infrastructure proliferates. AI teams
increasingly need FinOps-style controls for utilization, capacity
planning, and workload economics.

 AI AGENTS ARE TURNING ENTERPRISE DATA SILOS INTO AN INFRASTRUCTURE
PROBLEM (5 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Enterprise data stacks were largely designed around human access
patterns, but agents need reliable real-time access across systems.
That is turning data integration and context infrastructure into a
prerequisite for successful agent deployments.

 ANTHROPIC PLANS CUSTOMER-CONTROLLED STORAGE FOR FRONTIER-MODEL DATA
(1 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Anthropic plans to let enterprises keep required retained data inside
their own cloud infrastructure rather than Anthropic's systems.

 PORTWORX ENTERPRISE 3.6.2.2 ADDS STRONGER VSPHERE CERTIFICATE

 The release adds TLS certificate verification for vCenter connections
during cloud-drive operations and includes several Kubernetes and
disaster-recovery fixes.
