NEWS & TRENDS

 GOOGLE ASSISTANT SHUTTING DOWN ON ANDROID AND WEAR OS IN SEPTEMBER (3
MINUTE READ) [4]

 Google says it will begin shutting down Google Assistant on mobile
devices on September 4, moving users to Gemini instead. The transition
may take several weeks and will also affect smartwatches, headphones,
and cars using Android Auto, while users who already switched to
Gemini will see no change.

 CHINA LAUNCHES CYBERSECURITY REVIEW INTO PALO ALTO NETWORKS PRODUCTS
(3 MINUTE READ) [5]

 China's cyberspace regulator launched a cybersecurity review of Palo
Alto Networks products, citing potential risks to critical
infrastructure and national security without identifying specific
products or vulnerabilities. The move follows growing Chinese
restrictions on foreign cybersecurity suppliers and echoes its earlier
review that ultimately restricted Micron products.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 ELT IS MOVING BACK TO BEST-OF-BREED AND ORCHESTRATION IS THE MISSING
PIECE (11 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Data teams are moving away from all-in-one platforms toward a smaller
best-of-breed architecture centered on data movement, storage and
compute, and a control plane. Enterprise orchestration, observability,
metadata, incident management, and agent runtime are the missing
pieces needed to coordinate increasingly complex ELT pipelines and
reduce the operational burden of tracking failures and data freshness.


 NATJACK AT BLACK HAT: A NEW WAY TO CRACK NAT'S TRUST GAP (7 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 NatJack is a class of attacks that manipulates NAT
connection-tracking tables to hijack connections, poison DNS
responses, or trigger denial of service. Testing covered 32 products
and configurations across 13 vendors, with every implementation
vulnerable to at least some of the techniques tested.

 INFERENCE HOOKS: INLINE DATA LOSS PREVENTION FOR CLAUDE ENTERPRISE (5
MINUTE READ) [8]

 Anthropic's inference hooks for Claude Enterprise route prompts and
tool-call responses through a customer-controlled data loss prevention
server before Claude can process them. The beta works across Claude
surfaces, including chat, Claude Code, Cowork, and connected tools,
and supports a single organization-wide configuration with staged
enforcement options.

 AWS TARGETS AI COST CONCERNS WITH NEW MARKETPLACE INSIGHTS TOOL (4
MINUTE READ) [9]

 AWS Marketplace AI Insights explain complex product pricing in plain
language, including how costs scale, how multiple usage dimensions
combine, and what is included. The feature could help enterprises
compare consumption-based AI tools and forecast costs before
purchasing, although its accuracy depends on the pricing information
vendors publish.

MISCELLANEOUS

 AS SPACE GETS CONNECTED, SECURITY GETS CRITICAL (17 MINUTE READ) [10]


 As lunar and deep-space missions move toward interconnected networks,
communications systems must support high-volume data while resisting
eavesdropping, jamming, ransomware, and nation-state attacks. NASA's
planned LunaNet may use Delay/Disruption Tolerant Networking, 4G/5G
radio links, and optical inter-satellite links, alongside hardware
roots of trust, secure boot, post-quantum cryptography, and updateable
space-qualified systems.

 SURPRISE AI COSTS THREATEN ENTERPRISE IMPLEMENTATIONS (4 MINUTE READ)
[11]

 Unexpected AI expenses have caused one-quarter of enterprises to
delay or cancel projects, while nearly half have escalated spending
surprises to their boards, according to a survey of 396 organizations.
Costs are becoming harder to track as AI spending spreads across
models, infrastructure, developer tools, data platforms, and agentic
workflows, pushing CIOs to build cost controls directly into their AI
architecture.

 THE AGENT ACCESS MODEL (26 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Cloudflare's Agent Access Model (AAM) is a security framework that
treats each agent task execution graph as untrusted and authorizes
every action against the agent, task, and accumulated resource state.
Agent controls should use short-lived, sender-constrained credentials
and inline enforcement in the harness and network, while a one-way
Trust Ratchet can remove capabilities when protected events occur.

 HPE EXTENDS VALIDITY OF QUOTED HARDWARE PRICES, POSSIBLY FOR MONTHS

 HPE will honor hardware pricing from quote issuance until equipment
leaves its factories, easing pricing uncertainty caused by volatile
server, memory, and storage component costs.
