 ENTERPRISES ARE RUNNING AI IN PRODUCTION WITHOUT KNOWING WHAT IT
COSTS (5 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Two-thirds of surveyed enterprises now run AI workloads in
production, but many still lack visibility into infrastructure costs
and utilization. The gap is becoming more important as inference
becomes a continuous operational expense.

 95% OF ENTERPRISES HAVE DELAYED AI PROJECTS OVER INFRASTRUCTURE AND
GOVERNANCE GAPS (4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Cloudera found nearly every surveyed organization delayed or canceled
AI projects over the past year, with governance and compliance among
the biggest blockers. Nearly three-quarters said AI has made data
governance more complex.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 WILL SALESFORCE WIN THE AI ERA LIKE IT WON CLOUD? (5 MINUTE READ) [9]


 Salesforce's AI strategy will transform CRM into “agentic revenue
orchestration,” with agents handling prospecting, research, pipeline
management, and quoting. Its vision of a “headless CRM” surfaced
through tools such as Slack and ChatGPT, while claiming that AI will
augment rather than replace sellers and push pricing toward
consumption and outcomes.

 DON'T LOOK UP (32 MINUTE READ) [10]

 The future cloud growth of Microsoft, Google, and Amazon is
increasingly dependent on Anthropic and OpenAI sustaining enormous,
largely venture-funded compute spending. The two AI companies may need
to spend more than $200 billion on compute in 2027 and raise roughly
$250 billion to $300 billion to finance it, creating substantial risk
if end-customer returns fail to materialize.

 ENTERPRISE AI AGENTS KEEP GETTING CONFIDENT ANSWERS WRONG (6 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 A survey of 101 enterprises found bad context behind many confidently
incorrect agent answers. Organizations using governed semantic layers
were substantially better at detecting those failures.

 CITRIX EXPANDS PLATFORM FLEX WITH OBSERVABILITY AND SECURE DEVELOPER
SERVICES (4 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Citrix announced two services expanding Platform Flex: Experience
Insights Flex for managed workspace observability and SecurSpaces Flex
for hosted, secure developer and agentic workspaces. The services use
a shared credit pool, with Citrix managing analytics and
infrastructure while customers retain control of identity, code,
policies, and data.

 IBM AND TOGETHER AI BUILD A $240M INFERENCE CLUSTER (3 MINUTE READ)
[14]

 IBM will deploy a large Nvidia HGX B300 cluster for Together AI on
IBM Cloud, with availability expected in Q1 2027. The infrastructure
is designed specifically for large-scale open-model inference.

 DATABRICKS BUYS ELECTRIC TO BUILD DATABASES FOR AI AGENTS (4 MINUTE
READ) [15]

 Databricks acquired Electric, whose PGlite and sync technology lets
agents keep fast local Postgres state synchronized with centralized
databases. The team will join Neon to build infrastructure
specifically for agentic applications.

MISCELLANEOUS

 CAN SOFTWARE FACTORIES ACTUALLY WORK? (5 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Software factories, automated pipelines in which agents build, test,
and sometimes ship code, could solve the code-review bottleneck
created by faster agent-generated development. The factory model
contrasts human-reviewed systems with controversial “lights-off”
setups and requires validated agent output, broad adoption, and
failure-tolerant guardrails.

 AI IS CREATING A DATA CENTER CAPACITY CRISIS (5 MINUTE READ) [17]

 AI demand is colliding with long data center construction timelines
and constrained power availability. Enterprises may increasingly need
hybrid infrastructure strategies instead of assuming new GPU capacity
will always be available on demand.
