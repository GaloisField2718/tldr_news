NEWS & TRENDS

 NVIDIA OPEN AGENT SAFETY PLATFORM: A REFERENCE FOR CONTINUOUS
IN-SILICON AGENT MONITORING (7 MINUTE READ) [4]

 NVIDIA's Open Agent Safety Platform pairs OpenShell, an Apache 2.0
sandboxed runtime that turns operator instructions into verifiable
file, network, tool, and credential policies, with Sentry, a monitor
that runs on BlueField-4 DPUs. In Vera Rubin PODs, the DPU sits on the
node's only path to the model, so it can enforce policy at line speed
outside the agent's reach. NVIDIA's argument is that agents can't be
trusted to govern themselves, so safety controls have to sit below
them in hardware.

 MICROSOFT'S COPILOT SUPER APP COMES WITH A METER ATTACHED (3 MINUTE
READ) [5]

 Microsoft is splitting Copilot pricing in two. A per-user
subscription still covers "Everyday AI" in Office apps, while agentic
features like Cowork, Code, Autopilot, and frontier models are billed
by usage on top of the seat. Admins get spending caps and alerts, but
budgets that were fixed per head now have to absorb costs from agents
that run without a user prompting them.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 WHAT HAPPENS WHEN THE CLOUD BLOWS UP? (4 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Missile and drone strikes during the March 2026 Iran conflict hit AWS
facilities in the UAE and Bahrain. AWS says the damage exceeded what
its multi-AZ design can withstand, and restoration isn't expected
until early 2027. The piece argues that availability zones don't fail
independently when a whole region is physically attacked, and that
continuity plans should account for that.

 TEST-DRIVING JEV ON A SECURITY TASK: IDENTITY RESOLUTION (19 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 Vincenzo Iozzo tested Jev, a specialized classification model, on
matching accounts across systems to the same person in five real
organizations. Jev scored 0.948 F1 on large orgs at $0.62 per 1,000
accounts, beating Claude Haiku 4.5 (0.894 F1, 19x the cost) and Sonnet
5 (0.911 F1, 47x the cost) at roughly a tenth of their latency. His
conclusion is that small calibrated models beat prompted frontier LLMs
on bounded, high-volume security classification.

LAUNCHES & PARTNERSHIPS

 MICROSOFT ANNOUNCES GENERAL AVAILABILITY OF WSLC: LINUX CONTAINERS ON
WINDOWS (1 MINUTE READ) [8]

 WSLC, Microsoft's native way to run Linux containers on Windows, is
now generally available through a new wslc.exe command. Each session
gets its own storage VHD, stronger isolation, and a new networking
model compared with standard WSL. The code is open source in the
microsoft/wsl GitHub repository.

 INTRODUCING THREAT SIGNALS: AGENTIC SKILLS FOR OPEN-SOURCE THREAT
INTELLIGENCE, FREE FOR EVERY CLOUDFLARE ACCOUNT (5 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Cloudflare's Threat Signals uses agentic skills to read open-source
threat reports from RSS feeds, summarize them, extract indicators of
compromise, and tag threats. It then links those findings to WAF rules
while keeping the context of the original source. Cloudflare also made
its Threat Events Platform free for every account, with paid tiers for
more feeds, proprietary data, and custom skills.

MISCELLANEOUS

 BUILDING A POST-QUANTUM CERTIFICATE AUTHORITY WITH MERKLE TREE
CERTIFICATES (10 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Cloudflare is becoming a certificate authority that issues Merkle
Tree Certificates for free, and it is targeting Chrome's
quantum-resistant root store in early 2027. Post-quantum signatures
are about 40x larger than classical ones, so MTCs batch many
certificates under a single CA signature and build transparency
logging into issuance. In a Chrome experiment covering 50% of Beta
users, the lightweight MTC format already made handshakes 9% faster.

 GOOGLE SEEMINGLY CONFIRMS PLANS TO KILL CHROMEOS IN 2034 (2 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 A new Google support page for enterprise and education customers says
ChromeOS support will continue through mid-2034, and newer Chromebooks
will be migrated to the Android-based Googlebook OS. This is a problem
for schools, because Googlebooks start at $899 and won't have
comparable management tools for a year or more.
