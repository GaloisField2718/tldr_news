NEWS & TRENDS

 ACCENTURE WEIGHS $1.5 BILLION SALE OF ITS CHINA CONSULTING BUSINESS
(5 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Accenture has hired JPMorgan to explore the sale of its Greater China
consulting operations in a deal potentially worth up to $1.5 billion.
The strategic review follows unsolicited interest and comes as Beijing
restricts foreign consultants from advising state-owned enterprises.
Meanwhile, Accenture is redirecting capital and focus toward expanding
its artificial intelligence capabilities globally.

 OPENAI REPORTS MAJOR GAINS IN LEGACY-CODE MODERNIZATION (6 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 OpenAI introduced evaluations for building software across 20
programming languages and migrating COBOL while preserving behavior.
On its COBOL migration benchmark, GPT-6.1 Sol reached 86.6% accuracy
at low reasoning effort, compared with 5.4% for GPT-5.6 Sol,
suggesting progress on maintaining and modernizing legacy enterprise
systems.

 MICROSOFT EXITS UK DATA CENTER DEAL AMID POWER DELAYS (5 MINUTE READ)
[9]

 Microsoft reportedly terminated its agreement for capacity at
Nscale's planned Loughton facility amid electricity supply delays that
could extend into the 2030s. Nscale says the development will proceed
and its other Microsoft contracts remain unaffected, highlighting the
growing importance of power availability in AI infrastructure
planning.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 ENTERPRISE AI OUTCOMES DEPEND ON THE ART OF UNLEARNING (5 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Enterprise AI adoption often falls short because organizations
overlook the human element of change management. Successful digital
transformation in the agentic era requires empowering employees to
unlearn old software habits, supported by dedicated roles like Forward
Deployed Engineers and Agentic Design Leads.

 THE AGENTS NOBODY MEANT TO BUILD (7 MINUTE READ) [11]

 IT leaders face an emerging challenge with unsanctioned AI agents
created inadvertently by employees using approved desktop tools and
recurring workflows. Gartner forecasts that 40% of enterprises will
demote or shut down agents by 2027 due to governance gaps, security
risks, and a lack of ownership visibility. Managing this risk requires
placing hard limits on what agents can touch and integrating education
directly into tool access.

 ENTERPRISE AI NEEDS RUNTIME GOVERNANCE AND BETTER CONTEXT (5 MINUTE
READ) [12]

 Scaling enterprise AI requires coordinating what agents know with
what they are permitted to do. Runtime access controls, audit trails,
retrieval, and memory need to work together as agents operate across
enterprise systems.

LAUNCHES & PARTNERSHIPS

 LENOVO'S AI EXPRESS TARGETS ENTERPRISES WAITING FOR AI SERVERS (5
MINUTE READ) [13]

 Lenovo's AI Express is a quick-ship programme developed with Nvidia
that aims to cut enterprise AI server lead times to as little as 15
business days. The offering provides three pre-validated
configurations ranging from small inference setups to large generative
AI platforms using Blackwell hardware. The program is designed for
enterprises struggling with hardware scarcity. Orders are subject to
strict caps and eligibility checks.

 CLOUDFLARE EXPANDS ENTERPRISE INTEGRATIONS FOR ITS AI WORKSPACE (4
MINUTE READ) [14]

 Cloudflare expanded the partner ecosystem for Cloudflare OS with
integrations spanning Asana, Box, Canva, ClickHouse, GitHub, Gong, and
Notion. The announcement also adds professional services partners to
help enterprises train employees and adopt AI workflows connected to
their existing tools.

MISCELLANEOUS

 CISCO SECURE EMAIL GATEWAY ZERO-DAY EXPLOITED AGAIN (4 MINUTE READ)
[15]

 Cisco confirmed that a zero-day vulnerability in its Secure Email
Gateway, tracked as CVE-2026-76519 with a CVSS score of 9.8, is
actively being exploited in the wild. The path traversal flaw allows
unauthenticated attackers to write files and execute root-level
commands via crafted email attachments when File Reputation is
enabled. Security agencies, including CISA and Germany's BSI, have
issued warnings and urgent patching deadlines.

 WHY GPU CLOUD OBSERVABILITY IS HARDER THAN IT LOOKS (29 MINUTE READ)
[16]

 GPU cloud observability requires connecting physical infrastructure
topology with active workloads, yet traditional monitoring tools often
remain isolated by subsystem. Building a live, topology-aware model
enables operators to trace performance bottlenecks, manage capacity
fragmentation, and correlate hardware telemetry with distributed
training or inference jobs.
