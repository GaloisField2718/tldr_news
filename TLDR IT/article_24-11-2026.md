NEWS & TRENDS

 MICROSOFT MEGA OUTAGE: REPORT REVEALS TESTING GAPS AND EMAILS THAT
NEVER ARRIVED (4 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Microsoft's final incident report reveals how a Windows defect went
undetected for months before overwhelming Microsoft 365 gateway
servers. At its peak, more than 80% of North American gateway servers
were unresponsive. Microsoft identified gaps in load testing, failover
procedures, and infrastructure capacity, and plans to improve service
isolation and recovery mechanisms.

 CROWDSTRIKE TO PURCHASE ZSCALER FOR $43.9 BILLION (3 MINUTE READ) [6]


 CrowdStrike has announced an all-stock acquisition of Zscaler,
bringing endpoint protection, zero-trust networking, cloud security,
and threat intelligence under one platform. Zscaler CEO Jay Chaudhry
will continue leading the business and join CrowdStrike's board. The
deal represents a major step toward security platform consolidation
and could reshape enterprise security purchasing decisions.

 EUROPE PUSHES FOR CLOUD SOVEREIGNTY AMID US DOMINANCE (5 MINUTE READ)
[7]

 European governments and regulated industries are increasingly
incorporating digital sovereignty requirements into cloud procurement
contracts to reduce dependence on US technology providers. Although
high-profile contracts have been awarded to European and hybrid
providers, overall migration remains slow due to strong incumbent
lock-in and high migration friction.

 JENSEN HUANG RETURNS AS CEO OF NVIDIA (2 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Nvidia's board has reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO less than two weeks
after his resignation following consultations with senior US
government officials. The board determined that his reinstatement does
not pose a national security risk. Huang's return reduces immediate
leadership uncertainty at a critical point in the global AI
infrastructure buildout.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 AI IS CHANGING THE BUILD-VERSUS-BUY EQUATION. ARE CIOS READY? (9
MINUTE READ) [9]

 AI-powered development tools and vibe coding reduce software creation
costs but do not eliminate ongoing security, maintenance, and
integration expenses. CIOs should selectively reconsider building
specialized, non-critical SaaS applications rather than replacing core
enterprise platforms, especially as AI tools can introduce security
risks without necessarily improving development productivity.

 SHOULD APPLE REALLY MOVE FAST AND BREAK THINGS? (5 MINUTE READ) [10]

 As Apple faces pressure to accelerate its AI development, its
deliberate engineering culture could be a competitive advantage.
Introducing unreliable AI capabilities at the operating system level
carries greater consequences than standalone chatbot errors, making
reliability, privacy, and customer trust particularly important.

 HOW AI IMPACTED THE RELEASE OF POSTGRES 19 (6 MINUTE READ) [11]

 AI-assisted testing uncovered numerous defects during PostgreSQL 19
development, contributing to its delayed release. The experience
highlights an emerging engineering challenge: while AI accelerates
development and bug discovery, human validation, architectural
decisions, and quality assurance remain critical bottlenecks.

 AWS SYSTEMS MANAGER AUTOMATION NOW AUTOMATES OPERATIONAL TASKS IN

 AWS has expanded Systems Manager Automation to support multicloud
environments. IT teams can centrally automate configuration
remediation, resource cleanup, agent deployment, and infrastructure
changes without maintaining separate automation workflows across cloud

 TOKENLESS AI: SLACKBOT POWERED BY CLAUDE (5 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Slack has introduced Slackbot powered by Claude, offering flat-rate
pricing per person on Enterprise+ plans to eliminate per-token usage
costs for everyday AI tasks. The company argues that tokenless pricing
helps organizations avoid unpredictable AI bills and lets employees
use AI freely without rationing adoption. Early users report
significant reductions in overage charges and improved workflow
efficiency by routing routine queries through Slackbot.

 CLOUDWATCH OMNI (6 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Amazon has launched CloudWatch Omni, an AI-powered observability
solution built on open standards and delivered outside the AWS
Console. The platform unifies the monitoring and evaluation of AI
agents, applications, and infrastructure across multi-account
environments. Designed for development workflows, it integrates
directly into IDEs and provides features like auto-discovered
topology, natural-language querying, and continuous trace evaluations.


 WORKDAY COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF C1.AI (3 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Workday has completed its acquisition of C1.ai, formerly
ConductorOne, expanding into identity governance. Combining employee
lifecycle data with access management capabilities will help
organizations automate provisioning, permission management, and
security enforcement for employees and AI agents.

 HOW SECURITY VENDORS ARE TACKLING ACCESS-CONTROL CHALLENGES FOR AI
AGENTS (6 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Security vendors are introducing a range of access-control,
monitoring, and testing tools to address the unique challenges posed
by autonomous enterprise AI agents. Companies such as CrowdStrike,
Okta, and Cisco are deploying new capabilities to prevent agents from
executing unauthorized actions or falling victim to confused-deputy
scenarios. At the same time, industry groups like the Blueprint
Alliance are attempting to coordinate standards across vendors for
securing agent identities and tasks.

 AMAZON SCOOPS UP AI ACCELERATOR TECH TO KEEP CHIPS BUSY AT ALL TIMES
(4 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Amazon is acquiring Decart's AI optimization technology and
engineering talent to improve hardware utilization across Nvidia GPUs,
Google TPUs, and AWS Trainium chips, targeting faster inference,
higher throughput, and lower AI computing costs.
