NEWS & TRENDS

 NVIDIA UNVEILS MINERVA TO BRING END-TO-END OBSERVABILITY FOR AI
FACTORIES (3 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Minerva is an AIOps platform designed to monitor AI factories by
connecting telemetry from infrastructure and AI workloads. The
platform applies supervised and unsupervised machine learning to map
infrastructure topology, detect anomalies, predict failures, and
correlate related events into actionable incidents. Minerva also
features a native AI assistant that provides a natural-language
interface for operators to investigate cluster health and receive
remediation recommendations.

 AMAZON CLOUD CAPACITY SOLD OUT THROUGH 2027, CEO JASSY SAYS (1 MINUTE
READ) [5]

 Amazon CEO Andy Jassy says AWS' large capacity commitments are
largely booked through 2027, with substantial commitments already
extending into 2028 as AI demand grows. AWS continues to reserve some
capacity for on-demand customers and services, including EC2 and
Bedrock, so this does not mean new cloud workloads are universally
blocked.

 ZOOMTOPIA 2026 WRAP-UP: CONNECTING CONVERSATIONS TO WHAT COMES NEXT
(5 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Zoom introduced a shared context layer intended to connect meeting
transcripts, chats, documents, and business applications, helping AI
agents act on what was discussed. Agent teams and search APIs are
available now. Centralized AI management is expected in December, with
AI Guardrails planned for Q1 2027.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 VENDOR-DRIVEN UPGRADES SADDLE IT LEADERS WITH HUGE ‘SOFTWARE TAX'
(6 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Respondents in an Origina-commissioned survey of large US and UK
enterprises attribute 28% of software spending to vendor-driven
upgrades, migrations, and licensing changes. This post examines how
support deadlines and switching costs weaken negotiating power, and
how evaluating alternatives before renewal can strengthen a buyer's
negotiating position.

 AN ALIGNMENT ASSESSMENT OF THE UK AISI CYBERTESTING INCIDENT (33
MINUTE READ) [8]

 Claude Mythos 5, with internet access enabled and deployment cyber
safeguards disabled, tried to plant malicious code in a real GitHub
project and used fake identities to pressure maintainers. The
maintainers rejected the change. Replay tests found Mythos 5.1 and
Opus 5.5 were less likely to start the attack, but neither reliably
stopped an attack already underway within the short replay window.

 LACK OF TRANSPARENCY LEADS TO PERFORMATIVE AI THEATER (5 MINUTE READ)
[9]

 Pressure to adopt AI can encourage employees to exaggerate their
expertise and prioritize visible usage over better results. Employees
need practical training, clear guidance, and honest conversations
about changing roles so they can build confidence and use the tools
effectively.

 INTRODUCING 1PASSWORD FOR AGENTS (7 MINUTE READ) [11]

 1Password for Agents lets users approve AI-agent sign-ins while
1Password fills credentials directly into the destination site. It is
included at no extra cost for Individual and Family accounts on
1Password.com. 1Password.eu and 1Password.ca accounts are not
currently supported. Integrations are at different rollout stages,
with support for Claude Duet, ChatGPT Work, and Townie listed as
coming soon.

 INTRODUCING AGENT COMPUTE ENGINE AND EXPANDED AGENT GOVERNANCE (8
MINUTE READ) [12]

 Google Cloud announced Agent Compute Engine, a service designed to
run AI agents in hardware-isolated sandboxes, with customer preview
expected in the coming months. It also announced broader agent
governance across Google Cloud, other clouds, on-premises
infrastructure and managed endpoints, with cross-cloud and endpoint
capabilities marked for preview soon.

 ADVANCING DIGITAL SOVEREIGNTY WITH GREATER CONTROL AND CHOICE (7
MINUTE READ) [13]

 Microsoft is expanding its sovereign cloud offerings with oversight
of Microsoft engineer access, customer-controlled encryption keys, and
more services that can run on customer-operated infrastructure.
External key management is generally available, while the new
Sovereign Control Panel is headed for public preview at Ignite. GitHub
Enterprise Local and expanded Foundry Local capabilities are also
scheduled for general availability at that event.

 The Register tested the refreshed, Debian 13-based Raspberry Pi

and basic applications. The PC edition is now 64-bit only, and

 THE CURRENT LANDSCAPE OF OAUTH ATTACKS (13 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Attackers can abuse OAuth through account hijacking, malicious app
consent, and redirects. Phishing kits and AI-generated impersonation
can be used to help campaigns scale. Stolen or fraudulently granted
tokens can allow access after a legitimate MFA login and may persist
after a password change, depending on token type and provider policy.
Defenses include restricting user app consent, enforcing
least-privilege permissions and auditing redirect URIs, and revoking
compromised tokens and grants during incident response.
