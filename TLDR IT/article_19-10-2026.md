NEWS & TRENDS

 MICROSOFT PLANS TO PAY ANTHROPIC OVER $2B A YEAR TO KEEP COPILOT
COMPETITIVE (3 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Microsoft reportedly plans to pay Anthropic over $2 billion annually
to use Claude AI models within its Copilot assistant. The integration
responds to client performance pressures and aims to improve output on
tasks like financial automation and presentations. To manage costs,
Microsoft employs an internal model router that directs some workloads
to OpenAI models.

 ANTHROPIC COMMITS 5% OF ITS SHARES TO PUBLIC BENEFIT FUND (3 MINUTE
READ) [5]

 Anthropic is allocating 5% of its shares to a new fund that could
support nonprofits and direct distributions to populations affected by
AI. Together with founder and employee pledges, the company says
roughly 20% of its equity is now dedicated to public benefit.

 WORKDAY OPENS LIVE ENTERPRISE DATA TO AI AGENTS (5 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Workday Data Cloud gives agents access to live business data,
existing permissions, and business logic without requiring duplicate
datasets or rebuilt security controls. Agent Lake and Live Data Query
are generally available, while semantic models and tools for governed
agent actions remain in early access.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 SERVICENOW AI CONTROL TOWER: SELLING A PENTHOUSE WHILE BUILDING THE
FRAME (21 MINUTE READ) [7]

 ServiceNow's AI Control Tower provides a strong governance workflow
and system of action for enterprise AI. However, customer interviews
reveal that claims of end-to-end technical governance and a universal
runtime kill switch are overstated, with runtime enforcement typically
handled by external or custom tools. Additionally, customers face
meaningful friction regarding configuration costs, CMDB hygiene, and
pricing tier uplifts.

 IF YOU CAN'T AUTOMATE YOUR WORKFLOW, NEITHER CAN YOUR USERS' AGENTS
(7 MINUTE READ) [8]

 When developers struggle to automate their own workflows, AI coding
agents will encounter the exact same friction, making workflow
obstacles a critical product problem. Workflows designed around human
navigation, portals, and undocumented knowledge cause high failure
rates and excessive token costs for agents. Developers should evaluate
their products by testing them with goal-oriented agent prompts rather
than relying solely on usage telemetry or manual workarounds.

 WHAT HAPPENS WHEN AI AGENTS WORK MORE HOURS THAN EMPLOYEES? (12
MINUTE READ) [9]

 Anthropic's internal research found that interactive agent hours
outnumbered employee hours six to one by August, while output
increased and junior technical roles represented a smaller share of
hiring. Human attention is becoming a bottleneck, pushing employees
toward monitoring agent pipelines and hiring managers toward
prioritizing judgment and autonomy.

 SERVICENOW PLANS AI PASSPORT FOR ENTERPRISE AGENTS (2 MINUTE READ)
[11]

 ServiceNow's AI Passport is a governance tool designed to control and
verify the permissions of artificial-intelligence agents during
execution. Nvidia is a design and launch partner for the tool, which
is expected to become available in the fourth quarter of 2026
alongside other enterprise products.

 HONEYWELL LAUNCHES AI AGENTS TO MANAGE BUILDING ALARMS, MAINTENANCE
AND ENERGY (3 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Honeywell Technologies has introduced Forge Cognition, a platform
combining AI agents with live facility simulations to help operators
manage building alarms, maintenance, and energy use. Developed using
technology from NVIDIA and Dell Technologies, the platform offers
adjustable automation ranging from manual to autonomous oversight.
Early pilot testing at NVIDIA's Voyager campus and other sites
demonstrated significant reductions in unnecessary alerts and
potential energy savings.

MISCELLANEOUS

 FIREFOX ENTERPRISE 158 PUTS BROWSER POLICY, DATA CONTROLS AND AI
GOVERNANCE ON CUSTOMER-RUN INFRASTRUCTURE (5 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Mozilla announced the early access of Firefox Enterprise 158,
featuring a self-hosted admin console, domain-scoped data-loss
prevention, and governance controls for AI tools. Aimed at
organizations in France, Germany, Austria, and Switzerland, the
management plane runs on customer infrastructure rather than relying
solely on cloud-hosted alternatives or standard MDM policies.

 AI PRICING TOOLS FACE A CONSUMER TRUST PROBLEM (6 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Retailers are adopting AI-assisted pricing and digital shelf labels,
but consumers remain confused about how personal data influences
prices. The reporting separates actual practices from misconceptions
and highlights how limited transparency can undermine trust in
otherwise routine technology upgrades.
