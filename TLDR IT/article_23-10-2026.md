NEWS & TRENDS

 DEEPSEEK REPORTEDLY RAISES $15 BILLION TO CHALLENGE US AI LABS (2
MINUTE READ) [4]

 DeepSeek reportedly raised approximately $15 billion in one of
China's largest funding rounds, with Tencent and battery manufacturer
CATL among the backers. The funding strengthens its push to compete
with OpenAI and Anthropic as the company prepares for a potential IPO
and faces growing US restrictions over security concerns.

 OPENAI MODELS ARE COMING TO GOOGLE CLOUD (2 MINUTE READ) [5]

 OpenAI and Google Cloud are partnering to bring OpenAI frontier
models and Codex agentic workflows into Google Cloud's Model Garden.
Customers will be able to purchase the models through the Google Cloud
Marketplace and draw down GCP commitments, with general availability
planned in the coming weeks. Microsoft remains OpenAI's primary cloud
partner.

 GOOGLE CLOUD AND NVIDIA ANNOUNCE GENERAL AVAILABILITY OF NVIDIA VERA
RUBIN INFRASTRUCTURE (3 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Google Cloud and NVIDIA announced the general availability of
infrastructure powered by the NVIDIA Vera Rubin architecture,
featuring A5X instances designed for large-scale reinforcement
learning and agentic inference. Additionally, Google Cloud plans to
offer NVIDIA Confidential Computing at rack scale on bare metal
instances and expand confidential capabilities across virtual machine
offerings.

 HOW GITHUB IS REBUILDING GIT INFRASTRUCTURE FOR AI AGENTS (8 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 GitHub has placed copies of 99.99999% of its repositories in Azure as
agent-driven traffic and data center capacity constraints accelerate
its infrastructure migration. This engineering breakdown explains how
moving write coordination from entire repositories to individual
references reduces contention, delivering 3–4x higher throughput in
internal tests.

 WHY AI INFRASTRUCTURE SPENDING FORECASTS DISAGREE SO DRAMATICALLY (10
MINUTE READ) [8]

 This comparison examines forecasts from 12 research and financial
firms, explaining how different definitions produce vastly different
estimates of AI infrastructure spending. It connects those projections
to hyperscaler budgets, power availability, and the revenue needed to
justify the buildout.

 INTRODUCING CLAUDE DUET (7 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Anthropic has announced a research preview of Claude Duet, a new
proactive agent app designed for professionals and small business
owners. Built around Claude Opus 5.5, the app operates independently
in the background via a virtual machine with its own browser to manage
tasks, send daily briefs, and monitor connected apps. The application
includes robust security frameworks and safety classifiers to
safeguard sensitive user information before performing actions on
their behalf.

 SERVICENOW AND NVIDIA LAUNCH ARC, A NEW AI AGENT FOR ENTERPRISE (3
MINUTE READ) [11]

 ServiceNow and Nvidia have launched Arc, a personal long-running AI
agent built for enterprise use. Designed to run locally on Nvidia RTX
Spark devices or within Nvidia OpenShell, Arc assists with daily tasks
using productivity tools like email and Microsoft Teams. The companies
emphasize a multi-level security framework that utilizes ServiceNow's
Super Intelligence Control Tower, Nvidia OpenShell, and AI Passport to
enforce runtime and hardware-layer policies and manage agent
permissions.

MISCELLANEOUS

 MICROSOFT ENTRA ID ENDING NATIVE SMS AUTHENTICATION, PUSHING USERS TO
PASSKEYS (2 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Microsoft has announced that it will retire its native SMS and voice
authentication for Entra ID on February 1, 2027, for most customers,
with the deadline extended to July 1, 2027, for Global Administrators
and external users. Starting October 30, 2026, organizations will be
able to choose a third-party telephony provider through the Microsoft
Security Store. Microsoft recommends that organizations transition to
phishing-resistant passkeys instead of relying on telephony-based
authentication.

 CRUSOE HEADS TO PARAGUAY FOR HYDROPOWERED AI COMPUTE (3 MINUTE READ)
[13]

 Crusoe and Araico plan to build AI cloud infrastructure powered by
Paraguay's Itaipú hydroelectric dam, with an initial deployment
targeted for mid-2027. The project has a path to 106 MW of IT capacity
and will serve both US customers and demand across Latin America.
