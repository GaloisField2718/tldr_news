NEWS & TRENDS

 WORKOS DISCLOSES AI-ASSISTED DATA SECURITY INCIDENT (2 MINUTE READ)
[9]

 WorkOS disclosed unauthorized access to an internal sales application
on October 30, exposing business contact information, customer account
histories, and excerpts from customer communications. The company
identified the use of an AI-assisted offensive security framework to
automate reconnaissance and exploitation attempts. It has disabled
external access to the affected application while it investigates the
full impact.

 OPENAI BRINGS WORK FEATURES TO CHATGPT (3 MINUTE READ) [10]

 OpenAI is rolling out ChatGPT Work capabilities directly into regular
Chat while combining usage allowances across ChatGPT and Codex. The
changes are intended to simplify access to more advanced workflows and
give subscribers greater flexibility in how they use OpenAI's
products.

 OVER 1,000 GITLAB SERVERS EXPOSED TO ACTIVE ATTACKS (3 MINUTE READ)
[11]

 More than 1,000 internet-facing GitLab servers remain vulnerable to a
maximum-severity remote code execution flaw that attackers are
actively exploiting. The vulnerability, tracked as CVE-2026-97229,
affects GitLab's CI/CD Regular Expression Compiler and can allow
unauthenticated attackers to execute arbitrary code. CISA has added
the flaw to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog.

 CONNECTING GEMINI ENTERPRISE TO SNOWFLAKE DATA (6 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Google Cloud published a guide demonstrating how to connect Gemini
Enterprise agents to Snowflake using the Model Context Protocol and
Agent Development Kit. The architecture enables agents to translate
natural-language requests into SQL queries through Snowflake Cortex
while enforcing user-level permissions and existing data governance
controls.

 ARE AI CERTIFICATIONS STILL WORTH IT IN 2026? (12 MINUTE READ) [13]

 The AI certification landscape has shifted as cloud vendors retire
older credentials and introduce exams focused on generative AI and
agents. Meanwhile, employers are becoming increasingly skeptical of
certifications alone, pushing IT professionals to demonstrate
practical skills alongside formal credentials.

 CROWDSTRIKE AND DELL TEAM UP TO SECURE ENTERPRISE AI (3 MINUTE READ)
[15]

 CrowdStrike and Dell Technologies announced an integrated security
solution for the Dell AI Factory with NVIDIA, combining AI
infrastructure with protection for workloads, agents, and
applications. Customers will be able to purchase Dell's AI
infrastructure alongside CrowdStrike's Falcon security platform
through Dell's AI Ecosystem Program.

 MICROSOFT AND LUMEN EXPAND PARTNERSHIP FOR AI-READY NETWORKS (3
MINUTE READ) [16]

 Microsoft and Lumen are expanding their collaboration to help
enterprises connect data, applications, and infrastructure across
hybrid and multi-cloud environments. The companies plan to jointly
sell networking solutions and explore bringing additional Lumen
capabilities to Azure, simplifying connectivity for enterprise AI
workloads and agentic applications.

MISCELLANEOUS

 GARTNER WARNS ENTERPRISES TO RETHINK AI INFRASTRUCTURE (5 MINUTE
READ) [17]

 Gartner expects AI agents to play a growing role in incident triage,
change validation, and IT remediation, but warns that uncontrolled
token-based pricing could push costs more than 50% above expectations
by 2027. The firm recommends prioritizing measurable use cases,
strengthening observability, and preparing hybrid infrastructure for
AI workloads.

 SNOWFLAKE MAKES THE CASE FOR SMALLER AI MODELS (5 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Snowflake argues that enterprises should prioritize intelligence
efficiency over deploying the most powerful AI model for every task.
Its approach uses model routing to balance performance, latency, and
costs, with internal testing showing up to three times greater token
efficiency for certain agent workflows without compromising quality.
