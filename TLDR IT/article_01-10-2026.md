NEWS & TRENDS

 INTRODUCING DOTS (7 MINUTE READ) [4]

 OpenAI has introduced "Dots," an always-on AI agent platform powered
by GPT-6 Astra that operates its own cloud computer and browser to
assist users across various tasks. Dots can connect to over 4,000 apps
through plugins, learn user preferences over time, and execute
background tasks such as bug fixing and content drafting. Dots are
beginning to roll out across Pro, Business Premium, and Enterprise
plans, with specialist dots also previewed for organizational use.

 SAAS COMPANIES' NEWEST RIVALS ARE THEIR OWN CUSTOMERS (6 MINUTE READ)
[5]

 Companies including Spotify, Twilio, and West Monroe are using AI to
build internal tools that previously would have meant buying another
SaaS product. It's an important shift for IT leaders: build-vs-buy is
getting recalculated as custom internal software becomes dramatically
cheaper and faster to create.

 AI'S THIRD WAVE: COWORKERS BREAK THE SECURITY MODEL THAT WORKED FOR
AGENTS (4 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Persistent AI coworkers can operate continuously with standing access
rather than receiving temporary permissions for one task. That changes
the identity problem for IT and security teams, making lifecycle
management, least privilege, visibility, and offboarding increasingly
important for non-human identities.

 THE PROBLEM WITH WILD CODE? IT CAN'T SEE IT COMING (5 MINUTE READ)
[7]

 AI-assisted software development is making it easier for employees to
build applications and automations outside traditional development
processes. The bigger enterprise challenge may be visibility: knowing
what has been built, what data it touches, who owns it, and whether
security and lifecycle controls exist.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 THE CASE FOR META ENTERPRISE PLATFORM (8 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Meta's newly announced Meta Enterprise Platform serves a crucial
strategic purpose by acting as a secondary demand sink for the
company's massive artificial intelligence capital expenditures. By
offering its AI technology stack and agents to businesses, Meta can
mitigate the financial risks of overbuilding compute infrastructure
for consumer products while countering the market dominance of leading
AI labs.

 HOW DECADES OF DISCONNECTED MODERNIZATION CREATED OT SECURITY DEBT
MANUFACTURERS CAN'T ESCAPE (13 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Decades of disconnected modernization have created a severe OT
security debt crisis for manufacturers, as new technologies were built
on legacy systems without proper network segmentation or security
architecture. Flat networks, default administrator accounts, and
permanent remote access links have vastly expanded the attack surface,
turning single footholds into enterprise-wide risks. Addressing this
exposure requires asset visibility, realistic exposure management, and
tailored controls for brownfield environments rather than complete
system replacements.

 WHATEVER HAPPENED TO THE 36-MONTH IT SECURITY ROADMAP? (6 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Fixed multi-year security roadmaps are becoming harder to maintain,
as threats, business priorities, and technology change faster. Some
security leaders are instead moving toward tiered priorities and
continuously revisiting the roadmap while preserving longer-term
strategic direction.

 STOP CHASING AI SPEED, FIX THE REWORK FIRST (9 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Measuring AI success primarily through speed or hours saved can hide
rework and poor outcomes. Focus on business results and explicitly
define where AI assists, where human judgment remains necessary, and
how employees participate in redesigning workflows.

LAUNCHES & PARTNERSHIPS

 MONGODB LAUNCHES ATLAS AGENT ENGINE, ATLAS INFINITE AS MONGODB 9.0
GOES GA (3 MINUTE READ) [12]

 MongoDB has announced the public preview of Atlas Agent Engine, a
governed agentic platform featuring sophisticated memory architecture,
and Atlas Infinite, a deployment option that separates compute and
storage to improve scaling. Additionally, the company is making
MongoDB 9.0 generally available, bringing higher transaction
throughput and faster updates. Together, these products aim to
accelerate how enterprises move AI agents into production.

 ANNOUNCING FALKORDB 6.0 (9 MINUTE READ) [13]

 FalkorDB announced the release of version 6.0, the first version
built on its new Rust engine. The company states that the new engine
executes 39% fewer CPU instructions across benchmark queries, resolves
226 bugs, and introduces a new replication format called effects v3
while maintaining backward compatibility with existing Cypher queries
and integrations.

 INTRODUCING SKILLS IN THE GEMINI APP AND WORKSPACE, PLUS WHAT'S NEXT
FOR GEMS (5 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Google is introducing reusable Gemini "skills" that can be combined
inside prompts and shared across workflows, with Workspace rollout
beginning October 5. Skills will eventually replace Gems, so Workspace
admins should start planning for the transition and reviewing existing
Gemini and Workspace Studio use cases.

MISCELLANEOUS

 ANTHROPIC REVELATIONS SUGGEST A MUCH STRONGER AI NEGOTIATING STANCE
FOR ENTERPRISE CIOS (5 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Analyses of a confidential prospectus from Anthropic suggest that
enterprise CIOs may hold a stronger negotiating position than
previously assumed due to revenue concentration and a lack of
long-term customer contracts. Vendors like Anthropic depend heavily on
enterprise revenue, giving buyers leverage to negotiate price
protections, data portability, and advance notice for model
retirements. However, enterprises must also manage infrastructure
dependencies and cost structures as frontier AI providers capture a
dominant share of market revenue.

 GET TO KNOW OPENSEARCH 3.9 (15 MINUTE READ) [16]

 OpenSearch 3.9 introduces improvements across search, observability,
and cluster resiliency, highlighted by a faster native C++ engine for
neural sparse vector search and expanded vector storage options like
16-bit half_float types and 2-bit through 4-bit scalar quantization.
The release also brings a unified alerts view out of preview, adding
anomaly detection and forecasting to help users triage signals across
data sources from a single place.

 80% OF NETWORK PROS ARE OK WITH GIVING AI AN AUTONOMOUS ROLE IN
NETWORK OPERATIONS (5 MINUTE READ) [17]

 A Cisco survey of 1,000 IT professionals found significant
willingness to let AI perform autonomous network operations. The trend
suggests network management is moving beyond AI-assisted
troubleshooting toward systems that can potentially diagnose and
remediate issues with less direct human intervention.

 NETAPP TICKS ANOTHER HYPERSCALER BOX WITH ORACLE CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE
DEAL (4 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Oracle and NetApp are planning a fully managed, OCI-native storage
service built around NetApp ONTAP. The offering is intended to let
enterprises move databases, VMware, regulated workloads, and AI data
into OCI while retaining familiar NetApp management and protection
workflows.
