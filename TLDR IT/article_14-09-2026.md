NEWS & TRENDS

 SALESFORCE INTRODUCES THE TRUSTED ENTERPRISE AI HARNESS (7 MINUTE
READ) [5]

 Salesforce has introduced the Enterprise AI Harness, a composable
architecture featuring six trusted capabilities and an AI Control
Plane designed to give businesses a unified way to manage and control
agents and AI across their organization. The platform connects
open-ended AI reasoning with enterprise data, security, governance,
and business rules. The system allows enterprises to leverage
proprietary customer context securely while maintaining compliance and
flexibility across different models and third-party systems.

 MICROSOFT CONFIRMS WINDOWS 11'S UPDATE KILLS AUDIO ON SOME PCS, AND
THE BUGS KEEP PILING UP (3 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Microsoft has confirmed that September's Windows update can break USB
Audio Class 1.0 devices, causing missing sound, device errors, and
unresponsive audio settings. Switching to two-channel audio has helped
some users affected by multichannel failures, but a general fix
remains pending. The issue affects Windows 11 versions 24H2, 25H2, and
26H1-not all USB audio devices.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 THE HARD PART OF AN MCP GATEWAY IS AUTH (8 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Authorization, rather than integration, is the primary engineering
challenge when deploying AI agents at enterprise scale with a Model
Context Protocol gateway. Building robust identity, per-tool scopes,
consent mechanisms, and audit logs is essential for safely connecting
agents to internal services.

 SECURITY THROUGH OBSCURITY IS DEAD, AND AI DELIVERED THE FATAL BLOW
(6 MINUTE READ) [8]

 AI is eroding the protection organizations implicitly get from
obscure software and unfamiliar legacy systems. The bigger challenge
is remediation capacity: discovering more vulnerabilities accomplishes
little unless teams can prioritize fixes, validate patches, and
prevent the same weaknesses from recurring.

 DELL'S 52-INCH ENORMO-MONITOR IS A TSUNAMI OF IMPRACTICAL FUN (5
MINUTE READ) [9]

 Dell pitches its UltraSharp 52 as a replacement for four smaller
monitors, reducing the clutter of cables and stands. This hands-on
review finds an impressive display for visual work and multiple data
feeds, but also connection problems, awkward port access, and a setup
that is difficult to justify for ordinary office work.

LAUNCHES & PARTNERSHIPS

 WHEN SHOULD YOU ADD A 2ND PAYMENT PROCESSOR? (SPONSOR) [10]

 Adding a second payment processor can reduce downtime risk and close
coverage gaps, but the integration work and ongoing overhead can be
significant. Stripe's guide breaks down how to evaluate the decision,
what the resource investment actually looks like, and how an
orchestration layer can help you manage multiple providers. Read the
guide [11]

 INTRODUCING THE AGENTS API (5 MINUTE READ) [12]

 OpenAI has introduced a managed Agents API in public beta to help
enterprises build custom AI agents with reduced infrastructure
management and engineering complexity. The service hosts the
underlying harness and allows developers to run agents in
OpenAI-managed sandboxes, on their own infrastructure, or through
supported providers.

 BOOMI INTEGRATION AND AUTOMATION PLATFORM RELEASE – SEPTEMBER 2026
(15 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Boomi's September release makes Orchestrate generally available to US
customers and adds native tracking of Claude Managed Agents, including
their tools, models, and token usage. Administrators can now inspect
agent session logs across users, while new connectors cover Zoom, Ping
Identity, and other business apps. Knowledge Hub also enters early
access, bringing structured and unstructured company information into
a governed retrieval layer for people and agents.

MISCELLANEOUS

 WHEN ANYONE CAN BUILD SOFTWARE, WHO DECIDES WHAT NOT TO BUILD? (11
MINUTE READ) [14]

 As generative AI makes writing software cheaper and faster, the
primary bottleneck in enterprise software development shifts from
building code to making coherent architectural judgments. Code is
disposable while operational state and system integrations accumulate,
so organizations face a growing risk of fragmented, locally optimized
silos unless they maintain a deliberate enterprise-wide perspective
before committing resources.

 SOME CUSTOMERS EXPERIENCING BLURRY IMAGE PREVIEWS AND DOWNLOAD ISSUES
(2 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Slack resolved a weekend incident that made older image uploads
appear blurry or fail to download across desktop, web, and mobile
clients. A code change disrupted retrieval of existing files, while
newly uploaded images were unaffected. Slack deployed a fix and
confirmed that access was restored for affected customers.
