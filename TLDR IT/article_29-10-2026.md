NEWS & TRENDS

 GOOGLE AND ORACLE ARE PARTNERING TO BRING GOOGLE TPUS TO ORACLE CLOUD
INFRASTRUCTURE (3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Google Cloud and Oracle are partnering to bring Google Tensor
Processing Units to Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, making OCI the first
hyperscaler outside Google to offer TPUs. The companies plan to deploy
TPU capacity on OCI starting in 2027 to help organizations scale AI
workloads across data centers engineered for AI density.

 MICROSOFT BEATS QUARTERLY CLOUD REVENUE ESTIMATES (3 MINUTE READ) [9]


 Microsoft's Azure cloud business reported $33.17 billion in quarterly
revenue, growing 48% year over year and beating analyst expectations.
Microsoft is now disclosing Azure revenue in dollars, providing
greater visibility into its cloud performance as the company continues
investing heavily in AI infrastructure.

 OPENAI PLANS TO BUY GOOGLE'S AI CHIPS, THE INFORMATION REPORTS (2
MINUTE READ) [10]

 OpenAI reportedly plans to purchase Google's Tensor Processing Units,
expanding beyond its existing chip-rental arrangement. The potential
deal would give OpenAI another source of AI computing capacity
alongside Nvidia GPUs and its custom-chip development efforts with
Broadcom, while strengthening Google's position in the AI
infrastructure market.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 WHAT ENTERPRISES CAN LEARN FROM ANTHROPIC'S IPO PROSPECTUS ABOUT
VENDOR DEPENDENCY (5 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Anthropic's IPO prospectus reveals that nearly half of its sales are
routed through cloud partners Amazon and Google, highlighting
significant vendor dependency and concentration risk across the AI
ecosystem. Industry experts advise enterprise CIOs to treat AI
procurement as a resilience conversation by questioning supplier
supply chains and ensuring data and workflow portability.

 INFORMATION OVERLOAD IS HARMING ENTERPRISE PRODUCTIVITY - HERE'S HOW
IT LEADERS CAN SOLVE THAT (3 MINUTE READ) [12]

 A Forrester study commissioned by Staffbase found that fragmented
workplace communication is affecting employee productivity, with 48%
of respondents reporting reduced efficiency and 44% citing more errors
or missed deadlines. The findings suggest that consolidating
information and connecting workplace applications could reduce
unnecessary context switching.

 SNOWFLAKE AND ORACLE: BRINGING THE AI DATA CLOUD TO OCI (4 MINUTE
READ) [14]

 Snowflake and Oracle are expanding their partnership to bring
Snowflake's AI Data Cloud natively to Oracle Cloud Infrastructure as a
first-party offering. Planned as an integrated service purchased and
billed directly through Oracle, Snowflake on OCI aims to connect
enterprise data across Oracle applications and databases with other
sources to support analytics and AI workflows.

 NVIDIA AND ORACLE EXPAND NVIDIA VERA RUBIN TO MULTI-GIGAWATT SCALE (2
MINUTE READ) [15]

 NVIDIA and Oracle have announced plans to expand Oracle Cloud
Infrastructure Superclusters with NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 systems to
multi-gigawatt scale. The companies are also bringing NVIDIA Vera CPUs
and Vera Rubin GPUs to OCI to support AI training, inference, and
agentic workloads. OpenAI has also trained and served frontier models
on OCI infrastructure powered by NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs.

 GITHUB UNIVERSE 2026: EVOLVING EVERY LAYER OF THE STACK (6 MINUTE
READ) [16]

 GitHub has introduced a new Copilot app, Copilot Fusion, for
automatically routing coding tasks across multiple AI models and agent
workflows spanning repositories and development tools. GitHub also
announced that Enterprise customers can access Copilot features
without purchasing a separate subscription, with usage billed through
AI credits.

MISCELLANEOUS

 DISCOVERING AND EXPLOITING VULNERABILITIES IN CRITICAL, REAL-WORLD
SOFTWARE WITH CLAUDE (8 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Anthropic researchers used an internal research model to discover
critical vulnerabilities and construct sophisticated multi-step
exploit chains in widely used software, including browsers, mobile
operating systems, and kernel networking stacks. The model identified
issues such as remote code execution, sandbox escapes, and lock-screen
bypasses, which were reported to affected vendors under coordinated
vulnerability disclosure. Anthropic argues that AI-assisted
vulnerability discovery could help defenders identify and patch
security flaws before attackers exploit them.

 ORACLE CLOUD ACCELERON ADDS AI-ASSISTING NETWORKING ENHANCEMENTS (3
MINUTE READ) [18]

 Oracle expanded its Acceleron networking platform with new
capabilities for AI and high-performance computing workloads. Updates
include RDMA connectivity for front-end compute, expanded NVMe
block-storage options, and communication libraries designed to
maintain distributed AI workloads when network paths fail.
