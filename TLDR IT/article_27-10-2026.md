NEWS & TRENDS

 ANTHROPIC'S SOARING SALES, STEEP LOSSES PUT AI FRENZY TO IPO TEST (4
MINUTE READ) [8]

 Anthropic published its US IPO filing as investors scrutinize the
cost of the AI boom. Its first-half net loss was $80.7 billion,
largely driven by a non-operating convertible-bond item, while its
operating loss narrowed to $693 million. The prospectus also
highlights growing computing costs and dependence on a small group of
cloud and chip suppliers.

 MICROSOFT FINALLY BRINGS A UNIFIED INBOX TO NEW OUTLOOK FOR WINDOWS
(3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 New Outlook for Windows is rolling out an All Accounts view that
brings email from multiple connected accounts into one place while
keeping each mailbox separate. Microsoft expects rollout to finish by
late October, with administrator controls planned separately.

 GHEIST: ATTEMPTED SCALED ABUSE OF STOLEN GITHUB TOKENS STUMBLES (7
MINUTE READ) [10]

 A campaign tracked by Wiz uses stolen GitHub tokens to plant
workflows that steal repository secrets and can expose cloud
credentials. GitHub's approval controls blocked every malicious
public-repository run the researchers checked, but Wiz observed
successful runs and follow-on cloud activity involving private
repositories and enterprise environments. The report includes
indicators for investigating affected accounts and guidance on
revoking tokens and rotating exposed secrets.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 AZURE COMMUNICATION SERVICES IS RETIRING: NOW WHAT (10 MINUTE READ)
[11]

 Microsoft has announced the retirement of most Azure Communication
Services (ACS) features by September 30, 2028, limiting ongoing
functionality exclusively to Microsoft Teams integrations. New
customer onboarding was restricted starting in late 2026, and existing
standalone services such as chat, SMS, and WhatsApp will be shut down
entirely. This move suggests Microsoft's failure to capture a viable
standalone market, leaving enterprise users facing a difficult
migration.

 COGNITIVE MERCANTILISM (15 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Contemporary AI fears reflect a form of cognitive mercantilism, which
treats intelligence as a scarce resource and underestimates ordinary
people's ability to think independently. Much like the consumer
revolution that preceded industrial capitalism, widespread access to
AI could spark a cognitive industrious revolution. Abundant AI could
expand demand for intelligence and empower ordinary people to access
and apply it on an unprecedented scale.

 THE METRIC THAT MAKES YOUR IT ORG LOOK HEALTHIER THAN IT IS (7 MINUTE
READ) [13]

 Strong uptime, ticket-resolution, and patch-compliance percentages
can hide recurring problems and risks concentrated in a few critical
systems. Executive reporting should emphasize business disruption,
repeat incidents, and unresolved critical vulnerabilities, while
retaining operational metrics for the teams that use them.

 WIRELESS IS THE NEXT SECURITY FRONTIER, AND CISCO IS LEADING THE WAY
(5 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Cisco is launching the CW9176ISEC, described as the industry's first
Wi-Fi 7 access point with quantum-resilient capabilities aligned to
CNSA 2.0 standards. The company is also refreshing its Wireless 9800
Series controllers and introducing Auto MACsec to secure traffic
between access points and switches. In partnership with Qualcomm,
Microsoft, and Intel, Cisco is bringing post-quantum cryptography over
the air to protect enterprise wireless infrastructure and client
devices.

 AWS TRANSIT GATEWAY AND AWS CLOUD WAN NOW SUPPORT 1:1 NAT (2 MINUTE
READ) [16]

 AWS now provides managed one-to-one network address translation
directly on AWS Transit Gateway and AWS Cloud WAN attachments through
integration with Regional Private NAT Gateway. This capability enables
customers to connect private networks sharing the same IP address
ranges without renumbering networks, deploying NAT appliances, or
writing return-traffic rules. The service automatically applies
address translation in both directions across Amazon VPC, AWS
Site-to-Site VPN, AWS Direct Connect, and connected SD-WAN appliances.

MISCELLANEOUS

 HOW WE GAVE AI AGENTS THE KEYS TO PRODUCTION AND STILL SLEEP AT NIGHT
(10 MINUTE READ) [17]

 monday.com's Production Engineering team built a shared platform that
allows LLM agents to safely interact with production infrastructure
through standardized guardrails. After one quarter in production,
agents resolved about 50 percent of an average of 1,688 infrastructure
tickets per month without human escalation.

 CONSUMER AGENTS SITTING INSIDE ENTERPRISE SAAS (6 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Threat research has flagged a surge of consumer personal agents
connecting directly to core enterprise SaaS suites without corporate
IT oversight. C-level executives are granting these unvetted
third-party agents expansive OAuth privileges, creating unmonitored
non-human identities with access to sensitive corporate data and
files. Legacy security architectures are blind to these cloud-to-cloud
connections, necessitating active, context-aware SaaS and non-human
identity governance.
