NEWS & TRENDS

 SERVAL TRIPLES VALUATION TO $3 BILLION AS BUSINESSES ADOPT AI
WORKFLOW AUTOMATION (3 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Serval raised $150 million in a Sequoia-led round at a $3 billion
valuation, tripling its valuation since December. The company builds
AI agents for IT operations, including employee access management,
troubleshooting, and software and hardware asset management. Serval
positions its platform as an alternative to established IT service
management products such as ServiceNow, with permissions and approvals
controlled by IT and security teams.

 ORACLE WANTS TO BILL YOU FOR EVERY AI TASK, NOT JUST EVERY SEAT (4
MINUTE READ) [5]

 Oracle plans to introduce usage-based AI charges alongside existing
subscriptions for its Fusion enterprise applications. Customers will
consume AI units as automated tasks are completed, allowing Oracle to
grow revenue without adding user licenses. The approach reflects a
broader shift in enterprise software pricing from per-seat
subscriptions toward charging for automated work, introducing new
considerations for software budgets and cost forecasting.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 THE HIDDEN COST OF AI IS NOW GOVERNANCE (6 MINUTE READ) [6]

 As artificial intelligence projects transition from pilots to
production, governance has emerged as a significant hidden cost
spanning IT, security, legal, risk, and data teams. Retrofitting
controls late in the process can add months of project delays and
double expected costs, making risk-tiered governance and early
automation essential for scalable enterprise AI adoption.

 IT SKILLS SHORTAGE OFTEN A COMPLEXITY PROBLEM IN DISGUISE (8 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 Some enterprise IT staffing challenges stem from unnecessarily
complex systems rather than a lack of qualified candidates. Years of
customizations, undocumented dependencies, and fragmented
architectures create environments that require highly specialized
knowledge. Simplify infrastructure, document dependencies, and reduce
technical debt before assuming additional hiring or training will
solve the problem.

 WHEN YOUR AI VENDOR GOES BELLY UP (7 MINUTE READ) [8]

 As enterprises adopt products from a growing number of AI startups,
CIOs face the possibility that some vendors will shut down, be
acquired, or change their business models. Unlike conventional
software replacements, losing an AI provider can introduce
complications involving proprietary models, workflow dependencies, and
customer data. This article examines how organizations can prepare
exit strategies, preserve data access, and reduce their dependence on
individual providers.

 APPLE UNVEILS GROUNDBREAKING MACBOOK PRO WITH PROLED XDR DISPLAY AND
TOUCH FOR THE FIRST TIME ON MAC (5 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Apple introduced its first touchscreen MacBook Pro, featuring a
tandem OLED display, M5 Pro and M5 Max chips, and up to 24 hours of
battery life. Available in 14-inch and 16-inch models, the laptops
also include Wi-Fi 7, Thunderbolt 5, and up to 16TB of storage. Prices
start at $2,899, with availability beginning November 6. The launch
adds another premium option for enterprise Mac fleets and specialized
professional workloads.

 AWS LAMBDA DURABLE FUNCTIONS NOW EXPORTS LIVE TELEMETRY FOR EXECUTION
INSIGHTS (2 MINUTE READ) [12]

 AWS Lambda has announced the Durable Execution Telemetry Exporter
plugin, which captures step-by-step histories of durable executions
and exports them to 13 built-in destinations like Amazon S3 and
CloudWatch. Alongside the plugin, AWS released a companion Model
Context Protocol server that allows AI agents to query the telemetry
data using natural language. The exporter is available at no
additional charge across regions supporting Lambda durable functions
for TypeScript, Python, Java, and Go.

 INTRODUCING THE MICROSOFT COPILOT HARNESS: UNLOCKING THE POTENTIAL OF
TRULY MULTI-MODEL AGENTIC WORK (4 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Microsoft is making the GitHub-developed Copilot harness available
through Microsoft Foundry, providing developers and enterprise
customers with a managed multi-model orchestration layer. The harness
powers various Copilot experiences and supports over twenty frontier
models, open standards, and tools while routing work intelligently
based on tasks. Recently ported to Rust for improved performance, the
system manages the agent loop, sandbox, governance, and optimization.

 SLACKBOT GETS MORE PROACTIVE, IN MORE PLACES (7 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Slack introduced event-triggered Slackbot tasks that can respond to
messages, reactions, list updates, and channel creation events. The
update also includes Slackbot Mini, a desktop interface for accessing
the assistant outside Slack, and expanded Skills capabilities for
sharing reusable workflows across teams. Administrators and users can
configure approval requirements for automated actions, extending
Slackbot beyond conversational assistance into workflow execution.

MISCELLANEOUS

 CHINESE AI INVASION GIVES ENTERPRISES A NEW WAY TO CONTROL COST (5
MINUTE READ) [15]

 Enterprises are increasingly adopting affordable Chinese open-weight
AI models to reduce soaring token spending on US proprietary frontier
models. While some companies face integration hurdles, infrastructure
limitations, and geopolitical concerns that prompt hesitation,
organizations like Red Hat are successfully utilizing these models to
control costs by allocating workloads between proprietary and
open-weight alternatives.

 GRANOLA CRUNCHES UP A DICTATION FEATURE TO COMPETE WITH WISPR (2
MINUTE READ) [16]

 AI meeting-notes app Granola introduced a free dictation feature that
allows Mac users to speak and automatically convert their words into
formatted text across applications. The company plans to bring the
capability to Windows and iOS. The move follows Wispr's expansion into
meeting transcription, illustrating how voice productivity tools are
converging around similar capabilities.
