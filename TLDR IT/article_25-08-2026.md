NEWS & TRENDS

 OPENAI REGAINS GROUND IN THE CORPORATE AI MARKET (2 MINUTE READ) [4]

 OpenAI appears to be regaining ground against Anthropic among US
corporate customers, according to spending data from Ramp covering
more than 70,000 organisations. Anthropic led in May and July, but
third-quarter trends suggest faster growth for OpenAI, while
increasing overall spending on paid AI services indicates that both
providers may expand even as corporate loyalty remains fluid.

 NVIDIA PUTS GROQ 3 LPX INFERENCE CHIP INTO PRODUCTION (4 MINUTE READ)
[5]

 Nvidia's Groq 3 LPX accelerator is entering production as part of its
Vera Rubin platform, targeting ultra-fast token generation for agentic
AI. Nebius is the first cloud provider committed to deploying it.

 IBM BUILDS CHIP THAT RUNS ARM AND Z WORKLOADS ON THE SAME CORES (5
MINUTE READ) [6]

 IBM is developing a dual-architecture processor that can natively
execute both Arm and IBM Z instructions. The design could let
enterprises run modern AI and cloud-native software directly alongside
critical mainframe workloads.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 WHEN NOT TO USE STREAM PROCESSING: MESSAGING AND APIS ARE OFTEN
ENOUGH (20 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Stream processing is valuable for continuous event-driven computation
but is often the wrong architecture for request-response interactions,
direct state queries and corrections, or work requiring locks. As
evidence, Kestra rebuilt its messaging layer around plain queueing,
and Confluent moved Control Center's metrics pipeline from Kafka
Streams to Prometheus.

 AUTONOMY AND INNOVATION (13 MINUTE READ) [8]

 AI cybersecurity capabilities are inherently dual-use: the same
ability to discover and exploit vulnerabilities is needed to defend
infrastructure, with intent shaped by incentives and whoever controls
the system. Defenders must automate the full loop from vulnerability
detection through patching, deployment, and rollback.

 ATLASSIAN CODE CONTEXT GIVES AI AGENTS EYES ACROSS YOUR ENTIRE

 Atlassian Code Context is a Teamwork Graph capability that lets

MISCELLANEOUS

 ENSURING RELIABLE OPENTELEMETRY INGESTION AT SCALE (16 MINUTE READ)
[14]

 ClickHouse describes how its LogHouse observability platform evolved
to handle OpenTelemetry ingestion at 50 million events per second
without losing telemetry during ClickHouse backpressure. After finding
in-memory queues and local collector write-ahead logs inadequate, the
company designed a priority-based failover pipeline that keeps
ClickHouse in the hot path and uses blob storage as a durable overflow
buffer.

 CAN NEOCLOUDS CORNER AI COMPUTE? (10 MINUTE READ) [15]

 AI-focused clouds such as CoreWeave, Lambda, and Nebius offer
cheaper, highly optimized GPU infrastructure, but hyperscalers still
hold advantages in security, managed services, and enterprise
integration. Gartner expects neoclouds could capture 20% of the AI
cloud market by 2030.

 GOOGLE BRINGS ANTIGRAVITY UNDER GEMINI ENTERPRISE (3 MINUTE READ)
[16]

budgets, quotas, and overage controls. The move gives IT teams more
visibility over rapidly growing AI usage and spend.
