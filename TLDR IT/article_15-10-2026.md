ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 THE RISE OF DEPLOYCOS (6 MINUTE READ) [9]

 AI labs, hyperscalers, and incumbent consultancies are increasingly
establishing specialized deployment companies—or DeployCos—to
embed forward-deployed engineers directly inside enterprises. These
entities help bridge the "diffusion gap" or "capability overhang" by
assisting organizations with custom workflow integration, systems
access, and organizational change management.

 CHASING THAT 32 MS TAIL LATENCY IN CLICKHOUSE (14 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Engineers at ClickHouse investigated why simple SELECT queries
experienced an unexpected 32 ms tail latency after idle periods on
high-RTT networks. They discovered that unbatched metadata packets
sent alongside result data quickly exhausted the TCP congestion window
after it had been reset to its initial size during idleness. To
resolve the issue, ClickHouse implemented application-level batching
for these metadata packets, effectively halving the TCP segment count
and eliminating the tail latency penalty.

 REDESIGNING SOURCE CONTROL AT ANTHROPIC (16 MINUTE READ) [16]

 To handle massive scaling challenges caused by AI-generated code that
merged 30,000 changes daily, Anthropic built an in-house version
control system called Scion and fully migrated its monorepo from git
in five months. The company designed Scion around the assumption that
clients are always online, implementing a centralized server
architecture, horizontal scalability, server-side commits, and sparse
checkouts. This new infrastructure reduced CPU and memory requirements
by 200x compared to their previous git mirroring setup and accelerated
CI code checkouts from minutes to seconds.

 CISCO, NVIDIA TEAM UP TO BUILD FATTER, FASTER PLUGGABLE (4 MINUTE
READ) [17]

 Cisco and Nvidia have proposed a new optical transceiver form factor
capable of supporting up to 12.8 Tbps of bandwidth. The design
promises higher density and improved power efficiency for future data
center networks, although commercial deployments remain ahead.
