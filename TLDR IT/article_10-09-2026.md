NEWS & TRENDS

 APPLE UNVEILS FIRST FOLDABLE IPHONE WITH A20 PRO CHIP (4 MINUTE READ)
[8]

 Apple unveiled the $1,999 iPhone Duo, its first foldable phone,
alongside the iPhone 18 Pro lineup powered by its new A20 Pro chip.
The launch also pushes Apple further into on-device AI, with expanded
Siri capabilities and a continued focus on privacy.

 HP BRINGS DATA-CENTER AI INFRASTRUCTURE TO THE EDGE (3 MINUTE READ)
[9]

 HP is working with Red Hat and Nvidia on an enterprise AI platform
that can run production inference across devices, data centers, and
hybrid environments. The system combines HP hardware powered by Nvidia
Blackwell with Red Hat AI Factory for deployment and orchestration.

 CLAUDE FOR MICROSOFT 365 (2 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Claude for Microsoft 365 integrates Claude into Excel, PowerPoint,
Word, and Outlook with shared context across apps. Claude for Excel,
PowerPoint, and Word is now generally available on all paid Claude
plans, while Claude for Outlook is in beta. The offering emphasizes
reviewable edits, use of customer templates, and deployment through
existing cloud and compliance frameworks.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 THE EVAL YOU CANNOT TRUST IS YOUR OWN (12 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Enterprise AI evals fail when vendors can see POC test questions.
Teams need a permanently held-out eval that vendors never see. Google
DeepMind's double-blind frontier-model evaluation shows the model:
neither side saw the other's evaluation assets. Cleaner lab benchmarks
alone won't fix enterprise adoption failures.

 EVERY CUSTOMER GETS THE SAME SOFTWARE. THAT'S ENDING (5 MINUTE READ)
[12]

 AI will end the era of identical software for every customer by
making per-customer variants cheap to create and maintain. Vendors,
not open-source templates, will own these divergences, reviving
SAP-style customization without its historic migration cost. The
result will be the end of the common UI, where each customer uses a
recognizably different version of the same product.

 OPEN SOURCE IS RESHAPING THE AI INFRASTRUCTURE STACK (8 MINUTE READ)
[13]

 AI infrastructure needs stronger reproducibility and control across
hardware, drivers, models, and data than Kubernetes alone provides.
Governance and bandwidth constraints are expected to push more AI
workloads toward hybrid and distributed environments.

 GOOGLE SAYS ENTERPRISE AI ASSISTANTS ARE MOVING BEYOND CHAT (5 MINUTE
READ) [14]

 Google was named a Leader in Gartner's inaugural Magic Quadrant for
Enterprise AI Assistants. Google says the category is shifting from
basic copilots toward agents that connect enterprise systems and
automate multi-step workflows.

 GOOGLE CLOUD RACES TO CATCH UP IN THE AI DEPLOYMENT WARS WITH
ACCENTURE DEAL (3 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Google Cloud and Accenture have launched the Accenture Gemini
Enterprise Business Group, a joint unit to embed engineers in
enterprises to accelerate adoption of Google's AI tools. Google will
train up to 1,000 Accenture forward-deployed engineers to build custom
applications on the Gemini Enterprise platform. The move is aimed at
closing an enterprise deployment gap as Google holds about 6% of US
enterprise AI spending, far behind Anthropic and OpenAI.

 RED HAT LAUNCHES BLUEPRINT FOR SOVEREIGN AI CLOUDS (6 MINUTE READ)
[18]

 Red Hat and Rafay published a reference architecture that lets cloud
providers turn distributed GPU infrastructure into multi-tenant
sovereign AI services. The design covers self-service provisioning,
governance, metering, and isolated GPU environments.

 WRITER LAUNCHES A SHARED MEMORY LAYER FOR ENTERPRISE AI AGENTS (7
MINUTE READ) [19]

 Writer launched Enterprise Brain, a context layer that gives AI
agents access to shared organizational knowledge, business logic, and
live data. It also introduced team-level Agent Memory and integrations
for Slack, Teams, Zoom, and Google Meet.

MISCELLANEOUS

 WAN TRENDS, EXPLAINED (50 MINUTE PODCAST) [20]

 TeleGeography's seventh WAN Manager Survey finds enterprise networks
shifting from MPLS to Dedicated Internet Access as the primary
underlay, with SD-WAN mature and SASE/Zero Trust expanding. AI has
minimal current impact on bandwidth, with cloud and SaaS remaining the
main drivers, while satellite use stays niche and Network as a Service
adoption remains flat.

 LIGHTBITS TARGETS THE KV-CACHE BOTTLENECK IN AI INFERENCE (4 MINUTE
READ) [21]

 Lightbits unveiled Inferra, a KV-cache orchestration engine designed
to improve GPU utilization for long-context and multi-session
inference workloads. The company says the storage layer can help AI
infrastructure providers serve larger workloads without adding GPUs at
the same rate.
