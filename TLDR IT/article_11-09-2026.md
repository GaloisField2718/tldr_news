 ANTHROPIC ALIGNMENT LEAD SAYS AI HAS >10% CHANCE OF KILLING HUMANITY
(4 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Anthropic Alignment Science Lead Evan Hubinger said he personally
believes there is a greater than 10% chance AI could “kill all
humans” within the next decade. He added that Anthropic still does
not have a clear solution for aligning superintelligent AI, following
another researcher's resignation over the pace of development.

 ORACLE'S CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE REVENUE JUMPS 121% (3 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Oracle's cloud infrastructure revenue grew 121% year over year as
demand for AI compute continued to surge. The company spent $28.5
billion on capital expenditures during the quarter and maintained
plans for $90–95 billion in spending this fiscal year

 MICROSOFT GOES AFTER SALESFORCE AND ERP USERS WITH AI-POWERED
CONVERTER (2 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Microsoft has launched a public preview of Dynamics 365 Activate, an
AI-powered tool to help partners and customers migrate from Salesforce
to Dynamics 365 with less manual effort and lower risk. The company
says the tool profiles data, relationships, and customizations to
create a migration blueprint, and plans additional CRM and ERP
migration scenarios later this year.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 THE “LEFT” IN SHIFT-LEFT MOVED (13 MINUTE READ) [7]

 AI has shifted left from code repositories to coding agents on
developer endpoints with broad privileges, where AppSec has limited
control. AI-driven discovery is also flooding defenders with
vulnerabilities. The better approach is to eliminate entire
vulnerability classes rather than fix tickets one by one, while
rethinking human-in-the-loop oversight when agents can both write and
remediate code.

 OPENAI BOARD MEMBER WARNS INDUSTRY ISN'T READY FOR LOSS-OF-CONTROL
RISKS (5 MINUTE READ) [8]

 OpenAI nonprofit board member Paul Christiano says the industry is
not currently on track to prevent a potentially catastrophic loss of
control over advanced AI. His warning adds to growing concern around
how quickly increasingly autonomous systems are being developed.

 BOOST PRODUCTIVITY WITH 5 NEW AGENTIC AI CAPABILITIES (5 MINUTE READ)
[9]

 Google is rolling out five new agentic Gemini capabilities across
Workspace that complete cross-app tasks in the background without
switching tabs. Gemini can create Slides from Chat, build Sheets from
Drive, draft and send emails from Docs, turn Gmail threads into
structured Docs briefs, and convert Docs into branded decks. The
features are rolling out now to Business, Enterprise, Google AI Pro
and Ultra, and education plans.

 ACCENTURE AND GOOGLE CLOUD DEEPEN PARTNERSHIP WITH FORMATION OF NEW
ACCENTURE GEMINI ENTERPRISE BUSINESS GROUP (2 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Accenture and Google Cloud launched a new Gemini Enterprise group,
including a 1,000-person FDE workforce, to scale agentic AI adoption.
The group will help enterprises move from AI pilots to production with
industry-specific solutions and implementation support. YouTube saw
11% higher customer sentiment and 37% lower handle time using Gemini
Enterprise.

 BIG LAW STARTS BUILDING ITS OWN AI INFRASTRUCTURE (4 MINUTE READ)
[11]

 Latham & Watkins has bought Nvidia GPU servers and is running
customized open-weight models on infrastructure it controls. The move
shows large enterprises exploring private AI stacks for greater
control over sensitive data, costs, and model choice.

 MODERNIZING COMPLEX LEGACY CODE WITH AI AGENTS (6 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Mistral says it helped a European energy operator migrate 40,000
lines of Fortran 77 reservoir-simulator code to C++ using supervised
AI-agent workflows. The team built a numerical parity harness first,
documented the codebase with over a hundred agents, and found that
structured workflows with human review gates beat full autonomy. The
first sprint covered core functionality of a 300,000-line system.
