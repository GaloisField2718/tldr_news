 RACKSPACE SHUTS DOWN SPOT CLOUD PLATFORM AFTER SECURITY BREACH (3
MINUTE READ) [4]

 Rackspace will permanently discontinue its Spot Kubernetes service on
December 8 at 5 PM Central following a security breach. Customers must
migrate workloads and download their data before that deadline, when
remaining workloads will be terminated and stored data will become
inaccessible. All Spot customers are being told to treat API tokens,
kubeconfigs, and Kubernetes Secrets as potentially compromised, even
if they did not receive an earlier breach notification. Rackspace says
it has found no evidence that the incident extended to its core
infrastructure or other customer environments.

 HPE SHARES TUMBLE AS SHRINKING AI MARGINS OVERSHADOW UPBEAT FORECAST
(2 MINUTE READ) [5]

 HPE shares fell about 16% after lower margin expectations
overshadowed an improved fiscal 2027 revenue-growth forecast of
27–35%. Fourth-quarter revenue increased 50% to $14.55 billion,
helped by strong AI server demand. CFO Marie Myers said component
costs are rising faster than selling prices, with further margin
pressure expected as AI server shipments increase in the second half
of the fiscal year.

 EUROPEAN IT SPENDING SET FOR 9.9% SURGE IN 2027 AMID AI-OPTIMIZED
SERVER BOOM (2 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Gartner expects European IT spending to reach $1.57 trillion in 2027,
up 9.9% from 2026. Data-center spending is forecast to reach $167.6
billion, while software spending climbs 13.3% to $385.2 billion. AI
infrastructure demand and higher server prices are driving much of the
increase. Gartner also expects software purchasing to shift from
individual AI agents toward systems that coordinate workflows across
multiple business functions.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 DO LEGACY SYSTEMS WEAR OUT? (7 MINUTE READ) [7]

 This post examines how maintenance and vendor support shape the
reliability of aging infrastructure. Hardware components deteriorate,
but replacement parts, monitoring, and redundancy can keep an older
platform operating reliably. Software presents a different challenge:
changing workloads, missing patches, and the end of vendor support can
make a functioning system increasingly risky. Upgrade or retire
software before support ends, while maintaining the skills and parts
needed to operate older hardware.

 I BUILT AN AI AGENT. IT BROKE MY ASSUMPTIONS ABOUT MY OWN TEAM (8
MINUTE READ) [8]

 CTO Matthew Finlayson describes building four versions of an agent to
route customer issues to engineering teams. The project revealed that
assignments depended on undocumented ownership rules, exceptions, and
judgment that managers explained through reviewing real cases. He
reports that their feedback helped improve routing accuracy from about
60% to 95%. The experience also shaped how he evaluated the project,
including access to the right systems, ongoing validation, operating
costs, and the amount of manual work removed.

 INTRODUCING BITS MAINTENANCE, YOUR AI TEAMMATE FOR INFRASTRUCTURE
PATCHING AND UPGRADES (4 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Datadog is introducing Bits Maintenance, an AI teammate designed to
automate infrastructure patching and upgrades for open source
software. Currently available in preview, the tool tracks releases and
vulnerabilities, analyzes changes against environment configurations,
generates tailored upgrade plans, and executes pull requests based on
user-defined guardrails.

MISCELLANEOUS

 HOW WORKDAY'S CIO IS PREPARING IT FOR THE AI ERA (7 MINUTE READ) [12]


 Workday CIO Rani Johnson discusses rebuilding the company's data
lake, identity infrastructure, and Salesforce environment to create a
stronger foundation for AI. She describes aligning IT roadmaps with
business priorities and agreeing on how the value of investments will
be measured. Johnson says partnering with learning and development
helped raise enterprise AI adoption from below 50% to over 96% by
addressing skills and behavior changes. She also argues that CIOs
should actively create career opportunities for their teams as
technology changes the work they perform.

 MODERNIZING RETAIL KIOSKS WITH WINDOWS AUTOPILOT AND MICROSOFT INTUNE
(7 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Microsoft outlines how it moved retail checkout devices from
domain-joined, Configuration Manager-managed builds to Windows
Autopilot and Intune. The new setup uses Windows 11 Enterprise LTSC,
Microsoft Entra join, and provisioning without an individual user
account. The team registered existing devices before cutover,
separated pilot and production deployments, and used device
certificates for Wi-Fi access. The case study also explains how the
checkout application replaces the standard Windows desktop and how
application servicing accommodates store operations.
