 AI agents are joining the workforce, but the security policies aren't
keeping up. Research from JumpCloud found that 59% of organizations

policies used for employees. That's a problem as agents increasingly
access applications, execute tasks, and make decisions. With
JumpCloud's Agentic IAM [6] capabilities, IT teams can discover
agents, establish ownership, manage permissions, and govern access
throughout their lifecycle, alongside human and device identities.

NEWS & TRENDS

 SNOWFLAKE PREVIEWS AI AGENTS THAT WORK AFTER YOU LOG OFF (5 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Snowflake announced CoCo and CoWork Bots, agents in development that
will handle ongoing tasks in the background and request human input
when needed. Separate updates add recurring automations and planned
integrations with Slack, spreadsheets, and mobile devices.

 SUSPECTED CYBERATTACKS SPOIL FOOD AT EUROPEAN SUPERMARKETS (4 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 More than 40 supermarkets across seven countries were affected by
suspected attacks that remotely raised refrigeration temperatures,
destroying food. The incidents highlight the exposure of connected
operational equipment that can remain in service long after its
software support ends.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 IS AI SAVING TIME OR MAKING WORK LONGER? (5 MINUTE READ) [10]

 ActivTrak's latest research associates heavy AI use with longer
workdays, shorter focus sessions, and more app switching,
contradicting some of its earlier findings. Different samples and
measurement limitations make the results difficult to compare,
underscoring why usage metrics alone cannot establish productivity
gains.

 RUSHED AI LAYOFFS COULD LEAD TO COSTLY REHIRING (3 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Gartner predicts that 30% of employees replaced by AI will be rehired
by 2029, likely at a higher cost. The firm argues that companies
should assess how work needs to change before deciding which roles to
cut.

 SNOWFLAKE ADDS CENTRALIZED CONTROLS FOR ENTERPRISE AI AGENTS (5
MINUTE READ) [13]

 Snowflake's generally available Cortex AI Gateway centralizes
policies for agent connections, model access, and spending. Its
upcoming Business Ontology preview will give agents approved
definitions of business concepts, while sandbox execution and
agent-abuse detection remain in private preview.

 BULL AND SCHWARZ DIGITS PLAN A EUROPEAN SOVEREIGN AI STACK (3 MINUTE
READ) [14]

 Bull and Schwarz Digits signed an agreement to develop AI
infrastructure for European organizations in regulated and strategic
sectors. The planned architecture combines STACKIT's European cloud
with Bull's computing hardware, liquid cooling, and AI software.

MISCELLANEOUS

 NATIONAL GRID PLANS MAJOR SUBSTATION FOR DATA CENTER EXPANSION (3
MINUTE READ) [15]

 National Grid plans a major substation at Teesside to support a
proposed data center, battery storage, and other power-intensive
industries. Its initial 1,440 MVA capacity is scheduled to come online
by the end of 2029, illustrating the long infrastructure timelines
behind AI expansion.

 CIO SURVEY EXPOSES THE GAP BETWEEN AI ROLLOUT AND READINESS (10
MINUTE READ) [16]

 Accenture's survey of 300 technology leaders finds 64% are scaling
enterprise AI deployment, but only 7% are optimizing it at scale. User
adoption and training are the most frequently cited execution barrier,
while nearly half still require manual approvals for agents in IT
operations.

 UK BUSINESSES FACE LOOMING DIGITAL PHONE MIGRATION DEADLINE (3 MINUTE
READ) [17]

 Openreach warns that roughly 300,000 business copper lines remain
active ahead of the January deadline, including connections supporting
payment terminals, alarms, and building systems.

 AI AGENTS FUEL PROJECTED 55% GROWTH IN IDENTITY SPENDING (3 MINUTE
READ) [18]

 Juniper Research forecasts global identity and access management
spending will rise from $26.2 billion in 2026 to $40.5 billion by 2031
as businesses manage expanding populations of AI agents and other
nonhuman identities.
