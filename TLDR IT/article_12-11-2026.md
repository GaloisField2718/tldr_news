NEWS & TRENDS

 WORKING WITH CLAUDE SHEETS, WHITEBOARDS, AND INTERACTIVE CONNECTORS
(5 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Anthropic announced new workplace features, including Claude
Meetings, which brings Claude into video calls to begin working on
tasks while participants are still talking. New interactive connectors
allow users to open and work within third-party applications like
Adobe, Notion, Atlassian, Salesforce, and HubSpot directly inside the
Claude interface.

 ANTHROPIC TARGETS WORKPLACE COLLABORATION WITH CLAUDE MEETINGS (5
MINUTE READ) [8]

 Anthropic has introduced Claude Meetings, Whiteboards, and Sheets,
expanding its AI assistant into workplace collaboration and
productivity. Claude Meetings can participate in Google Meet calls,
take notes, retrieve information, and create action plans during
discussions. The meeting feature is in beta for Team and Enterprise
customers, bringing Anthropic into closer competition with Microsoft
and Google.

 YANDEX RESTORES 97% OF CUSTOMER DATA AFTER DRONE ATTACKS, THE REST IS
PERMANENTLY LOST (3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Russian cloud provider Yandex recovered approximately 97% of affected
customer data following drone strikes on three data centers in
October. The remaining 2.84% is permanently lost due to hardware
damage and the absence of customer backups. Yandex says it will enable
backups by default and expand its infrastructure across additional
independent locations.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 PLATFORM ENGINEERING NEEDS TO BECOME MORE HUMAN…AND LESS (11 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 The rise of AI agents and non-technical builders requires platform
engineering to evolve from serving predictable engineers to supporting
a chaotic mix of human improvisers and precise automated agents. To
manage this shift safely, platforms must automate unwritten guardrails
into machine-readable policies, enforce scoped identities, and adopt
control loops inspired by Kubernetes.

 CISCO TO LINK ALL FUTURE PRODUCTS TO ITS SAAS-Y CLOUD CONTROL
MANAGEMENT TOOL (5 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Cisco announced that all new products starting in 2027 will integrate
with its Cloud Control management platform, providing a unified
interface for networking, security, and observability. The service
will expand internationally beginning in February, with additional
countries following in May. Cisco also plans to simplify ThousandEyes
licensing and expand its Live Protect capabilities across more
networking products.

 GARTNER RESEARCH: ONLY 11% OF ORGANIZATIONS TURN AI AMBITION INTO
REAL VALUE (SPONSOR) [12]

 Most organizations are increasing AI investment, but very few
translate it into measurable business outcomes. Gartner's 2027 CIO
Agenda [12] reveals four critical disconnects and the three practices
leading organizations use to bridge them. Learn how to govern AI
economics, redirect investment, and redesign work [12].

 IBM AND MICROSOFT'S IMAGINE ENTERPRISE PUTS AGENTIC GOVERNANCE CLAIMS
TO THE TEST (2 MINUTE READ) [13]

 IBM Consulting and Microsoft have jointly launched Imagine
Enterprise, an agentic AI platform targeting regulated industries
including financial services, insurance, and retail. The platform
combines IBM Consulting's library of over 6,000 prebuilt AI agents
with Microsoft's Azure cloud, Entra identity management, Purview data
governance, and Defender and Sentinel security tooling. Enterprises
using the platform remain responsible for satisfying their own
regulatory obligations, even where the platform supplies governance
tooling.

 US BIT BARN BOOM SEES ENERGY NEEDS GROW 10X AS FAST AS GRID CAPACITY
(5 MINUTE READ) [14]

 US data center electricity demand has grown approximately 19%
annually since 2000, compared with roughly 2% annual growth in grid
capacity, according to an analysis by insurance broker Howden. The
firm estimates data centers could need to source as much as 58% of
their electricity outside the grid by 2030 to maintain their current
share of grid capacity. Power availability, lengthy connection delays,
and on-site generation costs are becoming major constraints on AI
infrastructure expansion.

 ADOBE OPENS CREATIVE CLOUD APPS TO THIRD-PARTY AI ASSISTANTS (7
MINUTE READ) [15]

 Adobe plans to let AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude directly
edit content within Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere, and After
Effects through Model Context Protocol integrations. The capability is
scheduled for private beta in early 2027 and aims to preserve manual
editing control while automating repetitive creative tasks. Analysts
see the move as part of a broader shift toward AI assistants becoming
the primary interface for enterprise applications.

 GRAPHRAG IS THE NEW WAY TO HOLD KNOWLEDGE (15 MINUTE READ) [16]

 GraphRAG significantly outperforms traditional RAG for business AI
applications by storing knowledge as interconnected entities and
relationships rather than disconnected text fragments. While GraphRAG
offers superior performance on multi-step reasoning and broad
summaries, it involves substantially higher indexing and processing
costs, though newer lightweight implementations are reducing that
overhead.

 ABAP PLATFORM BECOMING AN ENTERPRISE-GRADE EXECUTION BACKBONE FOR
AGENTIC AI (2 MINUTE READ) [17]

 The ABAP platform is an enterprise-grade execution backbone for
agentic AI within the Autonomous Enterprise vision. The role of agents
acts as an abstraction layer for outcome-driven interactions and
upcoming developments include governance, reliability, scaling, and
the ABAP Model Context Protocol server.
