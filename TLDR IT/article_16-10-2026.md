 WHAT'S NEW IN WINDOWS 11: EVERYDAY EXPERIENCES, REIMAGINED (6 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Microsoft's latest Windows announcement emphasizes lower memory use,
more reliable Bluetooth and sign-in, and fewer disruptive restarts. A
redesigned Search experience also lets users change settings directly
from search results, but that feature is currently limited to Windows
Insiders, with broader rollout planned later this year.

 GOOGLE WARNS SUSPECTED CHINA-LINKED HACKERS COULD EXPLOIT BEYONDTRUST
FLAW (1 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Google warned of potential widespread exploitation of a BeyondTrust
Remote Support flaw already used in targeted attacks in August.
BeyondTrust says its hosted services have been patched. Self-hosted
customers should apply the relevant BT26-05 patches immediately,
including the precautionary patch for Privileged Remote Access.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 AI SAFETY IS NOT A CORPORATE PLOT (14 MINUTE READ) [10]

 AI safety warnings cannot simply be dismissed as corporate marketing
or attempts to block competition. Commercial incentives do not
automatically invalidate genuine safety concerns.

 STEERING AI BEYOND AUTOMATION (25 MINUTE READ) [11]

 AI research focuses too heavily on replacing human work rather than
helping people do it better. This post proposes systems that make
their processes easier to inspect, respond quickly enough for
real-time collaboration, and let people continuously guide the work
instead of merely reviewing the final output.

 IT REINVENTION REQUIRES MASTERING THE ART OF TRANSLATION (14 MINUTE
READ) [12]

 Technical accomplishments gain business credibility only when their
impact is clear. Fewer system incidents should be described not just
as an operational improvement, but as reduced disruption, lower
recovery costs, and protection for the company's earnings and customer
experience.

 INTRODUCING GROK VOICE 3.0 (2 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Grok Voice 3.0 is available through the Voice Agent API at $0.08 per
minute, with the company reporting sub-second latency and improved
voice-agent performance. Applications using grok-voice-latest have
already been switched to the new model automatically, a detail worth
checking for teams running production voice workflows.

 GMAIL'S AI INBOX IS ROLLING OUT TO PAID WORKSPACE USERS (2 MINUTE
READ) [15]

 Google is expanding AI Inbox to eligible paid Workspace users,
surfacing suggested tasks and summaries of important email topics.
Availability is initially US-English only: Rapid Release began October
12, Scheduled Release begins October 26, and Starter and Education
editions will follow later. Google lists no feature-specific admin
control.

MISCELLANEOUS

 A FULLY PROGRAMMABLE SECURE WEB GATEWAY (13 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Cloudflare's new Forward action lets teams run custom code on web
traffic to build access-justification pages, modify requests, or
trigger Slack alerts without maintaining separate proxy servers.
Forward is in public beta, while custom functions for gateway policy
decisions are expected before year-end. Separate custom validation for
data loss prevention is available now, including checks to determine
whether a detected credential is actually active.

 APPLE'S ‘MINIONS' TECH TURNS IDLE MACS INTO AN AI DATA CENTER (5
MINUTE READ) [17]

 Apple's internal Minions system pools spare processing capacity
across Macs to run AI inference jobs, returning interrupted tasks to a
queue when a device becomes unavailable. It suggests another use for
existing employee hardware, but this remains an internal Apple
technology, not a publicly available product or an announced
enterprise service.

 TINYLITTLEOFFICE, WHERE THE LINK IS THE DOCUMENT (3 MINUTE READ) [18]


 TinyLittleOffice squeezes documents, spreadsheets, and presentations
into the URL itself rather than storing a conventional file on a
server. It is a clever lightweight approach to sharing, although links
can become unwieldy, images and video cannot be uploaded, and there is
no Google Docs-style live collaboration.
