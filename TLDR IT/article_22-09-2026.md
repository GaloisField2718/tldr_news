ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 REVOKING THE TOKEN DIDN'T KILL THE BACKDOOR (7 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Traditional incident response steps like revoking OAuth tokens fail
when an implant can dynamically replace its entire credential set and
OneDrive command-and-control identity via a single remote task.
Investigators should treat application registrations as the durable
object of compromise rather than relying solely on token revocation,
and hunt on the identity plane using cloud telemetry.

 WHO OWNS THE DATA? (8 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Data ownership is fundamentally about accountability for business
meaning, rules, and quality rather than technical possession or
storage. While different teams across an organization may store, move,
or consume data, business stakeholders remain responsible for defining
its rules and metrics. Roles such as data owners, data stewards, and
data custodians are distinct in managing enterprise data.

 VMWARE HAS QUIETLY WALKED BACK ITS SMARTNIC AMBITIONS (2 MINUTE READ)
[13]

 VMware has quietly stopped selling its distributed firewalls for
SmartNICs, walking back ambitions to bring hyperscale networking
models to on-premise private clouds. The effort involved complex
microcode integration with hardware from AMD and Nvidia, but struggled
with Intel chips and ultimately failed to attract paying customers as
conventional NICs improved. While standalone SmartNIC sales have
ended, VMware's Distributed Services Engine remains supported within
its Cloud Foundation suite.

 FASTLY LAUNCHES RUNTIME CONTROLS FOR ENTERPRISE AI (4 MINUTE READ)
[14]

 Fastly launched AI Runtime Control and AI Firewall to manage model
access, spending, routing, and agent interactions from its edge
platform. The company says machine-generated traffic now accounts for
more than half of traffic across its network.

 PRESIDIO OPENS ENTERPRISE AI INFRASTRUCTURE LAB (3 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Presidio opened its PATH AI lab to customers, allowing companies to
test workloads across private cloud, public cloud, and neocloud
infrastructure before committing to production deployments. The lab
includes Dell servers running Nvidia B300 and RTX PRO 6000 GPUs.
