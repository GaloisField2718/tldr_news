NEWS & TRENDS

 VMWARE RETURNS TO SMALLER DEPLOYMENTS WITH VSPHERE EXPRESS (4 MINUTE
READ) [6]

 Broadcom introduced vSphere Express for deployments of up to 128
processor cores, giving smaller customers an alternative to its larger
VMware bundles. It includes live VM migration, automated recovery, and
distributed switching, although individual installations cannot be
managed centrally.

 CROWDSTRIKE INTRODUCES A KILL SWITCH FOR AI AGENTS (4 MINUTE READ)
[7]

 CrowdStrike's new AI Kill Switch lets security teams block agents'
access to AI models through connected gateways. Broader controls,
including terminating endpoint processes and revoking agent
permissions, remain in development.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 WHAT DOES DISASTER RECOVERY LOOK LIKE IN AN AI-CENTRIC WORLD? (6
MINUTE READ) [8]

 As businesses increasingly rely on AI as operational infrastructure,
outages and dependencies outside direct control highlight the need to
update disaster recovery strategies. Experts suggest defining a
minimum viable company that includes AI models, RAG data, and identity
dependencies, while accounting for non-deterministic model outputs and
fast-acting autonomous AI agents.

 ACCELERATING ENGINEERING FOR THE PHYSICAL WORLD (7 MINUTE READ) [9]

 OpenAI is sharing advances in how its models help engineers design,
simulate, and build physical products across mechanical and electrical
engineering. In an internal test, the company used GPT-6 Astra to
define requirements and design the enclosure, printed circuit board,
and software integrations for a device without direct human edits.
Additionally, OpenAI is working with industry collaborators such as
Chip Ganassi Racing and Mazda to test these capabilities on real-world
engineering and prototyping problems.

 DISCONNECTED AI PROJECTS ARE MULTIPLYING ENTERPRISE COSTS (4 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Separate departmental AI projects create hidden costs through
duplicate platform purchases, repeated data preparation, and
unmonitored model usage. Maintain a shared inventory of tools and
pilots, then compare each project's operating costs against its
measurable benefits.

 BUSINESS ONTOLOGY: GOVERNED CONTEXT FOR AI (6 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Snowflake has announced the upcoming public preview of Business
Ontology, a new business modeling and governance layer within
Snowflake Horizon Context designed to provide AI agents with governed
business context. The feature allows organizations to define concepts,
metrics, and relationships, linking them to underlying semantic views
to ensure AI models rely on approved enterprise definitions. Snowflake
also helped found Apache Ossie, an open-source standard for semantic
models to enable interoperability across tools.

 OTARI BY MOZILLA AI: YOUR OPERATING SYSTEM FOR AI GOVERNANCE (2
MINUTE READ) [13]

 Mozilla AI has launched Otari, an open-source operating system and
gateway designed for AI governance and multi-provider choice. The
platform allows organizations to enforce tool restrictions, manage
user budgets, and trace AI sessions in one place, while enabling
seamless switching between model providers through a single API config
change.

 MONGODB BRINGS LIVE ATLAS DATA INTO MICROSOFT 365 COPILOT (4 MINUTE
READ) [14]

 MongoDB's generally available Atlas connector lets Microsoft 365
Copilot answer questions using live operational data. The integration
uses a managed MCP server to query Atlas directly, allowing users to
investigate orders, incidents, and business metrics without relying on
a static data copy.

 ADOBE BRINGS CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE AGENTS INTO MAJOR AI PLATFORMS (3
MINUTE READ) [15]

 Adobe's CX Enterprise Coworker is now available within ChatGPT,
Claude, Databricks Genie, Gemini for business, and Microsoft Copilot.
The integrations let marketers retrieve data and coordinate customer
experience tasks across connected systems, with AWS, IBM, and
Snowflake support planned.

MISCELLANEOUS

 SEAMLESS GRAPH INTELLIGENCE FOR YOUR SNOWFLAKE DATA ESTATE: THE NEO4J
AND SNOWFLAKE PARTNERSHIP (7 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Neo4j and Snowflake have partnered to integrate Neo4j's graph
intelligence capabilities into the Snowflake AI Data Cloud, aiming to
improve AI reasoning and enterprise data insights without
time-consuming ETL processes. The partnership introduces three core
deployment options: Virtual Graph for zero-data-movement querying,
Graph Analytics for Snowflake for on-demand SQL-based graph
algorithms, and AuraDB integration for real-time mission-critical
workloads.

 MICROSOFT ROUNDS UP WINDOWS MANAGEMENT CHANGES AND UPCOMING DEADLINES
(6 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Microsoft's latest IT roundup covers Windows recovery improvements
and Intune controls that let standard users change approved network
settings without broader admin access. It also flags Windows Update
certificate expirations in 2027 and the January 15 end of CNAME-based
discovery for Windows MDM-only enrollment.
