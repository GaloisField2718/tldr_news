NEWS & TRENDS

 SCANNING 7.6 PETABYTES OF HUGGINGFACE TRAINING DATA FOR SECRETS (14
MINUTE READ) [8]

 Truffle Security Co. says it scanned 7.6 petabytes of public Hugging
Face datasets and found 221,303 live, unique credentials across 6,003
datasets. The verified secrets included software supply-chain tokens,
cloud credentials, database logins, communications keys, and
AI-provider keys that the company says represent at least $920,000 in
potential annual inference charges at default spending caps.

 SCALE AI HIRES FORMER GOOGLE CLOUD COO AS CEO (3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Scale AI appointed Francis deSouza as CEO as it expands beyond
training data and evaluations into enterprise AI applications. The
company expects its applications business to become larger than its
original data operation within 18 months.

 EU PLANS SEVEN AI GIGAFACTORIES IN $11.4B COMPUTE PUSH (4 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 The EU plans to invest €10 billion in seven AI gigafactories, each
designed to house at least 100,000 advanced chips. Officials hope the
funding will attract another €20 billion privately and reduce
Europe's reliance on foreign AI infrastructure.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 I'M (MOSTLY) PICKING MODELS ON SPEED NOW, NOT INTELLIGENCE (5 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 AI models have reached a sufficient intelligence level for many
everyday tasks, making inference speed a more important factor in
model choice. 100–200 tokens per second feels fast for human use,
while tool calls and human oversight limit the benefits of faster
models and intensifying competition may drive prices down.

 MOST CORPORATE IT NOW RUNS OUTSIDE COMPANY-OWNED DATA CENTERS (5
MINUTE READ) [12]

 Companies now place 46% of their workloads with cloud and colocation
providers, compared with 44% in their own data centers. AI is also
making capacity planning harder while worsening staffing shortages and
the cost of infrastructure failures.

LAUNCHES & PARTNERSHIPS

 GYRUS-DEV/FROSTY: AI AGENT FOR SNOWFLAKE (GITHUB REPO) [13]

 Frosty is an open-source, self-hosted framework from Gyrus Inc that
converts plain-English requests into Snowflake queries and
administrative operations. The 153-agent system covers data
engineering, security, governance, cost monitoring, and inspection,
while its safety layer blocks DROP statements and requires approval
for CREATE OR REPLACE operations. Users can run it locally with their
Snowflake credentials and a supported language model.

 META SEES AN ENTERPRISE BUSINESS BEYOND AI AGENTS (3 MINUTE READ)
[14]

 Meta says it could sell businesses APIs, AI agents, compute capacity,
and other enterprise services. The strategy could turn its enormous
internal AI infrastructure investment into a new revenue stream beyond
advertising.

 STATIC DEVIRTUALIZATION OF TENCENT VM (12 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Classic virtual-machine obfuscators remain vulnerable to flexible
lifting, recompilation, and guided symbolic execution. A static
devirtualization approach for Tencent VM obfuscation analyzes the VM
dispatcher, boxed native instructions, and CET/SEH compatibility
mechanisms to symbolically recover bytecode-driven control flow and
virtualized loops.

 UBER IS BUILDING AN AUTONOMOUS VEHICLE EMPIRE, AND HERE'S EVERY
COMPANY IT'S USING TO DO IT (18 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Uber has partnered with or invested in more than 30 autonomous
vehicle companies over the past two years, rebuilding an AV strategy
focused on integrating outside technology and fleets globally.
TechCrunch's tracker details deals involving robotaxis, autonomous
trucks, sidewalk robots, drones, fleet operators and vehicle makers,
including major investments in Aurora and Lucid.
