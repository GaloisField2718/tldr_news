NEWS & TRENDS

 MICROSOFT 365 OUTAGE DISRUPTS EMAIL, TEAMS, AND INTUNE (3 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 A Microsoft 365 outage on November 12 disrupted email access, Teams
features, and administrative tools, including Intune. Microsoft
reported failing server health checks and an overwhelmed server
process, with recovery efforts including traffic rerouting and server
restarts.

 GEMINI DECISIONS API: FAST, CALIBRATED MULTIMODAL DECISIONS (6 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 The Gemini Decisions API, powered by the new Gemini Decision 1 model,
is designed for fast, closed-set decisions such as tool selection,
policy checks, and model routing. The model processes multimodal
inputs and up to 1,000 independent questions in a single forward pass,
returning probabilities and confidence scores with an end-to-end
latency of 150 to 250 milliseconds.

 AI-RELATED JOB CREATION TO OUTPACE LOSSES BY 2030, SAYS GARTNER (4
MINUTE READ) [9]

 Gartner forecasts that AI will create 10.6 million jobs and eliminate
8.9 million by 2030, excluding China and India. Separately, it expects
5 million jobs to be deferred as employers hold back hiring while
assessing AI's impact, with entry-level candidates particularly
exposed.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 WELCOME TO THE B2A ECONOMY (6 MINUTE READ) [10]

 AI agents are initiating a fundamental platform shift by
transitioning from answering questions to executing complex tasks on
behalf of users, creating a new "B2A" (Business-to-Agent) economy. As
agents increasingly drive web traffic, generate code, and make
purchasing decisions, businesses that treat AI agents as primary
customers will unlock new growth while others face strategic risks.
Early data indicates that AI-referred traffic yields higher engagement
and better conversion rates across retail, developer tools, and
software platforms.

 CLARIS HARBOR COULD TURN MAC FLEETS INTO ON-PREM AI SERVERS (5 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 Apple subsidiary Claris is previewing Harbor, which aims to turn
Apple silicon Macs into private AI servers. The article explores its
potential for businesses seeking local control over AI workloads,
while noting that product details remain limited.

 TALK IT THROUGH WITH GEMINI, NOW ON APPLE CARPLAY (3 MINUTE READ)
[14]

 Google is rolling out its Gemini app to Apple CarPlay globally,
enabling iOS users to have hands-free, conversational interactions
with Gemini Live from their car displays. The integration allows
drivers to prep for meetings, review daily briefs and emails from
Google Workspace, explore local landmarks, and use Gemini as a
brainstorming or rehearsal companion. Conversations sync automatically
with the Gemini app on the user's iPhone.

 SCALING AI AGENTS WITH OPEN STANDARDS (8 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Anthropic's donation of the Model Context Protocol (MCP) and Agent
Skills to the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation establishes
complementary open standards that help prevent fragmentation across AI
agent ecosystems. MCP standardizes live connections to tools and data
through a stateless transport model, while Agent Skills use
progressive disclosure to load procedural domain knowledge only when
needed. Together, they enable developers to build more reliable,
scalable, and interoperable production AI agents across platforms.

 UNITED AIRLINES BECOMES THE LATEST ENTERPRISE TO SHUT ITS DOOR TO
CUSTOMER BOTS (7 MINUTE READ) [16]

 United is restricting unauthorized personal AI agents, citing
unintended purchases, exposed account information, and regulatory
requirements. Analysts say inconsistent standards and difficulty
distinguishing bots from customers complicate enterprise support for
automated transactions.
