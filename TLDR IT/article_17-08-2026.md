NEWS & TRENDS

 IT'S FINAL! JUDGE SAYS HPE'S JUNIPER ACQUISITION IS COMPLETE (3
MINUTE READ) [6]

 A federal judge approved HPE's settlement with the Justice
Department, clearing the final legal challenge to its $14 billion
acquisition of Juniper Networks. The ruling removes lingering
uncertainty around the deal and lets HPE move ahead integrating
Juniper's networking portfolio into the combined company.

 CISCO RIDES ‘NETWORKING SUPERCYCLE' FOR STRONG Q4 (5 MINUTE READ)
[7]

 Cisco reported record quarterly revenue of $17.3 billion as
networking product orders jumped 40% year over year, with campus
orders up 20% and data center networking orders rising 25% for the
year. Cisco says only about 7% of its campus switching installed base
has been refreshed so far, suggesting the broader enterprise upgrade
cycle is still early.

 SALESFORCE, SERVICENOW DATA TARGETED IN ‘CITY-FORUM' ATTACKS (2
MINUTE READ) [8]

 Researchers identified a new campaign targeting data stored in
Salesforce and ServiceNow environments through their UI/API layers
rather than traditional infrastructure exploits. The attackers have
also been probing a little-documented native ServiceNow Service Portal
search endpoint, highlighting the growing risk around authenticated
SaaS access and APIs.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 WHY 6 GHZ WI-FI WILL MAKE OR BREAK THE MODERN ENTERPRISE (9 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 The additional spectrum available at 6 GHz can effectively triple
wireless capacity compared with legacy bands while moving modern
devices away from congested 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz channels. Wi-Fi 6E and
Wi-Fi 7 upgrades should now be treated as infrastructure modernization
rather than simply another wireless refresh.

 THE VENDOR CONSOLIDATION TRAP: WHEN ONE THROAT TO CHOKE COSTS MORE
THAN IT SAVES (7 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Reducing the number of technology vendors can simplify operations and
create short-term savings, but it can also destroy negotiating
leverage once switching becomes expensive. IT teams should keep at
least one credible alternative warm in major categories so vendors
know the customer can realistically walk away at renewal.

 APPLE'S PRICE HIKES ARE A WARNING TO IT (6 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Memory shortages are pushing hardware prices higher across Apple,
Dell, Lenovo, HP and other manufacturers, creating a new challenge for
IT procurement and device lifecycle planning. With DRAM supply
expected to remain constrained, enterprises may need to revisit
refresh schedules, purchasing commitments and hardware budgets.

 IBM PARTNERS WITH OPENAI TO DRIVE ENTERPRISE AI DEPLOYMENT (2 MINUTE

 IBM will embed OpenAI models and products including ChatGPT and Codex
into its consulting offerings while creating a dedicated OpenAI

partnership will focus on enterprise transformation, legacy

MISCELLANEOUS

 AN INDUSTRYWIDE POWER CRUNCH IS CHANGING HOW AMAZON RUNS ITS
E-COMMERCE EMPIRE (6 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Amazon is redistributing parts of its e-commerce infrastructure
across more AWS regions under an internal effort called Region Flex,
partly because power and data center capacity have become increasingly
constrained in major hubs. The project includes more than 100 software
migrations and illustrates how electricity availability is beginning
to influence application architecture and cloud-region strategy.

 WHITE HOUSE AUTHORIZES PRIVATE COMPANIES TO LAUNCH 'HACK-BACK'
CYBERATTACKS THAT DESTROY DATA AND SYSTEMS, TARGETING FOREIGN
CYBERCRIME ORGANIZATIONS (5 MINUTE READ) [15]

 A new US program allows vetted private companies to conduct
government-approved surveillance and disruptive cyber operations
against foreign cybercrime organizations. Individual operations
require federal approval, and participating companies can potentially
disrupt infrastructure controlled by targeted criminal groups.
