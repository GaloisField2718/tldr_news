NEWS & TRENDS

 GOOGLE CLOUD LETS TEAMS CAP PROJECT SPENDING (3 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Google Cloud has made spend caps generally available for eligible
services. Teams can use the Cloud Billing Budgets API to give every
new sandbox project a spending limit, then raise or remove it when a
developer needs more room.

 LINUX 7.4 SETS A RECORD WITH NEARLY 20,000 COMMITS (2 MINUTE READ)
[6]

 The Linux 7.4 merge window drew nearly 20,000 commits, the busiest in
kernel history. For comparison, Linux 7.3 took its entire release
cycle to reach 20,260 commits.

 AI IS FINDING BUGS FASTER THAN POSTGRESQL MAINTAINERS CAN REVIEW
FIXES (2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 A surge in AI-assisted bug reports and patches helped push PostgreSQL
19 past its usual fall release window. Maintainers still have to
verify proposed fixes and apply them to older versions. The project is
targeting November 19 for release.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 FIVE YEARS AFTER THE FORK, WHO STEERS OPENSEARCH? (6 MINUTE READ) [8]


 AWS created OpenSearch after Elastic changed the license for
Elasticsearch. Elastic has since restored an open-source licensing
option, but the projects have continued to diverge. This post examines
where OpenSearch has taken its own technical direction and how much
influence AWS still holds over its governance.

 ANDY JASSY THINKS BEDROCK COULD BECOME AS BIG AS EC2 (3 MINUTE READ)
[9]

 Amazon's CEO argues that companies will want to switch AI models as
price and performance change. His bet is that giving customers one
place to run models from several vendors could make Bedrock as large a
business as EC2, AWS' core computing service.

LAUNCHES & PARTNERSHIPS

 TENCENT CONNECTS WORKBUDDY TO ERP AND CRM SYSTEMS (3 MINUTE READ)
[10]

 Tencent's WorkBuddy office agent can now query business systems
through the Model Context Protocol. An employee can ask for a report
that draws on both customer and operations data, and the agent can
break the request into steps, retrieve records from each system, and
present the findings.

 SERVICENOW BRINGS OPENAI, ANTHROPIC, AND OKTA INTO ITS AI GOVERNANCE
ALLIANCE (3 MINUTE READ) [11]

 ServiceNow launched the Control Tower Alliance with 11 founding
partners spanning AI models, cloud infrastructure, identity, and
security. Members plan to deliver pretested integrations so customers
can use ServiceNow's Control Tower to govern AI systems from multiple
vendors.

 HOW DOCUSIGN TEACHES AGENTS TO READ AMENDED CONTRACTS (5 MINUTE READ)
[12]

 Figuring out what a company owes a supplier can mean reconciling the
signed contract with three amendments, a revised price schedule, and
an internal purchasing policy. In this interview, CTO Sagnik Nandy
explains how the company gives its Iris agents the context to answer
that kind of question.

 GITHUB ADDS THREE IP RANGES FOR WEBHOOK DELIVERIES (1 MINUTE READ)
[13]

 Teams that maintain a static firewall allowlist for GitHub webhooks
need to add three IP ranges to keep receiving deliveries. Teams that
automatically sync their allowlists from GitHub's Meta API already
pick up the additions.
