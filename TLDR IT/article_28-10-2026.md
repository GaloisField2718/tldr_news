NEWS & TRENDS

 SAP UNVEILS PHYSICAL AI AND EXTENDS THE AUTONOMOUS ENTERPRISE INTO
THE REAL WORLD (3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 SAP has unveiled its physical AI capabilities designed to connect
enterprise business systems with physical operations such as robots
and automated systems. At SAP TechEd, the company showcased
implementations with partners like Foxconn, Arçelik-LG, Intel, and
Holcim to demonstrate how business context can govern physical tasks
while automatically feeding structured execution data back into
enterprise workflows.

 MICROSOFT AUTHENTICATOR FLAW EXPOSES WORK ACCOUNTS (3 MINUTE READ)
[9]

 A patched flaw in Microsoft Authenticator for Android let malicious
apps impersonate trusted Microsoft apps and obtain work-account
tokens, exposing email, files, and calendars in researchers' tests.
Microsoft says exploitation requires a user to complete a sign-in flow
and reports no known attacks. The fix is available in version
6.2606.4246 and later.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 IS IT TIME TO RETIRE VPNS? (7 MINUTE READ) [10]

 As recurring vulnerabilities expose legacy VPN gateways to rapid
exploitation, organizations will need to transition to zero-trust
architecture. Traditional VPNs grant broad internal network access
once breached, a risk amplified by AI-driven vulnerability discovery.
Zero-trust models replace this perimeter approach by making internal
systems invisible to the public internet and restricting users and
agents to specific applications.

 CLOSING LINUX EDR GAPS (18 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Linux servers can show green in an EDR dashboard while collecting
little or no security telemetry because of kernel incompatibilities or
degraded sensor modes. Verify sensor health on each host before
expanding coverage or switching vendors, then pilot changes through
kernel updates and roll them out in stages. Container visibility and
after-hours alert response also need explicit coverage.

LAUNCHES & PARTNERSHIPS

 1 IN 3 ORGS RUN CODE ASSISTANTS. FEW HAVE A GOVERNANCE PROGRAM
(SPONSOR) [12]

 Code assistants like Cursor and Copilot are now in ~1/3 environments
Field Effect protects. Unlike chat tools, they execute: downloading
files and running scripts on developer endpoints with elevated
permissions. Field Effect's State of AI Adoption eBook [12] covers the
implications. Read the findings, plus more insights into agents and
on-device models: download the eBook. [12]

 IBM INTRODUCES IDENTITY AND CONTROL FOR AI AGENTS, SECURING FRONTIER
INTELLIGENCE FOR THE ENTERPRISE (9 MINUTE READ) [13]

 IBM announced new capabilities at TechXchange 2026 to provide
enterprise security and governance for AI agents, combining verified
identities, access controls, and performance evaluations. The updates
extend HashiCorp Vault to issue short-lived credentials for non-human
identities, integrate with NVIDIA OpenShell, and use watsonx
Orchestrate to discover, contain, and evaluate agents across
third-party platforms.

 ORACLE ADVANCES AUTONOMOUS AI LAKEHOUSE WITH BUSINESS SEMANTIC LAYER
AND EXPANDED SUPPORT FOR ICEBERG DATA (3 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Oracle is expanding its Autonomous AI Lakehouse with a Business
Semantic Layer and a Business Simulation Engine designed to ground AI
agents in enterprise data and improve analytical decision-making. The
company also announced expanded Apache Iceberg support to simplify
data management using SQL. The new capabilities are intended to help
organizations scale agentic applications and improve data consistency.

MISCELLANEOUS

 CISCO RETHINKS PRIVILEGED INFRASTRUCTURE ACCESS (4 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Cisco has introduced Privileged Infrastructure Access, a new offering
designed to provide network administrators and automated agents with
secure, identity-bound connections to critical network devices. The
system replaces long-lived credentials and static access paths with
short-lived, task-specific privileges that expire once work is
completed, reducing vulnerabilities that can be exploited by
AI-powered adversaries.

 FROM ORBIT TO ENTERPRISE: THE NEXT TABULAR AI FRONTIER FOR SAP AND
HPE (2 MINUTE READ) [16]

 SAP announced plans to deploy its tabular AI foundation model,
SAP-RPT, on the HPE Spaceborne Computer-2 aboard the International
Space Station. The deployment aims to test real-time edge telemetry
analysis, anomaly triage, and predictive maintenance in space before
applying the same structured-data models to terrestrial supply chains
and enterprise operations by early 2027.
