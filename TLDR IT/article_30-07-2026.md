NEWS & TRENDS

 AI-DRIVEN ATTACKS INCREASED 56% LAST YEAR, AND DATA BREACH COSTS ARE
UP 12% (5 MINUTE READ) [4]

 AI-driven attacks rose 56% over the past year and added an average of
$1 million to breach costs, according to IBM's analysis of incidents
at 602 organizations. The average breach now costs $4.99 million
globally, while organizations using AI and automation extensively in
security saved an average of $1.93 million per incident.

 CISCO CLOSE TO RELEASING MORE AI MODELS, THIS TIME FOR DEEP
NETWORKING OPS (3 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Cisco is preparing to release AI models trained specifically on
routing, switching, and network troubleshooting, alongside its Cloud
Control agentic operations platform. Cloud Control will let
administrators diagnose issues across Cisco products through a single
interface, explain the potential impact of actions such as reboots,
and support on-prem deployments, with US availability planned by the
end of August.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 “STATEFUL SYSTEMS ARE INCREDIBLY HARD TO BUILD”: HOW PERPLEXITY
THINKS ABOUT AI AGENT SANDBOXES (6 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Perplexity's SPACE platform separates short-lived Firecracker microVM
sandboxes from durable sessions that can be paused, resumed, moved, or
forked across clusters without losing an agent's work. The
architecture uses frequent snapshots, isolated credential handling,
and network controls to securely support long-running agents at a
scale of millions of sandbox creations and tens of millions of
reconnects.

 WHY INTUNE DEVICES BECAME NONCOMPLIANT AFTER THE JULY WINDOWS UPDATE
(8 MINUTE READ) [7]

 A Windows regression caused some newly enrolled or recovered Intune
devices to incorrectly report BitLocker, Secure Boot, and Code
Integrity as noncompliant, even when those controls were enabled.
Microsoft initially reduced the impact through an Intune service
change and recommends installing preview update KB5101684 for the
complete Windows-side fix.

LAUNCHES & PARTNERSHIPS

 SNOWFLAKE LAUNCHES AI AGENT GOVERNANCE LAYER TO TRACK ACTIVITY,
CONTROL COSTS (3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Snowflake unveiled Cortex AI Gateway, a runtime control plane for
tracking agent actions, enforcing access policies, and monitoring
token usage and costs across models, tools, MCP servers, and
enterprise systems. Built using technology acquired from Natoma, the
service integrates with Snowflake's Horizon Catalog and is expected to
enter public preview soon.

 ABNORMAL AI EXTENDS BEHAVIORAL SECURITY TO IDENTITIES, AI SYSTEMS,
AND INSIDER THREATS (4 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Abnormal AI launched three products that extend its behavioral
security platform into identity threat detection, AI governance, and
fraudulent applicant screening. The tools correlate email, identity,
SaaS, agent, and recruiting signals to flag compromised sessions,
risky AI activity, suspicious help-desk requests, and potentially
fabricated candidates before access is granted.

MISCELLANEOUS

 RECO ENHANCES AI RUNTIME WITH BROWSER-BASED AI SECURITY AND AUTOMATED
REMEDIATION (3 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Reco expanded its AI Runtime platform with browser-based discovery of
unsanctioned AI usage, automated permission remediation, and planned
controls that analyze and block risky prompts, responses, and tool
calls in real time. Browser Guard is in early access, while broader
runtime enforcement across agents and MCP connections is expected to
become generally available in October.

 US GOVERNMENT BANS NEW FOREIGN-MADE HUMANOIDS, ROBOT DOGS, AND SOLAR
INVERTERS, CITING RISKS TO NATIONAL SECURITY (3 MINUTE READ) [11]

 The FCC banned imports of new foreign-made humanoid robots, robot
dogs, and power inverters over concerns that connected devices could
be remotely controlled, used for surveillance, or exploited in
cyberattacks. Existing installations are unaffected, and exceptions
may be granted for devices deemed safe.

 YOUR AI AGENTS CAN REACH DATA NO ONE APPROVED (5 MINUTE READ) [12]

 A 1Password survey found that 71% of organizations' AI agents can
access sensitive data, while one-third of developers reported a
security incident tied to overprivileged non-human identities.

 SHADOW AI INCIDENT RESPONSE BEGINS WITH LOGS THAT MAY ALREADY BE GONE
(4 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Shadow AI investigations often stall because endpoint and firewall
logs have already rolled over, leaving organizations unable to prove
what data was shared or whether their controls worked.
