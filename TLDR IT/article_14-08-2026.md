NEWS & TRENDS

 IBM PARTNERS WITH OPENAI FOR ENTERPRISE AI (3 MINUTE READ) [4]

 IBM is launching a dedicated OpenAI consulting practice and plans to
certify tens of thousands of consultants. The companies will jointly
build AI offerings for industries including finance, government,
telecom, and retail.

 CISCO'S AI INFRASTRUCTURE ORDERS HIT $9.3B (3 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Cisco booked $4 billion in hyperscaler AI infrastructure orders last
quarter, bringing its FY2026 total to $9.3 billion. It expects AI
infrastructure revenue to climb to roughly $7.5 billion in FY2027.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 THE WRONG DEFAULTS IS WHY ENTERPRISE AI AGENTS FAIL AT ADOPTION (7
MINUTE READ) [6]

 Enterprise AI agents fail to gain broad adoption because
industry-standard interfaces force users to make too many engineering
decisions. The proposed alternative is a simpler default: one
persistent, general-purpose agent embedded in workplace chat, using
generic API access and live data rather than complex agent
configurations, knowledge bases, or fine-tuning.

 WHY CAPITAL ONE CHOSE OPEN MODELS FOR ITS AI AGENTS (7 MINUTE READ)
[7]

 Capital One built its multi-agent platform around open-weight models
as it pushes toward enterprise-wide AI. The approach gives it more
control over deployment, customization, and infrastructure.

 ENTERPRISE SSD PRICES ARE UP 6.5X IN A YEAR (5 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Prices for 30TB enterprise SSDs have risen roughly 6.5x year over
year, significantly changing the economics of AI infrastructure.
Storage is becoming a major cost factor for large GPU deployments.

LAUNCHES & PARTNERSHIPS

 A10 NETWORKS INTRODUCES AI GATEWAY TO SECURE AND MANAGE ENTERPRISE AI
(3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 A10 Networks has announced the general availability of the A10 AI
Gateway, a centralized control plane for routing, governing,
monitoring, and managing the costs of enterprise AI agents,
applications, and large language models. The company says it can route
requests by complexity, enforce identity-based access and usage
limits, and run in customer-controlled environments including
on-premises, private-cloud, and air-gapped deployments.

 WRITER LAUNCHES PALMYRA X6 FOR CHEAPER AI AGENTS (5 MINUTE READ) [10]


 Writer says Palmyra X6 can cut agent costs by 52% as enterprises face
growing token bills. The model is optimized for long-running agentic
workloads.

 ORACLE EXPANDS ITS ENTERPRISE AI STACK (4 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Oracle added NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Lightning, multi-node H100 serving,
expanded on-demand models, and new IAM controls to OCI Enterprise AI.
The updates give enterprises more flexibility over models and
infrastructure.

MISCELLANEOUS

 AWS BRINGS AI AGENT OBSERVABILITY ACROSS CLOUDS (8 MINUTE READ) [12]

 AWS detailed how AgentCore Observability can monitor agents running
on-premises, Azure, GCP, or AWS. OpenTelemetry telemetry feeds into
one dashboard for tracing actions, token usage, and reliability.

 MCP'S NEW SESSION MODEL SHIFTS MORE RESPONSIBILITY TO APPLICATIONS (6
MINUTE READ) [13]

 MCP's latest architecture makes models carry session handles rather
than relying on persistent server sessions. That improves scalability
but leaves applications responsible for reliable correlation,
authorization, and session management.
