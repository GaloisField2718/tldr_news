NEWS & TRENDS

 OPENAI SAYS ASTRA MAY HAVE REACHED CRITICAL CYBER THRESHOLD (2 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 OpenAI says preliminary evaluations indicate that its unreleased
Astra model may meet the Critical cybersecurity capability threshold
in its Preparedness Framework, although the classification is not
final. The company has tightened controls around Astra, including
isolated environments, restricted access, monitoring, and stronger
model-weight protection, while delaying broader availability until it
can be deployed more safely.

 AMAZON'S NEW DATA CENTER COULD BE POWERED BY ONE OF AMERICA'S BIGGEST
POLLUTERS (4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Amazon's planned Pecos County, Texas data center will initially rely
on a dedicated 7.65 GW natural-gas power project with 35 turbines. The
project highlights the growing tension between rapidly expanding AI
infrastructure and hyperscalers' climate commitments.

ANALYSIS & OPINIONS

 CHANGING DEVTOOLS IS CHEAP. OWNING THEM ISN'T (7 MINUTE READ) [9]

 AI agents make changing devtools cheaper but do not eliminate the
attention and maintenance costs of owning personalized forks. Rather
than widespread direct source modification, the likely future is to
maintain core tools with clear extension points and reusable building
blocks, preserving shared semantics for teams while enabling
specialized workflows.

 WHY AI INFRASTRUCTURE PLANNING MUST HAPPEN NOW (6 MINUTE READ) [10]

 AI inference and agent workloads are forcing enterprises to rethink
infrastructure across compute, networking, memory, cloud, and edge
systems. Flexible, interoperable architectures will matter more than
simply adding GPUs.

 AMAZON DSP ADVERTISERS GAIN A SINGLE GLOBAL ACCOUNT ACROSS 34
COUNTRIES (10 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Amazon Ads is automatically upgrading existing Amazon DSP-only
advertiser accounts into unified advertiser accounts spanning 34
countries. The change combines programmatic buying, Sponsored Ads,
Amazon Marketing Cloud, billing, permissions, and first-party data
management under one login, while preserving legacy identifiers and
adding new global and regional IDs for API access.

 OpenAI has acquired NextSlide, whose product turned prompts,

AI-generated productivity artifacts.

 WHY KNOWLEDGE GRAPH PROJECTS FAIL – AND HOW TO MAKE THEM SUCCEED
(11 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Knowledge graph projects usually fail because of flawed modeling,
unclear purpose, poor scope, weak long-term planning, and
inappropriate operational practices rather than because of a
particular technology stack. The recommended approach is to start with
use cases, build governed data incrementally, prefer federated graphs
where appropriate, expose controlled APIs, and let AI query graphs
instead of stuffing graph content into context windows.

 BIGQUERY EXPANDS DATA TRANSFERS WITH DIRECT ICEBERG INGESTION (5
MINUTE READ) [15]

 Google Cloud added direct ingestion from sources including Amazon S3,
Azure Blob Storage, and Google Cloud Storage into BigQuery-managed
Apache Iceberg tables. The update makes it easier to maintain
interoperable data architectures across multiple clouds.
