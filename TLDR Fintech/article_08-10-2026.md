NEWS & TRENDS

 VALON RAISES $150M SERIES D AT A $2.3B VALUATION TO DEPLOY AI ACROSS
MORTGAGE SERVICING (5 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Valon raised $150 million led by Ribbit Capital, with participation
from Andreessen Horowitz and existing investors, after opening ValonOS
to third-party mortgage servicers and signing more than $200 million
of ARR within six months. The company says 1 in 6 US mortgages is now
signed up to run on ValonOS, while its Ditto AI agent automates
complex servicing work such as homeowner communications, escrow
analysis, complaint research, and payment reallocations on top of a
unified system of record.

 PLAID LAUNCHES CREDIT, FRAUD AND PAYMENT RISK AI MODELS (2 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 Open finance fintech Plaid has unveiled AI models designed to help
financial firms make better lending decisions, catch more fraud, and
reduce payment risk. Plaid notes that millions of US adults fall
outside of the traditional credit lens, making them difficult to
evaluate with traditional scores alone. To help with this, the firm is
tapping into cash flow data, which shows how people earn, spend, and
pay their bills, giving lenders a fuller picture of their ability to
repay.

 PINE LABS LAUNCHES UNIFIED PLATFORM FOR MERCHANTS IN SINGAPORE (2
MINUTE READ) [8]

 Pine Labs, a global payments technology company, today announced a
unified platform for merchants in Singapore that combines payment
acceptance, acquiring, instalment payments, and customer engagement.

DEEP DIVES & REPORTS

 JOHN COLLISON SAYS AI AGENTS WILL REWIRE INTERNET COMMERCE (28 MINUTE
PODCAST) [9]

 Stripe co-founder John Collison argues that AI agents will
increasingly replace humans as the primary actors in online commerce,
forcing Stripe to redesign its APIs and infrastructure for
machine-driven transactions. He points to acquisitions including
Bridge, Privy, Metronome, and OpenRouter as building blocks for this
shift across stablecoins, wallets, usage-based billing, and model
routing, while computer use provides a compatibility layer for agents
to operate across today's web.

 CFTC UNVEILS PLAN TO BRING CRYPTO EXCHANGES UNDER FEDERAL OVERSIGHT
(3 MINUTE READ) [10]

 The CFTC proposed a framework that would let crypto exchanges
register under a new federal “crypto asset market” regime, with
leverage or margin serving as the main trigger for federal oversight
while non-leveraged exchanges could remain under state licensing. The
plan would also introduce broker, AML, proof-of-reserves, and
market-manipulation standards, marking a shift toward formal
rulemaking after the Clarity Act failed to advance in Congress.

 CLOUDFLARE'S $200B SHOT AT MACHINE PAYMENTS, $0.30 AT A TIME (15
MINUTE READ) [11]

 With Cloudflare's latest launch, agents can now pay at request time
rather than requiring subscriptions. The economics of software is
changing by becoming the economics of tokens and inference.
Intelligence acts upon data, and data is still gated all across
different parts of the web. In order to be able to answer questions
and book trips, robots need to query information and pull together a
lot of disconnected context. Little tentacles will extend from our
searches into machine brains, ready to purchase whatever mana is
needed to fulfill our wish.

 MODERN TREASURY APPLIES TO ESTABLISH NATIONAL TRUST BANK (3 MINUTE
READ) [13]

 Modern Treasury applied to the OCC to create a limited-purpose
national trust bank that would add federally supervised digital asset
custody and related fiat services to its existing payments platform.
If approved, customers could custody and move both stablecoins and
fiat through an integrated setup, while the proposed bank would remain
separate from Modern Treasury's software business and would not make
loans or issue stablecoins.

 STUUT RAISES $52.5M TO AUTOMATE INVOICE COLLECTIONS (3 MINUTE READ)
[14]

 Stuut connects to accounting, banking, sales, and payment systems so
its AI agents can pursue collections, resolve invoice disputes, and
match incoming cash rather than merely flagging work for employees.
The October 7 round, led by Insight Partners with Andreessen Horowitz
and Microsoft's M12 participating, brings total funding to $93 million
as the company expands from receivables into credit, lending, and
cross-border payments.

 HENRI STERN TAKES OVER STRIPE'S CRYPTO AND STABLECOIN STRATEGY (4
MINUTE READ) [15]

 Privy CEO Henri Stern is expanding his role to lead crypto and
stablecoins at Stripe, with a focus on building an open, global
developer platform that connects wallets, fiat rails, cards, asset
issuance, and stablecoin infrastructure. His roadmap centers on
practical business use cases, seamless fiat-crypto interoperability,
end-to-end products, open modular systems, and compliance, positioning
Stripe to make stablecoins easier for mainstream fintechs and
enterprises to adopt without rebuilding their entire stack.
