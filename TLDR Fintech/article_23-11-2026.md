NEWS & TRENDS

 RAMP IN TALKS TO RAISE $1B+ AT MORE THAN $70B VALUATION (2 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Ramp is in early discussions to raise at least $1 billion at a
valuation above $70 billion, which would be its third funding round in
a year and more than double its valuation over that period. The rapid
fundraising reflects continued investor demand for Ramp as it expands
beyond corporate cards into AI-powered expense management and broader
financial software.

 BILT SEEKS $18B VALUATION AS IT EXPANDS BEYOND RENT REWARDS (2 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 Bilt is seeking to raise $500 million at an $18 billion valuation, up
more than 60% from the $10.8 billion valuation it reached last year.
The company is using the new capital to expand beyond rent rewards and
build a broader neighborhood-based rewards platform.

 TPG TO TAKE BILL PRIVATE IN $6B DEAL WITH CORPAY (3 MINUTE READ) [10]


 TPG and Corpay agreed to acquire BILL Holdings for about $6 billion
in cash, offering $63.25 per share, a 31% premium to BILL's 60-day
volume-weighted average price before takeover reports emerged. Corpay
will invest $1.1 billion for roughly one-third of BILL and retain an
option to buy the rest in 2030, while the deal follows activist
pressure, workforce cuts, and intensifying competition in SMB
financial software.

 INTUIT SELLS MAILCHIMP TO BENDING SPOONS FOR $3.68B (4 MINUTE READ)
[11]

 Intuit agreed to sell Mailchimp to Bending Spoons for $3.68 billion,
less than one-third of the $12 billion it paid for the email-marketing
company in 2021. The sale marks a major retreat from Intuit's push
beyond tax and accounting software, as Mailchimp revenue declined 1%
and became a drag on growth, while Bending Spoons continues its
strategy of acquiring former high-growth software assets at sharply
reduced valuations.

DEEP DIVES & REPORTS

 MORE BANKS SAY CUSTOMERS USING AI AGENTS ARE ON THEIR OWN — EVEN IF
THEY GET HACKED (3 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Citi's updated agreement treats actions by an AI agent granted
account access as authorized, including actions that exceed the
customer's instructions. It extends that treatment to credentials
subsequently leaked by the agent, subject to applicable law. Citi
exempts tools it supplies itself, creating a meaningful difference in
the risks customers accept when choosing an independent financial
assistant.

 GIVE YOUR AGENTS CONTROLLED ACCESS TO ANALYZE YOUR DATA AND MOVE
FUNDS (4 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Stripe Treasury's public preview lets AI assistants pay vendors,
convert currencies, and top up accounts ahead of bills or payroll.
Agents connected through a user's Stripe account need confirmation for
every action that moves money, while agents with their own API keys
can work in the background under custom approval rules. A business
could let an agent make ACH transfers under $50 automatically and
route larger transfers to a designated approver.

 CHARGEBEE UNVEILS ARC: AN AGENTIC AI PLATFORM TO AUTOMATE ENTERPRISE
FINANCE WORKFLOWS (3 MINUTE READ) [17]

 The billing software provider is giving accounting teams a way to
turn signed contracts into proposed billing setups, draft collections
replies, and track how contract amendments affect recognized revenue.
Teams approve proposed actions before they take effect, and citations
let reviewers trace each generated value back to its source.

MISCELLANEOUS

 ROGO RAISES $350M AT $3.75B VALUATION TO AUTOMATE FINANCE GRUNT WORK
(2 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Rogo Technologies is close to raising about $350 million in a round
led by Bessemer Venture Partners and General Catalyst, valuing the AI
startup at $3.75 billion, up from $2 billion in April. Founded by
former Lazard and JPMorgan junior bankers, Rogo is building AI tools
to automate repetitive work across investment banking and other
finance workflows.

 ANTHROPIC CLOSES $119.5B IPO AND PRIVATE PLACEMENT (3 MINUTE READ)
[19]

 Anthropic closed its IPO at $83 per share, raising about $93.5
billion in gross proceeds and roughly $91.6 billion for the company,
with shares beginning to trade on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT.
Alongside the offering, AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft,
Nvidia, and TSMC invested another $26 billion in a concurrent private
placement, bringing total gross proceeds across both transactions to
approximately $119.5 billion.
