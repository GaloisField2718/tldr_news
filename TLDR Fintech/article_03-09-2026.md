NEWS & TRENDS

 KALSHI BECOMES FIRST-EVER PREDICTION MARKET PARTNER OF THE US OPEN (4
MINUTE READ) [8]

 Kalshi signed a multi-year deal with the USTA to become the exclusive
prediction market partner of the US Open. Branding, live market data
integrations, fan experiences, and hospitality will begin with the
2026 tournament. The partnership also includes an integrity framework
with the USTA and International Tennis Integrity Agency, including
data sharing and restrictions on markets tied to umpire decisions,
injuries, and code violations.

 HYPERLIQUID EXPLORES US PERPETUALS PUSH THROUGH KRAKEN PARENT PAYWARD
(4 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Hyperliquid Labs is reportedly in advanced talks with Kraken parent
Payward to offer certain Hyperliquid-linked perpetual futures to US
traders through Bitnomial, the CFTC-regulated derivatives platform
Payward agreed to acquire earlier this year. The potential structure
would give Hyperliquid a regulated path into the US market, but the
talks remain unconfirmed and subject to regulatory approval, while
speculation around a possible HIP-3 implementation has not been
verified.

 VISA BOOSTS A2A FRAUD PREVENTION TECH (3 MINUTE READ) [10]

 A2A transactions are projected to surpass 5.8 trillion by 2028, a
160% increase from 2024, making them increasingly attractive to
fraudsters. Visa's A2A Protect uses AI and sophisticated transfer
learning to give banks immediate access to critical global risk
insights on transactions, without waiting months for models to develop
intelligence from a bank's own transaction data.

DEEP DIVES & REPORTS

 THE US OPEN IS A CASINO NOW (7 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Ben Rothenberg argues that the USTA's new Kalshi partnership
accelerates an unhealthy fusion of tennis and gambling, pointing to
massive trading volumes on even low-level matches and growing
harassment of players by bettors. He also highlights the double
standard between tournaments taking betting sponsorships while players
face strict gambling rules, arguing that commercial incentives are
outrunning concerns about integrity and player welfare

 STABLECOINS ARE MOVING FROM TRADING TO PAYMENT INFRASTRUCTURE (8
MINUTE READ) [12]

 Stablecoins moved $33 trillion in 2025. Only about $390 billion of
stablecoin activity that year went to real-economy payments for goods
and services, roughly 1% of the total. The industry has largely solved
how to create and move digital dollars. What it has not solved is what
happens next: when someone wants to use that balance to pay a
supplier, send money home, or buy something without converting back
into fiat first.

 AIRWALLEX LAUNCHES T:0 AI-NATIVE ACCOUNTING PLATFORM FOR US STARTUPS
(2 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Airwallex launched T:0 out of private beta, making its AI-native
accounting platform available to startups across the US. The platform
automates bookkeeping and revenue recognition, keeps financials
continuously updated, and provides real-time visibility into metrics
such as cash, burn, and runway while supporting growing complexity
across entities, revenue models, and geographies.

 BLOCK OPENS CASH APP SCORE TO EXTERNAL LENDERS THROUGH NOVA CREDIT (4
MINUTE READ) [14]

 Block is making its proprietary Cash App Score available to outside
lenders for the first time through Nova Credit's Cash Flow
Intelligence Platform, using real-time Cash App activity such as
spending, saving, repayments, deposits, and peer-to-peer transactions
to supplement traditional credit data. Block says the score approves
38% more Cash App Borrow customers at the same loss rate as
traditional scores and could help lenders expand approvals across
credit cards, auto loans, personal lending, device financing, and
tenant screening without requiring consumers to link new accounts.

 GOLDMAN SACHS, BOFA AND 19 OTHER INSTITUTIONS PLAN JOINT DOLLAR
STABLECOIN FOR 2027 (3 MINUTE READ) [15]

 A consortium of 21 financial institutions including Goldman Sachs,
Bank of America, Citi, and Deutsche Bank plans to form a company this
year and launch a dollar-backed stablecoin in the first half of 2027,
with euro and other G7 currency tokens potentially following. The
initiative would put major banks in direct competition with stablecoin
incumbents like Tether and with rival banking consortium Qivalis,
though existing bank-issued stablecoins have so far seen limited
adoption.

 WALL STREET BANKS PUSH BIG LAW TO CUT FEES AS AI REDUCES LEGAL
WORKLOADS (5 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Morgan Stanley, Citi, and Goldman Sachs are pressing major law firms
to share the efficiency gains from AI through lower fees, competitive
bidding, fixed-fee arrangements, and other alternatives to traditional
hourly billing. The shift threatens Big Law's leverage model, which
depends on large teams of junior lawyers billing long hours, and could
accelerate smaller associate classes, more in-house legal work, and a
broader move toward pricing based on outcomes rather than time.

 RYAN BRESLOW IS RAISING UP TO $27M IN PAY-TO-PLAY BRIDGE FUNDING TO
SAVE BOLT (4 MINUTE READ) [17]

 The one-click checkout startup is seeking a financial lifeline after
its valuation collapsed 97% from $11 billion to $300 million, with
Ryan Breslow personally committing $5 million. Existing investors who
decline to participate risk losing much of their equity, suggesting
the company may be running short on options despite claims it is
nearing profitability. Breslow is betting that a 60-person, AI-powered
team and Bolt's new financial super app can return the company to
growth.

 SINGAPORE COMMITS S$220M TO FINTECH INNOVATION (2 MINUTE READ) [18]

 The Monetary Authority of Singapore will invest US$173 million over
three years to accelerate the development and adoption of technologies
including AI, blockchain, quantum computing, and digital assets. The
program will also fund shared infrastructure, attract global financial
institutions, and support at least 1,000 fintech internships as
Singapore builds on an ecosystem of more than 1,800 firms.
