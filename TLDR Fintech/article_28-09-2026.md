 MONZO IN TALKS ABOUT MULTIBILLION-POUND SALE TO BRAZIL'S NUBANK (6
MINUTE READ) [7]

 Monzo is in early talks with Nubank parent Nu Holdings about a
potential £8 billion to £10 billion sale, with Morgan Stanley and
Qatalyst advising as Monzo also weighs a new funding round to support
European expansion. A deal would give Nubank an immediate UK and
European banking foothold through Monzo's 16 million customers and
£1.7 billion of annual revenue, while likely ending Monzo's near-term
path to a standalone IPO.

 REVOLUT TRIALS PAY WITH SMILE (2 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Revolut customers can now pay for coffee with their face at three
Kiss the Hippo cafés in Bloomsbury, Chelsea, and Soho. The in-store
facial recognition checkout, Revolut Pay with Smile, allows customers
to pay for their purchases with a simple glance, with 0% processing
fees for merchants on all transactions.

 ACH USE RISES FOR B2B PAYMENTS (4 MINUTE READ) [9]

 ACH has overtaken checks as the most common US B2B payment method by
transaction count, rising from 25% of payments in 2015 to 41% in 2024
while checks fell from 32% to 13%. ACH transaction value nearly
doubled to $70 trillion over the period, though wires still dominate
by value at roughly $1 quadrillion, showing that businesses are
steadily shifting routine payments toward electronic rails even as
checks remain widely used.

DEEP DIVES & REPORTS

 AGENT WARS: AMAZON VS. MUSE (10 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Simon Taylor argues that Amazon's decision to block Meta's Muse agent
is an early battle over who owns the customer, captures commerce fees,
and bears liability when AI agents transact on users' behalf. He
expects the key infrastructure layer to be “Know Your Agent”
standards that authenticate agents, define delegated authority, and
allocate liability, with merchants, wallets, card networks, platforms,
and infrastructure providers all competing to control that trust
layer.

 DEEP DIVE: WHY AI IS BOOMING, BUT PRODUCTIVITY ISN'T (4 MINUTE READ)
[11]

 AI tools are already cheap enough to generate positive ROI, but
productivity gains are getting trapped inside inefficient
organizational processes, meetings, approvals, and poorly designed
workflows. The biggest gains appear to accrue to companies that
redesign how work gets done around AI, suggesting that AI may widen
the gap between strong and weak operators rather than lift
productivity evenly across the economy.

 NUMERAL RAISES $100M SERIES C TO EXPAND AI-POWERED TAX COMPLIANCE (5
MINUTE READ) [17]

 Numeral raised a $100 million Series C led by Insight Partners,
bringing total funding to $157 million as it expands its AI-powered
sales tax compliance platform across software, manufacturing,
distribution, and wholesale. The company says transaction volume is up
327% year over year and is positioning itself as end-to-end tax
infrastructure spanning nexus monitoring, registrations, calculation,
filings, remittance, and exemption certificates, with support for VAT
and GST in more than 90 countries.

 SEC CLARIFIES THAT CERTAIN STAKED ETHEREUM RECEIPT TOKENS ARE NOT
SECURITIES (3 MINUTE READ) [18]

 SEC staff clarified that liquid-staking receipt tokens tied to
non-security crypto assets like ETH generally are not securities when
they simply represent ownership of the underlying staked asset and do
not add separate rights, returns, or managerial promises. The guidance
builds on the SEC's earlier position on liquid staking, but remains
staff guidance rather than binding law, meaning its treatment could
change through future rulemaking, legislation, or a different
administration.
