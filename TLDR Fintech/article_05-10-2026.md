NEWS & TRENDS

 STRIPE AGREES TO ACQUIRE PARAFIN TO EXPAND EMBEDDED CREDIT FOR SMALL
BUSINESSES (2 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Stripe has agreed to acquire embedded lending platform Parafin, which
powers credit products for platforms including DoorDash, Gusto,
Jobber, and Mindbody and has provided financing to more than 60,000
businesses. The deal will expand Stripe Capital with Parafin's credit,
Pay Over Time, and Spend products, strengthening Stripe's push to make
its 18,000+ platform customers broader financial hubs for small
businesses.

 ROBINHOOD PUTS THE POWER OF HEDGE FUNDS IN EVERY TRADER'S POCKET (5
MINUTE READ) [9]

 Robinhood launched Robinhood Agents, a fully embedded AI experience
that can analyze the market, build strategies, and trade on your
behalf around the clock, and Agent Apps, which give your Robinhood
Agents another layer of intelligence with institutional-grade data and
specialized tools from leading third-party providers. It also
announced plans to launch 24/7 weekend trading for equities, perpetual
futures for eligible US customers, earnings contracts for key company
metrics, and more.

 AIRWALLEX LAUNCHES AGENTIC BUSINESS ACCOUNTS FOR MULTI-ENTITY
TREASURY (2 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Airwallex Business Accounts now allow you to see your finances in one
place and manage them with your preferred AI agent. Airwallex has
rebuilt its Business Account around one view of every entity in the
group, with sub-accounts for operating cash, yield, and credit. It has
also added agent integration so Claude, Cursor, or any MCP client can
read balances, prep FX, and issue cards inside the permissions you
set.

 EMVCO FRAMEWORK TARGETS CONSUMER INTENT IN AGENTIC PAYMENTS (4 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 EMVCo is exploring standards that would let payment participants see
what a consumer authorized an AI agent to purchase, identify the
agent, and flag its involvement in a transaction. The framework could
give merchants, networks, and issuers important new data for fraud
checks, disputes, and accountability as agents begin making card
payments.

DEEP DIVES & REPORTS

 HUMAN BASELINES FOR BENCHMARKS: AI NOW OUTPERFORMS JUNIOR ACCOUNTANTS
(6 MINUTE READ) [12]

 A study of 12 CPAs found that frontier AI models were faster, more
accurate, and dramatically cheaper than humans on four structured
month-end close tasks, with Claude Opus 5 scoring 100% while
accountants averaged about 37%. Mercor cautions that this does not
mean accountants are broadly replaceable, since the benchmark favors
detail-heavy file search and instruction following while excluding
skills such as client communication, judgment, and accumulated
business context, but it suggests substantial automation potential in
structured accounting work.

 🧠 FINTECH IS DEAD (10 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Simon Taylor argues that mobile apps, cloud infrastructure, and APIs
have become standard, shifting the next competitive edge to AI-driven
decisions, tokenized assets, and software agents acting on customers'
behalf. He points to Stripe, Ramp, and Robinhood as examples of
companies building toward financial services where software handles
the work and money moves around the clock.

 LONDON FINTECH ENTERS A CONSOLIDATION ERA AS FUNDING DRIES UP (8
MINUTE READ) [14]

 London's fintech market is shifting from rapid startup formation
toward consolidation, with funding at its lowest level since at least
2016 and capital increasingly concentrated in B2B companies and a
small group of scaled winners such as Revolut, Monzo, and Wise.
Consumer fintechs are losing favor, mid-stage companies face limited
exit options, and many founders are responding through mergers,
pivots, or acquisitions as the industry moves from unbundling
financial services toward larger, rebundled platforms.

 AMEX ADDS AI, EXPENSE TRACKING TO CORPORATE CARD TO COUNTER FINTECH
RIVALS (3 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Amex's September 30 rollout pairs a corporate cashback card with
software that captures spending, generates expense reports, and flags
policy violations. Aimed at businesses with $10 million to $300
million in annual revenue, it brings Amex closer to the integrated
offering popularized by Ramp and Brex. Additional AI agents and
accounts-payable features are planned after launch.

 SLEEPLESS ON WALL STREET: ALL-NIGHT STOCK EXCHANGES COMING SOON (4
MINUTE READ) [18]

 Nasdaq, NYSE Arca, 24X National Exchange, and Cboe EDGX plan to add
overnight trading from 9 PM to 4 AM ET starting December 6, pushing US
equities closer to a 23/5 market and responding to growing demand from
foreign investors as well as competition from crypto and prediction
markets. Overnight volume is still only about 1% of total equity
trading, but it has grown 358% year over year, while institutions
remain cautious about thin liquidity, wider spreads, and weaker price
discovery outside regular hours.

 PAYNEARME LAUNCHES AI SERVICING AND COLLECTIONS AGENT (3 MINUTE READ)
[19]

 PayNearMe launched an AI agent that calls and texts customers,
handles inbound inquiries, and helps customers make or schedule
payments. Embedded in its PayXM platform, the agent uses payment data,
business rules, and workflows to resolve issues and execute tasks, not
just answer questions, with human handoffs when needed. Indiana
Finance Company is piloting the tool for collections outreach and
inbound support, freeing staff to focus on cases requiring personal
attention.

MISCELLANEOUS

 PAYPAL HINTS AT BNPL HOLIDAY USE (3 MINUTE READ) [20]

 PayPal's survey suggests BNPL will play a larger role in holiday
shopping this year, with 75% of consumers who have used or considered
installment payments expecting to use them during the season.
Consumers are still planning to spend despite higher financial
anxiety, and BNPL is increasingly being used as a budgeting tool that
can also lift purchase conversion for merchants.

 BANK AI JOB POSTINGS JUMP 49% AS AGENTS GO TO WORK (3 MINUTE READ)
[21]

 Mentions of agent orchestration, the skill of coordinating multiple
AI systems to complete a task, climbed from 108 job listings in 2025
to 1,967 this year. Lenders are also seeking more governance and risk
management specialists to oversee increasingly autonomous workflows
across trading, compliance, and back-office operations
