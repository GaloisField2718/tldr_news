NEWS & TRENDS

 US SENATE ADVANCES CLARITY ACT AHEAD OF AUGUST RECESS (3 MINUTE READ)
[8]

 Senate Majority Leader John Thune moved to set up a procedural vote
on the CLARITY Act for when lawmakers return in September, giving the
crypto market structure bill a path toward the Senate floor. The
legislation would establish the first comprehensive federal framework
for digital assets, but passage still depends on securing at least 60
votes amid Democratic concerns over government officials' crypto
interests and ongoing disputes with banks over stablecoin rewards.

 EX-NUBANK EXECS LAUNCH AI WEALTH MANAGER DECADE FOLLOWING $85M SEED
ROUND (2 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Decade has emerged from stealth with an $85 million seed round backed
by Greenoaks, Benchmark, and Diffusion to build an AI-native wealth
management platform in Brazil. Founded by former Nubank CTO Vitor
Olivier and Hyperplane founder Felipe Meneses, the company combines
proprietary financial AI agents with human advisors to monitor
portfolios, consolidate financial data, and deliver personalized
investment guidance.

 JPMORGAN TO DEPLOY $750B FOR AFFORDABLE HOUSING BY 2035 (3 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 JPMorgan plans to help build or preserve 1 million lower-cost homes
and support 500,000 homebuyers, including 200,000 first-time buyers.
The bank expects to increase mortgage lending by roughly 40% and hire
850 home lending advisers while partnering with developers,
nonprofits, and governments to expand housing supply.

DEEP DIVES & REPORTS

 FINANCIAL FIRMS PUT AI TO WORK ACROSS 27 TASKS, STUDY FINDS (3 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 Large financial institutions are deploying AI most aggressively in
back office operations, with revenue recognition, credit risk
assessment, and sales forecasting emerging as the most widely adopted
use cases. A study found that 85% of financial firms plan to increase
AI spending over the next year, while customer-facing applications
remain limited as banks prioritize measurable outcomes, regulatory
compliance, and human oversight.

 BLOCK'S DORSEY EVANGELIZES AI REORGANIZATION FOR FINANCIAL SERVICES
(4 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Block beat Wall Street's earnings estimates and raised its full-year
outlook, crediting AI with helping the company develop and ship
products faster. Following a 40% workforce reduction earlier this
year, the fintech is leaning on AI to operate more efficiently while
exploring ways to monetize new tools such as its Buzz development
platform.

 BLOCK EXPLORES HOW TO PRICE AI (4 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Block has deployed AI assistants across Cash App and Square but is
deliberately holding off on monetization. CEO Jack Dorsey is
prioritizing adoption and product quality before committing to a
pricing model. Six months after cutting 40% of its workforce in an
AI-focused reorg, agentic AI now writes and reviews nearly all of
Block's production code. Q2 gross profit rose 25% to $3.2B.

 NOT ALL MCPS ARE CREATED EQUAL (6 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Brex argues that “MCP support” is a misleading checkbox because
implementations differ dramatically in scalability, accuracy, latency,
and permissioning. The company says its architecture continuously
pre-computes and denormalizes finance data so agents can query large
datasets in seconds with database-calculated answers, while reserving
a slower conversational layer for ambiguous questions that require
interpretation.

MISCELLANEOUS

 LIGHTSPEED LEADS $37M SERIES A TO AUTOMATE INTERNAL AUDIT (6 MINUTE
READ) [15]

 Lightspeed is leading a $37M Series A in Andera, an AI-native
platform automating internal audit end-to-end, from SOX control
testing to workpaper generation. The thesis: audit is a reasoning
problem that rules engines never cracked, public companies spend $3M
to $20M+ annually on audit fees, and the Big Four face an innovator's
dilemma since their hourly billing model prevents them from
cannibalizing themselves with AI.

 META IS ORDERED TO PAY $942 MILLION OVER CHILD SAFETY HARMS (2 MINUTE
READ) [16]

 A New Mexico judge ordered Meta to pay $942 million after finding the
company failed to adequately protect children on Facebook and
Instagram, including $567 million for youth treatment and prevention
programs on top of $375 million in civil penalties. The ruling also
requires new child safety measures such as improved AI-based age
estimation, hidden like counts for minors, better reporting tools for
schools, and greater transparency around platform risks. Meta plans to
appeal, but the decision marks the first trial victory by a US state
against a major technology company over child safety and could
influence similar lawsuits across the country.

 MAJOR HEDGE FUNDS TARGETED IN WAVE OF ATTEMPTED CYBER ATTACKS (5
MINUTE READ) [17]

 Hackers launched a coordinated wave of AI-enabled voice phishing
attacks against major hedge funds including Point72, Millennium
Management, Two Sigma, and Citadel, highlighting the growing
cybersecurity risks facing financial institutions. While firms
reported little to no impact on client data, the attacks demonstrate
how generative AI is making sophisticated social engineering campaigns
cheaper, faster, and easier to scale across Wall Street. FINRA has
increased information sharing with member firms as the industry braces
for a new generation of AI-powered cyber threats.
