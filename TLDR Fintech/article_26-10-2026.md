 RAMP-AIRWALLEX PARTNERSHIP REPORTEDLY COLLAPSED AS COMPETITION
INTENSIFIED (2 MINUTE READ) [8]

 A proposed partnership between Ramp and Airwallex to strengthen
Ramp's international payments capabilities reportedly fell apart in
April 2025 after Airwallex investors pushed CEO Jack Zhang to compete
with Ramp instead. Ramp co-founder Eric Glyman reportedly tried to
salvage the deal and later warned that the relationship between the
two fintechs could become less friendly as they moved toward more
direct competition.

 BARCLAYS TO SLASH 5,000 JOBS IN AI-POWERED COST-CUTTING DRIVE (3
MINUTE READ) [9]

 Barclays plans to eliminate roughly 5,000 positions, about 5% of its
workforce, as automation and AI reduce support costs, with developers
among the functions most affected. The bank has already cut around
1,000 roles and booked £201 million in third-quarter restructuring
charges.

 AMERICAN EXPRESS TOUTS AI, PLANS 5% DOWNSIZING (4 MINUTE READ) [10]

 CEO Steve Squeri framed the restructuring as a way to remove
management layers and speed up decisions, without directly attributing
the job cuts to automation. The company is also automating expense
reports, preparing a payroll system for early 2027, and developing
protections for purchases made by AI agents.

 SAM'S CLUB PICKS AFFIRM FOR BNPL (2 MINUTE READ) [11]

 AMEX TO SHRINK WORKFORCE AS IT ADJUSTS TO AI AND RISING COMPETITION
(4 MINUTE READ) [12]

 American Express plans to cut about 3,500 jobs, roughly 4.5% of its
workforce, as CEO Stephen Squeri pushes for faster decision-making,
higher productivity, and a leaner operating model. Amex also plans to
deploy more AI agents across its commercial and consumer products as
competition intensifies from JPMorgan, Visa, Mastercard, Ramp, and
Capital One-owned Brex.

 SEC ADDS TOKENIZATION TO 2027 EXAMINATION PRIORITIES (2 MINUTE READ)
[18]

 The SEC's Division of Examinations added tokenization to its fiscal
2027 priorities, with clearing agencies and Regulation SCI entities
facing greater scrutiny around tokenized securities and related
market-infrastructure risks. The move does not create new rules, but
signals that tokenization is becoming part of mainstream securities
oversight as regulators examine how blockchain-based infrastructure
affects clearing, recordkeeping, operations, and market integrity.

 CITI TAKES CAUTIOUS APPROACH ON AI AGENT ACCESS (6 MINUTE READ) [19]

 Citi is blocking external AI agents from transacting inside customer
accounts for now, allowing only limited read-only open-banking access
while it develops “know your agent” controls around identity,
authorization, entitlements and regulatory compliance. At the same
time, Citi is expanding internal AI across onboarding, document
processing, client chat, and embedded banking, showing a split
strategy: move quickly with controlled in-house AI, but slowly with
third-party agents that can take financial actions.

 NETANYAHU REBUKED FOR PLAN TO USE CRYPTO TO BYPASS ECONOMIC SANCTIONS
(2 MINUTE READ) [20]

 On October 25, Israel's central bank rejected Prime Minister Benjamin
Netanyahu's suggestion that cryptocurrency and blockchain could
protect the economy if foreign sanctions reached its banking system.
More than 100 economists warned the strategy would not preserve access
to trading partners and could instead accelerate capital flight,
unemployment, and financial isolation
