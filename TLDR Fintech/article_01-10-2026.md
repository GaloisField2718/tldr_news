 TIGER GLOBAL, DRAGONEER IN TALKS TO INVEST IN KALSHI'S NEW $1 BILLION
FUNDRAISE (3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 The proposed round could value the prediction-market exchange at
about $40 billion, up from $22 billion in May, though the financing
has not closed. Kalshi is looking beyond event contracts toward a
wider trading platform, putting it closer to established exchanges as
scrutiny of prediction markets grows.

 APPLE PAY ARRIVES IN INDIA (2 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Apple Pay is finally launching in India, entering a huge but
competitive market via a partnership with Axis Bank. Customers with
Axis Bank-issued Visa and Mastercard credit cards can now make
in-store, online, and in-app payments via iPhones, iPads, and Apple
Watches, with support for Mac coming soon.

 LONG LAKE COMPLETES $6.3B ACQUISITION OF AMERICAN EXPRESS GLOBAL
BUSINESS TRAVEL (3 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Long Lake completed its $6.3 billion all-cash acquisition of Amex
GBT, taking the company private at $9.50 per share and delisting it
from the NYSE. The deal pairs Amex GBT's global managed travel
platform with Long Lake's AI engineering and Nexus AI transformation
capabilities, with plans to deploy agentic customer tools and
AI-assisted travel services across the business.

 CITI EXPANDS COINBASE PARTNERSHIP TO POWER STABLECOIN PAYMENTS FOR
BUSINESSES (2 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Citi and Coinbase are expanding their partnership to connect
traditional bank payments with stablecoins for corporate customers.
Coinbase will use Citi's Virtual Account Wallet to let businesses
accept and hold fiat while automatically converting funds into
stablecoins, while Citi clients will be able to accept stablecoin
payments through Spring by Citi, with Coinbase converting the assets
back into fiat for settlement.

 PODCAST: HOW STRIPE'S TEMPO IS REBUILDING ON-CHAIN CASH SETTLEMENT,
WITH HEAD OF MARKET DEVELOPMENT SIMON TAYLOR (45 MINUTE PODCAST) [13]

 This podcast discusses why there is no Fedwire for the internet, and
why the next wave of on-chain volume is coming from enterprise money
movers like Deel rather than crypto-native builders. Simon Taylor
explains why tokenized deposits, stablecoins, and TradFi settlement
will converge on a single chain, why agentic commerce is still stuck
in its WAP-phone era, and how the Machine Payments Protocol is being
designed as an IETF-grade standard that can settle across Tempo,
Stripe, or any card network.

 PAYMENT REVENUE GROWTH TO SLOW (3 MINUTE READ) [14]

 BCG expects payments transaction revenue to keep growing through
2030, reaching $436 billion in North America and $1.03 trillion
globally, but at slower annual rates than in the prior six years. The
slowdown reflects mature digital-payment adoption, tougher
competition, regulation, and new payment rails, while faster growth
shifts toward regions such as Latin America, the Middle East, and
Africa.

 THE FDE BOOM IS A PRODUCT GAP (7 MINUTE READ) [15]

 The surge in forward-deployed engineers reflects a missing enterprise
AI product layer: companies still need humans to translate operator
judgment into instructions agents can reliably follow.
“ContextOps” is a continuous process for turning operational
evidence and expert judgment into versioned, tested, and approved
agent procedures. Every escalation improves future automation and the
company owns the learning loop instead of repeatedly relying on FDEs.

 STRIPE ALUMS SPARK NEW PAYMENTS PLAYS (8 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Stripe has become a founder factory for fintech, with former
employees launching companies across agentic commerce, banking
infrastructure, stablecoin payments, and vertical financial services.
Alumni startups including Circuit & Chisel, Increase, and Latitude
Global reflect how Stripe's technical culture, exposure to
cutting-edge internet businesses, and broad view of itself as a
software company are seeding the next generation of payments
infrastructure.

 AMEX ADDS AI, EXPENSE TRACKING TO CORPORATE CARD TO COUNTER FINTECH
RIVALS (3 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Amex's September 30 rollout pairs a corporate cashback card with
software that captures spending, generates expense reports, and flags
policy violations. Aimed at businesses with $10 million to $300
million in annual revenue, it brings Amex closer to the integrated
offering popularized by Ramp and Brex. Additional AI agents and
accounts-payable features are planned after launch.

 A Senate Democratic report found that 84% of 846 sanctioned wallets
it examined had transacted in USDT, prompting a call for US

 JEEVES RAISES $110M TO SCALE ITS STABLECOIN-NATIVE BANKING PLATFORM
(5 MINUTE READ) [20]

 Jeeves raised $110 million in equity funding led by CoinFund as it
expands its stablecoin-native financial operating system across 35
countries. The company says revenue has grown 4x in 14 months,
annualized platform volume has surpassed $5 billion, and
stablecoin-settled volume has reached $1.5 billion annualized, while
new products including a proprietary stablecoin wallet, global
payouts, AR, and AI spend tracking broaden its push to become the core
banking infrastructure for multinational businesses.
