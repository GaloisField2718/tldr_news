NEWS & TRENDS

 RAMP APPLIES FOR NATIONAL TRUST BANK CHARTER FROM OCC (4 MINUTE READ)
[8]

 Ramp applied to the OCC for a national trust bank charter that would
let it offer fiduciary investment management and digital-asset custody
under a federal framework, while not taking deposits or making
commercial loans. The application comes just after Ramp raised $1.85
billion at a $60 billion valuation and amid growing legal and industry
debate over the OCC's recent wave of trust-charter approvals for
fintech and crypto companies.

 JPMORGAN ANNOUNCES $5 BILLION IN INVESTMENTS IN COMPANIES CRITICAL TO
US NATIONAL SECURITY (2 MINUTE READ) [9]

 JPMorgan said it had invested $5 billion of its own equity in
venture, growth, and later-stage companies spanning advanced
manufacturing, energy, supply chains, and defense technology. The
deployment, alongside roughly $230 billion already financed through
its broader initiative, shows large banks competing to fund AI
infrastructure, automation, and other industries tied to US industrial
policy

 CHECKOUT.COM EXPANDS INTO IN-PERSON PAYMENTS (3 MINUTE READ) [10]

 After 14 years focused purely on online payments, Checkout.com is
moving into in-store payments, citing demand from enterprise merchants
who want its online acquiring performance in physical stores. The
offering is software-led and device-agnostic, so merchants can keep
their existing terminals, and online plus in-person run through one
acquiring relationship with the same Unified Payments API, contract,
and settlement. Mollie (350K+ merchants) is the launch partner,
keeping control of its hardware and customer experience while running
on Checkout.com's acquiring rails.

DEEP DIVES & REPORTS

 FINTECH FUNDING FELL 21% IN Q3 AS DEALS GREW SCARCER (7 MINUTE READ)
[11]

 Global fintech funding fell 21% year over year to $10.6 billion in Q3
2026, while deal count dropped 31% to its lowest level since at least
2018, showing that capital is concentrating in fewer companies and
larger rounds. Investors are increasingly separating AI-driven market
leaders from the rest, favoring infrastructure, payments, stablecoin
rails, and agentic commerce, while demanding stronger growth,
efficiency, and proof of durable demand from companies without clear
momentum.

 FINANCE IS ENTERING ITS MOD ERA (8 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Personal AI agents will become a primary channel for banking, the way
mobile did, as customers want their own agent to use a bank's product
rather than open its app. Letting others build on your product can be
good business, since trust, data, and integrations stay with the
provider. There is an emerging protocol stack for authenticating
agents and restricting what they can do. Reg E liability for agent
actions remains a grey area. Start with customers already asking for
agent access and give them limited, revocable, enforceable
permissions.

 MASTERCARD OPENS EARLY ACCESS TO AI AGENT PAYMENTS FOLLOWING PILOTS
WITH COINBASE, BASE, AND CLOUDFLARE (4 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Mastercard opened early access to Agent Pay for Machines, giving
developers APIs, a toolkit, and a sandbox for machine-to-machine
payments after pilots with firms including Coinbase, Base, Cloudflare,
BVNK, and Ant International. In one demo, an AI agent completed nine
purchases using Coinbase's x402 protocol, Coinbase wallet
infrastructure, and Base settlement, while Mastercard and BVNK handled
payment orchestration across fiat and stablecoins, showing how agents
could autonomously buy digital services within preset spending
controls.

 VISA, BILL, AND BILLTRUST LAUNCH LIVE PILOT FOR TRUSTED AI AGENT
PAYMENTS (5 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Visa, BILL, and Billtrust launched a live pilot that lets authorized
AI agents complete already-approved B2B invoice payments end-to-end
across supplier portals, removing a manual payment step that often
still requires human login and data entry. Visa's Trusted Agent
Protocol authenticates the agent and uses tokenized payment
credentials, while buyer-defined controls and supplier acceptance
rules remain in place, showing how agentic payments can automate AP
execution without giving agents independent payment authority.

 ANTHROPIC'S NEW AI AGENT PURCHASES BUSINESS SUPPLIES (2 MINUTE READ)
[17]

 Claude Duet checks connected apps for updates and keeps working while
users are offline, asking for approval before sending messages, making
payments, or deleting anything. Available by waitlist to US Pro and
Max subscribers, it supports checkout through Shopify's Shop Pay and
Stripe's Link, with Link requiring spending approval and using virtual
cards to keep real card numbers private.

MISCELLANEOUS

 PAYPAL CEO SAYS COMPANY NEEDS FASTER DECISIONS AND CLEARER
ACCOUNTABILITY (6 MINUTE READ) [18]

 PayPal CEO Enrique Lores says the company became too slow and layered
under prior leadership, and his turnaround plan centers on reducing
management layers, clarifying accountability, and investing more
heavily in Venmo, Braintree, and consumer financial services. PayPal
is also modernizing its technology stack with AI and preparing for
agentic commerce, although Lores expects meaningful agent-driven
transaction volume to remain limited until around 2028.

 RAMP BOLSTERS TREASURY MANAGEMENT CAPABILITIES WITH VESTO ACQUISITION
(1 MINUTE READ) [19]

 Vesto gives businesses a consolidated view of accounts held across
more than 12,000 institutions, adding cash-flow monitoring and
forecasting to Ramp's checking and investment products. Coming
immediately after Ramp's national trust-bank application and $1.85
billion financing, the acquisition advances its shift from expense
management toward a broader financial operating system.
