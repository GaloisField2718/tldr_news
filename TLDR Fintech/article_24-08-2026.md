NEWS & TRENDS

 WASHINGTON GOES ALL-IN ON CRYPTO AS CLARITY ACT NEGOTIATIONS
INTENSIFY (4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 President Donald Trump urged lawmakers to pass a “fair version”
of the CLARITY Act as crypto executives met with the White House over
remaining disputes, particularly ethics provisions tied to government
officials' crypto interests. At the same time, the CFTC warned it
could use existing authority to build its own crypto regulatory regime
if Congress stalls, while the SEC proposed its first crypto-specific
fundraising framework with new exemptions and a conditional safe
harbor.

 JPMORGAN DEBANKED POLYMARKET LAST YEAR OVER REGULATORY CONCERNS,
SOURCE SAYS (2 MINUTE READ) [9]

 JPMorgan ended a banking relationship with Polymarket in October 2025
as prediction markets faced growing scrutiny from regulators over
their resemblance to gambling. Polymarket pushed back on the
characterization of the relationship, saying it continues to work
closely with the bank across multiple entities and customer fund
flows. The episode highlights the regulatory uncertainty surrounding
the fast-growing prediction market sector, with platforms including
Polymarket and Kalshi facing increased attention from state officials.


 BANKS LOOKING TO INSURE AGAINST AI HALLUCINATION (4 MINUTE READ) [10]


 Testudo, a Lloyd's-backed startup, is offering coverage of up to $10
million for liabilities arising from generative AI, with annual
premiums typically ranging from $10,000 to $20,000. Interest is
emerging as financial institutions consider deploying tools that can
interact with customers or execute transactions, although some vendors
prefer to guarantee their own products and skeptics question whether a
separate category of coverage is necessary.

 INSIDE MAX LEVCHIN'S SLOW-AND-STEADY PATH TO AFFIRM PROFITABILITY (5
MINUTE READ) [11]

 Affirm has finally reached profitability after years of heavy
investment and more than $2 billion in cumulative losses, validating
Max Levchin's long-term strategy of building much of the company's
technology and risk infrastructure in-house. That philosophy can be
traced back to Levchin's experience fighting fraud at PayPal. The same
operational discipline helped turn Affirm into a roughly $25 billion
fintech company.

 SYSTEM OF ACTION PART 4: THE CASE FOR THE APPLICATION LAYER (10
MINUTE READ) [12]

 Foundation models alone cannot reliably “do the work” inside most
businesses because real jobs require domain-specific workflows,
deterministic software, permissions, semantics, human escalation, and
continuous learning loops. There is a major opportunity for vertical
SaaS incumbents and AI-native challengers to become the System of
Action for their industries, potentially expanding software TAM by
roughly 10x by capturing labor spend rather than just software
budgets.

LAUNCHES & PRODUCTS

 PAYPAL AND VENMO EXPAND INTO COLLEGE TUITION PAYMENTS (3 MINUTE READ)
[13]

 PayPal and Venmo have integrated with Illumia, Nelnet Campus
Commerce, and TouchNet to let students and parents pay tuition
directly through participating college payment portals. The move
expands PayPal's campus strategy beyond peer-to-peer payments and
athletics, giving Venmo and PayPal access to thousands of
higher-education institutions through existing payments
infrastructure.

 AIRWALLEX EXPANDS FROM CROSS-BORDER PAYMENTS INTO AUTONOMOUS FINANCE
(8 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Airwallex is using its recent $320 million Series H at an $11 billion
valuation to expand beyond cross-border payments into autonomous
finance, including T:0 for automated bookkeeping and Airi for agentic
commerce. The company now serves more than 675,000 businesses with
over $1 billion in annualized revenue, but president Lucy Liu says an
IPO still depends on market conditions as Airwallex expands globally
and navigates growing regulatory scrutiny in the US and Australia.

MISCELLANEOUS

 CITADEL UNWINDS MORE THAN 80% OF RISK FROM SITUATIONAL AWARENESS
PORTFOLIO (4 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Ken Griffin said Citadel has shed more than 80% of the risk from the
portfolio it acquired from Leopold Aschenbrenner's Situational
Awareness, using more than 100 block trades representing over $4
billion in market value. The deal came after the AI-focused hedge fund
was forced to liquidate public positions amid steep losses, while
Citadel's flagship Wellington fund returned 5.94% in July, its best
month since 2022.

 JPMORGAN HIRES BANK OF AMERICA'S DAVID FISHMAN TO LEAD NORTH AMERICA
TECHNOLOGY M&A (3 MINUTE READ) [16]

 JPMorgan is hiring veteran Bank of America dealmaker David Fishman as
head of North America technology M&A, while elevating Vineet Seth to
vice chair of investment banking and forming a new advisory group for
key technology clients. The move adds to a broader talent reshuffling
across Wall Street tech banking, with several senior Bank of America
executives recently departing for JPMorgan, Barclays, Deutsche Bank,
and Citi.
