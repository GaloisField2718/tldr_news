NEWS & TRENDS

 S&P DOW JONES PARTNERS WITH KALSHI FOR INDEX-BASED EVENTS CONTRACTS,
USE PREDICTION DATA (2 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Kalshi will offer event contracts settled against official S&P Dow
Jones index values, while S&P plans to turn Kalshi's probability data
into new benchmarks covering economics, politics, and sports. The
agreement gives the prediction-market operator institutional
credibility amid ongoing gambling-related criticism and moves event
contracts closer to mainstream financial-market infrastructure.

 PERSONAL AI ASSISTANT INSTINCT JUST GAVE AI AGENTS THEIR OWN SPENDING
MONEY (2 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Instinct lets users fund a wallet and set spending limits so AI
agents can make purchases independently. Stablecoins are converted
into dollars and spent through a Visa virtual card, letting merchants
accept payment through existing infrastructure. The broader tension is
over who controls agent payments: Stripe's virtual cards or Visa and
Mastercard's competing systems tied to customers' existing cards.

 RIPPLE GETS OCC'S UNCONDITIONAL CHARTER APPROVAL (3 MINUTE READ) [10]


 Ripple has secured final OCC approval to launch a national trust
bank, allowing it to provide digital asset custody and manage reserves
backing its RLUSD stablecoin, but not accept deposits or make
commercial loans. The company is also expanding its BNY partnership to
support institutional custody and tokenized assets, explore tokenized
trade finance, and eventually let clients mint and redeem RLUSD
directly through the bank.

DEEP DIVES & REPORTS

 ANTHROPIC IPO: S-1 BREAKDOWN (18 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Anthropic's S-1 shows extraordinary growth and operating leverage:
$16.3 billion of first-half 2026 revenue, roughly 900% net revenue
retention, more than $6.6 million of trailing revenue per employee,
and a profitable Q2, with consumption-based “pay-for-intelligence”
generating 80% of revenue. The biggest questions are durability and
infrastructure economics, as Anthropic remains highly dependent on
cloud partners, faces enormous forward compute commitments, and
operates in a market with low switching costs, even as customer usage
and compute efficiency are improving at exceptional rates.

 MEET THE GHOST CARD (12 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Fintech is rapidly converging around agents, stablecoins, and
programmable money: Meta's Muse is driving real consumer agentic
commerce, Ramp is pushing deeper into treasury and digital-asset
infrastructure, Mercury has crossed $1 billion of annualized revenue,
and Visa plus Mesh are turning wallet balances into card-network
spending through invisible “ghost cards.” The financial stack is
shifting from apps and standalone products toward agent-controlled
money movement, embedded credit, tokenized assets, and new trust
layers around identity, permissions, and liability.

 RAMP LAUNCHES INSPECT, A COLLABORATIVE CODING AGENT FOR FASTER
PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT (4 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Ramp is opening early access to Inspect, a cloud-based coding agent
that turns requests from Slack, GitHub, Linear, or the web into
visually validated, reviewed pull requests inside a company's
development environment. Ramp says Inspect now accounts for 74% of
merged PRs and has helped builders ship 3.4x more PRs per person than
a year ago, as the bottleneck shifts from writing code toward deciding
which ideas are worth shipping.

 RIPPLE STRIKES DEAL WITH ROBINHOOD TO EXPAND RLUSD STABLECOIN USAGE
(5 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Ripple and Robinhood are expanding RLUSD across institutional
trading, treasury management, and financing, with the stablecoin set
to support settlement through Ripple Prime and Bitstamp while also
being listed on Robinhood. The partnership shows stablecoins moving
beyond crypto trading into 24/7 financial infrastructure, where
issuers increasingly compete by bundling settlement, treasury,
payments, and financing around their digital dollars.

MISCELLANEOUS

 REDOTPAY REPORTEDLY PLANS US IPO BY YEAR-END AT UP TO $5B VALUATION
(2 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Hong Kong-based stablecoin payments company RedotPay is reportedly
preparing a confidential US IPO filing that could value the business
at about $5 billion, with JPMorgan and Goldman Sachs advising. The
company says it serves more than 9 million customers and has surpassed
$180 million in annualized revenue, although the offering would come
while RedotPay remains in an ongoing $473 million legal dispute with
Binance.

 GUIDEWIRE AGREES TO ACQUIRE HYPEREXPONENTIAL FOR UP TO $2.1B (4
MINUTE READ) [17]

 Guidewire agreed to acquire AI-native underwriting platform
hyperexponential for $1.7 billion in cash plus up to $400 million of
contingent consideration, bringing hx's pricing and risk-decision
technology into Guidewire's P&C insurance platform. The deal is aimed
at accelerating “agentic underwriting,” where AI agents work
alongside underwriters and actuaries across submission triage,
pricing, and portfolio decisions, and Guidewire says hx already
supports decisions across more than $75 billion of annual commercial
P&C premium.
