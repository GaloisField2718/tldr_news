NEWS & TRENDS

 BINANCE ARRANGES $6.34 BILLION TO COVER LOSSES FROM RECORD CRYPTO
HACK (3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Binance said October 30 that it has reserved corporate funds to
restore customer balances after attackers exploited internal
withdrawal systems, while partners separately froze roughly $3 billion
in affected assets. Withdrawals remain paused during security checks,
and the exchange has revised its initial explanation of a compromised
laptop after discovering additional breach points, leaving the full
failure under investigation.

 INSTINCT ROLLS OUT LINK BALANCE FOR AI SHOPPING WITH SPENDING LIMITS
(2 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Users can now let Instinct's assistant make purchases below a chosen
threshold without approving every checkout using a prefunded wallet
built with Stripe and Link. Funds are held in USDC, with no manual
conversion needed before purchases. The threshold applies per purchase
rather than to total spending. Users generally remain responsible for
transactions, including items the agent buys by mistake.

 POLYMARKET RESUMES TRADING AFTER $2.65M EXPLOIT TIED TO THIRD-PARTY
PROVIDER (2 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Polymarket temporarily halted new trades and relayer actions after an
attacker moved roughly $2.65 million through swaps, then said it
identified and fixed the issue. The platform attributed the exploit to
a compromised third-party provider and referred the matter to law
enforcement. It has resumed trading and said affected users would be
fully reimbursed.

 AI AGENTS WON'T PAY THE WAY YOU DO (4 MINUTE READ) [11]

 AI agents could materially change the payment mix because they will
optimize for user-defined goals such as rewards, discounts, cost, and
efficiency rather than simply following familiar checkout habits. That
could favor methods like pay-by-bank in some contexts and create a new
competition among cards, bank payments, and other rails to become the
payment option agents select on behalf of consumers.

 RAMP CEO SAYS 70% OF R&D IS FOCUSED ON UNRELEASED PRODUCTS (49 MINUTE
VIDEO) [12]

 Simon Taylor's conversation with Ramp CEO Eric Glyman covers why Ramp
sees itself as a productivity company more than a fintech, how AI is
changing software economics, and why non-engineers are increasingly
shipping code with tools like Inspect. Glyman also discusses Ramp's
trust charter, stablecoins, AI as a major new category of business
spend, and a future where autonomous agents may choose financial
infrastructure on behalf of companies.

 AI IS COMING FOR BILLABLE HOURS. INTUIT TURNS ACCOUNTANTS INTO GROWTH
ADVISORS (3 MINUTE READ) [13]

 New tools will automate month-end close, flag financial anomalies,
and let firms build custom agents, freeing up time for higher-value
client work. According to the company, its Accountant Suite serves
more than 260,000 firms, with users reporting savings of over nine
hours per client each month and many spending 30% more time on
advisory services. The platform will also match growing businesses
with firms, helping them replace revenue from routine tasks with
advice and new client relationships.

 APPLE PAY AND GOOGLE PAY LAUNCH IN UZBEKISTAN (2 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Uzbekistan has announced the arrival of Apple Pay and Google Pay into
its banking system as the Central Asian nation looks to open up its
financial services.

MISCELLANEOUS

 ICE MOVES TO BRING HYPERLIQUID PERPETUAL FUTURES TO THE US (3 MINUTE
READ) [15]

 Intercontinental Exchange, owner of the NYSE, signed a deal with
Trade[XYZ] to operate regulated US markets for perpetual futures built
on Hyperliquid, with ICE also taking a strategic stake in the company.
The planned offering would bring 24/7 leveraged perpetuals on assets
such as stocks, commodities and currencies into a regulated US
framework using KYC, futures brokers and ICE clearing infrastructure,
marking a significant bridge between crypto-native market structure
and traditional exchanges.

 CD&R TARGETS $40B BUYOUT OF GLOBAL PAYMENTS (2 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Private equity firm CD&R is reportedly working with Global Payments'
largest shareholder, GTCR, on a potential take-private of the payments
processor, which has an enterprise value of about $40 billion. The
talks come after Global Payments' $22.7 billion Worldpay acquisition
drew investor criticism and activist pressure, making a
private-equity-led reset a possible next step if a deal materializes.

 HARLEM CHILDREN'S ZONE BUYS GOALSETTER TO ENHANCE FINANCIAL EDUCATION
OFFERING (1 MINUTE READ) [17]

 The New York nonprofit plans to extend the platform's student-focused
tools into a continuous learning journey from early childhood through
adulthood. Its existing products include classroom modules for middle
and high schools and MoneyLingo, an app teaching college students and
young adults about saving, spending, investing, and credit. Founder
Tanya Van Court will join HCZ as chief fintech officer. Financial
terms were not disclosed.
