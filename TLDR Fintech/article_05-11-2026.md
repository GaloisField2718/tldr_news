NEWS & TRENDS

 REVOLUT'S VALUATION JUMPS 53% TO $115B AHEAD OF PLANNED US BANK
LAUNCH (4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Revolut completed a secondary share sale at a $115 billion valuation,
up 53% from a year earlier, as it prepares to launch a US national
bank in the first half of 2027 pending final approval. The company now
serves more than 80 million retail customers, while Revolut Business
generates about $1.28 billion of annualized revenue and processes
roughly $48.6 billion of transactions each month, giving the company
another major growth engine as it considers a potential IPO no earlier
than 2028.

 STRIPE TREASURY LAUNCHES IN THE UK (2 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Stripe, the programmable financial services company, today launched
Stripe Treasury in the UK, enabling founders to manage money from the
same Dashboard where they already accept payments, without navigating
across separate providers.

 SEQUOIA REWINDS 20 YEARS OF EXPANSION TO REFOCUS ON CONCENTRATED
VENTURE INVESTING (8 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Sequoia is simplifying its organization after two decades of
expansion, shutting its London office, ending the Arc accelerator and
Scouts program, merging early-stage and growth investing, flattening
the partnership, and eliminating its Monday partner meeting. The firm
says it has raised about $10 billion of new capital and is returning
to a more concentrated model built around fewer bets, a smaller team,
and deeper support for founders from its Bay Area base.

DEEP DIVES & REPORTS

 PAYMENTS ARE THE NEW ADS (8 MINUTE READ) [11]

 The one-time virtual cards behind most agent purchases today are a
fragile bridge, breaking rewards, purchase protection, and checkout
flows, while banks hold off on issuing agent network tokens until
liability is settled. Meanwhile, agent platforms are routing around
banks with prefunded stablecoin balances, where the bank sees one
top-up and the platform earns roughly 4x more per $100 than on a
virtual card. As agents erode ad revenue, platforms like Meta
(charging fees on Muse-completed sales) will turn to payments to
monetize, and banks risk losing the customer unless they step in to
own agent liability.

 NETSUITE FOUNDER SAYS “ERP AS WE KNOW IT IS OVER” (8 MINUTE READ)
[12]

 AI will change ERP more profoundly than cloud computing by shifting
software from a system people operate into one that understands
business context and increasingly performs work on their behalf.
NetSuite is pushing this through NetSuite Next, MCP-based AI
connectors and SuiteSkills, industry-specific agents, faster
AI-assisted implementations, and an “Autonomous Suite” where
agents handle tasks such as reconciliations, approvals, and close
exceptions while staying within existing permissions and controls.

 SWEAT THE DETAILS: WHY GOOD PAYMENTS GET DECLINED AND HOW TO OPTIMIZE
FRAUD, COST, AND ACCEPTANCE (14 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Payment optimization is a three-way trade-off between fraud, cost,
and acceptance, with small differences in issuer behavior, routing,
authentication, and payment data often determining whether a
legitimate transaction succeeds profitably. Optimize for net business
outcome rather than one metric in isolation, using detailed payment
data and issuer-specific knowledge to reduce fraud, lower fees, and
recover good transactions without creating new losses.

 MASTERCARD EXPANDS HDFC PARTNERSHIP TO SCALE IN INDIA (4 MINUTE READ)
[15]

 Mastercard expanded its partnership with HDFC Bank, India's largest
private bank, with a new small-business agreement that could support
roughly 3 million additional cards and more services sold through
HDFC's 100 million-customer base. The strategy is to grow alongside
India's UPI system, betting that greater digital-payment adoption will
eventually push more consumers and businesses toward debit, credit,
and other Mastercard products.

 ONEPAY INTRODUCES STABLECOIN DEPOSITS POWERED BY STRIPE (2 MINUTE
READ) [16]

 Starting today, select customers in the US can use stablecoins to

stablecoins from an external wallet to a deposit address, and they are
converted to US dollars and credited to your Checking balance within
moments, with no conversion fees at launch.

MISCELLANEOUS

 API-DRIVEN COMMERCE: HOW PROGRAMMABLE MONEY IS AUTOMATED FOR MODERN
B2B MARKETPLACES (5 MINUTE READ) [18]

 B2B marketplaces routinely process transactions involving three
suppliers, four tax jurisdictions, net-60 terms, and a platform
take-rate. Handling these payment flows via batch processing or manual
finance ops creates immediate bottlenecks. Moving money across complex
seller networks requires treating currency as software logic rather
than static banking entries.

 AI FINANCING DRIVES PRIVATE CREDIT GROWTH AS REFINANCING RISKS RISE
(3 MINUTE READ) [19]

 AI infrastructure is expanding private credit beyond traditional
corporate lending into land, power, semiconductors, and compute, with
hyperscalers and CoreWeave expected to spend nearly $1 trillion in
2027 using cash flow, debt, joint ventures, and SPVs. The risk is a
large refinancing wall from 2027 to 2029, especially for weaker
borrowers using PIK interest, while increasingly circular financing
between chipmakers, AI developers, and infrastructure providers could
create correlated losses in a downturn.

 PINE LAB BRINGS UNIFIED COMMERCE TO SINGAPORE WITH STRIPE (2 MINUTE
READ) [20]

 Leading merchant commerce platform Pine Labs and global programmable
financial infrastructure provider Stripe today announced a unified
commerce solution that enables eligible retailers in Singapore to
manage online and in-store payments through a single platform,
simplifying operations while helping retailers engage customers more
effectively across online and in-store channels.
