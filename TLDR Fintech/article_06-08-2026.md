NEWS & TRENDS

 VISA TO ACQUIRE BIOCATCH FOR $2.4B (3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Visa agreed to acquire behavioral biometrics and fraud-prevention
company BioCatch for $2.4 billion, strengthening its defenses against
account takeovers, scams, and other AI-enabled financial crimes. The
deal follows Visa's $1 billion acquisition of Featurespace and expands
its value-added services with technology that analyzes behavioral,
device, and network signals to distinguish legitimate users from
fraudsters in real time.

 WELLS FARGO TO ROLL OUT TOKENISED DEPOSITS FOR CORPORATE CLIENTS (3
MINUTE READ) [9]

 Wells Fargo is introducing tokenised deposits for corporate and
commercial clients, enabling them to move, programme and settle funds
24/7. The programme will roll out this autumn, with a limited US
dollar to British pound exchange, expanding over the course of 2027 to
more clients, countries, and currencies.

 MARC ANDREESSEN AND CHRIS DIXON ARGUE AMERICA NEEDS THE CLARITY ACT
(10 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Marc Andreessen and Chris Dixon argue that the CLARITY Act would
replace regulatory ambiguity with federal oversight, disclosure
requirements, anti-fraud protections, and clearer boundaries between
securities and commodities regulation for digital assets. They contend
that failing to pass durable legislation would favor offshore actors,
slow responsible US development, and weaken America's leadership in
crypto, while rejecting claims that the bill undermines sanctions
enforcement, securities law, or open-source development.

DEEP DIVES & REPORTS

 THE FUTURE OF PAYMENTS WILL BE DEFINED BY INTEROPERABILITY (10 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 The payments industry has spent years building faster, more
convenient, and increasingly digital ways to move money. Yet as
payment methods and financial networks multiply, another challenge is
becoming impossible to ignore: making these systems work together. A
customer may use one wallet, a merchant another provider, and a bank
connected to an entirely different infrastructure. Each system can
function effectively on its own, but the experience becomes more
complicated when they need to interact. This is where interoperability
becomes critical.

 AGENTIC PAYMENTS WILL NEED MORE THAN WALLETS (10 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Agentic payments are moving from demo territory into infrastructure.
Natural's recent raise is a useful signal for the category. The market
is beginning to organize around wallets, vaults, cards, merchant
acceptance, credit, billing, and direct rail access for AI agents.
That matters. Agents need ways to participate in payment flows:
holding value, accepting payments, triggering transfers, and
interacting with existing rails.

 WHY X MONEY IS WILLING TO LOSE MONEY TODAY (10 MINUTE READ) [13]

 X has begun rolling out X Money to US Premium and Premium+
subscribers, bundling a 6% APY cash account, a 3% cashback Visa debit
card, and free instant transfers between X accounts. The benefits are
aggressive and land at an interesting moment for the social media app.
X now sits inside SpaceX, down ~30% since IPO, and valued at $1.51T.

 CLEAR STREET OFFERS PRE-IPO ACCESS TO DATABRICKS THROUGH NEW PRIVATE
MARKETS PLATFORM (3 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Clear Street is launching a platform for accredited investors to gain
pre-IPO exposure to Databricks and eventually up to 30 private
companies, alongside dedicated research and financing for eligible
positions. Investors will buy interests through layered SPV structures
rather than directly owning Databricks shares, highlighting both Wall
Street's growing push into private markets and the legal and transfer
risks surrounding indirect secondary transactions.

 IKEA LAUNCHES UK HOME INSURANCE (1 MINUTE READ) [16]

 IKEA is expanding into financial services by launching home insurance
in the UK through a partnership with insurtech startup Urban Jungle.
Customers can purchase contents, buildings, or combined coverage
backed by Urban Jungle's AI-powered platform. The launch reflects the
growing trend of consumer brands embedding insurance into their
ecosystems to extend customer relationships beyond the initial sale.

 FBI AGENT CHARGED WITH STEALING NEARLY $1M IN CRYPTO TIED TO RUSSIA
(3 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Federal prosecutors charged former FBI supervisory agent Patrick
Yaroch with transferring roughly $925,000 from cryptocurrency wallets
linked to Russia into his personal accounts using private keys found
in FBI systems. According to court filings, Yaroch later confessed and
surrendered access to the wallets, while the FBI said it fired him and
opened a broader investigation.
