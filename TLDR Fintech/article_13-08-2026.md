NEWS & TRENDS

 ADYEN EXPANDS TOAST PARTNERSHIP INTO THE US (3 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Adyen is expanding its partnership with Toast into the US, adding its
payments infrastructure to a platform that facilitated more than $215
billion in GPV across roughly 180,000 locations over the past year.
The deal extends a relationship already spanning Ireland, the UK,
Canada, and Australia, while giving Toast greater processing stability
and creating potential to embed more Adyen financial products into its
restaurant and retail platform.

 CLOUDFLARE LAUNCHES CLOUDFLARE WALLETS (2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Cloudflare Wallets is a programmable wallet for the agentic Internet.
Starting today, you can claim a Cloudflare Wallet handle for your
account, which will provide a unique username to help you better
connect with merchants. Soon, you will be able to set up and use your
Cloudflare Wallet to pay for APIs and content.

 MONZO TOPS ONE MILLION BUSINESS ACCOUNTS (2 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Monzo has become the first British digital bank to surpass one
million business customers. Having launched the service in 2020, the
online-only bank has seen numbers double in just the past two years,
posing a challenge to the dominance of the UK's Big Four banks:
Barclays, HSBC, NatWest, and Lloyds.

DEEP DIVES & REPORTS

 THE FDIC WANTS A RULEBOOK FOR BANK-FINTECH PARTNERSHIPS (8 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 The FDIC is working with major banking and fintech trade groups on a
voluntary standard-setting and certification body for bank-fintech
partnerships, signaling a shift toward clearer baseline controls after
years of BaaS failures and regulatory fallout. The post also covers
Block moving more payments onto its own bank rails, Chime's second
consecutive GAAP-profitable quarter, Polymarket's potential $20B+
valuation, Bunq's rejected US charter application, and Cloudflare's
push into AI-agent wallets and payments.

 WHY INVESTORS ARE BACKING AI-NATIVE FINTECH STARTUPS BUILT FOR SPEED
(8 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Investors are increasingly favoring AI-native fintech startups that
can ship faster, scale with leaner teams, and generate more revenue
per employee, even as broader fintech deal activity declines. Durable
winners will pair that speed with proprietary data infrastructure,
disciplined AI deployment, and strong accuracy and security controls
rather than simply layering AI onto existing workflows.

 TRANSUNION WARNS LENDERS ON PRIVATE STUDENT LOAN RISK AFTER US RULES
(4 MINUTE READ) [11]

 New federal borrowing caps could push more student borrowers toward
private financing, but TransUnion says lenders will need to be
selective about who they serve. Roughly 30% of federal borrowers in
repayment are at least 90 days delinquent, compared with about 3% for
unsecured personal loans, creating significant underwriting risk as
private lenders pursue new originations and refinancing opportunities.

 KLARNA GOES LIVE WITH JP MORGAN PAYMENTS FOR US MERCHANTS (3 MINUTE
READ) [14]

 Klarna is now integrated into JP Morgan Payments' Commerce Platform,
allowing US merchants to offer pay-in-full, interest-free installment,
and longer-term financing options without a separate integration. The
partnership gives Klarna distribution through the largest US merchant
acquirer, which processes $2.6 trillion annually, while helping JP
Morgan merchants add flexible checkout options with less
implementation work.

 ROBINHOOD BRINGS ZERO-FEE DIGITAL ASSET TRADING TO UK (3 MINUTE READ)
[15]

 Robinhood is adding more than 50 digital assets to its UK investment
app through Bitstamp, with no trading, account maintenance, or custody
fees. The rollout also includes Cortex Digests, a generative AI tool
that explains market moves in plain English, as Robinhood pushes to
become an all-in-one investing platform spanning stocks, options,
futures, and crypto.

MISCELLANEOUS

 UBER INVESTS IN CHILE FINTECH IN PUSH INTO MOTORBIKE LOANS (4 MINUTE
READ) [16]

 Uber is backing Chilean fintech Galgo, which has deployed more than
$400 million in credit and will develop lower-cost motorcycle
financing exclusively for Uber delivery workers. Galgo, now profitable
and generating roughly $100 million in annualized revenue, plans to
enter another Latin American market next year and use the partnership
to invest further in technology, data, and AI. The deal extends Uber's
push into vehicle financing after its 2024 investment in mobility
fintech Moove.

 REPEAT FOUNDER RYAN WILLIAMS RAISES $10M SEED FOR AN AI STARTUP FOR
PRIVATE CREDIT MANAGERS (3 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Ellis AI is building AI agents to automate the fragmented back-office
work of private credit firms, connecting existing documents,
accounting systems, and spreadsheets in one platform. The agents can
monitor portfolios, flag data discrepancies, prepare reports, and help
close monthly books, while leaving material decisions to human
managers. Founder Ryan Williams previously co-founded real estate
investment platform Cadre, which raised more than $160 million before
being acquired by Yieldstreet in 2024.

 GOOGLE PAY INTRODUCES GEMINI-ENABLED AI TOOL FOR USERS IN INDIA (4
MINUTE READ) [18]

 Google Pay is turning its payments app into more of a personal
finance assistant, letting Indian users ask Gemini questions about
their spending, get categorized breakdowns, identify savings
opportunities, and learn about topics like credit scores and

 BRAZIL TO EXPAND PIX PAYMENT SYSTEM (2 MINUTE READ) [19]

 Brazil's central bank is considering expanding the scope of its
instant payments system Pix to integrate it with other international
systems.

 US REGULATOR REJECTS BUNQ'S BANKING LICENSE APPLICATION (3 MINUTE
READ) [20]

 The OCC rejected Dutch fintech Bunq's application for a US banking
license, citing concerns over whether the company would maintain
sufficient capital and whether management had enough experience with
unsecured credit cards.
