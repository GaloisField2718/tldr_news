NEWS & TRENDS

 NEW YORK SUES KALSHI OVER ALLEGED ILLEGAL GAMBLING OPERATION (4
MINUTE READ) [8]

 New York is suing Kalshi, alleging its sports, political, and other
event contracts constitute unlicensed gambling and seeking a permanent
injunction, user restitution, and penalties that could total $36
billion. Kalshi and the CFTC argue the federally licensed exchange
falls under exclusive federal oversight, escalating the broader fight
over whether prediction markets are financial derivatives or
state-regulated gambling.

 CHIME TO CUT 10% OF TOTAL WORKFORCE ON AI-DRIVEN EFFICIENCIES (2
MINUTE READ) [9]

 Chime is cutting about 10% of its workforce, or roughly 150
employees, as it restructures around AI and aims to improve
profitability as a public fintech. CEO Chris Britt said smaller,
flatter teams are enabling faster execution, reflecting a broader
shift across financial services as companies like Block, Visa,
Robinhood, and Mastercard use AI to reduce costs and increase
operating efficiency. For fintech investors, the focus is moving
beyond AI as a product feature toward AI as a driver of stronger
margins and leaner operations.

 AI INVESTOR LEOPOLD ASCHENBRENNER FORCED TO UNWIND PUBLIC STOCK
POSITIONS AFTER STEEP LOSSES (4 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Leopold Aschenbrenner's hedge fund, Situational Awareness, was
reportedly forced to sell its public equity portfolio after leveraged
bets on AI infrastructure stocks and shorts against software companies
moved sharply against it. Citadel agreed to acquire the public
positions as the fund's prime brokers worked to raise cash and meet
margin requirements, marking a major test of Aschenbrenner's
concentrated AI investment thesis.

DEEP DIVES & REPORTS

 WHY FINTECHS MAY SKIRT THE US (4 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Delays to US open banking rules are widening the payments innovation
gap with Europe, where standardized APIs and instant
account-to-account payments make it easier for fintechs to launch and
scale. Without clearer mandates, US banks may remain dependent on
costly integrations and insecure screen scraping, while global
fintechs prioritize markets with better data access, lower processing
costs, and more consistent regulation.

 FROM SYSTEMS OF RECORD TO OUTCOME-DRIVEN AI BANKING (7 MINUTE READ)
[12]

 The next generation of banking will be built around AI agents that
orchestrate end-to-end workflows and continuously make decisions,
shifting banks from maintaining records to delivering measurable
business outcomes. Rather than replacing core banking systems,
institutions can layer agentic AI on top of existing infrastructure to
automate lending, payments, fraud detection, and compliance while
improving governance and reducing operating costs. The fintech
opportunity is less about deploying standalone AI features and more
about building the orchestration, data, and governance layers that
enable banks to operationalize AI at enterprise scale.

 ROBINHOOD NOW EARNS MORE FROM PREDICTION MARKETS THAN FROM STOCKS OR
CRYPTO (15 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Robinhood's Q2 earnings included data showing prediction markets
overtook crypto and stock trading for the first time. Transaction
revenue was $776m, up 44%. Options did $342m, event contracts did
$156m, equities did $129m, and crypto did $100m, which fell 38% YoY.
The company also posted record Q2 revenue of $1.31bn, up 32% YoY, with
diluted EPS of $0.62 against a consensus nearer $0.42.

LAUNCHES & PRODUCTS

 RENT FINTECH FLEX APPLIES FOR ILC CHARTER (3 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Rent payments fintech Flex has applied for an industrial loan company
(ILC) charter, a move that would let it issue its own credit products
instead of relying on sponsor banks. Flex lets renters split a monthly
rent payment into smaller installments that better align with when
they get paid, helping users avoid late fees without compounding
interest or stacking loans. Since 2019, the company has processed more
than $40 billion in rent payments for 3.2 million users and says it
has helped customers avoid more than $780 million in late fees.

 JUNIPER SQUARE LAUNCHES AI-POWERED FUND ADMIN OVERSIGHT AGENT (4
MINUTE READ) [15]

 Fay is an AI agent that reviews fund administrator deliverables using
more than 150 checks across financial statements, capital account
statements, and supporting workbooks. The product combines AI-based
document extraction with deterministic calculations to flag errors,
reconcile figures across reports and periods, and reduce review time
for CFOs and controllers while preserving source-level traceability
and human judgment.

MISCELLANEOUS

 BANKS SEE MARKETING OPPORTUNITY ON COLLEGE ATHLETES' JERSEYS (5
MINUTE READ) [16]

 A recent NCAA rule change allowing corporate logos on college
athletes' jerseys has created a new marketing opportunity for banks
and fintechs. JPMorgan Chase's partnership with Ohio State and SoFi's
deal with Notre Dame highlight how financial institutions are
competing to reach students and young consumers through
high-visibility sports sponsorships.

 ELLIS EMERGES FROM STEALTH WITH $10M+ FOR AI-NATIVE PRIVATE CREDIT
OPERATIONS (4 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Ellis raised more than $10 million in seed funding led by First Round
Capital to build an AI-native operating platform for private credit
managers. The platform unifies data from fund administrators,
loan-servicing systems, general ledgers, banks, legal documents, and
spreadsheets, then uses specialized agents to automate reconciliation,
LP reporting, portfolio monitoring, and compliance while keeping
humans in control of approvals.

 STARTUP FOUNDER BUYS WASHINGTON BANK TO EXTEND FINTECH PUSH (1 MINUTE
READ) [18]

 Increase founder and former Stripe engineer Darragh Buckley has
acquired and rebranded a Washington community bank, giving his fintech
direct control over regulated banking infrastructure instead of
relying on partner banks. The move positions Increase to compete with
banking-as-a-service providers like Lead Bank and Column, offering
fintechs a more integrated platform that combines banking technology
with a licensed bank while continuing to serve the local community.
