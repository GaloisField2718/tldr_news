NEWS & TRENDS

 ANTHROPIC LAUNCHES CLAUDE PERSONAL FINANCE AHEAD OF NASDAQ LISTING (4
MINUTE READ) [8]

 Paid US subscribers can connect bank accounts through Plaid to track
spending, review subscriptions, and ask questions about their money.
The read-only tool cannot move funds or make investments, carries no
ads or sponsored recommendations, and excludes conversations inside
its finance hub from model training. Nearly two-thirds of US consumers
connecting accounts to AI assistants through Plaid had never used a
Plaid-powered personal finance app, suggesting these assistants are
reaching people traditional budgeting tools missed.

 VISA RELEASES FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND INDUSTRY BENCHMARK FOR AI SHOPPING;
ALMOST HALF OF TEST RUNS FAIL (4 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Visa tested 22 AI models on 15 shopping scenarios requiring them to
uncover customer requirements and correctly decide whether to
recommend a purchase. Its November 5 results show a 57.2% overall pass
rate, with 54% of failed attempts still producing a purchase
recommendation despite unmet or undiscovered requirements. The
benchmark exposes reliability problems before payment execution. Visa
plans to extend testing to checkout and order placement

 REVOLUT COMPLETES FUNDRAISING PROCESS ESTABLISHING $115 BILLION
VALUATION (2 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Revolut's November 4 transaction brings investors including Coatue,
T. Rowe Price, and Baillie Gifford into a deal that also gives
employees another opportunity to sell shares. The amount raised was
not disclosed. The valuation accompanies international bank launches
and conditional US charter approval, but deeper customer relationships
remain a challenge. Reuters reports that lending represented just 6%
of deposits at year-end 2025, leaving substantial room for expansion
alongside additional credit risk.

 ENTERPRISE AI SPEND HITS $177B AS AGENTS BECOME THE DOMINANT SOURCE
OF USAGE (12 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Enterprise AI deployment spending is estimated to reach $177 billion
in 2026, up 5.4x year over year, with agents now generating 80% of
OpenRouter token usage and driving spending far beyond enterprise
budgets. Anthropic and OpenAI together capture 86% of enterprise LLM
API spend, while AI-native companies dominate categories where agents
can act with strong context and verifiable outcomes, especially
coding. AI is expanding the software market rather than simply
replacing SaaS, with 55% of incremental AI spend coming from new
budgets.

 APPLIED AI AGENT LESSONS FROM PLAID, NUBANK, VISA, AND MERCURY (15
MINUTE READ) [12]

 This article covers 4 agent-related launches from some of the biggest
fintech companies. Mercury now lets AI agents work inside business
bank accounts, under limits. Plaid connects consumer bank data to
Claude. Visa tested whether AI models can shop for people, and about
four runs in ten failed. Nubank published how it tests its support
agents before customers use them.

 REMOTE LAUNCHES EXPENSE CARDS BUILT INTO EOR, BRINGING SPEND INTO THE
SAME SYSTEM AS PAY (3 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Launched November 5, Remote's virtual expense cards let eligible
employees pay for work purchases without advancing personal funds and
waiting for reimbursement. Spending controls and transaction records
connect to employment data already held in its employer-of-record
platform, reducing separate reconciliation work. Availability is
limited to eligible EOR customers in supported countries, with no
platform or per-card charge

 GOOGLE MAKES BIG PLANS FOR WALLET (3 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Google's wallet plans extend beyond in-store payments to receipts,
order tracking, refunds, and merchant offers. Google is also building
constrained credentials that let third-party agents pay without
exposing private data, and the company says the infrastructure already
exists with Gemini and Google Pay, so product experience is now the
focus. The move sets Google against Stripe's Link and agentic commerce
protocols from OpenAI, Visa, and Mastercard, though the article gives
no adoption figures.

 GOLDMAN SACHS RESTRICTED CLAUDE CODE FOR BANKERS — BUT MADE ONE
SURPRISING EXCEPTION (4 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Goldman Sachs has pulled Anthropic's Claude Code and Microsoft's
GitHub coding tools from bankers and traders for engineering work,
while exempting its quantitative strats. The bank weighs productivity
gains against potential risks when deciding who keeps AI tools.

 BMO TURNS TO AGENTIC AI TO RESOLVE CARD ISSUES (3 MINUTE READ) [17]

 BMO's November 6 launch gives Canadian contact-centre employees AI
tools that match transactions, retrieve policies, and prepare cases,
reducing the work of switching between systems. The bank targets
20%–30% shorter handling times for eligible calls and automatic
processing of roughly 40% of eligible credit refund requests,
potentially delivering same-day credits.

 MARQETA CEO SAYS CARDS REMAIN ONE OF THE STRONGEST TOOLS FOR CUSTOMER
ENGAGEMENT (4 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Marqeta CEO Mike Milotich says large companies are increasingly using
card products and embedded finance to increase customer engagement and
drive usage of their core platforms. Marqeta is positioning itself as
the infrastructure layer for these programs, bundling card issuing,
payments, and newer capabilities such as stablecoin-backed cards,
while Q3 processing volume rose 29% to $126.5 billion and revenue
increased 8% to $176.2 million.
