NEWS & TRENDS

 RAMP REACHES $60B VALUATION AS IT PUSHES DEEPER INTO AI (4 MINUTE
READ) [5]

 Ramp raised $1.85 billion at a $60 billion valuation, up from $44
billion just four months earlier, as annualized revenue surpassed $2
billion and annualized purchase volume reached more than $275 billion.
The company is expanding well beyond expense management into payments,
accounting, procurement, and AI infrastructure, including model
routing, token spend management, agentic cards, and Glass, positioning
itself as a broader financial and AI operating platform.

 MERCURY LETS AI AGENTS MOVE MONEY THROUGH MCP WITH USER APPROVAL (2
MINUTE READ) [6]

 Mercury expanded its MCP from read-only account access to financial
actions, allowing users to ask ChatGPT, Claude, and other agents to
move money, pay vendors, and categorize transactions directly from
chat. Agents can prepare actions, but Mercury keeps a human approval
step before transfers are completed, combining agentic workflows with
account-level controls and revocable access.

 AMEX WILL PAY FOR AGENT ERRORS, EXCEPT IN THE CASES AGENTS ACTUALLY
GET WRONG (5 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Amex calls Agent Purchase Protection an industry-first commitment to
cover Card Member losses from a registered agent's purchase error, for
the full transaction amount plus other reasonable charges directly
incurred. It is the first time a major issuer has put its name to the
liability question agentic commerce has been dodging.

DEEP DIVES & REPORTS

 STRIPE SAYS AGENTIC COMMERCE ACTIVITY WENT “VERTICAL” IN
SEPTEMBER (5 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Stripe President John Collison says agent-driven commerce on Stripe
began accelerating sharply in September, suggesting AI agents are
starting to move from product discovery into actual purchasing. He
argues that computer use was a key unlock because agents can now
navigate existing websites and complete purchases without native
integrations, while payments companies still need to solve issues such
as authorization, liability, and merchant acceptance.

 WINNING THE RELAY RACE: LONG-RUNNING AGENTS IN HIGH-VALUE CUSTOMER
JOURNEYS (9 MINUTE READ) [9]

 The next major AI opportunity is “long-running agents” that own
complex customer journeys across weeks or months, rather than
completing isolated tasks. By maintaining context, coordinating
sub-agents, and learning from each case, these systems could capture
value that currently leaks across human handoffs and let vendors price
against outcomes or revenue uplift, with go-to-market models ranging
from software platforms to outsourced services to fully integrated
AI-native businesses.

 PATRICK COLLISON SAYS PRIVY HAS SCALED RAPIDLY INSIDE STRIPE (3
MINUTE READ) [11]

 Patrick Collison says Privy has grown sharply since Stripe acquired
it, with assets in wallets up about 25x, monthly active developers up
3x, revenue up 9x, and transaction volume approaching $25 billion per
month. Stripe is now integrating Privy across Treasury, Link, and
Connect while combining it with Bridge, fiat rails, cards, and
stablecoins to build a programmable financial stack for both
businesses and AI agents.

 MASTERCARD AND BVNK LAUNCH GLOBAL MONEY PLATFORM FOR FIAT AND
STABLECOINS (4 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Mastercard and BVNK launched Global Money Platform, a single API that
combines multi-currency fiat and stablecoin accounts, real-time
conversion, stablecoin-linked cards, and local bank payouts through
Mastercard Move. The platform is designed to let banks, fintechs, and
enterprises manage and embed global money movement from one
infrastructure layer, with future support planned for agentic commerce
and additional regulated forms of money such as tokenized deposits.

 VISA DIRECT MOVES STABLECOIN-POWERED MONEY MOVEMENT FROM PILOT TO
PRODUCTION (4 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Visa is moving stablecoin capabilities in Visa Direct into commercial
production, with Inyo Global becoming the first partner to enable
businesses to prefund cross-border payouts using USDC in near real
time. The launch lets fintechs, money transmitters, and payment
companies use stablecoins for 24/7 liquidity and funding while still
relying on Visa Direct's existing global payout network, marking a
shift from stablecoin experimentation toward day-to-day payments
infrastructure.

 GI PARTNERS CEO LEAVES AFTER A YEAR, DELAYING FUND VII PLANS (4
MINUTE READ) [14]

 GI Partners CEO and head of private equity Travis Pearson left the
$49 billion firm after just over a year in the role, prompting GI to
reconsider the timing of its seventh private equity fund and push the
launch into 2027. The firm does not plan to name a new CEO, instead
relying on its executive committee while three senior investors
co-lead the private equity business.

 AI-NATIVE PAYMENTS PLATFORM MOVERE LAUNCHES (2 MINUTE READ) [15]

 A collection of banking and fintech veterans has unveiled Movere, an
