 THE LAST MILE PROBLEM FOR STABLECOINS (7 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Stablecoins let small banks transact directly across borders, but the
final leg into local currency still carries FX, compliance, and
funding costs. Mastercard's BVNK-powered Global Money Platform is the
center that absorbs them.

DEEP DIVES & REPORTS

 SHOULD TESLA AND SPACEX MERGE? (12 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Mostly Metrics weighs the industrial logic of combining Tesla and
SpaceX against the risk of creating a sprawling conglomerate that
investors may value less clearly. A merger could simplify shared
projects such as Terafab, improve capital and talent allocation, and
vertically integrate chips, energy, satellites, AI, and robotics, but
the companies have very different growth rates, margins, and capital
needs, and many of the operational benefits could potentially be
achieved through partnerships instead.

 BLOCK'S TURNAROUND STRATEGY: TWO ECOSYSTEMS, ONE BANK (12 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 Block's turnaround came from narrowing its focus, cutting more than
40% of staff, consolidating around Square and Cash App, and then using
its bank charter to scale consumer lending. Cash App now drives most
of Block's growth, with $19 billion of quarterly consumer lending
originations and rising gross profit per user, while Square is
re-accelerating through stronger distribution, but the strategy also
concentrates more risk in credit losses and regulatory controls.

 CAN REVOLUT DOMINATE THE US WITHOUT TAKING RISK? (16 MINUTE READ)
[12]

 Revolut's US bank will be credit-card led. It will match Amex and
Chase perks while keeping lending under 10% of the balance sheet and
securitizing the rest. Chase earned about $23.8 billion in card net
interest income in 2025 versus $2.2 billion in net non-interest card
revenue. Perks are acquisition costs and the loan is the product, so
Revolut must choose between dominating and taking little risk.

 LIFE AFTER THE SWIPE (10 MINUTE READ) [13]

 This post walks through what happens after a card swipe and how card
programs are built. Stablecoin-linked cards let issuers launch in 10
to 30+ markets from one dollar-denominated sponsor like Rain instead
of a bank partner per country. Visa and Mastercard now accept
stablecoin settlement seven days a week, so a multi-market program can
collapse separate prefunded pots into one balance. Issuers also get
new features: yield on dollar balances, loyalty rewards at any
merchant terminal, and global co-brand cards for fan bases. Large
domestic banks gain little from “T-shaped” programs (wide across
markets and deep in products) like this.

 PLAID LAUNCHES AGENT DETECTION TO IDENTIFY AI-DRIVEN FINANCIAL
ACTIVITY (4 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Plaid launched Agent Detection to help banks and fintechs distinguish
human activity from AI agents across login, onboarding, account
linking, and money movement, including identifying which AI provider
is behind a session. Plaid says the system detected 2.3x more agent
sessions than competitors in live testing, and views it as the first
layer of broader infrastructure for trusted agentic finance, where
institutions can recognize agents, assess risk, and eventually let
consumers authorize specific actions safely.
