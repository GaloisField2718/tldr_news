 OPENAI LAUNCHES CHATGPT WALLET FOR SECURE AGENTIC PAYMENTS (5 MINUTE
READ) [5]

 OpenAI is launching ChatGPT Wallet, initially for US ChatGPT Pro
users through Dots, letting agents complete purchases without seeing
users' actual card details while requiring biometric approval for each
transaction. Built with American Express, Mastercard, and Visa, the
wallet uses payment tokens that preserve existing card rewards and
dispute protections. OpenAI is working with payment processors and
fraud providers to help merchants recognize legitimate agent-driven
transactions.

DEEP DIVES & REPORTS

 STRIPE BETS TEMPO CAN BECOME THE PAYMENTS-FIRST BLOCKCHAIN FOR
STABLECOINS (7 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Stripe-backed Tempo is positioning itself as blockchain
infrastructure built specifically for payments, reserving most block
space for simple money movement so low-margin transactions are not
crowded out by higher-value trading activity. The network is targeting
cross-border payments, payroll, marketplace payouts, and emerging
agentic commerce, with Stripe's Bridge and Privy acquisitions
providing stablecoin and wallet infrastructure around the chain.

 STOCK TOKENS ARE WHERE STABLECOINS WERE IN 2023 (13 MINUTE READ) [10]


 Tokenized stocks and ETFs total about $3.2 billion in distributed
tokens, roughly 0.004% of the market, against about $431.6 billion in
real-world assets onchain per RWA.xyz. Fintech Brainfood author Simon
Taylor argues the field splits into four models, from wrapped tokens
with no shareholder rights to natively issued stock, and notes 56 of
62 companies opted out of the OKX and ICE pooled offering.

 GOOGLE CONNECTS WALLET TO GEMINI FOR PERSONALIZED FINANCE INSIGHTS (2
MINUTE READ) [13]

 Google is rolling out Google Wallet integration to Gemini users in
the US, letting the assistant analyze spending, find recurring
subscriptions, and help build personalized budgets using transaction
data. Users can also link bank and credit card accounts through Plaid
for deeper insights, while Gemini remains read-only and cannot move,
manage, or invest money.

 8 QUESTIONS FOR THE WELLS FARGO WEALTH UNIT'S AI CHIEF (4 MINUTE
READ) [14]

 Wells Fargo's AI Teammate assistant has logged hundreds of thousands
of interactions since launching last quarter, according to wealth and
investment management AI head Andre Mansour, who joined from Google
six months ago. Mansour reports the tool runs as an agent over Advisor
Gateway's 200-plus adviser tools, cuts meeting prep from hours to a
couple of prompts, and expects advisers to become an approval layer
for clients' own AI agents.

MISCELLANEOUS

 PAYPAL WANTS A BANK TO EXPAND MERCHANT LENDING AND DIVERSIFY FUNDING
(5 MINUTE READ) [15]

 PayPal says an industrial bank charter would support more lending to
its merchant base while giving the company a cheaper and more
diversified source of capital, including potential deposits. The move
fits PayPal's broader push into financial services across BNPL, Venmo,
and merchant credit, while management still intends to keep the
overall business relatively balance-sheet light by selectively holding
or selling loan assets.

 JANE STREET MORE THAN DOUBLED ITS NVIDIA STAKE IN Q3 FILINGS (4
MINUTE READ) [16]

 Jane Street disclosed 23.8 million Nvidia shares worth about $5.4
billion as of September 30, more than double its 11.8 million-share
position in Q2, making Nvidia its second-largest reported holding. The
filing does not show Jane Street's current exposure or short
positions, and its large options book makes the 13F an incomplete
picture of whether the firm was making a directional bet on Nvidia.

 EVERYTHING IS FAKE (4 MINUTE READ) [17]

 An FBI-uncovered North Korean operation called FaceStealer stole more
than $400 million in crypto over about 18 months using AI-generated
impersonations of colleagues on video calls. The same playbook could
hit banks, from urgent client transfers to account recovery with a
matching face and voice, so biometrics and video confirmation can no
longer stand alone. The FBI figures arrive secondhand, via press
reports, and the bank scenarios are Skinner's sketch rather than
documented cases.
