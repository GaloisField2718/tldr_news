MARKETS & BUSINESS

 CRYPTO HOLDS BIG WEEKLY RALLY AS WARSH'S JACKSON HOLE DEBUT COMES
INTO FOCUS (2 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Bitcoin held above $77,000 after a 21% weekly gain, with XRP posting
a 46% advance over the same period, as crypto markets diverged from
Asian equities where Samsung and Alibaba led regional index declines.
The rally was catalyzed by US Treasury liquidity interventions and an
SEC crypto regulatory proposal introduced the prior week. Traders are
watching Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh's debut at the Jackson Hole
economic symposium for rate signals that could shift risk-asset
positioning.

 CIRCLE'S GROWTH CYCLE CAN CONTINUE WITHOUT THE CLARITY ACT (3 MINUTE
READ) [5]

 Bernstein reiterated Outperform and a $140 price target (59% upside
from Friday's $87.98 close) after USDC supply added $1.7 billion in a
week following nearly six months of sideways decline. Analysts argue
Circle's growth is independent of Clarity Act passage, driven instead
by a macro regime shift favoring stablecoins, tokenization, and
stablecoin payment adoption. Circle holds ~80% of DEX trading/finance
volume, and its Agent Stack (launched May) now hosts 900+ paid
services with 99% of x402 agent-payment volume settling in USDC.

INNOVATION & LAUNCHES

 GEMINI TO PROVIDE CRYPTO PREDICTION MARKETS VENUE FOR APEX BROKERAGE
(2 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Gemini and Apex Fintech Solutions signed a letter of intent making
Gemini Titan the exclusive regulated venue for crypto event contracts
distributed through Apex's brokerage clients, with potential expansion
into sports, economics, and financial-market contracts. Gemini's Q2
2026 results show 225 million+ event contracts traded on Titan across
27,000+ cumulative traders, still dwarfed by Kalshi and Polymarket's
tens of billions in monthly volume.

 CIRCLE TARGETS SEPT. 16 FOR ARC PUBLIC MAINNET LAUNCH (4 MINUTE READ)
[7]

 Circle has set September 16 as the public mainnet launch for Arc, a
permissioned proof-of-authority blockchain where block production
stays restricted to authorized validators even as the network opens to
public users and developers. The founding validator cohort consists of
Circle and 11 institutions including BlackRock, DTCC, Mastercard,
Standard Chartered, and Visa, with documentation indicating around 20
validators total at launch. Arc's public testnet recorded 671.5
million transactions across close to 3 million wallets, and over 100
partners have activated on private mainnet, with Uniswap committing to
day-one deployment. The chain settles transaction fees in USDC and
maintains EVM compatibility, anchoring it within Circle's stablecoin
infrastructure and the broader Ethereum application ecosystem.

 A SECOND AND FINAL EULOGY FOR BITCOIN MAXIMALISM (8 MINUTE READ) [8]

 There is a structural parallel between Bitcoin maximalism and
19th-century Millerism: both movements sustain failed predictions
through unfalsifiable contingencies ("not yet," "too early") rather
than updating their core beliefs after disproof. Bitcoin trades at
all-time highs with broad institutional custody and government
recognition, but circulates as a regulated store-of-value asset rather
than the sovereign payment system maximalists predicted, with
Lightning Network failing to achieve mass adoption. The ideology's
core contradiction is that Bitcoin's success vindicates a
store-of-value thesis maximalists explicitly rejected, while the
institutional custody and government recognition they condemned are
the developments that drove price appreciation. The ideological death
is now irreversible as key proponents age out or abandon the
anti-custodian, hyperbitcoinization framework that defined the
movement.

 WARTIME ETHEREUM - BLITZKRIEG (5 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Bitcoin's potential close above $74,200 on August 23 would mark the
largest weekly dollar gain in its history, with the 4-hour RSI hitting
an all-time high. Paul Tudor Jones raised his IBIT position 18.9% to
688,529 shares ($22.9M) as of June 30, Citigroup announced plans to
launch Bitcoin custody services before year-end, and VanEck's
leadership directed staff to accumulate rather than wait for lower
prices. Jeff Park's July 8 call for an AI-to-crypto rotation has since
drawn named institutional capital into the trade, with Stanley
Druckenmiller rotating out of memory stocks into former Bitcoin
miners. Ethereum outperforming Bitcoin in this environment points to
the early stages of a broader altcoin rotation.

 BULL CASE FOR AERODROME (AERO) RIDING TOKENIZED-STOCK GROWTH (5
MINUTE READ) [10]

 Coinbase's expected launch of tokenized stocks on Base this week will
drive volume to Aerodrome, Base's dominant DEX (52% share vs.
Uniswap's 26%). Tokenized-equity spot DEX volume grew from $115M in
July 2025 to $6B in July 2026 (50x YoY, now 11% of spot DEX volume)
but remains under 0.04% of total US equity trading volume. Tokenized
equities could reach $8.3 trillion by 2030 (3.5% of global equities),
and in a bull case, Aero captures up to 40% of EVM DEX volume.

 YOU WERE LIED TO ABOUT STABLECOINS (5 MINUTE READ) [11]

 The bull case for stablecoins in cross-border payments overlooks that
fintechs already solved the cross-currency problem. Wise and peers
hold pre-funded currency pools in local markets, netting offsetting
flows without moving money across borders, cutting costs from the ~15%
of SWIFT-based correspondent banking to under 1% with near-instant
settlement. Stablecoins' real edge is narrower: dollar-to-dollar
transfers, unbanked recipients without fintech access, 24/7
settlement, and crypto-native workflows. The "stablecoins will
revolutionize remittances" narrative conflates the legacy
correspondent banking problem with the current fintech-disrupted
state.
