 TREZOR HIT BY ANOTHER SHIPPING PARTNER DATA LEAK (2 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Shipping partner Skladon was breached on November 7, exposing names,
phone numbers, addresses, emails, and order details for orders placed
between August 9 and November 7, but no wallet backups, PINs, or
passphrases. Some customers have reported scam calls impersonating
Trezor support. The breach follows a leak in August at ShipMonk that
affected about 13,700 customers.

 WELLS FARGO'S BITCOIN ETF HOLDINGS DOUBLE (2 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Wells Fargo's 13F filing disclosed 49.58 million IBIT shares worth
$2.35 billion as of September 30, a 103% share count increase from
Q2's 24.4 million shares, representing indirect exposure to roughly
28,070 BTC and placing Wells Fargo ahead of Millennium Management,
Jane Street, and Morgan Stanley as the largest reported IBIT holder
among investment managers. The filing aggregates securities across the
bank and its associated investment managers, including client-managed
positions, so the figures reflect reported exposure rather than the
bank's proprietary balance sheet. Bitcoin's approximately 42% price
gain between June 30 and September 30 contributed to the near-tripling
of dollar value alongside the share count increase. The snapshot
predates BTC's pullback from $74,000 to $63,000, during which IBIT
recorded more than $1 billion in outflows, making current positioning
uncertain from public data alone.

 ETHEREUM'S EIP-7999 MULTIDIMENSIONAL FEE ANALYSIS (5 MINUTE READ) [6]


 An Ethereum Foundation intern study benchmarks EIP-7999's
multidimensional fee market against three one-dimensional
alternatives, including EIP-8368 and EIP-8372 variants. At a 1/2
target-to-limit ratio, EIP-7999 delivers 4%-34% more execution gas per
block than the best one-dimensional benchmark while holding state
growth to 120 GiB/year across every tested configuration, versus
56.7-96.0 GiB/year for one-dimensional variants. Against an
EIP-4844-style one-dimensional variant at the 4/5 ratio, EIP-7999's
maximum-throughput configuration adds 19.5M-56.2M gas per block while
keeping near-limit block rates lower than the 38.8%-40.8% produced by
that one-dimensional approach. The paper concludes that the results
support transitioning Ethereum from separate resource metering to
separate resource pricing as the network scales, directly backing
EIP-7999's design.

 EXPLORING POST-QUANTUM SIGNATURE AGGREGATION FOR EIP-8288 (5 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 Researchers propose pairing EIP-8141 (programmable account
validation) with EIP-8288 (recursive STARK aggregation) to let
Ethereum wallets choose their own post-quantum signing scheme while
the network verifies a single common proof. Benchmarks across five PQ
candidates on Apple M4 Pro hardware show wide efficiency gaps: FORS
fits 100 signatures into a 298 KiB proof, Falcon-512 fits 32,
ML-DSA-44 fits 6, and standard Raccoon-128 fits only 1. Raccoon-G
exceeds the 268M-word witness cap per signature, requiring
Merkle-tree-style recursive aggregation across five leanSTARK
sub-proofs per signature, with 100-signature trees producing a 333 KiB
root proof plus 2.84 KiB auxiliary data.

 THE REAL CASE FOR TOKENIZED EQUITIES (3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Tokenized equities enable global access for users excluded from
traditional brokerage systems and onchain yield generation, which
matters more than 24/7 trading as the primary value proposition.
Tokenizing US equities strengthens American market dominance by
delivering superior technology to international investors who already
show strong demand for US-denominated assets. Major AI labs continue
routing payments through credit card networks and traditional
infrastructure rather than onchain systems, an observation that
positions tokenized equities as a credible candidate to become the
default investing layer inside AI-native financial applications. If
tokenized equities fill that role, crypto will stay at the center of
global finance rather than cede the payment layer to legacy rails.

 BITWISE SEES SOLANA REACHING $1,100 BY 2031 IN BULL CASE (4 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 Bitwise projects SOL reaching $1,100 by 2031 in its bull case (base:
$385, bear: $80) in a report from its onchain research team, with SOL
priced near $71 at publication. The bull case hinges on 5% of global
equities tokenized by 2031, with Solana capturing 65% of resulting
trading volume, implying ~$30.7 trillion in annual tokenized stock
transactions and 3.6 billion daily transactions at 23x present
throughput. Securitize's October 2026 tokenized US stock launch and
DTCC's planned H2 2027 tokenized securities rollout on Solana serve as
near-term catalysts anchoring these projections. Key risks include
tokenization pace, regulatory shifts, and infrastructure gaps, as the
base case alone demands 2.5x Solana's maximum block compute while
Ethereum and its L2s lead tokenized asset value.

 ONLY 9% OF REVOLUT'S STABLECOIN VOLUME CAME FROM CUSTOMERS (1 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Revolut's stablecoin volume hit $3.2B in July, but after breaking
down USDT and USDC transfers across its hot wallets on eight networks,
only 9% was customer activity. Customers normally account for about
25-30% of volume, and in July two-thirds was Revolut shuffling funds
between its own wallets ahead of the USDT delisting in the EEA. Raw
volume fell fourfold while customer flows only halved, and the decline
began in May, before Revolut's delisting letter.

 IS CRYPTO VC DEAD? (8 MINUTE READ) [11]

 A $15B LP dinner surfaced a structural math problem for large crypto
funds: a $2B vehicle targeting 3x needs $6B in returns, achievable in
prior cycles via L1s and infrastructure, but value has shifted to
app-layer projects with distribution rather than protocols, leaving no
clean path to venture-scale outcomes on crypto alone. Consensus put
crypto-only funds' runway at one or two more cycles before the asset
class merges into the broader frontier tech market, with large VCs
expanding mandates into robotics, physical AI, and compute. Near-term
opportunities cited include AI-native companies adopting on-chain
infrastructure via agentic payments and trading, DeFi and agentic
brokerages that have not yet reached millions of users, and tokenized
equities in emerging markets.
