 CFTC BACKS BLOCKCHAIN-BASED DERIVATIVES INFRASTRUCTURE (2 MINUTE
READ) [4]

 The CFTC approved Backpack and tZERO to run regulated derivatives
exchanges and clearinghouses that use blockchain to execute and settle
trades. Chair Michael Selig called it a milestone showing a regulated
pathway for blockchain-built market infrastructure. tZERO got its
exchange approval last week, after an earlier clearing organization
approval, and Backpack US President Mark Wetjen called its approvals a
“really big deal.”

 STANDARD CHARTERED SAYS JAPAN'S BOND TOKENIZATION COULD HELP DRIVE
RWAS TO $2 TRILLION (3 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Tokenizing Japanese government bonds could help the tokenized
real-world asset market grow from about $45B to the bank's $2T
forecast by the end of 2028, which so far relied mainly on tokenized
money market funds and equities at $750B each. Most tokenized
government debt is short-term, with about $13B in US T-bills and under
$1B elsewhere.

 MEGA'S $30M BET ON INFERENCE MARKETS (5 MINUTE READ) [6]

 MegaETH rebranded as MEGA and committed $30M from treasury to an
inference market launching in December, after co-founder Shuyao Kong
acknowledged the chain found no PMF despite TVL dropping from a $245M
May peak to under $10M. The product targets a "supply paradox" where
buyers overpay for familiar inference providers running identical open
models, addressed via a "Purified Intelligence" verification layer,
though the approach is undefined: Antseed already does fingerprinting
verification, TEE-sealed hardware certifies model runs, and Anthropic
open-sourced cryptographic model proofs, yet MEGA's paper names all
three without committing to one. MEGA's clearest potential
differentiation lies in prompt privacy and forward capacity markets
where buyers reserve specific model/volume/performance windows and
trade those reservations, pricing future model output rather than GPU
hours as ICE's planned GPU futures do.

 KOREAN FINTECH TOSS TO LAUNCH IN-APP NON-CUSTODIAL CRYPTO WALLET (2
MINUTE READ) [7]

 Toss Coin Wallet may let any Toss user create a non-custodial wallet
from the app's asset menu with no extra eligibility requirements, to
send, receive, and check balances of ETH, USDT, and USDC, with
contact-based transfers. It is Toss' first consumer crypto product,
and it is considering applying for a VASP license to support
stablecoin payments on its terminals. It has partnered with Solana
Foundation and Circle, and has over 30M cumulative users, about 60% of
South Korea's population.

 WINTERMUTE'S $10M/YEAR HYPERLIQUID LATENCY EDGE (5 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Wintermute spends about $10M per year ($26,000/day) to hold 76% of
Hyperliquid's gossip priority auction slots, securing a 50-60ms
latency edge in a protocol that re-auctions queue priority every three
minutes. That head start lets the firm cancel orders before large
incoming trades hit, adjust quotes toward incoming flow, and
cross-venue hedge on Binance before Hyperliquid prices reprice, a set
of strategies that underpins $227M in lifetime platform profits and
$18.8M over the past 30 days. Full access requires a direct Foundation
node connection gated at ~0.5% of network maker volume plus 10,000
HYPE staked (~$18.9M), locking smaller market makers out and forcing
them to widen spreads or exit altcoin books, with the cost ultimately
absorbed by retail traders.

 EVERYONE IS APPROACHING AGENTIC COMMERCE WRONG (10 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Agentic commerce benchmarks expose a structural breakdown: Walmart's
ChatGPT-driven conversion ran at one-third of walmart.com's rate, only
7% of tested agent checkouts completed, and Visa's own benchmark found
agents succeeded at assigned tasks just 57% of the time. Former Meta
payments engineer Vignesh Sreedhar traced the root cause to missing
incentive alignment, the same gap that plagued credit cards before Dee
Hock designed Visa's interchange model in 1966, where 86% of fees
return to cardholders as rewards and every network participant has a
self-interested reason to grow legitimate volume. Stored-value models
(Stripe Link Balance and Rain Agent Vaults) are the closest current
approximation because they give agents an economic stake, but they cut
issuers out of interchange revenue. Without treating agent companies
as a fifth economic party in payment networks, on par with issuers and
acquirers, the volume growth agentic commerce promises will not
materialize.

 WHY CRYPTO FEELS MISERABLE (6 MINUTE READ) [11]

 ETH and SOL price declines erased roughly $350B in industry wealth
since October 2024, while revenue grew only ~$8B, explaining why the
sector feels depressed despite bullish macro signals like
Hyperliquid's ICE partnership for onchain perpetuals and Treasury
Secretary Bessent projecting stablecoins to $2T. With $26.6B in annual
cash inflows, closing that $350B gap through revenue alone would
require 35x growth, equal to 350 Hyperliquid-scale businesses, because
crypto industry health tracks asset prices far more than operating
revenue. Two recovery paths exist: revenue-generating tokens like HYPE
accumulating at scale, or native asset appreciation requiring a
greater than 60% BTC move when adjusted for ETF holders who do not
recycle capital back into the ecosystem, with 2027/2028 as the
projected recovery window.

 USER-GENERATED MARKETS, TAKE TWO (8 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Most prediction market volume concentrates in a fraction of markets,
with 70% of Polymarket's closed markets clearing under $10K and only
35% of Kalshi's 7.6M combo tickers seeing any activity, yet no major
venue lets users create markets. Augur (2018) demonstrated the demand
but failed on four axes: bonds too cheap to deter abuse, vague
resolution rules exploited for invalid-market plays, wash trading cost
only gas, and dispute resolution required a 60-day fork process. XO
Market, on mainnet since November 2025, addresses each failure with a
mandatory $10 seed order, AI rule pre-screening, liquidity rewards
gated on real trading interest, and 20% fee rebates to creators of
active markets, reporting $783M in cumulative notional volume across
3,000+ user-created markets after 12 months.
