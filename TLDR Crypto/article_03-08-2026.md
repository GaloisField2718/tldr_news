MARKETS & BUSINESS

 BITCOIN LOSSES LINKED TO COLDCARD VULNERABILITY GROW TO $70 MILLION
(2 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Galaxy Research said nearly 1,200 addresses were drained of over
1,082 BTC (~$70.2 million) tied to a seed-generation flaw affecting
Coldcard Mk3 devices and later expanded to certain Mk4, Mk5, and
Coldcard Q firmware. Maker Coinkite released emergency firmware
updates and CEO Rodolfo Novak apologized, suggesting the bug may have
been found using AI-assisted code review. Coinkite is urging affected
users to update firmware, generate new seeds, and test with a small
transaction before moving all funds.

 RUSSIAN DECREE BANS CRYPTO MINING ACROSS MOSCOW REGION THROUGH 2032
(2 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Russia issued a decree banning crypto mining and mining pool
participation across Moscow, Moscow Oblast, and parts of Kursk Oblast
from August 15 through the end of 2032, citing estimates that mining
consumes roughly 1 GW of the region's grid capacity and could worsen
future electricity shortages. The ban extends a regional approach
Russia began in 2024 while the country otherwise pursues formal mining
regulation nationwide, including a 2025 equipment registry and pending
legislation allowing regulated retail crypto trading.

 TASKMARKET: A USDC-PAID COMPETITIVE MARKETPLACE FOR AI AGENTS (4
MINUTE READ) [6]

 Taskmarket is a competitive marketplace where AI agents submit work
on open tasks and earn USDC prizes judged on output quality, with
current competitions including a Civilization I clone and a GTA1
clone. Daydreams published a 10-point agent performance guide
alongside the launch, covering persistent worker roles with memory,
strict brief-to-checklist conversion, real media generation routes
over prose, three directed revision passes, and banking successful
methods as reusable skills. Market rank is the sole quality barometer,
and since entry is free with no cost for losing, participation risk is
zero.

 UMIA: TOKEN LAUNCH PROTOCOL WITH CONTINUOUS CLEARING AUCTIONS (3
MINUTE READ) [7]

 Umia has launched a testnet, introducing a token launch and
governance protocol built around two mechanisms: Continuous Clearing
Auctions (CCAs) and Decision Markets. CCAs run on Uniswap and route
tokens through clearing buckets where bidders submit market or limit
orders, settling all fills at a uniform clearing price, with full
refunds for unfilled bids or auctions that miss minimum raises, making
sniping and MEV extraction structurally difficult. Decision Markets
gate governance actions such as token mints and treasury spends behind
a prediction market, requiring traders to bet on whether a proposal
increases token value before it can execute. Mainnet is imminent, with
the first live auction expected shortly after launch.

 STRUCTURAL OEV ELIMINATION THROUGH STATE SYNCHRONIZATION (6 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 A new Ethereum Research paper proposes eliminating oracle extractable
value (OEV) at its structural root through atomic state
synchronization, contrasting with current order-flow auctions that
redistribute OEV without closing the extraction window. The paper
identifies three timing surfaces: backrunning oracle updates,
anticipating updates via statistical models (attributed to 39% of
liquidations in one study), and exploiting cross-domain arrival gaps
of 19-27 seconds across chains. The proposed bind-verify-commit cycle,
verified in Isabelle/HOL, eliminates the update-visibility window by
ensuring no reachable state exists where an oracle update is visible
but its bound consequence remains uncommitted. Key limitations
persist: the model lacks empirical validation, behavior under partial
synchrony is unestablished, and OFA-attached price feeds already show
liquidation bonuses rising 1.5-1 percentage point to offset
auction-added latency.

 STABLECOINS, AGENTIC MARKETS, AND A WIN FOR CRYPTO: JULY AT COINBASE
(5 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Mubadala Capital, a $600B AUM sovereign-backed fund, tokenized a
major private-market strategy across Base, Solana, and Sui, with

 ACTIVE CRYPTO VC FIRMS FALL TO 150, LOWEST SINCE 2020 (3 MINUTE READ)
[10]

 Active crypto VC participation dropped to 150 firms in July, the
lowest monthly count since November 2020 and an 87% decline from the
May 2022 peak of 1,177 investors. The contraction reflects the exit of
smaller funds, family offices, and angel syndicates that proliferated
during the 2021-2022 bull cycle. Dragonfly Capital managing partner
Haseeb Qureshi described the trend as a "mass extinction" of
blockchain venture capital, warning that dedicated crypto VCs could
lose relevance by 2030 as dominant platforms absorb users and
liquidity. Larger established firms continue deploying capital into
infrastructure and real-world assets, with Dragonfly closing a $650
million fund in February, marking a consolidation of the sector around
institutional players.

 HOW ONE SHARE IN SEOUL BROKE HYPERLIQUID'S LARGEST EQUITY MARKET (6
MINUTE READ) [11]

 A single SK Hynix share printed 29.96% below prior close on South
Korea's Nextrade pre-market at 8:00 am Seoul time on July 28, feeding
into TradeXYZ's SKHYNIX USDC-margined perpetual on Hyperliquid and
triggering $57-80M in liquidations across ~960 long accounts. TradeXYZ
controls 90%+ of HIP-3 open interest and ~98% of builder-market volume
on Hyperliquid, where HIP-3 has grown from ~2% to ~50% of total perp
volume since January, reaching $98B in 30-day volume and $3.6B
network-wide open interest. Galaxy Research's Will Owens concludes the
incident was a TradFi data quality failure rather than a crypto oracle
problem, since every data provider read the same corrupted NXT book,
and TradeXYZ's own order book held steadier than the external feed,
suggesting onchain equity markets are maturing into price references
rather than price consumers. TradeXYZ covered the liquidation losses
as a one-time discretionary decision while SK Hynix fell 14.65% in the
regular Seoul session and triggered circuit breakers on two
consecutive days.
