 SK HYNIX PERPETUALS HIT BY FLASH CRASH TO $900 ON HYPERLIQUID (3
MINUTE READ) [4]

 SK Hynix perpetual futures on Hyperliquid flash-crashed 20% to $900
on July 28 between 23:00 and 23:01 UTC, recovering above $1,000 within
one minute, with analysts attributing the move to thin overnight
liquidity that left the venue unable to absorb large orders at stable
prices. The crash preceded SK Hynix shares falling 15% when the Korean
Stock Exchange opened an hour later, alongside an 11% Kospi decline
and losses at Samsung Electronics and Hyundai Motor, underscoring
crypto perps acting as a price-discovery venue ahead of the underlying
equity market. The equity selloff connects to a broader AI hardware
correction: Nvidia fell 5% on reports of a $250 billion data-center
commitment to an OpenAI-affiliated venture, dragging SK Hynix, a
primary supplier of high-bandwidth memory chips for AI processors, to
a cumulative decline of close to 48% from its June 26 peak.

 CRYPTO STEADIES AS IRAN-US PAUSE SENDS OIL TUMBLING (3 MINUTE READ)
[5]

 An Iran-US ceasefire over the Strait of Hormuz drove Brent crude down
7% to ~$87/barrel, easing inflation concerns and boosting risk
appetite across asset classes. CME FedWatch pricing for a Fed rate
hike this week fell from 37.4% to 30.5% following the de-escalation,
with inflation running at 4.1% ahead of the decision. Bitcoin held
flat at $65,200 while Ether gained 0.51% to $1,963, approaching the
$2,000 level, and the CoinDesk 20 Index added 1.6% over 24 hours. DeFi
tokens led the recovery: AAVE and LDO each gained around 9%, ONDO rose
7%, and PUMP surged 12.24% toward an $800 million market cap.

 BASE LAUNCHES BASE VERIFY ONCHAIN FOR SYBIL-RESISTANT AIRDROPS (2
MINUTE READ) [6]

 Base Verify Onchain lets smart contracts enforce "one real person,
one claim" by generating a deterministic identity hash tied to a
verified Coinbase, Instagram, X, or TikTok account without exposing
personal data to the contract, replacing the need for developers to
run their own off-chain dedupe database. The underlying Base Verify
system has been used over 300,000 times for partners including Base
App and Bracket. Use cases include airdrops resistant to
wallet-farming, per-person token sale caps, and one-person-one-vote
governance.

 VISA STABLECOIN PLATFORM EXPLAINER: WHY VISA LAUNCHED WITH OPEN USD
(6 MINUTE READ) [8]

 The new Visa Stablecoin Platform (VSP) lets banks and fintechs mint,
burn, and manage stablecoins through a single Visa-run environment,
launching with Open USD because banks trust bank-governed stablecoins
over Circle or Tether. EY Parthenon found 63% of corporates want
stablecoin access via their own bank. VSP supports Ethereum, Solana,
and Tempo with maker/checker controls, passkey signing, and
wallet-as-a-service, mirroring how Visa DPS lets thousands of issuers
process debit without building their own infrastructure. Visa's
approach of not picking winners will scale quickly since every
participating bank becomes invested in the network's growth.

 THE RISE AND QUIET DEATH OF THE NEOBANK (8 MINUTE READ) [9]

 A live audit of 368 verified-active neobanks serving a combined 1.46
billion users finds Asia holding 817 million of those customers, with
WeBank alone at 400 million-plus and Nubank's 131 million Latin
American users exceeding the entire US neobank base, while Revolut's
50 million European accounts are a footnote by comparison. Only 127 of
the 368 hold a full banking license, leaving the majority dependent on
sponsor banks and BaaS intermediaries whose failures routed customers
into bankruptcy proceedings rather than deposit insurance, because the
ledger tracking which dollars belonged to whom was the layer that
broke. Among neobanks founded in the 2020s that remain active, 30% are
web3-native self-custodial apps versus 4% of the 2010s cohort, and an
audit of AI claims found only 67 of 368 companies (18%) with verified
production deployments, led not by Western incumbents but by thin-file
lenders in Nigeria, the Philippines, and Mexico where model-based
underwriting is the prerequisite for the business to exist at all.
Machine-to-machine banking rails for AI agents is a seven-company
niche today that structurally resembles where web3-native finance sat
in 2021.

 WHEN DO STABLECOINS BECOME A REAL MONETARY SYSTEM? (2 MINUTE READ)
[10]

 Stablecoins only become a true monetary system once they're used on
the liability side of the economy. This means paying suppliers, wages,
and taxes, and that high matching between stablecoin receivables and
payables lets economic activity run without constantly converting back
to bank deposits. Stablecoins don't eliminate banking but relocate it,
becoming a private monetary base on top of which a new form of banking
will be rebuilt to supply credit and elasticity.

READ) [11]

 Bitmine Immersion Technologies now holds 5.79 million ETH

decline, coinciding with Bitmine repurchasing 11.6 million common
shares since July 1 under a $4 billion buyback program.
