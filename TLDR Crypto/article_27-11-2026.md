 HOUSE COMMITTEE ADVANCES STRATEGIC BITCOIN RESERVE BILL (3 MINUTE
READ) [5]

 H.R. 8957, the American Reserve Modernization Act, would codify a
Strategic Bitcoin Reserve and a separate Digital Asset Stockpile, with
federally held bitcoin locked for at least 20 years and
proof-of-reserve reporting. The Financial Services Committee approved
it 28-21 on a party-line vote in September and sent it to the full
House on November 24. The 119th Congress is expected to adjourn within
weeks, so passage is unlikely. Polymarket gives a 4% chance of a
national bitcoin reserve by year end, down from 60% a year ago.

 TASKMARKET AND VISA LET TAP-REGISTERED AGENTS EARN ON PAID TASKS (6
MINUTE READ) [6]

 Agents registered with Visa's Trusted Agent Protocol can now compete
for paid tasks on Taskmarket, built with Visa, Tempo, and Daydreams
Labs. Registration requires linking one Visa card once, which is not
charged, and gives the agent a signing key listed in the Visa Agentic
Directory. Taskmarket checks the signature on entry and again before
payout, and rewards are escrowed and paid in OUSD on Tempo. An agent's
first 5 task entries are free, then each costs 0.001 OUSD. Starting
Thanksgiving, thousands of dollars of tasks open only to
TAP-registered agents will be posted, with examples paying $3 to $7.
Visa CLI is for US users, and the setup is a six-step command-line
process.

 ECLIPSE CHARTS: NEXT-GEN QUANTITATIVE CHARTING PLATFORM (2 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 Eclipse launched as a quantitative charting platform for crypto
traders, pairing proprietary indicators (PerpKit, Trend Oracle, and
LLNR) with statistical time-series models including ARIMA, Bayesian
Trend, and SARIMA. The platform adds neural network candles (nOHLC and
nHA), crypto-native orderflow tools (funding rates, open interest,
CVD, liquidations, and orderbook heatmaps), and on-chain token
charting to the core indicator stack. Traders write and backtest
custom scripts via CharmScript, while Premium subscribers at
$129/month gain webhook alerts and API access for automated workflows.
Pro runs $59/month with a 7-day trial. A free tier exposes the Bitcoin
daily chart with all Pro indicators active without an account.

 BEYOND TABLE STAKES: ARCHITECTURES FOR STABLECOIN ISSUANCE (5 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Stablecoin issuance has split into four architectures: chain-native
primitives (Tempo TIP-20, Base B20, and Solana Token-2022) that move
compliance into the protocol, OUSD's mixed setup, white-label
platforms like M0, and Paxos's reward-bearing Token V3. OUSD has
reached about $983M in supply across four chains since its
late-September launch, with 64% on Tempo, using native primitives on
Tempo, Base, and Solana, while Bridge built a custom two-layer ERC-20
stack on Ethereum. M0's base token M backs branded stablecoins such as
MetaMask's mUSD and has processed over $1B in mints across Solana and
15+ EVM chains, a design PYUSDx copies with PYUSD as the base. Paxos
upgraded USDG in place to V3 in 2026, splitting it into a core token
plus five facets and paying rewards to distribution partners through
onchain payout groups instead of to holders.

 COLDCARD POSTMORTEM: SEEDS CAME FROM A SOFTWARE RNG FOR FIVE YEARS (4
MINUTE READ) [9]

 Coldcard firmware generated wallet seeds from March 2021 through July
30, 2026, with MicroPython's Yasmarang software PRNG instead of the
STM32 hardware RNG, leaving about 40 bits (Mk2/Mk3) or 72 bits (Mk4,
Mk5, Q) of effective entropy, and attackers began regenerating
affected private keys offline on July 29. Galaxy Research counted
1,778.84 BTC stolen from more than 8,600 addresses, and Coinkite has
matched 300 loss-related support tickets to about 0.2% of its
estimated user base. At least 15 independent reviews between 2021 and
2026 missed the defect, including an AI-assisted review with the
OpenAI Cybersecurity Harness that produced 85 candidate findings.
Coinkite shipped fixed firmware on July 31 but says updates cannot
repair seeds created on affected firmware, and it is still evaluating
options to help affected customers.

 WHY TOKENS COULD BE ENTERING A DECADE-LONG BULL MARKET (3 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Five years of token bear market have flushed out inflated valuations,
weak tokens, and capital misallocation. The reversal of those
conditions sets up a decade-long secular bull market. This case rests
on cheap valuations and disciplined capital, Internet Finance products
such as stablecoins and RWAs that work but are hidden by negative
industry P&L, and network effects that turn onchain markets into a
global bazaar for cheaper capital and better assets. With fewer than
15 funds and under 1,000 serious people left in the industry, there's
a "dry forest" where the spark could be GENIUS in January, RWAs
reaching critical mass, easier macro liquidity, or a niche industry
raising capital onchain.

 HOW I GOT STARTED WITH CRYPTO HFT (8 MINUTE READ) [11]

 @0xyieldmaxi built a two-person prop trading firm in Switzerland
after post-FTX MEV alpha decayed, scaling from fixed-spread quoting on
Binance through cross-exchange arbitrage to CEX-DEX arb, which they
found highly profitable with thin competition at peak. The actual
trading logic ran under 100 lines of code. The bulk of the work was
exchange connectivity, WebSocket latency optimization via Avelacom,
and fee negotiation across Binance, Bybit, and OKX. A partner split in
late 2024 cut PnL, and they missed Hyperliquid while focused on
optimizing existing operations, with edge eroding enough to step away
by mid-2025. They're returning in 2026, targeting prediction markets
and using LLMs to compress the cycle from idea to testable strategy.
