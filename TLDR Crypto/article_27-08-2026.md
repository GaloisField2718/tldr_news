 SOUTH KOREA'S SHINHAN TO USE VISA'S STABLECOIN PLATFORM FOR "FUTURE
FINANCE” (2 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Shinhan Financial Group signed a strategic agreement with Visa to
build stablecoin-based financial infrastructure, using Visa's platform
to verify issuance, remittance, and redemption while co-designing a
Korea-specific model. The partnership will pilot stablecoins in card
payment settlement, AI-based payment models, and B2B/B2C payments,
combining Shinhan Bank, Shinhan Card, and Jeju Bank with Visa's
network. It follows Shinhan Card's April partnership with Solana
Foundation and an August MOU with Solana, Etherfuse, and Orca to test
a won-denominated tokenized fund, as South Korea works on its Digital
Asset Basic Act.

 ETHLABS' "FAST ETHEREUM" ROADMAP (5 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Ethereum's speed directly drives its economic value by reducing
friction (idle capital, wait times, and settlement costs) for
payments, swaps, and credit. There are four active workstreams: "fast
blocks" (removing the hardcoded 12-second block time, starting with a
proposed cut to 10 seconds in the upcoming "Hegotá" fork); "fast
finality" (an EF/Consensys proposal to cut finality from ~15 minutes
to a couple minutes in the following fork); the "Fast Confirmation
Rule" (near-production today, cutting confirmation to tens of seconds
with formally provable security, already adopted by one major bridge);
and "fast interoperability" via real-time zk proving to speed up
L2-to-L1 asset flows. Hegotá is possibly the last practical window to
kick off block-time reductions for years.

 BITWISE LAUNCHES AUTOMATED TOKEN PORTFOLIOS FOR TOKENIZED STOCKS (2
MINUTE READ) [7]

 Bitwise has launched Automated Token Portfolios (ATPs),
institution-grade model portfolios built on Coinbase's tokenized
equities (NVDAc, METAc, AAPLc, and GOOGLc) deployed on Base two days
prior. The structure is non-custodial throughout: Bitwise defines the
target allocation, users hold tokenized stocks in their own wallets,
and Glider's automated rebalancing protocol maintains alignment
without taking custody. The product brings TradFi model-portfolio
infrastructure to on-chain self-custody, giving non-US investors
access to institution-grade equity exposure without surrendering asset
control. Access is restricted to non-US persons under Regulation S,
confining the initial addressable market to eligible jurisdictions
outside the US.

GUIDES & TUTORIALS

 TOKENIZED DEPOSITS VS. STABLECOINS (3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 The "coffee vs. matcha" framing (stablecoins vs. tokenized deposits)
misses that the two financial tools can be used together. A tokenized
deposit is a direct bank liability suited for treasury and payments
within a controlled institutional perimeter, while a stablecoin is a
separate liability designed to travel across open networks. The more
likely architecture has tokenized demand deposits forming the
operational liquidity layer underneath a stablecoin's reserves. The
stablecoin issuer holds a deposit claim on the bank, and
minting/burning could synchronize atomically and 24/7 with reserve
receipt and redemption, rather than through today's manual
orchestration.

 NATIVE ACCOUNT ABSTRACTION DOES NOT NEED A GENERAL-PURPOSE
TRANSACTION LAYER (7 MINUTE READ) [9]

 EIP-8130 is the correct native account abstraction path for EVM
rollups, encoding the three primitives users need (transaction
batching, gas sponsorship, and key rotation) without the complexity of
EIP-8141's seven new opcodes, frame modes, and layered execution
model. EIP-8130's deterministic contract addresses enable cross-chain
account changes without bridges, and wallets receive a canonical
interface for secp256k1, P-256, and WebAuthn/passkey authenticators.
With Ethereum's Glamsterdam upgrade slipped to Q4 2026 and EIP-8141
not credibly activatable before late Q2 2027, L2s can adopt EIP-8130
now. Base is targeting EIP-8130 in its Cobalt upgrade for a 2x
per-transaction cost reduction, with Optimism partnering to extend
native AA across the OP Stack.

 ARE MERCHANTS LOSING MONEY TO X402 FACILITATOR FEES? (2 MINUTE READ)
[10]

 x402 hit 1-1.5 million transactions per day for the first time, with
volume up ~2x versus June/July, but average transaction size has
fallen to $0.023-$0.031. Since popular third-party facilitators
(Coinbase CDP on Base, PayAI on Solana) charge a flat $0.001 per
settlement, that fee now eats 32-43% of average transaction value.
Batching, live since May, could cut that cost by 90-99% by settling
thousands of transactions in one, but adoption remains near zero.
There remain questions around whether tooling, counterparty risk, or
accounting complexity is the blocker.

 WHY AIRWALLEX CUSTOMERS DIDN'T NEED STABLECOINS, AND WHY WE'RE
BUILDING FOR THEM NOW (5 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Airwallex, which moves cross-border payments across 120+ countries
via local currency pools and same-day local network settlement, is
integrating stablecoin support after years of deprioritizing it,
citing AI agent microtransactions and US and European regulatory
clarity as the primary catalysts. The company's existing rails cover
most enterprise payment needs, but card processing fees make
sub-dollar transactions uneconomical. Stablecoin wallets with fixed
balances solve both the unit-economics problem and spend-control
requirements for agent-driven API calls, compute purchases, and data
lookups at scale. To build out the required settlement layer,
Airwallex invested to incubate Metal, a settlement infrastructure
product built for regulated financial institutions to process
transactions at agent speeds, while also adding direct stablecoin
support in markets where regulators permit it. The firm positions
agent payments as the largest source of near-term stablecoin payment
demand, with slower clearing corridors as a secondary use case for
recipients able to hold stablecoins under local regulation.
