MARKETS & BUSINESS

 MOODY'S ASSIGNS INVESTMENT-GRADE RATINGS TO RIPPLE'S RLUSD STABLECOIN
(3 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Moody's gave RLUSD an A2 long-term and P-1 short-term deposit rating.
It cited reserve credit quality and market value, coverage, liquidity,
redemption arrangements, segregation, and third-party custody. The
reserve is mostly short-term US government assets and cash held at BNY
(Aa1), and the custodian targets 103% coverage. Ripple says it is the
first stablecoin to get an investment-grade rating from Moody's. RLUSD
has a $2.5B market cap and is used mainly inside Ripple's payments and
as collateral in Ripple Prime. The ratings could fall if reserve
quality, coverage, or liquidity weakens. Ripple also just got final
OCC approval for Ripple National Trust Bank.

 BITHUMB HACKED THROUGH EMPLOYEE SYSTEM (2 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Bithumb, South Korea's second-largest exchange, said an outsider
breached the internal mobile system employees use for work and
messaging, and personal information was stolen. No crypto funds were
reported lost. It says the system is separate from its trading
platform, so customer assets can't be reached that way. It has not
said how many people's data were exposed or whether deposits or
withdrawals were affected. Regulators are also assessing Daishin
Securities, which said data for 38,490 customers leaked, and Mirae
Asset Global Investments, which blocked abnormal access. The
regulator's governor said cyberattacks had hit 16 financial firms as
of October 14. In February, Bithumb mistakenly credited customers with
620,000 BTC instead of 620,000 won.

 LINK BALANCE: HOW STRIPE BUILT THE INFRASTRUCTURE FOR AGENTIC
COMMERCE (4 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Stripe Link balance is a pre-funded wallet for AI agents that removes
per-transaction approval by letting consumers deposit funds and set
spending limits upfront, with Instinct as the first integrated
assistant. The stack routes fiat through Stripe Crypto Onramp into
dollar-denominated stablecoins on Tempo, uses Privy's non-custodial
wallets for key management and signing, then converts back to virtual
debit cards via Stripe Issuing, so merchants receive standard card
transactions with no crypto integration required. Agentic purchases
through Link grew 38x in one month, with Muse reporting over 25% of
weekly users shopping via agent. The architecture deploys stablecoins
as settlement infrastructure rather than a user-facing asset class,
keeping crypto rails invisible to both merchants and consumers while
solving the approval-friction bottleneck that has constrained
autonomous agent commerce.

 FYND AMM: THE FIRST AMM THAT OUTBIDS COMPETITION IN REAL-TIME (4
MINUTE READ) [7]

 Fynd AMM introduces a last-mover pricing mechanism where the pool
sets its price during routing after observing all competing quotes,
rather than submitting a fixed price before DEX aggregators query it.
Traditional AMMs and propAMMs bid blind, causing LPs to either overbid
on trades they win or lose trades they could have priced to win. A
backtest of 60 ETH/USDG trades on Robinhood shows a standard 30 bps
pool captures half the trades but collects only 37% of available fees,
while Fynd AMM charges the maximum fee that still wins each trade,
generating 2.7x more LP revenue. The protocol is launching on Ekubo's
concentrated liquidity infrastructure and is already integrated on
Robinhood and Base.

GUIDES & TUTORIALS

 THE INTERNET IS NO LONGER FOR HUMANS (5 MINUTE READ) [8]

 CVE publications hit 14,939 in September alone, four times the 2025
monthly average, with Critical and High severity vulnerabilities up
161% year-over-year and now comprising more than half of all new
records. The surge coincides with a structural shift: internet
security was architected around human attackers, so CAPTCHAs, rate
limits, and fraud detection all break when AI agents can solve
challenges in milliseconds and fire thousands of requests per second.
Proposed fixes are crypto-native: verifiable on-chain agent identity,
cryptographic authorization proofs, and machine-to-machine payment
rails like x402. Hacktron is positioned as a security scanner for this
agent-centric web, using the same AI techniques attackers deploy.

 THE LONG ROAD TO BITCOIN PAYMENTS (8 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Spiral, Block's Bitcoin-focused subsidiary, published a report
arguing Bitcoin payments infrastructure is production-ready but
adoption remains a demand problem, not a technology gap. Lightning
runs in production, self-custodial wallets have abstracted most
complexity, and over one million US merchants can accept Bitcoin
through Square. The strategic concern is that major payment providers
are now updating financial rails with stablecoin support, and if those
rails exclude Bitcoin natively, switching costs could lock the current
payments upgrade cycle into stablecoin infrastructure. Spiral is
issuing a call for moonshot ideas to accelerate Bitcoin payment
adoption before that window closes.

MISCELLANEOUS

 BUYBACKS DO WORK (6 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Buybacks mechanically raise price, and crypto needs them because
token holders have no legal rights. There is positive evidence for
buybacks. However, buybacks compound slowly and fail if the business
is a flash in the pan. Teams that ran points programs have a moral
duty to show alignment through buybacks. Investors should buy coins
where the protocol is the marginal buyer.

 WHY PUMP.FUN ISN'T THE REAL PROBLEM (5 MINUTE READ) [11]

 The “Pump.fun is the enemy” backlash repeats what happened to
OpenSea when NFTs faded. When a speculative asset class stops paying,
the platform tied to it becomes the villain. Memecoins are exhausted,
liquidity is fragmented, and average traders now compete against
professionals. Removing Pump.fun wouldn't fix that, because another
platform would take its place. People are seeking a platform-level fix
for a market-level problem. However, some of Pump.fun's decisions
deserve criticism regardless.
