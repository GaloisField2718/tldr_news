 A16Z CRYPTO REBRANDS AS A16Z FRONTIER, EXPANDING TO AI AND FRONTIER
(4 MINUTE READ) [4]

 a16z Crypto is rebranding as a16z Frontier, expanding its mandate
beyond crypto to cover AI-native applications, autonomous machines,
and biomedical systems while continuing to invest in crypto and
advocate for sensible regulation. The rationale is that broad
blockchain adoption has blurred the line between crypto-native and
conventional companies, making a vertical-specific fund label less
precise than what portfolio companies actually build. New thematic
pillars include AI enabling user experiences built on new primitives,
next-generation computing form factors, and machine-to-machine
networks where stablecoins and on-chain rails serve as the payment
layer. The rebrand coincides with separate reports that a founding
member is departing the firm.

 ARGENTINE JUDGE CLEARS MILEI AND 10 OTHERS IN LIBRA CASE (5 MINUTE
READ) [5]

 A federal judge dismissed the criminal case against President Javier
Milei, his sister Karina, and nine others, including Hayden Davis, in
a 60-page ruling. He found that promoting a token or giving it
visibility is not by itself a fraudulent scheme. The ruling followed
the prosecutor's own request for dismissal, and none of the 11 were
questioned as a suspect. Evidence the judge said was inconclusive
included a note about $5M in payments and deleted chats. LIBRA's
market cap topped $4.5B before falling about 95%, and eight
team-linked wallets cashed out $107M.

 PHANTOM ROLLS OUT KALSHI PREDICTION MARKETS TO US USERS (3 MINUTE
READ) [6]

 Phantom began rolling out Kalshi event contracts to US users on
October 23, with access expanding over the coming weeks. Users need to
complete KYC on Kalshi and fund trades with Phantom's CASH stablecoin,
with winnings returned to their Phantom Cash accounts. Funds move to
Kalshi and are converted to fiat. Phantom charges a $0.02 per contract
feed fee plus a small network fee. General Counsel Kevin Jacobs says
the CFTC's jurisdiction is clear as long as Phantom stays within its
no-action position, and he expects the Supreme Court to eventually
decide. Phantom will block some categories by state: sports in
Michigan, sports, elections, politics, and entertainment in Nevada,
and a longer list in Washington.

 A HASH-ONLY ROLLUP (5 MINUTE READ) [7]

 The founder of bb84 payments has deployed an OP Stack L2 on Ethereum
mainnet (chain ID 42069000, live since 2026-09-30) where L2
transactions carry no ECDSA signatures, replacing them with
Lamport-style keccak-256 hash chains in which spending a transaction
reveals the next preimage rather than a cryptographic signature. Each
account derives from a secret seed generating a default 1,000-step
hash chain, tracked by a NullifierTree predeploy, with two new
EIP-2718 transaction types handling commit (0x7C) and reveal (0x7F)
steps, and a "first commit wins" rule activated on October 21 to blunt
front-running. The chain targets ~2^128 quantum work factor inside the
L2 against Grover's algorithm, but ECDSA remains at the L1 boundary,
fault proofs are absent, the sequencer is centralized, and stateful
wallets mean losing chain position can lock users out.

 BITCOIN 'TOP BUYERS' HALT BTC PRICE UPSIDE WITH $87.7K NOW KEY:
GLASSNODE (4 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Glassnode identifies the $87,000–$87,700 zone as the primary
resistance for Bitcoin, where 6-12 month holders sit near their cost
basis (~$87,700) and sell into rallies, absorbing new buy-side demand
before price can advance. Two additional levels reinforce this
ceiling: the 2026 opening price at $87,490 and the 20-month simple
moving average at $87,165, a band that preceded new all-time highs
when reclaimed as support in prior cycles. Below spot, the
$80,000–$81,500 range represents overlapping order-book and on-chain
support, anchored by the cost basis of US spot Bitcoin ETF holders
($81,500) and concentrated bid liquidity at $80,000. A breach of that
zone would expose a liquidation cluster just under $80,000, followed
by the true market mean at $77,500 and short-term holder breakeven at
$74,600 as the next structural floor.

 CRYPTO LONGS LOSE $404 MILLION IN FOUR HOURS AS BITCOIN BRIEFLY SLIPS
BELOW $80,000 (3 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Bitcoin briefly fell below $80,000 Saturday morning, triggering $404M
in long liquidations over four hours, the largest such figure since
October 14, as cascading stops swept $815M in total liquidations
across 24 hours and affected 201,012 traders. Ether absorbed $136.72M
in liquidations during that window, closing in on bitcoin's $158.45M
despite ETH representing about 18% of BTC's market cap, with longs
accounting for over 92% of forced exits on both assets. Spot bitcoin
ETFs closed the week with $304.73M in net inflows despite Friday's
$137.94M outflow led by BlackRock's IBIT, while spot ether ETFs posted
$220.60M in weekly net outflows and have shed $1.175B across 14 of 17
October trading sessions. Arthur Hayes closed Maelstrom's directional
book, exiting ETH, ENA, and UNI, citing deteriorating macro conditions
and elevated geopolitical risk tied to Iranian threats against US
embassy personnel in Jordan, Lebanon, Qatar, and the UAE.

 SEC'S TOKENIZATION EXEMPTION SPARKS QUESTIONS OVER WHICH BLOCKCHAINS
QUALIFY (2 MINUTE READ) [11]

 The SEC's tokenization exemption has introduced definitional
uncertainty around what qualifies as a "public and permissionless"
blockchain, with Canton Network (a private permissioned chain used by
Goldman Sachs and other institutions) and Ethereum L2s both sitting in
ambiguous territory. Only chains meeting that threshold benefit from
the exemption, putting pressure on the SEC to clarify whether
institutional permissioned infrastructure or L2s with centralized
sequencers can qualify. Companies are also objecting to having their
equity tokenized without consent, a question the exemption's current
framing leaves unresolved.

 ROBINHOOD STOCK TOKENS NOW LIVE AS COLLATERAL ON MORPHO (2 MINUTE
READ) [12]

 Morpho has launched credit markets for Robinhood Stock Tokens,
allowing users to post tokenized equities as collateral to borrow
stablecoins on-chain. Robinhood Chain provides the tokenization layer
while Morpho operates as the credit network, with the split giving
each protocol a distinct role in the stack. Any app can integrate
stock-backed lending by plugging into this two-layer architecture,
without building custom credit infrastructure.
