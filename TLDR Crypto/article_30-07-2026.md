 FED HOLDS RATES STEADY AS 3 OFFICIALS PUSH FOR HIKE (2 MINUTE READ)
[4]

 The FOMC voted 9-3 to hold rates at 3.5%-3.75% despite three
officials favoring a hike, citing Middle East energy shocks keeping
inflation above the 2% target even as the economy expands. Bitcoin and
ether both ticked up about 1% (to roughly $64,250 and $1,915) with
little reaction to the decision as analysts said geopolitical risk is
limiting crypto's upside even as improving conditions support risk
assets medium-term.

 SENATORS MAY TOUGHEN TRUMP CONCESSION ON CLARITY ACT'S LIMITS (5
MINUTE READ) [5]

 Senators Thom Tillis and Ruben Gallego have reportedly finalized new
language on the Clarity Act's ethics provision, which restricts senior
officials' crypto ties, though details haven't emerged and it still
needs White House and Democratic buy-in. Democrats have argued Trump's
earlier accepted version lets him avoid real compliance since DOJ
enforcement falls under his own appointees. Senate Majority Leader
John Thune said a vote is likely but uncertain given time constraints
before the August recess, with remaining sticking points including
illicit-finance/DeFi protections and stablecoin reward rules the
American Bankers Association wants tightened further. Coinbase CEO
Brian Armstrong called the bill "one yard line" from passage, though
many in the industry are shifting expectations to a September vote.

 SIP-5: OPT-IN QUANTUM RESISTANCE FOR ALL ACCOUNTS (2 MINUTE READ) [6]


 Sei Network's SIP-5 proposal gives every account an opt-in path to
quantum-secure cryptography, triggered if a credible quantum computing
threat against ECDSA-based signatures emerges. The proposal leaves
current account behavior unchanged while ensuring a defined migration
route exists before any threat becomes critical. In parallel, the Sei
team is developing new post-quantum signature schemes to reduce the
performance overhead that current quantum-resistant algorithms carry.

 ROBINHOOD CHAIN DEPOSITS CLIMB BUT VOLUME AND USERS FADE (3 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Robinhood Chain's TVL has climbed to $325 million in under a month,
but DEX volume fell 27% week-over-week to $553 million daily and
active accounts dropped 7% to roughly 275,000, revealing a split
between deposit growth and on-chain activity. The turnover ratio's
collapse from 9.25x to 1.68x confirms capital is arriving faster than
it is being traded, pointing to yield-seeking behavior rather than
organic speculative demand. CEO Vlad Tenev's remark that the chain
"works well for memes" sparked a CASHCAT surge of up to 962% during
launch week, while the Robinhood Earn program's 7% APY on USDG now
appears to be the main deposit driver. Robinhood's Q2 earnings report
this week may clarify whether the team orients toward Real World Asset
projects or doubles down on the memecoin-driven model that defined its
opening weeks.

 WHY LIQUIDITY ISN'T THE SAME AS BEING ABLE TO SETTLE (3 MINUTE READ)
[9]

 An entity can hold ample aggregate liquidity yet still fail to settle
an obligation because eligible cash or collateral may sit in the wrong
account, custodian, or correspondent bank at the critical moment.
Tempo's new report on liquidity mobility demonstrates shared
settlement layers with private execution could shrink the cash and
collateral buffers institutions must preposition, though faster
settlement doesn't automatically reduce funding needs if netting is
lost.

 HPC AND MULTICOIN BACK THE CFTC'S PREDICTION MARKETS FRAMEWORK (4
MINUTE READ) [10]

 Prediction market trading volume topped $50 billion in June, drawing
major exchanges and Wall Street firms into a space the CFTC is now
formalizing through an advance rulemaking on event contract oversight.
Hyperliquid Policy Center and Multicoin Capital filed a joint comment
supporting the proposed framework, arguing that Congress in 2010
granted the CFTC sole authority over exchange-traded event contracts,
preempting state gambling laws that several states are using to
challenge these markets in court. The comment urges the Commission to
codify a settlement-based reading of the statutory "involves" test,
limiting contract review to what a contract actually pays out on
rather than peripheral subject-matter connections, and to publish
reasoning when reviews conclude without a block, not only when
contracts are denied. Hyperliquid's onchain outcome-based contracts,
launched in May and recently reaching all-time high open interest with
all positions collateralized and settled onchain, provide the use case
HPC cites in arguing that uniform federal rules are the condition for
these markets' sustained growth.

 THAILAND'S FILES COMPLAINT AGAINST BITKUB OVER CONCEALED $47M HACK (3
MINUTE READ) [11]

 Thailand's SEC filed criminal charges against Bitkub and two former
directors for concealing a May 2021 cyberattack that stole
approximately $47 million in digital assets across 16 tokens. From May
to October 2021, daily capital filings showed no material change in
assets, hiding the loss in violation of Thailand's Digital Asset
Business Decree. Co-founders purchased replacement assets and
compensated customers by October 2021. Former director Sakolkorn
Sakavee claimed sole responsibility for altering the filings, citing
fears of a bank run, and the case has been forwarded to Thailand's
Economic Crime Suppression Division for possible public prosecution.
