 ARC MAINNET LAUNCHING SEPT. 16 WITH MAJOR FOUNDING VALIDATORS (1
MINUTE READ) [4]

 Circle's Q2 update confirmed Arc Mainnet launches September 16 with
founding validators including BlackRock, DTCC, Galaxy, Mastercard,
MoneyGram, Standard Chartered, and Visa. The company also received a
federal trust bank charter from the OCC for Circle National Trust, and
said USDC captured nearly 70% of stablecoin transaction volume in June
per Visa Onchain Analytics. CEO Jeremy Allaire separately noted these
institutions join over 100 ecosystem and institutional builders
already on Arc's private mainnet.

 BITCOIN ACTIVITY HITS 2026 HIGH AMID COLDCARD ATTACK AS K33 FLAGS
POTENTIAL BOTTOMING PATTERN (3 MINUTE READ) [5]

 K33 says roughly 890,000 BTC moved onchain over the past week, the
highest seven-day active supply of 2026, coinciding with the expanding
Coldcard exploit, which Galaxy Research now estimates involves at
least 15 attackers and roughly $130 million (~2,000 BTC) in losses.
K33's Vetle Lunde said such spikes in active supply have historically
coincided with local market tops and bottoms, arguing "panic is
visible onchain" as holders react to hardware wallet security concerns
and price declines.

 THE PERMAWEB AS A SUBSTRATE FOR AI (6 MINUTE READ) [6]

 AO, a hyper-parallel compute layer built on Arweave, provides AI
agents a trustless substrate by eliminating dependencies on
centralized API keys, model providers, and human-provisioned
infrastructure, letting agents self-provision via a generated keypair
that serves as identity, account, and payment method simultaneously.
Every AO computation produces a signed hashpath, a chain of receipts
any party can replay, making inference verifiability a structural
byproduct of execution rather than a separate audit layer. The
HyperBEAM Agent Device Stack composes inference, TEE GPU attestation,
stateless tool loops, and containerized execution as modular devices
over plain HTTP, with full agent state persisting on Arweave's
permanent message log at a one-time cost of ~$22/GiB. Apus Network
demonstrates this with a keyless OpenAI-compatible endpoint serving
gemma-3-27b-it on H100 in confidential-computing mode, returning TEE
attestation quotes published to Arweave for post-hoc verification.

 BLOCKSPACE: CONNECTING THE WORLD TO ETHEREUM (4 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Blockspace, an Ethereum infrastructure company founded by the Gattaca
and ultra sound teams, launched with a BlueYard-led funding round that
also included ether.fi Ventures, Quasar Builder, Sharplink, Breed VC,
Luganodes, and Stakely, targeting gaps in the out-of-protocol
infrastructure that carries more than 90% of Ethereum's blockspace.
The company monetizes in ETH by operating staking infrastructure,
capturing transaction fees from blocks its staked ETH proposes, and
compounding those earnings back into stake. To limit centralization
risk, Blockspace self-caps its ETH holdings at 15% of total stake, a
threshold the Ethereum community has cited as responsible. The launch
positions Blockspace as the commercial arm of the open-source
Blockspace Forum and is timed ahead of anticipated ePBS
implementation, which the team expects to reshape block production
economics.

 THE SIMPLE ECONOMICS OF L1 VALUE CAPTURE (7 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Written ahead of Solana governance votes on SIMD 550 (Double
Disinflation Speed) and SIMD 553 (Resource Fees), this piece applies
equity valuation theory to L1 token economics, arguing that
inflation-funded staking rewards dilute non-stakers without producing
protocol revenue, while fee burns and distributions to stakers are the
sole mechanisms that generate tokenholder surplus. Gas demand
elasticity estimates derived from EIP-1559 pricing data put short-term
price sensitivity at 0.6 to 0.8, meaning a 10% fee increase reduces
transaction volume by 6 to 8%, and uniform fee schedules fail to
capture the willingness-to-pay spread across heterogeneous users. The
author proposes value-based fee instruments, charging a percentage of
transfer notional rather than flat compute costs, as a more precise
revenue approach than compute pricing alone. Solana and Ethereum's
accumulated network effects in liquidity, applications, and
infrastructure give their fee streams more pricing power than current
market valuations reflect.

 7 PROJECTS BUILDING THE PAYMENT RAILS FOR THE AGENT ECONOMY (6 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 Traditional payment rails cannot serve AI agents because card
networks' OTP, 3D Secure, and chargeback requirements assume a human
operator, and Stripe's $0.30 flat fee consumed 97% of the ~$0.31
median agent spend in 2025. Incumbents are responding through
acquisitions (Stripe took Privy and Bridge, Mastercard acquired BVNK)
and pilots (Visa's stablecoin settlement reached a $7B run rate),
while seven crypto-native projects are building dedicated
infrastructure across the stack. The cohort spans a purpose-built EVM
L1 (Kite AI, $35M from PayPal, Coinbase Ventures, and General
Catalyst, with Google A2P and PYUSD as launch partners), x402-native
facilitators (PayAI's 35.8M transactions processed. Thirdweb's
pay-per-call API layer), stablecoin orchestration to resolve
gas/payment token mismatches (Eco), full-stack agent wallets with
virtual card issuance via Visa and Mastercard partnerships
(Crossmint), and a co-wallet protocol embedding budget mandates in
x402/A2A/MCP calls with ZK-batched settlement (FluxA's AEP2).
PaymanAI, backed by $13.8M from Visa, Circle, and Coinbase, closes the
set by targeting banks and credit unions with agent-driven transaction
execution, audit trails, and kill switches.

 PROOF OF PLAY CEASES OPERATIONS, OPEN-SOURCES PIRATE NATION (2 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 Proof of Play, the blockchain gaming studio behind the fully onchain
Pirate Nation, is ceasing operations after failing to build a
sustainable product proving blockchain gaming's thesis at scale. The
team is open-sourcing the Pirate Nation Unity app, smart contracts,
and an internal agent workflow tool, while releasing Founder Pirates
NFTs and all Pirate Nation artwork and IP under CC0. Proof of Play
points carry no redemption value, though the independent Pirate Nation
Foundation will continue supporting the $PIRATE token, and Shiba Story
Go! has been acquired by a third party and will continue operating.
