 LEDGER EXPANDS HARDWARE HACK INVESTIGATION TO GERMANY AND UAE (3
MINUTE READ) [7]

 Ledger says new tampered devices in Berlin and Dubai are the same
incident as the Southeast Asia campaign, with extra hardware that
captures the recovery phrase during setup, and one case involves the
newer Nano Gen5. It says there is no flaw in its chip or OS and no
confirmed case from ledger.com. Two users claim a combined $604,000 in
losses from a Dubai-authorized reseller, and Bitquery reports about
$1.68M stolen from 16 wallets in Dubai and Berlin, after earlier
claims of $102M from 335 wallets in Southeast Asia. Ledger has not
confirmed loss amounts and expects more devices to surface.

 KRAKEN PARENT PAYWARD ACQUIRES TRIBE CAPITAL TO ENTER PRIVATE MARKETS
(2 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Payward, Kraken's parent, agreed to acquire Tribe Capital, a venture
firm managing $2.7 billion chaired by Payward and Kraken co-CEO Arjun
Sethi, with closing expected in Q1 2027 at an undisclosed price. The
deal adds private-market investing to a vertical buildout that also
includes the pending purchase of derivatives exchange Bitnomial, a
$600 million acquisition of stablecoin payments firm Reap, and the
buyout of Magic Labs' wallet business. Sethi's concurrent role as
Tribe chair and Payward co-CEO positions the transaction as a formal
consolidation of an already-integrated executive relationship rather
than a standard external acquisition.

 KALSHI VS. POLYMARKET: DOMAIN EXPERTS WIN (4 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Kalshi and Polymarket launched within months of each other, but
Kalshi now posts roughly 4x the volume at 3x the valuation, with a
$65B raise in talks versus Polymarket's unannounced $21B round.
Kalshi's MIT math founders, with prior finance careers, sued the CFTC,
won regulatory standing, then expanded into single-stock perps on 50+
companies, indices, and AI compute futures this year. Polymarket
became a tab inside a memecoin application, sitting beside memecoins
and perps, its standalone-exchange identity absorbed into a retail
crypto product. Founder background drove the split: quant-finance
founders built toward prediction markets as a regulated asset class,
while operator-type founders built toward distribution as the product.


 THE EVERYTHING-APP RACE: STICKINESS, REV-SHARING, AND CROSS-MARGIN (2
MINUTE READ) [12]

 Robinhood, Polymarket, Kalshi, Coinbase, Kraken, and Pump.fun are
each expanding beyond their originating verticals, converging on a
shared product surface spanning stocks, crypto spot, perps, prediction
markets, and memecoins from divergent starting points across TradFi
and crypto-native distribution. Three decisive factors: customer
retention depth, rev-sharing structures that attract new users and
retain whales, and cross-margin capability across the full product
stack. No clear differentiation thesis has emerged.

 WHY BITCOIN NEEDS CAPITAL MARKETS AND HOW STACKS IS BUILDING THEM (6
MINUTE READ) [13]

 Bitcoin lacks yield, credit, and trading markets, and holders
shouldn't have to hand over BTC to earn. Stacks' Bitcoin staking is
live, with bonds targeting 3% annual yield paid in BTC from miners'
Proof of Transfer commitments. The first two bonds drew 735 BTC, with
Bullish staking in Bond 3 and Anchorage offering custody. In 2027,
Stacks plans an open auction through PoX-6 and borrowing stablecoins
against staked BTC. STX is required at 5% of the BTC value, and
SIP-034 raised block capacity up to 30x, with a 100x goal.

 MORPHO AND AAVE ARE IN A FULL BD WAR (3 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Onchain lending now depends on distribution, partnerships, and
integrations through neobanks, wallets, and exchanges, not just a good
app. Morpho is plugging into apps people already use: Coinbase Borrow
in New York, OKX Wallet on X Layer, earn products at Ledger, Bitso,
Pulsar, Abra, and Deel, Robinhood Stock Tokens as collateral, and new
APIs. Aave is pursuing infrastructure partners and new collateral
through V4: Circle Mint, Privy, and Base for fintechs, Ethena Pay's
Earn+, cirBTC, PAXGy, mWIN, and Coinbase tokenized stocks, plus the
Avon acquisition for fixed-rate lending. V3 Core going blue-chip only
pushes new assets to V4. The real race is owning tokenized non-crypto
assets through early issuer partnerships, and most onchain lending
will likely run on one of the two.
