 CIRCLE'S Q2 COUNTERS BEARISH CONCERNS OVER COMPETITION, RESERVE
INCOME (4 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Bernstein reiterated its Outperform rating and $140 price target on
Circle after Q2 revenue hit $701 million, up 7% year over year but
roughly 2% below consensus. Adjusted EBITDA of $143 million and EPS of
$0.19 beat expectations, with reserve income making up 95% of revenue.
Analysts cited Circle's OCC trust bank approval, growing Circle
Payments Network, and the September 16 Arc mainnet launch as revenue
streams not yet reflected in estimates. USDC circulation stood at
$73.3 billion, down 5% quarter over quarter but up 19% year over year.


 EU WATCHDOGS WARN SCAMMERS ARE POSING AS CRYPTO FIRMS AND REGULATORS
(3 MINUTE READ) [5]

 French regulator AMF, Dutch watchdog AFM, and ESMA warned of rising
scams exploiting the July 1 MiCA compliance deadline, with fraudsters
impersonating AMF staff and misusing ESMA's logo to direct victims to
fake sites. ~320 entities have secured MiCA licenses as of August 5,
while VASPnet estimates over 1,700 firms, including Binance, must wind
down or restrict EU services for missing the deadline. AMF has avoided
setting an aggressive shutdown timeline for unlicensed exchanges to
limit scam exposure.

 COINBASE LAUNCHES 24/5 US STOCK TRADING FOR UK USERS (4 MINUTE READ)
[6]

 Coinbase began rolling out 24/5 trading on nearly 4,000 US stocks to
eligible UK users on August 6, with zero-commission trades, fractional
shares from £1, and funding via GBP or USDC. Shares are custodied by
Apex Clearing, with orders routed through Coinbase Capital Markets
Corporation. UK CEO Keith Grose said the launch builds on Coinbase's
recent UK MiFID license and prior savings and crypto-backed lending
products. Plans for tokenized equities are pending regulatory
development.

 LITHIC AND LIGHTSPARK PARTNER TO POWER GLOBAL CARD PROGRAM (3 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 Lithic will process Lightspark's Visa card program, issued by Lead
Bank and settling in USDC. Lightspark holds account balances natively
in stablecoins and converts to local currency at point of spend, using
Lithic's Authorization Intelligence for unified card authorization,
device authentication, and fraud decisioning. CEO David Marcus said
the partnership matches Lightspark's need for speed across its
stablecoin-based settlement infrastructure.

 HYPERLIQUID Q2: RWA PERPS SCALE TO $213B (5 MINUTE READ) [8]

 HYPE hit an ATH of $76.90 in Q2, up 79% while BTC fell 14%, a second
consecutive quarter of decoupling from majors. RWA perpetuals on HIP-3
grew from 1.8% to 32.2% of matched volume across three quarters,
reaching $213B in Q2, with a private rocket company priced onchain on
a Saturday while the NYSE was closed and a Chinese chipmaker trading
above its Shanghai listing. Protocol revenue closed Q2 at $169M with
$141M returned via buybacks, cumulative revenue crossed $1B, and three
HYPE ETFs began trading after four asset managers filed in Q1.
Concentration risk remains: $5.6B in stablecoin float relies on a
single issuer and exchange volume runs through one deployer.

 EIP-8363 TAPERED ISSUANCE BURN FOR HEGOTÁ (5 MINUTE READ) [9]

 EIP-8363 (Tapered Issuance Burn) proposes burning a fraction of
consensus-layer validator rewards each epoch so that net staking yield
reaches zero once 50% of ETH supply (~60.25M ETH) is staked, submitted
two days before the August 6 ACDC call reviewing Hegotá hard fork
candidates. With the staking ratio already above 1/3 as of April 2026
and ~1.75M ETH entering per month, projections place total staked ETH
above 55% of supply by January 2028, well before roadmap milestones
that would redefine the validator role. TIB leaves execution-layer
income (MEV and priority fees) untouched, so as consensus rewards
compress, MEV becomes a growing share of total income, a variance that
large custodians and ETF issuers absorb through scale while solo
stakers and independent operators exit first, deteriorating the
Nakamoto coefficient. ETH staking yield serves as the base rate for
lending markets, leveraged staking loops, LST collateral, and
fixed-income protocols, so critics argue TIB makes yields
unpredictable and uneconomical across the onchain economy before the
validator role has been redesigned to justify the cut.

 NEOBANKS CHASING CRYPTO-NATIVE USERS (2 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Neobanks like Dakota, Rain, Stablecoin, MoonPay, and Blend are
building near-identical stablecoin-powered products for the same small
crypto-native audience instead of everyday consumers and businesses.
Most people don't care whether a payment settles via ACH, Fedwire,
SWIFT, or stablecoins. They care about speed, cost, and reliability.
The bigger opportunity is using stablecoin infrastructure to serve the
much larger legacy-banking market instead.

 AI16Z TOKEN DEAD, FOUNDATION WINDS DOWN (2 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Shaw, creator of the Eliza agent framework and ai16z, announced the
foundation is winding down after a Burwick class action settlement
consumed all remaining treasury funds and his personal savings, which
he described as a forced outcome since the project could not afford to
litigate. Shaw told token holders the position is "completely ngmi,"
with no buybacks, no foundation supply, and no further support
forthcoming. Shaw said he never sold his ai16z tokens and drew only an
engineer-level salary throughout the project's run, but the settlement
cost ended the foundation regardless. Eliza OS development continues
on Shaw's personal savings as an independent effort, and he committed
to never attaching a token to the Eliza brand again, a notable exit
for what was one of Solana's highest-profile AI agent tokens during
the 2024-2025 cycle.
