 COINBASE TOKENIZED STOCKS LIVE ON BASE (5 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Coinbase launched four tokenized equities on Base under the B20
standard (NVDAc, METAc, AAPLc, and GOOGLc), with shares held 1:1 by
Alpaca under Abu Dhabi Global Market supervision, restricted to non-US
users in eligible jurisdictions. Day-one activity reached $4.55M in
combined onchain value, $3.06M in DEX liquidity, and $10.8M in 24-hour
volume across approximately 50 integrated protocols including Aave,
Morpho, Aerodrome, 0x, and 1inch. B20 extends ERC-20 for full
composability with existing wallets and routers. Dividends and splits
settle via an onchain multiplier that keeps position balances static,
preventing disruptions to DeFi collateral positions. The launch enters
a $2.48B tokenized public equities market currently led by Ondo at
$872.7M, with agentic trading integrations already underway through
Virtuals and Treasures.

 AI AGENTS ARE CRYPTO'S NEXT BILLION USERS, PAYING WITH STABLECOINS (3
MINUTE READ) [5]

 AI agents are emerging as a distinct crypto user cohort, autonomously
spending stablecoins to pay for services without human approval loops.
Coinbase's head of AI product characterizes the current moment as the
"Napster/LimeWire era" of agentic payments, placing the category at an
early, pre-mainstream inflection point where infrastructure and norms
are still forming. Coinbase is building at the AI/crypto payments
intersection, targeting a wave of machine-initiated transactions that
operate outside traditional user onboarding funnels. The protocols and
rails that capture agent wallet share now could define the settlement
layer for machine commerce at scale, making stablecoin-denominated
micropayments a structural demand driver rather than a cyclical one.

 ELYSIUM: A NEW ERA FOR HYPERLIQUID (4 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Kinetiq, which holds the majority of HyperEVM TVL and economic
activity, is launching Elysium, an L2 built to resolve HyperEVM's
throughput constraints and swap costs that reach $20 per transaction.
Elysium adopts HYPE as its gas token instead of USDC, targets block
time parity with HyperCore, and ships an L1Read precompile that grants
PropAMMs top-of-block oracle freshness, enabling tighter quotes than
HyperEVM's architecture permits. A native token generation platform
creates an unbroken path from long-tail AMM bootstrapping through
Elysium PropAMM integration, HyperCore spot orderbook listing, and
eventual HIP-3 perpetual listing, all within one ecosystem. Sequencer
fees split 50% toward programmatic open-market KNTQ purchases burned
in full to the Hyperliquid Assistance Fund, with the remaining 50%
split equally between builders and the Kinetiq treasury.

 WHERE SHOULD YOUR STABLECOINS GO TO EARN? (6 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Kamino, Jupiter Lend, and Loopscale control roughly 92% of Solana's
tracked stablecoin lending TVL, each built on a distinct architecture.
Kamino's shared pool isolates riskier collateral in separate markets,
backed by 15 audits, 4 formal verifications, and no confirmed exploits
since its 2023 launch. Jupiter Lend routes both depositor capital and
leveraged borrowing through a single Liquidity Layer, with dynamic
withdrawal ceilings throttling liquidity-shock risk. Loopscale's
Credit Order Book pairs lenders and borrowers at fixed rates and
durations, offering rate certainty over pool depth, though it carries
the only confirmed exploit among the three: a $5.8M price feed attack
in April 2025 where all funds were recovered within 48 hours via a
negotiated 10% bounty. The choice maps to variable-rate liquidity
depth (Kamino), combined earn-and-borrow exposure with shock controls
(Jupiter), and predictable fixed rates with thinner markets
(Loopscale).

 BUILDING RISK-AWARE AI AGENTS FOR VAULT CURATION (4 MINUTE READ) [11]


 Gauntlet's Aria agent operates within a staged autonomy model (tool
to scoped delegate to orchestrator), with all production vault changes
requiring one or two human approvals before execution. In production,
Aria has triaged 450+ risk alerts and reviewed 200+ pull requests,
with 61 merged post-human approval. The core architectural principle
separates offchain reasoning (unconstrained access to live data and
compute) from onchain authority enforced via Aera's merkle-root
committed guardian permissions, making delegation cryptographically
precise and auditable. Gauntlet built its own agentic harness rather
than using commercial platforms, citing ownership of controls and
action verifiability as non-outsourceable requirements for adversarial
DeFi environments.
