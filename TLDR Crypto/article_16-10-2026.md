 BITCOIN DROPS BELOW $81,000 AS RISK-OFF SENTIMENT HITS ASIAN EQUITIES
(3 MINUTE READ) [4]

 BTC fell 3.6% to $80,764, and ETH fell 6.5% to $2,353 over 24 hours.

 STANDARD CHARTERED PUSHES $2 TRILLION STABLECOIN MARKET FORECAST TO
2030 (3 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Geoffrey Kendrick moved the bank's $2T forecast out from 2028 to
2030. Supply has stayed between $300B and $320B for 12 months and sits
near $309B. He calls the stall cyclical, tied to weak crypto activity.
Usage keeps growing: monthly transfer value averages nearly $10T this
year versus $4T in 2025, and velocity doubled to about 30x. USDC
velocity is around 80x (payments, early AI-agent payments) against
about 7x for USDT (emerging-market savings). He expects the most
competition in developed-market payments, from USDG, M^0 and the
multi-bank A+ network. Regulation is the main risk, especially if
other countries follow the GENIUS Act, which takes effect in January.

 RAPIDBLOCKS: EXECUTION PROOFS THAT OUTLIVE THEIR STATE (5 MINUTE
READ) [6]

 A new Ethereum research proposal restructures execution proof
validation by splitting monolithic pre-state-to-post-state proofs into
three components: one validating transaction execution against
declared inputs, and two handling Merkle input/output verification.
Block headers commit to the specific values a block reads rather than
a pre-state root, allowing proofs to remain valid across multiple
slots when state changes do not touch the accessed keys. The design
separates per-slot block validation from state root verification on an
independent schedule, so block consensus advances without waiting for
state computation to complete. The proposal remains early-stage: block
proofs must complete within 2.5 seconds from finalization, witness
data requirements for Merkle-Patricia tries are unresolved, and safety
proofs are verified only via bounded model checking on small
instances.

 SEC APPROVES BINARY OPTIONS ON COMPANY METRICS FOR COINBASE, NVIDIA,
TESLA (2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 The SEC has approved binary options contracts tied to company
performance metrics for Coinbase, Nvidia, Tesla, MARA, SpaceX, and 20
other firms. Each contract pays a fixed $1 if the chosen metric clears
a set threshold and expires worthless if it does not, structuring
payouts identically to event-based prediction markets. Cboe will list
more than 100 metrics spanning Coinbase trading volume, MARA Bitcoin
production, Nvidia data center revenue, and SpaceX total revenue.
Robinhood will be the first retail broker to distribute the contracts,
with no trading fees through year-end.

 AI AGENTS MADE 138 MILLION CRYPTO PAYMENTS IN A YEAR. MOST WERE TINY
(3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Chainalysis data shows autonomous AI agents executed over 138 million
cryptocurrency payments through Coinbase's x402 protocol on Base
between mid-2025 and Q3 2026, up from approximately 1,500 transactions
a year earlier. More than 87% of payments fell between 0.01 cents and
10 cents, validating stablecoin micropayments as a use case legacy
payment rails cannot serve at that unit size. American-linked wallets
drove 88.1% of AI model inference spending, while Chinese-linked
wallets led security and code-analysis service purchases despite
accounting for just 0.1% of inference spending. TRM Labs cautions that
a portion of the recorded activity may reflect scripts or self-dealing
rather than genuine autonomous agents, suggesting the headline figures
likely overstate actual agentic commerce volume.

 CANADA CRYPTO REMITTANCES TO ASIAN COUNTRIES SURGE 141% TO $2.36B (3
MINUTE READ) [9]

 Chainalysis data covering the 12 months through June 2026 shows
remittance-sized crypto transfers from Canada to India, China, and the
Philippines grew 141% year-over-year to $2.36 billion across 3.56
million transactions averaging $663 each, lifting those three
corridors to 19.4% of Canada's total outbound cross-border stablecoin
value from 10.9% previously. Canada's foreign-born population of 23%,
the highest share among G7 nations, concentrates demand in those
corridors and supports Chainalysis' ranking of Canada as the G7's
strongest peer-to-peer crypto economy. Stablecoins accounted for 44%
of Canadian dollar-denominated order book volume in June 2026, up from
30% a year earlier, reflecting growing preference for dollar-pegged
assets as a settlement layer in cross-border flows. The World Bank
estimates traditional remittance costs from Canada average 6.7%, while
blockchain settlement fees run lower, though currency conversion and
cash-out costs remain factors in the total cost of transfer.

MISCELLANEOUS

 HEX FOUNDER RICHARD HEART ARRESTED IN ISTANBUL UNDER INTERPOL RED
NOTICE (3 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Turkish authorities arrested Richard Heart (Richard James Schueler),
founder of HEX, PulseChain, and PulseX, in Istanbul's Beşiktaş
district during a joint intelligence and police operation, acting on
an Interpol Red Notice issued at Finland's request. The seizure
included $600,000 in cash, 7 firearms with 1,360 rounds of ammunition,
24 wristwatches, gold jewelry, 20 graphics cards, 14 SSDs, 12 mobile
phones, 6 laptops, and 2 drones. The SEC had charged Heart in 2023
with raising over $1 billion through unregistered crypto offerings and
misappropriating at least $12 million in investor funds, while
Finland's separate Interpol notice covers tax fraud and assault
allegations dating to 2024. Both Heart and his wife were taken into
custody.

 BLACKROCK'S CRYPTO ETF ASSETS SURGE 63% BUT REMAIN FAR BELOW 2025
PEAK (3 MINUTE READ) [11]

 BlackRock's digital assets under management climbed 63%
quarter-over-quarter to $79.6 billion by the end of September, with
$22.9 billion of the $30.8 billion increase driven by cryptocurrency
price appreciation rather than net inflows. The firm attracted $7.9
billion in fresh capital during Q3, reversing roughly $2.2 billion in
first-half 2026 outflows, while generating $39 million in quarterly
fees and securities lending revenue from these products. Despite the
Q3 rebound, AUM remains 23% below the $104 billion recorded a year
earlier, as $30.6 billion in 12-month market losses outpaced $6.3
billion in net new deposits over the same period.
