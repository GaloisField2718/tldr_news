MARKETS & BUSINESS

 SEC ENTERS FIGHT OVER WHO REGULATES PERPETUAL DERIVATIVES ON STOCKS
(3 MINUTE READ) [6]

 The SEC filed an amicus brief backing the CFTC after the Capital
Markets Alliance, whose members include Cboe and Citadel Securities,
sued to overturn the CFTC's approval of equity perps, claiming it
violated the Administrative Procedure Act and that the products belong
under SEC jurisdiction. The SEC rejected the picture of a “rogue”
CFTC and absent SEC, saying CFTC rule changes are subject to its
review and the agencies collaborate often.

 TRON, TETHER, AND CHAINALYSIS TO TACKLE AI-POWERED CRYPTO CRIME (2
MINUTE READ) [7]

 Tron, Tether, and Chainalysis have partnered to launch a “Digital
Crime Task Force” to identify and disrupt illicit activity involving
USDT on Tron, shortening the gap between detection and enforcement by
gathering intelligence and working with law enforcement to freeze
assets sooner. It replaces the 2024 T3 Financial Crime Unit with TRM
Labs, which Bloomberg reported was disbanded over slow response and
poor coordination. Chainalysis says AI-enabled scams were 4.5 times
more profitable than conventional ones in 2025, with impersonation
scams up more than 1,400%. Tether's CEO says it has frozen over $7B
tied to illicit activity with 360+ law enforcement agencies in 73
countries. Targets include scams, hacks, terrorist financing, and
North Korean attacks.

INNOVATION & LAUNCHES

 SYGNUM LAUNCHES ETH ALPHA FUND TARGETING 8-10% ANNUAL RETURNS IN ETH
(2 MINUTE READ) [8]

 The market-neutral fund for qualified and professional investors
opened November 2 and targets 8-10% net annual returns paid in ETH. It
combines native staking with options premiums, cross-venue arbitrage,
and derivatives carry, with gains and losses realized in ETH. Average
staking yields fell to 2.63% by the end of September, with 43.8M ETH,
nearly 36% of supply, staked.

 CIRCLE LAUNCHES AGENTIC WORKS (3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Agentic Works is an open research initiative that publishes AI agent
experiments built on Circle's USDC infrastructure. Published
experiments range from eight fully autonomous agents making live
football match predictions from individual USDC wallets (AW-001) to a
human-approved workflow where an agent moved USDC between Arc and
Hyperliquid to open and close a $10 BTC position (AW-003). Additional
experiments cover an agent self-provisioning a persistent Circle Agent
Wallet with no integration work across sessions (AW-002) and USDC
transfers sent directly from iMessage (AW-004). Circle ships
installable developer skills and open-source starter kits alongside
the initiative, though all published work is experimental and not
production-ready.

 ELASTIC ETHEREUM (6 MINUTE READ) [10]

 An ethresear.ch opinion post argues Ethereum should replace its fixed
slot time, gas limits, and pipeline depth with measurement-driven
elastic parameters, drawing an analogy to TCP congestion control's
adaptive window mechanism, which scales the same protocol across four
orders of magnitude of link speed. Xatu telemetry from 233
instrumented nodes over 100,548 mainnet blocks (October 27–November
9) shows median propagation reaches 95% of nodes in 0.38s, but
late-head rates range from 1.7% on company nodes to 11.0% on
individual contributor nodes, and 250–260 KiB blocks take 0.61s
versus 0.34s for 50–60 KiB blocks. The author maps Ethereum's
mempool to TCP's send buffer and orphaned blocks to packet loss,
identifying slot time (EIP-7782, EIP-8198), gas-limit votes, and
parallel pipelining via Rapidblocks as adaptive knobs, with EIP-8288
mempool aggregation as an elastic interval candidate.

 TREASURES SWAP: DEX AGGREGATOR FOR TOKENIZED STOCKS (2 MINUTE READ)
[11]

 Treasures Swap is a DEX aggregator for tokenized equities that
compares pricing across issuers, including Ondo, Dinari, and Backed,
on multiple chains. The protocol routes orders to maximize tokenized
stock received per USDC, applying the same aggregation model used by
1inch and 0x to the RWA equity vertical. Users connect self-custody
wallets directly, removing custodian dependencies. The launch
addresses fragmentation in the tokenized stock market, where the same
equity may be issued by multiple protocols at varying prices across
different chains.

 LIGHTER LAUNCHES GUILDS FOR COMMUNITY TRADING (1 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Lighter, an onchain order-book perps exchange, has launched Guilds, a
social layer that lets traders form groups, compete in team-based
Duels, and climb community leaderboards. The feature follows perp DEXs
like Hyperliquid in stacking gamification onto trading infrastructure
to drive retention and community growth.

 NOMURA EXPLORES SALE OF CRYPTO UNIT LASER DIGITAL (2 MINUTE READ)
[13]

 Nomura Holdings is exploring a sale of Laser Digital, its
institutional crypto subsidiary, with Marex Group among prospective
buyers evaluating a deal, though Nomura may ultimately retain the
business. Losses at Laser Digital from crypto market volatility pushed
Nomura's European operations into a quarterly loss, prompting
management to tighten risk controls across the unit. Founded in 2022,
Laser Digital covers institutional crypto trading, asset management,
and venture investment, and recently secured a Japanese crypto
exchange license alongside conditional approval for a US national
trust bank charter. The potential divestiture illustrates the P&L
exposure TradFi firms absorb when a crypto subsidiary's drawdowns roll
up into the parent's segment results, while Laser Digital's regulatory
footprint increases its value to any prospective acquirer.
