 BITCOIN FACES KEY HURDLE IN $81,000-$86,000 RANGE TO REACH JANUARY
HIGH (3 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Analysts have identified an $81,000-$86,000 supply zone as the key
barrier Bitcoin must clear to recapture its January 2026 highs, with
four factors overlapping in that band: long-term holders sitting near
breakeven, self-custody accumulation starting around $80,800, options
dealer hedges activating near $82,300, and dense short liquidation
clusters between $82,000 and $86,000. The current recovery was
catalyzed by the US Treasury's buyback expansion announcement,
followed by the largest short liquidation event since 2019 on August
19, which compressed futures open interest 11% in coin terms. Eight
consecutive days of ETF inflows totaling $2.8 billion support the
rally, with a close above $83,300 alongside continued inflows serving
as the signal that overhead supply is being absorbed. A retreat from
this zone would expose $62,900, the level that would erase the entire
recovery.

 REVOLUT LAUNCHES EURO STABLECOIN EURR ON ETHEREUM (3 MINUTE READ) [6]


 Revolut, the European neobank with 80 million customers globally,
announced EURR, a Euro-denominated stablecoin deployed on Ethereum
mainnet and targeted at European Economic Area users. The launch adds
a high-distribution issuer to Ethereum's Euro stablecoin segment,
which has lagged USD-pegged counterparts in adoption and liquidity
depth. Revolut's entry follows the PayPal PYUSD model of a regulated
fintech issuing its own stablecoin to capture payment flows onchain,
and arrives as MiCA's stablecoin provisions create clearer issuance
pathways across the EU. If Revolut integrates EURR into its core app
for transfers and spending, its existing euro user base could drive
adoption that reshapes Euro stablecoin market share on Ethereum.

 14 WAYS NEOBANKS MAKE MONEY (6 MINUTE READ) [8]

 A breakdown of neobank revenue streams beyond interchange:
on/off-ramp spreads, FX markup, yield/treasury products, credit cards,
float on customer deposits/stablecoin balances, lending spreads,
payment processing, wire/transfer fees, payroll, travel commissions,
subscriptions, brokerage/investing, and embedded verticalized software
(like AI billing or AR-collection agents). The core thesis is that the
more of a customer's financial life a neobank owns, the more
monetizable surfaces it creates and the harder the platform becomes to
replace.

 WHEN MULTIPLE POOLS BEHAVE LIKE ONE: IMPACT-CONSTRAINED CAPACITY IN
UNISWAP V3 (5 MINUTE READ) [9]

 A study of 293,273 capacity scenarios across 78 Ethereum token
families finds that Uniswap v3 pool multiplicity delivers almost no
distributed execution capacity in practice. The median HHI across
scenarios sits at 0.930, the top pool captures a 96.3% median share,
and only 0.67% of scenarios achieve an effective pool count above 2,
with multi-pool routing adding just 3.2% capacity on average.
Reconstructed daily pool states show actual trading concentration
exceeds the already-concentrated liquidity-layer capacity on 67.6% of
matched family-days, with a Spearman correlation of 0.841 between
observed and modeled distributions. The analysis covers
liquidity-layer capacity only and excludes gas costs, routing
friction, and MEV, meaning measured concentration represents a floor,
not a ceiling.

 WHAT WATCHING FOMO'S RISE TAUGHT ME ABOUT VECTOR (10 MINUTE READ)
[10]

 The Vector co-founder traces how the Solana social trading app,
acquired by Coinbase in late 2025, peaked at $20M daily volume and
~$1B all-time volume before stalling when it pivoted to pro desktop
tools after memecoin activity concentrated in ~5% of its user base.
Fomo pursued the inverse strategy, targeting TikTok and Instagram
audiences new to onchain trading rather than competing for existing
crypto-native traders, and recently crossed $100M in daily spot
volume. Crypto Twitter is a beachhead that validated the
social-signal-plus-execution thesis but represented only a fraction of
the eventual market. As equities, RWAs, and prediction markets migrate
onchain and AI sharpens signal discovery, a social trading graph
paired with execution rails could scale to a $1T category.

 ETHLABS WEEK 9: WHAT HAPPENED WHEN WE ALL GOT INTO ONE ROOM (7 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 Ethlabs, a 9-week-old nonprofit R&D lab focused on making Ethereum
the global settlement layer, emerged from its first in-person offsite
with four concrete priorities. EIP-8198 (Quick Slots), which makes
slot time configurable to bring the 12-second block time down in
stages, is advancing in the Hegotá fork discussion, and a major
bridge is preparing to adopt the Fast Confirmation Rule (FCR) in
production, promising up to 32x faster deposit confirmations in
best-case scenarios. On interop, the team landed on a 1-second
cross-chain movement goal and separated problems requiring protocol
changes from those needing ecosystem coordination alone. The lab is
also committing to substantive work on ETH as an asset, a topic
protocol organizations have long sidestepped in favor of
Ethereum-as-platform framing.
