 WALRASWAP: EQUILIBRIUM-BASED DEX INTEGRATING EXTERNAL LIQUIDITY (5
MINUTE READ) [6]

 Walraswap proposes an equilibrium-based batch DEX that integrates
external AMM liquidity, where a price vector exists such that all
submitted orders execute with no token deficit or surplus. Unlike CoW
Swap, UniswapX, and 1inch Fusion, which use uniform clearing prices
alone, Walraswap's equilibrium property requires including all
available trade orders, closing the solver manipulation vector where
order censorship shifts clearing outcomes. DAO-elected solvers retain
censorship as the sole attack surface, which the protocol treats as
detectable in a repeated game (analogous to block builders not
unbundling searcher bundles). The construction, originating at
Flashbots in 2023, frames settlement as a convex optimization problem.
Net trades at equilibrium are unique despite non-unique price vectors.


 BLOCK TIME AND PROPAMMS: FROM ETHEREUM TO BASE DENIM (5 MINUTE READ)
[7]

 PropAMMs face a core execution risk: the gap between an onchain
quoted price and the live market rate spans one block, letting fast
traders extract value at the maker's expense during that window. On
Ethereum's 12-second blocks, builders such as Titan address this by
guaranteeing a maker's latest price update lands before any competing
trade in the same block, a mechanism Flashbots is standardizing as
ERC-8324 and that Fermi has used to process hundreds of millions in
weekly DEX volume. Base's Denim upgrade converts each 200ms flashblock
slot into a full block, retiring the preview layer, shrinking the
staleness window from 2 seconds to 200ms, and giving aggregators and
solvers fresher quotes up to 2 seconds sooner. For propAMM operators,
Denim makes per-block exposure limits more precise and shifts the
execution advantage from tip size to sequencer latency, a dynamic
Biconomy has been testing with a major market maker on its own Base
propAMM since October.

 AGENTIC COMMERCE: 17 MONTHS LATER (6 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Seventeen months after the original agentic commerce piece, all three
predictions are holding: payment processors with identity and
tokenization infrastructure (Stripe, PayPal, and Adyen) are capturing
early value, consumers still want final authentication control, and
checkout is evolving rather than dying as purchase origination shifts
from brand search to AI engines and social. The Agentic Commerce
Protocol (Stripe and OpenAI) and Stripe Link Balance both launched in
the interim, moving the thesis from prediction to live infrastructure.
Shah's strongest current conviction is that agentic commerce creates
more capacity for SMEs than convenience for consumers, with agents
automating operations and cash flow management in place of headcount.
The economic model for hybrid stablecoin and card-rail settlement
remains unresolved.

 WHERE ONCHAIN CREDIT FITS IN THE AI BOOM (8 MINUTE READ) [11]

 AI compute is now a debt business, with 110 public GPU financings
totaling $197B since 2023, mostly led by big banks that only serve
borrowers large enough to justify diligence. That leaves small
neoclouds underserved, and USD.AI lends to them from stablecoin
deposits against GPUs as collateral, at 10-15% rates (12% median)
versus 9-10% fixed at banks, and says loans can clear in under 7 days.
It has originated $531M across 20 financings, and its sUSDai vault
targets a 7% yield. The new GLX program sells seasoned loans so the
cash can be re-lent, and the first exit was the $98.1M Duos Edge AI
loan, repaid within four months. CHIP buybacks are funded by the
protocol's 10% cut of interest, and onchain lenders hold under 1% of
the $48.4B GPU debt raised in 2026. Risks include fast GPU
depreciation, neocloud consolidation, and a mismatch between 30-day
withdrawals and roughly 3-year loans.

 BRIAN ARMSTRONG'S THREE-TIER AGENT WORKFLOW (2 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong restructured his executive workflow
around a three-tier agent hierarchy: a chief of staff agent overseeing
per-domain lead agents, which each manage sub-agents for code,
testing, and team coordination. All decisions and reviews funnel into
a single prioritized queue with screenshots and video demos per
feature, cleared in batches by voice and keyboard shortcuts. The
system builds a model of his preferences over time, with first drafts
and recommendations improving before they reach him, targeting fewer
corrections per change. Armstrong frames this as a live example of the
agent-economy infrastructure Coinbase is building.
