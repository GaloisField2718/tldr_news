MARKETS & BUSINESS

 FOGO HALTS MAINNET AFTER $3M TOKEN COMPROMISE (2 MINUTE READ) [4]

 The Fogo Foundation disclosed that an attacker received 400 million
FOGO tokens (~$3M, about 10% of the circulating supply) in a
compromise, initially saying the chain itself was unaffected. About 15
hours later, the network halted entirely so validators could upgrade
and restrict the affected addresses, with no restart time given.
Bitget and KuCoin both suspended FOGO deposits/withdrawals around the
same time. Fogo launched in January off a $7M Binance token sale at a
$350M valuation and had claimed 100% mainnet uptime until this halt.

 BITWISE SOLANA ETF HITS $1B AUM (2 MINUTE READ) [5]

 The Bitwise Solana Staking ETF (BSOL) crossed $1B AUM 10 months after
launch, more than half of all Solana ETF assets, despite trading ~40%
below its listing price as SOL sits 60% off its all-time high.
Cumulative spot Solana ETF volume has topped $13B since September 2025
with $1.7B in net inflows and no real outflow stretch. This comes
alongside growing institutional access as Charles Schwab plans to add
spot Solana trading, and Goldman Sachs is currently the largest known
holder of spot Solana ETFs (~$90M).

 BYBIT LAUNCHES 24/7 STOCK PERPETUAL OPTIONS (2 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Starting September 17, Bybit users can trade round-the-clock options
on SpaceX (SPCX) and Nvidia (NVDA) perpetuals, with fractional lots,
USDT settlement, and portfolio margin support for spreads, straddles,
and covered calls. Tesla, QQQ, SOXL, and Micron are planned next.
Positioned as the next step after Ondo's tokenized-stock-as-collateral
perps, amid a broader industry push (Coinbase, Kraken, and Robinhood)
toward 24/7 equity trading, tokenized-equity perp volume grew from
$85B in January to $470B in June, with SpaceX the most-traded name.

 AUTOLAUNCH: A LAUNCH RAIL FOR AGENT-OWNED, X402 ECONOMIES (6 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 Regents Labs built Autolaunch on Base, a fixed and non-upgradeable
system that bootstraps agent-owned tokens through Continuous Clearing
Auctions (CCAs) where bids are denominated in REGENT, then deploys a
Uniswap v4 pool at the auction's final clearing price with the LP NFT
sent to a dead address, making the initial position permanent. Each
launch mints 100 billion tokens split as 10% through the CCA, 5% to
initial liquidity, and 85% to a one-year vesting treasury. USDC
revenue passing through the canonical payment address is routed as 2%
to REGENT stakers off the top, 10% of the remaining 98% to AGENT-N
stakers, and the balance to the treasury. A 2% Uniswap v4 hook fee on
all swaps is split half to the agent treasury and half to REGENT
stakers. Agents accept roughly 10.8% of future USDC revenue as the
cost for a capital infusion within two days, with supply, fees, and
revenue routes all immutable after deployment. First autolaunches were
expected within a week of the announcement on August 27.

 SOCIAL TRADING AND THE GARDEN OF EDEN (8 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Social trading apps Fomo and Pump have reached 60K-100K daily active
users, matching Polymarket, Hyperliquid, and Phantom, while accounting
for roughly one-third of all builder code volume on Hyperliquid. Pump
has distributed over $450M to deployers, already 15% of Meta's 2025
creator payout total, with memecoin traders absorbing 2-3% per swap,
20-30x the cost of spot exchange fees. The central challenge for these
platforms is crossing the speculative onboarding funnel into broader
social finance use cases like tokenized stocks and stablecoin savings,
a transition complicated by brand names that signal risk-seeking
rather than wealth stewardship. Regardless of which app leads, their
aggregate volume flows to underlying infrastructure on Hyperliquid and
Solana, making that layer the structural winner of the social trading
race.

 PANTERA BLOCKCHAIN LETTER: THE BATTLE FOR 80 BILLION AGENT CUSTOMERS
(6 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Projections indicate that 8 billion people each operating about 10 AI
agents will produce 80 billion economic participants within a decade,
a shift that will influence $30 trillion in purchases by 2030. The
structural risk is AI sovereignty: platform-owned agents can
preference suppliers that pay the platform, hide competing products,
and extract a toll on every facilitated transaction, misaligning agent
owners with the platforms controlling agent behavior. Blockchains
address this by providing a persistent, neutral, programmable property
rights layer no single platform controls, with digital wallets serving
agents the way browsers served humans, providing identity, working
capital, spending approvals, and delegated authority with narrow
scopes. Coinbase's x402 protocol, which enables sub-$0.01 stablecoin
payments for API calls, is one of the payment primitives that allows
agents to transact at machine speed without human intermediation.

MISCELLANEOUS

 X402 PROCESSES 169M PAYMENTS IN YEAR ONE AS AWS, CLOUDFLARE, AND
GOOGLE INTEGRATE (4 MINUTE READ) [10]

 x402, which revives the dormant HTTP 402 status code as a stablecoin
payment rail for AI agents, processed 169 million payments across
around 590,000 buyers and 100,000 sellers in its first year. AWS added
native support to CloudFront, Cloudflare is running its own
facilitator and a Monetization Gateway, and Google embedded x402 into
the stablecoin layer of its Agent Payments Protocol, all without
Coinbase involvement. Coinbase transferred governance to the x402
Foundation under the Linux Foundation, positioning the standard as
neutral infrastructure rather than a proprietary Coinbase product. The
core economic argument is that AI agents, which may spin up, transact,
and terminate within seconds, cannot operate through bank accounts or
card networks built for human transaction timescales, making
HTTP-native stablecoin payments a structural requirement rather than a
preference.

 PURR JUMPS 15% AS HYPERLIQUID STRATEGIES UPDATES TREASURY (2 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 Hyperliquid Strategies disclosed a $1.9 billion HYPE treasury (29.3
million tokens) after deploying $773 million to acquire 16.5 million
additional tokens at an average cost of $46.77, sending PURR shares up
18% to $13.59 and pushing market cap to $1.8 billion. The company
holds $149.9 million in cash with zero debt and $709.9 million in
unrealized gains on staked HYPE positions, completing what CEO David
Schamis called a "fortress balance sheet." PURR's mNAV climbed to
1.35x, its highest since May, supported by HYPE's 77% gain during Q2
and over 50% rise in the prior month. Schamis framed the firm as a
long-term accumulation vehicle for a protocol he described as "winning
the parts of onchain finance that generate real fees," a positioning
that mirrors MicroStrategy's Bitcoin treasury model applied to a
DeFi-native asset.
