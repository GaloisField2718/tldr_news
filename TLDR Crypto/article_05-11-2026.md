 FRENCH BANK CRÉDIT MUTUEL ARKÉA LIKELY EXPLOITED FOR OVER $133
MILLION (3 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Crédit Mutuel Arkéa reportedly lost over $133M from centralized
exchange accounts, withdrawn as USDC and USDT. The attacker (unknown)
is swapping USDC to ETH, using instant exchanges, converting to XMR
and bridging. Possible OTC activity could push losses higher. The bank
hasn't confirmed or denied the exploit, so the claims are unverified.

 MORGAN STANLEY TOKENIZES US EQUITIES AND TREASURYS WITH DTCC (2
MINUTE READ) [5]

 Morgan Stanley tokenized US equities and Treasurys using DTCC's new
service, which opened last week with 20 participants, including
JPMorgan, Citi, Bank of America, Barclays, BNP Paribas, Fidelity,
State Street, and UBS. DTC holds $114T in custody, and the service was
built with input from 150+ firms, initially supporting Canton and a
private Besu network. Morgan Stanley now expects tokenized equities on
its ATS in Q1 2027, later than its prior H2 2026 target. NYSE/ICE are
also building a tokenized trading platform, with Broadridge,
Computershare, Equiniti, and Superstate signed as transfer agents.

INNOVATION & LAUNCHES

 SOLAR: PARADIGM'S 100X FASTER SOLIDITY COMPILER (2 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Paradigm's Solar is a new Solidity compiler that compiles contracts
over 100x faster than Solc, the current standard. Beyond compilation
speed, Solar produces more EVM-efficient bytecode than Solady without
requiring inline assembly, reducing on-chain execution costs for
deployed contracts. The compiler also resolves the Stack Too Deep
error, a long-standing constraint that forced Solidity developers to
restructure code or rely on workarounds. Solar is available now via
Foundry: run foundryup, then forge --use solar.

 MASTERCARD AND OPENAI BUILD THE PAYMENT RAILS FOR AI AGENTS (2 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 Mastercard formalized a strategic partnership with OpenAI anchored by
a four-product agentic commerce stack addressing payment trust, agent
trust, ecosystem trust, and merchant discoverability. The four
products are Agent Pay (tokenized payments for AI agents), Verifiable
Intent (buyer authorization capture), Agentic Trust Intelligence (bank
and merchant risk scoring for AI-initiated transactions), and Agent
Connect (merchant discovery by AI agents). Shopify's Shop Pay is among
the first live deployments. Mastercard is betting on network tokens
over one-time virtual card numbers as the core primitive. Visa,
Stripe, and PayPal are building parallel infrastructure. Holiday
shopping is expected to be the first high-volume stress test for
agentic payment rails at scale.

 SEC TOKEN TAXONOMY FAQ CLARIFIES DECENTRALIZATION STANDARDS FOR
BUILDERS (2 MINUTE READ) [8]

 The SEC released a new FAQ expanding its token taxonomy framework,
drawing on three clarifications flagged by a16z crypto General Counsel
Miles Jennings. Q 1.4 confirms that projects can maintain
decentralized governance while using legal entities like DUNAs
(Decentralized Unincorporated Nonprofit Associations), resolving a
structural question teams have faced when adding off-chain legal
wrappers to on-chain protocols. Q 1.5 draws a formal line between
permissioned and permissionless systems, and Q 1.6 separates
"functional" tokens from tokens that qualify as investment contracts
under securities law. Builders now have clearer criteria from the SEC
to determine whether their protocol architecture and token design fall
outside the agency's jurisdiction.

 CRYPTO PROTOCOLS ARE WINNING THE AI INFERENCE PRICE WAR (2 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 Centralized inference providers face structural pricing floors tied
to manual approval gates, regional electricity costs, and
capital-market margin requirements, creating an opening for
permissionless crypto routing layers. Surplus lets holders of excess
API credits from OpenAI, Anthropic, and DeepSeek sell at a discount
for USDC with no KYC, posting 57x token growth (10B to 570B
tokens/day) and 78x request growth (143k to 11.2M/day) over the past
90 days. Engy runs a global miner competition on hardware and
electricity efficiency, growing from 2.7B to 153B tokens/day while
buyback-directed revenue climbed from $363 to $8,000/day in the same
window. With the inference market at $160B/year and projected to reach
$350B, autonomous AI agents consuming 5x more tokens than humans will
concentrate purchasing power in the cheapest available providers,
positioning these protocols to capture a growing share of AI compute
spend as agent usage scales.

 AGENTIC COMMERCE OVERHYPED TODAY; UNDERHYPED FOR THE NEXT 3-6 MONTHS
(2 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Agentic commerce is overhyped on current usage but will be underhyped
on volume within 3-6 months, and products not optimized for agents
will be left behind. Agents still fail often (browser use is slow and
buggy, and credentials and payments carry friction), so 99% of
consumers churn at the first issue. Most consumers outside SF don't
use agents at all, and current products mainly serve high-income,
tech-forward users. Growth will likely come from ChatGPT (1.2B weekly
users) as it adds a wallet and in-chat purchases, with early data
showing AI-driven traffic converts better than non-AI traffic.

 KAMINO'S NO-EQUITY MODEL TESTS IF VALUE ACCRUAL IS DEFI'S LAST MOAT
(1 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Kamino Finance has structured $KMNO to eliminate the equity/token
split that has drained value from most DeFi protocols: the Kamino
Foundation holds all IP and captures 100% of revenue, including
institutional deals, with $KMNO as the sole ownership layer and no
separate equity class for founders, investors, or the team. CEO WeiszM
committed $20M over two years to expand Kamino into banks, brokerages,
and wallets, with his personal allocation vesting only if $KMNO hits
and holds a series of valuation milestones rather than on a standard
time schedule. This is a direct answer to the bank tokenization
challenge posed by products like StanChart's tokenized deposits in
Hong Kong: banks can earn spread from on-chain infrastructure but
cannot structurally route that upside to depositors, while a
foundation-owned revenue model can. User-owned value accrual, not
openness or permissionlessness, is DeFi's defensible moat as
traditional finance moves on-chain.
