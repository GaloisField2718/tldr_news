MARKETS & BUSINESS

 SPOT BITCOIN ETFS SEE LARGEST DAILY OUTFLOWS IN FOUR MONTHS (3 MINUTE
READ) [4]

 US spot bitcoin ETFs lost $643.3M on Friday, the largest outflow
since June 25. Spot ether ETFs lost $155.9M, their largest since
October 7, and $431.5M for the week. This followed the $6.3B Binance
hack, which the FBI attributes to North Korean hackers. Binance saw
$3.64B in net outflows in the first nine and a half hours after
reopening withdrawals Sunday. Analysts call the ETF move a
“sentiment shock” and a de-risking day, since ETF holders have no
direct Binance exposure and the outflow was under 1% of ETF assets.

 STRATEGY'S PREFERRED-STOCK FIX SETS UP RENEWED BITCOIN BUYING (3
MINUTE READ) [5]

 MSTR fell nearly 10% after Q3 results and traded at $129.90, down
about 27% this year. StoneX and TD Cowen kept buy ratings, with price
targets of $435 and $260. Strategy ended the quarter with $5.07B in
dollar assets, including a $4.83B cash reserve that covers about 3.2
to 3.4 years of preferred dividends and interest. It spent about $2B
buying back its STRC preferred, restoring it near its $100 par value.
The cost was bitcoin per share, which is down 3.7% this year.
Preferred holders approved daily dividend accrual, effective November
1 for STRC and January 1 for the rest.

 PERMAWEBOS: AO SHIPS 10 COMPONENTS FOR DECENTRALIZED COMPUTING (5
MINUTE READ) [6]

 AO shipped 10 components forming PermawebOS, including an Android
node runner that bundles for up.arweave[.]net with hardware-backed
keys and Verified Boot attestation, inference@1.0 bridging OpenAI,
Anthropic, OpenRouter, and Cerebras with on-device local inference,
and QEMU/Docker/andock support for full Linux VM execution inside AO
processes. HyperBEAM v0.11 closes 700+ issues with 2x-11x throughput
gains, ships LMDB 1.0, and finalizes AO-Core 1.0 alongside hashpaths
v2, which introduce ao:// URIs that record full computation provenance
for cross-node deduplication and verification. Agents running as AO
processes store mailbox, thoughts, and tool policy in on-chain message
logs, so no separate runtime holds a private conversation copy, and
node TLS certificates bind to the node's Arweave wallet key rather
than a traditional CA.

 KAST IS NOT LAUNCHING A TOKEN; POINTS ARE NON-SELLABLE “PIONEER
UNITS” (3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Per KAST's own FAQ, eligible Points convert to KAST Pioneer Units,
which aren't shares, can't be sold or transferred, and have “no
guaranteed cash value,” only a notional value that can rise or fall.
Cash-out is only planned, with no dates. Users who miss the in-app
acknowledgment deadline in the December update lose their KPUs with no
replacement. Eligibility depends on Points balance, KAST balance, and
country, and others may get non-withdrawable vouchers for eligible
spending. In July, the example was 10,000 Points worth $800 notional,
but KAST now gives no conversion rate. Points earning ends November
30, and details come in early December.

 THE CRYPTO END-GAME: BELIEF ASSETS AND CASHFLOW ASSETS (5 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 Crypto assets are either belief assets (value from shared conviction,
no cashflows) and cashflow assets (value from earnings), and token
creation is automated but durable demand is not. Belief assets share
five structural features: a cult figurehead, a near-death survival
that filters for devout holders, early backers made wealthy, a
meme-identity layer, and a one-liner rally cry. ZEC qualifies as an
emerging belief asset via its shielded-pool vulnerability survival,
30x run, and "private/quantum-proof bitcoin" positioning, with Pearl
(PRL) flagged as a possible "AI bitcoin" candidate. The endgame is a
two-tier structure: belief assets on top, cashflow assets underneath.

 AN ASYMMETRIC BET FOR YOUR INSTITUTIONAL FINANCE BRAND (3 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Five digital asset infrastructure companies pitch to the same fintech
founders while ignoring the risk leads, compliance officers, and CTOs
who gate institutional deals. The counter-move is niche media
positioning: stop publishing about your product and become the
searchable reference for your evaluator audience's hardest questions.
For a compliant onchain yield infrastructure seller, that means
long-form guides and newsletters helping risk teams assess yield
integrations, built around lawyers advising fintechs on yield and
treasury teams that have shipped integrations. The asymmetric payoff
comes from owning one specific question for two or more years while
competitors exhaust budget chasing founders who do not decide.

 VITALIK BUTERIN ON DIGITAL MINIMALISM (5 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Vitalik Buterin published an essay advocating "digital minimalism,"
the practice of choosing interfaces rather than accepting
platform-native defaults. After X's recommendation algorithm surfaced
pro-Hitler content and Grok produced antisemitic output in late 2024
and early 2025, he switched to Firefly, a multi-platform client
aggregating X, Bluesky, Farcaster, and Lens that bypasses X's
algorithmic feed. He reports the switch cut exposure to extremist
content and culture-war disputes without requiring him to abandon X.
The essay also criticizes "financial nihilism" in crypto circles, the
assumption that blockchains trend toward gambling, as a product of the
same engagement-optimized information diet warping broader online
discourse.
