 UTAH FILES CRIMINAL CHARGES AGAINST KALSHI AND EXECUTIVES (3 MINUTE
READ) [4]

 Utah's attorney general charged Kalshi, CEO Tarek Mansour, and COO
Luana Lopes Lara with promoting illegal online gambling, including
letting an undercover agent place wagers. It follows the state's civil
suit on October 20. The CFTC sued Utah on Tuesday to block enforcement
against prediction markets, and has sued at least 10 states. Kalshi's

didn't preempt state enforcement.

 TOKENIZED GOOGL DROPS NEARLY 12% ALONGSIDE AFTER-HOURS STOCK SELLOFF
(2 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Tokenized GOOGL fell nearly 12% on-chain as Alphabet shares sold off
after earnings, with a Uniswap pool processing roughly $603,000 in
volume over five minutes during the repricing. The near-instantaneous
price adjustment shows that on-chain tokenized equity pools can track
TradFi price events in real time, without waiting for the next trading
session. The $603,000 in five-minute Uniswap volume also reveals the
depth constraints of current tokenized equity infrastructure, where
thin liquidity amplifies price swings relative to the underlying
stock's exchange order book.

INNOVATION & LAUNCHES

 OKXICE OBJECTIONS RISE TO 33 AS STOCK-TOKEN LINEUP SHRINKS (3 MINUTE
READ) [6]

 OKX's tokenized equities venue OKXICE (xStocks) has received 33
issuer objections covering 31 of its 63 originally proposed symbols,
leaving only 32 symbols without a disclosed objection ahead of launch.
Public company issuers are opposing the listing of their equity
without explicit consent, forcing OKX to trim the tradable universe
before trading opens. The attrition exposes the consent gap RWA
stock-token platforms face when listing securities without formal
issuer authorization, a friction that could constrain the tokenized
equities market to willing issuers rather than the full public-equity
universe.

 VANECK LAUNCHES DIVERSIFIED ETF COMBINING STOCKS, BONDS AND BITCOIN
(3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 VanEck listed the Wealth Builder ETF (NYSE: WBMO), an actively
managed fund-of-funds blending stocks, bonds, gold, and real assets
with a 2% Bitcoin target (1%-5% permitted range), on NYSE, with BTC
exposure routed through a spot Bitcoin ETP rather than direct coin
holdings. The product converts VanEck's existing adviser-only Wealth
Builder model portfolios into a publicly tradeable wrapper, with
VanEck retaining discretion over allocations and rebalancing. VanEck
positions Bitcoin as a scarce, non-sovereign complement to gold for
purchasing-power protection, drawing on a $244.1 billion AUM base as
of September 30. The launch continues a distribution push that began
in March, when VanEck partnered with retirement platform Basic Capital
to offer its digital asset ETPs inside US employer 401(k) plans.

 MORE AMERICANS HAVE TRIED CRYPTO, BUT FEW ARE STICKING WITH IT (3
MINUTE READ) [8]

 A survey of 1,545 US adults, commissioned by the DeFi Education Fund,
shows lifetime crypto ownership rising to 22% from 18%, yet active
past-year usage held flat at 13%, pointing to a retention problem more
than an awareness gap. The lifetime figure grew because lapsed users
returned: among respondents who had not used crypto in the past year,
11% reported prior ownership, up from 5% a year ago, while new-user
entry did not accelerate. Trust in crypto exchanges (4% high trust)
and decentralized platforms (5%) sits far below the 42-48% high-trust
ratings earned by banks and credit unions, and DeFi familiarity
registers at just 2% "very familiar." Despite the GENIUS Act
establishing federal stablecoin rules, stated interest in learning
about stablecoins fell to 7% from 11%, and DeFi interest dropped to 8%
from 11%, showing that regulatory clarity has not translated into
consumer engagement.

 ETHENA PAY'S 12% EARN+: WHAT'S BEHIND THE YIELD (2 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Ethena Pay is only the interface, and funds move to an Upshift vault
curated by Steakhouse. The strategy supplies USDe on Aave V4 on
Avalanche, borrows USDT, swaps to more USDe, and repeats, at about 11x
leverage. Base USDe supply yield is about 0%, so the 12% comes almost
entirely from Merkl incentives and would be deeply negative without
them. Risks include liquidation if USDe or USDT dislocate (USDe
briefly hit about $0.65 on Binance in Oct. 2025), incentives ending or
diluting, and smart contract risk.

 WHAT YOU NEED TO SPEND FOR CRYPTO CARDS TO PAY FOR THEMSELVES (3
MINUTE READ) [10]

 This post compares six paid crypto card tiers against their free
versions to find the monthly spend needed to cover the annual fee. The
cheapest are MetaMask Metal ($199 a year, break-even $829 a month, 3%
vs 1% cashback capped at $10K a year) and Plasma One Core ($199, $900
a month, with rewards paid in XPL). In the middle are ether.fi Luxe
($199, $2,829 a month), ether.fi Pinnacle ($999, $5,530 a month) and
KAST Premium ($1,000, $5,667 a month). The extreme case is KAST
Private at $10,000 a year, which needs $28,778 a month, or over $345K
a year in card spending. KAST cashback can't be withdrawn and may
expire, and ether.fi and Plasma pay in tokens (ETHFI and XPL), so
token prices change your real return.

 BITCOIN DIPS BELOW $80,000 AS SAMSUNG-LED TECH JITTERS AND A HAWKISH
FED WEIGH ON CRYPTO (3 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Bitcoin fell from $81,700 to $79,800 during Thursday's Asian session
after Samsung shares dropped over 5% on a chip-division profit miss
(106.5 trillion won versus 108.5 trillion won expected), pulling the
Kospi down 2% and Nasdaq futures down 1.4%. The Federal Reserve's
hawkish hold at 3.75-4% added pressure, with Chair Kevin Warsh citing
persistent inflation and ING forecasting a December rate hike
following September's increase. $356 million in crypto futures
liquidated over 24 hours, including $205 million from long positions,
with BTC futures accounting for $94 million and ETH for $72 million.
Elevated real yields reduce the speculative capital pool, and a
December Fed hike would tighten that constraint into year-end.
