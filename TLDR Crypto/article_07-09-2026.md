 COINBASE FILES SEC AND CFTC REGISTRATIONS FOR SINGLE STOCK PERPS (2
MINUTE READ) [4]

 Coinbase filed SEC-notice registrations for a derivatives exchange
and broker, positioning the firm to offer single stock perpetual
futures to US traders under formal regulatory oversight. The
dual-regulator coordination with both the SEC and CFTC marks a
deliberate effort to pull a crypto-native derivative structure into
the US onshore market, expanding the range of instruments available
through a regulated US venue.

 LIQUID NETWORK PAUSED AFTER $320M "WHITE-HAT" WITHDRAWAL (2 MINUTE
READ) [5]

 ~4,000 BTC was withdrawn from the Bitcoin sidechain's federation
wallet by purported white-hat hackers via the SideSwap Peg-out
Authorization Key, which Liquid says was not itself compromised. An
on-chain message read, "We are whitehats, contact us on chain."
Blockstream paused the network, exchanges suspended LBTC
deposits/withdrawals, and other Liquid-issued assets were unaffected.
No confirmation yet on whether funds will be returned or a restart
timeline.

 PAYMENT CHANNELS: 1 MILLION PAYMENTS PER SECOND (5 MINUTE READ) [6]

 A new Solana primitive called payment channels addresses the per-call
signature bottleneck in agentic micropayment workflows by letting
agents authorize a spending ceiling once, meter usage off-chain via
signed messages at zero transaction cost, and settle the full session
in a single on-chain transaction across the x402 and MPP protocols.
Funds remain non-custodial throughout, held in program-controlled
escrow rather than operator balances, unlike prepaid credit systems
where balances are tracked off-chain by the service provider.
Benchmarks showed 100,000 unique wallets processing over 1 million
payments per second, equivalent to more than 80 billion payments in 24
hours at a processing cost of $0.0078 per million payments. The spec
is public, and the program is open source. Alibaba Cloud launched live
inference endpoints with payment channels on day one.

 WHAT 700+ X402-ENABLED API ENDPOINTS ARE TEACHING US ABOUT AGENT
COMMERCE (5 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Orthogonal has scaled its x402-based API marketplace to 700+
endpoints across 50+ providers, with commercial workflows in contact
enrichment, investor signal monitoring, and lead generation already
processing real agent transactions. Building production-grade agent
commerce on x402 rails required three layers the protocol does not
itself provide: refund management, request-time dynamic pricing, and
natural-language discoverability through the Bazaar catalog. An early
investor-signals deployment grounded 87% of surfaced company updates
within two weeks, up from near zero prior to the agent-driven
approach. Orthogonal's near-term roadmap targets governance and spend
controls, self-service API onboarding, and an enterprise control plane
for managing agent capabilities across providers.

 WHEN THROUGHPUT IS NOT ENOUGH: WHAT FINANCIAL MARKETS NEED FROM
BLOCKCHAINS (8 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Aggregate blockchain throughput has grown over 100x in five years,
with some production systems now clearing tens of thousands of TPS,
removing raw capacity as the primary barrier to onchain finance. The
conversation has shifted to market microstructure: millisecond
inclusion uncertainty forces market makers to widen spreads against
stale prices, and discretionary block-building gives MEV extraction
the same structural character as privileged order-flow access in
traditional markets. a16z researchers propose "Strong Chain Quality"
to prevent single-actor control over block inclusion by reserving
portions of each block for transactions surfaced through alternate
network paths, while protocol designers are evaluating timelock and
threshold encryption to hide transaction contents until settlement.
Predictable execution, unambiguous priority rules, resilient access,
and pre-execution confidentiality with post-execution transparency
together define what a settlement layer must deliver before onchain
finance works at institutional scale.

MISCELLANEOUS

 BITGO TO BUY NYDIG TRADING ARM FOR $42.5M IN CASH AND STOCK (2 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 BitGo has agreed to acquire NYDIG IF Holdings, the institutional
trading arm of Stone Ridge's bitcoin-focused subsidiary NYDIG, for
$42.5 million in cash and stock plus a $15 million earnout. The deal
is structured as $7 million in cash and approximately $35.5 million in
BitGo equity, meaning the target receives roughly 83% of the base
consideration in stock, with total consideration reaching $57.5
million if earnout targets are met. The acquisition adds institutional
trading infrastructure to BitGo's existing custody and settlement
stack, positioning the firm to serve a broader slice of the
institutional client workflow under one roof.

 BLOCK'S PATH TO BECOMING A PAYMENT NETWORK (2 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Block is assembling the pieces to become a full payment network
rivaling Visa/Mastercard. It already has millions of Square merchants
and 60M+ daily Cash App users, and its new "Neighborhoods" feature can
route payments directly between Block-owned accounts via Cash App Pay,
bypassing card networks and charging just 1% while still profiting.
Future phases could extend Cash App Pay to non-Square merchants
(leveraging fraud data from Cash App's user base) and eventually make
it an official debit network accepting all cards, positioning Block as
a genuine fifth payment network.
