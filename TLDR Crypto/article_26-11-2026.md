 COINBASE AND STATE DEPARTMENT HOST DUBLIN DPRK CRYPTO SYMPOSIUM (2
MINUTE READ) [4]

 Coinbase and the US State Department co-sponsored a two-day Dublin
symposium on North Korean cyber threats to crypto, joined by Global
Affairs Canada, Google, and Payward. FBI, FinCEN, and Ireland's Garda
National Cyber Crime Bureau participated in sessions covering two DPRK
threat vectors: crypto heists funneling proceeds into weapons programs
and fake IT workers using false identities to secure remote positions
at crypto firms. The event consolidates US, Canadian, and Irish law
enforcement with major crypto and tech companies into a shared
operational framework for countering state-sponsored financial crime.

 PUSH TO SCRAP SEC'S REGULATION CRYPTO AND INNOVATION EXEMPTION (3
MINUTE READ) [5]

 Senators Warren, Van Hollen, and Tina Smith, with other Banking
Committee members, sent SEC Chair Atkins two letters demanding the
agency withdraw its crypto offering rules and its tokenized stock
"innovation exemption." The August proposal exempts crypto offerings
up to $5M for four years and up to $75M for one year from
registration. The five-year innovation exemption would keep tokenized
securities venues from being treated as exchanges, which the senators
say creates a "shadow stock market" that fragments liquidity and
invites illicit finance.

INNOVATION & LAUNCHES

 BACKPACK LAUNCHES OPEN RFQ MARKETPLACE FOR STOCK ORDER FLOW (3 MINUTE
READ) [6]

 Backpack Exchange launched an RFQ system for stocks, letting any
market maker submit two-sided quotes that compete with Backpack's own
executing broker on each order. The system combines native Backpack
liquidity with traditional finance liquidity, routing retail stock
orders to whichever counterparty offers the best price at execution
time. Open access means any market maker can plug in without special
arrangements, increasing competitive pressure on each fill across
Backpack's stock order flow. The design mirrors RFQ mechanisms common
in traditional equity markets, where fragmented liquidity pools
compete on price rather than posting to a single central order book.

 OKXICE OPENS 24/7 STOCK TRADING WITH SIX LIVE TOKENS (3 MINUTE READ)
[7]

 OKX launched OKXICE, a tokenized stock trading platform routing six
equity tokens through Uniswap v4 pools for 24/7 on-chain trading. The
inaugural pools held $1.22 million in liquidity after the first day,
and OKX has confirmed a rollout pipeline of 113 total assets including
the current listings. The Uniswap v4 integration makes these tokens
composable with existing DeFi protocols, extending tokenized equities
beyond isolated wrappers into natively on-chain instruments.

 AAVE VS MORPHO: AN ASSET ISSUER'S GUIDE (9 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Aave V4 pools liquidity in Hubs that Spokes borrow from up to a cap,
so unrecovered debt becomes a Hub deficit shared by all suppliers of
that asset, while Morpho's isolated markets with five fixed parameters
keep bad debt inside one market and the curator vault that funded it.
Morpho holds about $11.3B in deposits against $15.8B on Aave V3, and
during April's Kelp rsETH exploit, Aave lost $8.45B in deposits over
48 hours while Morpho's losses stayed in rsETH-exposed markets.
Morpho's liquidation formula gives a 62.5% LLTV market such as
Robinhood's NVDA a 12.7% incentive and 2.7x theoretical leverage,
versus 2.6% and 11.8x at 91.5% LLTV. Recommended venues by asset:
Morpho first for yield-bearing stablecoins with Aave for scale,
wherever borrowers are for payment stablecoins, Horizon for credit
RWAs, and the distribution partner's venue for tokenized stocks, such
as Coinbase's Aave V4 Spoke and Robinhood's Morpho markets.

 HOW TO MARKET TOKENIZED ASSETS (6 MINUTE READ) [11]

 RWA products are good, but few people outside crypto know about them
because marketing speaks to crypto insiders. Lead with the problem
rather than the technology, explain what being onchain adds, and
market beyond Crypto Twitter through YouTube, TikTok, finance
podcasts, and creators. Partnering with fintechs, neobanks, and
brokers that already have users lets the blockchain stay invisible.
Content should explain where yield comes from, what backs the asset,
who is responsible, and what the risks are, since trust is part of the
product. Tokenization is early, like cards or Apple Pay once were.
