 CFTC WILL ACT ON CRYPTO REGARDLESS OF CLARITY BILL (1 MINUTE READ)
[4]

 CFTC Chair Brian Selig announced the agency will invoke its existing
statutory authorities to establish a crypto asset markets regulatory
regime if the CLARITY Act continues to stall due to Democratic
opposition in Congress. The statement positions the CFTC to set market
structure rules unilaterally, bypassing the legislative process rather
than waiting on comprehensive crypto legislation. Coinbase CEO Brian
Armstrong said crypto regulatory clarity will arrive through one of
two paths: a Senate bill requiring 60+ votes on September 15, or new
CFTC and SEC rules on September 16.

 BITCOIN RECORDS LARGEST WEEKLY DOLLAR GAIN IN HISTORY (4 MINUTE READ)
[5]

 Bitcoin gained $14,264 in a single week to close at $77,387 (up
22.7%), its largest-ever dollar gain, driven by the Treasury's buyback
expansion. The Crypto Fear & Greed Index hit 78, its highest since
December 2024, while spot bitcoin ETFs pulled in $1.92 billion in net
inflows for the week ended Aug 21, the strongest since October 2025.
Strive CEO Matt Cole called bitcoin's simultaneous breakout against
both the dollar and gold (bitcoin-to-gold ratio now 16.73oz, highest
since May) a sign of the strongest cycle yet, tied to a broader hunt
for scarcity in an AI-driven world.

 PER-WALLET 2FA CONTROL AND WALLET SECRET EXPORT (3 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Infinex has launched per-wallet 2FA controls, allowing users to
assign distinct security postures to individual wallets rather than
applying a single account-level setting. Wallets with 2FA enabled form
a security zone where intra-zone transfers require no authentication
code, while moving funds to wallets outside the zone triggers
verification, enabling a hot/cold wallet split within one interface.
Infinex also added wallet secret export, letting users retrieve seed
phrases or private keys after passkey signing and 2FA confirmation,
with the secret rendering inside Turnkey's hosted export iframe and
decryptable only within Turnkey's secure enclave, so neither Infinex
nor Turnkey can access the plaintext. Together, these features resolve
the friction trade-off that uniform account-level 2FA imposed on
active DeFi users who need both custody security and frictionless
daily transaction flow.

 SOLANA CUTS MAINNET SLOT TIME TO 350 MILLISECONDS (3 MINUTE READ) [7]


 Solana's Friday mainnet upgrade cut target slot time from 400ms to
350ms under proposal SIMD-0525, which plans further cuts to a final
200ms target in three more 50ms stages. The change reduces
confirmation latency but doesn't increase throughput, since each slot
now carries proportionally less work. Full finality remains far slower
(~12.8 seconds today) pending the separate Alpenglow overhaul, which
aims to eventually bring finality down to about 150ms.

 ONSHORING HYPERLIQUID: THE COMPLIANT PATH TO US MARKETS (5 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 Hyperliquid remains geoblocked from US markets because its
permissionless infrastructure conflicts with CEA requirements for
registered DCMs, DCOs, and FCMs, the exchange, clearinghouse, and
broker roles that TradFi separates across entities. HyperCore's
modular stack mirrors this separation: the protocol handles matching
and settlement, deployers set contract specs (staking 500k HYPE bonds
and earning up to half the fees their markets generate), and builders
onboard users as FCM equivalents. The Hyperliquid Policy Center has
lobbied the CFTC and SEC to allow regulated firms to access HyperCore
through permissioned HIP-3 DEXs, bearing KYC/AML obligations as
builders and deployers. Trump's announcement validating this onshoring
approach means US traders may gain access to Hyperliquid's perpetuals
infrastructure through regulated wrapper products rather than waiting
for the protocol itself to register.

 TIMING THE HEAD IN ETHEREUM POS (5 MINUTE READ) [10]

 New research on Ethereum's PoS consensus identifies two attack
vectors that let adversarial proposers exploit head vote timing in
LMD-GHOST to strip honest validators of attestation rewards. The
head-vote timing game has a proposer delay block broadcast to near the
4-second mark in a 12-second slot, pushing honest validators to attest
the parent block instead. Prysm experiments measured a 15.06% average
total reward loss for honest participants under this manipulation. A
second vector, the K-block withholding attack, allows adversaries
holding consecutive proposer slots to extend a private competing chain
and release it to trigger reorganizations, voiding honest attestation
rewards on the reorganized blocks. Researchers propose a
distance-weighted head reward mechanism that grants partial credit for
attestations pointing to recent canonical ancestors, reducing
timing-game losses from 15.06% to 4.71% without modifying core
consensus rules.

 BOUNCEBIT TO PERMANENTLY SHUT DOWN ITS L1 AFTER 286.5M BB EXPLOIT (2
MINUTE READ) [11]

 BounceBit is permanently shutting down its Evmos-based L1 after a
protocol-level authorization flaw allowed an attacker to drain
approximately 286.5 million BB tokens from nine mainnet accounts, with
no private keys or wallets compromised. The YZi Labs-backed project,
whose 2024 seed round was co-led by Blockchain Capital and Breyer
Capital, will reissue BB as a BEP-20 token on BNB Chain using a
pre-exploit snapshot. The 286.5 million exploited tokens will not be
recognized in the new issuance, zeroing out the attacker's position.
CeDeFi, Prime, and RWA products operated by the team were unaffected
by the breach.
