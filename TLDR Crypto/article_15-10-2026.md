MARKETS & BUSINESS

 BITMEX SUED IN UK FOR RETURN OF $27M BTC OVER UNLICENSED DERIVATIVES
(3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 A British software engineer has filed suit against BitMEX operator
HDR Global Trading in London's Commercial Court, seeking return of
324.8 BTC (~$27.2M) on the grounds that BitMEX conducted regulated
derivatives activity in the UK without FCA authorization. The claim,
filed under the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, targets 1,053
losing perpetual swap trades across five accounts from 2018 to 2022,
arguing those contracts are unenforceable against UK residents who
dealt with an unauthorized firm. A deceit claim accompanies the
restitution suit, alleging BitMEX knew it lacked authorization while
representing to clients it could lawfully trade with them. Barber's
legal team at Seladore Legal suggests other UK users entered similar
contracts, putting total potential exposure at hundreds of millions of
pounds, with BitMEX having closed operations on September 23.

 STRIVE BUYS 3K BITCOIN FOR $280M, NOW FIFTH-LARGEST CORPORATE HOLDER
(3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Strive acquired 3,311 BTC between October 5 and 9 for $280M at an
average of ~$84,500, bringing its total treasury to 32,773 BTC and
ranking it fifth among publicly traded corporate Bitcoin holders. The
purchase was financed through its Variable Rate Series A Perpetual
Preferred Stock (SATA), alongside a $208M warrant exercise, with cash
reserves growing to $310.3M through the period. Bitcoin holdings
expanded 11.2% against a 9.4% rise in effective common share count,
benefiting existing shareholders on a per-BTC basis. CEO Matt Cole has
targeted second-largest public corporate holder by year-end, though
Strive currently trails Metaplanet by 11,227 BTC.

INNOVATION & LAUNCHES

 RAIN LAUNCHES EMBEDDED STABLECOIN WALLETS, FILES FOR OCC NATIONAL
TRUST CHARTER (3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Rain launched embedded stablecoin wallets supporting USDC, USDT, and
other assets across 12 chains, including Base and Solana, paired with
Rain-issued Visa and Mastercard cards accepted at 175 million merchant
locations and USD payouts to 80+ countries. The launch follows Rain's
acquisition of stored-value platform Ansa two months ago and an OCC
national trust bank charter filing eight days prior, moves that shift
the customer relationship away from third-party wallet infrastructure
providers like Dynamic, Privy, and Turnkey. Rain's full stack spans
card processing via Lithic, Episode Six, and Paymentology, direct Visa
and Mastercard scheme memberships via Puerto Rico, and receivables
financing through Credit Coop. An approved OCC charter would unlock
custody monetization on top of a model that combines stablecoin rails
with traditional finance licenses to function as a full-service card
issuer.

 AGENT-NATIVE ELIXIR STACK WITH X402 PAYMENTS AND WEBMCP (5 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Regents Labs released an open-source Elixir/Phoenix/Ash stack for
building agent-native applications, bundling WebMCP for exposing page
tools to AI agents, SIWA for agent-specific authentication and sybil
resistance, and x402 stablecoin payments for monetizing agent
interactions. The stack separates agent and human identity layers,
with Privy handling shared human accounts across five production sites
already running the architecture. An open-source bootstrapping
template called ash-template lets developers spin up compatible apps
with payments, compute, and identity pre-wired, with Elixir chosen
over TypeScript/React for cost and horizontal scaling at agent
volumes. The platform distributes all revenue from its five properties
to $REGENT token stakers, operating without VC backing.

GUIDES & TUTORIALS

 THE DEATH OF THE INTERFACE (6 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Capable LLMs and stablecoin legislation enacted on two continents
(the US GENIUS Act, effective January, and Europe's MiCA, now in full
force) have converged. As AI agents displace app-based interfaces,
today's payment infrastructure breaks down because its risk stack
(authentication, fraud, disputes, and liability) assumes a human
completing each transaction step, a premise that fails entirely in
agent-to-agent commerce. David Marcus proposes "headless money" as the
required fix: a stablecoin-settled, API-only payment layer combining
embedded wallets, fiat connectivity, and card networks that agents can
call without any frontend. Lightspark, which he leads, is building
toward this stack, with the commercial logic that whoever controls
agent-mediated purchase decisions captures marketplace-level economics
rather than the 1-300 bps of a conventional payment rail.

 PAIR TOKENS: AUTO-COMPOUNDING ERC-20 LP POSITIONS VIA UNISWAP V4
HOOKS (3 MINUTE READ) [12]

 StonkBrokers introduced Pair Tokens, a new ERC-20 primitive built on
Uniswap v4 hooks that fuses two underlying assets into a single
fungible token representing a full-range liquidity pool position.
Unlike standard LP NFTs, Pair Tokens auto-compound through a
permissionless compound function that reinvests accumulated trading
fees back into the position, removing the need for manual fee
harvesting. The ERC-20 format preserves composability, allowing Pair
Tokens to be traded onchain, LPed, or integrated with launchpads,
while a 10% fee on compounded LP fees funds $STONKBROKER token
buybacks. Minting and redeeming are live on Robinhood Chain via the
StonkBrokers platform as of October 13.

 AAVE V4 DEPOSITS ARE CONCENTRATED IN PARTNER-BUILT HUBS (2 MINUTE
READ) [13]

 About 59% of deposits in Aave's new V4 markets sit in three
partner-focused hubs (EtherFi Cash, Global Dollar, and Coinbase
Stocks), together holding about $409M of the roughly $695M across 10
new markets. EtherFi's Cash hub alone holds $311.5M, nearly 45% of Q3
V4 deposits, and the DAO gets 20% of its revenue with no operating
costs. Sentora has proposed a similar curated structure with a 50/50
revenue split, pending governance approval.

 PREDICTION MARKET DATA RANKS F1 AS THE MOST DRAMATICALLY VOLATILE
SPORT (2 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Dragonfly Capital managing partner Haseeb Qureshi applied prediction
market data to rank sports by drama, measuring minute-by-minute odds
volatility as the metric for suspense rather than scoreboard activity.
The methodology treats real drama as outcome probabilities shifting in
real-time, discounting action that does not move win probability. F1
ranked first, followed by CS2, soccer, basketball, tennis, American
football, and baseball, with golf last by a wide margin. The framework
demonstrates prediction markets as a quantitative layer for analyzing
real-world phenomena, well beyond their primary use in financial price
discovery.
