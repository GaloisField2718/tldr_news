 MARA POSTS $634 MILLION Q3 NET PROFIT ON BITCOIN GAINS (3 MINUTE
READ) [5]

 MARA Holdings posted Q3 net profit of $634.4 million, up from $123.1
million a year earlier, as nearly $885 million in fair-value gains on
digital assets offset a 37% drop in mining revenue driven by the April
halving, pushing total revenue down 35% year-over-year to $164.2
million. After quarter-end, the company sold 13,597 BTC for roughly
$1.1 billion, using $750 million to fully retire its
bitcoin-collateralized loans and cutting total debt 37% year-to-date
to $1.4 billion. MARA slipped to fifth among corporate bitcoin
treasuries after Strive passed it, and is redirecting from
infrastructure expansion toward securing customer leases, free cash
flow generation, and AI/high-performance computing. CEO Fred Thiel
framed the shift as moving from asset accumulation to execution
discipline.

 NYSE SELECTS AVALANCHE AS BLOCKCHAIN FOR TOKENIZED US STOCK
SETTLEMENT (2 MINUTE READ) [6]

 The New York Stock Exchange selected Avalanche as the exclusive
settlement blockchain for a new 24/7 tokenized US equities trading
platform. NYSE cited Avalanche's transaction speed, throughput, and
compliance capabilities as key selection criteria. The platform
targets a Q4 launch pending regulatory approval, and will offer
instant settlement with stablecoin-based funding. The selection places
AVAX at the core of NYSE's equity tokenization infrastructure, marking
a direct integration between the US' largest stock exchange and a
public layer-1 network for securities settlement.

 SECURITIZE PUSHES INTO ASSET-BACKED FINANCE WITH RIVER ONE
ACQUISITION (3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Securitize agreed to acquire River One, a New York institutional
financing firm backed by Framework Ventures and Maple Finance, to
extend its tokenization platform into the $6.1 trillion asset-backed
finance market. The deal adds warehouse financing capabilities,
allowing clients to borrow against pooled loans ahead of refinancing,
as the ABF market is projected to exceed $9 trillion by 2029 per KKR.
Q3 results released alongside the announcement showed revenue of $18.6
million (+13% YoY), average tokenized assets of $5 billion (+39%), and
transaction volume of $4.3 billion (+20%), with $300 million in cash
and no debt on the balance sheet. The acquisition moves Securitize
beyond fund and equity tokenization into private credit's largest
sub-segment, deepening its utility for institutional clients including
BlackRock, Apollo, KKR, Hamilton Lane, and VanEck.

GUIDES & TUTORIALS

 YOUR PAYMENT PROCESSOR HAS A PAYMENT PROCESSOR (7 MINUTE READ) [8]

 This article examines the terms of three merchants of record (Whop,
Commas, and Dodo Payments) after seeing merchants report frozen funds
and shut accounts. None explicitly names the processor or acquiring
bank, so a decision made several layers up can freeze a merchant who
has no direct relationship with the decision-maker. Terms allow holds
of 180 days or longer, Whop reserves up to 100% for up to 180 days,
and Commas calls some risk decisions final. Other sellers' fraud or
disputes can also tighten rules for everyone on the platform. Although
the MoR model works and saves real compliance work, the label covers
different arrangements, so merchants should ask who processes
payments, how risk is assessed, what the reserve terms are, who can
release funds, and plan for a long hold.

 ETH AS STORE OF VALUE: WHY VITALIK IS SHIFTING THE NARRATIVE (3
MINUTE READ) [9]

 Vitalik Buterin contends that ETH's value proposition is better
grounded in store-of-value than in fee-burning mechanics, arguing that
optimizing for burn would require trade-offs that undermine
decentralization and capture resistance. EIP-1559's burn mechanism
stays intact, but Buterin treats it as incidental, stating fee burn
below 10% of breakeven is acceptable long-term since ETH's value comes
from elsewhere. The SoV case rests on Ethereum's CROPS properties,
which produce protocol-level stability, the attribute users placing
assets in the network value most. Buterin cites the AI disruption era
as the demand context where protocol-level stability becomes a
concrete SoV advantage for ETH.

 OPENFX PAYMENT VOLUME GREW FROM $1B TO $100B ANNUALIZED IN 2 YEARS (3
MINUTE READ) [10]

 OpenFX CEO Prabhakar Reddy says the company started by converting
currencies and delivering funds in under an hour, 24/7. After seeing
customers park money with it overnight, it launched Global Accounts
and Send in October so customers can hold funds and pay anyone in its
markets. He says revenue grew faster than volume despite lower prices,
and that licenses in the US, EU, UK, Canada, and UAE took two years.
He expects cross-border settlement to consolidate into one or two
companies, wants to be one of them, and hints at credit as a third
layer. The company has nearly 200 staff and is hiring.

 THE HOUSE OF AVALANCHE: CHAINS, FUNDS, CARDS, DEEDS, AND CLEARING (1
MINUTE READ) [11]

 Chain INK frames Avalanche as five layers. Consensus saw Helicon cut
block verification to 2ms in September. There are 71 active L1s,
including Evergreen, Spruce, Dinari, and FIFA. BlackRock's BUIDL
reached $486.5M on Avalanche in Q3, with Apollo, Franklin, and Janus
Henderson also onchain. Payments include Wyoming's FRNT stable token,
the Avalanche Card (record $10M Q3 spend), and Bergen County property
deeds. On November 9, NYSE picked Avalanche for tokenized stock
clearing, pending approval. The opening line notes Schwab listing SOL
and LINK as well. Pro-AVAX framing, with figures unverified.
