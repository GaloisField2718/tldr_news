 BITCOIN ETFS DRAW $528 MILLION AS ETHER FUNDS SNAP 5-DAY OUTFLOW

 STARKNET SUPPORT BECOMING AN INDEPENDENT POST-QUANTUM LAYER 1 (3
MINUTE READ) [5]

 More than 93.5% of voting power, across 2,800+ wallets, backed
researching a move from an Ethereum L2 to an independent post-quantum
L1 in a seven-day Snapshot poll that closed Thursday. StarkWare did
not vote. A draft whitepaper is expected in November, and STRK holders
must vote again on any actual transition. Starknet's hash-based
cryptography lets accounts adopt post-quantum signatures without
protocol changes, but it inherits Ethereum's security through its
bridge and data availability, so it is only as quantum-safe as
Ethereum.

 SOLANA'S BIGGEST NETWORK CHANGE SINCE 2020 IS LIVE (2 MINUTE READ)
[7]

 Solana activated Alpenglow on Wednesday. It targets finality of about
150 milliseconds under normal conditions, down from several seconds.
Validators now send votes directly to each other and combine approvals
into certificates, so a block can finalize after one or two voting
rounds. Before, votes were recorded as transactions across successive
blocks. The handover took about 900 ms, and 446 of 654 validators
voted on the starting block. Apps often showed payments as confirmed
before full settlement, so the change mainly gives stronger assurance.
Exchanges could credit deposits sooner, and shops could confirm
stablecoin payments faster. The 150 ms figure is a target, not a
guarantee, and Anza said some computers receive finality information
later than voting validators. Wallets, fees, and how apps run are
unchanged. Validator votes no longer appear as transactions, so
dashboards will show a sharp drop in transaction totals even though
user activity hasn't changed.

 CONFIDENTIAL INFERENCE: THE PRIVACY LAYER FOR ONCHAIN AI WORKLOADS (4
MINUTE READ) [8]

 Confidential inference is a sub-sector of onchain AI focused on
keeping model inputs and outputs private from infrastructure
providers, using trusted execution environments (TEEs), zero-knowledge
proofs, or fully homomorphic encryption. As protocols like Gensyn,
Ritual, and Hyperbolic expand onchain compute capacity, enterprise
users are demanding privacy guarantees that standard GPU inference
pipelines cannot provide. The economic structure supports a
premium-fee tier above commodity inference, with operators running
TEE-secured hardware positioned to charge more for provable privacy.
Enterprise AI deployment requirements and data-handling regulations
are expected to drive further adoption into 2027, as confidential
compute shifts from a niche offering to a standard procurement
requirement.

 XMTP SHUTS DOWN HOSTED NETWORK, GOES OPEN-SOURCE IN EARLY 2027 (4
MINUTE READ) [9]

 XMTP Labs is shutting down its hosted messaging network in early 2027
and transitioning to open-source, self-hosted software, after
admitting its early bet on Web3 identity, wallets, and a token failed
to generate developer demand. The project has confirmed no token will
launch, and developers can run the full stack, inspect it, and set
their own policies. CTO Nick Molnar consolidated the backend into a
single Rust binary supporting attachments, push notifications,
handles, and signed webhooks, with SDKs available for mobile, web,
servers, and agents without a wallet requirement. Self-hosted
deployments are isolated by default, and agents from different
companies can coordinate when connected to the same backend instance.

MISCELLANEOUS

 COINBASE FILES COMMENT LETTER ON SEC'S PROPOSED REGULATION CRYPTO
ASSETS (2 MINUTE READ) [10]

 The SEC under Chair Paul Atkins has proposed "Regulation Crypto
Assets," a framework designed to create clear regulatory pathways for
crypto tokens with disclosure-based protections for retail
participants. Coinbase Chief Policy Officer Faryar Shirzad filed an
official comment letter backing the proposal, signaling active
industry engagement with the rulemaking process. The filing stands in
contrast to the Gensler era, when the SEC pursued exchanges through
enforcement actions rather than seeking comment on proposed
frameworks. A finalized rule could establish a disclosure-based
compliance path for token issuers targeting US retail distribution,
replacing enforcement as the de facto regulatory mechanism.

 AERODROME AND VELODROME MERGE INTO AERO, LIVE ON 7 NETWORKS (3 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 Aerodrome and Velodrome merged into Aero on October 21, going live
across seven networks including Ethereum, Arbitrum, and Robinhood
Chain under a single AERO token. The protocol conducted no token sale
and took no venture backing. The full upgraded supply was distributed
to existing AERO, veAERO, VELO, and veVELO holders, split 94.5% to the
Aerodrome community and 5.5% to Velodrome, weighted by each protocol's
share of combined revenue over the prior 52 weeks. All exchange
revenue flows to sAERO stakers, who allocate AERO rewards to liquidity
markets, while LP incentives are capped to a multiple of projected
pool revenue so uncapped tokens go unminted. The combined protocol has
distributed over $580 million to stakers since inception, and ships
Slipstream V3 concentrated liquidity with MEV recapture and metaswaps
that bridge, route, and swap in a single transaction.
