 ETHEREUM STRAWMAP 2026 UPDATE: QUANTUM SAFETY, PRIVACY, ZKZK FRAMES
(3 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Vitalik Buterin updated Ethereum's 2026 Strawmap with seven
categories absent from the 2023 roadmap, including native rollups
enabled by SNARK maturity, blob and gas futures, and zkzk frames that
would expose a non-EVM ISA (leading candidates: leanISA and RISC-V) at
the protocol level, with the EVM becoming an intermediate
representation on top rather than an enshrined feature. Quantum safety
was elevated to a first-class priority, backed by leanSPHINCS
signatures and post-quantum aggregation, while privacy received
protocol-level treatment via keyed nonces, recent roots, FOCIL
components, lean privacy pools, and wormholes. Recursive STARKs now
span the execution, consensus, and data layers through a shared
"aggregate to union verified dependencies" primitive, with full formal
verification of the lean spec made tractable by modern AI tooling.
VDFs and many EVM improvements were deprioritized, and state expiry
was replaced by new state types offering specialized, scaling-friendly
mechanisms for high-volume use cases such as token transfers, swaps,
and privacy protocols.

 EIP-8363: THE UNMEASURED COST TO ONCHAIN CREDIT (5 MINUTE READ) [7]

 EIP-8363 tapers consensus rewards to zero net issuance at 60.25M ETH
staked (~half the supply), cutting total validator income ~50% at
current stake levels, while leaving MEV untouched, which increases its
share of validator revenue and strengthens incentives for aggressive
extraction. The EIP's own authors acknowledge that LST looping drives
most ETH lending yield, yet neither the proposal nor its cited
research models the credit markets priced off the staking rate, where
compressed spreads would cause borrowers to repay WETH debt, reduce
lending utilization, and push deposit APY down faster than staking
yield itself. With LSTs comprising ~40% of all onchain TVL, $2.6B in
stablecoin debt collateralized by ETH/LST, and $11.4B in ETF and
treasury inflows holding 7.8M ETH on corporate balance sheets, the
taper puts a large portion of Ethereum's TVL lead at risk without
modeled justification. Ship MEV burn first and model
staking-yield-dependent lending markets before revisiting issuance.
Post-Merge net ETH issuance already runs at 0.30%/year, below BTC
(0.82%), gold (1.7%), and M2 expansion (5.5%).

 ROBINHOOD CHAIN: CAN IT MOVE ROBINHOOD'S BOTTOM LINE? (7 MINUTE READ)
[8]

 Robinhood's Q2 2026 crypto revenue fell 38% YoY to $100M, dropping to
8% of total revenue and an all-time low 7% share of AUC, as retail
crypto volume hit its lowest level since Q3 2024 and institutional
Bitstamp volume surpassed retail for the first time. Robinhood Chain
launched July 1 and led all L2s tracked by growthepie in its first
month with $3.6M in REV, outpacing Polygon and Base, though memecoins
drove 51% of on-chain spot volume while RWAs accounted for just 5%.
Known annualized chain revenue totals roughly $54.8M ($43.2M REV,
$10.5M from USDG yield on $333.1M market cap, plus a Lighter perps
share), about 14% of Robinhood's $400M annualized crypto revenue,
leaving the chain immaterial to the bottom line. Morpho, integrated
into the main Robinhood app, reached the chain's 3rd-largest TVL
deployment within one month, while Lighter's wallet-only perps
integration attracted just 0.2% of its volume despite $25M in LIT
token incentives, indicating that main-app distribution is the
variable that determines whether chain activity scales.

 5 CHARTS: HOW CRYPTO CARDS ARE DRIVING STABLECOIN SPEND (3 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 Crypto payment card volume reached $759 million in July, up 2.5x
year-over-year from $306 million and from sub-$1 million at tracking
inception in October 2023, across nearly 9 million transactions
averaging $86 each, per Paymentscan. Settlement now concentrates on
Optimism (29%), Solana (19%), and Base (19%), while Gnosis has dropped
to roughly 2% after previously dominating as the home of Gnosis Pay.
Dollar-backed stablecoins have displaced euro alternatives: USDC
handles 58% of card spend and USDT 26%, compared to 48% and 7% a year
ago respectively, while EURe's share collapsed from 88% in early 2024
to about 2% today. Cards convert stablecoins to local fiat at point of
sale via Visa rails, allowing holders to access dollar accounts
without a traditional bank account, though the category's monthly
volume remains orders of magnitude below the trillions processed by
legacy card networks.

 WHAT MASTERCARD ACTUALLY BOUGHT IN BVNK (3 MINUTE READ) [10]

 On-chain analysis of Mastercard's $1.8B BVNK acquisition (closed
August 3) shows $32.9 billion in cumulative on-chain flow since
October 2024, with trailing-twelve-month volume of $23 billion against
$30 billion in reported annualized volume, close enough to suggest
most flow is real settlement. However, growth has plateaued as
although Q1 2026 volume was 2.6x the prior year, Q2 fell to 1.6x and
dipped below Q1 in absolute terms, with monthly flow peaking at $2.37
billion in March and holding near $1.9 billion since. Ethereum now
carries 62% of monthly flow versus Tron, while Base and Arbitrum
combined account for just 0.6% of all volume BVNK has ever moved,
despite two years of L2 payment narratives.

 EXPLORING THE OUTLOOK FOR BITCOIN (8 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Bitcoin ETFs have logged net $5B in outflows over 52 weeks while the
DRAM memory ETF pulled $10B in a single month, quantifying the demand
deficit that has pinned BTC below its 200-week EMA and around the 2021
ATH. On-chain and derivatives metrics cluster in deep-value territory:
MVRV is at accumulation-zone levels, spot turnover relative to market
cap sits at all-time lows, front-end implied volatility is at
multi-year lows, and BTC futures basis cannot clear T-bill yield
parity. The DAT supply overhang that pressured price through late 2024
and into 2025 is unwinding as treasury companies slow or reverse BTC
accumulation, while long-term holders absorb supply aggressively.
Recovery is a seller exhaustion event rather than a demand-led rally.
Large asset manager portfolio allocations are the primary upside
catalyst. DCA spot or buy cheap implied volatility via options over
the coming months.
