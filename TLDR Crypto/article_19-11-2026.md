 SOLANA FOUNDATION ACQUIRES MAJORITY STAKE IN COINGECKO (2 MINUTE

leading crypto data platform founded in Malaysia in 2014 by Bobby Ong

developers and businesses with pricing and market data.

the chain as the primary DeFi execution layer for the platform's next
product phase.

 STREET: BOARD-ALIGNED PRIVATE EQUITY SECONDARY MARKETPLACE (3 MINUTE

 Street launched out of stealth as a board-aligned private equity
secondary marketplace, disclosing $200M in open interest on day one.

become the American Exchange for Private Equity, competing with
SPV-based structures that have dominated private secondary markets.

 HOW TOKENIZED DEPOSITS ACTUALLY SETTLE (8 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Tokenized deposits like Citi Token Services and JPMorgan's Kinexys
are “on-us,” staying within one bank's legal entity. Moving them
between banks requires interbank settlement, since the receiving bank
otherwise holds an unsecured claim on the sender. Swift's Shared
Ledger locks the deposit and lets the receiving bank credit
immediately, but settlement is deferred through RTGS, leaving credit
exposure in between. Issuing a tokenized deposit on an internal ledger
is necessary but not sufficient for better interbank settlement.

 BEYOND THE BOT: MAPPING THE AGENTIC TRADING STACK (6 MINUTE READ)
[12]

 The agentic trading space can be mapped into four layers:
Intelligence (data and market reasoning models), Agency (harnesses,
terminals, and copilots), Execution (venue connectivity), and
Compliance and Observability (trust infrastructure for autonomous
decisions). In the Agency layer, OpenClaw accumulated ~250K GitHub
stars in 60 days, with trading-specialized harnesses like Senpi and
Supertake and cross-asset terminals such as Catalyst competing across
stocks, options, and prediction markets. The agent itself carries no
moat, since open-source models on open-source harnesses commoditize
agent capability, placing value with whoever owns the decision loop
(Intent-as-a-Service), platforms with the deepest liquidity, and
custodians trusted across the broadest asset class range. Coinbase
Ventures is investing across three pillars: harnesses and terminals
that deliver portfolio-building intent, proprietary intelligence and
specialized market reasoning models, and compliance/observability
infrastructure covering agent identity and tamper-proof decision logs.

 CHATGPT WALLET IS THE RIGHT BET ON THE WRONG TIMELINE (9 MINUTE READ)
[13]

 OpenAI chose network agentic tokens, the right long-term approach,
but incentives aren't aligned yet. Users get a worse experience
(credit cards only, Face ID on every purchase, and thin recurring
support). Merchants still carry dispute risk with no network liability
framework, and one in three of 174 top stores couldn't reach guest
checkout in one test. Issuers approve blind, with no standard view of
intent and no answer on who absorbs chargebacks, and Visa expects
agentic commerce to stay small through FY2027. OpenAI isn't taking a
cut yet and lacks an Apple Pay-style deal. One should expect a
fallback to raw card details in the near term.

 IF I WERE BUILDING A NEOBANK TODAY (2 MINUTE READ) [14]

 The post communicates 30 principles for a new neobank. Chief among
them: go B2B for $50M+ revenue businesses in boring, cash-rich niches,
with distribution as the only moat, credit products and underwriting
as defensibility, and lending your own capital for higher margins.
Dominate one industry first, choose the sponsor bank carefully,
support stablecoins from day one, obsess over onboarding speed and
compliance, and avoid competing on cashback alone. The biggest
competitor is JPMorgan, and it's not worth calling yourself a neobank.
