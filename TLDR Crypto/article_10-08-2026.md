MARKETS & BUSINESS

 ETHER.FI REMOVES RESTAKING FROM WEETH, NEARING FULL EIGENLAYER EXIT
(4 MINUTE READ) [4]

 ether.fi has unbundled restaking from weETH, moving that exposure to
a new Symbiotic-based token called weETHs, which holds just 9,136
tokens (~$18M) against weETH's 1.72 million token supply. Restaking
exposure in weETH is now below 1%, with the protocol's TVL at $3.3B
(down from a $12.43B August 2025 peak), and full EigenPod withdrawal
credential removal is planned for Q4 2026. ETHFI fell 11.4% on the
week and EIGEN dropped 20.8% monthly as liquid restaking sector TVL
contracts from its $18.3B December 2024 peak following incentive
reductions. EigenLayer's total holdings have declined from $22.06B to
$5.10B, and CEO Mike Silagadze called it the "end of an era," stating
restaking arrived prematurely.

 BITCOIN WHALES LOAD UP ON $1.2 BILLION IN BTC AS ETFS ATTRACT $750
MILLION (2 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Bitcoin whales and sharks (10-10,000 BTC holders) accumulated more
than 20,000 BTC (~$1.2 billion) between July 29 and August 7, while US
spot Bitcoin ETFs drew $754.69 million this week, the strongest weekly
inflow pace since April, with over $500 million booked in August
alone. Despite this combined buying pressure, BTC held near $64,910
without a breakout, with analysts characterizing the marginal buyer as
"more tactical than convicted." A $120 million Coldcard hardware
wallet hack on July 30 and the US Senate's expected delay on the
CLARITY Act are suppressing institutional appetite, with the latter
viewed as a prerequisite for broader capital deployment. Santiment's
diverging accumulation data points toward a $70K+ target, though
Nexo's Liya Kalchev says a decisive close above $65,000 is needed to
validate any recovery thesis.

INNOVATION & LAUNCHES

 HELIUS RINGS BRINGS PRIVACY TO SOLANA (3 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Helius, a Solana RPC and infrastructure provider, is launching Rings,
a two-tier privacy layer built on Solana. The public ring hides
transaction amounts while keeping sender and recipient addresses
visible, preventing amount-based MEV extraction, with DeFi
integrations planned across staking, trading, and borrow/lend. The
customizable ring is fully anonymous, concealing sender, recipient,
and amounts, and targets institutions and neobanks that need
configurable compliance controls. The architecture splits into a
permissionless public tier and a permissioned enterprise tier, both
running on Solana's existing infrastructure.

 HALO WHITEPAPER: DECENTRALIZED AI INFERENCE NETWORK (8 MINUTE READ)
[7]

 Warden Protocol's HALO launched a permissionless AI inference
marketplace on Base mainnet, settling operator payments in USDC, with
no geographic restrictions or account requirements for model access.
The verification mechanism, SPEX (Statistical Proof of Execution),
fingerprints model outputs as Bloom filters and tasks independent
verifiers with probing them, such that honest runs nearly fully
overlap the fingerprint while fabricated outputs fall to the filter's
false-positive floor, sidestepping trusted hardware and ZK proofs.
HALO token emissions mint only against verified usage on a fixed
declining schedule, protocol fees fund programmatic buybacks into a
treasury, and zero supply is reserved for venture investors. Users
fund accounts via card or crypto with session keys abstracting gas and
per-transaction signing while retaining self-custody, though one
routing layer remains centralized until federation to staked relayers
ships in H2 2026.

 VISA'S STABLECOIN STRATEGY: PROCUREMENT GATEWAY, NOT ISSUER (6 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Visa's Head of Crypto Cuy Sheffield told Bloomberg Crypto that Visa
isn't competing to issue stablecoins but wants to be the "procurement
gateway" that brings banks onchain and the "acceptance layer" that
makes issued stablecoins spendable. Its Visa Stablecoin Platform (VSP;
in beta) bundles wallet creation, Open USD issuance/redemption,
custody, and transfers, paired with Visa Consulting & Analytics for
use-case selection and launch support. Per Paymentscan, cumulative
crypto card volume has topped $11 billion with Visa holding 96.8% of
card network volume and powering 17 of the top 20 crypto card issuers
by transaction volume. Visa's Trusted Agent Protocol (TAP) is also
positioning it to connect stablecoins with agentic-commerce payment
protocols like x402.

 CIRCLE AND $USDC'S ROLE IN THE AGENTIC ECONOMY (4 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Legacy payment rails (cards, ACH, SWIFT, and correspondent banking)
were architected for human-initiated transactions, leaving a gap as AI
agent deployment demands programmable, instant, global, and
intermediary-free money movement. USDC and Circle's infrastructure
target this gap as the settlement layer for the agentic economy, with
onchain programmability enabling autonomous spending policies that
traditional card rails cannot replicate. Circle's developer platform,
CCTP cross-chain transfer protocol, and compliance tooling extend its
advantage as enterprise AI agent deployments scale.

 13 BEAR MARKET OBSERVATIONS ON CRYPTO'S CURRENT STATE (6 MINUTE READ)
[10]

 Crypto is at "peak depth," with sellers exhausted but no marginal
buyers yet, early-stage fundraising near-dead as VCs chase AI instead,
and hackathon/grant funding down ~95%, risking a shrinking, "legacy
industry" outcome if capital doesn't return by Q1 2027. Crypto has
narrowed to four markets with product-market fit: memecoins/spot,
perps, yield, and prediction markets, plus stablecoin payments.
Non-financial protocols (governance, social, identity, and gaming) are
effectively dead. The field is consolidating around "everything
exchanges" (Coinbase, Robinhood, Solana, and Polymarket) and yield
packagers (USDT, USDC, Aave, and Morpho), with only community-led
chains (ETH and Solana) and distribution-led chains (Base, Robinhood's
Tempo, and BSC) surviving.

 ROBINHOOD CHAIN'S DEX VOLUME FELL 72%, TX AND DEPOSITS SET RECORDS (3
MINUTE READ) [11]

 Robinhood Chain's DEX volume dropped 72.5% from an $878 million peak
on July 11 to $241 million by August 1, while the broader DEX market
expanded 39% over the same period, cutting Robinhood's market share
from 16.65% to 4.32%. On-chain metrics diverged: transactions hit a
record 13.3 million, TVL climbed 820% to $433 million, and stablecoin
supply reached $597 million, while average trade size fell 74% to
$27.82. The chain's incentive allocation explains the gap: over 90% of
$58,351 in daily rewards flows to stablecoin depositors via Morpho
lending campaigns, while 11 trading campaigns split just $4,543 per
day. A 0.63 correlation between CASHCAT memecoin activity and daily
DEX volume points to speculative trading as the driver of the initial
surge rather than organic usage growth.
