MARKETS & BUSINESS

 91% OF TOKENIZED STOCKS LEAD BACK TO ONE BROKER (2 MINUTE READ) [4]

 The tokenized stocks market has reached $4.60B, but $4.17B (91%) is
backed by shares held at a single US broker, Alpaca Securities, used
by seven issuers including Binance bStocks, Ondo, Robinhood, and
Coinbase. Those shares sit at DTC under Cede & Co., not under the
names of the 4.7M token holders spread across those products.
Securitize and Backpack route through RQD Clearing but still terminate
at DTC, while only Superstate and Securitize's ISTs hold shares on the
issuer's own register, removing both a broker and the depository from
the chain. An Alpaca insolvency or regulatory action would put 91% of
tokenized equity supply and all 4.7M affected holders at risk from a
single counterparty event.

 COINBASE CEO BACKS TOKENIZING COIN STOCK AS COMPANIES RESIST (2
MINUTE READ) [5]

 Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong backed tokenizing Coinbase shares on X,
arguing that restricting new shareholder categories harms the business
and that companies should serve customers on the platforms they use.
His position contrasts with resistance from 67 companies, including
Apple, Tesla, Nvidia, and Meta, that have objected to their shares
trading on OKXICE, a joint venture between OKX and Intercontinental
Exchange. The objectors represent 56 of OKXICE's 62 original target
companies, a 90.3% rejection rate. For tokenized equity platforms, the
OKXICE data shows that corporate buy-in, not technical infrastructure,
is the binding constraint on stock listings.

 TEMPO LAUNCHES CREDIT, MATCHING BUSINESSES WITH OUTSIDE LENDERS (1
MINUTE READ) [6]

 Tempo, a payments chain incubated by Stripe and Paradigm, now
connects businesses with outside lenders, with draws and repayments
settling in stablecoins in under a second, 24/7. Fintechs can borrow
for prefunding payouts, receivables, working capital, and consumer
credit through a licensed partner. Facilities can have no minimum
draw, draws can land as dollars in a bank account and be repaid by
wire, and the lender sets terms and takes the risk, not Tempo. The
first live case lets eligible El Dorado users in Bolivia borrow USDT
from Tala with no collateral.

 MEZZANINE LAUNCHES RISK-TRANCHED DEFI LOOPING VAULTS (5 MINUTE READ)
[7]

 Mezzanine is a DeFi protocol that automates yield looping and splits
positions into two risk tranches: Junior (higher yield, first-loss)
and Senior (buffered by Junior capital, lower yield). In an 80/20
Senior/Junior split on a 10% APY vault, Senior earns 8% and Junior
earns 18%. A 5% drawdown leaves Senior unchanged while Junior absorbs
the full loss at a 25% decline. The protocol's first vault, the Hastra
Optimizer, accepts USDC or wYLDS and loops Hastra's $PRIME and $AUTO
tokens across lending markets backed by US home equity and car loans,
with withdrawals taking up to two weeks per epoch. Tranche tokens are
composable primitives built for Pendle integration or use as
collateral elsewhere, with Mezzanine targeting a role as the
structured-risk layer for DeFi looping strategies.

 A NEW MARKET RATE FOR LIQUIDITY: AERODROME (5 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Traditional DEX LP compensation is capped by current swap fee
revenue, a constraint that causes liquidity to drain when volume
drops, prevents new markets from bootstrapping ahead of organic
trading activity, and misaligns tokenholders with LPs. Aerodrome
breaks this ceiling by paying LPs in AERO, a token that, when staked,
earns a share of total network revenue including dynamic fee premiums,
liquidity payments, and MEV capture, so staked AERO yields can exceed
any single pool's point-in-time fee output. The token capitalizes
claims on future network productivity rather than a fixed fee split,
so the protocol can subsidize current liquidity costs at a flexible
multiplier as the network grows. Token issuance to LPs transfers
future earnings claims from existing holders, and the model holds only
when the value the liquidity creates exceeds the dilution issued to
attract it.
