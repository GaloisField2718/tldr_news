 COINDCX CONFIRMS $16 MILLION LOSS FROM RETIRED WALLETS (4 MINUTE
READ) [4]

 CoinDCX admitted losing 15.99M USDT from deposit wallets it stopped
using after its July 2025 hack, and says it absorbed the loss. It says
an automated sweeping service failed for a period, so user deposits
built up at a deprecated address with unknown vulnerabilities.
FlashRescue's co-founder flagged the outflows on Wednesday. CEO Sumit
Gupta called it “a false alarm,” and the company's account called
the claims “misleading.” ZachXBT showed one address was used for
10 months after the 2025 incident and swept deposits as late as April
and May 2026. He advised users to withdraw and said the founders
either lied or were negligent. CoinDCX says user funds are safe in
cold wallets, and trading and withdrawals continued. The July 2025
hack cost about $44M. The founders were also arrested in March over a
fraud complaint.

 SOFI LAUNCHES TOKENIZED DEPOSIT THAT EARNS 4.4% IN STABLECOINS (3
MINUTE READ) [5]

 SOFIE is the first consumer-facing tokenized deposit from a US
nationally chartered bank, with each token representing $1 on deposit
earning 4.40% APY under FDIC insurance. Members convert SOFIE
one-to-one into SoFiUSD, which runs on Ethereum and Solana and settles
SoFi's card program via Mastercard's network, connecting yield-bearing
bank deposits to onchain rails. The product targets an estimated $64
billion in idle stablecoin capital (about 21% of the market) by adding
yield and insurance protections that pure stablecoins lack. SOFIE is
SoFi's fifth crypto product in under a year, following consumer token
trading across 40+ assets and the SoFiUSD stablecoin launch, extending
the bank's pattern of converting traditional financial primitives into
onchain equivalents.

 VULNERABILITIES FOUND IN GLAMSTERDAM, AGENTS CAME TO THE RESCUE (5
MINUTE READ) [6]

 ChainSecurity flagged vulnerabilities in EIP-8037/8038, two
state-pricing EIPs central to Glamsterdam's planned gas limit increase
from 60M to 200M, with at least 13 major DeFi protocols identified as
affected. The mechanism: EIP-8037's gas refill when storage slots are
freed breaks the 1/64 gas guarantee in nested contract calls, enabling
attackers to trigger outer-contract error handlers that lacked the gas
to execute under the prior pricing model. Researchers from the
Ethereum Foundation and Ethlabs each deployed AI agents overnight to
scan for vulnerable contracts, then merged their efforts into a shared
GitHub coordination repo where agents claim tasks via issue comments,
deposit findings in structured directories, and produce consolidated
memos without human intermediation. Core devs at the ACD paused any
Glamsterdam launch date pending full impact analysis. The same
agent-scanning framework is now running against EIP-8198 (Quick Slots,
12s to 10s slot time) ahead of Hegotá.

 CIRCLE LAUNCHES CIRCLE MATCH FOR INSTITUTIONAL USERS (2 MINUTE READ)
[7]

 Circle Match, available for eligible Circle Mint customers, is an
hourly auction that pairs institutional USDC mint and redemption
orders within the Mint platform. When orders clear, minting
participants receive a rebate while redemptions settle at a price the
redeemer sets, distributing economic incentives to both sides of the
institutional mint-redeem cycle. The underlying USDC flows remain
unchanged, with Circle layering a price-discovery and rebate mechanism
on top of existing infrastructure rather than altering the asset or
its peg mechanics.

 PERPS WILL EAT OPTIONS (8 MINUTE READ) [9]

 On-chain options hold under 1% of perp DEX volume despite
representing 3x futures volume in TradFi. The gap persists partly
because 10x perp positions carry a 47% liquidation probability over
180 days, making options the structurally superior instrument for
multi-week theses. The growth mechanism is reflexive: options sellers
hedge on perp markets, expanding perp OI rather than cannibalizing it,
a dynamic Deribit already demonstrates with $2B+ in futures OI sourced
from options hedging. September posted the best on-chain options month
on record at ~$5B notional (7% of Deribit volume), with Derive
commanding 90%+ of on-chain premium at $18B notional traded and DRV up
1,000%+ YTD at 66x annualized revenue. On-chain options are projected
to grow from 0.8% to 5% of perp volume. Hyperliquid via Hypercall's
shared margin, Derive, and Paradex will be the primary beneficiaries.

 KEYSTONE HARDWARE WALLET CONFIRMS CUSTOMER SHIPPING DATA BREACH (2
MINUTE READ) [11]

 Keystone Hardware Wallet confirmed on October 23 that a third party
accessed customer shipping data through its website, exposing names,
addresses, contact details, and order information, while payment
credentials, recovery phrases, private keys, and wallet passwords
remained unaffected. The breach was detected on October 20 and traced
through Shopify API logs. Keystone has blocked the access, patched the
vulnerability, and opened a formal investigation with customer
notifications underway. Keystone warns affected users to expect
phishing attempts from actors impersonating the company offering
refunds or replacement devices, and urges customers never to share
recovery phrases and to navigate to the site directly rather than
clicking unsolicited links.
