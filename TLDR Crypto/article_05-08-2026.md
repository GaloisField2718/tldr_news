MARKETS & BUSINESS

 RENEWED PUSHES TO ADD PREDICTION MARKETS PROVISION TO CLARITY ACT (3
MINUTE READ) [4]

 At a Senate Indian Affairs Committee roundtable, Indian Gaming
Association VP Tehassi Hill urged lawmakers to amend the Clarity Act
to bar sports and casino gambling via prediction markets and affirm
state/tribal jurisdiction over the CFTC, with Senator Tina Smith
suggesting similar language could be added to the Farm Bill. CFTC
Chair Michael Selig has argued the agency has exclusive jurisdiction
over prediction markets and sued several states, while platforms like
Polymarket and Kalshi favor CFTC oversight. Senate Agriculture Chair
John Boozman said he's sympathetic but doesn't think the amendment
belongs in Clarity, calling it a separate issue from crypto.

 BLACKROCK'S SPOT ETHEREUM ETF TO DO 1-FOR-3 REVERSE SHARE SPLIT IN
OCTOBER (2 MINUTE READ) [5]

 BlackRock will consolidate every three ETHA shares into one on
October 6, raising the fund's per-share NAV without changing
investors' total holdings. Bloomberg's Eric Balchunas said the move
should cut the trading spread from about 7bps to 2bps. ETHA remains
the largest ETH ETF with over $5 billion AUM, though its price is down
roughly 40% year-to-date alongside ether itself.

INNOVATION & LAUNCHES

 THIS WEEK ON BASE: X402, BASE VERIFY ONCHAIN, BANKR, AND MORE (4
MINUTE READ) [6]

 Base captured 90% of agentic stablecoin transaction volume in Q2 2026
and tripled its share of onchain spot volumes over the past year, now
leading in both BTC and FX markets. The chain launched Base Verify
Onchain on Sepolia, a smart-contract Sybil resistance primitive that
requires no personal data collection, while B20s (Base Native Token
Standard) hit $200M in trading volume within their first 30 days on
mainnet. Bankr platform coins surpassed $5B in cumulative trading
volume, and Morpho's market crossed $500M without incentives, with
Coinbase's borrow product returning to all-time highs as the largest
DeFi mullet integration. Axis Robotics closed a $12M seed (Hack VC)
for its Physical AI data engine on Base, Exa App raised $2.2M for
self-custodial onchain credit, and MoonPay rolled out conversational
AI trading on Base for Claude and ChatGPT users.

 PROPOSAL TO BURN VALIDATOR REWARDS TO CAP STAKING AT 50% (4 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 A draft EIP co-authored by Ethereum Foundation researcher Justin
Drake proposes a tapered issuance burn that destroys a rising share of
validator rewards as the staking ratio climbs, hitting a 100% burn
(zero net issuance) at roughly 60.25 million ETH staked (~50% of
supply), phased in over 18 months to prevent overconcentration among
large custodians. Aave's Stani Kulechov criticized the plan as likely
to kill ETH borrowing and DeFi yield use cases, while Grayscale's Zach
Pandl argued reduced inflation should be a first-order positive for
ETH's price. The proposal was submitted ahead of the deadline for
Ethereum's upcoming Hegotá upgrade.

GUIDES & TUTORIALS

 THE ENDGAME FOR WALLETS IS OPEN MONEY (7 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Traditional bank balances are claims on institutional ledgers
("closed money") and contrast with self-custody wallets that move
assets directly on public networks, making money programmable and
composable. MetaMask has built this out with mUSD, a wallet-native
stablecoin earning up to 6% APY through onchain lending markets with
no lockup, and a MetaMask Card that holds assets in self-custody until
the moment of merchant payment. ERC-7710 delegation and ERC-7715
permission requests allow users to grant scoped authority to an AI
agent, and x402 extends this to machine-to-machine HTTP payments per
request. Wallets are becoming the permission layer through which
user-delegated agents move money.

 BITCOIN IS FINE: A RIGOROUS LOOK AT THE QUANTUM THREAT (10 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 Bitcoin has two attack vectors: Shor's algorithm threatens Bitcoin's
ECDSA/Schnorr signatures, while Grover's algorithm against SHA-256
mining would require roughly 3% of the Sun's energy output, a physical
barrier that makes the mining threat negligible. Approximately 6.26M
BTC holds exposed public keys, with 1.7M BTC in legacy P2PK outputs
immediately vulnerable to a capable quantum computer and 5.2M BTC in
reused or Taproot addresses requiring migration before exposure
becomes critical. Expert timelines for cryptographically capable
quantum computing span 10 to 40 years, and BIP-360, introducing
Dilithium post-quantum signatures under a bc1z address type, merged to
the BIP repo in early 2026 with a projected 7-year full deployment
window. Classical cryptanalysis is the stronger reason to upgrade now.

MISCELLANEOUS

 ON THE NEXT CHAPTER FOR OCTANT AND THE GOLEM FOUNDATION (3 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Golem Foundation CEO Julian Zawistowski announced Octant's next epoch
will use a 100 ETH matching pool, down from prior levels, as the
foundation exits its role as primary matching contributor. Golem
Foundation will instead commit 5% of treasury capital proceeds to
Octant for direct project grants, currently split 3% to Ethereum Labs,
1% to Ethereum Institutional, and 1% at the foundation's discretion.
The restructuring shifts Octant toward thematic, results-focused
rounds with higher per-user rewards, while treasury diversification
into low-risk ETH-long instruments responds to declining staking
yields that previously funded the program. Zawistowski hinted at GLM
token utility overhauls in coming months, framing the changes around
Ethereum's 11-year anniversary, as the program has distributed 5,273
ETH in grants across 13 epochs since 2023.

 WHO ACTUALLY BANKS THE STABLECOIN NEOBANKS? (3 MINUTE READ) [11]

 A hands-on test of five stablecoin neobanks (RedotPay, EtherFi, KAST,
Avici, and Gnosis Pay) found EUR-to-stablecoin round-trip costs on a
€5,000 test ranged from about $2 to over $130, with RedotPay's
OpenPayd-powered account charging the highest effective fee (2%) and
taking ~60 hours to clear, while Avici's Iron-powered account had
near-zero fees but an invasive week-long KYC process. USD rails were
cheaper and more uniform across providers ($2–$16 round-trip via
ACH), with Bridge, Due, Noah, OpenPayd, and Monerium identified as the
main vendors powering these neobanks' banking infrastructure behind
the scenes.
