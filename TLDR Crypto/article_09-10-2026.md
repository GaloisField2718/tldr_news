 JUSTIN DRAKE CALLS FOR CRYPTO "BUNKER MODE" AS AI THREATENS ECDSA (4
MINUTE READ) [4]

 Ethereum researcher Justin Drake issued a call for "bunker mode,"
urging crypto holders to migrate assets to fresh addresses whose
public keys remain hidden behind a hash before a potential ECDSA
break. AI-driven mathematical progress, including OpenAI's recent
mathematical superintelligence release and a series of proofs
disproving long-held conjectures, may enable fast private key recovery
on GPU clusters within months. Elliptic curves carry rich algebraic
structure exploitable through AI-discovered techniques, and the
absence of cryptographic breakthroughs among OpenAI's 722 published
mathematical results raises the possibility of government-suppressed
cryptanalysis. The Ethereum roadmap already targets hash-based
cryptography with end-to-end formal verification, and Drake is pushing
to accelerate those timelines, while advising oracles and L2 security
councils to rotate ECDSA pubkeys with every signed message.

 STANDARD CHARTERED TO EXPAND CRYPTO CUSTODY SERVICES IN SINGAPORE (2
MINUTE READ) [5]

 Standard Chartered's Singapore unit plans to offer institutional
custody for selected crypto assets, stablecoins, and tokenized
real-world assets by the end of the year, subject to regulatory
requirements. It extends the bank's existing custody footprint in the
UAE, Luxembourg, and Hong Kong, and follows its move to bring Zodia
Custody, the institutional custodian it incubated with Northern Trust,
in-house.

 VITALIK ON AI-ACCELERATED CRYPTOGRAPHY: HASH-BASED OVER LATTICES (5
MINUTE READ) [8]

 Vitalik Buterin warns that AI-accelerated mathematical research may
erode the security of lattice-based cryptography (ML-DSA, LWE, and
FHE) through the same mechanism that GNFS degraded RSA, where decades
of optimization shrunk effective key strength by an order of
magnitude, and projects that AI could compress 50 years of math
progress into two years. Ethereum's lean roadmap has already pivoted
to hash-only signatures (WOTS and SPHINCS+), dropping lattices,
ML-DSA, Falcon, and lattice-based commitments from ZK proofs.
Public-key encryption cannot be constructed from hashes alone due to
long-standing mathematical constraints, which means secure messaging,
VPNs, and anonymizing protocols face an open problem that extends well
beyond blockchains. Buterin's practical recommendations include
keeping funds in addresses that have not yet broadcast transactions,
moving multisig confirmations offchain to avoid exposing signer public
keys, and routing encrypted notes through offchain channels rather
than publishing them onchain.

 Robinhood, eToro, Webull, and Coinbase all launched agentic trading

JPMorgan, while the US CLARITY Act failed a Senate vote, leaving the
SEC and CFTC to establish crypto regulatory rules.

CRYPTO INDUSTRY SPLIT OVER JUSTIN DRAKE'S AI WARNING (3 MINUTE READ)
[10]

Coinbase's head of cryptography Yehuda Lindell called Drake's warning
that ECDSA could break within months "FUD," saying there is no
evidence the hardness assumptions behind elliptic-curve cryptography
have weakened. Dragonfly's Haseeb Qureshi called it "a very sober
call," while Solana Foundation's Jacob Creech said Solana users do not
need "bunker mode" because its Ed25519 setup could migrate to a
quantum-resistant scheme through a hash-based proof.

 MORPHO'S FEE SWITCH IS NOT COMPARABLE TO UNISWAP'S (3 MINUTE READ)
[11]

 Morpho's fee would have to come from suppliers (lowering yield on
portable capital), curators (compressing margins and pushing them
toward riskier vaults or other platforms like Aave V4), or both,
unlike Uniswap, where liquidity benefits from the volume it attracts.
Morpho still needs to prove pricing power, noting that Uniswap's
recent strength came from an outlier month driven by Robinhood meme
speculation, with volume now down about 71% from its September peak.
