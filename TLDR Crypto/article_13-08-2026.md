 FLIGHTAWARE DROPS LAWSUIT AGAINST KALSHI ONE DAY AFTER FILING (2
MINUTE READ) [4]

 FlightAware voluntarily dismissed its suit against Kalshi over
alleged unauthorized data/trademark use in flight-cancellation markets
just one day after filing. Kalshi simultaneously changed its market
language from "verified from FlightAware" to "verified from Primary
Source Agency," suggesting the parties settled privately. Kalshi still
faces active suits and injunctions in New York, Washington, and
Michigan over sports-event contracts.

 CFTC SUES GOLIATH VENTURES OVER ALLEGED $397M DEFI LIQUIDITY POOL
PONZI (3 MINUTE READ) [5]

 The CFTC and SEC filed simultaneous civil suits against Goliath
Ventures Inc. and CEO Christopher Delgado, alleging a $397 million
Ponzi scheme from November 2022 through February 2026 in which
approximately 1,600 customers were told their BTC and ETH would earn
36% annual yields in DEX liquidity pools. Goliath issued fabricated
account statements while directing no capital to any DeFi protocol,
funneling $174 million to staff as recruiting commissions, $87 million
in Ponzi payments to existing customers, and $48 million to Delgado
personally for luxury real estate, vehicles, and jewelry. Delgado
pleaded guilty in June to wire fraud conspiracy and money laundering
charges, and a bankruptcy receiver was appointed in March. The
same-day CFTC-SEC filing demonstrates both agencies asserting parallel
jurisdiction over crypto fraud, with CFTC Chairman Michael Selig
citing an aggressive mandate to police crypto asset markets.

 PUDGY PENGUINS CO-FOUNDER LAUNCHES NFT MINT ON ROBINHOOD CHAIN (2
MINUTE READ) [6]

 Cole Villemain, removed from Pudgy Penguins in January 2022 amid
treasury misuse allegations and scrutinized by ZachXBT, sold out a
44,444-piece NFT collection called Spritehood on Robinhood Chain in 57
minutes on August 11, generating $1.28M (684.28 ETH) across two tiers

unverified on Robinhood Chain's Blockscout explorer, preventing buyers

verified contract code, and community observers have labeled the
project a cash grab with unresolved questions about viability on the
chain.

 NEUTRL: YIELD WITHOUT A CYCLE (5 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Crypto-native carry strategies face a structural timing mismatch:
funding rates and basis spreads are royalties on speculative activity,
so delta-neutral books earn worst when stablecoin demand peaks, a
dynamic visible in H1 2026 when top-30 altcoin perpetual volume fell
30%, from $8.4B to $5.9B daily. Neutrl sidesteps this by buying
private company locked shares at a 15% discount to the reference price
and shorting the equity perpetual referencing the same name, hedging
directional exposure from day one. Funding on the short leg averaged
10.9% annualized over a late-May to mid-July window, delivering
roughly 20.5 points of total return over a six-month hold.

 HOW TO TURN $HYPE FUNDING RATES INTO PROFIT (3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Boros protocol lets traders isolate and trade funding rates as a
standalone asset through Yield Units (YU), instruments that pay a
fixed rate against the floating funding of a specific perpetual venue.
A 4.03pp gap currently exists between HYPE's fixed rate on Hyperliquid
(7.93%) and Bybit (3.89%), representing a spread that locks in at
trade open and resolves over a 17-day window. The four-leg structure
shorts YU on the Hyperliquid market and longs YU on Bybit, then
offsets each leg with an opposing HYPE perpetual on the respective
exchange, canceling both floating funding obligations and directional
price exposure. The remaining return is the fixed rate differential,
scaling to 40.30% APR at 10x leverage with no HYPE price risk.

 $FIGR: THE MARKET MAY BE UNDERWRITING THE WRONG BUSINESS (8 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 Figure has transitioned from balance-sheet lender to capital-light
marketplace operator on Provenance blockchain rails:
partner-originated loans reached 78% of Q1 2026 volume, Figure Connect
grew from $8M to $1.6B in seven quarters, and ecosystem/technology
fees expanded from 5% to 28% of revenue. The pending Kiavi acquisition
adds $7B in annual residential transition loan origination at roughly
40% EBITDA margins, volume that should route through the same
infrastructure without requiring additional capital deployment.
Despite 91% incremental EBITDA margins in 2025 and adjusted EBITDA
expanding from negative 4% to 49%, FIGR trades at roughly 12x EBITDA,
in line with specialty lenders rather than the Tradeweb/ICE comps the
business now resembles. Thin sell-side coverage and FIGR's legacy
classification as a crypto proxy have left the market pricing the old
balance-sheet model rather than the fee-generating marketplace.

 WHO ACTUALLY CAPTURES VALUE IN CRYPTO (5 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Analysis estimates on-chain protocol revenue at roughly $8 billion in
2025 versus roughly $70 billion (range $60-100B) for
off-chain/centralized crypto companies (Coinbase, Tether, Binance,
Robinhood's crypto arm, etc.), an 8.5x gap. Applying generous
multiples, that implies on-chain's entire addressable market cap is
about $168 billion versus roughly $840 billion for centralized
players, meaning the whole centralized crypto industry is worth about
as much as a single AI lab like OpenAI or Anthropic. On-chain revenue
is dominated by L1/L2 chains (~49%). Off-chain is dominated by
exchanges/brokers (~66%) and stablecoin issuers (~19%). Centralized
players win by sitting closer to end users and abstracting away
crypto's complexity.

 TOKENIZED STOCKS: THE NEXT BATTLEGROUND (8 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Tokenized equities reached ~$1.9B in market size, a 6.5x increase
since January 2025, while the tokenized Treasury market stagnated at
$15B after April 2026. The SEC's January 2026 taxonomy established
four structural models. The Linked Security structure
(DeFi-composable, no voting rights, unavailable to US investors) has
captured the most share, with Ondo at 45.9% ($851M across 406
equities) and xStocks at 26% ($482M across 183 equities), both
operating via Jersey-regulated SPVs. Traditional market infrastructure
accelerated entry in mid-2026: DTCC began limited QQQ/SPY tokenization
on July 15 after an SEC no-action letter, Nasdaq secured rule-change
approval in March 2026 and is building a permissionless gateway with
Kraken parent Payward, and NYSE filed its own rule change while
signing an MOU with Securitize. Coinbase announced a 1:1 stock-backed
token product for non-US customers with onchain usability but has not
yet launched.
