 POWER PERPS ON HYPERLIQUID (4 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Paragon introduced Power Perps on Hyperliquid, a family of perpetual
contracts that track an asset's price raised to a power, with HYPE²
as the first instrument delivering 4x exposure when the underlying
doubles. The structure carries no strikes or expiry dates,
consolidating trader interest into a single persistent order book per
underlying rather than fragmenting liquidity across an options chain
as conventional instruments do. Hyperliquid's portfolio margin allows
cross-margining of spot, linear perps, and Power Perps in one account,
while market makers hedging delta in existing spot and perp books add
order flow that tightens Power Perp spreads. Paragon frames these
contracts as composable building blocks: a squared perp paired with a
short linear approximates a straddle, while the reverse combination,
short power and long linear, earns funding when the market trades
sideways.

 BLAST L2 SHUTS DOWN, OPENS 12-MONTH ASSET RECOVERY WINDOW (1 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 Blast L2, launched in early 2024 with native yield on deposited ETH
and stablecoins and peak TVL in the billions, has shut down. Users
with assets in L2 wallets, pending withdrawals, or Blast Mobile can
recover ETH and USD via the team's recovery site through November 4,
2027. Unclaimed funds after that date may be unrecoverable. The
closure ends one of 2024's prominent yield-bearing L2 experiments,
where protocol-level auto-yield on deposits served as the primary TVL
acquisition mechanism rather than external incentive programs.

GUIDES & TUTORIALS

 PAYMENTS ARE THE NEW ADS (6 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Agentic commerce has exposed a structural gap in payments: AI
shopping agents have four paths to pay, each with material friction,
and broken flows have deterred 60% of UK adults from trusting agent
purchases after a single failure. Stripe's Instinct Balance, a
prefunded stablecoin wallet, sidesteps bank authentication friction
and earns Stripe ~$1.95 per $100 spent compared to ~$0.40 on virtual
cards, a 4x unit-economics advantage over the network-token path.
Banks have a counter through agent-authenticated network tokens
modeled on Apple Pay's 2014 liability deal, but Chase has not joined
any major agent platform, leaving the token infrastructure incomplete.
If stablecoin balances capture agentic spend before banks resolve
token liability, those funds exit bank deposits for good, shifting the
customer-ownership fight from card rails to stablecoin custodians.

 REUSABLE NETWORKS: WHY AI MAKES CRYPTO'S NETWORK EFFECTS VALUABLE (3
MINUTE READ) [9]

 As AI drives software production costs toward zero, value migrates
from applications to the networks they compose with: protocol
liquidity, on-chain identity graphs, reputation systems, and
settlement rails. AI can clone interfaces and regenerate code in
minutes, but it cannot reproduce the provenance of an NFT, the
liquidity depth of Uniswap, or the trust accumulated in USDC. This
reframes crypto's composability thesis: when any application becomes a
commodity, the scarce asset is access to the shared state layer
underneath. Blockchains were designed as canonical internet-layer
state, and in a world where agents need reliable, permissionless
coordination infrastructure, that original premise becomes a stronger
value proposition.

MISCELLANEOUS

 NO ONE NEEDS BLOCKCHAIN (5 MINUTE READ) [10]

 The CFTC's approval of single-stock perpetual futures, matched by
Kalshi's same-day launch, illustrates a structural trend: 2026's
crypto growth concentrated in businesses treating blockchain as
financial rails, while onchain-native DeFi lagged. Kalshi's
architecture is representative, with orders matched in a proprietary
engine and clearing running through a registered DCO, leaving
blockchain to handle only stablecoin deposits/withdrawals and
tokenized positions on Solana. The two growth clusters,
speculation-driven (prediction markets, perps, and memecoins) and
TradFi-connected (stablecoins, RWAs, and vaults), share the same
pattern: platforms owning user relationships capture revenue, and
blockchain captures infrastructure fees. This creates a value capture
problem for the ecosystem, which risks commoditization unless more
businesses emerge where blockchain is indispensable to core logic
rather than settlement infrastructure.

 WHY STRIPE WILL LOSE AGENTIC PAYMENTS (2 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Stripe's two agentic payment methods, Shared Payment Tokens (zero
merchant adoption and opposed by card networks) and issued virtual
cards (single-auth limits, no rewards, and identity mismatches), both
fail to satisfy networks and end users, while Shopify now carries
Visa, Mastercard, Amex, and Discover agent tokens natively through
Shop Pay without routing credentials through Stripe. The structural
argument is that the industry will skip Stripe's intermediary layer
entirely, moving from raw PANs directly to native agent token
standards like VIC and AgentPay, which makes Stripe's existing network
effects irrelevant. Startups that build UX around native agent token
flows rather than Stripe credentials are positioned to capture agentic
commerce distribution as credential ownership shifts to the card
networks themselves.
