 EIP-8288 MAXIMALISM: VITALIK'S CASE AGAINST NEW ETHEREUM PRECOMPILES
(2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Precompiles and the EVM are the wrong venue for cryptography support
in Ethereum because both require data to pass through the main chain
as calldata, capping throughput at roughly 8 MB/s even with PeerDAS.
Vitalik Buterin's proposed solution is EIP-8288 maximalism: compute
heavy cryptography client-side, generate a STARK proof, and push only
the proof through the EIP-8288 recursive aggregation mechanism, so the
chain records the result without seeing the underlying data or
computation. This design lets developers use any signature scheme or
cryptographic primitive without triggering new protocol-level
precompile additions. Broader adoption would freeze Ethereum's
precompile surface while keeping the base layer lean.

 FASTER, AGGREGATABLE, AND QUANTUM SAFE PRIVACY POOLS (5 MINUTE READ)
[8]

 A new ethresear.ch proposal ports Tornado Cash-style privacy pools to
Flock, a transparent hash-based SNARK using SHA-256 for the Merkle
tree and nullifier derivation, removing the discrete-log assumptions
in Pedersen commitments and Groth16. A stepwise circuit that runs one
SHA-256 compression 34 times with a sumcheck glue argument cuts prover
time 28x, from 810ms to 28.6ms at depth 32, with a 266 KiB proof size.
An aggregation layer lets users prove withdrawals locally in ~19.5ms
each, then folds N proofs into a single ~344 KiB proof that verifies
in 46-64ms regardless of batch size. The scheme also includes a ZIP
2005-inspired quantum recovery protocol: if a cryptographically
relevant quantum computer is announced, the pool freezes, and users
withdraw through a post-quantum recovery verifier seeded from their
original 256-bit key, leaving nullifier tracking intact.

 TRUST COMPANY LAUNCHES BITCOIN CUSTODY WITH UP TO $370 MILLION IN
INSURANCE PER CLIENT (3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 AnchorWatch Trust Company launched as a Nevada-chartered qualified
bitcoin custodian targeting fiduciaries, public companies, trusts, and
high-net-worth individuals, with two models: Flagship, where
AnchorWatch holds qualifying keys and clients retain additional key
control, and Distributed, which splits keys across AnchorWatch, BitGo
(US), and CoinCorner (Isle of Man). Monthly fees are fixed-dollar and
capped at $10,000 regardless of portfolio appreciation, decoupling
custodian revenue from BTC price performance. Optional insurance
through A+ rated Lloyd's of London syndicates covers up to $370
million per client, with each policy issued in the customer's name
covering losses from violent coercion, kidnapping, and
AnchorWatch-caused fraud.

 MASTERCARD UNVEILS STABLECOIN SECURITY TOOL TO SCREEN PAYMENTS (3
MINUTE READ) [10]

 Announced at Money20/20 USA, the service gives financial institutions
a risk score before stablecoin funds move onchain. It checks where the
money came from and looks for links to fraud, scams, and sanctions
violations. It delivers results through an API so banks can plug it
into existing compliance systems. Borderless.xyz and Keyrails are the
first partners. It launches first in North America, with worldwide
availability expected in 2027. Mastercard also plans to bring BVNK,
which it acquired in August, into the product next year.

 DEFI DEVELOPMENT BOOSTS CHAD DIVIDEND TO 14% TO GROW SOLANA HOLDINGS
(2 MINUTE READ) [11]

 DeFi Development Corp. (DFDV) raised the annual dividend on its CHAD
preferred stock from 13% to 14%, effective November 2, lifting the
per-share payment to $1.40 from $1.30 on CHAD's $10 stated value.
Proceeds from CHAD issuances fund the company's Solana treasury, which
generates returns through staking and validator operations. DFDV added
about 22,070 SOL the prior week, bringing total holdings to
approximately 2.59 million SOL. CHAD reached record trading highs the
same week, and the board authorized a program to repurchase up to all
outstanding CHAD shares. CEO Joseph Onorati described the higher rate
as a step toward building CHAD into a primary digital credit
instrument, with the goal of broadening investor participation as the
company scales its SOL accumulation strategy.
