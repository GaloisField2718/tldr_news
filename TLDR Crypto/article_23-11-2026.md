 A $2 MILLION EXPLOIT LEADS TO A $3.7 MILLION FREE-FOR-ALL (4 MINUTE
READ) [6]

 An attacker used a privileged bridge-minter role to mint billions,
then quintillions, of unbacked ACE on BNB Chain and sold them for
about $2M, crashing the price to around $0.0001 against roughly $0.04
on exchanges. Traders then bought the cheap tokens and sent them
through Fusionist's bridge, emptying its 53.7 million real ACE (about
$3.7M) across 24 addresses. One wallet turned about $50 into $17,670.
About $1.87M of the attacker's proceeds went through Tornado Cash.
Fusionist told followers to pause ACE activity on BSC and hasn't
updated since, and Gate rolled back ACE/USDT trades, leaving some
users with negative balances, and will delist its ACE perp. ACE is
down about 20%.

 KALSHI FACES MONDAY HEARING ON NEW YORK ORDER (4 MINUTE READ) [7]

 A federal judge denied the CFTC's second emergency bid to block New
York AG Letitia James' enforcement case, clearing the way for a state
judge to hear her request for a temporary restraining order on Monday
at 11:30 AM ET. The proposed order would bar Kalshi from operating
"within or from New York," which Kalshi says would shut down its
exchange for every customer and force liquidation of open positions,
while James' office says that isn't its goal. Federal appeals courts
are split, with the Fourth, Sixth, and Ninth Circuits siding with
states and the Third with Kalshi, so the fight could reach the Supreme
Court. The CFTC and DOJ have a separate suit against New York pending
since April.

INNOVATION & LAUNCHES

 BACKPACK COMPLETES CFTC TRIPLE LICENSE STACK: DCO, DCM, AND FCM (2
MINUTE READ) [8]

 Backpack secured NFA approval for a Futures Commission Merchant (FCM)
license, completing the full CFTC regulatory trifecta alongside its
Derivatives Clearing Organization (DCO) and Designated Contract Market
(DCM) designations approved earlier this week. With all three licenses
held in-house, Backpack can operate as broker, exchange, and
clearinghouse without routing through third-party intermediaries, a
vertical stack Kraken assembled via $550M in acquisition costs. Only
two other US firms, Kraken and Kalshi, hold equivalent in-house
clearing capacity for leveraged perpetuals. The Solana-based exchange
positions itself as the first CFTC-regulated venue to offer leveraged
perps with unified global liquidity onshore, with a waitlist now open
for early access.

 TEMPO LAUNCHES ALTERNATIVE X402 FACILITATOR (2 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Tempo released an alternative facilitator for the x402 machine
payment protocol, allowing developers to accept USDC or Base payments
while routing settlements through Tempo's network rather than
Coinbase's official facilitator. Migration from the reference
implementation requires a single URL change, lowering switching costs
for existing x402 service operators. The launch, endorsed by
Paradigm's Georgios Konstantopoulos, introduces direct competition for
x402's settlement layer and reduces developer dependence on Coinbase's
infrastructure for agentic payment routing.

 ANATOMY OF A FILL: PROPAMMS ON ETHEREUM (7 MINUTE READ) [11]

 PropAMMs, live on Ethereum mainnet since May, decouple the 12-second
Ethereum slot clock from the quote clock by letting a single market
maker stream signed price updates to block builders, cutting
stale-price exposure from up to 12 seconds to roughly 1 second per
fill. Fermi and Metric together cleared $458M in the week of September
14-20, capturing 7.6% of Uniswap's $6B that week, with Fermi turning
over its $5.4M liquidity pool 23 times daily versus Uniswap v3's 0.6x.
The design shifts ordering authority to four builders (Titan, Quasar,
BuilderNet, and Bombora) operating on voluntary commitments with no
protocol enforcement, Titan alone routing 69% of propAMM trades while
producing 44% of blocks. ERC-8324 proposes standardization but remains
undeployed, while PropAMMs account for roughly 40% of volume on the
largest Solana venues and 5-10% on Ethereum mainnet.

 HOW DO PEOPLE EARN FROM THE AI ECONOMY? (5 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Agents can expand what individuals can do and let more people share
in the AI economy. They can handle discovery, coordination, and
payment. Visa says its nearly 5 billion payment credentials can verify
that an agent is authorized by an accountable person through its
Trusted Agent Protocol, now extending to agent wallets and task
marketplaces including Taskmarket. It launched Visa CLI to US users as
an experimental agent wallet for OUSD, built on Tempo and the Machine
Payments Protocol. Visa has invited wallet and marketplace builders to
integrate.

 EIGEN FALLS 48% IN FOUR WEEKS, LAGGING BITCOIN, ETHER (2 MINUTE READ)
[13]

 EIGEN fell 48% over four weeks, lagging both Bitcoin and Ether, as
EigenLayer's restaking collateral contracted 28.5% to $4.52 billion
from its October 21 level. Token unlock pressure, with large tranches
of EIGEN becoming liquid, combined with reduced appetite for restaking
yield in the current rate environment, drove the sell-off.
EigenLayer's EigenCloud product launched during the drawdown but did
not stem the decline.

 THE AGENTIC STORY ON THE EVE OF THE 2026 HOLIDAY SEASON (1 MINUTE
READ) [14]

 Stripe reports daily spend on Link Agent Wallet, which powers Muse,
Instinct and Grok, was nearly 80 times higher in mid-November than at
the start of September, and doubled in the last month. Over 40% of
volume last week was airfare, travel, and lodging, about a third
retail, and a quarter other categories. More than half of consumers
who make one agentic purchase make a second, typically in the same
category. Stripe plans more updates around Black Friday and Cyber
Monday.
