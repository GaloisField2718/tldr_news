 TURKEY CONFIRMS HEX FOUNDER RICHARD HEART'S ARREST (3 MINUTE READ)
[4]

 Justice Minister Akın Gürlek confirmed Heart (Richard Schueler) was
detained in Istanbul on October 14 with his wife, and three bodyguards
were detained later. Searches of 15 addresses turned up $7.15M in
cash, six cold wallets, seven firearms, 1,400 rounds, 28 watches, and
97 pieces of jewelry. Heart has been under an Interpol Red Notice

judge dismissed the SEC's case on jurisdictional grounds in February
2025, and the SEC did not refile.

 LIGHTER EVM DEVNET LIVE WITH OPENVM AND AXIOM (3 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Lighter launched an EVM-equivalent rollup devnet, built with Axiom
and proved by OpenVM, running alongside Lighter Core, their existing
high-performance order book engine. Both execution layers settle to
Ethereum with a single shared ZK proof, so the EVM rollup and the
order book achieve joint finality in one proof cycle. Developers can
deploy smart contracts on the EVM layer while Lighter Core handles
order matching, with both environments settling atomically on Ethereum
rather than through separate proof pipelines.

 AI IS MAKING ETHEREUM MORE AMBITIOUS: FORMAL VERIFICATION AT SCALE (6
MINUTE READ) [7]

 AI agents formally verified Ethereum's Fast Confirmation Rule (FCR)
in Lean in 4-5 days, producing machine-checked safety proofs that
FCR-confirmed blocks remain canonical under its assumptions. When
researchers adapted the model to upcoming Gloas changes, the proof
system surfaced a spec-level fork-choice bug before it shipped,
pinpointing the exact failure cause. The Ethereum Foundation's earlier
investments in formal verification infrastructure and post-quantum
research now compound with AI tooling, with Lean already integrated
into the BLS, KZG, SSZ, and consensus specs. The combination enables
tighter fork timelines and more ambitious protocol features without
reducing the security bar, with the upcoming Hegotá upgrade as the
first test of this accelerated cadence.

 THE WALLET WARS (6 MINUTE READ) [9]

 AI agents require payment credentials to execute purchases, creating
two distinct competitive fronts: who supplies the credential and who
lets the agent transact with the user's full identity. Stripe's Link
and Shopify's Shop Pay have moved fastest on integration, but Stripe's
virtual-card approach routes purchases through a Stripe-issued card
rather than the user's original card, stripping loyalty rewards and
breaking direct merchant relationships. Visa, Mastercard, and AMEX are
building scoped agentic tokens that map transactions back to the
user's original card, preserving benefits that virtual-card
intermediaries remove. The competitive trajectory mirrors the
AOL-to-open-web shift: open-standard agentic tokens are positioned to
displace proprietary virtual cards as the dominant payment layer for
agents.

 GPUS ARE FINANCED FOR YEARS. AI WORKLOADS RUN FOR WEEKS (8 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 GPU supply contracts lock capacity for 36 months while AI workloads
demand compute for weeks. Hyperbolic exploits the duration mismatch by
buying multi-year bare-metal capacity at contracted rates and
reselling short-duration slots at a premium, growing from near-zero to
$80M annualized revenue run-rate between June 2025 and September 2026.
Neocloud lenders require contracted off-take, which structurally
biases supply toward long-term commitments, and Hyperbolic hedges Year
3 tail risk with put options on compute prices to protect the back
end. Exchange-traded GPU rental contracts are forming as compute
follows the commodity arc from bilateral deals to traded benchmarks,
with physical settlement as the foundation derivative indices build
on, mirroring electricity's 30-year path from vertically integrated
utilities to CME/ICE contracts.

 BIS: STABLECOINS AND BANK DEPOSITS ACT AS SUBSTITUTES (1 MINUTE READ)
[11]

 A BIS working paper using US data finds stablecoins and bank deposits
are direct portfolio substitutes. A 1-basis-point rise in bank deposit
rates cuts 10-week stablecoin market cap growth by about 3 percentage
points, and by up to about 9 under an IV approach. The effect is
stronger for USDC (-4.8%), which is tied to the US onshore market,
than for offshore-focused USDT (-1.9%). It also holds against bank CD
rates and DeFi lending yields. The conclusion is that bank deposit
pricing acts as a drag on stablecoin growth.

 Network State announced an acquisition of Farcaster, the
Ethereum-based decentralized social protocol whose Warpcast client
serves as the primary hub for crypto-native social activity. This
comes after the original Farcaster team handed off ownership of the
protocol to Neynar, a Farcaster-oriented developer platform.
