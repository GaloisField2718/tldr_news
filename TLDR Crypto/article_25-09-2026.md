MARKETS & BUSINESS

 CANADA'S BIG SIX BANKS EXPLORE TOKENIZED DEPOSIT SYSTEM (2 MINUTE
READ) [4]

 BMO, CIBC, National Bank of Canada, RBC, Scotiabank, and TD jointly
announced they're exploring a Canadian-dollar tokenized deposit
system, with the first phase focused on moving tokenized deposits
efficiently between institutions. The project follows Canada's OSFI
clarifying that tokenized deposits are "not legally distinct from
traditional deposits," and builds on March's Project Samara pilot
testing blockchain-based government bond issuance.

 CFTC WARNS PREDICTION CONTRACTS CARRY HEIGHTENED MANIPULATION RISK (3
MINUTE READ) [5]

 The CFTC's Division of Market Oversight issued an advisory warning
that prediction contracts betting on whether someone will "mention"
specific words or attend an event carry heightened manipulation risk,
and can only be listed in limited circumstances under the Commodity
Exchange Act. The agency has already charged a former White House
teleprompter operator and ex-Rep. George Santos over trades tied to
advance knowledge of "mention markets" on Kalshi, and it's telling
exchanges to assess whether the person controlling a contract's
outcome faces independent obligations that deter manipulation.

INNOVATION & LAUNCHES

 RAIN LAUNCHES REAL-TIME FUNDING FOR STABLECOIN CARDS (4 MINUTE READ)
[6]

 Rain introduced real-time funding, letting cardholders connect a
wallet and approve a spending limit once, with each purchase
authorizing only the exact amount needed directly from the wallet
instead of requiring users to pre-fund a separate collateral balance.
The feature is in beta for card programs where Rain manages
authorization, launching with USDC on Base and Arbitrum and USDT0 on
Plasma.

 CIRCLE OPENS BITCOIN-BACKED USDC BORROWING TO MINT CLIENTS, ROUTED
THROUGH MORPHO (3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Circle has launched a Bitcoin-backed USDC borrowing facility for Mint
clients through Morpho's Arc market, wrapping deposited BTC as cirBTC
to serve as collateral. The cirBTC/USDC market grew ~10x in five days,
from $1.37 million on September 17 to $14.3 million in borrowed USDC
against 287 cirBTC tokens. Galaxy and Keyrock supply the dollar
liquidity, while Circle handles cirBTC issuance and wallet
infrastructure, and Morpho governs loan terms. The structure gives
institutional Bitcoin holders access to USDC without liquidating their
BTC positions.

GUIDES & TUTORIALS

 CME ADDS BITCOIN CASH AND UNISWAP FUTURES AS CRYPTO DERIVATIVES PUSH
GROWS (3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 CME Group plans to list futures on Bitcoin Cash and Uniswap on
October 19, pending regulatory approval, with standard and Micro
contract sizes of 250/25 BCH and 10,000/1,000 UNI. The addition brings
CME's single-asset crypto derivatives roster to 11 tokens, with
Uniswap marking the first DEX governance token the exchange has
listed. CME averaged 279,800 contracts per day in H1 2026, about $8.3
billion in daily notional, while altcoin-specific contracts generated
more than $1 billion in notional for the year. The listings follow
CME's June launch of a 24/7 crypto futures and options market, as
institutional participants seek regulated venues for altcoin price
risk management across a widening asset set.

 POST-POSEIDON: HASH FUNCTION VARIANTS FOR ETHEREUM (5 MINUTE READ)
[9]

 The emergence of Flock as a practical post-quantum proof system for
binary circuits removes Ethereum's requirement for circuit-friendly
hashes like Poseidon, opening five protocol layers (consensus
signatures, CL aggregation, state tree construction, EL signatures,
and EL aggregation) to standard hash function candidates. Performance
targets set the bar at proving 2^25 bytes per second with LeanVM and
approximately 1 million hash calls per second, a throughput floor that
filters the field. BLAKE3 leads on raw speed at 7 GB/s parallel and
1.1 cycles per byte on long messages but carries no
indifferentiability proofs and a thin cryptanalytic record, and SHA-3
holds 50+ published cryptanalytic papers and sponge-mode
indifferentiability proofs but runs at only 0.2 GB/s natively. The
research ranks SHA-3 and BLAKE2s as the lowest-risk selections, with
SHA-2's length-extension property disqualifying it as a random-oracle
substitute despite 45+ cryptanalytic papers.

MISCELLANEOUS

 TETHER WALKS AWAY FROM EUROPE'S MICA STABLECOIN FRAMEWORK (5 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Tether abandoned MiCA compliance in the EU over a rule requiring 60%
of reserves in commercial bank deposits, with CEO Paolo Ardoino
arguing it would expose 400 million users to risk since EU deposit
insurance caps out at €100,000 per account. Tether holds roughly 80%
of its $183 billion in reserves in US Treasuries, and USDT was
delisted from all EU exchanges on July 1, though Ardoino said Tether
would reconsider "when MiCA becomes safer." The ECB and all 27 EU
central banks have separately called to scrap the 60% bank-deposit
rule.

 KALSHI REBUTS WASH TRADING ALLEGATIONS ON PERPETUALS PLATFORM (4
MINUTE READ) [11]

 Kalshi published a detailed rebuttal to wash trading allegations on
its perpetuals platform, arguing that its flat-fee liquidity incentive
programs mirror standard structures at CME, CBOE, and Nasdaq. The core
defense turns on trade economics: analyzed trades showed aggressors
captured roughly $98,000 in profit potential, a signal of genuine
price discovery rather than coordinated self-dealing. Kalshi also
cited 350,000+ lifetime perpetual traders, open interest doubling over
the past 30 days, and a July 2026 fee holiday structure for
Self-Clearing Members that caps rebates at exact fees paid per trade,
blocking the net-negative outcomes that would characterize artificial
volume.
