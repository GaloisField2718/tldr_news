 OCC DEBUTS FINAL RULE IMPLEMENTING TRUMP-SIGNED GENIUS ACT (3 MINUTE
READ) [4]

 The OCC is the first regulator to finalize its GENIUS Act stablecoin
rule. Effective January 18, it creates pathways for banks and
non-banks to issue payment stablecoins, sets standards for foreign
issuers, and sets standards for custody. The final rule drops a
presumption in the proposal that certain issuer relationships with
third parties could be unlawful, since the OCC saw no “nascent
market” for it. The ban on issuers directly paying interest stays.
An OCC official said third-party reward arrangements will be judged
case by case, with authority to act against clear evasion. Rewards are
also a main sticking point in the stalled Senate CLARITY Act.

 UTAH LIKENS KALSHI TO A ‘BACK-ALLEY GAMBLING DEN' (3 MINUTE READ)
[5]

 Utah's attorney general sued five Kalshi companies plus Susquehanna
International Group and Susquehanna Government Products for running an
illegal gambling business. The complaint says calling bets “event
contracts” doesn't change what Kalshi offers. It also targets the
firms on the other side of user bets, which sports attorney Daniel
Wallach called the first state enforcement action against
prediction-market market makers. Utah estimates Kalshi collected
$30.6M to $61.2M in fees from Utah accounts through October 19. It
wants sports, election, politics, culture, and “mentions” bets
blocked, tighter location checks, and money returned plus damages.

INNOVATION & LAUNCHES

 MASTERCARD AGENTS NOW PAYING FOR DIGITAL SERVICES VIA COINBASE X402
(1 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Mastercard's AI agents can now pay for digital services on-chain via
Coinbase's x402 protocol and Cloudflare, making Mastercard one of the
first major card networks to deploy HTTP-native agentic payment rails
at scale. They released Agent Pay for Machines (AP4M), a pilot
protocol that lets merchants get card-network settlement guarantees
while settling onchain via Base and Coinbase's x402 facilitator.

 CELESTIA FIBRE GOES LIVE ON MAINNET (2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Celestia deployed Fibre on mainnet, its largest protocol upgrade
since the network's 2023 launch, achieving 3.07 Tb/s data availability
throughput for rollups and modular chains dependent on the DA layer.
The upgrade targets throughput demands for global financial settlement
and AI agent-driven commerce, extending Celestia's modular
architecture into workloads at a scale well beyond its prior DA
capacity. TIA gained 59% over the four weeks before the launch and
reached a 2026 high of $0.68 as traders positioned ahead of the
upgrade.

 MORPHO BRINGS RECEIVABLE-BACKED CREDIT MARKETS ONCHAIN VIA MORPHO
MIDNIGHT (4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Morpho is bringing receivable-backed credit markets onchain through
Morpho Midnight, enabling businesses to borrow against accounts
receivable via programmable fixed-rate lending with 100% capital
utilization and automated repayment priority enforcement. The launch
targets a market where global factoring turnover reached €4.04
trillion in 2025, with Morpho's stated goal of capturing a portion of
the $200T global credit market onchain. Core technical requirements
include onchain proof of receivables, anti-double-financing
mechanisms, and compliance-based access controls, allowing global
capital to compete openly for deal flow. The product extends DeFi
collateral from wallet-held crypto assets to business cash flows,
broadening the asset base that onchain lending markets can underwrite.


 FIVE EMERGING DEFI PROJECTS WORTH WATCHING (5 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Mezzanine applies TradFi-style tranching to single-asset yield
vaults, splitting deposits into junior, mezzanine, and senior
tranches, with its debut Hastra Optimizer vault audited by Sherlock
and ChainSecurity and verified by Certora ahead of launch. INFINIT's
APEX MultiStrategy vault returned 7.18% over 82 days (31.94% APR) on
$506K USDC by trading long/short across crypto, equities, and
commodities on Hyperliquid using regime-detection filters that sit out
when no setups clear its scoring threshold. IRIS overlays fixed-rate,
fixed-term loans atop Morpho Blue and Aave V3 via solvers who post
bonds to cover the fixed-floating spread, removing the need for a
standalone liquidity pool. RISEx runs a perps orderbook on-chain
inside the RISE L2 EVM with 43 markets and $39M open interest, while
Boros expands its funding-rate swap infrastructure to cross-venue
spreads between Hyperliquid and Gate, with the short side quoting
25-35% fixed APR at launch.

 BLOCKCHAIN ASSOCIATION SUBMITS SEC COMMENTS ON PROPOSED REGULATION (3
MINUTE READ) [10]

 The Blockchain Association submitted a formal comment letter to the
SEC on its proposed Regulation Crypto Assets framework, offering
recommendations to improve practical workability for builders,
investors, and market participants. The SEC's proposal marks the
agency's most comprehensive digital asset rulemaking effort to date,
and the public comment period gives industry groups a formal channel
to influence the final framework. The Association, backed by major
crypto companies, characterized the proposal as a meaningful step
toward fit-for-purpose US crypto rules while flagging specific
implementation concerns in its letter.

 BLOOMBERG'S JOE WEISENTHAL RECONSIDERS HIS SKEPTICISM OF BLOCKCHAIN
TECHNOLOGY (2 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Bloomberg journalist Joe Weisenthal, who spent years dismissing the
"blockchain not Bitcoin" camp, posted a reassessment acknowledging
that separating Bitcoin's speculative nature from blockchain's
underlying utility may have been a defensible position. The post drew
nearly 3,000 likes and 292 retweets from a mainstream finance
audience, reflecting how widely his credibility extends in TradFi
circles where that skepticism has persisted. The reversal arrives as
enterprise blockchain adoption in settlement, tokenization, and
custody has grown concrete enough to pull even longtime critics toward
reexamination.
