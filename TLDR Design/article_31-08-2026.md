 APPLE APPARENTLY TESTED A STYLUS FOR THE FOLDABLE IPHONE, THEN NIXED
THE IDEA (3 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Apple reportedly explored and even prototyped a smaller Apple Pencil
designed for the upcoming foldable iPhone Ultra, with plans for
magnetic attachment and charging similar to the iPad. However, the
company apparently abandoned the idea because a stylus could interfere
with the folding mechanism and potentially damage the softer surface
of the foldable display. While an Apple Pencil is unlikely to launch
alongside the first-generation foldable iPhone, the concept may return
in future models if foldable screen technology becomes more durable.

 GEMINI OMNI 1.1 FLASH LETS YOU BUILD WITH MORE CONTROL (2 MINUTE
READ) [5]

 Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash gives developers more control over AI-generated
video, moving the model closer to a production-ready creative tool
rather than a simple video generator. The update can extend existing
scenes while maintaining visual and narrative consistency, generate
smooth transitions between specified first and last frames, and use
video references to preserve characters and motion. It also adds
faster, lower-cost 360p drafts for experimentation and upscaling to
1080p or 4K for finished work.

 APPLE WILL BE LAUNCHING ITS FIRST PAIR OF SMART GLASSES NEXT YEAR:
HERE'S WHAT'S COMING (3 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Apple is reportedly preparing AI-powered smart glasses for launch in
2027 that focus on voice and camera-based assistance rather than
augmented reality displays. The glasses would use Siri and Visual
Intelligence to analyze the user's surroundings, answer questions,
provide context, translate signs, and save visual reminders, while
also functioning as open-ear audio devices. Apple is developing
multiple frame styles and premium finishes in-house, with future
versions potentially adding health features such as heart-rate
monitoring. Unlike Meta's smart glasses, Apple may limit photo and
video capture due to privacy concerns. Current reports suggest the
product will be unveiled at WWDC 2027 before shipping later that year.

 AI-GENERATED IMAGES CAN PERFORM AS WELL AS STOCK PHOTOGRAPHY (7
MINUTE READ) [7]

 In a study of 77 participants rating a fictional consulting firm's
webpage, AI-generated hero images produced no perception penalty
against real stock photos. Ratings for trustworthiness,
professionalism, and authenticity were slightly higher for the AI
images, though only the authenticity difference reached statistical
significance, by 0.4 points. Participants who suspected AI involvement
rated pages less favorably, and perceived diversity and representation
shaped impressions more than image source.

 RETHINKING DATA VISUALIZATION: A UX APPROACH TO DASHBOARDS THAT
ACTUALLY DRIVES DECISIONS (16 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Roughly 80% of what determines whether a dashboard succeeds happens
before any chart is drawn, in defining context, audience and the
intended insight. Dashboards fail not because the data is thin but
because nobody designed them to answer a specific operational
question. A B2B talent-management project shows how radar charts,
product-coded color, and a comparison view turned passive reporting
into weekly planning.

 AI CAN'T REPLACE REAL RESEARCH IN EMPATHY MAPPING (5 MINUTE READ) [9]


 Empathy maps are most valuable when they are built from real user
research, such as interviews, usability testing, support tickets, and
field observations. While AI can generate believable user quotes,
emotions, and behaviors, these outputs are assumptions rather than
evidence and often lack the specific context that reveals genuine user
needs and pain points. AI is best used to organize, cluster,
summarize, and synthesize existing research data, not to invent users
or fill gaps where research is missing. Relying on AI-generated
empathy-map content can create a false sense of understanding and lead
teams to make decisions based on fictional users instead of real-world
insights.

 WHAT CRITICAL THINKING MEANS FOR SENIOR DESIGNERS (AND HOW TO APPLY
IT) (5 MINUTE READ) [12]

 As AI tools put design output within everyone's reach, the senior
designer's job shifts from producing the artifact to justifying it in
language non-designers can act on. Spotting that a Figma Make mockup
breaks is craft - critical thinking is translating that feeling into
behavioral and business consequences. Ask what path a screen requires
of the user, then what competes with it.

 ON UNFURLING INTERFACES (7 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Small interface surfaces work better when they unfold from the edge
or control that caused them, rather than arriving through a generic
fade and scale. The closed state is a narrow seam built with an inset
clip path that preserves the surface's own radius, so corners never
square off mid-reveal. Components share a small motion language while
keeping their own placement, accessibility, and native lifecycle.
