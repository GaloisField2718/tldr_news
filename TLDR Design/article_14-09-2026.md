 WHATSAPP ROLLING OUT REDESIGNED IPAD INTERFACE WITH MAC-LIKE SIDEBAR
(1 MINUTE READ) [4]

 WhatsApp is expanding its Liquid Glass redesign for iPad with a new
Mac-style sidebar that replaces the bottom tab bar, offering quicker
access to chats, calls, communities, Meta AI, and other sections.
Users can hide the sidebar for a larger conversation view, creating a
more tablet-friendly experience, and the update is gradually rolling
out to beta testers and some users on the stable App Store version.

 PREMIERE ADDS AI MEDIA GENERATION (5 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Premiere's new Generative Media tool lets editors fill timeline gaps
with AI-generated video and sound effects without leaving the project.
Editors can pick among models like Adobe Firefly, Google Veo, Runway,
Luma, and Kling, while beta tools add music and soundscape generation,
speaker separation, and an After Effects AI Assistant.

 FIREFOX IS GETTING ITS BIGGEST REDESIGN IN YEARS. THEN MOZILLA GOT
COLD FEET (2 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Firefox 157 brings Project Nova's warmer colors, softer tabs,
refreshed icons, and rounded corners across desktop and mobile. Early
designs had floating toolbars, chrome-to-page gaps and pastel
gradients, but complaints about wasted space pushed Mozilla to soften
the rounding and restore Compact Mode. Public testing and listening to
what users hated counts as good UX, even as the rounded,
gradient-heavy look risks modernizing Firefox into sameness.

 THE IPHONE DUO GIVES IOS ROOM TO BREATHE, AND IT'S A WHOLE NEW
EXPERIENCE (4 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Apple's iPhone Duo introduces a foldable-optimized version of iOS 27
with redesigned navigation, seamless transitions between its two
displays, Split View multitasking, flexible foldable modes,
dual-screen camera features, and upcoming Apple Pencil support. While
many ideas borrow from existing foldables, Apple's strength lies in
refining the software experience, making the Duo feel like a cohesive
blend of iPhone and iPad rather than simply a folding phone.

 TEST COMPLEX INTERACTIONS EARLIER WITH AI PROTOTYPING (4 MINUTE READ)
[8]

 AI prototyping tools make it possible to quickly create realistic,
interactive prototypes for complex interfaces—such as dashboards,
filters, and conversational AI—allowing teams to test real user
behavior and uncover edge cases much earlier in the design process.
While AI dramatically speeds up execution, designers must still define
the interaction, provide clear guidance, and critically review the
results, as polished prototypes are not production-ready simply
because they look complete.

 THE DEATH OF THE BUTTON: WHY THE BEST INTERFACE IS NO INTERFACE (10
MINUTE READ) [9]

 A new wave of "Intent-Driven Design" replaces multi-step
point-and-click interfaces with tools that capture a user's goal and
execute the steps invisibly. Perplexity AI synthesizes answers instead
of ten blue links, Vercel v0 generates full interfaces from a text
prompt, and Goblin.tools breaks overwhelming tasks into checklists.
This shift rests on contextual goals, generative UI, and agent
execution, but risks a loss of control and opaque "black box"
decisions needing transparent feedback.

MISCELLANEOUS

 A SHORT HISTORY OF LOGOS MADE OF DATA (12 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Logos that embed real data—not just symbolic references—have
existed for over a century, from transit maps and scientific diagrams
to charts, weather-driven identities, and geographic forms, creating
visual identities that change or derive directly from meaningful
information. While these logos often require explanation to reveal
their deeper meaning, they represent a distinct category of branding
where data actively shapes the design rather than simply inspiring it.


 HOW KENT WOOD PIVOTED TO MOTION DESIGN AT 30, AND WHAT HE'S LEARNED
SO FAR (4 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Motion designer Kent Wood left a struggling wholesale business, moved
from the Caribbean to the UK, and launched Kwoo Studio, specializing
in motion identity systems that bring static brands to life. By openly
sharing his career transition and work online, he has built a network,
attracted new opportunities, and encouraged other creatives to embrace
change and use their online presence as a digital business card.

 ANATOMY OF AI INPUT (5 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Today's AI input has evolved far beyond the plain textbox and send
button ChatGPT shipped in 2022, now layering auto-growing text,
context bars, and file uploads. Controls like model selectors, tool
triggers, and an assist layer of ghost text or suggestions should stay
discreet and keyboard-accessible without overwhelming the field.
Across idle, loading, streaming, and error states, the input must
remain typable and never lock up, letting users edit or stop a
response at any time.
