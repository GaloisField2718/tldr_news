 CANVA ENTERS THE AI IMAGE GENERATION RACE WITH CRAFT, AND IT'S
THINKING BEYOND PRETTY PICTURES (2 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Canva Craft is the company's first in-house image generation model.
Built specifically for design work such as posters, presentations,
social graphics, and other layouts where accurate text and composition
matter, its two-stage process first plans object placement, scale,
hierarchy, and typography before generating images at up to 2K
resolution across 30 aspect ratios, with Magic Layers able to convert
results into editable designs. Trained on millions of human-created
Canva templates, Craft is available through Dream Lab and Canva's API,
with integrations for AI assistants and faster Craft variants also
planned.

 SOME IPHONE DUO DISPLAYS ARE CRACKING, SEEMINGLY ALL IN THE SAME WAY
(2 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Some iPhone Duo owners are reporting inner displays cracking along
the center hinge, typically accompanied by a horizontal block of
pixels stuck glowing white. Around ten separate cases have surfaced so
far, sometimes during initial setup, with a defective or misaligned
hinge component emerging as one possible explanation. Most affected
Apple Store customers appear to have received replacements, and the
small number of reports means it remains unclear whether this
represents a broader hardware problem.

 THE AMBITIOUS EA SPORTS REBRAND LOOKS GREAT FROM EVERY ANGLE (2
MINUTE READ) [6]

 DixonBaxi has rebranded EAFC27, turning the triangular cursor logo
adopted after the 2023 split from FIFA into a free-form shape that
changes with context. The studio behind the F1 and Roblox identities
left TV sports conventions behind for a look closer to TikTok and
Twitch. The system is built to move from campaign to culture, letting
players of every playstyle, age, and background make the brand their
own.

 WEBSITES SHOULD BE PRINTABLE (3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Websites containing substantial text can provide a much better
printed experience with a relatively small set of dedicated CSS rules.
Print styles can remove navigation, introduce print-only information,
resize typography, adapt code blocks, and turn colored links black so
pages work better on paper. Supporting printing may be a niche
requirement, but it reflects a broader approach to good software in
which small, thoughtful accommodations give users more ways to
experience content.

 HALF MY DESIGN SYSTEM METRICS DON'T WORK ANYMORE (7 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Traditional design system metrics such as total component counts,
downloads, MCP calls, and self-reported agent success reveal little
about whether a system actually produces better work. More useful
measures include conformance, accessibility, design-code parity,
hard-coded values, component friction, agent adherence, cost per
shippable output, and how quickly humans and agents can successfully
build with the system. As AI agents become design system users, teams
also need repeatable evals that give models real tasks and score their
output against intended design principles, implementation rules, and
visual quality.

 YOUR PRODUCT IS NOT THE CENTER OF THE UNIVERSE (4 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Established products still assume they sit at the center of users'
workflows, a model nearing its end as personal agents work across many
tools at once. In its place, products show up wherever the user's
agent works, expose their capabilities to any agent, and rely on its
memory. In-conversation UI, domain expertise, proprietary data, and
fulfillment like hosting, shipping, and payments keep them from
becoming commodities.

 FONT PAIRINGS 2026: WE CHECKED THE FONTS ON 2,190 WEBSITES DESIGNERS
LOVED. MOST OF THEM USED JUST ONE (12 MINUTE READ) [14]

 An analysis of 2,190 design-gallery websites found that 60% use the
same typeface for headlines and body copy, relying on differences in
scale, spacing, and weight rather than traditional font pairings.
Among the 871 sites using two families, there were 811 different
combinations, while Inter was the most common overall typeface, PP
Neue Montreal led paid options, and monospace fonts frequently
appeared as a third family for labels and small text. The data
suggests contemporary web typography is increasingly defined by craft
within a small number of families, with regular-weight, tightly
tracked large headlines and carefully controlled spacing doing more
visual work than elaborate pairings.

 EAST SURREY COUNCIL REVEALS IN-HOUSE BRAND IDENTITY AHEAD OF 2027
LAUNCH (3 MINUTE READ) [15]

 East Surrey Council's new identity was developed entirely in-house by
designers from six local authorities at no additional cost to
taxpayers ahead of the authority's April 2027 launch. The selected
mark uses layered leaf-like forms to reference the region's contours
and countryside while hiding a lowercase “e,” with colors
representing woodland, countryside, community, waterways, and
heritage, and Lexend providing accessible primary typography. The
identity will be introduced gradually as existing signage, vehicles,
and other assets reach scheduled replacement points, minimizing waste
and transition costs.

 EVERYONE IS STARTING OVER (5 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Designers at every career stage are effectively starting over, as
tools, expectations, and team structures shift fast enough to turn old
strengths into liabilities. Reinvention is a habit that demands
tolerance for embarrassment. Critical thinking is its counterweight -
test each new idea so openness to change never means becoming easy to
fool.
