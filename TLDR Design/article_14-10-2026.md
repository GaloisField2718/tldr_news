NEWS & TRENDS

 PRIZE-WINNING IMAGE WHICH SPARKED BACKLASH WAS AI-GENERATED, NIKON
RULES (2 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Nikon has disqualified the winning video of its Small World in Motion
microscopy contest, ruling that generative AI used in its composition
broke the rules. Scientists, including former judges, had flagged the
footage of cilia in a child's airway as fake. The camera-maker is now
reviewing the procedures of the annual contest, which has run for 15
years.

 AS THEY SUPPOSEDLY BATTLE AI SLOP, MAJOR PUBLISHERS REPORTEDLY COVER
THEIR BOOKS IN IT (2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Three of the big five publishers reportedly use AI for cover art and
publicity copy without telling authors or the public. Wired's report,
drawn from more than two dozen anonymous employees, names tools
including ChatGPT, Claude, and image generators. Leadership subscribes
to AI tools and runs brainstorming sessions asking staff to find uses,
even as the big five sue Meta over copyright.

 FACEBOOK IS GIVING USERS MORE CONTROL OVER THEIR ALGORITHM (2 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Facebook's new “Your Algorithm” feature lets users describe in
their own words what they want to see more or less of in Feed and
Reels, with Meta saying recommendations update in real time. An
updated three-dot menu provides access to the tool alongside
“Interested” and “Not interested” options, and Facebook may
ask follow-up questions to understand users' feedback. The launch
brings controls already introduced on Instagram to Facebook, giving
users a more direct way to shape their recommendations.

OPINIONS & TUTORIALS

 THE FAKE DOUBLE DIAMOND IS DEAD. THE REAL DOUBLE DIAMOND IS IMMORTAL
(6 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Synthetic users and AI-generated insights offer the false certainty
of up-front discovery without its substance, and they never remove the
need to iterate. Shipping everything and letting analytics decide
fails too, because real experiments need a hypothesis, an end point,
and cleanup of failed bets. The fake double diamond trades real
divergence for synthetic urgency. Useful software needs problem
definition and iteration in one shared system.

 WHAT ARE WE WILLING TO SACRIFICE FOR AI? (7 MINUTE READ) [10]

 If AI eventually allows companies to produce goods and services with
far fewer workers, economies built around exchanging labor for income
may face a fundamental contradiction. Three broad responses each
demand a sacrifice: businesses could preserve jobs at the expense of
potential profits, society could restrict AI and forgo some of its
benefits, or economic systems could provide guaranteed income or
broader ownership while accepting greater institutional dependence.
The deeper question is whether existing ideas about liberty and
economic independence can survive if meaningful opportunities to earn
a living through work become scarce.

 FIX THE CONTRAST WITHOUT LOSING YOUR BRAND (3 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Low-contrast text appears on 83.9% of the top million home pages,
making it the web's most common accessibility failure. Clearleft's
tool “This hue shall pass” maps every pairing in a palette against
WCAG AA or AAA and fixes failures by nudging lightness. Hue stays
fixed, so brand colors keep their character while passing contrast.

 TYPETYPE UNVEILS TT RATIONALIST TYPOGRAPHY (3 MINUTE READ) [16]

 TypeType's updated TT Rationalist modernizes the slab serif with
trapezoidal serifs, softer stroke contrast, humanist proportions, and
curved details that make dense text feel less rigid and more
comfortable to read. The family spans 22 styles, including ten upright
weights, matching true italics, and two variable fonts, with 1,127
glyphs per style and 31 OpenType features. Support for more than 235
Latin- and Cyrillic-based languages makes the family flexible enough
for everything from editorial layouts and cultural posters to apps,
watch interfaces, and compact digital typography.

 PENTAGRAM CREATES DYNAMIC BILINGUAL IDENTITY FOR DESIGN DOHA BIENNIAL
2026 (4 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Pentagram's identity for the 2026 Design Doha Biennial replaces a
static logo with a flexible typographic system built around
connectivity and equal treatment of Arabic and English. Extended
strokes stretch between elements like mapping lines or network
constellations, while regional travertine beige and deep maroon are
combined with bright orange and the blue established by the inaugural
edition. The same structural logic extends across sub-brands and
exhibitions, allowing individual programs to develop distinct
expressions while remaining part of a coherent bilingual system.
