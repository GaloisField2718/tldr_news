 ARTIST SUES AI MEME GENERATOR FOR SELLING DEEPLY PERSONAL COMIC AS AD
TEMPLATE (6 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Filipino artist Elmer Saflor sued Memes Apps, alleging its AI meme
generator sells subscriptions that reproduce his viral "Running Away
Balloon" comic as an ad template without permission. Legal expert Eric
Goldman notes precedent favors Saflor, since prior rulings suggest
meme use in ads isn't fair use, though proving harm and targeting the
generator rather than advertisers could complicate the case. Saflor
seeks a court order halting the template's use and an accounting of ad
profits, framing the suit as a broader push to protect creators'
rights as AI platforms scale.

 GEORGE CLOONEY, TOM HANKS, AND MERYL STREEP BACK NEW ‘HUMAN CONSENT
STANDARD' FOR AI LICENSING (2 MINUTE READ) [5]

 A group of Hollywood actors and producers, including George Clooney,
Tom Hanks, and Meryl Streep, are backing a new "Human Consent
Standard" letting people set terms for how AI systems use their
likeness and creative work. Overseen by the nonprofit RSL Media, the
standard builds on last year's Really Simple Licensing Standard and
lets AI systems check permissions via robots.txt and a registry
launching in June. Unlike RSL, which applies to content at specific
URLs, the new standard covers a person's identity, character, or work
wherever it appears online.

 META AI IS MOVING INTO YOUR THREADS DMS, WHERE AWKWARD AI CHATS
BELONG (2 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Meta is moving Meta AI into Threads DMs after feedback showed that
many users were uncomfortable interacting with AI publicly in their
feeds. Users can privately ask questions about posts, conversations,
and other topics, with options to delete chats or mute the AI account.
Meta is still testing public Meta AI interactions in the Threads feed
in a limited number of countries while it gathers more feedback.

 I HANDED A UX REVIEW OVER TO AI. HERE'S WHAT HAPPENED (6 MINUTE READ)
[7]

 A UX practitioner tested Claude Fable 5 and ChatGPT-5.6 Sol against
manual analysis on a real portal review, feeding both models the same
brief, research, and analytics data. Both AI models generated reviews
filled with irrelevant or invented issues, though Claude proved
somewhat more accurate and produced a single coherent document. The
author concluded AI works best as a partner grounded in expert-defined
heuristics rather than a replacement, and plans to build a custom
"agent skill" encoding personal UX judgment for future projects.

 HOW FIGMA STAYS AHEAD OF VULNERABILITIES WITH AGENTS (17 MINUTE READ)
[8]

 Figma's security team built agentic systems that guard code
generation, review pull requests, and audit its decade-old monorepo,
all governed by one shared policy. Prioritizing precision over recall
proved essential, since historical bugs only measure recall while
trust requires developers see mostly valid findings—the team hit 70%
precision before enabling PR comments, later reaching 80%. The
resulting policy became a de facto threat model, powering hooks that
steer secure code generation, adjudication passes that boost recall,
and feedback loops that continuously refine detection from bug bounty
reports and false-positive dismissals.

 A CONCRETE DEFINITION OF “PRODUCT SENSE” (AND HOW TO BUILD IT) (7
MINUTE READ) [9]

 As AI takes over more execution work, product sense—the ability to
decide what to build—is becoming increasingly valuable, but it
requires more than consuming product critiques, research, and industry
knowledge. Strong product sense develops by repeatedly completing the
full product cycle: understanding problems, making decisions,
implementing solutions, measuring outcomes, and reflecting on whether
experience-based patterns actually applied. Building reliable
intuition therefore requires staying close enough to your work to see
its consequences, while recognizing when unfamiliar or misleading
situations demand evidence rather than instinct.

 ACCESSIBILITY IS RESISTANCE! (8 MINUTE READ) [13]

 A blind author explains why they rely heavily on AI tools, using an
inaccessible EAA document and image-poor product pages as examples.
Screen readers, ChatGPT/Claude, Meta Ray-Bans, and apps like Oorion
help them navigate text, describe images, and locate objects
independently. They distinguish "AI as augmentation" from "AI as
compensation," arguing better human-built accessibility would reduce
reliance on AI, and urge readers to prioritize accessibility as a form
of resistance.

 PLAYFUL RETAIL IS SERIOUS DESIGN WORK (4 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Interactive retail spaces that feel spontaneous, like the Coach Charm
Playground at Selfridges, actually require immense hidden planning and
discipline to execute safely and smoothly, according to Studio XAG.
The project involved navigating regulations, testing over 150 sample
sets, and executing overnight installs with precision compared to a
Formula One pit stop. Key lessons highlighted include protecting the
core idea through production, learning from workshop fabricators, and
prioritizing real human behavior over portfolio aesthetics.
