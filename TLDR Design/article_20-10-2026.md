 MICROSOFT REDESIGNS PHOTOS FOR WINDOWS 11 (1 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Microsoft's redesigned Photos for Windows 11 adds a new Gallery,
Editor, and Viewer, rolling out first to Windows Insiders, with wider
availability planned later in 2026. Gallery merges local, OneDrive,
and iCloud photos with natural-language search, while the Editor
groups tools into Crop, Enhance, Repair, and Stylize.

 REPLIT MAKES OPENAI ITS DEFAULT OVER ANTHROPIC, CEO SAYS (3 MINUTE
READ) [5]

 Replit's CEO initially said the AI coding platform had stopped
working with Anthropic over cost, partnership, and performance,
claiming ChatGPT now builds vibe-coded apps better. Insiders familiar
with the partnership rejected that, with one saying it remains in
place. Replit's site and posts show Anthropic models in use as
recently as a few weeks ago. He later clarified that OpenAI is the
default, while Claude remains available.

 WHATSAPP WANTS TO MAKE STICKERS LOUDER AND CHATS A LITTLE LESS
CRAMPED (3 MINUTE READ) [6]

 WhatsApp is testing sound stickers on Android that let users attach
up to 10 seconds of recorded or existing audio to custom stickers,
while video-based stickers can retain five seconds of their original
sound. Another option removes the outline around custom stickers for a
cleaner appearance. A redesigned two-line compose box is also being
tested, separating the typing field from additional controls to create
more space and accommodate future shortcuts.

 PRODUCT DESIGNER IS NO LONGER A JOB DESCRIPTION (8 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Product designer no longer describes a single job, so four attributes
(Style, Strategy, Builder, and Operator) offer a shared vocabulary for
candidates and hiring managers. Craft is the floor beneath all four.
Most strong designers lead with one or two.

 WHERE DECISIONS LIVE (4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Giving different kinds of project information distinct homes makes
both teams and AI agents better at distinguishing current decisions
from outdated discussions and unfinished thinking. A simple system can
keep chronological observations and evolving ideas in running notes
while maintaining a separate dated decision log that records what is
currently true without deleting previous decisions. AI makes large
amounts of context searchable, but chronology and structure remain
essential because a searchable mess is still a mess.

 UX DESIGNERS ARE NOT DESIGNING INTERFACES, THEY DESIGN PERSONALITIES
(12 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Digital products increasingly translate human behavior into metrics
such as sleep scores, ratings, streaks, story points, and follower
counts, which can gradually influence how people understand themselves
and behave. These measurements expose only selected parts of reality,
yet their apparent objectivity can give them greater authority than
unmeasured experiences, relationships, and intuition. UX designers
therefore shape more than interfaces and experiences: by deciding
which metrics products emphasize, they influence how people perceive
themselves and should deliberately leave room for realities that
cannot be quantified.

 WCAG 3: A DIFFERENT FUTURE FOR ACCESSIBILITY (7 MINUTE READ) [13]

 WCAG 3, still a draft, is the first ground-up rebuild of the standard
since 1999, proposing to replace binary pass/fail with a graded model
where meeting core requirements is a floor. Bronze, silver, and gold
levels would let organizations be recognized for going further.
Accessibility laws point to WCAG, so the change must keep regulators
able to adopt it.

 FULHAM F.C GETS A BRAND REFRESH BUILT ON HAND-RENDERED LETTERING
FOUND ON THE WALLS OF ITS STADIUM (5 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Fulham F.C's refreshed identity by Music and F37 Foundry is built
around FFC Original, a bespoke typeface inspired by hand-rendered
lettering discovered inside Craven Cottage, London's oldest football
stadium. Rather than placing historical references alongside a
contemporary identity, the system uses the club's physical heritage as
the source of its modern visual language. The typography now extends
across matchday graphics, campaigns, retail, moving image, and a
2026/27 away-kit campaign inspired by the visual language of the
1990s.

 STRUCTURE FIRST: THE INVERTED PYRAMID AND VISUAL ORGANIZATION (5
MINUTE READ) [15]

 The inverted pyramid makes web content easier to consume by putting
the essential message first and progressively adding supporting
detail, ensuring readers understand the key point even if they stop
early. Individual paragraphs should similarly lead with their main
idea, while descriptive headings, short paragraphs, lists, and
selective emphasis provide visual entry points for people scanning
rather than reading linearly. A useful test is whether someone can
understand something meaningful from a page within five seconds
without reading its body copy. AI-generated writing is particularly
worth checking for conclusions buried beneath unnecessary setup.
