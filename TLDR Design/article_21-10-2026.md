 BLUESKY EXPANDS ITS AI-POWERED ATTIE APP TO LET ANYONE BUILD
WEBSITES, GAMES, AND MORE (2 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Bluesky's experimental Attie platform now lets everyday users build
websites, quizzes, games, and polls, experiences that previously only
software developers could make. CEO Toni Schneider calls it "social
vibe coding," with creations tied to ATProto identities and able to
reach Bluesky's 47 million registered users. Support for private data,
the top developer request, is in alpha with a rollout planned by
year's end.

 IPHONE DUO ORDERS LIMITED TO TWO PER CUSTOMER AS APPLE TACKLES
SCALPING (2 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Apple is limiting iPhone Duo purchases to two per customer across
markets including the US, UK, Japan, and Spain after shipping dates
quickly slipped into January 2027. The restriction applies regardless
of configuration and appears designed to reduce scalping, with
resellers already listing devices for more than $5,000. Purchase
limits cannot eliminate organized scalping entirely, but they could
make bulk buying more difficult and improve availability for regular
customers.

 FIGMA CO-FOUNDER DYLAN FIELD THINKS MCP IS NOT A PERFECT PROTOCOL (2
MINUTE READ) [6]

 Figma is adding 20 MCP partners even as co-founder Dylan Field argues
that the protocol favors frontier AI labs and needs improvement. He
says the company wants people to use their Figma data but must limit
exposure to reduce potential abuse. Field also pointed to unequal
sharing of context across platforms and said Figma plans to support
the open-source tool Pi.

 TOP ACCESSIBILITY ERRORS IN PRODUCTION AND HOW TO AVOID THEM (10
MINUTE READ) [7]

 Almost 96% of the top million home pages fail at least one automated
accessibility check, and the same errors have led WebAIM's report for
seven years. Low contrast, missing alt text, unlabeled inputs, and
empty links and buttons top the list, each fixable without a rebuild.
Automated scans catch only about 30 to 40 percent of issues, so
keyboard and screen reader testing still has to happen before release.


 PURE UI IN THE AGENTIC ERA (8 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Treating UI as a pure function of state lets developers see how a
screen varies across roles, plans, billing statuses, and flags, which
matters more now that agents take on front-end implementation work. In
a worked example, an agent that tested only an owner account reported
success on a delete action, yet viewers still saw the button despite a
“View only” header. Rendering every state combination side by side
exposes such gaps, so teams should make display-relevant state
explicit and ask what an agent actually checked before merging.

 “YOU'VE ALREADY PROOF-TESTED THE ANSWER FOR YEARS” (4 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 A formal design degree is not necessarily required to build a
successful creative career when years of experience, skills,
self-study, and leadership already demonstrate professional ability.
The 70-20-10 learning model suggests that most professional
development comes from practical experience and relationships, with
formal education accounting for a relatively small portion. Structured
courses can still be valuable when they address a specific knowledge
gap, build confidence, or support a desired career direction rather
than simply validating an already proven career.

 DIGITAL ACCESSIBILITY LAWS FOR UX DESIGNERS: WHICH RULES APPLY TO
YOUR PRODUCTS (9 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Following accessibility best practices doesn't guarantee legal
compliance, and the rules differ across the US, UK, and EU. A
four-question check covering market, provider, product type, and
exemptions helps identify which of the ADA, Section 508, the European
Accessibility Act, and UK laws may apply. Cases involving Domino's,
Target, and Fashion Nova show the legal and commercial cost of
ignoring them.

 DOES BRAND DESIGN HAVE A LONGEVITY PROBLEM? (8 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Brands increasingly chase launch-day novelty and short-lived visual
trends at the expense of accumulated recognition and long-term
usefulness. Durable identities instead build on principles, flexible
systems, typography, proportion, composition, and other foundational
elements that can evolve without abandoning the equity they have
accumulated. Longevity does not require brands to remain visually
static, but to preserve their core ideas while adapting their
expression to new contexts over decades.

 HAL CASINO IS A SINGLE-WEIGHT SERIF, SANS AND MONO (3 MINUTE READ)
[16]

 HAL Typefaces' HAL Casino combines serif, sans, and monospaced styles
within a single-weight family, allowing contrasting typographic voices
to work together as one system. Its forms draw on historical serif
designs and expressive swash lettering while balancing expressive
details with enough consistency for the styles to be mixed freely. The
result gives designers multiple distinct textures without requiring
them to leave a single coordinated type family.
