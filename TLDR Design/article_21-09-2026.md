 GOOGLE'S NEW ‘CC' IS AN AI AGENT THAT HELPS FAMILIES RUN THEIR
HOUSEHOLDS (3 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Google is testing a family-focused version of CC that turns shared
emails and messages into daily briefings, calendar events, and tasks
for up to six household members. The agent has its own Google account
and can help fill out permission slips, create shopping lists, and
plan meals, while each person controls what information they share.
The experiment is available through a waitlist for US adults with
personal Google accounts.

 ANTHROPIC TRIES TO MAKE CLAUDE STICKIER WITH LAUNCH OF DOCS AND
SLIDES (5 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Anthropic launched Claude Docs and Slides, a rich-text editor letting
users draft documents and presentations from chat while Claude asks
clarifying questions and comments on its choices. Files land in the
Artifacts tab, export to Word, PDF, Google Docs, or Markdown, and
support real-time collaboration, though Team and Enterprise still lack
version history. Analysts see it as an attempt to make Claude stickier
by pulling productivity work into the AI, unlike Microsoft and Google
embedding AI into existing office tools.

 DISNEY'S FIRST CTO LED AN AI STARTUP IT ONCE ACCUSED OF COPYING ITS
CHARACTERS (1 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Disney has appointed former Character.AI CEO Karandeep Anand as its
first chief technology officer, reporting to CEO Josh D'Amaro. The
hire comes after Disney accused Character.AI of using its copyrighted
characters without permission and demanded their removal. Anand
previously held leadership roles at Meta and spent 15 years at
Microsoft, bringing consumer technology and AI experience to Disney's
push to make technology a larger part of its entertainment business.

OPINIONS & TUTORIALS

 FOUNDATIONS MAXXING: WHY YOUR DESIGN SYSTEM IS NOT READY FOR AI (7
MINUTE READ) [7]

 AI can dramatically speed up product development, but only if it's
built on a solid design system. Teams should prioritize auditing and
strengthening foundations—such as design tokens, components,
documentation, decision logs, and design-to-code pipelines—before
making systems machine-readable, while emphasizing collaboration with
developers and a UX-first mindset to avoid creating inconsistent,
unscalable products.

 LOST DOG—OR LOST DESIGNER? (8 MINUTE READ) [8]

 AI-generated design is not inherently ethical or unethical—the
morality depends on the context, including whether automation was
necessary, who benefits, who is harmed, and whether it replaces
existing design work or simply enables people without the resources to
access design. The more meaningful ethical question is when its use
becomes unjust and how the costs and benefits of automation are
distributed across designers, businesses, customers, and society.

 A SIMPLE GUIDE TO CALM UI (2 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Calm UI comes down to two core rules: avoid cascading loading states
and skip toasts in favor of showing an action's effect directly in the
list. Four bonus rules round it out: no error toasts, pinning modals
that change height to the top, letting Enter submit forms, and
auto-focusing a dialog's first input.

 ONE MORE THING ABOUT THE IPHONE 18 PRO: THE BIGGER/SMALLER DYNAMIC
ISLAND (2 MINUTE READ) [12]

 The iPhone 18 Pro moves its Face ID infrared camera beneath the
display, allowing Apple to shrink the Dynamic Island cutout while
expanding its usable space to support up to three simultaneous Live
Activities. Although the pixels above the hidden sensor appear
slightly softer than the rest of the display, the change marks another
step toward Apple's long-term goal of a true edge-to-edge, all-screen
iPhone.

 BETTER ICON AND LABEL ALIGNMENT (3 MINUTE READ) [13]

 When list item text wraps to multiple lines, a centered icon ends up
looking misaligned against the first line of text. Switching
align-items to start and offsetting the icon with a translateY
calculated from the lh unit keeps it aligned regardless of icon or
font size. The same lh-based offset, paired with aspect-ratio,
flex-shrink, and background-size: contain, fixes alignment for icons
built as pseudo-elements too.

 THE PARADOX OF CLARITY: WHY DATA DESIGN IS OUR MOST TREACHEROUS
MEDIUM (5 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Data visualization is having a cultural moment, with MoMA's Full
Disclosure, Refik Anadol's Dataland, and OMA/AMO's Diagrams each
treating data as translation, aesthetic medium, and political
instrument. Across all three, the real danger isn't scarce
information, but the false authority clarity lends to flawed data, as
Lombroso's debunked 19th-century iris charts once showed. The next
phase of data design may favor curation over abundance and structural
transparency over deceptive clarity, trading spectacle for restraint.
