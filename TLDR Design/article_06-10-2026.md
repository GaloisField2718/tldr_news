 OPENAI LAUNCHES VISUAL ADS THAT APPEAR ALONGSIDE IMAGE GENERATION
RESULTS (2 MINUTE READ) [4]

 OpenAI is adding visual display ads alongside images generated in
ChatGPT, starting later this month in the US with a small group of
advertisers. The ads will be clearly labeled and, according to OpenAI,
will not influence ChatGPT's answers. The company is also expanding ad
measurement partnerships and developing brand suitability evaluations
as it builds an ad-supported business for users on free and low-cost
plans.

 JOHN TERNUS IS TAKING A MORE ‘HANDS-ON' ROLE IN APPLE'S DESIGN
TEAMS AS CEO (2 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Apple CEO John Ternus has reportedly become deeply involved with the
company's industrial design and human interface teams, working in the
design studio several times a week and sometimes visiting multiple
times per day. Rather than appointing a new chief design officer,
Apple has Molly Anderson leading industrial design and Steve Lemay
leading human interface design, with Ternus acting as the overarching
decision-maker. Apple is also rebuilding teams depleted by departures
to companies including Meta and OpenAI through internal promotions and
external hires.

 THE BRISBANE 2032 OLYMPICS LOGO IS HIDING A TRIPLE DESIGN IN PLAIN
SIGHT (2 MINUTE READ) [6]

 The Brisbane 2032 Olympic emblem combines three ideas in a single
vivid form: an Olympic flame, the crest of Australia's native
cockatoo, and the shape of Queensland. Its pink-and-red form gives the
identity an energetic, playful character intended to feel distinctly
connected to the host region. The identity was accidentally revealed
early through an explainer uploaded to Brisbane 2032's YouTube
channel.

 WHAT ARE THE PRINCIPLES OF PROTOTYPING? (4 MINUTE READ) [7]

 A prototype is a way to learn something before making a bigger
commitment, in any form, from an email or text message to an event.
Nine principles, refined by 124 people at Services Week 2026, hold
that a prototype answers a question, is tested with future users, and
can be archived. The tools have changed, with AI-assisted ones now in
the mix, but the purpose and principles of prototyping have not.

 10 UNCOMFORTABLE UX LESSONS FROM REAL DESIGN MISTAKES (12 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Ten uncomfortable lessons from real design mistakes offer a way to
make better design choices before running more UX tests. Minimalism
failed on an ERP shipment form, and a standard landing page beat an
artistic, scroll-snapping one in an A/B test. Other lessons favor
preventing errors over recovering from them, and giving users an
opt-out instead of forcing AI into manual workflows.

 TRAINING AI TO PAINT WITH CODE (8 MINUTE READ) [9]

 A language model was trained with reinforcement learning to create
watercolor images by writing editable p5.brush JavaScript rather than
generating fixed image files, giving people direct control over the
resulting artifact. Early training exposed problems with overly
complex reward systems, while switching from absolute scores to
pairwise comparisons against a hand-rated reference pool produced
better images with dramatically shorter code. The experiment shows
that applying reinforcement learning to creative work is fundamentally
a design problem: subjective qualities like taste need carefully
constructed reward structures that provide direction without forcing
the model toward a single aesthetic.

 WORKFLOW LAB: FROM DESIGN SYSTEM TO CAMPAIGN IN FIGMA WEAVE (6 MINUTE
READ) [13]

 A product designer at a fictional museum uses Figma Weave to turn the
museum's design system into campaign collateral for a new exhibition.
Image Describer nodes read existing designs and color families, and
the Gen Effect node gives every image a shared look through color
grading and hazy edges. The Figma node stays connected, so
design-system updates flow downstream, and the node graph can be
reused for future shows or published as a Weave tool.

 DESIGN IS A NICE-TO-HAVE (1 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Design feels like a nice-to-have again, according to a designer who
now builds software himself and finds news about design craft and
tools flat. Once programming became trivial, design lost the spotlight
it briefly held when SaaS commoditized software and distribution was
the hardest part. Craft still matters, but a "good enough" UX
suffices, since usefulness to the market was always what mattered
most.

 BRAND IDENTITY: KOTO'S STACK OVERFLOW REBRAND (3 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Koto has repositioned Stack Overflow as a source of human-validated
knowledge in an AI-first world, consolidating its portfolio and
building the identity around the concept “Always in Build.” A
simplified stack-shaped logo expands into a generative visual language
of geometry that continuously reformats and rebuilds, supported by an
expanded orange-led palette and the custom Stack Sans typeface with
distinctive notched letterforms. A custom generative tool lets Stack
Overflow's team produce new compositions at scale, while the system
stays restrained inside the product and becomes more expressive across
campaigns and the Developer Survey.
