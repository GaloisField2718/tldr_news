 TRY GOOGLE PICS: EASY IMAGE CREATION AND EDITING IN GOOGLE WORKSPACE
(2 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Google Pics, an image creation and editing tool built on the Nano
Banana model, reaches Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers and most
Workspace business customers. Object segmentation, in-image text
editing and translation, and collaborative editing give precise
control over an image rather than regenerating it from scratch.
Integration is available in Docs and Slides, with Drive arriving in
the coming weeks.

 UPDATES TO GENERATIVE PLUGINS AND SHADERS (1 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Custom generative plugins and shaders, built by prompting the Figma

 ECOSYSTEM DESIGN (6 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Ecosystem design pushes past the product boundary, shaping the
conditions and capabilities people need to succeed on a platform
rather than the interface itself. Integrations, partnerships,
marketplaces, developer platforms, community development, and
corporate development are the six surfaces where a product becomes a
world other people build on. Five practices follow: design the whole
customer loop, treat third parties as users with business models,
build trust, tend community, and treat docs as a product.

 IS PRODUCT DISCOVERY A PHASE OR A WEEKLY HABIT? (18 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Product discovery works as weekly customer touchpoints by the team
building the product, not as a kickoff phase that hands a backlog to
engineering. Discovery and delivery are two kinds of work on one team,
and a high kill rate is the test that discovery is real. Recruiting is
the bottleneck: a Monday-to-Friday cadence survives only when a
conversation is already on the calendar, even a customer-support
ride-along.

 AI IS MAKING PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT FASTER. BUT WHERE DID THE WORK GO?
(11 MINUTE READ) [9]

 AI can dramatically speed up individual tasks like research
synthesis, prototyping, and code generation, but that doesn't
automatically make the entire product development process faster.
Often the work doesn't disappear—it shifts into review,
verification, refactoring, or downstream teams, creating hidden costs
that can erode the initial gains. The real question isn't whether AI
increases local productivity, but whether it improves the whole
system's ability to turn ideas into reliable products without
accumulating technical debt, instability, or rework.

 ON ANIMATING WHAT YOU CAN'T SEE (4 MINUTE READ) [13]

 A ghosted double image in DuckDuckGo for iOS's floating toolbar
survived three rounds of retiming because the bug was view ownership,
not animation timing. Recording the transition at 60fps and stepping
through it with ffmpeg showed the tab-count badge fully visible 24
frames before the snapshot animation began. Two failure modes
generalize: snapshotting a view's bounds instead of what it draws, and
resizing a snapshot instead of scaling it.

 RENÉ BIEDER'S NEURATH X IS A GEOMETRIC TYPEFACE MADE FOR THE AGE OF
AI (4 MINUTE READ) [14]

 A new typeface family explores how far geometric reduction can be
pushed without sacrificing readability, drawing inspiration from early
modernist experiments in typography and visual communication. Built as
both proportional and monospaced families with extensive stylistic
alternates, it reimagines the unrealized radical potential of classics
like Futura while balancing mathematical purity with practical
usability. The release extends beyond the typeface itself through a
coordinated visual identity of 3D animation, motion design, and sound,
all expressing the same philosophy of clarity, reduction, and
geometric transformation.

 THE NEW DISNEY WORLD LOGO DESIGN IS ACTUALLY KIND OF GENIUS (1 MINUTE
READ) [15]

 Disney World's upcoming 55th-anniversary logo is earning praise for
cleverly combining two “5”s into unmistakable Mickey Mouse ears
while reviving elements of the resort's original 1971 globe logo. Fans
have responded far more positively than they did to the
50th-anniversary mark, praising its simplicity, nostalgia, and
stronger visual concept.
