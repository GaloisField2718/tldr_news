 GOOGLE'S LATEST HOME PAGE REDESIGN MAKES GEMINI EVEN MORE PROMINENT
(2 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Google's AI Mode now has a collapsible sidebar listing recent
searches, both AI and organic, so users can jump back to earlier
queries. The addition pushes Gemini further into desktop search
despite complaints from users and employees. Google appears focused on
keeping its AI search lead over rivals like ChatGPT and Claude.

 FIGMA'S AGENT IS NOW IN FIGJAM AND SLIDES (1 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Figma's agent is now generally available in FigJam and Figma Slides
on all paid plans, with connectors and attachments supported in both.
It pulls context from tools like Hex, Slack, and Asana to generate
boards, diagrams, and decks. Messy FigJam boards become organized
Specs, and decks can start from a Spec outline and brand template.

 NEW YORK TIMES PROHIBITS PHOTOGRAPHERS FROM USING PHOTOSHOP 2027 (3
MINUTE READ) [6]

 The New York Times has instructed freelance photographers and
contributors not to use Photoshop 2027 on Times assignments because
some of its editing features incorporate generative AI without making
that sufficiently apparent to users. The concern is not simply the
presence of AI but the difficulty of distinguishing conventional photo
editing from generative manipulation. The publication's strict ethical
standards require photographs to accurately represent real events,
prohibiting the addition, removal, or alteration of people and objects
while allowing minimal color adjustments.

 CONNECTING THE TOOLS IS BECOMING THE WORK (8 MINUTE READ) [7]

 With DuckDuckGo engineering now running a shared AI coding harness,
the constraint has shifted to manual handoffs between separate AI
design and engineering tools. A small machine-readable design spec at
each stage, carrying the chosen direction, screenshots, strings, and
tokens, would give the next stage enough to act on. A design check
would then run inside the coding agent's loop and again in CI,
flagging low scores for a designer.

 THE AI PROBLEMS NOBODY HAS SOLVED YET (9 MINUTE READ) [8]

 An internal UX assessment of Meta AI across Facebook, Instagram, and
WhatsApp revealed fundamental challenges in evaluating experiences
that produce unpredictable responses. Traditional usability methods
struggle to accommodate inconsistent outputs, variable response times,
and hallucinations that appear just as convincing as accurate
information. Designing effective AI experiences requires new
approaches to evaluating model performance, communicating uncertainty,
managing user expectations, and establishing responsibility for
detecting errors.

 FORWARD-DEPLOYED DESIGN AS PLUMBING (8 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Forward-deployed design positions designers as hands-on problem
solvers who address underlying organizational and operational issues
rather than simply improving interfaces. As AI makes software
development more accessible, the greatest obstacles to improving
public services increasingly involve coordination between
institutions, governance, permissions, and competing responsibilities.
Experimental public-service prototypes developed in a University of
Michigan classroom demonstrate how designers can use functional
software to expose these institutional barriers and explore practical
solutions.

MISCELLANEOUS

 HOW MATERIAL CONSTRAINTS SHARPEN DESIGN THINKING FOR PHYSICAL
PROJECTS (6 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Budget, environment, and lifespan give designers concrete criteria
for revisiting choices made on screen before anything goes to
production. Pentagram's bronze drain covers, PUMA's Clever Little Bag
with 65% less cardboard than a shoebox, and the Eames plywood chair
show constraints shaping form. Full-size samples, each testing one
defined question, settle what flat layouts and renderings leave open.

 LIGHTING THAT FEELS JUST RIGHT (20 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Real-time global illumination supplies what simple Blinn-Phong
lighting lacks: light that bounces, bleeds color, and tints
neighboring surfaces. Screen-Space Global Illumination comes first,
followed by a more practical probe-based approach to estimating
indirect light in browser-based 3D scenes. Reflections, sky light,
volumetric lighting, and atmospheric scattering are then layered on
for the finished look.

 THIS BEAUTIFUL LOGO DESIGN WAS CREATED TO GROW LIKE NATURE (3 MINUTE
READ) [16]

 Philadelphia-based Smith & Diction has redesigned the Pennsylvania
Horticultural Society's identity ahead of its 200th anniversary,
replacing a generic tree emblem with a dynamic representation of plant
life. The new PHS lettermark acts as a root system from which
different native plants grow, with illustrations by Summer McClure
arranged within rectangles inspired by Philadelphia's street grid.
Frame-by-frame animations by Henrique Barone depict plants growing
from the letterforms, while additional animations and a printed
flipbook bring the identity's organic movement to life.

 FONT VS TYPEFACE: THE ULTIMATE GUIDE (7 MINUTE READ) [19]

 A typeface describes a shared visual design across a family of
characters, while a font traditionally refers to a particular weight,
width, and style or its corresponding digital file, although variable
fonts have blurred this distinction by combining multiple styles
within a single file.
