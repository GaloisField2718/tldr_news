 DOCKER REBRAND 2026: NEW LOGO, COLORS, AND STRATEGY EXPLAINED (6
MINUTE READ) [4]

 Docker has introduced a new visual identity and the positioning "Run
on Trust," reflecting its expansion from application containers toward
secure execution environments for autonomous AI agents. The redesign
simplifies its recognizable whale logo into a containment-inspired
symbol, replaces Docker Blue with a more violet-toned blue, and
introduces GT Planar and GT Standard Mono typography. A darker website
and expanded color palette accompany the strategic shift, positioning
Docker as infrastructure for safely running both traditional
applications and AI agents.

 BIG CHANGES TO THE ADOBE FONTS WEBSITE (2 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Adobe Fonts' biggest website upgrade in years merges search and
browsing into one interface that filters 36,000 fonts by OpenType
features and variable axes. A new AI recommendation page suggests
fonts matching the mood of uploaded inspiration images, even ones
without any text. Family pages now also show glyph maps and
illustrated OpenType feature breakdowns.

 CANVA NOW LETS USERS TURN OFF GENERATIVE AI FEATURES (2 MINUTE READ)
[6]

 Canva has introduced settings that let free and paid users disable
generative AI tools, including Magic Write, Magic Design, Magic Edit,
Dream Lab, and Canva Code. Non-generative features such as Background
Remover and Magic Grab remain available because they manipulate
existing content rather than create new material. A separate
preference reduces AI-generated templates and graphics in search
results, prioritizing human-created content without guaranteeing that
AI material disappears entirely.

 SHOULD YOU ACTUALLY ADD AI TO THAT? A FOUR-QUESTION AUDIT FOR PRODUCT
TEAMS (17 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Adding AI helps only when what it returns in saved effort, caught
errors, or consistency outweighs what users spend learning and
verifying it. This post presents a four-question audit that asks
whether the feature cuts total effort, beats the current method, lets
users catch errors, and preserves verification skills after a year. A
benefits-appeals tool became worthwhile once rescoped from drafting
determinations to surfacing relevant provisions.

 IT'S OFFICIAL: CREATIVES ARE USING AI DAILY, BUT MOST AREN'T MAKING
EXTRA MONEY FROM IT (7 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Envato's survey of 1,033 creatives across 103 countries found that
87% use AI at work and 58% use it daily, although increased
productivity rarely translates into higher earnings. Among creatives
who report working faster with AI, only one in four see a financial
benefit, while 53% of AI users have used AI on important client work
without disclosing it. Taste and judgment ranked as the most valuable
skills at 53%, followed by craft at 49%, while prompting fell to last
place among eight skills at 19%.

 DESIGN BY PLAYGROUND (2 MINUTE READ) [9]

 AI coding agents are making it practical for designers to create
temporary, purpose-built design tools instead of relying exclusively
on general-purpose applications like Figma. These interactive
playgrounds let designers manipulate variables, compare alternatives,
and test real content, animation, loading states, and edge cases
directly in the environment where the software will run. Agents can
build them in minutes, so playgrounds can be created for individual
design questions and discarded afterward, with successful decisions
exported as code or design tokens.

 THE MULTIPLIER EFFECT: HOW AUTHORING TOOLS CAN TRANSFORM WEB
ACCESSIBILITY (5 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Website builders and content management systems could multiply web
accessibility by prompting for alt text and offering only
WCAG-conforming options. With 74% of new web pages in April 2025
containing AI-generated content, well-designed AI could improve
accessibility while poor implementations multiply barriers. W3C is
updating its decade-old Authoring Tool Accessibility Guidelines and
invites anyone interested to join the ATAG Community Group.

 THIS NON-ALCOHOLIC APERITIF PACKAGING WAS MADE WITH REAL PLANTS,
SUNLIGHT, AND EMBOSSED GLASS (5 MINUTE READ) [17]

 LOVE. has refreshed the identity and packaging of non-alcoholic
aperitif Everleaf, combining sustainable glassmaking with botanical
illustrations created through traditional cyanotype photography. The
redesigned bottles use 100% post-consumer recycled glass, weigh 23%
less than their predecessors, and feature embossed acacia leaves
alongside a refined wordmark. Real plants exposed onto light-sensitive
paper produce distinctive illustrations for the Forest, Mountain, and
Marine varieties, connecting each product's ingredients to its visual
identity.

 OMOI'S IDENTITY FOR ECLIPSE SPACE LOOKS BACK TO THE APOLLO PROGRAM (7
MINUTE READ) [18]

 Stockholm-based Omoi has developed an identity for satellite
infrastructure company Eclipse Space inspired by the optimism,
engineering, and visual language of NASA's Apollo program. The system
combines a custom Eclipse Space Display typeface, Gorton-inspired
interface labels, vibrant space-themed colors, and interactive website
buttons modeled on the Apollo Guidance Computer's physical keys. Its
distinctive solar corona symbol is generated through a custom tool
developed with Claude Code, producing unique variations that follow
consistent visual rules and reinforce the company's positioning around
independent ownership of satellite networks.
