 THE XIAOMI 18 FOLD LEAKS, AHEAD OF THE IPHONE ULTRA LAUNCH (1 MINUTE
READ) [4]

 The smartphone industry appears to be coalescing around the
book-style foldable design, where a phone unfolds into a small tablet,
rather than the flip-phone format that folds a standard phone in half.
Samsung's latest Galaxy Z Fold, Apple's rumored iPhone Ultra, and
leaked images of Xiaomi's upcoming 18 Fold all point to growing
momentum behind larger-screen foldables, suggesting that this form
factor is emerging as the preferred direction for premium folding
phones.

 ADOBE BRINGS ACR'S POWERFUL NON-DESTRUCTIVE EXPOSURE TOOLS INTO
PHOTOSHOP ITSELF (3 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Adobe has released a major Photoshop update focused on making image
editing faster and more intuitive, with new features including a Light
Adjustment Layer that brings Camera Raw-style lighting controls
directly into Photoshop, Firefly-powered editing tools that can
automatically mask and modify specific parts of an image from text
instructions, and AI Markup tools for visually communicating edit
requests. The update also introduces a beta AI-Assisted Editor,
allowing users to make complex edits through natural-language prompts
while Photoshop automatically selects and combines the appropriate
tools. Adobe says the goal is to let creators spend less time
navigating software and more time focusing on creative decisions.

 GAMMA ACQUIRES ACCEL-BACKED DESIGN STARTUP LICA (3 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Presentation startup Gamma has acquired Accel-backed Lica to build a
design research lab, which Lica's co-founders will lead. Lica raised
$4 million in 2024 and worked with e-commerce sites on brand-compliant
marketing videos. Gamma now wants to explore fluid, multimodal
presentation formats through the new division.

 THE CREATIVITY WE OUTSOURCE — AI, CREATIVITY AND THE LOST VALUE OF
DOING THINGS YOURSELF (6 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Generative AI can deliver a solution before there is time to explore
the problem, removing the detours where creative learning actually
happens. Photoshop began stripping away that friction. AI takes it
much further, especially for students and young designers. The
proposal is not banning AI but designing for Learning Experience
alongside User Experience.

 SPEAKING DESIGN AT SCALE (4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Design system pattern names like Overview and PageHeader are
shorthand for decisions a team should not have to reopen every time.
Learning that vocabulary takes a person months, but an agent can read
the repository and retrieve the code, guidance, and examples behind
each name. That lets more people describe a screen in the system's own
language and judge the result.

 THE PRODUCT DESIGNER'S ROLE ISN'T DISAPPEARING. IT'S GETTING WEIRDER
(9 MINUTE READ) [9]

 AI is collapsing the traditional boundaries between product design,
product management, and engineering, making the old handoff-based
workflow increasingly obsolete. As AI tools can now turn ideas,
requirements, and design systems into working prototypes and code,
designers are being pushed toward a broader role the author calls a
product architect—someone who combines product thinking, systems
design, technical understanding, and creative judgment. Rather than
focusing on screens, specs, or deliverables, the most valuable skill
becomes deciding what should be built, how it should work, and whether
it solves a real problem. This can be an opportunity rather than a
threat: design isn't disappearing, but evolving into a more strategic,
multidisciplinary role where taste, judgment, and systems thinking
matter more than execution.

 ANIMATION IS HAVING A PUNK ROCK MOMENT – AND IT SAYS A LOT ABOUT
HOW PEOPLE VIEW AI (5 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Niu Lai, a nearly zero-budget animated film made largely by a Chinese
mother and son on second-hand computers, has become an unexpected box
office hit. Made by former interior designer Xin Yumeng over five
years, the crude, low-quality animation grossed over ¥25.67 million
despite starting with almost no publicity or earnings. Its success is
framed as a reaction against polished, AI-generated visuals, echoing
how punk rock once rebelled against overproduced music.

 PERFECTION IS NOT OVER-ENGINEERING (3 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Over-engineering means solving the wrong problem, not caring too
much, or building something too good. With every constraint on the
table, only one solution fits, and that solution is the perfect one.
Over-engineering is therefore a failure of requirements gathering, and
systems need treating as products with real users.

 THE CBI'S LATEST AI REPORT ON HOW DESIGN BUSINESSES CAN BRIDGE AI'S
EXECUTION DIVIDE (4 MINUTE READ) [14]

 The real value of AI now comes from integrating it into core business
operations rather than running isolated experiments. Design teams
benefit most when AI supports entire workflows from concept creation
to production. AI's biggest near-term impact is augmenting human work
rather than replacing it, making skills like judgment, creativity, and
critical thinking even more important. Automation is reducing
traditional entry-level design tasks, creating a risk to the future
talent pipeline unless companies redesign junior roles around
AI-assisted workflows while also addressing growing legal and
copyright concerns around AI-generated work.
