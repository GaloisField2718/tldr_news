 INSTAGRAM'S EFFECT BUILDER MAKES NEW FILTERS FROM AI PROMPTS (1
MINUTE READ) [4]

 Instagram's new Effect Builder, available now on the web to US
residents over 18, turns AI prompts into custom Stories filters. Users
can attach style reference images, preview effects on their own
photos, and refine through follow-up prompts. An AI prompt-rewrite
option adds paragraphs of style detail yet barely changes the result,
and every effect must pass a safety review before others can use it.

 ADOBE AND DISNEY TEAM UP TO BUILD AI TOOLS FOR THE NEXT GENERATION OF
STORYTELLING (1 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Adobe has become Disney's Official AI Innovation Partner under a
multi-year deal focused on storytelling, creativity, and responsible
AI use. Adobe will give Disney early access to Firefly, Firefly
Foundry, Premiere, and Photoshop, plus its own engineers and
researchers. Through Firefly Foundry, Disney's models learn its
characters and art styles, and Imagineering R&D already uses them to
turn sketches into 2D concept art.

 GOOGLE MESSAGES HOMEPAGE REDESIGN: PINNED CHAT AVATARS, BOTTOM
SEARCH, CORNER GEMINI (3 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Google Messages is testing a redesigned Android homepage that
displays pinned conversations as large contact avatars above the
regular message list, similar to Apple's iMessage. Search moves from
the top of the screen to a floating button near the bottom, alongside
a new-chat button that changes shape while scrolling to improve
one-handed usability. The Gemini shortcut also moves to the
upper-right corner beside the profile image, with the changes
currently rolling out selectively to beta testers.

 WELCOME TO THE BEIGE ERA OF PRODUCT DESIGN. HERE'S YOUR WAY OUT (2
MINUTE READ) [7]

 Digital products are converging on the same look, driven by AI tools
that repeat common solutions, vibe-design tools, shared UI libraries
like shadcn/ui, and optimization culture. Convergence is "the floor,"
so designers should standardize what users expect. Borrowing
automotive design's idea of "jewelry," teams should protect a few
distinctive details, such as emotional moments like a first payment or
a signed contract.

 MAKING IMMERSIVE LLM CHAT EXPERIENCES MORE ACCESSIBLE (22 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Chat interfaces like Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, and Gemini are
interactive web content, so WCAG applies to them like any other page.
A single live region announcing replies after streaming ends, hidden
per-message headings, and sparing landmark use keep screen reader
navigation workable. Skip links, Control+F6 shortcuts, user
preferences, and research with disabled participants round out the
approach.

 WHEN TOUCH MAKES SENSE ON A LAPTOP (4 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Touchscreen laptops can make certain interactions faster than using a
trackpad, particularly when selecting large targets or performing
brief, direct actions. In a single-participant experiment across six
tasks, touch averaged 1.39 seconds compared with 1.74 seconds for the
trackpad, while previous research found similar advantages for some
interactions. Touch remains less accurate for small targets and can
cause fatigue during extended use, making it better suited as a
complementary input method rather than a replacement for traditional
laptop controls.

MISCELLANEOUS

 "X IS DEAD" IS DEAD (3 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Figma, wireframing, design files, and design systems aren't
disappearing, but the standardized toolchains that once defined
product design are fragmenting into increasingly personalized
workflows. Designers now move between sketching tools, AI prompts,
coding agents, and other applications, making it harder for
organizations to enforce consistency through shared software alone.
Design systems must therefore become tool-independent infrastructure
that communicates not just components and tokens but also product
principles, decision-making context, and the intentions behind design
choices.

 THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN VISUAL DESIGN AND USER EXPERIENCE DESIGN (17
MINUTE READ) [14]

 Visual design is only one part of UX, usually among the last
decisions made, and it depends on an interdisciplinary team. Jesse
James Garrett's five planes run from strategy through scope,
structure, and skeleton to surface, where visual design sits.
Continuous testing feeds back into decisions at every level.
