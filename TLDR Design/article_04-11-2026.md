 FIGMA ADDS GOOGLE DRIVE AND INTERNAL WEAVE TOOL SHARING (1 MINUTE
READ) [4]

 Figma's agent and Make can now pull research, data, and images from
Google Drive into a design conversation and create new Drive files.
Connectors reach every plan, including Starter, though Google Drive is
unavailable with the Governance+ add-on. Organization and Enterprise
teams can also publish Weave tools internally, beside Figma's own and
Community tools in the Tools tab.

 NUBANK UNVEILS GLOBAL BRAND REFRESH BY KOTO AHEAD OF US LAUNCH (4
MINUTE READ) [5]

 Koto has refreshed Nubank's global identity ahead of its US
expansion, creating a flexible brand system designed to accommodate
more than 140 million customers and an expanding portfolio of
financial products. The redesign retains the recognizable Nu logo
while refining its signature purple, introducing proprietary Nu Sans
Display and Nu Sans Text typefaces, and establishing a global library
of photography, illustration, and motion assets. The system also
extends into conversational AI, in-app animations built with Rive, and
a redesigned global website, bringing greater consistency to Nubank's
growing international presence.

 IPHONE 18 WILL HAVE TWO UPGRADES INSPIRED BY ‘PRO' MODELS, PER LEAK
(2 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Apple's upcoming iPhone 18 will reportedly inherit two improvements
from the iPhone 18 Pro: longer battery life and a more efficient
processor. The new A20 chip is expected to use TSMC's 2-nanometer
manufacturing process, potentially delivering significant performance
and efficiency gains over previous generations. The standard model is
expected in early 2027, but the smaller Dynamic Island will reportedly
remain exclusive to Pro models, and a price increase is possible.

 IS THIS THE MOST CONTROVERSIAL FONT SINCE COMIC SANS? (3 MINUTE READ)
[7]

 AI-generated posters are becoming recognizable through their repeated
use of aggressive, upward-slanting brushstroke lettering, prompting
comparisons with the much-maligned Comic Sans. ChatGPT attributes the
repeated aesthetic to training data that associates oversized,
distressed lettering with dramatic, attention-grabbing advertising and
promotional graphics. Unlike Comic Sans, which was designed for a
specific communication purpose, the AI-generated lettering often
imitates expressive typography without understanding its context,
turning a once-distinctive style into a visual cliché.

 DESIGN FEEDBACK FOR AI AGENTS (3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Design systems and agent instructions can guide AI-generated
interfaces toward consistency, but they cannot anticipate every
combination of components, layouts, and product states. A lightweight
feedback loop helps close the remaining gap by letting designers
annotate screenshots, comment in Figma, or provide spoken and written
instructions that agents use to correct visual problems. Intent now
integrates this workflow through a macOS screen-feedback feature,
while human judgment remains essential for deciding which corrections
should become reusable design system rules.

 WHAT DOES DESIGN SYSTEM AI READINESS MEAN, ANYWAY? (12 MINUTE READ)
[9]

 Making a design system AI-ready requires more than machine-readable
documentation, design tokens, MCP servers, and agent skills. AI agents
need constraints that guide them toward approved components, patterns,
and implementation rules while reducing opportunities for inconsistent
or invented solutions. The real measure of readiness is whether AI can
participate in the design process without lowering quality, with human
judgment remaining essential wherever tacit knowledge and context
cannot be fully encoded.

 “DESIGNING A GOOD POSTER IS A DARK ART” – HIGHLIGHTS FROM THIS
YEAR'S INTL POSTER COMPETITION (8 MINUTE READ) [13]

 This year's INTL poster competition drew 2,670 entries from 78
countries, with 190 posters by more than 200 designers on display in
Glasgow from November 12–22. Co-founder James Gilchrist calls a
great poster near-impossible to design and singles out three trends:
layered surfaces, 1990s-inspired collaged typography, and deliberately
unprofessional, playful work.

 DESIGNING A BETTER FUTURE: YOUR GUIDE TO DESIGN CONTRIBUTION IN OPEN
SOURCE (12 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Open-source projects often lack the UX expertise needed to make
powerful software approachable, while designers struggle to discover
contribution opportunities in developer-oriented environments such as
GitHub. Meaningful contributions can include user research, clearer
project descriptions, onboarding improvements, navigation, error
messages, accessibility, and better documentation of design processes
rather than just visual assets. Designers can begin by exploring
projects they already use, reviewing contribution guidelines, finding
relevant issues, and collaborating directly with maintainers, even
without extensive technical knowledge.

 SUPERIOR TYPE'S HEAVEN IS A SANS SERIF BUILT ON OVERSIZED ROUND
SHAPES (3 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Superior Type's Heaven is a brutalist grotesque designed by Petr
Kucera around oversized rounded shapes that appear compressed into
rectangular spaces. The typeface includes four families, Heaven 01,
03, 06, and 09, each offering 18 styles from Thin to Black with
italics, Latin and Cyrillic support, and a SuperVariable version that
transitions from soft, rounded forms to boxier geometry. Distinctive
curved letterforms, unconventional diagonals, and optional alternates
give designers flexibility for expressive logos and display typography
while retaining enough balance for longer text.
