NEWS & TRENDS

 GOOGLE TESTS STICKY CITATIONS IN DESKTOP AI OVERVIEWS (1 MINUTE READ)
[4]

 Google is testing a new desktop AI Overview interface that keeps
source citations visible as users scroll through AI-generated answers.
The citations move with the content instead of disappearing offscreen,
making it easier to access supporting websites while reading longer
responses. The experiment also introduces a horizontal divider
separating the AI Overview from conventional search results, and the
design has been spotted across multiple browsers.

 SPOTIFY'S NEW TOOL AIMS TO STOP DEEPFAKES FROM BEING ATTRIBUTED TO
REAL ARTISTS (2 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Spotify is rolling out Artist Profile Protection to all artists,
letting them review and remove releases submitted under their name
after an eight-month beta. Artists get alerts when new music appears
under their name, and an advanced setting holds releases until they
manually approve them. The launch follows Billboard's report that Sony
Music sought removal of more than 260,000 deepfake songs from digital
platforms.

 HOW .RAW REWORKED STOW'S IDENTITY TO MAKE ITS COFFEE ROASTING VISIBLE
(5 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Slovenian branding studio .raw has redesigned specialty coffee
company STOW's identity to communicate the scientific precision behind
its sourcing, roasting, and testing processes. The system retains
STOW's original factory-inspired symbol while introducing a geometric
wordmark, Aeonik Mono typography, structured layouts, and square
brackets borrowed from scientific notation. A restrained black, white,
and gray palette is complemented by five accent colors representing
different flavor profiles, making the brand's technical expertise and
sensory qualities visible across packaging, menus, and other
applications.

 AI IS THE NEW OLD DESIGN MATERIAL (8 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Designers have always learned their medium by working it, and AI
models are the next material. A mockup shows one sample, while a model
returns a distribution of answers that only sampling code reveals.
Running each prompt 50 times, bringing the worst answers to design
review, and saving failures as shared test cases puts that spread in
front of the team.

 AI SPED UP THE BUILD: HOW CAN ACCESSIBILITY TESTING KEEP UP? (9
MINUTE READ) [8]

 AI has sped up planning, design, and development, but only 30% of
professionals in Level Access' survey of 2,500+ report gains in QA and
testing. WebAIM found detected errors per home page rose 10.1%, while
organizations that keep improving use AI at the same rate but invest
more in training and culture. Accessibility judgment moves earlier
through annotated handoffs, gated pull requests, and AI-drafted fixes
reviewed in context, each leaving a regression test behind.

 YOUR PATH TO DESIGN ENGINEERING (11 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Design engineering combines the ability to solve design problems with
responsibility for implementing those solutions, but the skills needed
depend on whether someone comes from design, frontend engineering, or
a different background. Frontend engineers can develop design judgment
by exploring alternative layouts and understanding visual hierarchy,
while designers can learn to translate their decisions into functional
interfaces and handle real-world implementation constraints. This
choose-your-own-adventure learning guide offers practical exercises
for each starting point, emphasizing small projects, iterative
practice, and understanding the reasoning behind design and
engineering decisions.

 DESIGN ENGINEERING HAS A HANDOFF PROBLEM (6 MINUTE READ) [13]

 AI-generated components from designers were meant to lighten
engineers' load, but ones with hardcoded values and inline SVGs now
pile up in engineers' review queues. The handoff has simply moved down
a level, with designers lacking the governance, permissions, and
shared tooling engineering built over years. The fix starts with
holding designer code to the same merge bar, running deterministic
checks before human review, and giving designers a lighter
environment.

 AI'S ROLE IN THE CREATIVE PROCESS (4 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Debates pitting AI enthusiasts against skeptics miss the real
question: which parts of creative work to automate and which to keep.
Tedious labor like VFX green-screen cleanup can go, but friction such
as trimming a shot or drafting a brief is where thinking happens.
Figma's Gaussian splat campaign, generated with Seedance 2.5 in Weave,
still took long rounds of riffing before its shots felt finished.

 PREVENT UX THEATER WITH CROSS-FUNCTIONAL COLLABORATION (2 MINUTE
READ) [15]

 UX theater occurs when organizations perform design activities
without meaningfully involving users or allowing research to influence
decisions. Cross-functional collaboration can help counter this by
bringing designers, researchers, developers, and other stakeholders
into shared discussions about user needs and product priorities.
Meaningful collaboration requires teams to move beyond workshops and
deliverables toward evidence-based decisions, shared accountability,
and genuine participation in the design process.
