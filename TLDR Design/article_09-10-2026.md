NEWS & TRENDS

 CHATGPT IS GETTING A LOT MORE VISUAL, WITH THE LAUNCH OF A NEW
INTERFACE (2 MINUTE READ) [4]

 OpenAI's Intelligent UI lets ChatGPT combine text with interactive
diagrams, charts, buttons, and task-specific tools instead of using
the same format for every answer. Users can explore explanations,
change inputs, or use a calculator directly within a conversation,
with the layout adapting to the question. The feature is rolling out
with GPT-6 across paid and free plans, and users can reduce visual
elements when they prefer simpler responses.

 GOOGLE RELEASES NANO BANANA 2.1 FOR IMAGE GENERATION AND EDITING (1
MINUTE READ) [5]

 Google's Nano Banana 2.1 reached the Gemini API for image generation
and editing, at 1K, 2K, and 4K resolutions. Google reports better text
rendering, prompt adherence, and character consistency across
successive edits, plus fixes for tiling artifacts in wide and
panoramic images at 2K and 4K. The model is available as
gemini-nano-banana-2.1, and Google has deprecated
gemini-3.1-flash-image without announcing a shutdown date.

 CHATGPT CAN IMITATE A CARTOONIST. NOW IT'S SIGNING THEIR NAME, TOO (3
MINUTE READ) [6]

 ChatGPT has generated cartoons containing signatures associated with
more than 15 New Yorker contributors, potentially making AI-generated
work appear to have been created by real artists. Cartoonists
including Brendan Loper and Emily Flake have encountered images
bearing recognizable versions of their names despite having no
involvement in creating them. OpenAI has introduced warnings for some
New Yorker-style requests, but signed outputs have reportedly
continued to appear, raising concerns that style imitation can cross
into false attribution.

OPINIONS & TUTORIALS

 SLACK CONTENT DESIGN IN THE AGE OF AI (8 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Slack's AI-drafted UI copy is almost indistinguishable from human
work, and content designers are uniquely equipped to close the trust
gap that "almost" opens. When Slackbot appeared to leak private
information that was actually hallucinated, the team redesigned the UX
to fetch correct data and explain why LLMs hallucinate. Three tenets
guide the work: trust is the product, human judgment is irreplaceable,
and language is infrastructure for AI agents.

 BUILDING A GENERAL-PURPOSE ACCESSIBILITY AGENT—AND WHAT WE LEARNED
IN THE PROCESS (11 MINUTE READ) [8]

 An experimental general-purpose accessibility agent piloted at GitHub
has reviewed 3,535 pull requests with a 68% resolution rate. It gives
engineers real-time guidance in Copilot CLI and VS Code, and fixes
straightforward issues before code reaches production. A parent agent
directs two sub-agents, a passive reviewer and an active implementer,
and escalates drag-and-drop, data grids, and rich text editors to
human specialists.

 IMPERFECTION (5 MINUTE READ) [9]

 A personal website redesign became an excuse to explore deliberately
playful details, including fake game cartridges for past jobs,
draggable photo prints, paper-like shaders, animated labels, unusual
links, and dynamically generated social cards. Many of these touches
serve little practical purpose, but that lack of optimization is
precisely what makes the process enjoyable and gives the site its
personality. Personal projects can create space for experimentation
that professional work often removes, making unnecessary details,
imperfection, and prolonged tinkering valuable in themselves.

 TESTING THE IPAD AIR M4 AS A MULTI-DISCIPLINARY ARTIST: DOES IT STACK
UP? (7 MINUTE READ) [13]

 The M4 iPad Air's 12GB of unified memory brings it much closer to the
iPad Pro for demanding creative work, comfortably handling large
Procreate files, 3D models approaching 20 million polygons, and ProRes
video editing. Its M4 chip delivers roughly 42% higher single-core and
44% higher multi-core Geekbench performance than the M2 Air, while
Apple Pencil Pro support provides the same drawing experience as
higher-end models. The iPad Pro still offers advantages such as an
OLED display, nano-texture glass, and more memory, but many artists,
illustrators, 3D artists, photographers, and video editors may no
longer need to pay the premium.

 MOTHER DESIGN EXERCISES RESTRAINT WITH NEW M&S PACK SYSTEM (5 MINUTE
READ) [14]

 Mother Design has created a restrained packaging system for thousands
of M&S Home and Lingerie products by removing competing sub-brands and
hierarchies and looking to classic information design rather than
other retailers for inspiration. The system standardizes MS London
typography, introduces a geometric numeral set called MS London Geo,
and uses grids, consistent hierarchy, restrained colors, and
color-coded sizing to make complex product information easier to
navigate. Packaging materials, photography, and tone adapt between
Home and Lingerie while maintaining a shared structure designed to
strengthen the M&S master brand and scale into additional categories.
