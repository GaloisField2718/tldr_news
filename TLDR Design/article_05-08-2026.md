 CODE, CRAFT, AND THE MAKING OF NESTED FOLDERS (9 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Figma's nested folders are a long-requested feature that helps
growing teams stay organized. Creating the feature required rebuilding
the company's content and permissions system from scratch. The team
abandoned traditional product development in favor of leading with
code, blurring roles across engineering, design, and product, and
replacing handoffs with ongoing collaboration and shared craft.
Customer feedback from early-access programs and community outreach
shaped the rollout, which is expected to reach all users within a few
weeks.

 APPLE MOVES FOR PRELIMINARY INJUNCTION IN OPENAI TRADE SECRETS
LAWSUIT (1 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Apple has asked the court for a preliminary injunction in its trade
secrets lawsuit against OpenAI, arguing that it is being irreparably
harmed as OpenAI continues to use allegedly stolen confidential
information in its hardware development. While OpenAI agreed to stop
using Apple's confidential information, preserve evidence, and halt
disclosures, it refused Apple's requests for forensic inspections and
comprehensive searches of its systems. Apple is also seeking expedited
discovery, as OpenAI continues to publicly reject the allegations and
criticize the lawsuit.

 GOOGLE PAUSES AI SATELLITE IMAGES, AFTER FEARS OF DEEPFAKES IN THE
SKY (4 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Google has removed a newly launched Google Earth feature that let
users generate AI satellite images, citing screenshots violating its
policies. Open-source researchers and journalists had shown the tool
could produce convincing fake imagery of disasters, like fires on
Iran's Kharg Island or Capitol flooding, raising misinformation
concerns. Experts warned such tools threaten satellite imagery's role
as a trusted verification source, especially as fake satellite images
had already circulated during the US-Israel-Iran conflict.

 IDEAS ARE CHEAP. INVENTION IS NOT (7 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Since AI can generate endless ideas, the real bottleneck shifts to
selecting, validating, and applying judgment to those ideas, rather
than producing them. A structured invention pipeline—discovering
opportunities, generating concepts through methods like SIT, TRIZ, and
cross-domain collision, then stress-testing, scoring, and
validating—turns raw ideation into genuinely usable, defensible
proposals. Invention is fundamentally about killing weak ideas and
distilling survivors into a clear, actionable brief.

 DESIGNING FOR THE PROXY (7 MINUTE READ) [8]

 AI has become the first audience for much of our work, interpreting
and summarizing content before people see it, which makes
machine-readable structure increasingly important. However, the goal
should be to optimize for AI without designing for it, as the real
value of design still lies in the invisible decisions—systems,
accessibility, information architecture, and judgment—that
AI-generated interfaces can't replicate. The challenge is to create
experiences that are easy for machines to understand while remaining
focused on the humans they're ultimately meant to serve.

 NO, VERMONT'S NEW LOGO DESIGN ISN'T COMIC SANS (4 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Vermont Tourism has unveiled its first dedicated brand identity,
created by DNCO, with a custom wordmark inspired by the state's
hand-painted signs, landscapes and cultural heritage. Rather than
relying on generic travel imagery, the branding highlights Vermont as
a "State of Inspiration," using a flexible design system to showcase
its artistic community, natural beauty and local traditions. While
some critics compared the logo to Comic Sans, the design's details
intentionally reference Vermont's hills, roads, moonlit landscapes and
folklore, making it part of a broader identity rather than a
standalone logo.

MISCELLANEOUS

 FROM FRICTIONLESS TO MEANINGFUL (10 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Frictionless design is often forgettable and meaningless, while
friction can bring memory, reflection, connection, and personal growth
back into experiences. Embracing friction, rather than eliminating it,
is the path toward greater awareness, transparency, and growth.

 DESIGNING SYSTEMS THAT DECIDE (2 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Runtime-assembled AI interfaces require a fundamental shift in design
thinking, not just new tactics. Rather than shipping fixed screens,
designers must move toward outcome-oriented thinking—defining goals,
constraints, and rules for generation, much like an improv director
shaping a scene instead of scripting every line. This essay outlines
ten outdated design instincts to unlearn, replacing assumptions like
"same input, same output" with approaches suited to radically
adaptive, agent-driven interfaces.

 ADORATORIO STUDIO TAKES A NEW LENS ON EYEWEAR WITH A SOFT BUT
TECHNICAL REBRAND FOR INDICE (2 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Adoratorio Studio rebranded luxury eyewear retailer Indice for its
10th anniversary by repositioning it as "The Eyewear Lounge"—a
welcoming, culture-driven destination rather than a traditional
optical store. Inspired by the shop's interiors, the new identity
features a flowing logo based on the curves of a De Sede sofa (which
also resembles an eyelash), a muted color palette drawn from the space
itself, and a mix of expressive typography and soft film-style
photography. The result is a brand system that reflects the store's
atmosphere of craftsmanship, intimacy, and slow, considered shopping,
making the visual identity feel like a natural extension of the
physical experience.
