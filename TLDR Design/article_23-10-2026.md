NEWS & TRENDS

 CHATGPT SLIDES HAS SHIPPED, BUT ON WINDOWS 10 THE DESKTOP APP GIVES
YOU A POWERPOINT FILE (3 MINUTE READ) [4]

 ChatGPT Slides began rolling out on October 16 to Pro, Business, and
Enterprise users with Space access, but the Windows 10 desktop app
provides a PowerPoint file instead of a collaborative deck. Decks live
inside Space and inherit its view-and-edit sharing, with ChatGPT
joining in when @mentioned like a colleague. Exports reach PowerPoint,
PDF, or Google Slides, while Free, Go, and Plus plans still have no
release date.

 GOOGLE SEARCH TESTS LAYOUT CHANGE FOR LOCAL KNOWLEDGE PANEL (1 MINUTE
READ) [5]

 Google is testing a redesigned local knowledge panel that moves the
business name, rating, description, and action buttons above the
photos, bringing desktop results closer to the existing mobile design.
The lower section has also been tightened to create a more compact
overall layout. The changes are subtle but could affect how
prominently actions such as calls, directions, and website visits
appear to searchers.

 APPLE'S ‘DESIGNERS OF TOMORROW' HIGHLIGHTS CREATIVE PROJECTS FROM
EMERGING DESIGNERS (3 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Apple and Design Miami's second Designers of Tomorrow showcase
features work from Salem S. Charabi, Isabel Rower, Yoonjeong Lee, and
Parafernalia centered on the theme of presence. Their projects explore
how physical objects can capture attention and shape experiences,
ranging from lighting and furniture to pieces designed for shared
interaction. Apple devices are integrated throughout their creative
processes, including MacBook Pro for 3D modeling, iPad Pro and Apple
Pencil for sketching, and iPhone, Procreate, FaceTime, and iCloud for
documenting and collaborating remotely.

OPINIONS & TUTORIALS

 DESIGN SYSTEM FOUNDATIONS (2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Any team producing designed outputs already has a design system,
which turns the work into incremental improvement rather than a build
from zero. Before excitement takes over, everyone needs to agree
whether the system means a component library, a shared Figma file, or
documentation. Starting with color and typography distilled from
existing practice gives guardrails, not straitjackets, and avoids the
objections that come with imposed rules.

 DON'T PROMPT AI. DELEGATE TO IT (4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Prompting is fading as a personal skill, and a weekend-built Jira and
Confluence briefing agent shows the alternative: letting AI write the
prompt while a human defines what good looks like. Four or five
rewrite rounds produced a prompt that groups tickets by business
topic, flags unverifiable claims, and obeys one key line: do not
invent facts. Research backs the approach, with LLM-written
instructions matching or beating human ones on 19 of 24 tasks in the
2022 APE paper.

 A FOUNDER WITH A TOOL IS NOT A FOUNDER WITH A CLUE (22 MINUTE READ)
[9]

 AI has dramatically lowered the cost of producing software, allowing
solo founders to handle work that previously required designers,
engineers, and other specialists, but easier production does not
automatically provide the expertise required to judge whether the
result is good. Product quality still depends on hard-earned
understanding of users, trust, interaction, strategy, and countless
subtle decisions that generative tools can execute without truly
evaluating. Small AI-enabled teams can be extraordinarily effective,
but treating specialist expertise as unnecessary confuses the ability
to make something with the ability to recognize, shape, and refine
something worth making.

MISCELLANEOUS

 REVIEW AI-GENERATED UI CHANGES WITH STORYBOOK AND CHROMATIC (8 MINUTE
READ) [13]

 Sprawling AI-generated UI changesets can pass linting yet render
broken layouts, which makes raw diff review structurally inadequate. A
four-step playbook scopes each prompt to a single component, isolates
changes in Storybook, automates visual regression with Chromatic, and
adds a PR checklist. Together they close the gap between code that
compiles and code that renders correctly.

 DEBUNKING AI AGENT ACCESSIBILITY CLAIMS AND SOLVING AI ALIGNMENT FOR
ACCESSIBILITY (7 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Agents routinely claim work they never did, and having one agent
validate another is like letting the fox guard the henhouse. Deque's
report found that 88 percent of engineering leaders trust their
AI-generated code is accessible, while 64 percent cite accessibility
as a top driver of rework. A deterministic harness asking AI only
narrow yes-or-no questions creates proof-of-work records for human
verification, with Deque reporting that a 30-to-45-minute keyboard
test can take seconds.

 THE STATE OF TYPOGRAPHY IN DESIGN SYSTEMS (8 MINUTE READ) [15]

 A survey of 94 UX and product designers found that 78% can easily
change or suggest fonts, while brand alignment is the leading
consideration when choosing typefaces, followed by accessibility. Most
design systems use fewer than 10 typographic styles, potentially too
few to cover common needs, while token adoption remains inconsistent
and decisions about typography are rarely revisited after
implementation. Scaling type across screen sizes and choosing
appropriate styles emerged as the biggest challenges, alongside
maintaining cross-platform consistency and getting developers and
stakeholders to support better typographic decisions.
