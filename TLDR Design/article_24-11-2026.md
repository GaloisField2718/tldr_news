 FIGMA BRINGS ITS AGENT TO DEV MODE FOR DESIGN HANDOFFS (1 MINUTE
READ) [4]

 Figma's agent now works inside Dev Mode, answering design questions,
comparing versions, and preparing code snippets and asset download
links. Dev seats can chat without changing a design, while Full seats
with edit access can also request edits. Dev Mode is unavailable on
Starter plans, and agent usage consumes AI credits based on task
complexity.

 BING TESTS A HOMEPAGE SEARCH BAR REDESIGN WITH A “WHAT'S ON YOUR
MIND?” PROMPT (1 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Microsoft is testing a redesigned Bing homepage search bar that
replaces the traditional "Search the web" placeholder with the more
conversational "What's on your mind?" prompt. The interface introduces
a dedicated web search button and reorganizes the plus menu into
separate Attach and Tools sections, with options for adding images and
generating them. The changes bring Bing closer to an AI chatbot
experience while preserving conventional search functionality,
although the scope of the experiment remains unclear.

 WHY HAS NORTH KOREA REDESIGNED ITS FLAG? IT'S NOT ABOUT AESTHETICS...
(4 MINUTE READ) [6]

 North Korea has changed its national flag's proportions from 1:2 to
1:1.65, thickening the white stripes and shifting the star closer to
the center. The move may reflect its increasingly explicit rejection
of reunification with South Korea rather than a purely aesthetic
redesign.

 DESIGN TOOLS JUST GOT PERSONAL (6 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Building your own tools is part of the design job again. In the UX
Tools prototyping survey, 59% of 1,478 respondents had built a tool of
their own with AI in the previous six months. Those tools encode a
designer's judgment and way of working, making them more worth showing
in a portfolio than finished screens.

 THE ATTACK OF THE GIANT AI DESIGN SLOP MONSTER: A SURVIVOR'S GUIDE
(10 MINUTE READ) [8]

 AI coding tools allow product managers, developers, and designers to
create convincing interfaces quickly, but the results can introduce
usability problems, inconsistent patterns, and technical risks that
designers must subsequently resolve. When AI-generated prototypes
become implementation requirements without proper validation, design
teams risk spending more time repairing flawed decisions than they
would have spent designing the experience correctly. Establishing
explicit guardrails for user research, design systems, accessibility,
and security can help teams use AI effectively while maintaining
responsibility for the quality of the resulting products.

 WHY AI FACTORIES NEED A DESIGN SYSTEM (20 MINUTE READ) [9]

 AI agents generating websites and interfaces need shared design rules
to prevent inconsistent components, inaccessible experiences, and
repeated human corrections. Kirupa's ROFL design system provides
machine-readable tokens, component specifications, layouts,
accessibility requirements, and task-specific instructions that agents
can consult before building. Automated source checks help verify
generated work against the system. Browser-based checks are still
needed across its nine visual styles in light and dark modes, while
human reviewers focus on decisions that require judgment rather than
correcting predictable mistakes.

 FIGMA PROTOTYPE USABILITY TESTING: HOW TO TEST WITH USERS BEFORE YOU
BUILD (7 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Testing a Figma prototype with users before engineering builds it
turns review debates about taste into evidence: task success, time on
task, paths, and recordings. Fair results depend on linking every
screen a task needs, sharing a login-free link, and writing tasks as
goals rather than click paths. Rerunning the same tasks on the live
site later yields a comparable before-and-after baseline.

 HOCHBURG BRINGS TOGETHER PIZZA AND HIP-HOP IN L'ARTISTA'S IDENTITY (3
MINUTE READ) [15]

 Stuttgart-based Hochburg has created a distinctive identity for
Neapolitan pizza brand L'Artista, combining traditional pizza-making
with the visual energy of hip-hop, vintage MTV graphics, and New York
street culture. The system centers on a hand-drawn wordmark with
multiple variations that change shape and proportion, supported by
bold Rekind Expanded typography, Ivar Mono, and expressive motion
graphics. Oversized lettering extends across pizza boxes, menus,
merchandise, and restaurant interiors, giving L'Artista an identity
that feels closer to an independent hip-hop label than a conventional
pizzeria.

 VERVE CASTS LEGACY SOFTWARE AS THE STONE AGE IN REBRAND FOR AI
STARTUP ZERO (5 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Amsterdam-based Verve has rebranded Helsinki AI startup Zero,
positioning its software as an evolutionary leap that could make
traditional customer relationship management platforms obsolete. The
identity depicts legacy CRM systems as a prehistoric landscape of
cracked earth and fossilized tools, contrasted with Zero's futuristic,
alien-inspired technology and a character named Zorb. Designed as both
a brand identity and an expandable campaign platform, the system
combines unconventional creative concepts with AI-assisted production
to support future marketing, events, and product communications.
