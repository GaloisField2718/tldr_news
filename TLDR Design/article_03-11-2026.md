NEWS & TRENDS

 CANVA UNVEILS BRUSH, AN AI MODEL BUILT TO EDIT DESIGNS AT LOWER COST
(5 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Brush, Canva's in-house vision-language model, edits designs up to
69% faster than the previous design agent at 46% lower cost per task.
It reads both content and layout across full designs like multi-page
decks, writes and runs small pieces of code, and inspects results
before each next step. Brush is in beta for Canva Pro subscribers,
with Brand Kit support and better image-heavy layout handling planned.


 AMSTEL UPDATES GLOBAL VISUAL IDENTITY TO HIGHLIGHT AMSTERDAM HERITAGE
(2 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Amstel has redesigned its packaging and logo around its 1870
Amsterdam origins, adding bespoke typography inspired by historic
canal bridge signage and a refined golden circle. The red, white,
black, and gold system also features a wave pattern, a horizontal
split evoking the Amstel River, and three coins representing the
founders, city, and waterway. Already a Red Dot winner, the identity
is rolling out across 11 markets, with all remaining territories due
to transition by the end of 2027.

 OPENAI IS FINALLY MERGING CHATGPT'S CHAT AND WORK TABS (1 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 OpenAI is preparing to merge ChatGPT's Chat and Work tabs into one
experience, letting users move from discussing ideas to completing
tasks with files, plugins, and web tools without switching tabs. The
change will first reach select users for feedback, while Codex remains
a separate section of the desktop app. ChatGPT and Codex share a token
allowance, which could mean more frequent waits for usage resets for
some users.

 HOW WE'RE TRAINING A FIGMA-FLUENT EDITING MODEL (4 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Specialized post-training lets Figma's first-party editing model
compete with frontier models on precise, iterative edits to existing
designs. A Llama 3.3 70B model trained on 250,000+ examples first
became competitive with top models, but imitation learning limited
generalization, so Figma now invests in reinforcement learning. Since
beta, typical tasks run about 20% faster, users rate results 33%
higher, and they undo the agent's work 60% less often.

 DESIGN IS MORE THAN CREATION (4 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Design is the deliberate process of deciding how something should
work, respond, and be experienced, not simply the act of producing
something that looks convincing. Conditions such as accessibility
needs, localization, unpredictable content, failures, and different
input methods should shape software from the beginning rather than
being treated as edge cases to fix afterward. AI makes polished
software dramatically faster to create, but that speed increases the
importance of understanding which decisions were made, which
assumptions they rely on, and whether they survive contact with
reality.

 EXPERIENCE DESIGN: DROPPING THE “USER” FROM “USER EXPERIENCE”
(6 MINUTE READ) [12]

 AI agents complicate the traditional definition of UX because people
may increasingly accomplish goals without directly interacting with
the companies, applications, and interfaces involved. Experience
design provides a broader framing that includes traditional UX while
also considering digital systems used by agents acting on humans'
behalf and journeys involving multiple human and nonhuman actors.
People's goals remain central, but designers must now consider
experiences in which the immediate user of a product may be an AI
rather than a person.

MISCELLANEOUS

 HOW TO WRITE BETTER COPY FOR GRAPHIC DESIGN PROJECTS (6 MINUTE READ)
[17]

 Better graphic design copy starts with the project's purpose: a
plain-language explanation, the audience, the most relevant benefit,
objections, and the desired next step. Specific details persuade where
vague praise does not, and a consistent brand voice should sound like
the owner speaking face to face. Copy must fit its space, CTAs must
match the reader's decision stage, and a final proofread should test
every link and QR code.

 IN THE AGE OF AI, CURIOSITY IS OUR GREATEST CREATIVE TOOL (7 MINUTE
READ) [18]

 AI makes generating images and concepts easy, so the harder work is
deciding what is worth making, whose perspective is missing, and
whether it communicates. At DesignThinkers, RGD's annual conference in
Toronto, speakers covered information design, blending strategy with
creativity, and designing a city. AI-accelerated trends risk polished
sameness, so "raw, messy curiosity is the most valuable thing we can
bring to the table."

 STUDIO KILN DESIGNS AN IDENTITY FOR RESET THAT ADAPTS LIKE ITS APP (4
MINUTE READ) [19]

 Studio Kiln's identity for Reset reflects the weight-loss app's
personalized approach to eating, building flexibility and
responsiveness directly into its visual system. A dynamic identity
uses typography, color, imagery, and graphic elements that can shift
across different contexts while maintaining a recognizable core. The
result gives Reset a distinctive presence without locking a product
built around personalized meal guidance into a rigid visual language.
