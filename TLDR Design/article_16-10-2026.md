NEWS & TRENDS

 GOOGLE PLAY REDESIGN ADAPTS TO CAR DISPLAYS (1 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Google's redesigned Play Store for cars with Google built-in adapts
to different display sizes and orientations, rolling out automatically
over the coming weeks. Entertainment collections for parked use sit
apart from driving-related options, and a left-side rail becomes a top
bar in portrait. YouTube Premium subscribers get background audio when
the car shifts into drive, and Firefox and STARZ join the lineup.

 ADOBE SOFTWARE DUPE MAKER TAKES ON MICROSOFT OFFICE, RELEASING FREE
VERSIONS OF WORD, POWERPOINT, AND EXCEL (2 MINUTE READ) [5]

 A developer who used AI to recreate Adobe's creative apps in Rust as
free, open-source tools is now taking on Microsoft Office. WordCraft,
DeckCraft, and GridCraft arrive as free stand-ins for Word,
PowerPoint, and Excel under the ArtCraft brand. Some subreddits
removed posts over cybersecurity and ethics concerns about
AI-generated code. Supporters frame the apps as a protest against
Adobe's subscription tactics.

 OPERA AIR GETS A MINIMALIST MAKEOVER WITH A FOCUS ON DISTRACTION-FREE
BROWSING (2 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Opera Air has been redesigned around distraction-free browsing, using
a cleaner interface that keeps additional tools hidden until they are
needed. New wallpapers use open spaces, perspective, and natural
textures that adapt to light and dark themes, while built-in
Soundscapes combine ambient sounds and original music to encourage
focus or relaxation. The browser also includes customizable break
reminders, breathing exercises, neck stretches, an ad blocker, and a
free VPN while allowing messaging and social apps to remain accessible
through the sidebar.

OPINIONS & TUTORIALS

 CRITIQUE IN THE AI ERA (6 MINUTE READ) [7]

 AI can produce twelve credible variations before the coffee cools, so
the design crit should become a debrief, like an F1 team studying
telemetry over finishing position. Teams ask about reasoning, friction
with the model and verification, and also critique the setup: briefs,
constraints, and rubrics. AI critics tend to agree with the designer,
and great AI-first designers sense limits, give precise feedback, and
know when to override the model.

 SCALING THE DESIGN PROCESS (1 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Static personas capture real-world complexity poorly in
intelligence-driven products, where outcomes are probabilistic and
vary across people and contexts. AI lets teams build products that
adapt to individual users at scale, which means designing for a
spectrum of outcomes.

 AGENTIC SYSTEMS ARE THE FUTURE OF BRANDS (8 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Traditional brand guidelines struggle when teams and AI agents must
constantly make decisions that were never explicitly documented,
causing otherwise competent work to gradually drift toward generic
outcomes. Agentic brand systems can instead encode decisions about
voice, color, typography, layout, components, and behavior into
structured, machine-readable instructions that both humans and agents
can apply directly. As AI dramatically increases the volume and speed
of production, documenting not just what a brand looks like but why
and how its decisions should be made becomes essential for maintaining
coherence.

MISCELLANEOUS

 STUDIO HERRSTRÖM REBRANDS SONY MUSIC IMPRINT MILKSHAKE (4 MINUTE
READ) [13]

 Studio Herrström's identity for Sony Music imprint Milkshake
combines references from car culture, flavored drinks, and pop music
into a single energetic visual language. The original splash mark has
been redrawn as a rounded emblem inspired by fluid, metal, and
reflections, accompanied by neon pinks, high-vis yellows, asphalt
blacks, soft lilacs, and the neo-grotesque Gates typeface. Information
layouts borrow from car window permits and milk carton nutrition
tables, creating structured containers for track titles, release
dates, copyright information, and other details across digital and
promotional materials.

 FIT HEIGHT TO BACKGROUND PATTERN SIZE WITH CALC-SIZE() (4 MINUTE
READ) [14]

 CSS background patterns can end awkwardly when a container's content
produces a height that isn't an exact multiple of the background tile,
while `background-repeat: round` fixes the edge by distorting the
pattern itself. Combining `calc-size()` with `round()` instead lets
the browser calculate the element's natural height and round it up to
the nearest multiple of the tile height. The result behaves similarly
to `min-height`, preserving both the content-driven size and the
original pattern proportions, though browser support remains limited.
