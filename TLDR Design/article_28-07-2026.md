 MIDJOURNEY ACQUIRED THE ASTROLOGY APP CO-STAR (1 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Midjourney, an AI lab known for its image and video generators, has
acquired social astrology app Co-Star, with deal terms undisclosed.
Co-Star, which has roughly 4.3 million monthly active users, helps
people generate and share astrological birth charts and uses both AI
and humans for horoscopes and compatibility readings. Co-Star's two
dozen employees have joined Midjourney, whose growing consumer lineup
already includes medical and spa ventures alongside its Discord-based
image tools.

 APPLE'S SMARTWATCHES WILL LOOK THE SAME FOR AT LEAST ANOTHER YEAR, OR
TWO (2 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Apple is reportedly preparing the Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4
for this fall, but neither is expected to receive a significant design
overhaul. The biggest improvements will instead focus on a
long-awaited chip upgrade and more advanced health and fitness
tracking, while major features like blood-sugar detection remain years
away. Apple Watch has settled into a conservative cycle of incremental
refinement, even as Apple takes bigger design and technology risks
with products like smart glasses and camera-equipped AirPods.

 BLACK FOREST LABS LAUNCHES FLUX 3 CAPABLE OF GENERATING IMAGES AND
20-SECOND VIDEO WITH AUDIO (11 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Black Forest Labs unveiled FLUX 3, its first public video generation
model. Early access requires approval, and the company has not yet
disclosed pricing, service-level commitments, or full benchmark
methodology. The video tier generates up to 20-second clips with
native audio and multi-shot continuity. A related model, FLUX-mimic,
extends the same architecture toward robotic manipulation tasks.

OPINIONS & TUTORIALS

 MINIMALISM IS DEAD. WELCOME TO THE AFTERPARTY (8 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Design's decade-long minimalist era is ending, giving way to a new
phase that reintroduces depth, texture, and personality. Minimalism
served as a necessary discipline that taught designers structure and
hierarchy, but by the late 2010s it had curdled into sterile,
forgettable sameness exemplified by trends like Corporate Memphis.
Emerging styles such as neumorphism, referential skeuomorphism, and
brutalist web design now build whimsy and emotional richness on top of
that structural foundation rather than rejecting it.

 THE ARCHITECTURE OF GUILT: HOW DUOLINGO WEAPONIZED NEGATIVE EMOTION
TO BUILD A DESIGN SYSTEM (17 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Duolingo drives retention by using loss aversion, streaks, guilt, and
an emotionally responsive mascot to make maintaining progress feel
more compelling than earning traditional rewards like points or
badges. Its system also anticipates inevitable failure with mechanisms
like Streak Freeze, helping users recover without abandoning the habit
entirely. These techniques demonstrate the power of behavioral design
while raising an important ethical boundary: persuasion supports goals
users genuinely value, while coercion manufactures anxiety primarily
to improve engagement metrics.

 YOUR WEBSITE IS BORING (BUT THAT MIGHT JUST SET IT FREE) (7 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 Years of optimizing websites for conversion, accessibility, SEO, and
templates have made the web increasingly homogeneous. AI agents now
threaten to reduce brands even further into interchangeable,
machine-readable commodities. The future could instead become a
bifurcated web: a “Silent Web” of structured data optimized for
machines alongside an “Experiential Web” designed around
distinctive, memorable, human-centered brand experiences. AI can help
enable that shift when treated as a creative partner for storytelling
and interaction rather than simply another tool for automation and
efficiency.

 A SIDE PROJECT IS THE FASTEST WAY TO UPSKILL IN THE AGE OF AI (5
MINUTE READ) [13]

 Side projects have become the fastest route to upskilling in the AI
era, now that cost, coding, and time barriers have largely
disappeared. Rather than relying on no-code tools like Lovable,
working closer to "the metal" with Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor is
recommended, learning through iterative back-and-forth instead of full
automation. One app, Pegs Out, built in two weeks, serves as a
concrete example of how accessible this process now is.

 THE SIX LAWS OF PSYCHOLOGY FOR GOOD UX DESIGN (6 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Six cognitive-psychology principles—covering decision time, target
size, familiar patterns, contrast, memorable endings, and visual
polish—shape whether users complete tasks smoothly. Applying laws
like Hick's, Fitts', Jakob's, the Von Restorff effect, the Peak-End
rule, and the Aesthetic-Usability effect reduces support tickets,
speeds task completion, and boosts conversions. Designers should map
interfaces against each law, test early with prototypes, and re-test
after changes to confirm improvements.
