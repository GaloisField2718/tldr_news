 IPHONE 20: RUMOR HAS IT APPLE IS PREPPING A RADICAL REDESIGN (2
MINUTE READ) [4]

in September 2027, will bring a radical redesign with curved,

6.9 inches, potentially dropping the notch or Dynamic Island entirely.
A leaker also claims solid-state buttons with haptic feedback could
replace mechanical controls for power, volume, and camera.

 WE'RE LAUNCHING LYRIA 3.5 IN GOOGLE FLOW MUSIC (1 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Google Lyria 3.5 is a new music generation model that started rolling
out in Google Flow Music on July 29. The model brings notable
improvements in musicality, lyric quality, and vocal realism, aiming
to help users create richer, more emotionally expressive songs. It
also adds finer creative control, letting users more easily adjust the
tempo and duration of generated tracks.

 JONY IVE'S FIRST OPENAI HARDWARE DEVICE SOUNDS RATHER LIKE … A

 OpenAI continues to tease its first AI hardware product, and recent

upcoming HomePad.

 INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE IS THE FOUNDATION AI IS STARVING FOR (12
MINUTE READ) [7]

 Poor information architecture, not weak models, drives AI
hallucinations and wrong answers, since retrieval only surfaces the
best-matched text, not the most accurate one. Unlike human users who
tolerate ambiguity, AI systems reproduce mislabeled or unstructured
content confidently and at scale, turning a once-invisible cost into
measurable failures and financial liability, as seen in the Air Canada
chatbot case. This cost is now trackable in retrieval accuracy and
hallucination rates, so organizations should audit and fix their
content structure before purchasing new AI models or tools.

 HOW FIGMA USES 3 COLLABORATION UX PATTERNS TO DRIVE PRODUCT ADOPTION
(5 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Figma turned collaboration into a growth engine through three UX
patterns: real-time multiplayer editing, contextual on-design
comments, and effortless link sharing, now used by 95% of Fortune 500
companies. Each pattern lowers barriers for non-designers to
participate, letting files support up to 500 collaborators and turning
every shared link into a potential new user. These patterns succeed by
embedding teamwork directly into the product itself, creating network
effects as adoption spreads naturally through everyday collaboration
rather than top-down rollout.

 HOW TO IMPROVE WEBSITE PERFORMANCE: BEST PRACTICES FOR FASTER SITES
(7 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Website speed shapes user trust, bounce rates, conversions, and
search rankings, making performance optimization a priority for
growing sites. Seven tactics are recommended, including image
optimization, code minification, browser caching, reduced third-party
scripts, and stronger hosting. Common mistakes—like disabled
compression, excess metadata, and missing server cache—can undermine
these efforts if left unaddressed.

MISCELLANEOUS

 ON LAYOUT CHOREOGRAPHY (6 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Layout choreography gives interface reflows a visible path, letting
users track where elements moved instead of experiencing a
disorienting jump. FLIP animates the transform property rather than
layout properties like top or height, since layout has already
occurred by the time the animation runs. Effective choreography
sequences movement by importance—the triggering element responds
first, then surrounding content adapts—while still requiring a
reduced-motion fallback that preserves content and clarity.

 LITTLE GREEN ROOMS REPOSITIONS AROUND HOMEWORKING WITH NEW IDENTITY
AND WEBSITE (2 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Little Green Rooms has repositioned its brand around the idea of
“Headspace Found,” shifting its focus from selling garden
buildings to creating dedicated spaces for home working and
creativity. The rebrand includes a refined visual identity, a
content-led website showcasing real customer stories, and messaging
centered on productivity and well-being rather than construction
features. To launch the new direction, the company created an
interactive “WiFi Garden” installation at the WOMAD festival,
giving visitors a hands-on experience of its garden studios.

 TYPE DESIGNERS HAVE CREATED A FREE FONT THAT "POISONS" AI (3 MINUTE
READ) [15]

 ShieldFont is a free, open-source typeface that protects online
content from AI scraping by displaying readable text to humans while
subtly altering the underlying source code that many AI crawlers
ingest, potentially contaminating training datasets. Unlike previous
anti-AI fonts, it works as a practical web font and includes a toolkit
that allows the same protection to be applied to other compatible
typefaces. Its creators describe it as a way to make unauthorized
content scraping more difficult without opposing AI itself, arguing
that publishing work online should not be treated as consent for AI
training.
