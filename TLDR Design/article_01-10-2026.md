 MIDJOURNEY ADDS LIVE STYLE PREVIEWS AND MORE REVERSIBLE CONTROLS (1
MINUTE READ) [4]

 Midjourney added live style previews in the Styles sidebar, saved
prompt defaults, and undo for project deletion. Experimental previews
rely on fast models and prompt text alone, and picking a style submits
the prompt with it. Midjourney says its updated edit model preserves
pixels outside the selected area, a claim shown only in an official
demonstration, not an independent test.

 Figma has expanded its London office at Elder Yard on Norton Folgate,
deepening investment in one of its largest markets outside the United
States. Two thirds of the FTSE 100 build with Figma as of Q2 2026, and

 THE BOX DESIGN SYSTEM: AI‑READY FIGMA FILES FOR ENTERPRISE SOFTWARE
(8 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Flexy Global's Box Design System organizes complex enterprise product
behavior into bounded Figma sections, making design files easier for
people to navigate and for AI coding agents to use. Boxes form a
feature, flow, and screen-state hierarchy built around behavior rather
than pages, with self-contained context and arrows showing
transitions. They cannot fix an undefined product or replace the
design system and codebase, but as design files become inputs to
AI-assisted development, this structure is becoming part of the
product.

 HOW AI AGENTS ARE RESHAPING UX (3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Decagon CEO Jesse Zhang says personal AI agents such as Meta's Muse
will reshape customer experience, since agents skip product displays
and purchase-inducing flows. Amazon blocked Muse while Shopify opened
a dedicated payment channel, though Zhang expects Amazon to back down
because customers want AI assistant integration. Companies need to
verify agent authority, set negotiation limits, handle exceptions, and
stop fraud running at machine speed, with AI systems ultimately
transacting machine to machine.

 WHAT DOES AI-READINESS MEAN IN DESIGN SYSTEMS? (7 MINUTE READ) [9]

 AI-ready design systems depend less on specialized agent tooling and
more on strong engineering fundamentals that make components, APIs,
tokens, and documentation easy for machines to discover and
understand. Testing with the Open Design System Bench found that clean
separation of concerns and a single source of truth can outperform
additional instruction files, which may actually hurt results when
they contain stale or duplicated information. Mechanical correctness
is only half the challenge: design systems also need principles and
guidance that help AI exercise good design judgment rather than simply
generate technically valid interfaces.

 A SHORT UPDATE ON DESIGNING FOR FOLDABLE DEVICES (1 MINUTE READ) [13]


 Three years after the Google Pixel Fold, the iPhone Duo announcement
brings a fresh look at designing for foldable devices. CSS media
features, a Viewport Segments API, and a Device Posture API work only
in Chromium browsers, with no sign yet of Safari support. Websites
needn't adapt to two screens. A dual-screen design makes sense when an
experience has two simultaneous contexts, such as an inbox beside an
open message.

 DO PRODUCT DESIGNERS NEED UI/UX SKILLS? (6 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Product designers do need UI/UX skills, because product design spans
research, wireframes, prototypes, testing, and developer
collaboration, and UI/UX principles apply at nearly every stage. UI
covers what users see, while UX covers whether the product works, and
a beautiful app nobody can figure out still fails. Designers need not
master every corner, but skills in research, wireframing, prototyping,
and usability testing help them read user behavior accurately and keep
decisions grounded.

 THIS PACKAGING FOR READY-MADE DINNERS CAN TEACH YOU TO COOK BETTER (4
MINUTE READ) [15]

 Center's identity for chef Daniel Humm's Riff turns ready-to-cook
meal packaging into a lightweight cooking lesson, showing optional
fresh ingredients alongside explanations of how each changes a dish's
flavor. The visual system combines a chunky curved wordmark, 12
food-inspired colors, three contrasting typefaces, deliberately
unstyled food photography, and a playful mascot called Dan the Pan.
Packaging reinforces the idea of improvisation with cooking tips,
recipe variations, and QR codes linking to Humm's cooking videos,
positioning convenience food as a starting point for learning rather
than a finished product.
