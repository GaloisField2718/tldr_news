 INSTAGRAM INTRODUCES A REDESIGNED WORDMARK (1 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Instagram has introduced a redesigned wordmark for the first time
since 2016, giving its name a sharper, more modern look while keeping
references to the original style. The new cursive-inspired lettering
has received mixed reactions, with some praising the fresher design
and others joking that the stylized “r” makes it look like
“Instagzam.” Instagram has not said whether the wordmark update
will be followed by broader visual changes, such as a new app icon.

 IPHONE ULTRA'S CAMERAS MIGHT GET VERY UNORTHODOX PLACEMENT, PER LEAK

 MEASURING TIME SAVINGS FROM FIGMA MAKE (7 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Figma's Data Science team ran a randomized controlled trial with 100
participants to measure how much time Figma Make actually saves on
design tasks. Design work was found to be 20% faster and 16% easier
overall, with product managers seeing the largest gains—23% faster
and 37% easier—though designers benefited most on complex tasks
while PMs gained more on simpler ones. Figma plans further RCTs,
including one testing its new design agent's parallel-agent
capabilities.

 AI GOVERNANCE IN DESIGN TOOLS: WHAT HAPPENS TO YOUR DATA WHEN AI
ENTERS THE WORKFLOW (11 MINUTE READ) [8]

 AI governance frameworks bring documentation, traceability, and
accountability to AI features embedded in design tools. Building a
framework involves defining scope and owners, classifying design data,
standardizing vendor evaluation, and embedding controls into workflows
and CI/CD processes. Penpot positions itself as a governance-friendly
alternative through self-hosting, open file formats, and its MCP
server, letting organizations control AI models and data flows on
their own infrastructure.

 BIAS IN LLMS, OR HOW TO DESIGN FOR EVERYONE WITH TOOLS BUILT FOR
SOMEONE (9 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Large language models are not neutral and often reflect or amplify
biases related to language, geography, gender, culture, and
socioeconomic status because of the data they are trained on and the
feedback systems used to refine them. As a result, AI-generated
personas, research summaries, copy, and recommendations can
overrepresent dominant groups and present their perspectives as
consensus while overlooking minority viewpoints. Designers should
treat AI outputs as inputs for further thinking rather than objective
conclusions, and actively review them for hidden assumptions, missing
perspectives, and demographic bias.

 NINE PERSONA MISTAKES THAT ARE COSTING YOU CUSTOMERS (49 MINUTE READ)
[13]

 Nine common persona mistakes undermine product design, from
over-relying on AI to using outdated, generic, or ignored personas.
Personas should be research-based, focused, specific, updated per
project, and actively shared across teams to be effective. Avoiding
these pitfalls helps teams build products that better match customer
needs, improving satisfaction and business outcomes.

 GOT NO TIME FOR PRD (3 MINUTE READ) [14]

 PRDs still suit large teams aligning around a known target, but they
assume the destination is already chosen, which doesn't fit early
exploration. A lighter six-line alternative—Intent, Bet, Bar, Scope,
Loop, Never—distills into one sentence short enough to hand directly
to an AI agent. Unlike lengthy PRDs models can read but which lock in
premature decisions, this compressed brief keeps both humans and
agents flexible while testing risky assumptions.
