 GITHUB PREVIEWS A REDESIGNED PULL REQUEST OVERVIEW (1 MINUTE READ)
[5]

 GitHub's redesigned pull request Overview page began rolling out on
November 5 as an opt-in public preview. A compact summary at the top
now shows merge readiness, what is blocking it, and available actions,
alongside new comment filters. Users can return to the classic view
from the same menu. GitHub calls this an early iteration rather than
the finished redesign.

 APPLE AIMING TO MAKE SIRI ‘MORE LIKE AN AGENT' AS SOON AS NEXT
YEAR: REPORT (2 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Apple is reportedly working to transform Siri into a proactive AI
agent capable of tracking goals, planning next steps, and completing
tasks without requiring users to initiate every action. The company
could introduce these capabilities as early as 2027, building on the
personalized Siri features delivered with iOS 27 and potentially using
its App Intents framework to interact with applications more
efficiently. Apple is also adjusting its iOS 28 development process to
give engineers more time for coding and experimentation, reflecting
the growing influence of AI-assisted development.

 RAISING THE CEILING MUST BE EARNED (5 MINUTE READ) [7]

 AI tools can raise the baseline quality of creative work, but access
to powerful technology does not automatically increase someone's
ability to produce exceptional results. Developing taste and judgment
requires understanding design fundamentals, recognizing weaknesses in
generated work, and knowing how to direct improvements rather than
accepting whatever looks convincing. AI-assisted prototyping can
accelerate learning, but raising the ceiling of what's possible still
depends on practicing the craft, understanding how things work, and
developing the ability to critique them.

 NO FIGMA, ONE BRANCH, A 3-4 DAY SPRINT, AND WHERE THE ENGINEERS TAKE
OVER (6 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Design engineers can shorten the distance between design and
development by creating functional prototypes directly in a product's
codebase instead of producing static Figma handoffs. In one team's
workflow, a design engineer collaborates on discovery, builds and
tests prototypes with users in three to four days, and delivers
finished components with Storybook documentation while software
engineers handle backend systems and architecture. The role combines
front-end development, rapid prototyping, user research, and
AI-assisted coding, allowing designers to contribute directly to
production without replacing specialized engineering expertise.

 WHY I MAKE MOOD BOARDS (7 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Mood boards can become more than collections of visual references
when the arrangement of images creates meanings that none of the
individual pictures communicate alone. Juxtaposition, proximity, and
contrast generate unexpected relationships between otherwise unrelated
subjects, turning the act of collecting and arranging images into a
form of visual thinking. Building collections gradually around themes
such as machines, time, or technology allows new connections to emerge
through experimentation rather than following a predetermined
narrative.

MISCELLANEOUS

 THERE'S NO BETTER NAME FOR DESIGN SYSTEMS (4 MINUTE READ) [13]

 No better name for design systems exists, because what matters to
stakeholders is what the system enables, not the components inside it.
Product, methodology, and infrastructure each translate it for
different audiences, with infrastructure traveling furthest across
functions. Success means every team knows what the design system does
for them, so it becomes simply the way things get built.

 WHAT GREATER DEVOLUTION COULD MEAN FOR DESIGN (5 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Greater financial autonomy for regional governments in the UK could
create new opportunities for design-led regeneration, with mayors
gaining additional control over business rates, borrowing, and future
income tax revenues. More predictable funding could encourage
long-term investment in workplaces, infrastructure, and adaptive reuse
projects that preserve local character while reducing construction
waste and embodied carbon. Successful regeneration will depend on
combining regional knowledge with broader design expertise, improving
collaboration between public and private sectors, and creating spaces
that reflect the communities and businesses they serve.

 THE VOLLEYBALL WORLD CUP LOGO LOOKS... WRONG (3 MINUTE READ) [15]

 The identity for the 2027 FIVB Men's Volleyball World Cup in Poland
features an abstract white emblem inspired by the tournament trophy,
surrounded by concentric red circles. Its design combines several
symbolic references, including the trophy's silhouette, the Earth, a
volleyball net, players reaching for a ball, and fans represented by a
wave at the base. Although these elements are individually
identifiable, their combination produces an ambiguous mark that raises
questions about how much symbolism a logo can communicate before
losing immediate visual clarity.
