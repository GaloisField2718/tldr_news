 FIGMA MCP: RUN WEAVE TOOLS, RIGHT FROM YOUR FAVORITE AGENT (1 MINUTE
READ) [4]

 Figma Weave tools can now be run from external AI clients like
ChatGPT, Claude, and Cursor through the Figma MCP server. Users can
describe a task, such as running a Brand mockup tool, and the agent
finds it, executes it, and returns results directly in the
conversation. This lets users stay within their chat workflow while
preserving context like prompts, images, and brand guidelines.

 WHATSAPP'S NEXT IPHONE UPDATE COULD MAKE SEARCHING CHATS MUCH EASIER
(1 MINUTE READ) [5]

 WhatsApp is testing a new in-chat search button for iPhone that would
make it much easier to find older messages without navigating through
multiple menus. Instead of opening the chat info screen to access
search, users would be able to start searching directly from the
conversation, with the button appearing when scrolling through older
messages. The change streamlines a common task and reflects WhatsApp's
ongoing efforts to reduce friction in everyday interactions.

 ANTHROPIC TO START WATERMARKING CLAUDE-GENERATED TEXT, IMAGES (2
MINUTE READ) [6]

 Anthropic will embed an invisible watermark in text and images
generated by Claude, aligning it with the EU AI Act's voluntary Code
of Practice. Text watermarking applies to Claude models released after
August 2nd, and the mark persists when text is copied to other
applications or edited. Images will be marked using C2PA metadata,
though detection may fail on short or heavily edited text snippets.

 TASTE IS ALL THAT'S LEFT (16 MINUTE READ) [7]

 With AI collapsing the distance between idea and working artifact,
the friction that once rationed output and trained judgment has
disappeared. Taste, the wordless verdict on what is worth keeping, is
the one part of the work machines cannot perform. The scarce act is no
longer making but choosing what deserves to exist.

 IS THERE A RETIREMENT CRISIS IN THE CREATIVE INDUSTRY? (5 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Many senior creatives are considering early retirement due to
economic uncertainty, shrinking margins, and concerns about AI
reducing demand for production-focused creative work. However,
retirement is not a guaranteed solution and human expertise remains
valuable in areas such as strategy, branding, problem-solving, and
specialised craft. The most resilient creatives will be those who
adapt their skills and shift their value from execution to
higher-level thinking and decision-making.

 GOING FROM SENIOR TO STAFF DESIGNER ISN'T ABOUT DOING THE BEST WORK
ANYMORE (7 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Moving from Senior to Staff or Principal designer is a shift from
shipping the best work to setting direction. The harder skill is
externalising taste so other designers can act on it, rather than
developing more of it. Six practices are offered, including writing
principles instead of preferences and pre-stating review criteria.

 A UX DESIGN PERSPECTIVE ON IMPROVING AI SAFETY (6 MINUTE READ) [12]

 A lawsuit over a teenager's death after months of conversations with
a Character.AI companion bot frames the limits of on-screen
disclosures. State laws such as Washington's HB 2225 now mandate
repeated non-human disclosure and self-harm protocols, which risk
becoming banner blindness. A duty of care is proposed, with public
reporting on how often safeguards fail rather than descriptions of
perfect guardrails.

 THISSAWAY REFRESHES HISTORIC GOLF RESORT THE BELFRY FOLLOWING MAJOR
EXPANSION (4 MINUTE READ) [13]

 The Belfry Hotel & Resort has unveiled a new brand identity by
Thissaway to coincide with major investments in its facilities,
including a new conference centre and wellness hub. The rebrand is
built around the historical meaning of a belfry—a bell tower that
brings people together—which reflects the resort's role as a
destination for celebrations, events, and shared experiences. The
updated identity features a refined bell symbol, contemporary serif
typography, an expanded colour palette, and a flexible system that
extends across the resort's spa, club, dining venues, signage, digital
channels, and marketing materials.
