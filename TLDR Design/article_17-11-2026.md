 APPLE ENGINEERS ‘ACTIVELY WORKING' ON FACE ID FOR IPHONE DUO 2'S
OUTER DISPLAY (3 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Apple is reportedly developing Face ID for the outer display of its
second-generation foldable iPhone Duo, potentially replacing the Touch
ID button used on the original model. Engineers have already built
prototypes with facial recognition, although the technology may not be
ready for the device's expected fall 2027 launch. Other priorities
include integrating Action Button functionality into Camera Control,
adding a telephoto camera in future generations, and making the
foldable thinner and lighter.

 WHO IS GETTING HIRED IN UX RESEARCH IN 2026, AND WHAT THEY'RE GETTING
PAID (6 MINUTE READ) [5]

 The US UX research job market has contracted sharply, with
approximately 1,800 researchers hired over the past year, 66% fewer
than in 2022. An analysis of 1,593 job postings found that only 10%
targeted early-career researchers, while 43% sought senior candidates
and employers increasingly prioritized quantitative skills, AI product
experience, and strategic influence. The strongest compensation
opportunities are concentrated among experienced researchers at major
technology companies, raising concerns about how the profession will
develop its next generation of talent.

 STARK'S ACCESSIBILITY POWER ARRIVES IN CODEX AND CHATGPT AS A PLUGIN
AND SKILLS (2 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Stark now ships as a plugin with skills in OpenAI's Codex and
ChatGPT, scanning Figma files, code, Storybook, mobile apps, and live
URLs from a connected workspace. Codex can pull violations with full
context and code-level fixes, flag the projects carrying the most open
issues, and summarize compliance status. Five bundled skills tailor
each fix to the role asking, from contrast ratios and tokens for
designers to ARIA and focus order for front-end developers.

 NATIVE IS A FLOOR, NOT A CEILING (8 MINUTE READ) [7]

 AI mockups now hand everyone a polished look, but they copy the
picture of iOS rather than its behavior, often still drawing the
pre-iOS 26 design. Native components bring what an image can't: back
gestures, scalable text, built-in accessibility, and automatic updates
like Liquid Glass when apps recompile. Native sets the floor for
navigation, sheets, and controls, while the ceiling — typography,
illustration, and tone — belongs to the brand.

 WEB DESIGN IS TURNING INTO WEBMCP (12 MINUTE READ) [8]

 WebMCP allows websites to expose structured tools that AI agents can
call directly instead of navigating visual interfaces, fundamentally
changing how digital experiences are designed. In Shopify's tests,
agents completed all 60 checkout attempts through WebMCP in an average
of 10.3 seconds, compared with 56 successful attempts averaging 27.4
seconds through conventional browser interaction. As browsers and
platforms adopt the emerging standard, designers will increasingly
need to consider tool descriptions, permissions, error handling, and
machine-readable workflows alongside interfaces intended for humans.

 DESIGN WRITING: WORKING WITH AI AGENTS (7 MINUTE READ) [9]

 AI-generated interface copy often contains recognizable vocabulary,
unnecessarily complex language, and generic phrasing that can
undermine readability, trust, and brand consistency. Automattic's
design writing team developed two installable agent skills, one for
interface copy and another for voiceover scripts, combining explicit
writing guidance with automated linting and human review. The skills
encourage AI agents to ask clarifying questions instead of making
assumptions, helping teams produce clearer, more consistent language
without surrendering editorial judgment.

 BUILDING INTERFACES THAT UNDERSTAND WHAT WE WANT TO DO (33 MINUTE
READ) [14]

 Chrome is building a Decisions API that runs a decision model on the
user's own computer, free and private, picking only from options a
site defines. It lets an interface act on what people mean, turning a
typed hotel request into Beachfront, Quiet location, and Pet-friendly
filters. Still an experiment behind a Chrome flag, the API can be
paired with a cloud fallback like Gemini.

 LANDOR REVEALS SNOWFLAKE-INSPIRED VISUAL IDENTITY FOR CRYSTAL SKI (5
MINUTE READ) [15]

 Landor has rebranded TUI-owned Crystal Ski around the positioning
"Escape to higher ground," shifting its focus from practical ski
holidays toward the emotional appeal of mountain experiences. The
identity introduces the Crystal Lens, a graphic device inspired by the
sixfold symmetry of snowflakes and constructed from the Y in the
wordmark, which refracts photography to reveal different aspects of
alpine holidays. Crystalline letterforms, Season typography from
Display Foundry, and a mountain-inspired color palette create a
flexible visual system designed to attract a broader generation of
travelers.

 JOURNEY MAPPING 101 (7 MINUTE READ) [16]

 A journey map visualizes the process someone follows to accomplish a
goal, combining their actions, thoughts, and emotions into a timeline
that reveals the overall experience. Effective maps typically identify
a specific persona and scenario, organize the experience into phases,
and highlight opportunities for improvement, while variations such as
experience maps, service blueprints, and user-story maps serve related
but distinct purposes. Journey mapping helps teams develop a shared
understanding of customer needs, identify gaps between touchpoints,
and prioritize improvements based on the complete experience rather
than isolated interactions.
