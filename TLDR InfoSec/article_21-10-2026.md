ATTACKS & VULNERABILITIES

 HACKERS USE NEW ROMULUS CLICKFIX PLATFORM TO DELIVER LEGIONLOADER AND
OTHER MALWARE (2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 ROMULUS, a ClickFix delivery platform, was discovered using fake
landing pages to make users run malicious commands. It was found to
serve PowerShell staging scripts that deploy trojanized MSI installers
or padded encrypted archives containing Legion Loader. Researchers
found six IP addresses running the platform. One also hosted a
ClickFix script linked to malicious ChatGPT Custom GPTs and Legion
Loader, then retrieved further payloads.

 META MUSE EXPOSES USERS' PERSONAL AND BUSINESS SECRETS WHEN THEY
SHARE CONVERSATIONS (3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Researchers at Claroc found 6,800 Meta Muse share links through
passive DNS searches. The links included conversation titles in their
subdomains, making them searchable. Samples exposed executive-search
notes, revenue reviews, loan applications, bank-fraud details,
property records, and staff calendars. Meta paused the sharing feature
on October 20 while reviewing the report.

 BEYONDTRUST REMOTE SUPPORT 26.2.3 SUFFERS AUTH BYPASS CVE-2026-102764
(4 MINUTE READ) [9]

 BeyondTrust Remote Support versions 26.2.3 and earlier are affected
by CVE-2026-102764, a critical authentication bypass and arbitrary
file upload vulnerability with a CVSS score of 9.3. CVE-2026-102764
lets unauthenticated attackers perform file operations, escalate to
root, and access credential vaults. A suspected Chinese
state-sponsored espionage group, Armory Castle (UNC5221), has actively
exploited this zero-day flaw since late August to deploy custom
webshells such as BARECLAW for persistence and lateral movement.
BeyondTrust released security patches in October for self-hosted
customers, prompting CISA and the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security
to advise organizations to restrict external access to appliances and
conduct forensic reviews for signs of compromise.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 INSIDE CAIRN: OPEN WINDOWS INTO MASSIVE AI-DRIVEN CYBER OPERATIONS
(16 MINUTE READ) [10]

 NorForge found more than 20 publicly exposed Cairn panels used for
malicious intrusion work. One Chinese-language cluster ran over 1,100
attack projects against e-commerce and payment systems in the US,
Japan, Australia, and Canada, seeking unauthenticated RCE, file-upload
webshells, SQL injection, and authentication bypasses. Another cluster
targeted merchant payment, crypto, and pharmaceutical sites for card
and cryptocurrency theft. The panels exposed targets, credentials, API
keys, agent instructions, and successful compromises. Trufflehog found
hundreds of secrets, with 11% verified valid. NorForge is notifying
victims and seeking panel takedowns.

 PACKAGE MANAGER SANDBOXING (7 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Homebrew developer Andrew Nesbitt explains Homebrew 7.0's split fetch
and install phases as well as the Landlock and Seatbelt-based
sandboxes. Nesbitt then reviews the sandbox implementations, allowlist
implementations, and sandbox proposals from other package managers.
Some package managers like Go and Elm go a step further by fully
removing code execution.

 LAMBDA WEB FUNCTIONS: PUBLIC BY DEFAULT, AND ALREADY IN YOUR LAMBDA:*
GRANTS (5 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Lambda Web Functions are a new AWS feature that allow for running a
Node.js HTTP server behind a managed HTTPS domain. By default, the
functions run with no authentication, and role credentials are
included as environment variables, which could potentially be leaked
for the full duration of the execution environment. Existing roles
that grant lambda:* permissions have access to web functions by
default, existing SCPs may not cover them, and visibility tooling may
also miss functions, leading to the potential for shadow Web Functions
being created and going unnoticed.

MISCELLANEOUS

 A HACKER BROKE INTO JOHN DEERE AND SHARKNINJA WITH AN ARMY OF AI
AGENTS (2 MINUTE READ) [17]

 A hacker used autonomous agents on the open-source Cairn platform to
breach more than 100 sites, reportedly including John Deere,
SharkNinja, and Cummins. The attacker supplied intrusion tactics in
under 100 Chinese characters. The breaches took about 23 hours.
Norforge found over 20 likely malicious Cairn instances. John Deere
said no sensitive data was accessed. SharkNinja contained the
incident.

 DHS TO BAN DEEPSEEK FROM FEDERAL CIVILIAN AGENCIES (2 MINUTE READ)
[18]

 The Department of Homeland Security announced a new ban on the
procurement of products from DeepSeek. The directive cited national
security concerns stemming from the potential for sensitive data to be
stored or processed in China. Agencies will have 60 days to request
exceptions to the policy.

 OUR LATEST DEFENSES AGAINST EVOLVING WEB SCAMS (3 MINUTE READ) [19]

 Chrome's Enhanced Protection added ClickFix detection that scrubs PII
from copied commands, sends them to Safe Browsing for analysis, and
warns users when the verdict comes back dangerous. Google also moved
its Gemini scam detection from on-device to a server-side model that
scores page intent and brand impersonation, and it added a
lower-confidence "suspicious site" warning tier beneath the familiar
red interstitial. The company claimed Enhanced Protection users were
ten times safer than Standard Protection users, a figure drawn from
Google's own measurement.
