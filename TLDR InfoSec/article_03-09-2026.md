ATTACKS & VULNERABILITIES

 BGP HIJACK INFECTING NETWORKS CAUSED BY A COMEDY OF ERRORS THAT'S NOT
FUNNY AT ALL (4 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Attackers hijacked Softaculous' 162.55.80.0/24 prefix and redirected
Virtualizor update traffic to malicious servers. Hetzner's RPKI
settings accepted /24 routes, while a forged AS path matched its ROA,
so the attackers also obtained TLS certificates during the diversion.
Virtualizor did not cryptographically verify update packages, and
Softaculous cannot identify every affected server and urges customers
to investigate.

 MALICIOUS APACHE MODULES HIJACK BRAZILIAN GOVERNMENT SITE TRAFFIC TO
PUSH BETTING PAGES (3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Gambling Goblin installed malicious Apache modules on Brazilian
government and education servers. The modules reverse-proxy visitors
to fake Google Play, Microsoft Store, and Amazon pages promoting
betting sites. Operators deployed DownPro, AlphaAgent, oRAT, 3snake,
SSH brute-forcing, and reconnaissance tools. Administrators lack
published module hashes, filenames, or paths for direct Apache checks.


 UNITREE G1 HUMANOID ROBOT FLAWS ALLOW UNAUTHENTICATED ROOT RCE OVER
BLUETOOTH (3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 A security researcher discovered two independent flaws in Unitree G1
humanoid robots that could enable RCE on the devices. The first
vulnerability allows an attacker within BLE range to obtain an AES-128
key without requiring the device to pair. The researcher found that
the Unitree cloud API would decrypt the key without verifying
ownership or serial numbers, allowing the researcher to access
protected BLE operations, eventually allowing the attacker to exploit
a buffer overflow to achieve RCE. The second path involves a
path-traversal vulnerability in the robot's AI service, which enables
an attacker to upload a file to the bashrunner's whitelist directory,
enabling RCE.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 SHAI-HULUD: WHOEVER CONTROLS YOUR PACKAGE REGISTRY CONTROLS YOUR
PIPELINE (7 MINUTE READ) [9]

 The npm worm lineage has evolved to a point where standard supply
chain provenance mechanisms can no longer guarantee safety. During the
August 2026 ChainDrop campaign, threat actors compromised over 400
packages in under four hours by hijacking a maintainer's legitimate
signed release pipeline. The attackers utilized authorized
infrastructure, so every poisoned package version successfully carried
a perfectly valid SLSA attestation. To further evade traditional
domain blocklists, the campaign hosted its command and control
operations within an Ethereum smart contract. This attack builds upon
the previous Shai-Hulud worms, which have recently pivoted to
targeting authentication tokens for AI development tools like Claude,
Codex, Cursor, and Gemini. Security researchers warn that these
identical mechanics could easily compromise infrastructure-as-code
pipelines, proving that current attestation models verify origin
rather than actual code legitimacy. To defend against these pipeline
hijackings, organizations must enforce strict version pinning, utilize
curated registries, implement OIDC-scoped credentials, and apply
aggressive network egress restrictions.

 FINANCIALLY MOTIVATED THREAT ACTOR BREEZE COMET TARGETS BRAZIL (10
MINUTE READ) [10]

 Since 2024, BREEZE COMET has targeted Brazilian banks, payment firms,
retailers, exchanges, and fintech providers to make fraudulent Pix,
STR, and Boleto transfers. Initial access included password spraying,
fake IT-support calls, booby-trapped tax files, exposed JBoss servers,
and rogue devices on retail networks. The group steals mTLS
credentials, API keys, CI/CD secrets, and cloud tokens, then moves
through SMB and RDP. Its toolkit includes REALBREEZE, COBALTSPIN,
LIGHTPAINT, MILDFROST, KICKPLATE, and BOATBEAM. Mandiant recorded two
transaction waves within 24-48 hours after access to core financial
applications.

 CROWDSTRIKE 2026 THREAT HUNTING REPORT: EXPLOITATION WINDOW CLOSES AS
AI USE ACCELERATES (4 MINUTE READ) [14]

 CrowdStrike reports that 88% of observed vulnerabilities were
actively exploited within 48 hours of a public proof-of-concept
release between January and June. Advanced threat groups like VAULT
PANDA and GENESIS PANDA weaponized flaws in under 24 hours, while
UMBRAL BISON targeted Linux privilege escalation vulnerability
CVE-2026-31431 roughly 20 hours after disclosure. The report also
highlights a rapid shift in alternative attack vectors, noting a
fifteenfold increase in device code phishing alongside a massive
concentration of software registry threats targeting npm.

 FBI PROBES SERVICE SELLING 153M+ DRIVERS LICENSES (8 MINUTE READ)
[15]

 A dark web service named Nexus recently advertised over 153 million
scanned US and Canadian driver's licenses alongside millions of other
identification documents on a Russian cybercrime forum. By correlating
GMT timestamps embedded in the leaked image filenames with real-world
travel records, researchers traced the infrared and ultraviolet scans
back to a potential breach at the identity verification vendor
idscan.net. Following the discovery of this continuous exfiltration
campaign affecting major corporate clients, the FBI opened an official
inquiry, and the Nexus site immediately went offline.
