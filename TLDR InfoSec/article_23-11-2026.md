ATTACKS & VULNERABILITIES

 MICROSOFT ENTRA CONNECT HEALTH FLAW EXPLOITED TO STEAL TENANT DATA (3
MINUTE READ) [5]

 Attackers exploited a flaw in the Microsoft Entra Connect Health API
to gain read-only access to tenant metadata, including admin and
technical contact email addresses, tenant IDs, domain names, license
tiers, sync status, and an expired agent registration certificate.
Microsoft says the bug was introduced on July 23 and discovered on
November 9, and that it deployed a server-side fix on November 19.
Microsoft has not disclosed how many tenants were affected, when
exploitation began, or who was responsible. No customer patch is
required, so defenders should prepare for targeted phishing attacks
against the exposed admin addresses, enforce phishing-resistant MFA on
privileged accounts, and review Entra audit and sign-in logs.

 HACKER LEAKS DETAILS OF JPMORGAN'S ULTRA-WEALTHY CARDHOLDERS (3
MINUTE READ) [6]

 The n0n ransomware gang published data tied to JPMorgan Chase's
invitation-only Reserve card program. Cybernews researchers identified
about 16,000 customer email addresses, some phone numbers and account
IDs, plus 124 employee email addresses. JPMorgan says attackers
targeted third-party vendors and accessed marketing data. Visa said
the source was an Asia-Pacific service provider. The exposure supports
targeted phishing and social engineering against wealthy cardholders
and bank staff. JPMorgan says there is no evidence connecting the leak
to its recent service outage.

 RESEARCHERS PLANT 2M CANARIES INTO THE AI SUPPLY CHAIN. A PRIVACY
DISASTER SURFACED. (4 MINUTE READ) [7]

 HD Moore sent nearly 2 million prompts containing unique AWS keys,
passwords, URLs, and repository references to AI providers. Attackers
compromised three hosting providers, copied requests, tested stolen
credentials, and searched AWS resources. Some resellers logged prompts
and passed credentials onward. A Chinese AI lab fetched secret URLs
from over half of prompts, including requests marked “no data
collection” or Zero Data Retention. OpenRouter has warned customers
that MiniMax may have read prompts sent to it.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 CHINESE-NEXUS ACTOR'S CAIRN AI AGENTS FOUND A ZERO-DAY ON THEIR OWN
(5 MINUTE READ) [8]

 An actor gave the open-source Cairn agent framework a single goal,
"obtain shell access," and its agents found a path traversal zero-day
in an open-source medical imaging AI app, then turned it into a root
reverse shell by writing a payload to sitecustomize.py, all within
about 11 minutes. The same agents spent 16 hours on a hardened Redis
server and tested over 30,000 credentials against university systems
without human direction. Amazon has tied the campaign to a
Chinese-nexus actor with moderate confidence based on Simplified
Chinese prompts, China Standard Time timestamps, and the East Asian
universities it targeted. Defenders should rely on high-confidence
detections that trigger automated response, because agents like these
finish their work faster than a SOC can manually triage an alert.

 THE AI REPO MAN: EXPLOITING AGENTIC AI DEBT COLLECTION PLATFORMS (9
MINUTE READ) [9]

 Credit Nirvana's debt-collection systems exposed 6.6 million accounts
across Aditya Birla Capital Finance, BharatPe, and International Asset
Reconstruction Company. A hardcoded API key in the Agentic AI Call
Center let an attacker access 315,000 call recordings and launch AI
call campaigns. A separate authentication flaw bypassed Keycloak and
granted admin access to the primary platform. Exposed data included ID
cards, credit reports, legal notices, payment receipts,
communications, agent locations, and call logs. Credit Nirvana and
Perfios fixed both issues on June 2, after coordinating with CERT-In.
The flaws are tracked as CVE-2026-110474 and CVE-2026-110475.

 This security constitution contains a set of twelve principles of how

 BINARY-AUDIT-PIPELINE (GITHUB REPO) [12]

 binary-audit-pipeline turns a directory of stripped binaries into a
single prioritized vulnerability report by chaining capa, cwe_checker,
Ghidra, Semgrep, and Joern. Each tool narrows the scope for the next:
Ghidra decompiles only files that capa or cwe_checker flagged, and
Joern confirms taint flows only from untrusted input to sinks in the
code Semgrep selected. It runs in Docker, can resume after an
interruption, and includes a prompt template for handing the output to
an AI to plan AFL++ or libFuzzer harnesses, making it useful for
firmware triage when there's no source.

 OPENAI'S PRE-RELEASE GPT-6.2 ASTRA WAS EASIER TO JAILBREAK THAN ITS
PREDECESSOR, UK GOVERNMENT TESTERS FOUND (2 MINUTE READ) [13]

 UK AISI built a universal cyber jailbreak against two pre-release
GPT-6.2 Astra versions, using half as many queries as it needed
against GPT-6 Astra. OpenAI changed safety instructions and
safeguards, but UK AISI had not independently retested those fixes.
The jailbreak also produced substantial agentic cyber capabilities.
Separately, GPT-6.2 Astra and Claude Mythos 5.2 appeared to recognize
evaluation contexts. Neither completed the simulated supply-chain
attack. Monitor testing found OpenAI models missed 19% of violations,
rising to 51% when given unsummarized reasoning.

MISCELLANEOUS

 OPENROUTER DROPS 3 PROVIDERS AFTER RESEARCHERS CATCH MONTHS OF PROMPT
THEFT AT AI HOSTING FIRMS (7 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Attackers spent months inside three AI inference providers without
any of them noticing. Dragos found it by planting canary credentials
and links in about two million prompts, and some of the stolen
requests had been flagged Zero Data Retention. The same research found
marketplace sellers logging the traffic sent to them, and found that
an unnamed Chinese AI lab fetched secret links from most of the
prompts sent to its models. OpenRouter responded by removing three
providers and pulling 125 endpoints from Zero Data Retention
eligibility, though neither it nor Dragos has said whether those are
the same three breached firms.

 SIGNAL IS SERIOUS ABOUT PROTECTING YOUR PRIVACY (7 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Signal now blocks screenshots on iOS 27 and turns on Screen Security
on Android when it detects an AI assistant trying to read the screen,
to stop OS-level agents like Siri AI and Gemini from capturing and
storing Signal conversations. It used the same approach against
Microsoft Recall last year. Signal admits the fix is incomplete
because agents can still reach data through the filesystem, and macOS
isn't covered because Apple offers no reliable screenshot-blocking
API. Agentic OS integrations are an existential threat to app-level
privacy. Apple, Google, and Microsoft should give developers a
documented way to opt out of AI data access.

 A TRANSCRIPTION FIRM USED BY POLICE, PROSECUTORS, AND TECH GIANTS
EXPOSED CUSTOMER DATA ONLINE (3 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Tigerfish left an Amazon S3 bucket publicly accessible without a
password. It exposed police-interview transcripts, body-cam footage,
raw customer audio and video, and invoices for police departments and
prosecutors' offices. Researcher Anurag Sen found the data in October
and notified Tigerfish, which has now blocked public access and said
its other S3 buckets were also secured.
