ATTACKS & VULNERABILITIES

 ATTACKERS HAVE BEEN EXPLOITING CRITICAL ZIMBRA FLAW TO STEAL EMAILS
(2 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Attackers are exploiting CVE-2026-73570 in Zimbra Collaboration
Suite. A crafted email can trigger unauthenticated OS command
execution when zimbra-snmp and SNMP notifications are enabled, and
Microsoft observed web shells, reverse shells, privilege escalation,
remote-access tools, email backups, and credential collection.
Shadowserver found 274 compromised instances.

 REVENGE OF THE SD-WAN: EXPLORING AND EXPLOITING YET ANOTHER CRITICAL
CISCO SD-WAN VULNERABILITY (CVE-2026-76504) (6 MINUTE READ) [5]

 A single unauthenticated POST to /%6a_security_check mints an admin
session on Cisco SD-WAN vManage through CVE-2026-76504, a critical
authentication bypass Cisco disclosed on September 30 as an exploited
zero-day. Cisco's login module checked the raw request URI while
WildFly matched the decoded path, so the encoded request skipped the
password check and fell into a session-continuation branch that
trusted hardcoded internal usernames like viptela-reserved-cloudops.
With ~1,500 vManage instances internet-exposed per VulnCheck,
defenders should follow Cisco's advisory and hunt
serviceproxy-access.log and vmanage-server.log for encoded
j_security_check paths and viptela-reserved- usernames.

 FORTINET WARNS OF CRITICAL FORTIMAIL FLAW EXPLOITED IN ZERO-DAY
ATTACKS (3 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Attackers are exploiting CVE-2026-104286 as a zero-day, a CVSS 9.8
path traversal and NULL byte flaw in the FortiMail management
interface that lets unauthenticated HTTP requests write arbitrary
files and execute code. FortiMail 7.2.0 through 8.0.1 is affected, and
fixed builds 7.4.9, 7.6.7, and 8.0.2 are not yet available, so admins
should disable IBE or restrict the management interface to trusted
networks. Compromise indicators include an added
/data/etc/ld.so.preload and /data/lib/liblog.so, plus an archive
account named archive234 pointing to 79[.]141[.]169[.]187, and CISA
gave federal agencies until October 4 to triage and mitigate.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 SOLVING THE IDENTITY CRISIS FOR AI AGENTS (7 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Uber faced two issues with agentic identity: its current identity
model wasn't designed to account for agents acting on behalf of users,
and provenance wasn't properly carried across different agents in a
workflow. To address this, Uber implemented an identity system in
which workflows begin by requesting a workload ID from SPIRE. That ID
is then presented to a custom STS, which verifies in its agent
registry that the requested agent is allowed to run on that workflow
and returns a JWT with the relevant details to the workload. To
simplify adoption, Uber built a standardized Agent-to-Agent client
that provides an automatic, secure-by-default path.

 WHAT WE SHOULD DO INSTEAD OF TRUSTED CYBER ACCESS PROGRAMS AND OPEN
WEIGHT RESTRICTIONS (5 MINUTE READ) [8]

 It is pivotal to provide defenders with access to the most powerful,
cyber-capable models, whether open-weight or frontier models. Instead
of restricting access to these models, we should shift the paradigm to
focus more on restricting attackers' access. Frontier labs should
implement low-friction, cross-lab Know Your Customer (KYC) programs,
do more to detect and block attackers, and publish
time-to-detection-and-response metrics. Victims of AI cyberattacks
should also be encouraged to share more detailed information about the
attack and the attackers.

 BITGET HACKED VIA ZERO-DAY IN THIRD-PARTY SECURITY PRODUCTS (3 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 A zero-day in a third-party security product gave Bitget's attacker a
foothold on August 31, 25 days before the $387.5 million theft. The
attacker later planted a web shell and C2 on a second appliance and
pivoted to the production wallet job server, where a custom withdrawal
tool forged risk-control parameters and drained wallets in under three
hours. The vendors remain unnamed, and the North Korea attribution
comes only from Bitget's CEO, so the takeaway is to monitor security
appliances like production hosts and enforce withdrawal risk checks
outside the wallet host.

 GEMINI 4 ARGON: OUR NEXT ERA OF FRONTIER INTELLIGENCE (9 MINUTE READ)
[10]

 Google is releasing Argon first to trusted cyber defenders through
its Fairwind Program. The model can find, validate, and patch
vulnerabilities, including a healthcare flaw exposing patient data. It
scored 68% on CWE-bench v1 and found exposures across 20 programming
languages. Google is testing safeguards against prompt injection,
misuse, and unsafe agent actions before wider access.

 DROP (GITHUB REPO) [12]

 Drop allows users to create sandboxed environments that isolate
programs and agents while attempting to preserve as much of the
development environment (user, selected config files) as possible.

MISCELLANEOUS

 GOOGLE: AI IS CHANGING THE PACE AND PROFILE OF VULNERABILITY
DISCOVERY (4 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Monthly vulnerability disclosures doubled in 2026, reaching over
10,000 by midyear. High-risk flaws rose 167%, and exploited
vulnerabilities averaged 18 per month, up from 10.5 in 2025.
AI-discovered bugs more often enable remote code execution (50% vs
26%) and skew toward medium and low risk. Researchers saw rapid
weaponization of n-days, including a BeyondTrust flaw exploited within
four days of disclosure. AI-related CVEs also climbed, with 2,076
tracked since early 2025, though zero-days against AI infrastructure
remain unconfirmed.

 METAMASK SECURITY INCIDENT PROMPTS EXIT OF AFFECTED ETHEREUM
VALIDATORS (1 MINUTE READ) [14]

 MetaMask is responding to an ongoing security incident affecting part
of its infrastructure. The company is proactively exiting affected
validators in its non-custodial staking operations, coordinating with
clients and partners. Lido confirmed the exits are underway, with
final validators expected to be exited by October 7, likely incurring
foregone rewards and possible downtime penalties.

 DISRUPTING A COORDINATED MODEL-DISTILLATION CAMPAIGN (5 MINUTE READ)
[15]

 OpenAI detected an extraction campaign beginning July 1, with spikes
on July 24–25 involving 16,000 requests from more than 4,000 users.
Related activity affected over 15,000 users and was disrupted by July
28. Operators replayed encrypted reasoning and used cross-conversation
prompts, with OpenAI linking a core cluster to Moonshot AI personnel,
restricted accounts, closed replay paths, added output checks, and
shared indicators with partners.

 CHINA-NEXUS HACKERS COMPROMISE 350 SYSTEMS ACROSS ASIA WITH NEW
ANTINO BACKDOOR (4 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Antino, a new Rust-based Windows backdoor that runs C2 entirely
through Microsoft Graph API calls to attacker-controlled Outlook and
OneDrive, compromised roughly 350 endpoints across Asia in a
China-nexus espionage campaign that Cisco Talos tracks as UAT-11587.

 SIGNAL COMPLETES ROLLOUT OF SECURE CHAT BACKUPS ACROSS ALL PLATFORMS

 Signal 8.30 brought end-to-end encrypted on-device backups to iOS and
desktop, completing the secure backups rollout across all platforms
with a standardized format, though attackers have reportedly begun
targeting the recovery keys.
