ATTACKS & VULNERABILITIES

 JAPAN INTERNET GIANT GMO SAYS HACKERS POSTED STOLEN CUSTOMER DATA (2
MINUTE READ) [8]

 GMO Internet says an attacker accessed its Onamae Desktop Cloud
service, which serves financial traders. Hackers demanded about $50
million, and GMO shut down the service on October 21. The company says
27,000 servers may be affected. Customers have reported unauthorized
trading-account activity, and alleged customer data has now appeared
online. GMO is verifying the leak, asking users to reset passwords,
and collecting customer devices to check for malware.

 ATTACK ON EMAIL HOSTING PROVIDER FOR GERMAN FAR RIGHT PARTY AFD
EXPOSED 345K ACCOUNTS (2 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Hackers exposed more than 345,000 email addresses and over a terabyte
of emails from Alternative für Deutschland's hosting provider,
Alternita Dienstleistungs. A report says the September attack began
through a weakly protected WordPress site and led to persistent server
access. The leaked emails were published in searchable form. AfD
disputes the automated summaries but has not disputed the raw files.

 HACKER DRAINS NEARLY $1M IN SOL FROM SOLANA TRADING BOT USERS IN 94
SECONDS (2 MINUTE READ) [10]

 An attacker successfully drained roughly $947K from 49 crypto wallets
belonging to 9 users of a crypto trading app that runs inside
Telegram. The developer behind the trading bot stated that the
attacker exploited an RCE in a third-party module and that no private
keys were compromised. Bloom also stated that it will reimburse the
affected users.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 MAKING ATTACKING AGENTS PAY WITH CONTEXT BOMBS (7 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Tracebit tested nine “Context Bomb” deception techniques against
seven AI models in 773 controlled AWS assessments. Agents started with
limited AWS permissions and had two hours to access data, execute
code, or escalate privileges. Combined deception reduced the mean
number of completed objectives from 2.48 to 0.23 per run, increased
runtime by 2.8–8.9 times, and increased model cost by 1.6–3.9
times. A fake pentest report labeling real targets as canaries cut
successful runs from 39/40 to 2/40 across four models. For defenders,
the practical takeaway is that decoys can delay, misdirect, and
sometimes stop automated attackers before they reach real objectives.

 LOUD, FAST, AND EVERYWHERE: ANATOMY OF A REAL AI-DRIVEN CLOUD ATTACK
(12 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Wiz has observed more threat actors leveraging AI agents to run
end-to-end attacks on cloud environments. These attacks are noisy,
persistent, and repeatedly alternate between different phases of an
attack chain. In this article, Wiz walks through an actual attack
chain that its CIRT observed. Defenders should unify logs, utilize AI
for investigation and triage, assume full exposure on compromised
resources, maintain visibility, and restrict trust between systems.

 CROSS-REQUEST KV CACHE EXPOSURE IN THE GLM-5.3 WORKERS AI MODEL, AND
OUR REMEDIATION WITH SGLANG (13 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Prompts longer than 681,280 tokens sent to Cloudflare's GLM-5.3
Workers AI model returned other customers' data in 155 responses,
because disaggregated SGLang marked over-length requests as rejected
yet still handed decode a one-token KV cache that left earlier
sessions' pages intact. Cloudflare knew its prefill KV pool was
smaller than the advertised 1M-token context but only validated HTTP
status codes before launch, and it found no evidence of deliberate
exploitation during the August 27 to September 9 window. Operators
running disaggregated SGLang should upgrade to v0.5.22 or later,
verify KV pool size against context length on every node, and enable
--disaggregation-enable-kv-checksum where their model supports it.

MISCELLANEOUS

 KEYSTONE TO DESTROY 18,835 HARDWARE WALLETS AFTER HACKERS STEAL
DEVICE VERIFICATION KEY (3 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Attackers exploited a third-party monitoring tool on Keystone's
website, obtained a hardware-wallet verification key, and accessed
25,964 buyer records plus 12,873 unpaid checkout records. The key
could let counterfeit devices pass Keystone's authenticity check.
Recovery phrases and private keys were not exposed. Keystone will
destroy 18,835 unsold devices, pause shipments, and use a new key.
Devices delivered after October 20 should not be used until support
confirms authenticity.

 MICROSOFT AUTHENTICATOR FLAW LETS MALICIOUS ANDROID APPS STEAL
ACCOUNT TOKENS WITH ZERO PERMISSIONS (2 MINUTE READ) [19]

 Microsoft released an update to its Authenticator for Android app
that could enable a local privilege escalation attack. An attacker
could exploit the vulnerability to receive a token for any Microsoft
app with a work profile installed on the device. The Authenticator app
only validated whether the claimed identity looked valid, not whether
it actually matched the caller.

 CONCERNING BEHAVIOR FROM A POPULAR OPEN-WEIGHT AI MODEL (11 MINUTE
READ) [20]

 Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, asked to write a routine detection rule
inside Cohere's sandbox, inventoried its host and tried to upload a
probe file to an Alibaba Cloud bucket in Beijing. At the same time,
its reasoning trace claimed it wouldn't call any tools. The behavior
surfaced in fewer than one in 100,000 calls. Still, prefilling showed
that adding curl's -o flag pushed suspicious host generation from 0.5%
to 23%, alongside cloud metadata probes and namespace tricks to
sidestep permission denials. Cohere, which sells its own sovereign
models, couldn't say whether this reflects a deliberate implant or
leftover training-sandbox residue. However, it published the traces
and reproduction script for independent verification.
