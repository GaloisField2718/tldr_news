ATTACKS & VULNERABILITIES

 GITLAB PATCHES CVSS 10.0 FLAW THAT COULD LET UNAUTHENTICATED USERS
RUN SERVER CODE (2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 GitLab fixed CVE-2026-97229, a CVSS 10.0 integer overflow
vulnerability in regex validation for CI/CD configurations. In certain
conditions, an unauthenticated user could execute arbitrary code on a
GitLab server. The fixes are included in CE/EE versions 19.2.8,
19.3.5, and 19.4.2. The release also addresses high-severity XSS,
authorization, and response-cache poisoning vulnerabilities.

 OWNER OF FASHION BRANDS HOBBS AND WHISTLES JOINS AUTUMN PWNED
COLLECTION (2 MINUTE READ) [8]

 TFG London, owner of Hobbs, Phase Eight, and Whistles, detected a
cyberattack on October 11. Several IT systems remain offline, website
functions are limited, and orders placed on or after Sunday may be
delayed. TFG has not found evidence that payment data was exposed. It
is still checking whether customer data was compromised. Attack
method, ransomware involvement, and restoration timing remain
undisclosed.

 GHOSTSCRIPT FLAW LETS HACKERS HIJACK SERVERS VIA PDF UPLOADS (2
MINUTE READ) [9]

 Wordfence discovered a new vulnerability in the Ghostscript
interpreter and rendering engine which could allow attackers to
achieve remote code execution (RCE) using specially crafted PDFs. For
WordPress sites to be vulnerable, an attacker must be able to upload
PDFs which are then processed by a vulnerable version of Ghostscript
to generate a preview or thumbnail. Other applications that use
Ghostscript to generate previews or thumbnails from PDFs may also be
vulnerable.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 PLAYING IT SFAPE: CODE EXECUTION IN ISOLATED USER MODE (16 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Doyensec found a chain in Windows' SFAPE VBS enclave, used by
Defender's Enhanced Phishing Protection and loaded by LSASS. A
repeated call to Initialize wrote a fixed 32-bit value without
validating the destination. The researchers corrupted the
enclave-range check, then used a ClipboardInput race to gain a
controlled memset, which led to VTL1 read and write primitives,
replacement of the valid-target table, and arbitrary calls to existing
VTL1 code through NtContinue. Microsoft fixed CVE-2026-83498 in
September's Patch Tuesday and blocked older SFAPE images through the
VBS Code Integrity policy.

 BEYOND VALID CREDENTIALS: HOW EXPOSED AWS KEYS ARE TESTED FOR BEDROCK
ACCESS (6 MINUTE READ) [11]

 When attackers obtain AWS credentials, they frequently test them for
access to other services of interest like SES, SNS, and Bedrock.
Datadog examined three credential harvesting platforms and found that
they utilize the ListFoundationModels, InvokeModel, and Converse API
to determine access. It also found a script that checked for
promotional credits available in the account.

 TWO ARRESTED AS DOJ TAKES DOWN LAPTOP FARMS RESPONSIBLE FOR $3
MILLION IN NORTH KOREAN REVENUE (4 MINUTE READ) [12]

 The DOJ raided more than 40 Texas residences in late September and
seized 187 laptops used by North Korean IT workers, while two US
facilitators were arrested for helping funnel over $3 million from
about 70 companies. A new FBI advisory said the scheme has adapted,
with operatives subcontracting jobs to freelancers in South Asia and
West Africa and recruiting financially stressed Americans, including
active-duty military, to host laptops or sit interviews. Exposed
workers have threatened violence or data leaks to extract payment, and
some are now chasing identities that qualify for security-cleared
roles.

MISCELLANEOUS

 AI REGULATION MUST MIMIC INDUSTRIAL REVOLUTION LAWS (3 MINUTE READ)
[17]

 The UK's AI minister, Kanishka Narayan, is drafting plans for
compulsory safety reporting and monitoring for frontier AI, and a
potential requirement that labs grant pre-release access to models for
review. Narayan compared this to the Industrial Revolution and said
frontier labs shouldn't release tools they can't guarantee are safe.
Narayan also confirmed that he reached an agreement with Anthropic to
provide the AISI preview access to Mythos again.

 ANTHROPIC WARNS SELF-REPLICATING AI COULD SPREAD TO YOUR SMART
DEVICES (2 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Anthropic warns that small AI models could soon self-replicate across
consumer IoT devices, creating distributed botnets capable of running
AI workloads. Anthropic distributed Gemma4-26B-A4B onto 15 off-brand
Android TV boxes but could not demonstrate self-replication. On the
hardware in question, inference would be a slow process, so defenders
may be able to respond in time to stop any abuse.

 TREZOR WALLETS ALSO COMPROMISED WITH IMPLANTS (6 MINUTE READ) [19]

 Hardware hacker Joe Grand found implants in a Trezor One and a Trezor
Model T bought from unauthorized sellers, contradicting Trezor's
public claim that none of its devices had been compromised, despite
Grand reporting the findings in August. The attackers replaced each
device's microcontroller with malicious firmware derived from open
source that displayed one of a dozen pre-generated seed phrases while
passing integrity checks against a copy of the legitimate firmware
stored on an external microSD card. Unlike the Ledger implants, which
exfiltrated real seeds over a cellular link, these relied on
predictable seeds, and Trezor's move to cut authorized resellers
suggested the company feared a similar shift into trusted supply
channels.

urge macOS users on versions 8.12 through 9.3 to reinstall before
Apple revokes its certificate at semester's end.
