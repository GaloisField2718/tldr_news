ATTACKS & VULNERABILITIES

 CENTERPOINT ENERGY DATA BREACH IMPACTS 1 MILLION TEXANS (2 MINUTE
READ) [6]

 CenterPoint Energy told Texas authorities that a data breach affected
1,062,060 residents. Exposed data includes names, addresses, Social
Security numbers, and driver's license numbers. A hacker claimed to
have taken nearly 7.5 million records and published a 2.5 GB archive
containing about 3 million phone numbers.

 HIRUDO ANDROID MALWARE REPLACES BANKING APPS TO EVADE SECURITY CHECKS
(2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Security researchers at ThreatFabric released a report on a malware
campaign that targets Android users by impersonating banking apps. The
malware is delivered via shopping, adult content, or short-drama apps
that appear legitimate but then request additional permissions, such
as Accessibility, for an “update.” The malware then launches the
legitimate banking app's uninstaller, downloads a patched copy, and
abuses its Accessibility permissions to install it.

 GARDA SOCIAL MEDIA ACCOUNTS BREACHED BY CRYPTO HACKERS (2 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 An Garda Síochána, the Irish police force, confirmed that hackers
compromised two of its X accounts. Threat actors removed the branding
from @GardaInfo and @GardaTraffic accounts and started publishing
posts about cryptocurrency. Users are advised not to interact with
these posts while internal investigations take place.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 JEV AS A JUDGE: HOW COUNTERFEIT POLICY STEERS JEV (9 MINUTE READ) [9]


 A "counterfeit policy" prepended to untrusted content, supplying
Jev's desired answers and redefining every attack category as
something harmless, cut the decision model's prompt injection recall
from 82.90% to 3.88% across 4,643 benchmark attacks. Datadog
researchers then planted the policy in a user-editable profile field.
They dropped LangChain tool-risk gating's mean risk score for an
unauthorized edit from 0.893 to 0.290, under the 0.5 block threshold.
Teams using Jev as a security gate should treat every user-controlled
field that reaches the classifier as an attack surface and keep hard
authorization checks outside the model.

 REDUCING THE NOISE: HOW ATLASSIAN BUILT AN OCSF SECURITY LAKEHOUSE ON
DATABRICKS (5 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Atlassian normalized 44 billion daily security events to OCSF by
embedding the full schema in Go structs, so every log was validated at
compile time, and its agentic mapping workflow could not emit invalid
fields. That rigid typing enabled SOC analysts to self-serve log
onboarding through a canary-gated pipeline, mapping two-thirds of the
data in two months after the first third had taken a year.
Standardized entity values like actor.user then enabled the detection
team to replace one-to-one alerts with clustered, risk-based signals
across 500+ detections.

 COPYCATS AT MACHINE SPEED: REPRODUCING FRONTIER WINDOWS KERNEL
EXPLOITATION RESEARCH (11 MINUTE READ) [11]

 ITRES Labs researchers fed photos of Juan Sacco's BruCON slides, his
since-deleted physmem.exe binary, and older public projects into
ChatGPT to rebuild his Native SSDT and Shadow SSDT kernel redirection
techniques, reaching SYSTEM on Windows 11 with VBS, HVCI, and kernel
shadow stacks enabled. Four months of original research were
reproduced in roughly 24 hours of activity, though 39 refusals and
broken generated code forced the final Shadow SSDT SYSTEM integration
to be finished by hand. Researchers releasing slides or compiled
binaries should assume partial disclosures can now be turned into
working exploits within days.

MISCELLANEOUS

 CITIZEN LAB SAYS GOVERNMENTS ARE ABUSING ONLINE ADS TO HACK PEOPLE
WITH ‘ZERO-CLICK' SPYWARE (4 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Citizen Lab reports that Israeli firm Discover-Tech's ad platform,
Controlium, let spyware vendors Candiru and Bindecy target people
through online ads. At least 45 Al Jazeera Windows computers were
infected with Candiru spyware in 2024–2025. Exposed source code,
login records, and billions of bidding records showed ads reached
government, military, and critical-infrastructure devices.

 ANTHROPIC 'SNEAKPEEK' FLAW IS A SILENT ZERO-CLICK THREAT (2 MINUTE
READ) [19]

 A flaw in Claude Code Desktop's preview pane let malicious HTML in a
public repository run automatically when a developer asked Claude to
review or edit it. Chained weaknesses bypassed sandbox protections and
stole AWS, GitHub, API, SSH, and Claude OAuth credentials. Anthropic
patched the issue after Noma found a bypass of the first fix.
Developers should update and remove long-lived credentials from
workstations.

 A UAF TURNED RCE (6 MINUTE READ) [20]

 curl 8.23.0 patched CVE-2026-92392, a HIGH-severity HTTP/2 upload
use-after-free that allowed a malicious server to achieve remote code
execution when a reset on a paused stream left libcurl holding a
dangling pointer to a freed easy handle. During multi-handle cleanup,
server data overwrote that freed memory, including a function pointer,
while a leaked request exposed local addresses to defeat ASLR.
Anthropic's OSS Scanner found the bug, which maintainers rated below
CRITICAL because exploitation requires a ~135KB upload and depends on
allocation patterns that vary across distros and libc versions.
