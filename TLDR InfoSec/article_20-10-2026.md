ATTACKS & VULNERABILITIES

 CVE-2026-108082: SYMFONY REDOS. ONE REQUEST. SECONDS OF CPU (7 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 A crafted Cache-Control header triggers catastrophic regex
backtracking in Symfony's HeaderUtils::split(). Symfony parses it on
every request, before routing or authentication, and Laravel parses it
three times. One request can consume up to 12.7 seconds of CPU, and
four merged 8 KB headers can force timeouts. The flaw affects Symfony
4.1 through the October 2026 patches, including Laravel, Drupal,
PrestaShop, and Shopware.

 ONE COMPROMISED LOGIN EXPOSED MILLIONS OF CALIFORNIA STUDENTS'
RECORDS (3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 An unauthorized party used a compromised login from May 22 to
September 18 to copy California student files from an Educational
Testing Service SFTP server. The data covers current and former
students dating to 2006 and may include names, addresses, IDs,
disability status, accommodations, and test results. The breach did
not expose Social Security numbers, passwords, or financial details.
ETS disabled the account, took the site offline, and is building a
replacement system.

 2.76M PIECES OF CUSTOMERS' INFO AT HOLLYS LEAKED IN CYBERATTACK (2
MINUTE READ) [9]

 Korean coffee chain Hollys disclosed that it suffered a data breach
impacting 2.76M pieces of personal information. The breached data
includes dates of birth, phone numbers, addresses, and emails. The
data was contained in temporary files provided for a past membership
service overhaul.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 WEB ENVIRONMENT INTEGRITY VS. PRIVATE ACCESS TOKENS - THEY'RE THE
SAME THING! (5 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Web Environment Integrity (WEI) and Apple's Private Access Tokens
(PAT) both use signed browser or device attestations. A site can
request proof that a visitor uses a genuine, non-jailbroken iPhone
with Safari, then reject Firefox on Linux. WEI can additionally bind
an attestation to a specific operation. The two proposals differ
mainly in presentation: PAT is framed around fewer CAPTCHAs, while WEI
is framed around service-provider verification.

 PWNESCAPE: GUEST-TO-HOST KVM ESCAPE (CVE-2026-99999) (17 MINUTE READ)
[11]

 Researchers found a Linux networking flaw that lets an untrusted VM
guest alter IPv4 header bytes after the host kernel validates them.
Guest-controlled checksum metadata can change the IPv4 header length,
causing the kernel to copy 32 bytes beyond the packet's logical data
and transmit them as apparent IP options. In a controlled KVM lab, the
disclosure supported selected 64-byte host-kernel writes and execution
of a host-root helper. The upstream fix, commit 6785011f8b16,
validates checksum_start before checksum completion across five IPv4
consumers.

 MANAGING AGENTIC SECURITY AT SCALE (8 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Salesforce divides vulnerabilities into strategic and tactical
vulnerabilities to address the large amount of vulnerabilities that
are discovered by LLMs. Strategic vulnerabilities are vulnerabilities
due to a core, systemic issue where many vulnerabilities can be
resolved by fixing the core issue. Tactical vulnerabilities represent
exploit chains that chain together smaller severity issues for a
larger impact and require exploit verification in a staging
environment.

MISCELLANEOUS

 PLEX VULNERABILITIES BEING EXPLOITED IN THE WILD TO DROP A
CRYPTOMINER (4 MINUTE READ) [17]

 A honeypot running Plex Media Server 1.43.2 observed live
exploitation on October 17. Attackers chained CVE-2026-96652,
CVE-2026-96654, CVE-2026-96655, and CVE-2026-96656 to obtain remote
code execution, then downloaded a cryptominer and a proxy component.
The observed source IPs were 45.194.92[.]222, 91.92.40[.]202, and
176.65.139[.]228, and the payload host was 217.60.194[.]116.

 HOW A MASSIVE CHINESE CRIME RING HACKED STAPLES TO STEAL MILLIONS (3
MINUTE READ) [18]

 404 Media reports on a Chinese threat actor that compromised Staples
to issue over $5M worth of gift cards that were used to funnel goods
back to Chinese addresses. The actors found a way to access Staples'
Nexus service, which it believed was only exposed internally, and
performed heap dumps to hijack sessions of logged-in Staples customer
service representatives. The attackers used 4,630 gift cards totaling
around $425K.

 ANTHROPIC QUIETLY PITCHES ITS OWN AI SAFETY BILL TO LAWMAKERS (2
MINUTE READ) [19]

 Representatives from Anthropic have quietly pitched a draft AI safety
bill to lawmakers. Under the bill, the government could only block a
model's release or use if it poses a foreseeable, imminent risk of
more than $1B in damages, or of killing or seriously injuring more
than 50 people via the creation or release of a biological weapon, the
model evading its developers' controls, or a cyberattack. Independent
evaluators would register with the Department of Commerce and report
any such risk within 72 hours.
