 WE REPLACED REDIS WITH MYSQL FOR INVENTORY RESERVATIONS—AND IT
SCALED (2026) (13 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Shopify transitioned its inventory reservation system from Redis to
MySQL to improve reliability and scalability during peak sales
periods, particularly on high-traffic days like Black Friday. The
change involved adopting MySQL's SKIP LOCKED feature, allowing for
more efficient management of inventory reservations and improved ACID
compliance while reducing the potential for overselling or
underselling products.
