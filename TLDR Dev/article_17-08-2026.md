ARTICLES & TUTORIALS

 AUTO-RESEARCH WITH CODEX: HOW I ACHIEVED A 232X FASTER KERNEL OVER
BASELINE WITH CODEX IN GPU MODE'S QR_V2 PROBLEM (31 MINUTE READ) [7]

 In a recent auto-research contest focusing on GPU optimizations, an
impressive 232x speedup over the baseline was achieved in implementing
the batched square compact-Householder QR factorization problem, with
the author finishing 12th out of 183 participants. The project
involved using techniques like the blocked Householder algorithm,
continual kernel profiling, and iteration on ideas presented through
advanced AI models.

 MAXIMIZING THE VALUE OF YOUR CLAUDE CODE SESSIONS (5 MINUTE READ) [8]


 To maximize the efficiency of Claude Code sessions, users should
clear prior context when starting new tasks, set the model and effort
level early, and use @-mentions for files to reduce unnecessary token
usage. Reducing tokens isn't about using fewer overall but making sure
the tokens spent directly contribute to the task at hand, as more
tokens can accumulate when irrelevant context is included.

 MODELS ARE GETTING DUMBER ON PURPOSE (7 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Recent advancements in AI models show that while their reasoning
capabilities continue to improve per parameter, their factual recall
has notably declined, suggesting a deliberate trade-off where
knowledge is sacrificed for reasoning skills. This shift allows models
to operate effectively by relying on external information sources to
provide up-to-date facts, reducing issues like hallucination by
enabling the model to reference and verify information against live
data rather than fixed weights.

OPINIONS & ADVICE

 SOFTWARE ENGINEERING FUNDAMENTALS MATTER MORE THAN EVER (6 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Software engineering involves carefully navigating choices and
problem-solving, especially in the context of AI tools and agentic
engineering. While large models show potential in software
development, effective coding still relies heavily on human reasoning
and thoughtful design for maintainable and debuggable systems.

 DON'T BE A MEAT PROXY (2 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Using AI-generated responses as a middleman in communication can
detract from the value of conversations, as it leads to
misunderstanding and unnecessary complexity. Instead of only relaying
AI output, individuals should engage with the content, understand it,
and respond in their own words to add real value to discussions.

 WHY DOES OPUS 5 FEEL WORSE TO WORK WITH? (3 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Working with Opus 5 feels less effective compared to its predecessors
primarily because it requires more oversight and fails to seek
clarification in ambiguous situations. This issue comes from a focus
on developing self-improving AI systems that excel on benchmarks,
which often focus on bold assumptions over the necessary inquiry and
flexibility required in real-life tasks.

MISCELLANEOUS

 WHO ARE THE TOKEN BROKERS? (5 MINUTE READ) [17]

 “Token brokers" are individuals who purchase unused API credits
from startups to resell them. They have commercialized the token relay
market. This emerging market is characterized by various online
platforms offering discounts on credits for major cloud and inference
providers.

 THE TEMU-FICATION OF SOFTWARE, DIGITAL GOODS & SERVICES (19 MINUTE
READ) [18]

 The trend towards the "TEMU-fication" of software and digital goods
predicts a future where the majority of content will be produced
cheaply by AI, resulting in lower quality and a two-tier market, with
human-created products becoming a luxury niche. As mass-produced
digital content becomes the norm, it will be needed for consumers to
consciously choose between generated and authentic works.

 A FORTUITOUS DECADE AS AN INDIE SOFTWARE DEVELOPER (6 MINUTE READ)
[19]

 After quitting his job in 2016, an indie software developer faced
struggles for five years, culminating in a turning point with the
release of iOS 15, which revitalized his business and allowed him to
rebuild his savings. Despite facing ongoing challenges and not
replicating his initial success, he embraces a spontaneous approach to
his career, continuing to innovate and adapt without long-term plans.
