ARTICLES & TUTORIALS

 HOW CHATGPT'S AD STACK WORKS (12 MINUTE READ) [10]

 ChatGPT's ad system uses a post-trained LLM to orchestrate retrieval,
ranking, relevance checks, and auctions as user intent evolves across
conversations. Stateful ad sessions preserve context, while separate
models, services, and code keep ad selection outside ChatGPT's
answer-generation path.

 FRONT-END OBSERVABILITY AT SCALE WITH CLICKHOUSE (11 MINUTE READ)
[11]

 OpenReplay's largest customers ingest billions of events and roughly
6 TB daily on a single ClickHouse instance, with sub-second queries.
Moving behavioral analytics out of Postgres also replaced disruptive
retention cleanups with TTL rules and simplified data pipelines
through materialized views.

 COMPUTING BOUNDING BOXES (6 MINUTE READ) [12]

 tldraw transforms shape vertices into page coordinates, then takes
their minimum and maximum values to compute fast axis-aligned bounding
boxes. The walkthrough covers nested transforms and rotated selection
handles, with approximate bounds filtering candidates before more
expensive geometry checks.

OPINIONS & ADVICE

 WHO HOLDS THE OFF SWITCH? (24 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Five imagined coffee purchases in 2035 illustrate different models of
data control, from state surveillance and privacy regulation to
commercial data markets, public infrastructure, and biometric
identity. Privacy increasingly depends on who can stop collection. AI
assistants and paid privacy tiers will make that question more
pressing.

 THE WEB IS MORE THAN ENOUGH (6 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Modern browsers already offer cryptography, native-speed code,
graphics, and audio, while AI makes those capabilities easier for
individuals to use. Falling development and hosting costs could expand
personal software creation and challenge app-store economics.

 ASKING LLM FOR CONFIDENCE (8 MINUTE READ) [15]

 An LLM's stated confidence is generated text, so a precise-looking
number should not be treated as a calibrated measure of correctness.
The proposed alternative derives scores from observable evidence and
historical outcomes, then checks calibration regularly on the same
kinds of tasks.

 EVALUATING AGENTS ON HIGHLY SUBJECTIVE TASKS (20 MINUTE READ) [19]

 Reliable evaluation of open-ended agent tasks combines calibrated
human judgments, automated judges or rubrics, and production
monitoring. Power analysis, confidence intervals, and repeated runs
help distinguish improvements from noise, while realistic, diverse
tasks, and production feedback keep benchmarks useful.

 INSIDE OPENAI'S JALAPEÑO AI CHIP (18 MINUTE READ) [20]

 OpenAI's Jalapeño inference chip links 64 dedicated cores to
individual HBM memory banks to reduce data movement and improve
utilization. This interview explains the tradeoff in memory-management
complexity, the choice to keep prefill and decode on fungible
hardware, and how AI-assisted optimization accelerated chip design and
kernel development.

 WHY HARDWARE SPECIALIZATION IS BACK (11 MINUTE READ) [21]

 Slower transistor scaling, memory bottlenecks, and power limits are
renewing the case for specialized AI inference hardware. Matching
different inference phases to different accelerators can improve
performance per watt while adding deployment and software complexity
and leaving room for GPUs to absorb successful ideas.
