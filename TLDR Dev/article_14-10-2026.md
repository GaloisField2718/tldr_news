ARTICLES & TUTORIALS

 REDESIGNING SOURCE CONTROL AT ANTHROPIC (14 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Anthropic replaced Git with Scion after daily merges grew from
roughly 2,500 to more than 30,000. The centralized system uses
server-side commits, sparse checkouts, and layered caches, reducing
its source control infrastructure's CPU and memory footprint by 200
times and freeing 25% of CI CPU capacity.

 REWINDING THE EXPONENTIAL: HOW WE KEEP HARDWARE USAGE DOWN (12 MINUTE
READ) [6]

 Cloudflare's data pipeline handles roughly two billion events per
second while hardware capacity grows more slowly than traffic.
Product-specific schemas, zstd compression, later replication, earlier
sampling, and aggregation have collectively deferred the equivalent of
two and a half years of hardware expansion.

 A YEAR OF FAST APPLY: THE PATH TO 10K TOKENS PER SECOND (13 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 Relace explains how it built a small model that applies code edits at
a reported 10,000 tokens per second using high-quality merge data,
LoRA fine-tuning, FP8 quantization, and speculative decoding. Its
training pipeline filtered 200,000 candidate examples down to about
145,000, using a judge calibrated against 500 manually reviewed
merges. Relace Apply 3 supports a 256,000-token context and multiple
diff formats, although improving frontier-model edit accuracy could
eventually reduce the need for specialized apply models.

 Programming for curiosity and enjoyment can restore motivation

exploration, and documenting the process, with permission to switch
projects or stop when they stop being fun.

 OPENAI ENGINEER ARRESTED OVER ALLEGED CONFERENCE ROOM BUGGING (5
MINUTE READ) [14]

 Federal prosecutors charged an OpenAI engineer with using or
attempting to use a device to intercept conversations after a
concealed recorder was found in an executive conference room. OpenAI
says it referred the discovery to the FBI and has found no indication
that model weights, source code, or customer data were improperly
accessed or taken.

 ARE HARNESSES WORTH THE COST? (20 MINUTE READ) [15]

 In ten runs on one Java API service, a behavioral harness raised
GPT-6 Luna's acceptance rate from three successes to ten, while GPT-6
Sol passed all ten runs with or without it. Harnessed Luna cost about
33% less per run than unharnessed Sol, but those estimates exclude
harness maintenance and the unusually detailed brief limits how
broadly the results apply.

 THE “ALIEN” ECONOMICS OF AI MONETIZATION (6 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Two-thirds of the Forbes AI 50 companies use some form of usage-based
pricing as variable AI costs push businesses toward hybrid
subscriptions, credits, and outcome charges. Examples from AI
companies show why spending controls, flexible packaging, measurable
customer value, and pricing pages readable by both people and agents
are becoming part of the product.
