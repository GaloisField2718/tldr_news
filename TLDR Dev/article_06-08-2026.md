ARTICLES & TUTORIALS

 BUILDING AN ADVANCED AGENTIC HARNESS (19 MINUTE READ) [6]

 An advanced agentic harness for production-sized agents is proposed
here, emphasizing the need for well-structured operations through
components like typed tools, directed acyclic graphs (DAGs), tiered
memory, and verification hierarchies to create a reliable system
capable of planning, acting, recovering, and self-evaluating. This
architecture uses the composition of small, testable primitives to
improve efficiency and performance while making sure the system
remains extensible and adaptable.

 THE SECURE WAY TO RELEASE AN NPM PACKAGE IN 2026 (25 MINUTE READ) [7]


 The increasing threat of supply chain attacks targeting npm packages
requires heightened security measures for developers. Implementing
strategies such as Trusted Publishing, npm Provenance, and Staged
Publishing can reduce vulnerabilities while making sure that only
approved releases are published.

OPINIONS & ADVICE

 SHOULD YOU SELF-HOST INFERENCE? (14 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Self-hosting AI inference is good primarily when processing over two
million tokens daily or when data cannot leave the organization's
network. For most companies, a hybrid approach combining local and API
models offers the best balance of cost and control.

 THE VALLEY OF WEBHOOKS (12 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Building systems that rely on webhooks for data synchronization
consistently proves problematic due to their inherent nature as
notifications rather than reliable data delivery methods. A proposed
alternative is a designated change log that would eliminate issues
like data drift and the need for complex mitigation strategies by
allowing consumers to request updates directly from providers.

LAUNCHES & TOOLS

 CLOUDFLARE OS: AN OPEN PLATFORM FOR AGENTS, APPS, AND WORK (13 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Cloudflare OS is an open-source platform designed to provide
organizations with customized agent workspaces that helps with
collaboration, task automation, and app development based on the
company's internal knowledge and context. The platform incorporates a
security framework to ensure safe access to internal data and
resources, allowing users to create and share applications while
managing access and costs effectively.

 INTRODUCING HARK HANDOFF (8 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Hark Handoff is a computer use agent designed to automate online
tasks by navigating the internet as a human would, freeing users from
mundane online activities. The agent excels in handling end-to-end
tasks such as ordering food, shopping, making reservations, and
conducting research.

 PRIME AGENT: A SELF-IMPROVING RLM AGENT (20 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Prime Agent is a self-improving coding harness that uses a Recursive
Language Model and Continual Harness for better context management,
multi-agent communication, and long-term evaluation, positioned as an
effective tool for coding assistance and research while maintaining
open-source accessibility.

 WHY THE LEGENDARY ERDŐS PROBLEMS ARE FALLING TO AI (21 MINUTE READ)
[14]

 Recent advancements in AI have led to breakthroughs in solving
classical mathematical problems proposed by Paul Erdős, regarding the
use of unique mathematical techniques previously unexplored in the
context of these challenges. As AI tools demonstrate increasing
capabilities, they are reshaping the landscape of mathematical
inquiry, attracting attention from both professional mathematicians
and enthusiasts.
