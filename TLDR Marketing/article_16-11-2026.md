NEWS & TRENDS

 THE DEATH OF OWNED MARKETING (6 MINUTE READ) [6]

 AI agents are changing how buyers discover and evaluate brands. Brand
perception increasingly forms through third-party sources like
customer reviews and online communities rather than company websites.
Positioning needs to stay consistent across these sources, while
original research should offer unique insights that earn independent
citations. Brands should also test how AI tools recommend their
products. As agents take on more of the buying process, pricing and
checkout need to be accessible without human navigation.

 THE ONLY WAY TO GO IS DOWN (3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Americans spend more than 13 hours a day consuming media, but only 25
hours a week receive their full attention. As media consumption grows,
focused attention is shrinking. Social media and in-person
entertainment are gaining a larger share of that attention. These
channels have something in common. They encourage shared interests and
community connection rather than passive consumption. The trend points
to a growing divide between content that fills people's time and
experiences that earn their attention.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 6 TIPS ON CREATOR MARKETING (2 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Gamma's creator marketing drove 32% of signups as the company scaled
to $100M ARR. Its growth lead recommends measuring results across
creator cohorts since individual post performance is highly
unpredictable. Allocate 70% of budget to lower-cost creators and use
platform-specific agencies. Track attribution through onboarding
surveys rather than links. Refresh creative to prevent audience
fatigue and combine professional creators and existing customers to
balance reach.

 SHOW ONLINE WHAT SHOPPERS CAN'T TOUCH (2 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Product images can recreate the in-store experience by showing
shoppers textures and details they cannot experience online. Backdrop
and Fireclay Tile use close-up photography and directional lighting to
highlight surface details that flat color swatches miss. Lush
Cosmetics shows bath bombs dissolving in water and scrubs spread out
to demonstrate their consistency. Feature texture and product details
early in the gallery, then use in-action images to show how products
look and behave when used.

 HOW TO TAKE YOUR BEST HOOK TO ANOTHER COUNTRY (15 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Subtitled hooks rarely travel, since they run on local jokes and
references that translation strips out. Expansion works best one
country at a time, starting where the app already ranks without any
content. Some hooks only need a local store or city swapped in, while
an in-joke that lands in one language needs a full rewrite. Local
creators then film in their own dialect, with the app kept out of the
first 2 seconds of the video.

 HOW TO BUILD AN AI CUSTOMER RESEARCH AGENT IN CLAUDE CODE (59 MINUTE
VIDEO) [13]

 This podcast episode explains how to build an AI customer research
agent in Claude Code to uncover why people abandon sign-up or cancel.
The agent emails customers at key drop-off points using customer data
from a CSV and custom instructions. It asks one question at a time,
then categorizes replies and connects feedback to purchase behavior.
In one example, Ancestry found that shipping concerns were a bigger
conversion barrier than subscription fees. An LLM judge checks that
replies are grounded in company documentation before sending.

 HOW I BUILT AN AI MARKETING TEAM WITH GROK BOT WITHOUT WRITING CODE
(3 MINUTE READ) [14]

 A solo founder uses specialized AI bots to run marketing tasks on a
schedule. Each bot has one defined role and follows a shared rules
file with factual checks and clear limits. The marketing bot drafts
content but requires human approval before anything is published. A
weekly report adds lessons and updates instructions so future jobs
improve. Batching content into a 21-day calendar reduces daily
planning, while a separate bot checks actual outputs and flags stalled
work.

 THE DUTCH BROADCASTER THAT STOPPED TRACKING ITS VIEWERS AND MADE MORE
MONEY (8 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Personalization doesn't always improve ad performance, and
third-party tracking may add less value than the industry claims.
Dutch broadcaster NPO eliminated third-party cookies and sold ads
based on content and context instead, increasing ad revenue by 61%.
Research found tracking added only 4% to publisher revenue. A travel
site experiment found generic brand ads outperformed retargeting ads
featuring hotels shoppers had viewed. Personalized ads performed
better after shoppers visited a review site and narrowed their
choices.
