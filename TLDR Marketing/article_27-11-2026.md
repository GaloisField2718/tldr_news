NEWS & TRENDS

 NON-BRANDED TRAFFIC IS DECLINING. BRANDED TRAFFIC IS COMING DOWN WITH
IT (7 MINUTE READ) [5]

 B2B organic search is becoming more reliant on branded traffic, but
that does not mean branded demand is growing. Across 34 B2B websites,
non-branded clicks fell 35% year over year, while branded clicks fell
19%, making branded traffic a larger share of a shrinking organic
channel. Generic informational content is becoming less reliable as a
traffic source because AI search can answer many queries without
sending users to websites. B2B marketers should invest more in product
pages, comparisons, pricing, customer proof, and other high-intent
content while building brand visibility across reviews, communities,
and third-party sources. Organic performance also needs broader
measurement beyond clicks, including AI visibility, brand
recommendations, buyer consideration, and revenue.

 GOOGLE'S ADVICE FOR AI OVERVIEW CITATIONS IS ORDINARY SEO (3 MINUTE
READ) [6]

 Google says optimizing for AI Overviews and AI Mode is still
traditional SEO, relying on indexed pages with accurate and up-to-date
content rather than tactics like rewriting for AI or adding llms.txt
files. However, ranking highly in organic search doesn't guarantee AI
citations. Ahrefs found only 37.1% of cited pages ranked in Google's
organic top 10. SEO teams need to track which pages AI answers
actually reference.

 EDITING WORKFLOW FOR 94 SEM LANDING PAGES (2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 AI was used to rewrite 94 paid search landing pages while keeping
quality under human control. The work started with writing rules and
client-approved messaging to guide the drafts. AI then tailored each
page to its target search intent and campaign goals. Every draft went
through source-based fact-checking and a 10-role review panel before
the original and revised versions were compared by hand.

 HOW BRANDS ARE WINNING WITH BOOMERS (4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Brands targeting boomers are finding growth by tailoring their
creative, media mix, and purchasing experience to an underserved
audience rather than trying to appeal to younger consumers. Skechers
markets hands free shoes through familiar personalities like Martha
Stewart, while Boarderie uses calmer Meta ads focused on sending gifts
to distant family members after discovering women 65 and older were
its largest buyer group. Laura Geller brings QVC-style live shopping
to TikTok Shop through 18 to 20 hours of weekly livestreams and more
than 20,000 affiliates. Frøya Organics concentrates its skincare
advertising on Facebook and Instagram rather than chasing TikTok
trends.

 THE UX BEHIND A NOTIFICATION DOT (2 MINUTE READ) [9]

 A subtle notification badge can encourage account creation more
effectively than an immediate pop-up because it sparks curiosity
without interrupting browsing. The badge works best when it offers a
low-commitment reason to click, such as order tracking, saved
preferences, or faster checkout based on the visitor's activity.
However, sending visitors to a generic login prompt undermines the
experience by asking for an account before they understand its value.
Test three approaches: no notification, an entrance pop-up, and a
persistent badge tied to visitor activity. Measure login starts,
account completions, add-to-cart rates, and purchases to see which
approach builds customer relationships without hurting conversions.

 AI SENTIMENT ANALYSIS FOR BRANDS: HOW TO TRACK AND IMPROVE WHAT AI
SAYS ABOUT YOU (14 MINUTE READ) [12]

 AI sentiment analysis shows how AI assistants describe your brand,
the criticisms buyers encounter, and the strengths they associate with
competitors. Track both provoked prompts that test known concerns and
unprovoked prompts that reveal opinions AI introduces on its own.
Compare sentiment across competitors, products, platforms, and topics,
then investigate the cited sources to determine whether negative
claims reflect outdated information, factual errors, or genuine
product weaknesses. Use those findings to update website content,
request corrections from publishers, address product problems, and
develop marketing assets such as comparison pages, case studies, ads,
landing pages, and sales emails.

 ORGANIC SEPHORA SALE HAULS SHOW WHETHER BEAUTY MARKETING IS WORKING
(5 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Repeated creator recommendations can have more impact than a single
viral post. AI tools can track which brands and products appear in
social videos even when they aren't tagged. Product-level mentions
reveal which items drive interest, while sentiment analysis helps
distinguish genuine recommendations from negative feedback. Brands can
also use these insights to understand differences in shopping
preferences across age groups.

 TOP 5 REASONS YOUR CONTENT IS FAILING IN 2026 (3 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Most failing B2B content tries to appeal to everyone and never asks
for the sale. Teams skip choosing a narrow audience and positioning,
so posts end up as generic advice readers can already get from AI
tools. They publish without a repurposing system, which leads to
stop-and-go posting that most abandon within weeks. They also hide
their offer and skip outreach to people who are already engaged, which
can lose half of potential deals.
