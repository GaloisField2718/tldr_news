 META SETTLES SOCIAL MEDIA ADDICTION CASE WITH CALIFORNIA, OTHER
STATES FOR $16.7 BILLION (3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Meta's settlement doesn't just bind Meta. States are set to keep 70%
of the $16.7 billion payout regardless, but the remaining 30% only
unlocks if Google's YouTube and TikTok adopt the same kind of daily
time limits, age-assurance checks, and night mode Meta is now required
to build. That structure effectively uses Meta's settlement to
pressure its rivals into matching child-safety changes without suing
them directly.

 THE TURBULENT AI ERA IS HERE. THE CHOICES WE MAKE NOW ARE CRITICAL
(20 MINUTE READ) [9]

 AI will reshape work faster than previous technological shifts,
creating huge opportunities while putting millions of jobs at risk.
The priority should be making sure its benefits reach everyone, not
just those with wealth and access. Leaders should build stronger
social safety nets and global AI policies.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 WHEN AI ANSWER ENGINES QUOTE A PERSON, THAT PERSON IS ON LINKEDIN 74%
OF THE TIME (4 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Individual employees, not company pages, are what AI answer engines
cite when they quote a named opinion. Named posts made up 32.8% of
social citations across an audit of 63 B2B companies, compared with
just 6.7% for company pages, and the person quoted had published on
LinkedIn 74% of the time. A model needs a name and title to attach to
a claim, something a brand page speaking in aggregate can't offer the
same way.

 THE 5 MOST EFFECTIVE LINKEDIN ADS TACTICS IN Q3 2026 (6 MINUTE READ)
[11]

 Pair LinkedIn Audience Network with highly targeted third-party
audiences to expand reach, turn customer wins into ungated case study
document ads, and gate high-value offers when ungated content falls
flat. Thought leader ads with preview links can drive more inbound,
while branded single-image TLAs can combine strong engagement with
brand building.

 RETHINK YOUR PRESS RELEASE LINKS (1 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Link the first mention of your brand in a press release to the most
relevant page for the announcement, not the homepage. This gives
search engines and AI systems a stronger signal about what your brand
should be associated with. Keep the homepage link in the boilerplate
and make every other link serve a clear purpose.

 THE 4 ROLES EVERY MODERN MEASUREMENT FUNCTION NEEDS AND ALMOST NO
COMPANY HAS STAFFED ALL 4 (6 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Modern marketing measurement needs four distinct roles: measurement
scientist, experimentation lead, finance translator, and decision
architect. The scientist builds trustworthy models, the
experimentation lead produces causal evidence, and the finance
translator reconciles modeled impact with financial results so finance
can trust the numbers. The decision architect connects those findings
to budget processes by defining decision rights, timing, and override
protocols. Combining roles may work temporarily, but understaffing
usually leaves the relationship and decision work undone and turns
measurement back into a modeling exercise.

 BEFORE DECLARING “_ _ _ IS DEAD”, CONSIDER THAT YOU MAY JUST BE
BORING (3 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Marketers and creators often mistake their own feeds for market
research, declaring trends “dead” when they're really just tired
of seeing them. Algorithmic feeds create highly personalized views of
culture, so repeated exposure can make a format feel saturated even
when other audiences are still discovering it. Before writing off a
trend, look beyond your own feed and separate personal fatigue from
actual audience behavior.

 THE GREAT META MIGRATION (6 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Meta's ad playbook is spreading well beyond Meta, with alumni now
running ad product and sales at OpenAI, Amazon, TikTok, and Roku, all
chasing the same self-serve formula that took Meta's own ad business
past a $1 trillion market cap. Turnover has been fueled by real
turmoil inside Meta, including rolling layoffs and morale issues
dating back to 2018. The strategy isn't foolproof, since Pinterest
brought over a wave of Facebook talent in its early days and it still
couldn't replicate Facebook's growth curve.

 BOOTS' REACTIVE MARKETING STRATEGY CAPITALIZES ON INFLUENCER'S IDEA
TO GIVE BACK TO ARIANA GRANDE FANS (3 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Boots turned a creator's TikTok idea into a $10K Ariana Grande
concert activation within hours, inviting 22 fans to the show. The
move generated more than 793K views across related creator content and
sparked positive brand sentiment. The activation shows the value of
giving social teams freedom to act quickly when relevant opportunities
emerge. It also highlights growing audience fatigue with brands taking
influencers to high-demand events instead of genuine fans.
