 AI SEARCH ACTIVITY IS NEARLY INVISIBLE IN GOOGLE ADS SEARCH TERMS
REPORTS: STUDY (4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 An analysis of 400+ Google Ads accounts found no significant increase
in longer search queries or conversions despite Google's claim that AI
Mode queries are three times longer than traditional searches. The
findings were consistent across the US, UK, and France, suggesting
that AI Overviews and AI Mode activity remains difficult to identify
in standard search terms reports. Google may omit low-volume queries
or replace actual prompts with approximations of user intent, limiting
advertisers' visibility into the searches triggering their ads. As
Google reduces keyword-level reporting, paid search is becoming more
like paid social, making it harder to understand exactly which queries
generate ad impressions and conversions.

 MARKETERS ARE BURNED OUT - AND NO ONE GIVES AF (5 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Leadership treated AI as a shortcut, so output expectations rose as
headcount fell. Single marketing roles now cover brand, paid media,
SEO, email, and even front-end design, often at junior salaries. Fear
of layoffs pushes people to say yes to everything, and the reward is
more responsibility rather than more pay. Burnout reflects that
structure more than a bad fit for the field, so the fix is sharing
credit with sales and measuring success beyond short-term ROI.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 STOP PICKING B2B INFLUENCERS BY FOLLOWER COUNT (2 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Prioritize creators trusted by their target buyers over those with
the largest followings. Find them by checking which creators 10
target-account employees engage with, then vet their comments for
actual buyers. Sponsor strong posts as LinkedIn Thought Leader Ads and
test Brand Awareness for cold audiences versus Engagement for warm
ones. Budget to reach 50% of your target audience twice. Calculate
spend using audience size × cost per 1,000 reached/1,000.

 WHAT TO PUT IN YOUR MEDIA KIT (2 MINUTE READ) [11]

 A B2B creator media kit should fit on one page and make it easy for
brands to justify a partnership. Include a one-line description of
your content and audience. Add 90-day performance metrics with
analytics screenshots and audience demographics by job title and
industry. Link to relevant posts that showcase potential product
integrations. Include simple starting rates for posts and videos, plus
contact links for all platforms.

 HOW TO TALK TO EVERY ONE OF YOUR CUSTOMERS WITH AI (28 MINUTE READ)
[12]

 Use AI to automate personalized email conversations with customers at
key points in the customer journey, including incomplete sign-ups,
trial abandonment, reduced product usage, and cancellations. Connect
an AI agent to customer data from a CRM or data warehouse, then use
lifecycle triggers to send relevant, plain-text emails that ask one
simple question and let customers guide the conversation through
follow-ups. Configure the agent to respond quickly and use evaluation
loops to check responses against company documentation, brand voice,
and predefined guardrails before sending. These campaigns can generate
reply rates of 20% to 40% while uncovering customer objections,
identifying product issues, and recovering potential conversions.

 YAHOO'S ECONOMISTS RAN 25 AD EXPERIMENTS AND COULD NOT TELL PROFIT
FROM LOSS (7 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Yahoo's research shows that most advertising lift tests lack the
statistical power to distinguish profitable campaigns from
unprofitable ones, even when they reach millions of people. Its 25
experiments found that the median confidence interval for ROI exceeded
100 percentage points, making it difficult to separate real impact
from normal sales fluctuations. Instead of running many small tests,
allocate testing budgets to the two or three channels that account for
most of your spend, and run larger, longer, repeated experiments.
Before launching a test, define its minimum detectable effect and
report the confidence interval alongside the estimated lift. Treat
results that cannot reliably detect meaningful returns as directional
signals, not proof that a channel works.

 INSIDE CLAY'S GTM MACHINE (9 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Clay automates almost every step of its sales process except the
conversations themselves, with a person editing and approving each
draft before it sends. Accounts get a 0 to 100 fit score built partly
from custom research, such as whether 10%+ of headcount works in
sales. That score feeds a weekly list of each rep's 5 priority
accounts. Follow-up emails are written in each rep's own voice,
modeled on what they've sent and said on calls.
