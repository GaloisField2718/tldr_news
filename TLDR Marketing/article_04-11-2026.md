 AI TRAFFIC DRIVES 83% MORE REVENUE PER VISIT (2 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Adobe found that AI-referred traffic generated 83% more revenue per
visit and converted 90% better than non-AI traffic in September. AI
visitors also spent 73% more time on site and added products to carts
53% more often. However, the average retail homepage scored just 63%
on Adobe's AI readability measure. Product specifications, setup
guides, and customer service pages were easier for AI to understand.

 COMPANIES MAY NEED TO RESTRICT AI USE TO MANAGE COSTS (3 MINUTE READ)
[9]

 OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, and Meta are spending billions on AI
development but struggling to turn their investments into profits.
OpenAI reported more than $20B in operating losses in the first half
of 2026, while Anthropic's infrastructure costs have surged 329% year
over year. xAI has already restricted Grok access because of high
operating costs. AI companies may introduce tighter usage limits to
control spending as pressure to deliver returns grows.

 HOW TEXT OVERLAYS IN SOCIAL MEDIA IMAGES AFFECT ENGAGEMENT (2 MINUTE
READ) [16]

 Large or centered text overlays lower engagement when the image
underneath is dynamic. A busy image already draws the eye, so big
central text creates a competing focal point that makes the post look
disorganized. Static images can carry larger, centered text, which
guides attention instead of fighting for it.

 AUDIENCE ANALYSIS FOR CRM AND EMAIL: HOW TO UNDERSTAND PEOPLE WELL
ENOUGH TO SEND THE RIGHT MESSAGE AT THE RIGHT TIME (8 MINUTE READ)
[17]

 Effective CRM marketing starts with understanding what people need
when you contact them, not just who they are or what triggered an
email. Track opt-in type and relationship stage in your CRM to
distinguish intentional subscribers from people who signed up for a
download or were added to a list. Analyze customer motivations and
emotions around key triggers, collect the language they use to
describe their needs, and review their email history to spot
irrelevant or repetitive messages. Turn these insights into CRM
properties and workflow rules that inform messaging at every stage of
the relationship. Measure success through engagement by opt-in type,
conversions by intent level, and fewer emails per conversion.
