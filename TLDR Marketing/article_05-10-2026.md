 IS 3RD PARTY DATA THE BIGGEST LEVER IN GEO? OUR DATA SAYS NO (2
MINUTE READ) [5]

 3rd party pages make up 85% of AI citations, but they're not the
strongest predictor of whether a brand gets mentioned. Of the answers
that mentioned the brand, 87% cited its own site, while 3rd party
pages featuring it were cited far less often. When the brand's site
was cited, it was mentioned 90% of the time in Google AI Overviews and
73% in ChatGPT.

 THE OVERSUPPLY ERA: CLASSICAL FRAMEWORKS FOR WINNING WHEN THE MAP IS
FULL (14 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Most markets are crowded because building is cheap and distribution
is easy. Supply keeps growing while demand stays flat and buyers stick
with familiar options. Customer research often confirms what
competitors already know. Growth increasingly comes from redefining
the category by challenging an accepted convention. Find what the
industry treats as non-negotiable and break it where doing so creates
new value for buyers.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 CHEAPEST LINKEDIN ADS RETARGETING SOURCE (1 MINUTE READ) [7]

 A low-cost way to build a LinkedIn retargeting audience is to run
60-70 second videos with a Website Visits objective. Use a headline
that discourages clicks because advertisers only pay when someone
clicks. Anyone who watches at least 50% of the video is added to the
retargeting audience. This tactic reportedly builds audiences for
about $0.50 per member versus $1-2 per click for standard engagement
campaigns.

 AN EVENT-LED COMMUNITY STRATEGY GUIDE (6 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Community events work best as part of an ongoing community strategy
rather than one-off activations. Assess whether the community is ready
for in-person interaction, then design smaller peer-led formats that
prioritize conversation over production value. Plan follow-ups before
the event with a dedicated conversation channel. Turn attendees into
active members by helping them build connections and stay involved.

 WHY YOU'RE WORTH IT (8 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Furniture brand Vitsœ uses its pricing page to justify why its
products cost more than cheaper alternatives. It breaks the cost into
smaller time-based amounts and points to resale prices as evidence of
durability. It also avoids sales and financing options that could
boost short-term revenue. These choices make its pricing claims more
credible because customers can verify them and see what the company
gives up to maintain them.

 GOOGLE ADS BRINGS A/B TESTING TO PERFORMANCE MAX ASSET GROUPS (1
MINUTE READ) [10]

 Google Ads is rolling out A/B testing for Performance Max asset
groups. Advertisers can test new headlines, descriptions, images, or
videos against existing creative without immediately replacing it.
Tests can isolate changes such as messaging or UGC to measure their
impact. Advertisers cannot make other asset changes while a test runs.


 APPLE HAS FAR-REACHING PLANS TO BLOCK HUNDREDS OF PROGRAMMATIC DATA
COMPANIES FROM IOS (2 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Apple's iOS 27 may expand WebKit's blocking of ad tech beyond
companies such as The Trade Desk, LiveRamp, and ID5. A new remote list
reportedly covers hundreds of CDPs, DMPs, DSPs, data sellers, and
identity providers. Apple can now add or remove companies without an
iOS update, and vendors may not know their status.

 HOW TO PRICE AND PACKAGE FOR D2C WITHOUT BREAKING YOUR GAME ECONOMY
(5 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Only 2-3% of players spend money in mobile games, making the first
purchase especially difficult when it requires leaving the app.
Publishers should assess the game economy before setting D2C offers.
Extra entitlements can outperform discounts because they preserve
revenue per purchase while adding value. Direct checkout reduces
friction. Creator codes, loyalty currencies, and exclusive web content
can drive purchases without lowering prices.

 CAN YOU SEO YOUR WAY INTO AN AI AGENT'S RECOMMENDATION? (7 MINUTE
READ) [13]

 AI agents recommend products mostly from what they learned in
training, not from what search returns at the moment of the question.
On advisory questions, agents rarely search unless prompted, and
search behavior depends on the prompt and tools set by whoever runs
the agent. Even when every search was forced and every query secretly
rewritten to favor one vendor, that vendor was selected only 17.1% of
the time, up from 9.2%. SEO can still shape future training data, but
it has limited influence on recommendations in the short term.
