NEWS & TRENDS

 ‘MODEL FATIGUE' SETS IN AS AI LABS RACE TO ROLL OUT NEW VERSIONS AT
FRENETIC PACE (5 MINUTE READ) [5]

 AI labs are releasing model updates at a faster pace, making it
harder for businesses to evaluate which tools are worth adopting.
Anthropic, Meta, Google, and OpenAI all launched updates in one week.
The rapid rollout is creating “model fatigue” as teams spend more
time comparing capabilities and performance.

 IS THE LIKE BUTTON DEAD? (3 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Likes and comments are dropping across social platforms. Instagram
likes are down 73% on carousels and 81% on Reels. Platforms are
relying on signals like watch time, completion rate, replays, and
dwell time to rank content. Engagement metrics are becoming less
useful, while retention and strong hooks matter more.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 ENTITIES ARE THE FOUNDATION OF MODERN SEO (10 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Entity SEO is now a core part of making a brand understandable to
Google and AI systems. Start by auditing owned properties so brand
names, people, products, relationships, and structured data are
accurate and consistent. Then build credibility through relevant
third-party mentions, expert commentary, and evidence that supports
what the brand claims to be known for. An entity map can reveal gaps
between intended positioning and how search data and the wider web
actually connect the brand to people, products, services, and topics.

 DO 3-DAY OR 7-DAY TRIALS PERFORM BETTER? (4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 For mobile apps, 7-day trials convert 10% better than 3-day trials
overall, with paywall-to-paid conversion rising from 1.55% to 1.70%.
The difference is larger on Android, where 7-day trials deliver a 20%
lift, compared with 5% on iOS. However, results vary by region. In
North America, 3-day trials outperform 7-day on iOS, while 7-day
performs better across most Android markets. The data suggests trial
length should be tested by platform and region.

 MAKE POSTEINGÄNGE SEXY AGAIN (4 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Welcome emails already draw close to four times the click rate of a
regular campaign, proof that readers respond fastest to something that
feels personally addressed to them. Newsletters that open on a
specific moment or an odd, memorable detail get read as correspondence
instead of content to skim past. Confiding in a reader works better
than performing for an audience, since specificity is what makes
writing sound like an actual person rather than a content calendar.
Replying when someone writes back extends that same logic into a
growth strategy, not just a courtesy.

 DRIVE AWARENESS WITH VIDEO SEQUENCE ADS (2 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Google Ads video ad sequence campaigns let advertisers build a
planned narrative across multiple YouTube videos, moving viewers from
brand introduction to reinforcement, differentiation, or action. Each
step can use a different video format and transition based on whether
viewers saw, watched, or skipped the previous ad. You can also test
headlines, CTAs, and landing pages at each stage while using frequency
caps to control exposure.

 DON'T MAKE THE MISTAKE OF BUYING ATTRIBUTION TOO SOON (5 MINUTE READ)
[14]

 Custom attribution is premature until a channel has proven it works
and the marketing mix is genuinely difficult to measure. Attribution
helps answer where to put the next dollar across multiple working
channels, not which channel works in the first place. Test channels
first, then fix tracking, standardize ROAS, and model conversion value
before building an attribution model. Custom attribution can pay off
above roughly $3M in annual paid media, but the right threshold
depends more on the complexity of the channel mix than the budget
itself.

 HOW 7 BREW'S FIRST CMO IS MARKETING THE FASTEST-GROWING BRAND IN THE
US (5 MINUTE READ) [15]

 7 Brew's TikTok strategy runs on movie-parody videos tied to real
brand moments, from a 2001 A Space Odyssey riff for its new app launch
to earlier takes on Indiana Jones and Mean Girls, and has built a
following of more than 685,000 with 7.5 million likes. The tactic
works by riding cultural references fans already have affection for
rather than manufacturing a campaign from scratch. That approach sits
on top of a brand that already rates first in consumer satisfaction
among US QSR chains, so the content amplifies existing goodwill
instead of trying to create it.
