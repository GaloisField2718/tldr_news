 TRUST BEATS REACH IN CREATOR MARKETING (2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Creator partnerships are more effective when brands prioritize trust
and audience relevance over follower count or engagement rate. TikTok
research found that creator relevance increased brand memory by 35%,
compared with just 10% for fame, while combining both produced the
strongest results. A separate analysis of 231 brand lift studies found
no significant relationship between engagement rate and brand recall,
suggesting that likes and comments do not reliably measure a creator's
influence on brand perception. Instead, brand fit, audience relevance,
emotional connection, and trust are stronger indicators of a creator's
advertising value.

 YOUR TEAM'S MARKETING AI SPEND IS ABOUT TO BECOME YOUR PROBLEM (9
MINUTE READ) [8]

 As marketing teams build AI agents and automate more workflows, their
AI costs are growing. Spending is spread across subscriptions, APIs,
and cloud platforms, making budgets harder to track and justify.
Leaders can control costs by auditing tools and unused automations and
turning repetitive work into scripts or reusable skills. Technical
savings come from prompt caching and batch processing.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 AI SHOPPING ERRORS: WHAT TO FIX TO HELP SHOPPERS CHOOSE YOUR PRODUCTS
(6 MINUTE READ) [9]

 ChatGPT and Google can misrepresent products or send shoppers to
competitors when product data is incomplete or outdated. With AI
traffic to US retail sites forecast to grow 130% this holiday season,
brands need to audit how their products appear in AI recommendations.
Check for missing products and incorrect prices or specs, align
product feeds with website and retailer listings, add product
identifiers and schema, and rerun buying prompts regularly to catch
errors and monitor rankings.

 HOW BRANDS GO VIRAL WITH $0 MARKETING BUDGET (5 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Brands with no media budget are borrowing attention from formats and
moments audiences already watch. immi runs a street-interview account
where its ramen barely appears, so people discover the content first
and the brand second. Rally Pickle Juice handed out shots to cramping
runners near mile 23 of a marathon, an event already drawing
attention. Sourmilk's founder leans on the familiar “I quit my
job” hook, telling how she left private equity to start a yogurt
brand.

 THE GROWTH META: HOW TO BUILD A SALES PIPELINE (4 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Founders are the first salespeople in the company, and early-stage
startups are building pipelines in 3 main ways. They turn sales calls
into brainstorming sessions, asking what customers want to build and
where the product fits into that vision. They lead with proof instead
of a pitch, like offering a free security scan of the prospect's own
site in place of a demo. They also start enterprise GTM on day one,
staying close to companies that don't fit their ICP yet because that
can change within six months.

 OPTIMIZING THE PERFORMANCE OF CLIENT-SIDE EXPERIMENTATION (6 MINUTE
READ) [14]

 You can run frequent experiments without sacrificing site speed by
optimizing how tests load and execute. Keep JavaScript snippets small
through page-specific bundles, longer cache times, and reusable code.
Use client-side testing for visual changes and server-side testing for
performance-sensitive pages to avoid adding JavaScript to the browser.
Measure the impact through real user monitoring (RUM), tracking Core
Web Vitals like LCP, INP, and CLS. Segment results by device,
geography, and connection speed to identify performance issues that
averages can hide.

 PEDIALYTE IS A GREAT POSITIONING STORY (6 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Positioning starts with deciding what a product is, and Pedialyte has
answered that question in 2 different ways. The first answer was a
rehydration drink for sick babies, sold to parents. The second came
from customers after people on Twitter began using it for hangovers in
2009, turning it into an adult hydration drink backed by a
pharmaceutical maker. The product never changed, so the company only
needed a new audience and a new message, not a rename or a
hangover-cure claim that could harm its initial positioning.

 CAN YOUTUBE CREATORS MAKE SINGLES' DAY AN AMERICAN SHOPPING HOLIDAY?
(5 MINUTE READ) [16]

 AliExpress is using its 18-day Singles' Day sale to build US
awareness and compete with Black Friday through a new YouTube Shopping
partnership. Creators can tag products in videos and livestreams while
earning commissions. On November 1, US sales surged 2,828% in 3D
printing and 923% in gaming compared with October daily averages.
AliExpress is pairing creator-led product demonstrations with verified
brand sellers and delivery guarantees to drive purchases and build
customer trust.
