NEWS & TRENDS

 SHOULD THERE BE AEO AGENCIES? (6 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Content was never the real value of SEO agencies. Growth was.
Agencies made content the deliverable because it was easy to scope,
and rankings made progress easy to measure. AI is now exposing the
weakness of that model by making content cheap and easy to produce.
Agencies need to shift from content production to strategic work that
drives growth, rather than simply rebranding old SEO services as AEO.

 WHAT DRIVES GEN Z BRAND LOYALTY IN 2026? (5 MINUTE READ) [5]

 64% of Gen Z say they stick with brands they like most or all of the
time, but only 11% feel brands understand them really well. Feeling
understood is strongly linked to loyalty, with 45% of those who feel
truly understood saying they are always loyal. Price and value (82%)
and product quality (80%) matter most, while influencer
recommendations rank last at 9%. Relevance starts with understanding
customers, but loyalty still depends on delivering a product worth
coming back to.

 HOW TO HANDLE NEGATIVE COMMENTS ON REDDIT (WHEN THE CRITICISM IS
ALREADY RANKING) (5 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Treat negative Reddit posts as a community management issue, since
high-ranking criticism can shape search results and AI answers.
Respond selectively as a named, disclosed person by correcting
misinformation or adding useful product context. Positive and negative
threads receive similar upvote weight, so helpful brand contributions
can reach audiences at a similar scale. Build credibility early by
listening and using REACT to decide when to engage or step back.

 YOUR PUBLIC BOOKING FORM IS A BOT MAGNET. HERE IS HOW TO SEAL IT (3
MINUTE READ) [7]

 Public forms can attract spam bots as soon as a product launches,
especially without login requirements. The author recommends a
two-layer system. Use Cloudflare Turnstile on every public form and
verify its token server-side before storing submissions. A database
trigger adds another safeguard by rejecting invalid submissions that
bypass the application. Turnstile is free for up to 1M monthly
requests, making it a low-cost way to protect public forms from spam.

 TIKTOK JUST MADE PAID SOCIAL AGENTIC (3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 TikTok's new agentic AI tools can automate paid social execution, but
the bigger shift is teaching AI how your team works. TikTok Ads MCP
connects AI to campaign data and workflows, while Agentic Hub and
Skills can package processes like creative fatigue analysis, budget
optimization, reporting, and QA. Automate repeatable work, but keep
humans involved in strategic decisions.

 REDDIT MAX CAMPAIGNS ARE NOW AVAILABLE TO ALL ADVERTISERS (2 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Reddit is making Max campaigns available to all advertisers,
alongside 3 updates designed to expand their value. Max campaigns
automate targeting, creative selection, placements, and budget
allocation, with early tests showing 17% lower CPA and 27% more
conversions. New asset groups let advertisers organize and measure
different products or use cases within one campaign, while API and
Smartly support make campaigns easier to manage at scale.

 GOOGLE'S LATEST STUDY REVEALS HOW LLMS RECALL FACTS (6 MINUTE READ)
[12]

 Getting recalled correctly by AI models does not require more content
- it requires making existing content easier to retrieve. Advanced
models store 95% to 98% of the facts they are trained on but reliably
recall only 26% to 34% of them. Recall breaks down most on facts asked
backward from how they were learned, or on facts that are simply rare.
Auditing where AI tools already mention or skip a brand's key facts
shows exactly which messages need restating in both directions.
