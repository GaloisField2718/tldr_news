 WHY REDDIT'S INFLUENCE ISN'T GOING DOWN (8 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Reddit influences product research and purchase decisions through
peer recommendations and discussions that shape Google results and
AI-generated answers, even without direct citations. Its organic
traffic grew from 46M monthly visits in November 21 to 495M in October
26. An analysis of 58 clients found a median of 9% of LLM responses
cited Reddit. Threads also shape buyer expectations through product
comparisons and real-world feedback, which can influence third-party
reviews and AI recommendations.

 SHOULD YOU INCORPORATE AI IN YOUR POSITIONING? (10 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Companies have three ways to position AI in their product strategy.
They can pivot into a new AI category when it offers stronger growth
potential and solves a clear customer need; they can use AI to stand
out in an established category, as Gamma does against PowerPoint and
Google Slides; or they can keep their existing positioning and treat
AI as a supporting feature. The choice should reflect what customers
value rather than AI hype.

 WRITE TO THE CUSTOMER'S EXCUSE (1 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Product copy should address not just why customers want something,
but why they hesitate to buy it. Instead of repeating benefits and
features, identify objections and use copy to overcome them. For
example, a water bottle brand could acknowledge that customers already
own one, then explain why they might want to replace it if it leaks or
makes water taste metallic. Listing reasons customers might not buy
can reveal opportunities to improve conversion copy.

 YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION UPDATES LINKED TO 359% RISE IN AI RETRIEVALS (1
MINUTE READ) [10]

 Updating YouTube descriptions may improve video visibility in AI
search. A Peec AI experiment found that videos with updated
descriptions saw a 359% increase in daily AI retrievals, compared with
17% for unchanged videos. Retrievals also rose 293% in Google AI
Overviews and 129% in AI Mode. The experiment updated descriptions on
15 of roughly 60 videos. Adding structured details and manual
timestamps could help AI systems surface existing videos.

 A PRACTITIONER'S FIELD GUIDE TO MARKETING ENGINEERING (9 MINUTE READ)
[14]

 Marketing engineering uses technical skills to turn repetitive
marketing tasks into automated systems. Instead of manually running
campaigns, marketers build systems that continuously monitor market
activity and identify opportunities. These systems can also produce
and distribute content. The goal is to scale marketing efforts and run
more experiments without adding headcount. AI coding tools make this
possible without requiring a computer science degree.

 AI KNOWS WHAT YOUR BRAND DOES, BUT NOT WHO IT'S FOR (15 MINUTE READ)
[15]

 AI answers named a brand's ideal customer correctly only 60% of the
time. Proof points like customer counts rarely appeared, and half of
detailed brand descriptions included none. Where information was
missing, engines often guessed, and 34% of answers contained an
invented fact, most often a price. Positioning held up best when
brands stated it simply and repeated it everywhere.

 WHAT IS AN AI AGENT FOR SEO? HOW IT DIFFERS FROM SEO TOOLS (5 MINUTE
READ) [16]

 Traditional SEO tools provide data and recommendations but leave
execution to users. AI SEO agents go further by working across
connected tools to complete tasks such as keyword research and
performance reporting. They can run on schedules or respond to
requests with limited human input. More advanced agents can manage
entire workflows, but publishing and technical changes should still
require human approval.
