 INSTAGRAM COLLAB POSTS DRIVE HIGHER ORGANIC ENGAGEMENT ACROSS BRAND
SIZES (7 MINUTE READ) [6]

 More than 73% of brands used Instagram collabs in H1 2026, and collab
posts consistently outperformed standard posts. Creator-led
collaborations delivered the strongest results, with smaller brands
seeing 5X more interactions than non-collab posts. In ecommerce and
retail, accepted collabs generated more than 5X the shares and reposts
of standard posts. Reels saw the biggest engagement gains.

 DO CHATGPT & GOOGLE AI GET YOUR BUSINESS DETAILS RIGHT? (7 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 AI search often gives inaccurate business information, especially
when a company leaves key details vague or unpublished. In a study of
600 businesses, 27% of Google AI Overview answers and 20% of ChatGPT
answers included details the business did not publish. Google AI
Overviews got 84% of answers right when businesses published clearly,
compared with 63% when information was vague or missing. AI systems
often fill gaps from third-party directories, so you should audit
those listings and keep website copy, Google Business Profiles, and
structured data consistent. Agencies can turn this into a quick audit
by checking how Google and ChatGPT describe each client and correcting
the sources behind inaccurate answers.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 COMPETITOR EMAIL MONITORING: WHAT TO TRACK AND HOW TO DO IT (7 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Competitor email monitoring helps brands spot patterns in how others
approach email. Start by tracking 5-8 direct competitors each month
and compare their send frequency and offer rates. Read their subject
lines to see how they frame promotions and other messages. Open
unusual emails and note what stands out or changes over time. Use
those findings to identify gaps and choose one idea to test, rather
than copying a competitor's strategy.

 THE AMAZON CRO PLAYBOOK: HOW TO OPTIMIZE YOUR LISTINGS AND BEAT YOUR
COMPETITORS (18 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Amazon CRO works best as a research and testing process rather than a
series of listing tweaks. Start by finding funnel problems in Seller
Central, then use customer feedback, reviews, and user testing to
identify specific objections and turn them into targeted listing
changes. Since Amazon allows only one A/B test per listing and tests
can take 8 to 12 weeks, prioritize bold changes and validate ideas
through off-platform usability testing before committing to a live
experiment. The main image, title, bullets, A+ Content, price, and
promotions offer the biggest levers, but sellers should also optimize
their advertising when paid traffic converts poorly rather than
assuming the listing is the problem.

 HOW TO CREATE A PRODUCT LAUNCH PEOPLE TALK ABOUT (6 MINUTE READ) [10]


 A product launch only generates lasting attention when it gives
people something to do beyond scroll past it, whether that is showing
up somewhere in person, guessing what is coming, or filming something
unusual enough to send to a friend. The brands getting the most
mileage out of a launch treat it as an ongoing event instead of a
single announcement, letting creators and customers do the
distribution for free. None of it requires a bigger budget, just a
reason for people to keep talking after day one.

 PINTEREST UNVEILS VISUAL SEARCH ADS, BRINGING NEW PERFORMANCE
ADVERTISING TO VISUAL DISCOVERY (2 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Pinterest is turning visual search into a lower-funnel advertising
channel through Visual Search Ads, which place sponsored products in
search results and Pin closeups as users compare options and approach
a purchase decision. More than half of Pinterest's 80 billion monthly
searches are commercial, giving advertisers a large pool of users who
already show buying intent. It is also adding Priority Products and
self-serve A/B testing to give advertisers more control over automated
campaigns.

 CUSTOM GPT RETIREMENT AND MIGRATION FAQ (5 MINUTE READ) [13]

 OpenAI plans to retire custom GPTs and transition users to plugins
across all ChatGPT plans. Existing GPTs remain usable until
retirement, with Enterprise creation ending on September 25 and GPTs
scheduled to stop running on December 11. Migration will turn GPT
instructions into a plugin skill and connected apps into apps, but
custom actions will need to be rebuilt separately. Migrated GPTs
become read-only, and users should test the replacement before
switching. Plugin access and sharing will depend on account and
workspace permissions.

 PERPLEXITY IS BETTING BIG ON CREATORS (3 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Perplexity has filled a creator marketing role overseeing hundreds of
influencer partnerships, 100+ creator campaigns, and
multi-million-dollar budgets. The strategy ties creator campaigns to
conversions and product adoption, with creators demonstrating real
Perplexity use cases rather than simply endorsing the product. AI
companies are increasingly using creators to drive product adoption
and communicate their value.

 OPENAI REPORTS 6 NEW INSTANCES OF ‘CONCERNING MODEL BEHAVIOR' SINCE
MARCH (3 MINUTE READ) [15]

 OpenAI disclosed cases of unexpected model behavior. In two cases,
models inserted instructions into chat summaries to hide mistakes from
users. Another model used a leaked API key without authorization and
fabricated data. OpenAI is creating a new framework for investigating
and publicly reporting these incidents as pressure grows for stronger
AI safety measures.
