 61 MILLION CONSUMERS ASK AI WHAT TO BUY. THEN THEY BUY IT ON AMAZON
(4 MINUTE READ) [7]

 23% of US consumers, or roughly 61 million people, now start retail
research with AI. Average AI-assisted spending rose 17% from $889 in
June to $1,039 in August. Yet 60% of AI-assisted purchases still close
on Amazon. In a controlled test, 40% of consumers chose a marketplace
even when told other retailers offered the same product, price,
shipping, and returns.

 INSIDE MCDONALD'S NEW MARKETING MODEL AS IT ENTERS MEDIA NETWORK FRAY
(4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 McDonald's is piloting a media network across 450 US company-owned
restaurants, selling ad space on its app, kiosks, and menu boards,
with the goal of building a billion-dollar business. Its marketing is
shifting to fewer, bigger global campaigns built on its own IP. It is
roughly doubling spending on long-running programs about its brand and
food quality while cutting short-term promotions.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 HOW TO FIND REAL HUMAN STORIES FOR PR AND JOURNALISM (14 MINUTE READ)
[9]

 Brands can strengthen PR pitches by pairing their data or news angle
with a real customer or person who has experienced the issue
firsthand. A strong human story gives journalists an interviewee who
can provide specific details and memorable quotes that make the story
more relatable. Brands can find these people through targeted social
media searches and OSINT techniques, then offer the verified source to
journalists as part of the pitch. Only 5% to 10% of people contacted
cold on social media may respond, so you may need to contact many
potential sources and verify each person carefully.

 AI GUIDE TO BUILDING A TOP-TIER INBOUND AI AGENT (7 MINUTE READ) [10]


 SaaStr's inbound AI agent held 17,000 conversations with prospects
and booked about 600 meetings in a year. Together with a newer
self-serve agent, it drove a 60% jump in new business, and three
people created the whole setup. The on-site agent sits on the sponsor
page, qualifies visitors on budget and goals, then books the meeting
itself. For buyers who skip the chat, a second agent swaps the PDF
prospectus for a page unique to each company. It checks first-party
signals, then drafts a pitch that a human approves and that updates
the same link in place.

 IS THIS THE LAST PLACE TO AUTOMATE? (8 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Use AI to research prospects, but write the outreach yourself. Find a
genuine connection on LinkedIn, tie it to a specific buyer problem,
and address likely objections before they come up. Write 50 emails
Monday through Wednesday, send them Thursday and Friday, then follow
up within 48 hours over the weekend. On intro calls, skip generic
discovery questions and let the buyer talk. If someone asks to
reconnect later, respect the timing and nurture them with useful
insights instead of “just checking in” messages.

 Google Veo lets you turn existing static assets into 5 or 10 second
video ads for Performance Max and Demand Gen campaigns. Strong, high
resolution product images matter because the tool animates two images
into sequential scenes and supports 16:9, 9:16, and 1:1 formats. The

 HOW TO WIN AI SEARCH WITH CREATORS (6 MINUTE READ) [16]

 AI answers lean on third-party voices, so less than 10% of what they
say about a brand comes from the brand's own content. 1 in 3 answers
cite creators directly. Follower count barely matters, since 57% of
cited YouTube videos come from channels under 100K. This playbook
recommends combining SEO keyword data with real customer questions to
build a shared prompt list, picking creators for authority and
specificity, and reposting content stuck behind Instagram and TikTok
login walls to YouTube as transcripts that AI crawlers can reach.

 HOW GOOGLE AND UBER MEASURE WHAT CANNOT BE RANDOMIZED (5 MINUTE READ)
[17]

 Major marketing investments that can't be randomized still need
causal measurement, rather than post-hoc narratives. Google's
CausalImpact and Uber's synthetic control methods show how you can
estimate the effect of national launches, sponsorships, and other
whole market interventions by building counterfactuals from historical
data or comparable markets. These methods are weaker than randomized
tests, so you should preregister the analysis, establish a strong pre
period, run placebo tests, and report uncertainty intervals. Synthetic
control should be a last resort for spending that cannot support
randomization.

 IS THE TRADITIONAL MARKETING TEAM DYING, SHRINKING, OR TURNING INTO
SOMETHING ELSE? (6 MINUTE READ) [18]

 The modern marketing team is built around three principles. 0-1
marketers use AI systems to take work from idea to shipped output with
fewer handoffs. Pods replace traditional team hierarchies with small
groups that share context, own a specific outcome, and ship and learn
weekly. Depth of craft becomes more valuable as AI handles production,
giving specialists more time to apply the judgment and expertise
needed to make standout work.
