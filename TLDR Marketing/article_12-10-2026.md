 WE CHECKED 56 OF AMERICA'S BIGGEST BRANDS: NEWSROOMS LOCK AI OUT,
EVERYONE ELSE LEAVES THE DOOR OPEN (10 MINUTE READ) [7]

 68% of major US brands have no AI-specific crawling rules. Newsrooms
are more likely to block AI bots, with 75% restricting at least one
compared with 6% of other brands. None of the 52 accessible robots.txt
files fully blocked OpenAI's or Anthropic's AI search bots by name,
though some blocked training crawlers. Forbes blocks training bots
while allowing AI search, while GEICO explicitly allows all 12 AI
crawlers it names.

 HOW WE INCREASED ORDERS BY 9% WITHOUT A DISCOUNT CODE (2 MINUTE READ)
[9]

 A South American gift company increased orders by 9% by adding 2 free
services to its checkout summary without offering a discount. Research
showed customers hesitated at the final price and wanted better value.
The test highlighted existing benefits, including premium gift
wrapping and a satisfaction guarantee, with both listed as free beside
the total. This reframed the purchase around what customers received
rather than just what they paid.

 CHURN HAPPENS EARLY (6 MINUTE READ) [10]

 SaaS churn is heavily front-loaded, with the biggest drop-off often
happening during signup and initial product use before retention
stabilizes. Track retention by signup cohort rather than total user
churn to identify where new users leave and prioritize fixes with the
greatest downstream impact. In one example, reducing
signup-to-first-project churn could generate 164 additional paywall
views compared with 59 from fixing the paywall step itself. Improve
early experiences by helping users reach value milestones quickly,
since every user lost early reduces the pool available for later
conversions and referrals.

 A NEW VISION FOR SPONSORSHIPS (4 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Substack is expanding its sponsorship program with 3 formats that let
brands fund free paid subscriptions, unlock paywalled posts, or curate
themed collections from established writers. These partnerships help
publishers reach new readers while generating revenue and creating
opportunities to convert free readers into paying subscribers.
Substack is also partnering with Kantar to measure campaign impact on
brand awareness and consideration beyond impressions and clicks.

 CLAUDE CITATIONS: CLAUDE CITES THE PAGE THAT LOOKS LIKE THE QUESTION
(5 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Claude's citations closely match query format. Best-X questions
pulled listicles 90% of the time. A-vs-B questions pulled comparison
pages 87% of the time, while how-to questions pulled guides 85% of the
time. Create content for each buyer query type and publish competitor
comparison pages to avoid losing visibility to competitors. Sonnet and
Haiku often cited different URLs for the same questions, so check both
models to assess Claude visibility.

MISCELLANEOUS

 NOT SURE WHAT TO LAUNCH NEXT? START TALKING TO YOUR CUSTOMERS (7
MINUTE READ) [13]

 Study how customers use existing products to uncover winning product
ideas rather than chasing trends or competitor launches. Zouk created
its bestselling office bag after noticing working women needed one bag
for laptops and personal items. Observe customers in real-world
settings and identify workarounds that reveal unmet needs. Turn
recurring complaints into product briefs and call customers to
understand their frustrations. Solve functional problems first, then
layer in design and cultural relevance.

 BEFORE YOU REBRAND: HOW A BRAND AUDIT REVEALS WHAT ACTUALLY NEEDS TO
CHANGE (5 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Conduct a brand audit before rebranding to identify the real problem.
Compare internal strategy with customer feedback to determine whether
the issue requires clearer messaging or a new identity. Review public
touchpoints to find gaps between the intended brand and customer
perception. Match each finding to the right fix rather than defaulting
to a full redesign. Preserve existing brand equity and ensure the
customer experience supports the brand promise.
