NEWS & TRENDS

 AI SHOPPING COULD MEAN FEWER RETURNS (2 MINUTE READ) [8]

 AI shopping could reduce ecommerce returns by helping shoppers
resolve uncertainty before they buy. Adobe found that 69% of consumers
who used AI for shopping said they were less likely to return an
AI-assisted purchase, while AI-referred traffic converted 60% higher
than non-AI-referred traffic. Merchants can improve these results by
giving AI assistants detailed product information such as
specifications, dimensions, compatibility, variants, delivery
estimates, reviews, and FAQs. Better product data can help AI
recommend products that fit shoppers' needs more accurately,
potentially producing both more sales and fewer returns.

 MARKETERS ARE REBRANDING AS ‘ENGINEERS' TO SURVIVE THE AI ERA (3
MINUTE READ) [9]

 The marketing engineer is pitched as part builder and part marketer,
someone who deploys AI agents to automate work like media buying and

attempt to borrow engineering's sense of precision.

 HOW BRANDS ARE FIGHTING AI SLOP (6 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Human and physical signals are becoming a way to show that content
wasn't machine-made. J.Crew's Barn Jacket campaign featured 160 real,
recognizable people across five cities, giving it cultural context
that AI can imitate visually but can't manufacture. Other brands are
commissioning artists, showing the messy process before a launch, and
moving into print and physical retail. The more content AI can
produce, the more value shifts to proving what a human did that AI
didn't.

 REDDIT MODERATOR OUTREACH: HOW TO BUILD REAL RELATIONSHIPS WITH
SUBREDDIT MODS (6 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Start Reddit moderator outreach by researching the subreddit, reading
its rules, and understanding what its members care about. Use a named
account with genuine activity, then introduce yourself and your
company clearly in the first message. Explain what you can contribute
to the community, such as expert answers, product context, or early
access, and commit to following its rules without asking for
promotional permission upfront. If a moderator says yes, respect its
preferred communication style and keep checking in even when you have
nothing to promote. Over time, consistent participation can turn
moderators into trusted partners and valuable sources of community and
product feedback.

 AI VISIBILITY AT SCALE: LEARNING FROM 9M PROMPTS AND 400+ ENTERPRISE
BRANDS (6 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Data from 9 million AI answers shows that most common GEO assumptions
are wrong. Sentiment shows no positive link to citations, and
listicles lose out to ordinary, thorough pages. YouTube delivers the
biggest third-party lift at 2.8x, ahead of LinkedIn and Reddit, while
up to 58% of citations never reappear. Up to 75% of answers citing a
brand's page never name the brand, so citation counts alone overstate
impact. Volatility tracks the brand far more than the platform, and
the most stable performers share deep topical coverage and strong
third-party mentions.

 INTRODUCING DOTS (7 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Dots are always-on AI agents powered by GPT-6 Astra that can work
independently toward goals using their own cloud computer and
connected apps. They learn your preferences and context over time,
then use that knowledge to handle ongoing work without needing
step-by-step direction. Dots can also work across ChatGPT, Slack, and
Teams. You control what they can access and which actions require
approval. OpenAI is also developing specialist dots for specific
organizational responsibilities.

 68 SECONDS: HOW ONE FORGOTTEN WEBSITE TOOK DOWN 19 OF MINE (10 MINUTE
READ) [15]

 A forgotten WordPress site became the entry point for an attack that
infected 19 websites on the same hosting account. The attacker used a
stolen password and a file manager plugin to install malware, which
spread in 68 seconds and eventually created roughly 400,000 casino
spam pages. The malware also hid the pages from site admins, blocked
SEO crawlers, and added rogue Google Search Console access. The author
details how he contained the breach, traced the source, and rebuilt
the sites.

 HOW CREATORS ARE SHOWING UP IN AI ANSWERS IN 2026 (6 MINUTE READ)
[16]

 56% of people who shop with AI prefer recommendations that draw on
creator reviews. Creators matter most early, building confidence and
shortlists, while ratings and retailer data still carry the final
purchase decision. Shoppers want AI to prioritize everyday customers
sharing real experiences (52%) over celebrities (13%), and only 21%
say a large following makes a creator credible. Interest falls with
age, from 65% among Gen Z and Millennials to 34% among Boomers.
