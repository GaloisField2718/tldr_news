NEWS & TRENDS

 PARTICIPATION IN BLACK FRIDAY-CYBER MONDAY SHOPPING EXPECTED TO HIT
10-YEAR HIGH (5 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Deloitte reports that 87% of consumers plan to shop during BFCM this
year, with average spending flat at $624. Gen Z leads with 94%
planning to participate and $752 in expected spending, up 23% year
over year. Social media and GenAI use are linked to higher spending,
with shoppers using both expecting to spend 61% more than those using
neither. Meanwhile, 72% are delaying some purchases in anticipation of
better deals.

 AI CITATIONS TO SOCIAL MEDIA & UGC GREW 52% ON GOOGLE'S AI PLATFORMS,
FELL 44% ON CHATGPT (8 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Citations to 18 UGC domains grew 52% across Google's AI platforms
from January to August, but fell 44% on ChatGPT. YouTube led AI
Overviews with 45% of tracked citations, while Reddit dominated
ChatGPT at 52%. 17 of 18 sites gained citations on AI Overviews, while
12 lost citations on ChatGPT. TikTok saw the strongest growth across
both. Despite low AI referral traffic of just 0.14%, UGC can shape
brand visibility in AI answers. Citation patterns vary by category,
making it important to identify which sources AI platforms reference
most in your market.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 HOW TO MAKE HIGH-QUALITY AI ADS (10 MINUTE READ) [7]

 AI ads start with a product selling point and a customer pain point.
Build a short story around one feature that solves a real problem,
then test the hardest shot before generating the rest. AI reels often
use a recognizable situation, an unusual opening frame, and a single
product-focused payoff, usually within 15-30 seconds. Control the
output by specifying shot sizes and color grading in consistent
language. Generate short clips, make pacing and text adjustments in
the edit, and change one prompt variable at a time.

 THE B2B DEMAND GEN CHECKLIST (5 MINUTE READ) [8]

 B2B companies can capture more existing buyer demand by improving how
they handle high-intent leads. Route every demo and contact request to
the right sales rep with instant booking or a response within 30
minutes. Track speed-to-lead as a KPI. Install ad pixels for
retargeting and conversion tracking. Identify high-intent search and
AI queries such as competitor comparisons and pricing. Target these
through SEO, AEO, and paid search while organic rankings build.

 TEST YOUR ECOMMERCE SITE FOR AI ACCESS (2 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Ecommerce sites can test whether AI crawlers can access and read
their content using 3 checks. A free tool from a former Google
employee tests whether AI bots are blocked by robots.txt or server
settings. Asking ChatGPT to find a sentence hidden in collapsible
content can reveal whether it retrieves JavaScript-rendered text.
Chrome Reading Mode offers another check by stripping away navigation
and pop-ups to show whether key content such as product titles,
descriptions, pricing, and reviews can be extracted cleanly.

MISCELLANEOUS

 NEW HUBSPOT DATA REVEALS HOW MENTIONS AND CITATIONS RELATE ON THE AI
ENGINE LEVEL (9 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Model releases now swing AI visibility the way algorithm updates once
swung SEO. After an update to ChatGPT on August 6, cited URLs per
prompt rose 51% and domain-specific searches rose 45x. Gemini's 3.7
Flash release cut citations per prompt 27% the same week it launched.
Perplexity went the other way, doubling cited URLs per answer to about
18 while referral traffic fell 14%. Sources shifted too, with ChatGPT
citing competitor sites more and forums and videos far less.

 CAN WE DESIGN EMOTIONAL LOYALTY? (5 MINUTE READ) [13]

 An emotional loyalty map treats loyalty as a series of moments, each
with a specific emotion to design for. For every moment, from opening
the app to a service failure, it compares what the member feels now
with what they should feel and picks an action to close that gap. A
new member opening the app may feel confused, so showing clear
progress toward the next reward leaves them informed and motivated.
After a service failure, the goal is reassurance, which comes from a
fast fix and a reply that recognizes their membership.
