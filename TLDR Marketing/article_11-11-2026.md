NEWS & TRENDS

 CHATGPT JUST BECAME THE NEW GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE, AND CMOS FINALLY
GET THE NUMBERS (3 MINUTE READ) [4]

 OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT, giving brands verified
profiles to update company information and track mentions, profile
views, and website clicks. The dashboard offers the first official
data on ChatGPT visibility but doesn't reveal the prompts driving
mentions. Like Google Business Profile, it could become a standard
business listing with ads built in. The launch also introduces
Business Agents that let shoppers ask questions and compare products
inside ChatGPT.

 ABOUT A THIRD OF AMERICANS HAVE LEFT AN ONLINE COMMUNITY (3 MINUTE
READ) [5]

 About one-third of adults in the US have left an online community.
These include groups where people connect or participate together
through social media, forums, messaging, or online games. More than
80% cite losing interest as a reason, while 57% point to too much
arguing and 43% to harassment. Other reasons include excessive
political discussions (40%) and spending too much time online (37%).
Younger adults are more likely to leave, with 44% of those under 30
having exited a community compared with 19% of adults 65 and older.

 GO FOR A 70-20-10 SPLIT ON LINKEDIN (1 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Grow your LinkedIn following with a 70-20-10 content split across
roughly 20 monthly posts. Spend 70% (14 posts) on top-of-funnel
content like industry news, humor, and relatable topics to reach a
broader audience. Use 20% (4 posts) for tactical playbooks that solve
specific audience problems. Reserve 10% (2 posts) for social proof,
client wins, and promotional offers. Maximize reach and follower
growth over lead generation, with optional weekend posts or
experimental content to build familiarity.

 CHATGPT IS NOW SHOWING ADS WHILE IMAGES ARE BEING GENERATED (4 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 For free ChatGPT users, image generation now comes with ads attached.
Labeled, multi-image units run while a picture renders for US users on
the Free and Go plans. They fill the seconds a user already spends
waiting for the picture, so the ad interrupts nothing. Free users can
opt out of ads, but only by giving up image generation.

 BEFORE YOU SPEND MORE ON ADS, FIND OUT WHAT HAPPENED TO YOUR LAST 300
LEADS (7 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Reviewing recent leads shows where the process breaks before more
money goes into ads. If cost per lead is rising, the campaigns
themselves likely need work. If many leads were never contacted, the
problem is sales capacity, and more ads would only lengthen the queue.
If leads were contacted quickly but rarely qualified, the problem is
likely the offer, targeting, or form questions.

 HOW SOFTWARE BUYERS USE AI (5 MINUTE READ) [12]

 AI is introducing new software vendors to buyers who already have a
shortlist. Wynter's survey found that 63% of buyers with existing
vendor candidates added another option through AI. Buyers then use
follow-up prompts to compare vendors and evaluate pricing,
integrations, setup, and product fit. After using AI, 73% visited
vendor websites and 48% used Google to continue their research.
Marketers need to build brand awareness before buyers start searching
and make sure their products appear in AI recommendations across
multiple platforms.

 WE JUST GOT RID OF TITLES AT BEEHIIV (8 MINUTE READ) [13]

 beehiiv eliminated job titles to reduce internal bureaucracy and keep
employees focused on customers rather than hierarchy. Managing titles
created unnecessary work around leveling, promotions, and pay
negotiations. These discussions led to meetings and internal politics
without improving the product. Instead, employees are now identified
by their job family, with “Lead” added for people managers. The
company argues that growth should be measured through greater
responsibilities and contributions rather than titles or rank.
