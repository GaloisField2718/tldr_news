 ORGANIC TRAFFIC IS DOWN, BUT IT'S NOT ALL AI (7 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Organic search traffic fell 13% in the US and 24% in France over two
years, but losses vary by industry and site size. Publishers lost
about 42% of organic traffic in both markets, while smaller sites
remained roughly flat. Paid search traffic grew 21% in the US as
larger ad blocks pushed organic results below the fold. AI referrals
account for just 0.5% of total visits, suggesting that AI alone
doesn't explain declining search traffic.

 THE END OF THE INTERNET (11 MINUTE READ) [9]

 The internet's shift from open blogs and social networks to
algorithm-driven platforms has made discovery harder and independent
businesses less sustainable. AI could accelerate this decline by
flooding the web with content and creating more security risks.
Smaller communities and businesses offer an alternative. Paid niche
publications can thrive on loyal readers while private group chats
build stronger connections. AI also makes it easier to create custom
apps for individuals or small teams without needing mass-market
demand.

 STEAL YOUR COMPETITOR'S CUSTOMERS FOR <$100 (2 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Wispr Flow pays $35 for every paying Granola user who switches,
covering their remaining subscription and adding 3 free months. The
strategy recruits competitor affiliates by offering higher referral
payouts. Existing customers can also refer contacts who use rivals,
while sales teams can re-engage prospects who chose a competitor
earlier in the year. Covering the remainder of their subscription
reduces switching costs and gives them a reason to reconsider.

 INFLUENCE IS ONLY AS GOOD AS THE RULE BEHIND IT (2 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Influence can be a useful leading indicator when measured by
engagement rather than impressions alone. An analysis of 1,000+
advertisers and $80 million in LinkedIn ad spend found that companies
reached in 3 or more separate months entered the pipeline 1.5X more
often. Those with 5 or more paid engagements entered 2.2X more often.
Recent reach and engagement were stronger signals than longer lookback
periods, supporting consistent advertising to future buyers.

 IN A ZERO-CLICK WORLD, YOUR BEST MARKETING DOESN'T SHOW UP IN YOUR
ANALYTICS (1 MINUTE READ) [16]

 In a zero-click world, last-click attribution misses the content that
builds trust long before a purchase. Tutorials and videos can
influence buyers for years without getting credit for the sale. Talk
directly to customers to learn what convinced them to buy and what
keeps them loyal. Use AI to analyze their responses and uncover what
your metrics miss.

 LEGO AND DAFT PUNK'S TEAM-UP HIGHLIGHTS EXACTLY HOW BRANDS SHOULD
INTERACT WITH FANS (4 MINUTE READ) [17]

 LEGO built its Daft Punk collaboration around fan participation by
turning a fan-designed set into a product after it won a LEGO Ideas
contest and gained 10K supporters. It teased the partnership with a
cryptic video that encouraged fans to decode and discuss the reveal.
The launch film blended both brands' visual styles without letting
either dominate. Rather than rush out products, LEGO treats the
partnership as a long-term relationship with its audience.
