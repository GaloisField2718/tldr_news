NEWS & TRENDS

 THE HARMS OF SHORT-FORM VIDEO ON THE BRAIN ARE STARTING TO SHOW (5
MINUTE READ) [8]

 Heavy short-form video use may impair attention and memory. A
meta-analysis of about 70 studies found stronger links to cognitive
problems than mental health issues, with compulsive use predicting
worse outcomes. Other research links more phone time to poorer working
memory and uncontrolled short-form use to weaker episodic memory.
Memory and imagination share neural circuitry, so these effects could
also make it harder to envision and plan for the future.

 HAS MAINSTREAM CULTURE DISAPPEARED? NEW YOUTUBE TRENDS RESEARCH SAYS
NO (3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Mainstream culture isn't disappearing, but brands can no longer rely
on celebrities or broad media to create cultural relevance. YouTube
creators and memes are now seen as widely known as Hollywood stars.
Cultural momentum often starts in niche online communities before
spreading through sharing and creating. YouTube says the platform can
turn that momentum into broader cultural validation, with 65% of
respondents saying something feels more real or legitimate when they
see it there.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 "PROOF OF HUMANITY" IS THE BIGGEST MARKETING OPPORTUNITY RIGHT NOW (3
MINUTE READ) [10]

 66% of people under 30 already react negatively when a business
visibly uses AI in its ads, a reversal for the generation assumed to
be AI's biggest fans. That backlash is turning restraint itself into a
content opportunity, since publicly declaring what a brand won't
automate can work as marketing in its own right, not just a compliance
footnote. Aerie's pledge to skip AI-generated models became its
most-liked Instagram post ever, with a 23% sales bump the same
quarter.

 ARE WE BUILDING BRANDS FOR A WORLD THAT NO LONGER EXISTS? (5 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 Brands still rely on growth tactics built for a simpler, more stable
world. This piece argues for a “both/and” approach. Keep
evidence-based marketing principles while accounting for trust,
context, and long-term consequences. Set rules against manipulative
tactics. Use brand touchpoints to explain real-world decisions.
Dedicate 10% of activity to retention, employee pride, and referrals.

MISCELLANEOUS

 DOES RETAIL MEDIA ADD VALUE? (2 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Retail media only adds value when ads drive profitable incremental
sales. For advertisers, high ROAS can be misleading if shoppers would
have purchased anyway. Retailers can also hurt margins if ads displace
organic conversions or degrade the shopping experience. Both sides
should measure whether advertising changes outcomes.

 ARE YOU DOING MARKETING…OR BUILDING SOFTWARE? (7 MINUTE READ) [16]

 95% of enterprise GenAI projects show zero return. The biggest gap is
in areas with strong off-the-shelf options, such as rank tracking,
citation monitoring, and content scoring. Vendors serving thousands of
customers have already solved these integration challenges. Custom
workflows are harder to outsource, but buying proven expertise can
still beat learning through trial and error.
