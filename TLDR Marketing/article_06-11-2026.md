 MORE THAN HALF OF MARKETERS HAVE NO CLEAR READ ON UGC CREATOR ROI (3
MINUTE READ) [7]

 57% of marketers have no clear understanding of whether UGC creator
spending delivers positive returns, with 38% not measuring creator
costs against acquisition economics. Nearly half of teams allocate
less than 5% of ad spend to UGC. Instagram and TikTok are the top
creator sourcing channels at 74%. Authenticity is the top creator
selection criterion, with 96% prioritizing creators who are authentic
on camera.

 IS SOMETHING UP WITH YOUTUBE'S AD BUSINESS? (2 MINUTE READ) [8]

 YouTube's ad revenue growth is slowing despite its growing share of
TV viewing, raising questions about whether it can convert its CTV
audience into more advertising dollars. Its ad revenue grew 9% to
$11.2 billion in Q3, even as it captured 15.8% of total TV viewing.
Several factors may explain the gap: YouTube missed out on political
ad spending, some brands still view FAST platforms as more traditional
TV environments, and advertisers are diversifying their CTV budgets
instead of concentrating spend on YouTube. Growing competition from
Meta and YouTube's expanding ad-free subscription options may also
limit its advertising growth.

 WIN REPORT: HOW AN INFOGRAPHIC INCREASED CONVERSIONS BY 15% (2 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 DiamondBack Covers increased product page conversions by 15% by
replacing an early product gallery image with an infographic that
paired key product features with their customer benefits. Research
found that 43% of buyers cited security as their main purchase reason,
yet fewer than 13% of visitors scrolled far enough to see important
selling messages about protection and durability. The infographic
highlighted four selling points: durable aluminum construction, gas
spring-assisted openings, weatherproof seals, and secure locking
mechanisms. Pairing each feature with a specific benefit gave shoppers
both a reason to care and proof to support the claim.

 WHY AI INFLUENCERS WORK BETTER WITH A HUMAN FRIEND (3 MINUTE READ)
[10]

 Seeing an AI influencer relate to a real person makes it seem more
human, which raises trust and lifts engagement. Posts showing them
sharing a genuine moment with a real person, like eating together or
posing arm in arm, drew about 21% more engagement in a sample of
10,000 IG posts. A bystander in the frame or a second AI character has
no effect, and the lift is biggest for the most artificial-looking
avatars.

 FIND SITE ERRORS WITH AI BEFORE ADS RUN (2 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Use AI to audit campaign landing pages before ads go live and catch
problems that could prevent shoppers from converting. Tools like
ChatGPT and Claude can review screenshots, page content, and crawl
reports to identify broken links, conflicting offers, inaccurate
product availability, missing information, and mobile layout issues.
Pair AI analysis with technical tools like Google Lighthouse and
Screaming Frog, then manually test the shopping journey on desktop and
mobile, including checkout and ad conversion tracking. Prioritize
issues by their potential impact on customers, and repeat the audit
before every campaign.

 REDDIT JUST STOPPED GIVING ITSELF AWAY (5 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Reddit is testing a logged-in-only experience to reduce unmonetized
traffic and protect its content from AI scraping. The move follows a
53% revenue increase but slower user growth in the US. Meanwhile,
Discord is making public communities searchable on Google to attract
new members. Both platforms are adapting to AI-driven search by
balancing public content discovery with member access.

 WE PUSHED ACQUISITION TOO HARD. COMPLAINTS FOLLOWED (5 MINUTE READ)
[15]

 After increasing co-registration subscribers for its Trivia Nut
newsletter, a publisher saw Gmail's spam complaint rate exceed 0.30%
for 4 days and peak at 0.49%. It cut incoming subscriber volume by
50%, bringing the next reported rate down to 0.06%. The publisher
recommends monitoring Gmail's spam rate separately through Postmaster
Tools and checking acquisition sources when complaints rise.

 NARRATIVE INTELLIGENCE VS. SOCIAL LISTENING: WHAT'S THE DIFFERENCE?
(9 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Social listening measures what people discuss online through metrics
like mentions, reach, and sentiment. Narrative Intelligence adds
context by revealing how topics are interpreted through different
narratives. It identifies who is shaping conversations and which
communities are driving or encountering them. The two approaches work
together to help teams understand why conversations matter. For
example, overall sentiment may appear neutral while a specific
narrative contains concentrated negative sentiment.
