 HALF OF US SHOPPERS VERIFY AI RECOMMENDATIONS ON REDDIT BEFORE THEY
BUY (3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 AI recommendations accelerate the start of a purchase decision but
don't remove the uncertainty around it, so shoppers are increasingly
turning to Reddit. Half of US shoppers and more than 1 in 3 in the UK
say they verify AI recommendations there, trusting the platform's
community more than they trust friends or family to check AI's
suggestions. Reading real, honest experiences on Reddit is now about
twice as likely to be the deciding factor in a purchase as reviews
from professional critics or influencers. More than 1 in 5 shoppers
now type Reddit directly into their search queries to find that human
perspective.

 ANTHROPIC'S CLAUDE WILL ADD WATERMARKS TO AI-GENERATED TEXT AND FILES
(3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Anthropic is adding invisible watermarks to Claude-generated text and
files, including images, to comply with EU transparency rules and help
identify AI-generated content. The markers are designed to survive
copying and editing, while images will include provenance metadata
that can reveal tampering. The system has limits, since heavy editing,
paraphrasing, translation, or screenshots can remove or prevent
watermarks.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 WIN REPORT: LESS FUNNEL, 26% MORE REVENUE (2 MINUTE READ) [10]

 A/B testing can unlock larger revenue gains by removing choices that
steer customers toward lower lifetime value outcomes. In this case,
sending paid traffic directly to in-person booking increased revenue
26%, even though online subscriptions declined. Research showed that
in-person customers converted better, stayed longer, and produced
higher LTV, making the higher value path more important than
maximizing initial conversion volume. The test shows that CRO can
require removing a successful funnel stage rather than making
incremental improvements to it.

 B2B GROWTH DOESN'T END AT CLOSED-WON (5 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Closed-won only grants access to a solution, not evidence that it's
working, and B2B growth increasingly depends on generating that
evidence fast. The strongest post-sale strategies identify the first
meaningful outcome during the sales process itself and carry it
directly into implementation, since confidence built in the first 30,
60, and 90 days shapes both renewal and expansion later. AWS' newer
partner program illustrates the shift at a bigger-company scale, tying
partner funding to documented customer outcomes rather than to the
initial deal alone.

 HOW TO TRACK IFRAME FORMS WITH META PIXEL (1 MINUTE READ) [13]

 If your forms are embedded via iFrame (Typeform, JotForm, Calendly,
etc.), standard Meta tracking won't work due to cross-origin
restrictions. There are 3 workarounds to make tracking possible:

 HOW CLOUDFLARE IS MAKING AI PAY FOR CONTENT (9 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Cloudflare is turning the reverse proxy position it holds in front of
a large share of the web into a payment counter for AI traffic. Its
Pay Per Crawl tool lets a site block, allow, or charge a flat fee per
crawl, but more than half of crawls from well-behaved bots just
re-fetch pages that haven't changed, so payment is shifting from the
crawl to the use. Web Bot Auth verifies a caller's identity with
cryptographic signatures, and x402 settles payment through the HTTP
402 status code before the origin ever responds.

 HOW TECHNOLOGY EXPANDS ONE PERSON'S CAPACITY (4 MINUTE READ) [16]

 AI and automation can increase marketing output, but they can also
weaken judgment when they remove the work that helps spot mistakes.
Automate tasks when there's a clear standard for evaluating the
output. Keep human oversight for decisions that require context, such
as customer segmentation and positioning. For each automated task,
define how a bad output will be caught before it reaches customers,
then turn that check into a repeatable process.

 WHY INDEED'S CMO STEERED ITS MARKETING STRATEGY TOWARDS BRAND
BUILDING (9 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Humor stopped working in job-search advertising once AI made the
application process feel dehumanizing on both sides, with job seekers
sending hundreds of applications into an AI-sorted void and employers
turning to AI screening tools they know strip out the personal
connection hiring still depends on. Indeed's response leans into
empathy instead, building campaigns around the idea that most of the
world's jobs - healthcare, education, retail, hospitality, and
construction - are still fundamentally human jobs. The same logic
reshaped who the company markets to, expanding from active job seekers
toward passive and future ones reached through cultural relevance
rather than search intent.
