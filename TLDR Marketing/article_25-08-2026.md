 DOUBLE-DIGIT ECOMMERCE GROWTH RETURNS (2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 US ecommerce sales grew 12.2% YoY to $340.2B in Q2 2026, marking a
second straight quarter of double-digit growth and outpacing overall
retail growth of 6.7%. Ecommerce now accounts for 17.1% of retail
sales. Growth varied sharply by category, with general merchandise up
21.6% while clothing rose just 3.8%. The acceleration suggests renewed
ecommerce momentum, though inflation and Amazon's earlier Prime Day
may have boosted Q2 figures.

 IS THE ERA OF THE 15,000 PLUS MARKETING TECHNOLOGY LANDSCAPE COMING
TO AN END? (21 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Consolidating a martech stack is less about cutting applications and
more about whether each tool provides unique value a broader platform
cannot. AI is making many single-purpose tools redundant by combining
functions that once required separate systems. However,
over-consolidation risks vendor lock-in and losing specialized
capabilities. The likely outcome is a smaller core of shared
platforms, with specialized tools kept only when they add clear value.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 HOW LONG SHOULD BRANDS TEST A CREATOR PARTNERSHIP? 28 CREATOR ECONOMY
EXPERTS WEIGH IN (17 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Brands should test creator partnerships broadly, then give creators
3-6 months or at least three activations to prove repeatability. A
single post measures content performance, not the partnership, while
short attribution windows can undercount conversions by 40-60%. Match
evaluation to the goal and sales cycle, then shift budget toward
creators showing brand fit and long-term potential.

 YOUR BEST CUSTOMERS MAY NOT BE YOUR MOST ENGAGED (5 MINUTE READ) [10]


 More engagement doesn't always mean more value. The right lifecycle
strategy starts by defining what healthy usage looks like for each
product. A dating app may succeed when users leave, while a utility
product may only need monthly use. Customer replies can also reveal
intent that clicks and opens miss. As automation makes it easier to
build and launch more campaigns, teams need stronger QA and guardrails
to manage the added complexity.

RESOURCES & TOOLS

 META'S AI-FIRST AD BUYING IS FORCING A NEW OPERATING MODEL FOR PAID
SOCIAL TEAMS (4 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Meta's AI-first ad buying is reducing the need for manual campaign
optimization and shifting paid social teams toward managing the inputs
that guide the system. Clean conversion signals become critical
because they shape how Meta optimizes campaigns. Creative production
also becomes a bigger performance lever as targeting gets automated.
Teams will need to rethink workflows and governance as fewer campaign
decisions remain under direct human control.

 SAAS DATA MOATS: WHY PROPRIETARY DATA IS THE STRONGEST COMPETITIVE
ADVANTAGE IN 2026 (8 MINUTE READ) [12]

 As AI makes software features easier to replicate, proprietary data
is becoming a stronger competitive moat. The advantage comes from data
that is unique and improves the product with each customer
interaction. A data flywheel can make this advantage compound as more
usage creates better insights. However, data only becomes a moat at
sufficient scale and with strong governance. Otherwise, it can become
an expensive liability rather than a defensible advantage.

 SERVED VS SEEN: WHICH BRAND ARE YOU? (4 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Brands are optimizing for impressions and media efficiency while
business growth declines. Being served an ad doesn't mean it was seen
or remembered. Test creative organically on social platforms before
investing in paid media, then put spend behind content that has
already proven it can earn attention.

 MARKETERS, THE FREE TOKEN LUNCH IS OVER. IS YOUR STACK READY? (3
MINUTE READ) [16]

 AI costs are changing as models use more tokens for reasoning and
companies deploy them across more tasks. The biggest model isn't
always the most economical choice. Simple decisions can use cheaper
models, while complex problems may justify higher-cost reasoning
models. The key metric is shifting from token price to the cost of
getting the right answer. AI stacks need to match each task with the
right model, rather than defaulting to one model for everything.
