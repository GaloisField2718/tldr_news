NEWS & TRENDS

 CHATGPT ALREADY KNOWS WHO IT'LL RECOMMEND BEFORE IT SEARCHES (12
MINUTE READ) [5]

 A study of 60 ChatGPT conversations found that brands appearing in
ChatGPT's initial search queries were cited 33X more often than brands
discovered through retrieval, with 68.9% vs. 2.1% mention rates. In 21
of 27 conversations, ChatGPT named brands before fetching search
results. Brands that consistently appeared in these queries were more
likely to earn citations, while retrieved pages had only a 3.1%
citation rate overall.

 THE WEB'S NEWEST WEAPON AGAINST AI SCRAPERS IS A FONT (4 MINUTE READ)
[6]

 ShieldFont swaps out an average of 24.5% of a page's words, and 45.8%
of its content words, into subtly wrong substitutes that only appear
in the raw HTML source scrapers pull down, while human readers still
see the correct rendered text. The swaps use words with a similar part
of speech but an unrelated meaning rather than obvious synonyms or
gibberish, since a smart filter can catch either of those. Tested
across six scraper pipelines, more than 90% of ShieldFont pages were
rejected outright, and the small share that got through still carried
real, correctly spelled sentences that assert nothing true.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 WE ADDED AN INSTAGRAM-STYLE STORY RAIL TO THE HOMEPAGE (1 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 An ecommerce brand added an Instagram Stories-style row to its
homepage, placing circular, horizontally scrollable product bubbles
above the hero section. Each bubble featured a product image and an
outcome-focused label like “Lean Protein”, “Gut Health” or
“Clean Energy” and linked to the relevant collection. The change
increased conversion rate by 15.44% and collection page views by
6.51%, while AOV stayed flat.

 TEARDOWN OF KATELYN BOURGOIN'S NEWSLETTER LANDING PAGE (1 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Katelyn's Unignorable newsletter landing page uses a single-focus
design with no navigation or archive links. The email form stays above
the fold on desktop and mobile and only asks for an email address. It
also uses subscriber count and photos with recognizable figures as
social proof, plus a specific value proposition to differentiate the
newsletter. Two suggested improvements were a more compelling CTA and
an immediate welcome gift to increase sign-ups.

 A competitor analysis prompt can identify differentiators and turn

 24 MCP WORKFLOWS TO BRING YOUR GTM STACK INTO CLAUDE (27 MINUTE READ)
[10]

 MCPs can turn Claude from a standalone chatbot into a command center
for GTM work by connecting it to the tools, data, and workflows teams
already use. The most useful setup combines multiple MCPs, reusable
skills, and scheduled routines so Claude can pull live context,
analyze data, create assets, and write results back into systems of
record. The showcased workflows span campaign planning, CRM analysis,
SEO updates, AI search monitoring, personalized landing pages,
performance reporting, and unstructured customer research, showing how
teams can automate work that normally requires jumping between tools.
The broader strategy is to keep purpose-built GTM tools as the system
of record while using MCPs to make their capabilities accessible
through Claude.

 IS YOUR BRAND MISSING OUT ON THE FASTEST-GROWING SOURCE IN AI SEARCH?
(6 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Creator and social content is becoming a larger source of citations
in AI search as owned brand pages lose ground. AirOps analyzed 3.5B
citations and found creator/social citation share grew 140% from
August 2025 to June 2026, while brand.com/product citations fell 10%.
YouTube drove most of the growth, with its citation share rising 158%.
The impact varies by industry and AI engine, but creator content is
becoming an important source of evidence for AI-generated answers.

 BLOOM NUTRITION MARKETING AND TIKTOK GROWTH STRATEGY (4 MINUTE READ)
[12]

 Bloom Nutrition's founders describe their approach as a second-mover
advantage, taking an already-understood category and out-executing on
flavor, branding, and community instead of inventing something new. It
rebuilt energy drinks, long marketed to men, around a soothing
self-care ritual for Gen Z and millennial women, then carried that
positioning into convenience stores with no shelf space left for
another energy brand. Rather than compete with Monster and Red Bull on
volume, it pitched operators on the female foot traffic they were
missing, landing first at Texas travel-center chain Buc-ee's before
7-Eleven and Circle K followed.

 THE FIRST DRAFT IS FREE. THE FINAL JUDGMENT IS NOT (8 MINUTE READ)
[13]

 AI operates on what researchers call a jagged frontier, capable
enough on some tasks to make people faster and more accurate, then
unexpectedly unreliable on a similarly difficult task next to it. The
failure mode isn't an obviously broken draft - it's a plausible one,
since AI output now arrives organized, confident, and complete enough
that reviewing it feels like editing instead of investigating. In one
study, accuracy on a harder business problem fell by up to 24
percentage points among consultants using AI, even though the same
tool had earlier produced dramatic productivity gains on easier tasks.
