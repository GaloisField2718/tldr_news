NEWS & TRENDS

 CREATORS NOW RIVAL SEARCH FOR GEN Z PRODUCT DISCOVERY (3 MINUTE READ)
[7]

 33% of Gen Z now discover products through creator posts, 4 points
behind online search, and 67% bought something in the past year
because an influencer endorsed it. Small creators convert as often as
famous ones, since buyers weigh honesty and actual product use well
above popularity. Trust in influencers among Gen Z and millennials has
risen 13 points since 2019 as trust in friends and family fell,
cutting the gap between them from 33 points to 15.

 SHOPPING HABITS BY GENERATION: WHAT BRANDS NEED TO KNOW (7 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Shopping habits vary by generation, shaping where consumers discover
products and how they buy. 66% of shoppers purchased products
discovered on social media, rising to 79% for Gen Z and 76% for
Millennials. Younger shoppers respond to creator content and AI
recommendations, while Boomers favor trusted retailers and in-store
shopping.

 8 TIPS TO IMPROVE SEO & AEO (2 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Improve SEO and AEO by targeting high-intent customer questions and
tracking Google rankings, leads, and AI mentions. Fix technical issues
and refresh pages with low click-through rates. Publish weekly content
with original examples and verified information. Link articles to
relevant product or service pages. Build credibility through customer
reviews, consistent business listings, and industry mentions. Review
performance monthly and prioritize content that drives qualified leads
and sales.

 GEO IS A DISTRACTION (6 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Optimizing for ChatGPT citations and other LLM mentions is a
distraction because AI tools generated just 0.73% of Google Organic's
traffic across 100 websites, while Google Organic accounted for 96.5%
of tracked conversions. Focus on conversions and user satisfaction
across Google Search, including AI Overviews and AI Mode, rather than
chasing AI visibility metrics. Longer term, there are opportunities in
agentic commerce through Google Merchant Center and Universal Cart,
the Universal Commerce Protocol for agent-led purchases, WebMCP tools
that let AI agents use website functions, and smart eyewear that
enables new shopping and service interactions. You can begin preparing
by improving product data and experimenting with agent accessible
tools as these technologies develop.

 YOUR NEWSLETTER HAS TWO READERS NOW (5 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Your newsletter now has two readers: the human subscriber and the AI
that may summarize, prioritize, or retrieve your email on the
subscriber's behalf. AI summaries in Apple Mail, Outlook, and Yahoo
can replace your preview text or condense your content, potentially
changing what readers notice and whether they click. A Twilio and
Inbox Monster test found that subject lines and live text near the top
of an email helped shape Apple's summaries, while images and alt text
provided less useful information. Test how your newsletter appears in
these inboxes, make your main stories and sponsor messages clear in
live text, and measure engagement through multiple signals rather than
relying on open rates alone.

 COMMISSION ISN'T ENOUGH: 57 CREATORS SAY THEY WANT MORE FROM
AFFILIATE PROGRAMS (4 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Affiliate programs are more effective when brands treat creators as
long-term partners rather than relying on commissions alone. Although
68% of surveyed creators still accept affiliate commissions, many say
the rates don't justify the work without additional payment, product
gifting, and brand support. 72% of creators said brands don't provide
new products often enough, and most prefer monthly gifting. Creators
also want more hands-on management, including regular communication,
performance reviews, and promotional resources. Tiered commissions,
bonuses, and ongoing relationship management can help brands keep
affiliates active and build sustained revenue.
