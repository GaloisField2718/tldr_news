NEWS & TRENDS

 "GAMERS DON'T HATE ADS; THEY HATE BAD ADS" – WHY EA AND XBOX ARE
PUTTING ADVERTS IN THEIR GAMES (7 MINUTE READ) [8]

 In-game advertising offers publishers a growing revenue stream, but
player experience determines its success. EA plans to sell ads
integrated into game environments, while Xbox is testing ads before
streaming sessions in exchange for free access. Mobile in-game ads
generated $28B in 2025, but console and PC players are more resistant.
Research shows 1 in 5 players would play more with high-quality ads,
while 1 in 5 have quit due to poor ad experiences.

 AI SEARCH META ANALYSIS - Q4 2026 (11 MINUTE READ) [9]

 AI Overviews are appearing on more commercial searches, with one
study finding coverage across 88% of its sampled keywords. Their
presence can reduce organic clicks by up to 40%. AI platforms are also
influencing brand discovery and product shortlists, though visibility
doesn't always translate into website visits or purchases. Consumers
remain less comfortable letting AI agents complete purchases.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 HOW TO NOT DO PR (10 MINUTE READ) [10]

 PR campaigns need a newsworthy headline backed by substantial data.
Focus each article on one story and use clear charts to visualize
findings. Add your own commentary and secure expert quotes to
strengthen credibility and encourage sharing. Include methodology and
contact details to build trust. For outreach, find relevant
journalists through recent Google News coverage. Start with niche
publications and pitch on Tuesday, then follow up on Friday.

 LINKEDIN SAYS INFLUENTIAL VOICES ARE CRITICAL FOR AI CHATBOT
CITATIONS (3 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Accounts with over 10K followers and company pages make up 65% of
LinkedIn citations in AI chatbot responses. Executive and VP-level
articles also earn more citations per article. LinkedIn recommends
building authority through consistent employee posts across 3-4 themes
and partnerships with 1-2 relevant creators.

 USER REQUIREMENT BOX IN CHATGPT (1 MINUTE READ) [15]

 ChatGPT is adding “User Requirement” boxes to commercial prompts,
asking users to select criteria like budget and product features
before generating recommendations. These questions reveal what buyers
consider when evaluating solutions. SEOs can use them to identify
common buying criteria and align product pages and help documentation
with the information ChatGPT uses to recommend products.

 MCP FOR MARKETERS: WHAT MODEL CONTEXT PROTOCOL MEANS FOR MEDIA AND
MEASUREMENT (10 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Google, Amazon, and TikTok now offer MCP integrations that let AI
applications retrieve advertising data and manage campaigns. This
overview explains how MCP works and compares what each platform's
integrations allow agents to do. It examines how inconsistent
attribution creates gaps in cross-channel measurement and how
independent data can improve budget decisions. It also covers 6 stages
of agent adoption, from answering performance questions to executing
campaign changes with defined permissions and human oversight.

MISCELLANEOUS

 HOW DO YOU ADVERTISE AI TO AN INCREASINGLY SKEPTICAL PUBLIC? (4
MINUTE READ) [17]

 Consumer ads are moving away from the term “AI” as it risks
alienating skeptical audiences. Instead, campaigns focus on product
benefits without mentioning the technology. Messaging works best when
it stays neutral and avoids framing AI as either revolutionary or
something to apologize for.

 PERMISSION IS A USEFUL MARKETING SIGNAL (2 MINUTE READ) [18]

 An informational newsletter offers a simple way to gauge whether
content is resonating. When someone subscribes, they give permission
for ongoing communication, signaling that your content has earned
their interest. Tracking sign-ups helps identify where potential
customers are engaging or losing interest without relying on complex
attribution tools. Newsletters can also fuel social posts and resource
offers that encourage more subscriptions.
