NEWS & TRENDS

 CONVENIENCE OR DISCOVERY: WHICH MISSION WILL YOUR STORE SERVE? (12
MINUTE READ) [7]

 AI is splitting physical retail into two distinct missions.
Convenience stores should reduce friction with easy navigation and
reactive service. Discovery stores should drive dwell time through
immersive experiences and specialized associates. More than 90% of
consumers prioritize locations that fit their daily routines, while
78% cite product testing and 76% cite product guidance as drivers of
discovery visits.

 WORKERS ARE WARMING UP TO AI - BUT GEN Z REMAINS SKEPTICAL (4 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 AI confidence is growing across the workforce, with 53% of US workers
saying AI makes them more efficient. Gen Z remains more hesitant. Only
38% plan to build AI skills in the next 6 months, while 52% are
nervous about AI's impact on their jobs. At the same time, AI literacy
is becoming more valuable in the workplace. LinkedIn finds it is
associated with stronger hiring and promotion outcomes for Gen Z.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 AUDIT IF YOUR FAQ IS DISCOVERABLE BY SEARCH ENGINES AND LLMS (2
MINUTE READ) [9]

 FAQs can improve visibility in search and AI systems only if the
content is present in the page source when it loads. Many FAQ
components hide their text until a user clicks to expand them, which
means search engines and LLMs may not access the content by default.
Use Chrome DevTools to check whether FAQ text remains discoverable
after a page reload.

 CHATGPT READS YOUR YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION, NOT YOUR TRANSCRIPT (1 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Treat YouTube descriptions like landing pages for AI search. ChatGPT
can retrieve a video's title, metadata, and full description, but not
its transcript. That makes the description a key source for
understanding the video. Include relevant terms and clear context. Use
structured sections and timecodes so ChatGPT can understand what the
video covers and where specific topics appear.

 THERE IS NO AEO ALGORITHM TO HACK (4 MINUTE READ) [11]

 AEO is not an SEO ranking game with factors to reverse engineer. AI
visibility depends on whether a brand is clearly associated with a
category and supported by credible evidence. Fix positioning gaps and
answer real buyer questions. Build comparison content that helps
buyers evaluate vendors. Strengthen third-party corroboration instead
of manufacturing mentions. Then track commercial prompts and connect
AI visibility to buyer conversations and pipeline, not just brand
mentions.

 ALMOST NOBODY GETS XML SITEMAPS RIGHT (4 MINUTE READ) [13]

 XML sitemaps tell search engines which URLs a site wants in search
results. They should contain canonical, working URLs that the site
wants indexed. Google attempts to crawl each URL as listed, so
redirects, errors, noindex pages, or conflicting canonicals make the
sitemap less useful and can waste crawl activity. Missing or repeated
‘lastmod' dates can be acceptable. Fields like ‘priority' and
‘changefreq' are ignored by Google.

 DOES REDDIT HAVE AN ASTROTURFING PROBLEM? (8 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Reddit is often treated as a source of unbiased product advice.
However, some buying recommendations are unusually concentrated among
a small group of accounts. The top 5% of brand-heavy accounts produced
11.3% of knife recommendations, versus 7.9% expected by chance. For
three brands, that share reached 20% to 31%. This could indicate
astroturfing, where paid accounts create the appearance of organic
recommendations, but their histories looked more like genuine fans.

 THE USEFULNESS OF ATTRIBUTION FOR AI SEARCH (6 MINUTE READ) [15]

 AI is making click-based attribution less useful as discovery
increasingly happens through AI answers that never generate a website
visit. Treat attribution as one imperfect signal rather than a
definitive measure of channel value. Triangulate exposure, behavioral,
and business metrics such as AI Share of Voice and CRM data. Use
incrementality testing where possible to measure causal impact,
especially for activities that attribution cannot capture.

 DISSECTING WHY CANVA IS PARTNERING WITH JIO (5 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Canva is using Jio's 524.4M customers to tackle a freemium problem.
Many casual users get enough value from Free to justify paying for
Pro. Bundling Pro with Jio plans lets users experience premium
features without making a separate purchase. The real test is what
happens after the bundle ends. Canva needs to see whether users build
habits around Pro and become willing to pay for it.
