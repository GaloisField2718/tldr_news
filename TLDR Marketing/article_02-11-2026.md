 MAJORITY OF TEENS AND TWEENS NOW USE CHAT BOTS, NEW RESEARCH FINDS (2
MINUTE READ) [6]

 AI chatbots are becoming a normal part of life for American teens and
tweens. Nearly half of 9-12-year-olds have bypassed the 13+ age
restrictions. Students use chatbots for homework and emotional
support. A separate study of 5,000+ students found that using AI
during emotional distress is associated with worse mental health
outcomes. Researchers have not established whether AI contributes to
these issues or attracts students already struggling.

 AI SEARCH IS GROWING FASTER THAN ITS BUSINESS MODEL (2 MINUTE READ)
[7]

 AI search is growing faster than platforms can monetize it, prompting
Google and OpenAI to expand conversational ads. Google search queries
grew at their fastest rate in 5 years while ad revenue growth slowed
to 12%. More ads could erode trust in AI-generated answers, which 70%
of regular users in the US and UK currently trust.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 CHUNKING YOUR CONTENT FOR AGENT READABILITY (13 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Chunking breaks documentation into smaller passages that AI agents
retrieve independently, so each section needs to make sense without
the full page. Keep sections under your retrieval system's token limit
(Google's Agent Search defaults to 500 tokens) and use specific
headings with proper HTML tags. Break up long tables and procedures
into smaller sections. Keep code samples self-contained and describe
important images in text. Test how your content is actually chunked
using an agent skill or retrieval system API.

 RUN YOUR TONGUE ACROSS YOUR TEETH (5 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Claude Hopkins built Pepsodent's campaign around a problem customers
could recognize, using a 3 second test to demonstrate sticky film on
teeth. He offered free samples with instructions to test the
toothpaste's effectiveness. Unique coupon codes helped track headline
performance and revealed that leading with “free” reduced response
by 75%. He also tested campaigns in one city before expanding
nationally.

 HOW TO TEST INCREMENTALITY BEFORE PEAK SPENDING STARTS (3 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Run incrementality tests before peak season to identify which
marketing channels actually generate new sales rather than taking
credit for purchases that would happen anyway. Use geographic or
audience holdouts to compare exposed customers against groups that
don't see a channel. Aim for 20-30% holdouts and run tests for 4-8
weeks. Audit UTMs and tracking before launch. Use incremental ROAS and
new customer acquisition to reallocate budget toward channels that
drive genuine lift rather than those reporting the highest last-click
revenue.

 HOW WE RUN OUR NEWSLETTERS ON BEEHIIV'S MCP (6 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Strategy Breakdowns uses beehiiv's MCP integration with Claude Code
to automate newsletter production. Claude analyzes hundreds of past
A/B tests to identify winning subject line patterns. Testing 4
variations before sending to the full list increased open rates by at
least 1.24 percentage points. It also segments subscribers and
researches topics using trusted chart sources. A custom dashboard
called SB Dash connects these workflows and automates sponsor
reporting. This cut production tasks from 2-3 hours per edition to 10
minutes a day. Human editors still write every word.

 THE WEBSITE GO‑LIVE CHECKLIST: 5 CRITICAL CHECKS BEYOND GOOD LOOKS
(5 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Before launching a website, review it from the customer's perspective
to ensure the offer is clear and visitors can easily find information
and take action. Check that the design works on mobile without
unnecessary scrolling or distracting elements. Score clarity,
branding, and usability against specific criteria rather than relying
on subjective feedback. Use tools like Loom or Tella to record and
track revisions.

MISCELLANEOUS

 NOBODY YET KNOWS HOW TO MEASURE AN AD INSIDE A CHATBOT ANSWER (7
MINUTE READ) [14]

 Ads in ChatGPT and Google AI Mode reach consumers while they're
expressing purchase intent, but neither platform can prove they
generate incremental sales. Conversion tracking shows which purchases
follow an ad but not whether they would have happened anyway. Facebook
research found observational measurement can misstate results by 3X or
more. Treat AI ads as an experimental channel and use geographic
holdouts to measure their actual impact until platforms offer
randomized lift studies.

 WHY AI FAILS IN MARKETING: THE ORGANIZATIONAL PROBLEM LEADERS
OVERLOOK (4 MINUTE READ) [15]

 AI in marketing mostly fails for organizational reasons, not
technical ones. Marketing owns revenue and retention targets but
rarely controls pricing or product, so AI insights go unused. The
highest-value uses also depend on sales, finance, and product sharing
goals and data. Judged on cost savings alone, marketing AI tools just
makes ineffective activities cheaper, not more effective.
