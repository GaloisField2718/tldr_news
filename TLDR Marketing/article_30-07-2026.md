 INFLUENCER MARKETING DIDN'T BLOW UP, FIVE YEARS OF DEAL DATA SHOWS IT
MATURED INSTEAD (5 MINUTE READ) [4]

 A newly released report based on five years of influencer deal data
shows that influencer marketing has matured into a more structured and
efficient channel. Average creator contract values grew from $3,065 in
2019 to $7,400 in 2025 while average deal timelines fell 36% as
contracts and campaign processes became more standardized. Brands get
better results from long-term creator relationships because 24% of
partnerships now include repeat engagements, and repeated campaigns
build audience trust over time. Micro-creators often generate stronger
revenue per follower while larger creators still deliver greater
overall reach.

 THE 100X CMO JOB IS HERE. CLICKUP IS PAYING UP TO $1M FOR ONE PERSON
TO BE THE ENTIRE MARKETING DEPARTMENT (5 MINUTE READ) [5]

 ClickUp is hiring a $500K - $1M solo CMO to run its marketing
function using AI agents, as companies rethink org structures. While
Fortune 500 firms are eliminating the CMO role, ClickUp is
consolidating it into one high-leverage operator focused on systems
and judgment. The role shows how AI compresses execution and raises
the value of taste and decision-making. It also introduces risks
around scale and reliance on a single individual.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 SEO TIP: TRACK THE CHANGES YOUR COMPETITORS ARE MAKING ON G2 /
CAPTERRA FOR AEO (1 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Find a competitor profile on G2 or Capterra, open it in Ahrefs Page
Inspect, and compare the current version to one from 6 to 12 months
earlier to see every content change. Use those edits to spot new
product descriptions, structured feature sections, and outcome-focused
messaging that can improve AI search visibility. Apply the strongest
ideas to your own third-party profiles instead of guessing what works.


 7 TIPS TO WINNING ON PRODUCT HUNT (2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Being featured on Product Hunt now depends less on total upvotes and
more on consistency and credibility. Schedule your launch early to get
featured, secure an experienced hunter, and activate supporters in
advance with real engagement. Avoid asking for upvotes directly, since
coordinated behavior gets penalized. Focus on generating thoughtful
comments and replying quickly. Stagger promotion across time zones to
maintain steady momentum. Skip paid upvotes, as detection systems can
remove votes or derail your entire launch.

 17 MARKETING TASKS YOU CAN STOP DOING MANUALLY (6 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Automatable marketing tasks repeat in a predictable pattern and stay
easy to review before anything public goes out. Meeting recaps, search
term sorting, pulling campaign summaries, and customer feedback
clustering are all automatable tasks that can consume a lot of time
but require little original thinking. An estimated 10 to 15% of a
marketer's time can already be automated with current technology.

 ANALYZE YOUR SOCIAL AND VIDEO PLATFORM CONTENT PERFORMANCE IN SEARCH
CONSOLE (5 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Google Search Console now tracks how social and video content
performs in Google Search. It gives you search queries, top content,
countries, devices, and traffic trends across platforms like TikTok,
Instagram, X, and YouTube. Use this data to spot rising search terms,
compare formats such as Shorts versus full-length videos or Reels
versus posts, and repurpose content that already attracts search
traffic. Track title and caption updates over time and promote content
across platforms when search interest starts to grow.

 SO, YOU WANT TO BE A CONTENT CREATOR? (9 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Content creators build stronger audiences by sharing real expertise
around a specific niche instead of chasing viral posts or engagement
metrics. The author grew a LinkedIn audience from a few thousand
connections to 40,000 in one year by posting original frameworks,
contrarian ideas, and patterns from real work, then expanded to nearly
100,000 newsletter subscribers by owning distribution outside social
platforms. Consistent engagement in the comments and content rooted in
firsthand experience created trust and opened new revenue through
subscriptions and sponsorships. Audience growth creates business
opportunities, but long-term success depends on credibility,
authenticity, and solving real problems instead of optimizing for
impressions.

 WHY DOORDASH, INSTACART, AND UBER EATS INTEGRATED LLMS INTO SEARCH
THREE DIFFERENT WAYS (9 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Food delivery search breaks classical keyword matching in every way
at once, from synonyms and typos to compound constraints like "vegan
chicken sandwich", which is why DoorDash, Instacart, and Uber Eats
each rebuilt it around LLMs differently. DoorDash uses an LLM to
enrich an existing knowledge graph offline, then constrains query
parsing to only the labels that graph already knows, lifting dish
carousel triggers by roughly 30%. Instacart fine-tuned a Llama-3-8B
model to handle rare tail queries in real time under 300ms, cutting
scroll depth 6% and tail-query complaints in half. Uber Eats went
furthest, embedding every query and document through a fine-tuned Qwen
model shared across every vertical, market, and language.

 PRODUCTS ‘MADE BY MISTAKE' SELL MORE (3 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Accidental product flaws can become a marketing asset when a brand
presents them as unique instead of hiding them. Across seven
experiments, people were 27.3% more likely to buy a product with an
accidental flaw and were willing to pay up to 71.8% more in some
cases. This approach works best for established brands that sell
products for enjoyment rather than function because uniqueness
increases appeal in those categories. Pairing the mistake with a clear
explanation and a limited-time promotion can help turn excess
inventory into higher demand.
