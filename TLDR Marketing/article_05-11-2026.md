 CHATGPT HAS HIT ROUGHLY 20% OF GOOGLE SEARCH VOLUME (1 MINUTE READ)
[8]

 OpenAI says ChatGPT search volume has reached roughly 20% of Google
Search's volume, making it Google's biggest search competitor yet. The
estimate counts prompts that trigger a web search and may not be
directly comparable to Google's search figures. ChatGPT's growth is
driving Google to expand AI features in Search. OpenAI also says users
value ChatGPT's personalization and is considering expanding its use
of ads.

 IS B2B SAAS STILL BLOGGING? (7 MINUTE READ) [9]

 An analysis of blog posts from 219 mid-market B2B SaaS companies
found total publishing volume across 187 companies increased 37% YoY
and 151% since ChatGPT launched. Half of companies published more
posts this year while median output rose from 42 to 62 posts annually.
52% of posts were how-tos, explainers, or comparisons. Only 2.4%
contained original research or perspectives. Posts were also 55%
longer, and posts per author increased 73%.

 HTML VS. XML SITEMAPS FOR ECOMMERCE (2 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Ecommerce sites should use both XML and HTML sitemaps to improve page
discovery, crawling, and indexing. XML sitemaps automatically list
every URL intended for indexing, while HTML sitemaps provide users and
search engines with links to important categories, subcategories, and
site pages. HTML sitemaps can also strengthen internal linking and
help reduce the number of pages that Google crawls but does not index.
Neither replaces a well-organized site hierarchy or strong internal
linking, but HTML sitemaps can serve as a temporary solution during
site restructures.

 WHICH TYPES OF MENTIONS CORRELATE THE STRONGEST WITH AI VISIBILITY?
(9 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Off-site brand mentions correlate with visibility in ChatGPT, AI
Mode, and AI Overviews. Reddit mentions had the strongest correlation
(0.75), ahead of general web mentions (0.70) and press coverage
(0.67). Brands with 10X more Reddit mentions typically received 5.5X
more AI mentions, while 10X more webpage mentions correlated with 3.7X
more. These are correlations, not proof that mentions cause AI
visibility.

 AI AGENT MESSAGING: WHAT 1,014 SOFTWARE BUYERS WANT (5 MINUTE READ)
[16]

 Describing an AI product as a team of specialized agents generates
more purchase interest than describing it as a single agent. A survey
of 1,014 software buyers found that team framing increased purchase
intent by 6.3 percentage points compared to single-agent framing.
However, calling a product an agent alone did not increase purchase
interest and slightly reduced it. Buyers also perceived products
described as multiple agents as harder to use, while perceptions of
helpfulness and trustworthiness remained largely unchanged. The
findings suggest that breaking a product into distinct functions can
increase its perceived value through a psychological phenomenon known
as the unpacking effect.

 THE GROWTH PLAYBOOK EVERYONE KEEPS USING (9 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Growth teams keep recycling the same tactics, like price increases,
upmarket moves, and big launches. Most have worked at some point,
which is why they return every few years under new names. The trouble
is that they're often used to avoid fixing what's broken in the
product or the company. Tactics are easy to assign owners and report
progress on, while core problems don't fit a quarterly roadmap. Strong
growth leaders stand out by knowing which problems are worth solving,
then picking tactics that fit.
