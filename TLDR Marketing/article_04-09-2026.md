 US COURT RULES GOOGLE WILL NOT HAVE TO SELL AD EXCHANGE AFTER LOSING
ANTITRUST CASE (2 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Google's ad tech dominance will largely remain intact despite the DOJ
proving that it illegally restricted competition in parts of the
online ad market. The court rejected the government's push to force
Google to sell its ad exchange, avoiding a remedy that could have
disrupted its broader advertising business. Google will likely face
fines and business practice changes, but the exact penalties remain
sealed for now. The outcome shows that even after three major
antitrust losses, Google retains substantial control over digital
advertising and can continue investing aggressively in emerging
markets such as AI.

 ‘A CRISIS OF AUTHENTICITY': THE TRUTH ABOUT FOOD INFLUENCERS (18
MINUTE READ) [6]

 Positive coverage in food influencing is less a preference than an
economic default, since restaurants that stop offering free meals or
payment quickly see coverage dry up. That system still drives real
reach, with Instagram Reels revenue growing from about $2 billion in
2022 to a projected $50 billion and roughly half of Instagram's time
now spent on them. Most restaurant influencers still don't get paid
well, with typical per-post rates staying in the low hundreds to low
thousands and Instagram not paying for views at all. Viewers have
noticed the pattern, and experts predict today's relentlessly positive
accounts will lose ground to more critical voices.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 WIN REPORT: HOW SUPPLY-SIDE CRO LIFTED SIGN-UPS BY 15% (2 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 Supply-side CRO can unlock major marketplace growth by optimizing for
the actual intent of people who provide capacity, not just the
customers who buy. A tutoring marketplace found that many tutor
recruitment visitors came from job search sites, so it replaced a
generic registration form with a real student job request, clear job
details, pay information, and “apply” language. The change
increased tutor applications by 15%, or about 20,000 additional tutors
in the first year. Supply-side improvements may not show immediate
revenue, but they can strengthen marketplace capacity and create an
overlooked source of high-impact conversion gains.

 THE IMPACT OF CONTENT FRESHNESS ON AI CITATIONS (9 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Content cited by AI assistants runs 25.7% fresher than what ranks
organically on Google, with roughly half of all citations tracing to
material updated in the last 13 weeks. That edge decays fast, with a
median citation half-life of about 4.5 weeks across platforms.
Retrieval systems re-rank the entire candidate pool on every query
rather than relying on a persistent ranking, so recency becomes an
early filter for weeding out sources that might already be wrong.
Perplexity and Grok weigh it most heavily, while Google's AI Overviews
stay tied to core search and actually skew slightly older.

 CAPTURE ATTENTION THAT LASTS WITH 15-SECOND ENGAGED VIDEO VIEWS (3
MINUTE READ) [9]

 Reddit launched a 15-second Engaged Video Views goal that optimizes
video ads toward users likely to watch for at least 15 seconds or to
completion. It's designed for longer-form creative such as product
demos and testimonials, with advertisers paying only for qualifying
views. Alpha testers saw average gains of 51% in 15-second
view-through rate and 71% in completion rate. The feature is now
available globally in public beta, with split testing available to
compare it against 6-second views.

 BUILD VS. BUY: MARKETING TOOLS IN THE VIBE CODE ERA (6 MINUTE READ)
[10]

 AI is changing how marketing teams should think about build vs. buy.
Teams should build when customization or proprietary data creates an
advantage, and buy when vendors handle complex infrastructure that's
expensive to maintain. They also warn against letting vibe-coded tools
become one-off projects that only one person uses. When evaluating
software, ask what would break if you canceled it and whether you're
buying the product or simply a way to organize data you already own.

 CREATORS AS A SALES ENGINE: HOW CHINA IS RETHINKING THE ENTIRE
CUSTOMER JOURNEY (5 MINUTE READ) [11]

 China is showing how creators are becoming part of the sales
infrastructure, not just a communications channel. On platforms like
Rednote, creator content can intercept search, shape product research,
trigger retargeting, and drive purchases through livestreams and
in-platform ecommerce. The result is a more connected customer journey
where content, search, community, and commerce work together rather
than as separate channels. As platforms like TikTok expand into
commerce, this model offers a preview of how social could take on more
of the customer journey in Western markets.

 STICKI ROLLS IS TURNING STICKERS INTO THE NEW SOCIAL CURRENCY (4
MINUTE READ) [12]

 Sticki Rolls turns stickers into social currency by designing the
product around sharing and self-expression. It has expanded into
bracelets, sticker books, pendants, and bag charms that fans can
collect and show off. The brand also works with creators who shaped
its original collections and uses unexpected spaces like nail salons
to bring the product into beauty and fashion. Its pop-ups further turn
fans into participants, with activities like sticker manicures and BFF
bracelet-making.

 SKIMS MARKETING STRATEGY: KIM KARDASHIAN'S BILLION-DOLLAR BRAND (5
MINUTE READ) [13]

 Behind SKIMS' celebrity headlines is a disciplined owned-channel
operation, with the brand sending roughly five emails a week
announcing drops, restocks, and retail collaborations. Before a major
collection ships, SKIMS also seeds unboxing packages to thousands of
micro-influencers across a range of body types and skin tones,
producing unedited fit videos that build trust faster than a polished
ad could. Both tactics are cheap, repeatable, and aimed at an audience
that already opted in, the opposite of the celebrity-cash-grab
reputation the brand is often given credit for.
