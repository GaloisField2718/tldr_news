NEWS & TRENDS

 LONELY PLANET: WHY ARE WE ADDICTED TO THE SOCIAL MEDIA SCROLL WHEN IT
IS SO CLEARLY HARMING US? (4 MINUTE READ) [6]

 People keep scrolling because social media exploits a tension between
connection and loneliness. While feeds create the feeling of being
connected through endless updates, curated lives and online
communities often leave people feeling more isolated and inadequate.
The scroll is reinforced by FOMO, habit and the search for belonging,
even when it worsens feelings of emptiness. Many users stay because
these platforms offer brief moments of connection and entertainment,
despite the emotional cost of constant comparison and digital
dependence.

 AI IS BECOMING A SECOND BRAIN AT THE EXPENSE OF YOUR FIRST ONE (8
MINUTE READ) [7]

 Belief offloading describes what happens when people let AI perform
the labor of forming a judgment instead of testing an idea against
experience, values, and other people's pushback. A chatbot delivers a
calm, complete-sounding answer within seconds, creating the feeling of
having reached a conclusion without doing that work. AI can strengthen
memory and function as a second brain, but repeated reliance risks
weakening the first brain responsible for judgment and accountability.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 SEO TEST: WE INFLUENCED MAJOR LLMS TO POSITION US EXACTLY HOW WE WANT
(1 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Consistent brand messaging across owned and third-party channels can
influence how AI search describes a brand. In this test, updates to
the homepage, About page, LinkedIn profiles, and other sources led
most major LLMs to describe the company as serving B2B technology
companies with $20M+ ARR within one week. That consistency can help AI
recommend a brand to the right audience when it compares vendors.
Smaller companies may see faster results because they have fewer
conflicting signals across the web.

 USE AI BOTS TO REPLY TO FOLLOWERS (3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Using a clearly labeled AI bot to reply to social media comments can
increase future engagement if the replies are timely, relevant, and
match the brand or creator's voice. In a study of more than 22,000
follower interactions, people who received an AI reply commented 41%
more often on later posts and reposted content 43% more often than
people who received no reply. The strongest results came when the AI
responded within 45 minutes and fit the conversation. Test AI replies
on platforms that allow them and reserve personal or sensitive
conversations for human responses.

 HOW REDDIT USES COMMUNITY CONVERSATIONS TO IMPROVE AD TARGETING (4
MINUTE READ) [10]

 Reddit is turning community conversations into a core advertising
signal across targeting, creative, campaign optimization, and
commerce. It uses AI to match ads and products to discussion context,
generate Reddit-specific creative, and automate campaign decisions.
Early results show Max campaigns cut cost per acquisition by 15% and
increased result volume by 28%, while Redditor Highlights increased
clickthrough rate by 10.7%. The strategy has helped Reddit reach $762
million in advertising revenue and ~70% year-over-year growth in
active advertisers.

 MYSPACE IS MAKING A COMEBACK JUST AS WE'RE GROWING TIRED OF SOCIAL
MEDIA (4 MINUTE READ) [12]

 MySpace's comeback taps into fatigue with algorithm feeds and a
growing desire for a more personal internet. It once gave users full
control to build expressive profiles that felt human. Today's
platforms feel uniform and optimized for engagement. As people crave
more ownership and unpredictability, its return reflects demand for
more self-made spaces.

 THE HANK GREEN AI BACKLASH IS BIGGER THAN YOU THINK (3 MINUTE READ)
[13]

 The Hank Green AI backlash is a broader debate about transparency and
AI use in the creator economy. After viewers questioned his use of AI,
Green openly reflected on his reliance on tools like ChatGPT and
decided to scale back. The situation shows that audiences are still
forming expectations around AI, with some accepting thoughtful use
while others see any use as a loss of authenticity. As creators face
pressure to produce more, the challenge will be using AI while
maintaining transparency and audience trust.

 90% OF CONSUMERS DISTRUST INFLUENCERS. HOW CAN MARKETERS REBUILD
INFLUENCER AUTHENTICITY? (3 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Audiences respond to whether a sponsorship gets acknowledged honestly
rather than to the sponsorship existing at all. Influencers who openly
discuss what they're being paid for, especially with some humor, read
as more trustworthy than ones who stay quiet about it, and the same
holds for disclosing AI use in content creation. Authenticity turns
out to hinge less on staying in a narrow lane and more on being
upfront about what's really going on behind a post.
