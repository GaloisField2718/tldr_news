NEWS & TRENDS

 GOOGLE SEARCH RANKING VOLATILITY GETS HEADED OCTOBER 24TH (3 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Google Search rankings showed significant volatility on October 24,
suggesting an unconfirmed update that began Friday and continued into
Saturday. SEO tracking tools recorded movement while site owners
reported traffic drops and unstable rankings. Some also described
declining Google Discover traffic and outdated news results. This
follows several bouts of volatility earlier in October and the
September spam update, which ran from September 24 to October 8.

 PENNSYLVANIA SIGNS AI DECEPTIVE ADVERTISING ACT. WHAT IT MEANS FOR
BRANDS (4 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Pennsylvania's AI Deceptive Advertising Act requires brands to
disclose AI-generated content when it creates a false impression about
a product or service. Disclosures must appear at the first point of
consumer exposure, and the law applies to businesses of all sizes.
Since it remains unclear what qualifies as a false impression, brands
can reduce legal risk by disclosing AI use more broadly. California
and New York are also pursuing their own rules for synthetic
advertising.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 EVERY STORY NEEDS A VILLAIN. SO DOES YOUR BRAND (6 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Build your brand around one clear enemy that customers already
recognize. Dollar Shave Club challenged overpriced razors, while
Snickers built years of ads around hunger with its “You're not you
when you're hungry” campaign. Patagonia fights throwaway culture
through durable clothing and repair programs. Turn your brand's
villain into a content strategy by showing how the problem affects
customers and connecting it to the deeper belief your brand stands
for.

 400,000 FOLLOWERS OR 40 REGULARS (3 MINUTE READ) [11]

 IRL influencers build influence by bringing people together
repeatedly, not just attracting online followers. LA Chess Club grew
its free weekly chess nights to hundreds of attendees who returned for
the connections. With 51% of Gen Z feeling lonely on weekends and 62%
wishing online friendships led to more in-person plans, look beyond
reach and impressions. Someone who brings 40 people back every week
may have more meaningful influence than someone with 400K passive
followers.

MISCELLANEOUS

 YOUR MARKETING TEAM WILL BE ON GITHUB WITHIN A YEAR (5 MINUTE READ)
[15]

 Once a team shares more than a handful of AI skills and source files,
Drive and Claude Projects stop working as their home. The files depend
on each other, so teams need a change history to trace off-brand
output, one-step rollback, and plain files any AI tool can read. A
content team at a B2B SaaS company built that on GitHub without
developer support, where every member's Claude reads the same
positioning and voice files and a newly pushed skill reaches the whole
team at once.

 GIVE THE MACHINE A MANDATE BEFORE YOU GIVE IT A BUDGET (7 MINUTE
READ) [16]

 Knight Capital lost $460M in 45 minutes, and Zillow lost $304M after
automated systems made costly decisions without adequate controls. Ad
platforms pose similar risks when algorithms control spending without
clear limits. Before launching automated campaigns, define what the
system can buy and which decisions require approval. Set spending caps
for brand searches and existing customers. Use independent holdout
tests to measure results and set clear criteria for pausing campaigns.
