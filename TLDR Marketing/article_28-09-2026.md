 CHATGPT ADS SEEM TO LIFT ORGANIC TRAFFIC. IS PAYING TO BE FOUND THE
FUTURE OF AI SEARCH? (7 MINUTE READ) [8]

 ChatGPT ads appear to coincide with higher organic ChatGPT traffic,
but some of that lift may come from ad clicks being misclassified as
organic referrals in GA4. OpenAI says ads don't influence its answers,
and there is no public study proving whether ads affect organic
visibility. It recommends tagging ad URLs, auditing ‘oppref'
traffic, running holdout tests, and tracking organic mentions
separately from traffic.

 CLIPPING 101: INSIDE THE CLIPPING ECONOMY (7 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Clipping is emerging as a performance-based distribution model where
brands pay networks of creators to turn existing content into
short-form clips and only pay when posts reach verified view
thresholds. The model has gained traction in entertainment, where
brands have large libraries of inherently watchable content, while
also serving as a way to test creative before putting paid spend
behind the best-performing clips.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 HOW TO USE CUSTOMER LANGUAGE FOR POWERFUL MARKETING COPY (11 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Pull customer language from 15 to 20 recent reviews, support tickets,
sales calls, Google Ads search terms, and AI conversations. Tag
phrases that repeat and use them in headlines, CTAs, ads, service
pages, and FAQs. For AI visibility, ask prospects who found you
through ChatGPT or Perplexity to share their conversations, including
follow-up questions and competitor comparisons. Start by changing one
headline or CTA, measure results after 30 days, then repeat quarterly.


 HOW LONG SHOULD YOUR FREE TRIAL BE? (9 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Test trial length based on how quickly users experience value, how
often they need to return, and the cost of free access. Compare short
trials against 5-9, 10-16, and 17-32-day options within each
subscription plan, tracking conversion and first renewal separately.
For monthly plans, test 10-16 days rather than assuming longer is
better. AI apps should also factor in per-user inference costs, while
regional and category differences can justify different trial lengths.

 HOW X'S RANKING ALGORITHM WORKS (3 MINUTE READ) [14]

 X's ranking algorithm favors posts that people are likely to share,
reply to, or quote rather than simply like. A copy link share carries
40X the weight of a like, while replies and quotes each carry 10X the
weight. Posts also need to stay focused on a clear topic to reach
relevant nonfollowers, and negative actions such as mutes and reports
carry heavy penalties. The algorithm also reduces the value of
repeated posts from the same author and stops considering posts after
48 hours. Posting less often and creating highly shareable content can
improve reach.

 AI MARKETING IS MOVING INTO THE DATA PLATFORM (14 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Agentic CDPs bring AI directly into governed customer data, giving
agents the context needed to build audiences and design campaigns.
Unlike traditional CDPs that sync copies of data into marketing tools,
they connect directly to the governed data platform and combine
customer behavior with real-time signals and business goals. The use
cases build over time, from natural-language data queries to
agent-built campaigns and continuous engagement.

 THE STACK LADDER: WHAT COMPANIES RUN AT 10, 50, 200, 1,000, AND
10,000 PEOPLE (9 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Startups don't necessarily switch to enterprise software as they
grow. Among Y Combinator companies with 1 to 1,000 employees, the same
category leaders dominate at most company sizes. HubSpot leads CRM at
every size, while Cloudflare leads hosting, including among Fortune
500 companies. Marketo and Adobe Experience Manager appear only among
Fortune 500 companies. What changes with headcount is adoption. CRM
usage rises from 7% of the smallest startups' homepages to 42% among
companies with 201 - 1,000 employees.
