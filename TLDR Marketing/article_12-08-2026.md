NEWS & TRENDS

 GEN Z SPORTS FANDOM + THE MEGA-MOMENT (21 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Gen Z sports fandom is shaped by culture and content, not just
competition. 49% have discovered a new sport or athlete through
fan-created content or off-field moments. Viral videos were the top
trigger at 25%, followed by movies and TV at 23%. Fandom is also
fragmented, with 48% falling into a “New Bandwagon” group that
follows athletes or social content without being die-hard fans.
Meanwhile, 92% of Gen Z sports fans use their phones while watching
games, underscoring the importance of content beyond the live
competition.

 THE RISK OF MONETIZING TRUST: WHAT AI ADVERTISING COULD GET WRONG (3
MINUTE READ) [6]

 Every ad platform eventually monetizes the attention it gathers, but
conversational AI is different because it runs on trust rather than
discovery. A recommendation delivered inside an ongoing, personal
dialogue reads as advice rather than an ad, so a paid placement
blended into that dialogue risks eroding the confidence that makes the
format work. Credibility built through expertise and strong reviews
may end up mattering more than paid placement once that tension
surfaces. AI advertising becomes valuable or corrosive depending on
how thoughtfully the industry handles that distinction.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 HOW TO TURN FIRST PURCHASES INTO REPEAT ORDERS (2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 For replenishable brands, build the post-purchase journey around
repeat purchase timing. Use days 1-14 to reinforce product usage, set
expectations, and help customers build habits. Use days 14-21 for
check-ins, results guidance, and social proof. Trigger replenishment
reminders around the product's actual usage cycle with one-click
reordering or subscription offers. If customers still haven't
reordered after 30 days, escalate with incentives or stronger social
proof.

 YOU SHOULD BE RUNNING CONTENT EXPERIMENTS (4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Use TikTok to test content variables before scaling them, since
duplicate posts can be tested with different text hooks, verbal
openings, or higher posting volume. Use Instagram Trial Reels to test
multiple versions of an idea with non-followers, then move the best
performer to the main feed. For bigger content ideas, micro-test the
concept on Stories, Notes, or similar low-lift channels before
investing in production. Track each variable against the brand's own
performance baseline to identify repeatable winners.

 THE A/B TESTING FRAMEWORK WE USE FOR ECOMMERCE (11 MINUTE READ) [12]

 A strong ecommerce A/B testing program should build cumulative
customer knowledge instead of treating each test as an isolated win or
loss. The framework recommends tagging tests across eight purposes,
such as Brand, Product Detail, Price & Value, and Usability, then
using results to decide which areas deserve more testing. Replace
predictions with questions to reduce bias and produce more useful
learning from each test. Track funnel metrics, segment results, and
define a specific follow-up test so each experiment informs the next
one.

 NEW OPPORTUNITIES TO EARN AND CHANGES TO THE YOUTUBE PARTNER PROGRAM
(2 MINUTE READ) [14]

 YouTube's raising the bar for creators to enter its monetization
program starting February 1. It will require 8,000 watch hours or 20M
Shorts views, up from the current thresholds. Existing Partner Program
members won't be affected, but Shorts creators must maintain 10M views
every 90 days to keep earning from the Shorts Creators Pool. YouTube
is also expanding Premium Lite globally, giving creators another
source of subscription revenue.

 OWNED MEDIA IS NOT A NEWSLETTER. IT'S AN OPERATING STRATEGY (6 MINUTE
READ) [15]

 Owned media is more than launching a newsletter. It requires a clear
editorial focus, original content, distribution across multiple
channels, and measurement tied to audience quality. Newsletters can
serve as a direct audience channel, but they're only one part of a
broader system designed to build an audience around content the brand
controls.

 HOW TO FIGHT CLICKBAIT: META, LINKEDIN, & YOUTUBE CASE STUDIES (9
MINUTE READ) [16]

 Feed ranking is quietly moving from rewarding what gets clicked to
rewarding what a post is actually about, and the three largest
platforms landed on three different architectures for it. LinkedIn
replaced five separate retrieval systems with a single language-model
dual encoder that serves its whole feed through nearest-neighbor
search. Meta went the other way on Instagram, keeping over a thousand
specialized models in a staged funnel so engagement, diversity, and
integrity stay independently tunable. YouTube's PLUM system skips a
search index entirely, generating a video's identifier straight from a
language model.

 AI-WRITTEN REVIEWS ARE MORE EXTREME (3 MINUTE READ) [17]

 AI does not just make reviews easier to write - it changes what they
say. A comparison of 117,816 hotel reviews written with and without
ChatGPT access during a four-week ban in Italy in April 2023 showed a
2% rise in review volume and a 9% rise in length once AI was
available, alongside a clear shift in tone. Extremely negative reviews
written with AI grew harsher and more subjective rather than factual,
since AI reframes ordinary complaints into stronger language than the
experience itself warranted.
