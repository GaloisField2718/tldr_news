NEWS & TRENDS

 AI HALFTIME REPORT: H1 2026 (8 MINUTE READ) [4]

 AI is changing search, buying behavior, and brand visibility faster
than marketers can measure its business impact. Trust now matters more
than rankings because 88% of users accepted AI Mode product
recommendations and trusted brands won even if they were not the top
result, so brands need to optimize for mentions, recommendations, and
unique content instead of traditional SEO positions. AI visibility is
hard to track because 91% of citations appear in only one major AI
search engine, so prompt tracking needs a broader approach than rank
tracking. Companies also spent heavily on AI without clear ROI, public
markets cut many software valuations based on perceived AI disruption
rather than performance, and publishers now face a 33% referral
traffic decline as AI changes how people discover content.

 MICRODRAMAS ARE BLOWING UP. BUT THEY MAY NEVER BE A BIG AD VEHICLE (5
MINUTE READ) [5]

 Microdramas generate $11 billion in annual global revenue, but almost
none of it comes from advertising. Subscription and token payments
drive nearly all of that total, since a viable ad break for a
90-second episode often runs longer than the episode itself. Platforms
like DramaBox and ReelShort already resemble mobile games more than
TV, monetizing through payments that let users skip friction while
spending heavily on Meta and TikTok to acquire new users rather than
to sell ad space. TikTok's free Pine Drama and Canela Media's upcoming
Zully are betting a more ad-friendly model can still work.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 DOES SCHEMA MARKUP IMPACT SERP RANK? (11 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Schema markup alone does not improve Google rankings, so treat it as
a support tool instead of a ranking tactic. A 10-week study across 29
local business websites found no direct ranking or search visibility
gains from adding LocalBusiness schema in Google. Schema still adds
value because it enables rich results for content like jobs and
products and helps search engines and AI systems understand page
content more clearly. Use schema where it unlocks search features or
improves content structure, but focus SEO effort on tactics that
produce measurable ranking and visibility gains.

 ANATOMY OF THE B2B SAAS COMPARISON HUB (2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Comparison hubs help companies capture buyers researching
alternatives by creating a connected system of pages instead of
isolated comparison content. A category-level hub introduces the
company's position in the market and links to competitor comparisons,
while each comparison page helps buyers make a decision with clear
tradeoffs and proof points. The approach supports organic search, LLM
visibility, and paid campaigns by meeting buyers where they're already
evaluating options. Be transparent about competitors' strengths and
weaknesses while shaping the narrative around why your product's the
right fit.

 DON'T TRUST GOOGLE TRENDS FOR CONTENT PLANNING (1 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Google Trends may no longer give a reliable view of real search
demand because AI search fan-out queries can inflate topic interest.
Validate content ideas by comparing Trends data against Search Console
impressions and clicks instead of relying on relative interest scores
alone. Use keyword tools such as Ahrefs or Semrush for search volume
estimates and check engagement and conversion data to confirm audience
interest.

 JASPER VS. CHATGPT: WHICH IS BETTER? (8 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Jasper is built for marketing teams with brand voice training, 100+
marketing workflows, and SEO integrations like Surfer SEO. ChatGPT
offers more flexibility with custom GPTs, data analysis, research, and
broader task support. While Jasper provides more guided content
creation, ChatGPT is more affordable with a free plan and lower-cost
paid options.

 AI IS NOT REPLACING MARKETING. IT'S CROWDING THE ROOM (6 MINUTE READ)
[13]

 Radio still captured 61% of daily ad-supported audio listening in the
US in late 2025, more than podcasts and streaming music combined at
21% and 15%. That split, not a radio collapse, is the same pattern
showing up in search, where Google still held 87% of the US market in
June even as AI chatbot use climbed to 49% of adults. Marketers keep
asking which channel AI will kill next, but the more useful question
is what job each channel plays once a new one arrives. The biggest
strain on teams is managing that many channels at once, not any single
channel's decline.

 THE ‘DEAD INTERNET THEORY' IS REAL. AND IT'S KILLING THE WEB AS WE
KNOW IT (5 MINUTE READ) [14]

 The 30-year-old online advertising model was never really selling
attention, it was selling access to a human being who could be
flattered or worn down into a purchase. Agents don't have that
vulnerability, so surveilling one produces nothing to sell, and more
than half of all web traffic is now agents rather than people. What a
business can still offer an agent is qualification on merits like
price, reliability, and data quality, not persuasion, which is a
fundamentally different kind of customer relationship to build for.
The model isn't being reformed by regulation or backlash, it's dying
because the traffic itself changed completely.
