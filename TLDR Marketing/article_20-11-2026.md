NEWS & TRENDS

 BRANDS ARE HIRING FORMER JOURNALISTS FOR STORYTELLING. OR ARE THEY?
(4 MINUTE READ) [5]

 More brands are hiring former journalists as storytellers, often to
produce the original-data stories and service pieces that AI search
tends to cite. More than 2,800 journalists have moved into brand
editorial roles over the past decade. However, many hiring managers
still default to candidates with linear marketing careers, and former
editors with brand experience tend to win out over reporters.

 TECH OUTAGES ARE CAUSING PEOPLE TO PANIC-BUY (2 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Recent tech outages across Microsoft, US airlines, and JPMorgan are
driving consumers to stock up on emergency supplies. Costco is
benefiting, with US comparable sales rising an estimated 11% to 12% in
the final week of October compared with its typical 6% to 7% growth.
Momentum continued into November as 21 of 26 generators tracked on
Costco's website sold out. Sam's Club and home improvement retailers
are also seeing increased demand.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 WHEN INFLUENCERS POST SPONSORED CONTENT, YOUR BRAND SHOULD... (2
MINUTE READ) [7]

 Your brand should actively support influencer content after it goes
live rather than leaving all the engagement to creators. Have your
social team comment on sponsored posts, reply to every comment,
involve public-facing employees, share posts to Instagram Stories, and
use reposts and tagged content to feature creator posts on your brand
profile. For campaigns with multiple influencers, send creators links
to each other's posts and encourage them to engage. Discuss these
tactics with creators beforehand so everyone understands their role in
promoting the partnership.

 MAKE THEM TYPE THE PROMO CODE: WHY A LITTLE EFFORT BEATS AN AUTOMATIC
DISCOUNT IN DIGITAL SHOPPING (7 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Requiring customers to enter a promo code can generate more sales
than automatically applying the same discount. A study found that a
15% discount redeemed through a promo code converted at 1.45%,
compared with 0.91% for an automatic discount. The small action makes
customers feel like they earned the deal, increasing their sense of
value and likelihood of redeeming it. The tactic works best for modest
discounts of 10% to 15%, but the effort should remain minimal.
Additional steps can create frustration and eliminate the benefit.

 HOW BRANDS ARE GOING VIRAL WITH ADVENT CALENDARS (5 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Beauty and CPG brands are filling TikTok feeds with advent calendars
before Black Friday, since one box can fuel weeks of unboxings and
debates when each door gives people a reason to keep watching. Burger
King releases only 110 calendars a day, so fans follow the drop before
the doors even open. Red Bull added one pack-exclusive flavor that has
fans debating whether to buy the whole box for it.

MISCELLANEOUS

 PAYPAL SAID HONEY FOLLOWED LAST-CLICK ATTRIBUTION, WHICH WAS THE
WHOLE PROBLEM (7 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Last-click attribution can overstate the performance of advertising
channels that capture sales rather than generate them. This is
especially common in brand search, retargeting, coupon extensions,
cart abandonment emails, and affiliate marketing. A 2015 eBay study
found that turning off paid search ads had no significant impact on
sales, even though attribution reports credited those ads with strong
returns. Run incrementality tests to measure actual sales lift,
exclude brand searches from automated campaigns, and revise affiliate
terms to limit commissions for partners that only capture credit at
checkout.

 SAM ALTMAN WARNS AGAINST A FUTURE WHERE AI, NOT HUMANS, CALLS THE
SHOTS (3 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Sam Altman and Greg Brockman oppose the idea of gradually handing
control of society to AI as models become more powerful. Both believe
humans must remain in charge but differ on how to prevent an
accidental or intentional handover. Brockman stressed the growing need
for better AI monitoring and security as models become harder to track
through traditional chain-of-thought monitoring. Following a security
breach, OpenAI redirected 25% of production engineers to security
work.

 AGENTIC SHOPPING - MAYBE NOBODY WANTS THIS (3 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Agentic shopping faces consumer trust barriers as shoppers remain
hesitant to let AI agents spend their money. While AI is used to
research and compare products, retailers want to retain control of the
actual purchase and customer relationship. Catalyst Brands is
investing in loyalty programs to strengthen direct customer
relationships across brands like JC Penney and Brooks Brothers.
