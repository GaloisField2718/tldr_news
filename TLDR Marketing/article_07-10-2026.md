NEWS & TRENDS

 TIKTOK'S AGENT CHECKOUT ENTERS THE RACE (3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 TikTok announced Shopping Assistant and Buy Direct at Advertising
Week NYC on October 5, both now in eligibility-based testing in the
US. Shopping Assistant is a conversational agent that handles product
details, sizing, and availability, then hands the purchase to the
in-app browser. Buy Direct is a one-click checkout inside the For You
feed using stored payment and address details. Both run on Google's
Universal Commerce Protocol, and the brand remains the merchant of
record.

 THE EVIDENCE IS CLEAR: AI SEARCH IS COSTING PUBLISHERS (4 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 AI search is reducing the traffic publishers receive from traditional
search while AI referrals remain too small to make up the difference.
One study found AI Overviews reduced outbound organic clicks by 39.8%.
Pew found traditional-result clicks fell from 15% to 8% when an AI
summary appeared. Meanwhile, ChatGPT generated just 0.02% of publisher
referrals compared with 7.3% from Google Search.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 4 SEO STRATEGIES FOR AI-DRIVEN BRAND SEARCH (1 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Google now shows AI Overviews for roughly 80% of branded searches.
Keep brand messaging consistent across owned channels so AI systems
get clear context, then create content to fill gaps where third-party
sources currently shape answers. Monitor competitor content and review
sites because negative claims can influence AI-generated brand
answers. An accurate AI instructions page can also provide a direct
source for brand information and become a frequently cited page in AI
Overviews and AI Mode.

 WHEN TO DO SALES REMOTELY VS IN-PERSON (2 MINUTE READ) [11]

 The best mix of remote and in-person sales meetings depends on
product complexity. Using the right mix can improve sales performance
by up to 27.3%. For simple products, about 37.9% of meetings held in
person produced the best results, while complex products performed
worse when sales teams relied only on remote meetings. In-person
meetings help build trust and uncover customer needs, which matters
more when products require more customization or evaluation. Sales
teams should use more in-person meetings early in complex sales
relationships, then reduce them as they learn the customer's needs,
while still accommodating customers who prefer remote meetings.

 5 TIPS FOR OPTIMIZING EXISTING CONTENT FOR CHATGPT (2 MINUTE READ)
[12]

 Existing content can be optimized for ChatGPT without publishing new
posts. Lead with the answer before adding context so key claims are
easier to extract. Use clear statements instead of hedging. Mention
relevant brands and competitors to provide useful context. Turn
headings into specific questions that mirror user queries. Refresh old
posts with new data, screenshots, and sections rather than simply
changing the publication date.

 HOW TO WRITE A GREAT EXPERIMENT PLAN (12 MINUTE READ) [15]

 An experiment plan should prevent costly errors, not serve as a
ceremony. Start with an evidence-based hypothesis and one primary
metric, then size the test around the smallest change worth shipping.
Count only users who reached the change, since including everyone
assigned dilutes the effect. Before launch, agree on stopping
thresholds and on what happens under each outcome, so that early peeks
don't drive the decision. After launch, confirm the traffic split
holds.

 ADAM HARRIS ON WHY FOLLOWER COUNT IS THE NEW NIELSEN BOX (5 MINUTE
READ) [16]

 Follower count is becoming a poor measure of creator value as
platforms increasingly serve content based on interests rather than
follows. Adam Harris, co-founder of creator platform Gaggl, says 70%
of video views now come from non-followers. Gaggl's campaign data also
shows micro-creators generate 5X more engagement than larger creators.
Larger creators remain useful for awareness while smaller creators
drive deeper engagement. Harris recommends starting with small creator
tests before scaling based on results.

 LLMS ARE QUIETLY WATERMARKING AI CONTENT. NOW WHAT? (6 MINUTE READ)
[17]

 AI labs are watermarking generated content by subtly changing word
choices during generation. These tiny changes accumulate across longer
outputs and can survive copy-paste and light edits. The bigger concern
is AEO because labs may filter AI-generated content from training data
to avoid model degradation. Teams should monitor branded and
non-branded AI prompts to separate retrieved from trained-in
visibility. Audit how much content is fully AI-generated and
prioritize human rewriting for content that needs to influence future
model knowledge.

 HOW TO SURVIVE A DOUBLE LIFE AS AN OPERATOR-CREATOR (9 MINUTE READ)
[18]

 Operator-creators run a flywheel where the day job makes the content
credible and the audience becomes a distribution channel for the
employer. Elena Verna keeps it working by disclosing everything to her
employer, audience, and sponsors, ideally before the first day of
work. She also sets boundaries up front, keeping the audience as her
own while giving Lovable work first claim on her time.
