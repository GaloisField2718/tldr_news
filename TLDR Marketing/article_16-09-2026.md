NEWS & TRENDS

 COMPANIES ARE HIRING EDITORS TO RUN THOUGHT LEADERSHIP AND MEASURING
THEM ON PIPELINE (4 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Companies are hiring senior editors to run thought leadership and
tying the function to revenue. Workato, for example, measures its
program on win rates, deal velocity, and average sales price instead
of content output. Research shows thought leadership can influence
buying decisions and reach “hidden buyers” who rarely interact
with sales. Recurring research and publishing can also build category
authority over time. Owned media is becoming a permanent function with
editorial leadership and a pipeline-focused scorecard.

 GEN Z ROMANTICISED THE LIBRARY INTO RELEVANCE AGAIN (3 MINUTE READ)
[6]

 Gen Z is driving a library revival through viral TikTok and Instagram
content. Creators frame libraries as aesthetic, free places to study,
work, and socialize. 67% report visiting public libraries, including
many who are not active readers. The trend also ties into growing
interest in physical books and digital detox. For libraries, social
content can turn cultural relevance into real-world visits and
stronger evidence for continued public funding.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 I AUDITED 4 WEEKS OF MY LINKEDIN POSTS. HERE'S WHAT GREW MY FOLLOWING
(4 MINUTE READ) [7]

 A 4-week LinkedIn audit found that conversation-starting posts,
relatable humor, and provocative takes drove the most follower growth.
Shorter copy also performed better than long paragraphs. Memes were
fast to produce and often earned strong engagement, but they didn't
build followers or trust as effectively as video. The best mix is
repeatable, high-engagement formats like memes alongside videos and
posts that spark debate or make the audience feel seen.

 DON'T USE QUESTIONS IN YOUR TITLES (1 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Use statement-based headlines instead of questions to increase
engagement with marketing content. A Stanford study found that
question titles generated 71.6% fewer comments and 12.6% fewer clicks
across experiments and analyses of more than 22,000 news articles, 3
million academic articles, and 53,000 Reddit posts. Questions may
perform worse because they signal uncertainty and provide less
immediate information to readers. Test statement formats such as “X
compared to Y” against question-based titles, then check that title
changes don't hurt SEO.

 STOP TRYING TO GO VIRAL. YOU NEED THE RIGHT 100 PEOPLE TO KNOW WHO
YOU ARE FIRST (3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Personal branding isn't a numbers game, it's a positioning game.
Being known for one specific thing, not several, is what makes a name
and a topic become inseparable in someone else's mind. The first time
an idea gets posted, it's scrolled past. The fifth time, it starts to
register, and by the twentieth repetition a name becomes the person
others associate with that topic. That kind of association compounds
into leverage long before any follower count does.

 AI OVERVIEWS AND PAID SEARCH: WHEN TO FIGHT, INFLUENCE, OR GENERATE
DEMAND (7 MINUTE READ) [11]

 AI Overviews make it harder to use one bidding strategy across every
search query, so marketers should classify terms by intent and AI
visibility before allocating budget. High-intent terms belong in the
“Fight” bucket, where paying for absolute top placement can still
generate direct sales. Informational terms belong in “Influence”,
where SEO and PPC should work together to earn citations and brand
mentions in AI answers. Zero-click opportunities belong in “Generate
Demand”, where budget should move to YouTube, Demand Gen, and
content that builds demand before the eventual commercial search. In
one B2B account, 83.3% of queries triggering AI answer blocks were
informational, showing why reallocating budget across these buckets
can matter more than simply adjusting bids.

 SALESFORCE AND NVIDIA'S NEW REASONING MODEL IS EVERYTHING THE AI LABS
SHOULD FEAR (3 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Koa answers a problem Salesforce couldn't solve without Nvidia, a
reasoning model trained for sales and support work that never has to
touch a customer's actual data. It was post-trained entirely on
synthetic conversations, including simulated irate customers and
negotiating sales reps, and its architecture is built to reach a
token-efficient answer faster than a general-purpose frontier model
would. Its own AI gateway can now route reasoning-heavy requests to
Koa instead of paying frontier-lab rates for the same work.

MISCELLANEOUS

 DOES TV ACTUALLY SUCK NOW? (4 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Streaming didn't shrink television's quality - it multiplied its
supply, turning a manageable weekly lineup into a catalog large enough
to produce real decision fatigue. An audience now half-watching on a
second screen while scrolling through that catalog is bound to feel
worse about whatever it lands on, regardless of how well made it
actually is. The fix is a shift in viewing habits, not a demand on the
industry to make better shows.

 THE JOB MARKET HAS BEEN CONTRACTING FOR 31 MONTHS (4 MINUTE READ)
[14]

 White-collar hiring has contracted for 31 months, with professional
and technical roles shrinking despite stable overall employment. The
bigger change is repeated headcount deferrals, leaving existing teams
to absorb work indefinitely. Instead of waiting for hiring to recover,
identify what you're actually paid for beyond your job title. Turn
those skills into something clients or smaller companies would pay
for. Build a small version while still employed, then review your
progress in 6 months.
