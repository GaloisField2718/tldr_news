 ALGORITHMIC CAPITAL AND THE INEVITABLE RISE OF THE CHIEF CREATOR
OFFICER (4 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Brands are now hiring creators into executive roles as social
platforms make earned attention harder to buy. Blenders Eyewear named
creator Jordan Howlett its first Chief Content Officer, while Edelman
appointed a Global Chief Creator Officer. Brands are bringing creator
expertise in-house rather than relying solely on campaign
partnerships. These roles reflect growing demand for people who
understand how to build trust and engagement in algorithm-driven
feeds. Creator marketing is now a more embedded part of brand
strategy.

 MOVING BACK HOME AFTER COLLEGE? ALL YOUR FRIENDS ARE DOING IT (5
MINUTE READ) [5]

 More young college graduates are moving back home as entry-level job
prospects weaken and housing costs remain high. More than 25% of
college graduates ages 23-27 lived with their parents last year, up
from about 18% in 2001. Recent graduate unemployment reached 5.7% in
Q2, compared with a much slower rise in overall unemployment. The
trend is also becoming more socially accepted as more young adults use
living at home to reduce expenses and take more time to find the right
job.

 WHAT IT ACTUALLY TAKES TO WIN ON REDDIT: LESSONS FROM SONOS (3 MINUTE
READ) [6]

 Sonos built its Reddit presence around employee Keith Nieves rather
than a corporate account. During an app update crisis, he kept
recurring office hours running and shared a public Trello-style
tracker showing what was being fixed. He also maintains direct
relationships with subreddit moderators and brings highly upvoted
feedback into product meetings.

 CONTENT PRIORITIZATION IN AN AI SEARCH ERA: A FRAMEWORK + WORKSHEET
(13 MINUTE READ) [7]

 AI search makes content prioritization more important as generic
information can often be answered without a click. This guide outlines
which types of content are more likely to retain value, including
original research, current information, and content that helps users
make decisions or take action. It also identifies content that can be
deprioritized when AI can easily replicate it. A downloadable
worksheet is available to help teams assess their own content.

 USE CHROME DEV TOOLS TO EMULATE HOW CHATGPT + CLAUDE SEE YOUR SITE (1
MINUTE READ) [9]

 Chrome DevTools can show how AI crawlers view a site by changing the
browser's user agent. In DevTools, open Network Conditions, paste the
user agent string for an LLM like ChatGPT or Claude, and refresh the
page. This reveals how AI systems receive site content compared with
human visitors, which is becoming more useful as sites serve
AI-specific versions of their pages.

 11 BEST ALTERNATIVE SEARCH ENGINES TO GOOGLE IN 2026 (20 MINUTE READ)
[10]

 Search engines have four layers: the query interface, web index,
ranking system, and answer layer. Most alternatives, including
DuckDuckGo and Startpage, use Bing or Google's index behind a
different interface, while only a few, like Brave and Europe's
emerging EUSP, crawl the web independently. Comparing alternatives by
interface alone can be misleading, and a 12-query test found
DuckDuckGo's no-AI mode closest to Google's results despite relying on
Bing.

 TIME'S AI-ONLY ADS MAY BE MARKETING'S NEXT FRONTIER (2 MINUTE READ)
[11]

 Time magazine runs a machine-only version of TIME.com that serves
sponsored content to AI crawlers instead of humans. Bots like
ClaudeBot, OAI-SearchBot, and PerplexityBot see marketing claims and
FAQ-style content promoting Ally Bank, while Google's crawler gets the
standard page. Since June, an adtech partner has reformatted pages
into “agent ads” designed to influence AI assistant responses
rather than individual readers.

 CONSUMER FOCUS ON PROTEIN, DIGESTION AND HEALTH IS RESHAPING THE
DAIRY AISLE (7 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Dairy is being reshaped by demand for protein and digestive health.
High-protein, low-sugar, and lactose-free options are gaining
traction, while social media is driving new uses for cottage cheese
and butter. Brands are also expanding premium products and
cooking-focused formats as more people eat at home. Cottage cheese is
emerging as a major growth area, with demand outpacing supply and
prompting manufacturers to expand capacity.
