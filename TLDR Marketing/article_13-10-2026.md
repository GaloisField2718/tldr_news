NEWS & TRENDS

 YOUTUBE OVERTAKES TV AS TOP SOURCE OF POLITICAL NEWS (2 MINUTE READ)
[8]

 YouTube is now the most-used individual source for political and
election news in the US at 16.5%, narrowly ahead of TV news at 16.1%.
Social platforms collectively lead traditional news sources 60% to
32%, while 61% of Americans trust independent news creators as much as
professional journalists on political topics. Credibility depends more
on reporting than credentials, with 67% saying the quality of the
reporting matters more than formal journalism training. Publicly
correcting mistakes is the strongest trust signal, with 86% saying it
increases their trust in independent creators.

 THE STATE OF AI IN MARKETING: Q4 2026 (6 MINUTE READ) [9]

 More than 90% of marketers use AI, but many still rely on copying and
pasting outputs instead of integrating AI into their workflows.
Disconnected tools and poor data quality limit its effectiveness,
while generating more content creates extra review work and can hurt
customer experience. Teams need better workflow integration and data
governance to realize AI's business value.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 META CREATIVE ANALYSIS WITH CLAUDE (1 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Analyze Meta ad creative performance by exporting daily ad data to a
CSV and uploading it to Claude or ChatGPT. Generate 3 charts to track
monthly creative churn, weekly production versus hit rate, and spend
share from ads under 30 days old. Use 20-30% monthly churn and a
10-15% hit rate as benchmarks. If older ads still drive significant
spend or hit rates fall while production stays flat, refresh creative
before performance declines.

 DON'T TEACH CUSTOMERS EVERYTHING BEFORE THEY BUY (1 MINUTE READ) [11]


 Improve retention by separating pre-purchase education from
post-purchase guidance. Before buying, explain features that build
confidence in the decision. After buying, share tips that help
customers get more value over time. Teaching customers things they
didn't know about owning a product encourages long-term engagement.

 HOW TO FIND OUT WHO GEMINI CITES FOR YOUR QUERY, AND WHY IT ISN'T YOU
(14 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Gemini's citations shift between runs, so a single answer says little
about which pages it actually relies on. This free checker asks Gemini
the same question 5 times with Google Search grounding on, then counts
how often each source and claim comes back. It compares the recurring
claims with a page's raw HTML, labels each one as stated, touched, or
missing, and turns the biggest gaps into suggested edits using wording
from cited pages.

 As AI makes specialized skills obsolete, career resilience comes from

 HOW BRANDS ARE SKIPPING GEN Z (5 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Brands can stay relevant without chasing Gen Z trends or trying to
look young. Older consumers offer greater spending power and less
competition for attention. Tactics include creating distinctive
mascots and featuring diverse ages and skin types. Brands can also
control their retail experience and design products for everyday use.
Choosing relevant celebrities and highlighting scientific expertise
can build credibility without relying on influencers.
