NEWS & TRENDS

 THE NEXT BIG CREATORS WILL BE EXPERTS (4 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Audiences are growing tired of polished, interchangeable creators and
increasingly value experts who offer firsthand knowledge, credible
stories, and informed perspectives. Brands should look beyond follower
counts when choosing creators and prioritize people with deep subject
expertise, a proven track record, and the ability to support their
claims with evidence. Four qualities matter when evaluating expert
voices: a genuine obsession with their subject, the ability to hold
attention beyond short clips, transparency about sources and
uncertainty, and the willingness to challenge a brand's assumptions.
Pairing experienced experts with younger creators who understand
modern formats can also help turn specialized knowledge into content
that travels widely.

 AS ‘DEINFLUENCING' TREND SHAPES CHRISTMAS SHOPPING, “BRANDS NEED
TO MEAN MORE” (6 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Deinfluencing encourages consumers to avoid overhyped products and
buy less amid cost-of-living pressures. Searches for “deinfluence
me” surged 5,000% year over year in 2026. Consumers are becoming
more selective about what they buy and expect brands to prove their
value.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 SIMPLE SEO HACK (1 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Use a regex filter in GSC to find search queries with 8 or more
words. These specific searches reveal the questions people ask before
finding your site and can also inform ChatGPT-focused content.
Prioritize queries with high impressions but low clicks, then answer
them near the top of the pages already ranking.

 HOW TO PITCH TV NEWS WITH JODI GRALNICK (FORMER CNBC PRODUCER) (14
MINUTE READ) [10]

 Effective TV pitching starts with matching your story to the right
outlet, show, producer, and audience. TV needs strong visuals,
graphics, and a relevant human guest, while digital can support deeper
research, more data, and written quotes. Research each outlet and
journalist before pitching, and write subject lines as headlines the
outlet would actually publish. Get ahead of predictable news events by
offering expert sources and unique perspectives before journalists
start building their coverage. Pitches also need a timely news angle,
objective audience relevance, and exclusive information, since 99% of
pitches are irrelevant to the person receiving them.

 5 GTM PLAYS WORKING RIGHT NOW (9 MINUTE READ) [11]

 The strongest GTM plays right now take a channel that already
converts and automate the manual work around it rather than adding new
channels. An AI skill replaced incident.io's ads agency, cutting ABM
campaign launches from 2-3 days to 3 minutes. Granola pays 200+
product users with LinkedIn followings to post about it, and those
posts average 10x the engagement of its other LinkedIn ads once
boosted. Vercel went from 1-2 to 3-6 field events a month without new
headcount after building an internal app that automates invites,
seating, and follow-up.

 INTRODUCING REDDIT REVIEWS (3 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Reddit is testing Reddit Reviews, an AI-powered feature that
organizes product discussions into dedicated review pages. Each page
summarizes what users like and dislike, shows positive, mixed, and
negative sentiment, and links to original conversations. Shoppers can
filter discussions by topic and browse products by category. The
initial rollout covers roughly 10,000 products across 4 categories for
US users.

 SMARTLEAD'S BILLBOARDS WERE NEVER FOR THE STREETS (5 MINUTE READ)
[15]

 An outbound sales platform bought billboards in 5 cities to be
photographed and shared on LinkedIn, not to reach people walking past.
Customers appeared as the models, giving each one a reason to post
their own board. Copy changed city by city around local in-jokes, and
a bench of customer creators kept the photos circulating. With no QR
codes or tracked links on any board, branded search still rose 43% and
direct traffic 30%.

 WHAT IS COMMS STRATEGY? A PRACTICAL DEFINITION, WITH THREE REAL
EXAMPLES (7 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Comms strategy turns a brand idea into a plan for what to say and how
to reach audiences. This guide breaks down 8 elements, from audience
insights and barriers to messaging and channel roles. It uses 3
award-winning cases from the IPA and APG archives to show how this
works in practice. AJ Bell built brand awareness through a distinctive
audio identity. Uber used local creative to grow in Northern England.
TfL encouraged cycling by making it a social activity rather than
focusing on safety concerns.
