 HOW TO BUILD MORE BUYABLE B2B BRANDS (5 MINUTE READ) [5]

 B2B campaigns that build relatability help buying groups feel more
confident defending their purchasing decisions internally. Campaigns
with strong buyability signals were 1.7X more likely to qualify as
“Strategic Assets”, the highest rung of WARC's B2B Effectiveness
Ladder, reserved for campaigns that drive sustained commercial growth.
Yet 52% of campaigns featured only one or none of the 3 signals:
recommendations, relationships, and relatability. Customer
testimonials and use cases appeared in 64% of high-buyability
campaigns versus 16% of lower-buyability campaigns.

 WHY GEN Z ISN'T FULLY LOCKED INTO THEIR JOB ‘SITUATIONSHIP' (3
MINUTE READ) [6]

 Gen Z is increasingly reluctant to commit to employers as layoffs and
AI-driven job disruption weaken trust in job security. More than 30%
of workers could see at least half their job tasks disrupted by
generative AI. Meanwhile, 85% could see at least 10% of their tasks
affected. This uncertainty makes exploring other opportunities more
appealing. However, staying detached can limit career growth by
weakening workplace relationships and access to promotions.

 DELETING BLOG POSTS HURT AI VISIBILITY, BUT ORGANIC CLICKS TOLD A
DIFFERENT STORY (7 MINUTE READ) [12]

 AI visibility scores track how often a brand appears in AI answers,
not whether those answers send anyone to the site. Blog posts tend to
drive that exposure while service pages drive the clicks, so pruning
the blog can sink AI reports as organic clicks hold or rise. Tracker
data is also unstable, since answers change on nearly every run and
the tested prompts aren't real searches. The solution is to compare
Search Console's AI impressions with clicks to the same pages, and to
add a “How did you hear about us?” field to inquiry forms.

 THE LINK BUILDING INDUSTRIAL COMPLEX IS DYING (3 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Traditional link building is losing value as brands question buying
backlinks based on domain rating rather than relevance and
credibility. AI search requires more than backlinks or brand mentions,
since being cited doesn't guarantee a product recommendation. B2B
brands may get more value from visibility on review sites like G2 and
Gartner than from paid guest posts. Podcasts, influential
publications, and videos answering buyer questions are stronger ways
to build credibility and earn relevant links.
