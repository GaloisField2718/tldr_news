NEWS & TRENDS

 HOW CONSUMER DESIRE FOR MORE TACTILE, IRL EXPERIENCES CONTINUES TO
TRANSFORM MARKETING (20 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Gen Z is seeking offline experiences as digital fatigue grows, with
82% saying social media breaks improve their mental health and 70%
planning to take up hands-on hobbies. Across the US, 75% see a simpler
relationship with technology as a lifestyle upgrade. 68% collect
physical items and 84% of Gen Z go out of their way for in-person
shopping.

 HALF OF MARKETING TEAMS ARE REPLACING PEOPLE WITH AI. ALMOST NOBODY
CAN PROVE IT'S WORKING (4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Nearly 48% of marketing teams replaced human tasks with AI in the
past year, but few can prove it works. A Basis survey found 65% of
respondents either don't measure AI's return on investment or don't
know if their organization does. Meanwhile, 51% expect substantial job
losses across the industry within 3-5 years, even as AI's impact on
marketing performance remains largely unproven.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 DOES AI DRIVE MORE DIRECT TRAFFIC? (6 MINUTE READ) [9]

 An analysis of 12 GA4 properties found no reliable evidence that
AI-driven visits are hiding in Direct traffic. Total Direct sessions
rose 141% from January to September, but engaged sessions stayed
nearly flat due to bot traffic. Across 632 tests, AI visibility also
showed no consistent link to subsequent branded search or Direct
traffic. To measure AI's impact, track brand mentions across
consistent prompts and platforms. Ask leads how they discovered you,
then connect their responses to qualified leads, purchases, and
revenue.

 HOW A24 MADE ‘YOU CAN SEE EVERYTHING' A MUST-SEE FILM (7 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 A24 had just 40 days to market its Elizabeth Holmes documentary,
despite audiences already knowing the Theranos story from TV dramas
and years of trial coverage. The campaign used cryptic teasers and
provocative posters to make this version feel fresh. It built intrigue
without spoiling the film's biggest surprises or making Holmes look
like she was staging a comeback.

 GOOGLE ADS NOW IN AI MODE WITH PERSONAL INTELLIGENCE (2 MINUTE READ)
[14]

 Google is testing ads in AI Mode for users who enable Personal
Intelligence by connecting apps like Gmail and Google Photos. While
ads won't be based directly on inboxes or photo libraries, Google may
use summaries and inferences from connected apps to personalize ad
targeting. Advertisers won't have access to users' connected apps, and
sensitive information won't be used to tailor ads.

MISCELLANEOUS

 CHATGPT, CLAUDE, AND GROK CHATS REACH TRACKERS, STUDY FINDS (3 MINUTE
READ) [15]

 A study of 17 AI assistant experiences found that 8 shared
conversation links, titles, or text with advertising and analytics
firms. ChatGPT, Claude, and Grok all leaked shared chat links, while
Grok also sent screenshots and prompts to Google, Meta, and TikTok.
Rejecting cookies left 80.8% of trackers active, and 6 of 9 web
assistants still contacted Google Ads. The findings highlight privacy
risks for users discussing sensitive topics, even when AI providers
offer training opt-outs.

 CUSTOMER-CENTRIC THINKING IS TOO NARROW (9 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Customer-centric marketing can overlook people who never buy but
still influence how a market discovers and evaluates a brand.
Journalists and practitioners shape public conversations and the
information that search engines and AI systems use. Audience-centric
marketing considers these groups when creating content and prioritizes
resources that are easy to access, cite, and share. This can make
ungated research and public documentation more valuable than
lead-generation metrics suggest.
