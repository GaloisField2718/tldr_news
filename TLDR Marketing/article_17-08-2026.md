NEWS & TRENDS

 A NEW CLASS OF CUDDLY MASCOTS IS SEEING EVERYTHING FROM AI TO TAP
WATER (4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Brands are bringing back mascots as a way to build recognizable
identities in a crowded media landscape. Crocs is launching Niles, a
6-foot crocodile, joining Liberty Mutual's Liberty Biberty and Stanley
Black & Decker's talking toolbox Stan. The appeal is consistency and
emotional association. Unlike celebrities, mascots can become
long-term brand assets that reinforce the same personality across
campaigns and markets.

 FROM UNIQLO TO ZARA, CLOTHING BRANDS TRY TO WIN OVER GEN Z WITH A
NEEDLE AND THREAD (6 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Clothing brands are turning repairs and sewing into customer
experiences as Gen Z embraces secondhand shopping and saving money.
Levi's is expanding its repair services and teaching sewing skills,
while Uniqlo offers repairs and customization, and Zara offers
alterations and resale. The programs give brands new ways to stay
connected with customers after the initial purchase.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 YOUR SUBSCRIBERS ARE YOUR BEST LIFECYCLE QA TEAM (3 MINUTE READ) [10]


 Email Love discovered a lifecycle bug when a subscriber replied that
three different emails had arrived at once. Each automation looked
fine in the dashboard, but the combined experience was broken. Treat
subscriber replies as a QA channel, not just customer service noise.
Replies can surface automation collisions and purchase objections that
dashboards miss. Asking specific questions, like why someone has not
purchased, can also turn replies into a source of customer insight.

 FROM KEYWORDS TO LANDING PAGES: THE CLAUDE LOOP (1 MINUTE READ) [11]

 This Claude Loop is a 7-step workflow for turning paid-search demand
into landing pages at scale. Claude handles keyword analysis, intent
clustering, gap identification, and briefing. Claude Design takes the

The final step covers CMS setup, forms, tracking, integrations, and
go-live. In this case, two people produced 20 landing pages in one
week.

 WHAT PEOPLE *REALLY* THINK ABOUT IC WORK (18 MINUTE READ) [17]

 71% of people want at least half their time spent on IC work, while
66% cite lack of influence as the biggest downside. Former managers
who moved into IC roles are particularly satisfied, with 75%+ saying

 THE MOAT PARADOX (2 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Companies often avoid the hard problems that could make their
businesses more defensible. Difficult, uncertain problems tied
directly to customer value are the best early indicator of a moat.
However, that uncertainty pushes teams toward smaller tasks that
provide faster feedback, even when they don't create a lasting
competitive advantage. Traditional moats like scale and switching
costs only emerge later.

 AS AI REWRITES SEARCH FOR MARKETERS, REDDIT FIGHTS TO STAY THE SAME
(4 MINUTE READ) [19]

 Reddit is becoming an AI-search battleground as brands look to
influence the conversations that AI models cite. However, Reddit users
and moderators are pushing back against overt brand activity.
Brilliant Earth's attempt to hire a Reddit strategy manager sparked a
warning from r/jewelry moderators about potential promotional content.
Brands can't treat Reddit like another distribution channel and expect
to manufacture positive sentiment.
