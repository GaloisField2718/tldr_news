 TELLING AI TO ACT AS YOUR CUSTOMERS MAKES ITS ANSWER WORSE (7 MINUTE
READ) [5]

 AI-generated customer personas can overestimate purchase intent and
mislead product or campaign decisions. In one test, AI predicted that
52% of shoppers would buy a product when only 33% did. In another, AI
personas were less accurate at predicting headline clicks than a model
asked directly to choose which headlines readers would click.

 GOOGLE'S WHEN TO BUY GUIDE (8 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Google's 2026 holiday shopping guide uses historical pricing data to
identify when discounts peak across retail categories. Black Friday
weekend offers the best selection and savings for electronics, shoes,
fragrances, and appliances. Late December brings deeper discounts on
cosmetics, handbags, and kitchenware. Search data also reveals
last-minute shopping patterns. “Next day shipping” searches peak
December 22, while “same day pickup” peaks December 24.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 GATED CONTENT IS THE #1 ENEMY OF AI SEARCH (7 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Ungate content that helps buyers research categories and compare
vendors to improve AI search visibility. Reserve forms for genuinely
exclusive assets such as personalized ROI audits and proprietary data.
For research reports, publish key findings and methodology on an
accessible landing page while gating the full download. Noindex gated
landing pages and hosted PDFs when appropriate to prevent them from
competing with primary website content.

 YOUR NEXT MARKETING HIRE SHOULD PROBABLY SURPRISE YOU (6 MINUTE READ)
[8]

 As AI makes content production cheaper and product comparisons
easier, brands need to stand out during the awareness stage. Viral
Producers test entertaining campaigns that get people talking about a
brand. Internet Comedians create culturally relevant content people
want to share. Creator Deal Makers build partnerships that help
creators grow their audiences while expanding brand reach. These roles
help brands earn attention rather than compete on content volume

WordPress drafts. Its guide shows how to automate repetitive content

before publication to catch errors and prevent broken automations from

 HOW THE AI BOOM MIGHT REALLY END: 5 SCENARIOS (7 MINUTE READ) [13]

 The AI boom may end not because demand disappears, but because
profits shift away from major AI labs. Open-weight models are driving
prices down while efficiency breakthroughs could reduce computing
costs. AI companies risk failing to capture the value they create
while chipmakers and infrastructure providers could still benefit from
growing AI usage even if leading AI labs lose value.

 WHAT ON EARTH IS GOING ON WITH AI PRICING AND PACKAGING? (8 MINUTE
READ) [14]

 AI companies are struggling to price products because usage costs
vary widely and customers can't predict what credits or tokens will
buy them. ChatGPT and Claude advertise vague usage limits, while
Lovable sells credits without explaining what customers can build with
them. Unlike traditional SaaS pricing based on seats or transactions,
AI costs depend on the complexity of each task and the resources
needed to complete it. Companies must make usage limits and costs
easier to understand while managing unpredictable computing expenses
and preventing abuse of free plans.
