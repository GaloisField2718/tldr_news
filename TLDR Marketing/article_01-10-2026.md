NEWS & TRENDS

 WHO'S BUYING CHATGPT ADS? (8 MINUTE READ) [8]

 ChatGPT Ads adoption is accelerating, with roughly 15K companies
detected using OpenAI's ad tracking pixel. Early adopters skew toward
software and tech, growth-stage companies, and US businesses. Reddit
advertisers show the strongest overlap, with 9.6% also using ChatGPT
Ads. Companies already investing in ChatGPT visibility are also far
more likely to advertise there, suggesting some businesses are
beginning to treat ChatGPT as a broader acquisition channel spanning
organic visibility, referrals, and paid ads.

 THE SECOND PAGE OF GOOGLE WAS A PLACE WEBSITES WENT TO DIE. NOW THEY
DIE ON THE FIRST (12 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Search is growing even as the click disappears. Google Search revenue
rose 17% to $63.3 billion last quarter, while revenue from the ads it
places on publishers' sites fell. The remaining traffic has moved from
news, health, and cooking sites to sites that sell products. How-to
content no longer pays because the answer strips out the brand, but a
buying question can't be answered without naming a product. That makes
detailed product documentation the content worth publishing and
paywalled expertise the content worth selling.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 DON'T MAKE THEM COUNT (2 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Reduce purchase hesitation on ecommerce pages by expressing
quantities in the terms shoppers already use. Gush labels its pack
sizes by occasions, while The Farmer's Dog shows portions and prices
them per day, so shoppers don't have to calculate how much they need
or what it costs. Product pages should spell out bundle contents, show
prices per relevant unit, and answer “How long will this last?”
beside the quantity selector. Watching shoppers choose a quantity can
show you where unclear options create hesitation.

 WIN REPORT: HOW REWRITING ONE BUTTON INCREASED SIGN-UPS BY 39% (3
MINUTE READ) [11]

 A clearer CTA can increase conversions by making an existing offer
easier to understand and less intimidating. Exclaimer changed its
homepage button from “Start free trial” to “Try it free for 14
days,” which made the trial length explicit and framed the action as
a low-commitment opportunity. The rewrite increased trial sign-ups by
39% without changing anything else on the page. The result shows that
improving how an offer is communicated can sometimes outperform
creating a new offer.

 LINKEDIN SPLIT THE FEED IN TWO (AGAIN) (6 MINUTE READ) [13]

 LinkedIn is testing separate For You and Network feeds, with Network
showing only posts from connections and followed accounts. The ranking
system itself is unchanged, but connections could become more
important for distribution. Comments are now ranked differently for
each viewer based on relevance and connections. LinkedIn also launched
a Premium proofreading tool and says long-form LinkedIn articles are
cited more often in AI search than feed posts.

 YOU'RE TAILORING THE WRONG DOCUMENT (7 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Stop tailoring your resume for every job and optimize your LinkedIn
profile for the roles you actually want. Compare 3-5 target job
postings and identify repeated titles, skills, and responsibilities.
Use those terms in your headline, About section, current title,
skills, and Open to Work settings. Replace internal job titles with
market-standard ones and move relevant experience higher on your
profile. Small changes to a few fields can improve recruiter relevance
faster than rewriting resumes for every application.

 A BRUTALLY HONEST READ OF APPSFLYER'S STATE OF CREATIVE OPTIMIZATION
2026 (8 MINUTE READ) [15]

 More AI-generated ad variations aren't producing more winning ads.
The growth is concentrated among the biggest spenders and is mostly
localization and small edits rather than new ideas. Ad algorithms,
meanwhile, put a growing share of spend behind a handful of winners,
and the traits that win on video often lose on statics.

 FREELANCER OR FULL-TIME? LET THE WORK DECIDE (9 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Choose a staffing model based on the work, not just the workload or
budget. Ongoing work that requires ownership and institutional
knowledge points toward a full-time hire. Temporary workload spikes or
specialized projects are better suited to freelancers or contractors.
Contract-to-hire can bridge the gap when it's unclear whether a need
will become permanent. Distinguish a temporary capacity problem from
an ongoing role before adding headcount.

 CMOS MUST RETHINK ENTRY-LEVEL TALENT AS AI ALTERS MARKETING NEEDS (2
MINUTE READ) [17]

 18% of senior marketing leaders have already eliminated roles due to
AI, and a majority of high-performing marketing teams are expected to
cut traditional entry-level jobs by 2030. Cutting those roles entirely
would break the talent pipeline. Junior hires should instead take on
higher-value work earlier, such as evaluating AI outputs and tying
recommendations to outcomes, backed by coaching and mentorship.
