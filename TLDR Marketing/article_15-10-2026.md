 SOCIAL MEDIA AND WELLBEING: IT'S COMPLICATED (4 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Moderate social media use is associated with better well-being than
either low or high use, though differences are generally small. 81% of
adults used social media in the past week, rising to 95% among ages
15-29. High use of 2 or more hours daily is linked to more anger,
loneliness, and sadness. 47% of young adults are high users compared
with 12% of adults aged 50+. High use is also associated with lower
life evaluations than moderate use in high-income countries, but not
elsewhere.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 USE GOOGLE AI MODE TO FIND CONTENT GAPS (1 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Use Google AI Mode to identify content gaps on local service pages.
Search for a service with a city name and review the follow-up
questions to understand what information Google considers relevant
when matching customers with businesses. Interview the business owner
about their services, pricing, and processes, then add those original
insights to service pages to answer customer questions and give AI
context competitors can't easily replicate.

 YOUR AI AGENTS SOUND GENERIC BECAUSE THEIR CONTEXT IS A MESS (5
MINUTE READ) [8]

 AI agents produce generic outputs when their context is poorly
organized, not because they need better prompts or models. To improve
results, separate context into 3 layers. Keep brand voice and audience
profiles in a lean knowledge base. Store transcripts and playbooks in
a searchable library that retrieves relevant passages on demand. Fetch
changing metrics like traffic and pipeline directly from APIs. Map
recurring questions to their data sources before building workflows,
remove outdated content, and assign owners to maintain accuracy.

 HOW TO TURN YOUR SAAS PRICING PAGE INTO A FIRST 30 DAYS CONVERSION
TOOL (7 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Trial users revisit a SaaS pricing page several times in their first
30 days, and each visit asks something different, from whether the
product is a serious option to what an upgrade unlocks right now.
Build the page for repeat scrutiny with a full plan-comparison table
and trust signals. Keep one price framing synced across banners,
emails, and checkout. Hold upgrade pitches until users have made
something, then spread them out rather than stacking them in the
trial's last 48 hours.

 “YOU DIDN'T DO IT RIGHT” (4 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Frameworks like OKRs can fail when they don't fit a company's
culture, team dynamics, or existing processes, even when implemented
correctly. After four failed attempts at OKRs, WP Engine found success
with a metrics framework that let teams choose goals they personally
valued and could directly control. Teams started with one metric for a
quarter, then added one or two more based on what they learned. Rather
than rolling out a framework company-wide, test it with one team,
gather feedback, and adapt it or try another approach if it does not
work.

 WE NOW KNOW THE #1 RANKING FACTOR FOR AI AND LLMS, THE DATA (40
MINUTE READ) [14]

 Contextually relevant backlinks had the strongest correlation with AI
rankings across 17 AI search channels. For GPT-6 Astra, the
correlation averaged 0.57 across industries and was positive in all
100. Unlinked brand mentions also correlated with rankings at 0.41
compared with 0.55 for total backlinks. The findings suggest
prioritizing links and coverage from relevant industry sources over
simply increasing backlink volume.

 HOW SAIE BEAUTY BECAME THE GLOBAL POWERHOUSE EVERYONE'S TALKING ABOUT
(7 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Saie builds products around specific needs it spots in public, then
uses how the product came about as the marketing story. Its Bellini
eyeshadow was developed in its Italian lab with a creator whose
followers had watched her test over 50 shades in three years, while
its City Set spray was named after the Knicks dancers and led to an
official team partnership. The approach shows up in how consumers see
the brand, with 53% saying Saie meets their needs against a 43%
category average.
