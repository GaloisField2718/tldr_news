NEWS & TRENDS

 BUILDING BETTER PUBLIC SIGNALS FOR AI IMPACTS (7 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Analysis of 137,000 r/ChatGPT posts shows how users' experiences and
expectations changed as ChatGPT became part of everyday life. Posts
about therapy and companionship increased after the release of GPT-4o
in May 2024 and reached about 10% of subreddit posts, while discussion
of prompting declined as users became more familiar with AI. The
researchers' real-time monitoring system could have detected the rise
in emotional engagement by October 2024, months before broader
discussion of sycophancy and AI psychosis. The findings suggest that
Reddit can reveal emerging patterns in AI use and potential societal
impacts that may not appear in traditional usage data.

 BIG TECH IS CUTEWASHING ITS AI AGENTS (6 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Cuteness triggers the brain's pleasure centers before conscious
thought, which makes people want to keep cute things close and more
willing to forgive their mistakes. That makes big-eyed mascots a
useful wrapper for AI agents that still hallucinate and need mass
adoption. The same move made the early internet feel less intimidating
in the late 1990s, then faded once the technology became routine.
However, a look shared by every major AI company stops identifying any
one of them and can become shorthand for what people distrust about
the industry.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 THE REASON TO BE ON REDDIT ISN'T FOR AEO (7 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Reddit is more valuable as a mid-funnel research channel than an AEO
play because developers use it to compare tools and real-world
experiences before buying. Treat it like earned media by joining
existing conversations without pitching. Build credibility for about 3
months before approaching moderators about AMAs or sponsored events.
Focus posts on problems and lessons rather than products. Brand
accounts can work when they consistently teach. Measure impact through
customer interviews since Reddit influence rarely appears in
analytics.

 6 STEPS TO BUILDING FAST LANDING PAGES (3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Instead of waiting weeks for overloaded web teams to build campaign
pages, build lean landing pages in Claude Code and get them into
market quickly. Start with a clear conversion problem, then write a
7-line brief and give AI a reusable context file covering brand voice,
product details, customer language, and design rules. Ship at 70-80%
polish while validating high-risk copy. Have stakeholders review a
live preview, then hand off the approved page as a launch-ready
bundle. If it performs, the web team rebuilds it properly.

 5 THINGS CONTENT MARKETERS & SEOS SHOULD STOP DOING IN 2026 (6 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Zero-click search has made five common content habits
counterproductive. Building pages around keywords misses how people
now query, since the average ChatGPT prompt runs 23 words. Grading
content on traffic ignores that the top-ranked page loses 58% of its
CTR under an AI Overview. Publishing more matters less than
maintaining what exists, as 75% of AI-cited pages were updated in the
past year. The last two are relying on owned channels, when 85% of AI
brand mentions come from third parties, and publishing generic guides
with no original data.

 GOOGLE SEARCH DATA ON CRAWLING, INDEXING & SERVING TIMELINES (3
MINUTE READ) [10]

 Google shared new internal data on how long crawling, indexing, and
serving processes typically take. New URLs take about 20 hours to be
discovered while known URLs take about 30 days to refresh. End-to-end
indexing takes about 1.5 hours when processes run normally but can
stretch to months or never. Title and snippet updates typically take
1-2 days, while site moves take 1-3 months. Core update recovery can
take 3-6 months. Delays can stack because each process depends on the
ones before it.

 HOW WE CATCH MESSAGING CAMPAIGN DRIFT BEFORE IT SPREADS (8 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 Campaign drift happens when the messages a business sends shift away
from what was approved. This system for SMS and RCS converts each
message into an embedding and matches it against clusters of
previously judged traffic. Unmatched messages are grouped into new
clusters, and an LLM issues one verdict per cluster against the
approved campaign details rather than per message. Human reviewers
confirm or override each flag before a sender is notified, restricted,
or blocked.

 HOW WRONG IS YOUR MARKETING MIX MODEL (MMM)? (21 MINUTE READ) [12]

 MMM becomes more reliable when media budgets are deliberately phased,
so channels move independently and spend reaches different levels.
Build variation into the media plan by using tactics like weekly
budget changes, dark periods, and peak spend periods instead of
relying on model changes to solve weak data. In the simulation, the
combined approach cut variance by 70% and bias by 47% while keeping
each channel's annual budget unchanged. The tradeoff was a 3.42% loss
in channel-driven revenue, so use lighter phasing when that cost is
too high.
