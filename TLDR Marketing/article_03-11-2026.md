NEWS & TRENDS

 GOOGLE SAYS AI CHATBOTS ARE HURTING ITS BOTTOM LINE (2 MINUTE READ)
[7]

 Google's AI search features are putting pressure on its core
advertising business. Search volume growth slowed from 19% in Q1 to
12% in Q3 while search revenue remained flat. Google says AI Mode
queries generate less ad revenue and plans to expand ads across AI
Overviews and AI Mode to offset the impact. As more users turn to
chatbots for answers, Google faces a challenge in maintaining ad
revenue while investing in increasingly costly AI features.

 CAN ADVERTISING FUND FREE AI? EVIDENCE FROM CHATGPT'S INTERNATIONAL
ADVERTISING ROLLOUT (2 MINUTE READ) [8]

 ChatGPT's current advertising model cannot yet fund free access
because its ads deliver less value to advertisers than established
search channels. Ads reduced organic referral traffic by 6% to 8%, but
conversion rates remained unchanged at 2.21%. Paid ChatGPT visitors
generated $0.89 in revenue per session compared with $2.45 for Google
Search and converted at less than a third of the rate. ChatGPT would
need to lower ad prices substantially to compete with Google and make
its free tier financially sustainable.

 LOW-HANGING FRUIT FOR DEMAND GEN: OFFER-FOCUSED IMAGE ADS (1 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 Drive Q4 sales with offer-focused Google Demand Gen image ads
promoting deals like free gifts or limited-time drops. Use a simple
layout that puts the product front and center alongside its biggest
benefit and a high-contrast offer badge. Highlight 3-6 features, then
test benefit-driven and curiosity-driven headlines. Descriptions can
expand on the benefits or reinforce the deal with social proof.

 SUBSTACK IS A $0 CHEAT CODE FOR YOUR BRAND (3 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Substack can send a newsletter roughly 50X more subscribers than
Google, since every signup surfaces a few newsletters the writer
recommends. Companies are tapping that loop with behind-the-scenes
posts from the people building the product. PostHog recommends 10
engineering newsletters that all recommend it back, while Ramp gets a
recommendation from TBPN's Substack as part of its sponsorship.
Substack's audience stats rank which newsletters already share a
brand's readers, pointing to the best swap partners.

 REACH IS NOT PROOF (4 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Retailer-owned shopping agents have the strongest evidence of sales
impact, while ChatGPT and Google have massive reach but no published
purchase results. Amazon reports nearly $12B in incremental annualized
sales from its assistant, while Walmart says Sparky users spend 40%
more per order. Brands should make product data complete and
accessible so agents can accurately evaluate and recommend their
products. AI-driven discovery is already happening, but sales impact
remains unproven across most categories.

 WHEN BUNDLING HURTS THE BOTTOM LINE (6 MINUTE READ) [14]

 When demand far outstrips inventory, selling items separately beats
package deals and earns nearly as much as the best possible bundling
mix. Bundling works with ample stock because it averages out how much
each buyer values each item, widening the customer base. Under
scarcity, that same averaging caps how high a bundle can be priced,
while separate prices capture each item's top bidders. Selling
separately can start to win once available inventory falls below about
30-60% of potential customers.

 CREATOR SPONSORSHIP PRICES ARE NEGOTIATED UPWARD FROM A LOW FIRST
OFFER, DATA SHOWS (5 MINUTE READ) [15]

 Creator sponsorship deals often close well above a brand's first
offer, which averaged just 27% of the creator's quoted price across
124 negotiations. Creators can negotiate higher rates by adjusting
deliverables rather than cutting prices and using average views and
CPMs to justify fees. Paid usage adds 30-40% to the rate. Longer-term
partnerships also provide more predictable income while helping
creators reduce reliance on one-off sponsorships.
