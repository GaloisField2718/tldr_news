NEWS & TRENDS

 EXPERIENTIAL MARKETING TREND: BRAND GAZETTES (3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Brand gazettes are becoming a way for brands to extend physical
events into tangible content experiences. Publications are designed
specifically for an activation and give attendees something to read
and keep after the event. Grey Goose used its Gazette to tell stories
about the brand's French heritage while Disney recreated the fictional
Runway magazine from The Devil Wears Prada 2 as a real 90-page issue.
QR codes and other interactive elements can also connect print content
to digital experiences.

 CASH-STRAPPED AMERICANS TAP HOME EQUITY AND AI TO KEEP SPENDING (4
MINUTE READ) [9]

 Consumers are maintaining spending despite higher prices by finding
ways to offset rising costs. Grocery prices are up 32% since 2020, yet
spending on cars, clothing, and restaurants still increased in August.
Some consumers are using credit or drawing from savings while others
are cutting costs elsewhere. AI is also becoming a budgeting tool,
with one consumer using ChatGPT to compare grocery prices and save
about $100 a month.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 YOU AUTOMATED YOUR BUILD. YOUR GROWTH IS STILL MANUAL (4 MINUTE READ)
[10]

 Growth compounds when each marketing activity creates inputs for the
next one. The author focuses on 3 growth loops: app installs can
improve store visibility, content can inform the next post, and
product usage can generate reviews and user content. The content loop
is often the slowest because it requires the most manual work, yet it
can have significant results. Find the slowest step in each loop and
remove unnecessary manual handoffs so each cycle happens faster.

 WE VIBE CODED OUR OWN CALENDLY. OUR AI AGENT PUSHED US TO DO IT, AND
IT WAS RIGHT (3 MINUTE READ) [11]

 A 3-person team built its own scheduling tool in 20 mins after its AI
agent identified a limitation with Calendly. The custom booker
connects scheduling to Salesforce and prospect data, showing each
prospect a personalized prospectus and routing them to the right
salesperson based on account ownership. The team could have integrated
Calendly, but the agent determined that most of the custom logic would
still need to be built. The default remains buying software, with
custom builds reserved for narrow workflows where proprietary data
creates an advantage.

 BUILDING ADVERTISING FOR THE WAY PEOPLE USE AI (4 MINUTE READ) [14]

 OpenAI is testing visual ads in ChatGPT that show products and
services through images during image generation. The format lets
advertisers demonstrate products in use and link to more information.

 PREDICTION: THE NEXT MASSIVE GOOGLE UPDATE IS AROUND THE CORNER (16
MINUTE READ) [15]

 Google tends to rewrite its documentation shortly before a major
update, and it has just rewritten its guidance on AI content and
authorship. Publishing AI text at scale without manual review now
counts as little to no effort, and invented authors with AI headshots
count as deception. The GEO tactics built to win AI answers, including
self-ranking listicles and paid mentions, repeat old SEO spam patterns
that Google has historically shut down. Cutting or consolidating
scaled content before an update is often easier than recovering after
one.

 I SHOPPED FOR SOCCER SHOES WITH CHATGPT, CLAUDE, AND MUSE. ONE GOT ME
CLOSER TO BUYING (3 MINUTE READ) [16]

 AI shopping assistants need to balance choice with guidance to build
purchase confidence. ChatGPT won by showing 3 soccer shoe options with
a recommended pick and clear comparison. Claude offered useful product
details but lacked images and purchase links. Muse made shopping more
engaging with interactive questions and visuals but recommended the
wrong shoes. The test suggests giving shoppers a few options plus a
clear recommendation can make AI-assisted purchases easier.

 THE ECONOMICS OF THE STREAK: LOYALTY PROGRAMS AND STARS (10 MINUTE
READ) [17]

 Loyalty programs use loss aversion and goal gradients to make
accumulated points feel like possessions worth protecting. A 100-day
streak can become more motivating to preserve than the underlying
habit, while being close to a reward or tier encourages extra
spending. Programs also benefit from unredeemed points through
breakage and collect purchase data. They can reward behaviors
customers already value or drive spending and engagement mainly to
avoid losing accumulated progress.
