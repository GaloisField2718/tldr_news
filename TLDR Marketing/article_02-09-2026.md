 GOOGLE AI OVERVIEWS PUSHING SEARCHERS INTO AI MODE, DROPS SHOW MORE
BUTTON (2 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Google is expanding AI Overviews for some queries, showing a fuller
AI answer and the “Ask anything” box by default instead of
requiring users to click “Show more”. This pushes traditional
organic results further down the page, giving publishers and SEOs less
visibility while Google steers more searches toward AI Mode.

 FTC SUES AMAZON, ACCUSING THE E-COMMERCE GIANT OF MISLEADING
ADVERTISERS (3 MINUTE READ) [5]

 The FTC and 22 state attorneys general sued Amazon on Monday,
alleging it secretly overcharged advertisers by more than $20 billion
through hidden auction surcharges dating back to a 2019 rule change.
Amazon had told advertisers its second-price auction system only
charged the minimum bid needed to win, but the FTC says Amazon quietly
added an undisclosed "soft reserve price" that pushed costs higher.
Amazon calls the lawsuit misguided and says its auction system saved
advertisers $8 billion between 2021 and 2025, with a trial set to test
which version holds up.

 HOW WE'D MARKET A $400 SKINCARE DEVICE BEFORE BLACK FRIDAY (7 MINUTE
READ) [6]

 Black Friday marketing starts well before the sale for
high-consideration products. SparkToro's research found LED skincare
shoppers spend months comparing products, reading reviews, and
researching results. They use search, AI tools, YouTube, Reddit, and
beauty publications. Brands can use this research phase to answer key
questions and influence buying decisions early. That way, BFCM
campaigns can focus on converting shoppers instead of introducing the
product.

 THE PAGES YOUR AGENCY BUILT TO WIN AI CITATIONS JUST LOST HALF OF
THEM (3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 ChatGPT and Google are reducing visibility for the listicles and
comparison pages that have powered many agency AI visibility programs
since 2024. ChatGPT citations for listicles fell 50.5%, from 15.77% to
7.80%, after its August 6 update, while product pages rose to 16.39%
of retrieved pages. Brands should audit citation performance by page
type and invest more in primary product and pricing pages that provide
specifications and real information directly on the vendor's domain.
AI visibility now favors useful source material over pages built
mainly to match "best," "top," or "vs" queries.

 THE STORE ISN'T DEAD—BUT THE MERCHANT MODEL IS (8 MINUTE READ) [11]


 More than 7,300 US stores closed last year, the most since 2020,
while store openings held just as strong, evidence that retail formats
are evolving rather than physical stores disappearing. The actual weak
point is the merchant model, which has retailers forecasting demand
and owning inventory months before they know if it will sell. A
growing number of retailers are shifting to a store-in-store setup
instead, renting curated space to brands that carry their own
inventory risk, the way Harrods lets luxury labels run their own
sections and set their own prices. AI shopping agents are pushing the
shift further, since faster demand swings raise the cost of the
merchant model's forecasting bets.

 YOUR MARKETING PROBLEM MIGHT NOT BE A MARKETING PROBLEM (3 MINUTE
READ) [12]

 Marketing problems often stem from internal misalignment rather than
weak marketing strategy. Product, Sales, leadership, and Marketing can
tell different stories about the customer, problem, differentiation,
and value, which creates confusion that campaigns cannot fix. A simple
alignment test is to ask five leaders to describe the company in one
sentence and compare their answers across five questions about the
target customer, problem, differentiation, brand reputation, and buyer
beliefs. Marketing becomes more effective when the business first
agrees on what it stands for and what buyers need to understand.

 SHOW THE COST OF EACH AI PROMPT (2 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Showing users the cost or remaining capacity of each AI prompt can
lead to more intentional usage and better outcomes. Researchers
analyzed 1.8M+ prompts from 61,000+ users and found that users spent
5% more time refining prompts and 8% less time generating duplicate
image variants when usage costs were visible. AI products should make
consumption easier to understand by showing prompt costs, token usage,
or remaining prompts instead of abstract quotas. Teams using AI should
also expose usage limits so employees make more deliberate choices
about when and how they prompt.
