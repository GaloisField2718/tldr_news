NEWS & TRENDS

 INSTAGRAM PUTS NEW LIMITS ON UNDISCLOSED AI PROFILES (2 MINUTE READ)
[6]

 Instagram is renaming its “AI creator” label to “AI-generated
profile” to make it clearer when an account features a fully
AI-generated person. It will also limit recommendations for unlabeled
AI profiles, while creators who proactively disclose their AI identity
won't see a reach penalty. The change targets transparency around AI
personas, not creators who use AI tools in their content workflow.

 MUSIC LICENSING CLAIMS ARE COMING FOR MARKETERS (6 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Music licensing claims are becoming a growing risk as brands and
influencers use copyrighted content without proper clearance. Old
social posts can also create exposure when licensing records are
missing. Brands need to audit existing content and ensure influencer
agreements cover licensing responsibilities.

 ADD G2 BADGES TO RETARGETING AD CAMPAIGNS (1 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Use G2 badges in retargeting ads to give hesitant buyers an extra
push. The familiar design builds recognition, while third-party
validation can reduce the perceived risk of choosing your product.
Pair the badge with a product benefit, category, or testimonial so it
supports the ad rather than carrying it alone.

 WHAT MAKES A GOOD LISTICLE? (6 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Freshness is the strongest ranking signal for listicles, so keep
dates recent and easy for search engines to interpret. Explicit list
counts also tend to perform better, with larger numbers often ranking
higher. By contrast, word count, images, author bios, schema markup,
and extra headings don't reliably improve rankings. Self-promotional
listicles can still perform well, but putting your own product first
doesn't appear to drive that growth.

 NO AI, NO UPLOADS, NO ADS: DIVINE'S CEO SAYS FRICTION COULD SAVE
SOCIAL MEDIA (5 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Divine, the app reviving Vine, bans AI content and requires videos to
be filmed and verified live on-device. With no ads or algorithmic
feed, it's betting that authenticity and familiar faces will matter
more as AI floods social media with content.

 THE NEXT ERA OF MARKETING MAY BE A LOT SMALLER THAN YOU THINK (3
MINUTE READ) [15]

 Marketing is moving away from mass reach and toward smaller,
high-trust audiences. Brands like Urban Outfitters and Tracksuit are
prioritizing participation and niche creators over follower counts,
while Charli XCX is building intimate fan experiences before going
mass. Deep audience relationships are becoming more valuable than
sheer scale.
