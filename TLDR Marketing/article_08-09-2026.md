 THE BIG WINNERS AND LOSERS IN US WEBSITE TRAFFIC OVER THE PAST YEAR
(7 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Google and YouTube still dominate web traffic in the US, but AI is
reshaping the landscape. ChatGPT traffic jumped 48.4% year over year
to 1.09B monthly visits, while Bing fell 50.4%. Meanwhile, GA4 is
losing visibility into AI-driven traffic, with “direct” traffic
increasingly absorbing visits it can't attribute.

 STREAMING IS RAISING PRICES FASTER THAN CABLE EVER DID (3 MINUTE
READ) [5]

 Streaming prices have risen 3X faster than inflation since 2022. The
8 major services now cost around $151/month without ads or bundles, up
from $90 four years ago. As subscription fatigue grows, Gen Z's
“streaming cycling” trend is gaining traction. Consumers subscribe
to one service at a time, then cancel and switch to another.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 THE BEST ADS DON'T CREATE DESIRE, THEY BORROW IT (7 MINUTE READ) [6]

 The best copy doesn't create desire. It taps into wants customers
already have, then connects the product to those deeper motivations.
Identify the emotional driver behind the purchase, name the obstacle
standing in the way, and show the customer what success looks like
through a vivid, specific scenario. Rather than simply stating a
benefit, make readers experience it before they buy.

 SHE PAID PEOPLE TO CANCEL HER COMPETITOR (2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Cursor turned a giveaway into a competitor-poaching tool by offering
its $200/month plan free to customers who canceled a rival and proved
it with a screenshot. Claiming meant DMing the employee running the
offer from her personal account, creating a private channel for
retention offers and switching feedback. A second run added a 24-hour
deadline and converted even better. Customers also posted cancellation
screenshots publicly, creating free marketing.

 NEW GOOGLE ADS AUTOMATED PROMOTIONS (1 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Google Ads can now automatically extract active promotions from
landing pages and attach them to eligible Search and Performance Max
ads. The account-level feature can surface offers such as “20%
off” or “buy one get one free” without advertisers manually
uploading each promotion. It applies only to eligible campaigns using
location assets that do not already have manual promotions.

 DO WE STILL NEED GOOGLE DISAVOW? (2 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Google's Disavow tool is now mainly a recovery tool for sites hit
with a manual action for unnatural links. Most sites don't need it
after Penguin shifted spammy backlinks toward page-level devaluation.
SEO efforts are better spent on useful content, UX, and earning
credible links. Treat disavow as a targeted response to a confirmed
manual action, not routine backlink cleanup.

 HOW NETFLIX MEASURES THE MARKETING YOU CANNOT A/B TEST (5 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 Netflix uses geographic holdouts to measure channels that can't be
A/B tested at the user level, including TV, OOH, and sponsorships. The
approach measures incremental impact but requires careful decisions
around test size, timing, media spillover, and carryover. Repeating
tests builds stronger evidence, while campaigns without historical
data require different methodologies. The challenge is getting
leadership to protect test windows and accept the tradeoff between
media delivery and measurement certainty.

 THE AI HOURS NOBODY ON YOUR MARKETING TEAM IS COUNTING (6 MINUTE
READ) [12]

 Every AI workflow becomes a small maintenance job once it ships.
Models change, connected tools get updated, and new edge cases
surface. The person who built it is often the only one who knows how
to fix it, so the workflow can quickly revert to manual when they're
unavailable. Teams rarely plan for this upkeep, leaving it to compete
with slower, harder-to-measure work like building visibility and
trust.
