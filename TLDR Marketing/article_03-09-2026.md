 AFFILIATE HAS OUTGROWN THE BOTTOM OF THE FUNNEL (5 MINUTE READ) [4]

 Affiliate marketing is undergoing four major shifts: it must
influence customers before they are ready to buy, trust matters more
than traffic alone, creator and affiliate strategies need to operate
as one partnership strategy, and measurement must extend beyond last
click. Brands should expand partner programs beyond coupon and
cashback sites to include publishers, comparison sites, reviewers, and
creators that shape discovery and consideration. They also need
broader measurement and shared data to value influence across the full
customer journey. These changes position affiliate as a growth
strategy that helps create demand, not just capture existing demand.

 AMID GROWING AI DISTRUST, HUMANITY TAKES A FRONT SEAT IN ADVERTISING
(4 MINUTE READ) [5]

 The growing backlash against AI is prompting brands to make humanity
itself a central theme in advertising, either through explicit
messaging or more tactile, imperfect creative. Tech brands such as
Anthropic and Meta are using optimistic narratives to address concerns
about AI, while brands such as Polaroid, Pinterest, and Heineken are
encouraging consumers to reconnect with the physical world. Brands can
reinforce this positioning through film, handmade aesthetics, natural
elements, and behind-the-scenes content that shows real people
creating the work. The strategy works best when humanity reflects a
genuine point of view rather than becoming another empty brand
buzzword.

 4 FUNNEL FIXES (2 MINUTE READ) [6]

 A creator with 10K email subscribers and a paid community had a solid
foundation, but an audit uncovered 4 ways to grow revenue. First, fix
leaks in the opt-in flow with a thank-you page and by removing double
opt-in. Then, encourage monthly members to switch to annual plans for
more upfront revenue and stronger retention. From there, build
evergreen onboarding and cancellation sequences, and add monthly
promotions to drive more signups without relying solely on regular
emails.

 TURN SITEMAPS INTO SEO INTEL (1 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Competitor XML sitemaps can act as a weekly SEO change log. Save a
snapshot of a competitor's sitemap, then compare it each week to spot
new pages, removals, and emerging content clusters. This tactic can
reveal what competitors are building before those changes show up in
rankings. A Google Sheet tracker and Apps Script is provided in the
comment thread.

 YOUTUBE AEO GUIDE: HOW TO DOMINATE AI SEARCH WITH YOUTUBE (7 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 YouTube now draws roughly 200x more AI citations than any other
platform, and its share of social citations 4x in four months to
overtake Reddit as the top-cited social source in Q1 2026. Automatic
transcripts are the core driver, turning every explainer video into a
searchable text document AI models can parse directly instead of
relying on tags or descriptions alone. That citation strength
concentrates almost entirely in long-form video, since AI models
weight depth and comprehensive topic coverage that Shorts aren't built
to carry.

 HOW TO FIND AND PITCH REAL CASE STUDIES FOR DIGITAL PR WITH BECCA
PEEL (11 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Case studies are becoming a critical part of digital PR because
journalists need real people who can make data credible, relevant, and
harder to replicate with AI. PR teams should start with their own
networks, then verify each person through signals such as LinkedIn,
business listings, websites, and direct calls before pitching. Strong
case studies offer a genuinely new insight, connect directly to the
campaign data, and give journalists an exclusive human angle they
cannot simply find through Google.

 HOW E.L.F. BEAUTY IS BUILDING AI INTO ITS COMMUNITY MANAGEMENT (2
MINUTE READ) [13]

 E.l.f. Beauty's AI reply tool, E.l.f.luencer, is trained on the
brand's own stylebook and years of past community-manager responses to
answer social comments automatically, with every reply still reviewed
by a human before it posts. The tool now generates 90% of the brand's
TikTok and Instagram replies in E.l.f.'s specific tone, language, and
emoji use, pushing the response rate from 30-40% before launch to more
than 80% now. That volume would be impossible for a manual team to
match, and the same AI investment underpins a broader program of 85
agentic pilots the company is moving toward full production.
