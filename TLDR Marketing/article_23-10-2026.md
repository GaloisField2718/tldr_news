NEWS & TRENDS

 YOUTUBE CULTURE & TRENDS REPORT: HOW TECHNOLOGY IS CHANGING PERSONAL
EXPRESSION (6 MINUTE READ) [5]

 AI is becoming a tool for personal expression rather than just
content production. 72% of video posters aged 14-44 have used AI to
create or edit images or videos, and 61% are interested in using AI to
insert themselves into content. Creators use AI to restore childhood
memories, and hobbyists turn fashion sketches into realistic designs
and bring fan fiction to life.

 HOW GEN Z IS CHANGING PACKAGING (5 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Brands are moving from safe, minimal packaging toward designs Gen Z
wants to display and collect, since the package stays visible in
content long after purchase. Mane wraps heat protectants and smoothing
products in consistent pink packaging that makes the whole line
recognizable before anyone reads a label. Touchland keeps its
sanitizer format fixed while rotating limited character
collaborations, and Reale Actives pairs playful names like Go Calm
with clear descriptions of what each product does.

 WIN REPORT: HOW A SIMPLER HOMEPAGE INCREASED SALES BY 24% (3 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 The Raw Honey Shop increased sales by 24% by simplifying its homepage
and making key selling points easier to find. The original page buried
product information and trust signals in lengthy brand storytelling,
and 50% of desktop visitors never reached the products section. The
redesign replaced long paragraphs with a scannable checklist of
product benefits, connected a beekeeper's photo to a specific trust
claim, and added a “Shop our honeys” button near the top of the
page. The test shows how clear value propositions and prominent calls
to action can help visitors make purchase decisions with less effort.

 HOW TO CREATE LINKS FOR SEO GOOGLE CAN'T IGNORE (7 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Build links from topically relevant sites rather than chasing volume.
Place links high in article text with descriptive surrounding context
and natural anchors that reflect each page's unique purpose. Earn
independent editorial links that add new information instead of
relying on sitewide links or mass syndication. Space link-building
efforts over time and keep the source page, anchor text, surrounding
content, and destination closely aligned. Avoid redirects or content
changes that weaken a page's topical relevance.

 IS EMAIL MARKETING DEAD? NO, BUT EVERYTHING HAS CHANGED (5 MINUTE
READ) [13]

 Email marketing still builds valuable audience relationships, even as
open and click rates become less reliable measures of performance.
Apple's Mail Privacy Protection inflates opens, while AI summaries can
answer subscribers' questions without a click, making email's
influence harder to track. Treat your email list as an audience to
nurture rather than a pipeline of customers to convert, especially
since roughly 95% of the B2B market is out of market at any given
time. Measure performance across quarters using branded search,
retention, replies, referrals, leads, and sales instead of relying on
opens and clicks.

 THE FORM BEFORE THE ANSWER: HOW CHATGPT QUESTIONS BUYERS BEFORE
RECOMMENDING ANYTHING (8 MINUTE READ) [14]

 79.4% of buying prompts trigger a follow-up questionnaire before
recommendations. Most forms arrive pre-filled, with 89.7% of budget
questions defaulting to the middle price range. Brands can use these
forms to identify the criteria ChatGPT uses to narrow choices. Track
them across repeated prompts and markets, then align product content
with those criteria and ensure current prices and purchase links are
easy for AI to retrieve.
