NEWS & TRENDS

 265 MILLION PEOPLE STILL LOVE CANVA. THE MARKET TOOK $11 BILLION OFF
ANYWAY (7 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Canva has 265M monthly users, but AI is changing how those users
interact with the product. AI agents can now create designs without
sending users to Canva, potentially bypassing the platform entirely.
At the same time, AI assistants are recommending Canva, with users
having 26M+ conversations with its ChatGPT app. The shift creates a
new challenge for brands. AI can both send customers to a product and
remove the need for it.

 AMERICANS AREN'T SOLD ON BUSINESSES USING AI IN ADVERTISING (2 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 Consumers aren't rejecting AI in advertising outright - they're
drawing a sharp line at whose face and voice ends up in the ad. Most
Americans accept AI for brainstorming and drafting, and just over half
are fine with AI producing the final ad content itself. However,
nearly two in three say using AI to create a person's likeness or
voice crosses a line, even when a business discloses it. Young adults
under 30 are the least forgiving group by far, with 66% viewing
AI-made advertising negatively overall and 73% rejecting AI-generated
people or voices specifically.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 WIN REPORT: WHY ADDING AN EXTRA STEP TO A FUNNEL INCREASED ORDER
VALUE BY 28% (2 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Moving an upsell to its own step can increase order value without
hurting conversion when it removes competing decisions from the main
purchase page. A South American gift company moved add-ons from the
bottom of its product pages to an interstitial page after customers
selected a gift. The change increased revenue-per-order by 28% and had
no impact on overall conversion. The test shows that funnel friction
depends on context, and giving secondary offers their own moment can
make them more visible without making the purchase harder.

 CHATGPT DEFAULTS TO BRANDED SEARCH FOR "X BEST SOFTWARE" QUERIES (3
MINUTE READ) [11]

 ChatGPT often starts product searches from a preselected set of
brands it already associates with a category, making category
eligibility more important than traditional SEO rankings. In one
example, its first search for enterprise AI agent platforms already
included Microsoft, Salesforce, Google, ServiceNow, IBM, and AWS
before any neutral market scan. Challenger brands need repeated
category association through owned content, comparison pages,
third-party validation, analyst coverage, customer proof, and clear
product messaging to enter these default vendor sets. Research
suggests that appearing in ChatGPT's generated search query can be
worth about 33 times more than simply being discoverable.

technology partners practical triggers for disclosing AI-generated or
AI-assisted text, images, video, audio, synthetic voices, digital

 ChatGPT for Teens has added safety measures and tools designed to
reduce AI-assisted cheating. The experience includes Study Mode, which
guides students through problems instead of giving quick answers, plus

and partnering with CodeAI to teach teens how to use and question AI.

 WHY TYLER SPEEGLE TREATS HIS CREATOR BUSINESS LIKE A MARKETER WOULD
(5 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Creator Tyler Speegle uses his marketing background to treat content

 ANTHROPIC'S TEXT WATERMARKING PROVES AI COMPANIES DO NOT CARE AT ALL
ABOUT WRITING (7 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Anthropic's new watermarking works by quietly changing which word
Claude picks when two options seem equally good. It swaps in a hidden
key instead of plain randomness, so the pattern stays invisible to
readers but detectable later. The company treats that kind of choice
as low stakes, the difference between grey and overcast. However, word
choice for a human writer usually carries intent, mood, and context
that a statistical model can't see. Anthropic says internal testing
found no measurable drop in quality or creativity from the change. The
bigger problem isn't the watermark itself. It's that a company
building writing tools thinks words are interchangeable in the first
place.
