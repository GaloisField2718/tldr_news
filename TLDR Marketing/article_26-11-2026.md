NEWS & TRENDS

 CONSUMER TRUST AROUND BLACK FRIDAY DEALS IS DOWN (4 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Black Friday has stretched into a month of discounts, which removed
the urgency that once made a 30% off banner work. Nearly
three-quarters of shoppers now call the event more hype than genuine
savings, even at brands whose deals match last year's. More sale days
spread the same demand thinner, but brands say opting out is not an
option. The ones adapting rotate individual products through deals to
rebuild momentum or move the promo budget into a brand campaign
instead.

 AI IS MOVING WAY TOO FAST (9 MINUTE READ) [6]

 AI capabilities are advancing faster than society can adapt. While
human expertise still plays a critical role in directing AI today,
that advantage may not last as models develop stronger reasoning and
research abilities. The bigger concern is that AI labs are racing
toward superintelligence without clear plans to control it or align it
with human interests. Political systems may struggle to keep pace with
the technology's rapid evolution.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 WIN REPORT: THE THREE WORDS WE ADDED TO A PRODUCT PAGE THAT INCREASED
CONVERSIONS BY 12% (2 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Adding context to customer review counts can make social proof more
persuasive and increase product conversions. DiamondBack Covers tested
replacing an unlabeled review count with the phrase “2,863
Real-World Reviews” next to its 4.9-star rating. The change
increased conversions by 12%. Research showed that 16% of skeptical
buyers cited positive reviews and research as a reason they ultimately
purchased. Clearly identifying reviews as real customer experiences
can help reduce purchase hesitation.

 BEHIND GOOD CONTENT: AEO EXPERT KEVIN WHITE (34 MINUTE PODCAST) [8]

 This podcast features Kevin White sharing how brands can improve
their visibility in AI search. He explains the 3 stages of AEO - being
cited, mentioned, and recommended - then breaks down Scrunch's
strategy for increasing its AI mention rate from 15% to 40% in 2
months. The playbook focuses on updating existing content, earning
placements on trusted third-party sites, and publishing targeted FAQs.
He also explains why AEO requires ongoing updates, as citations
typically decay within 6-8 weeks.

 AI SHOPPING AGENTS BOUGHT A FAKE DEAL 37% OF THE TIME – CLAUDE OPUS
NEVER DID (4 MINUTE READ) [10]

 AI shopping agents often prioritize ratings and low prices over signs
of fraudulent listings, creating an opportunity for misleading
products to win recommendations. In a test of 9 AI models across 6,156
shopping trips, 8 models bought an obviously fake deal 37% of the
time, even when they recognized suspicious pricing. Listing order also
mattered: agents bought the fake 65% of the time when it appeared
first, compared with 34% elsewhere. When asked to find the best value,
agents chose the fake 53% of the time, suggesting that AI shopping
recommendations can be vulnerable to misleading reviews, pricing, and
product positioning.

 HOLIDAYS HAPPEN ON TIKTOK (7 MINUTE READ) [11]

 TikTok's holiday guide on seasonal trends, creator recommendations,
and shopping content. This year's emerging trends include Whimsy
Christmas décor, DIY gifts, nostalgic decorations, and cozy holiday
experiences. Posts about stocking stuffers are up almost 200%, while
beauty advent calendar posts have grown nearly 640%, highlighting
demand for smaller, giftable products. Holiday searches are also
surging, including a more than 40x increase in searches for Christmas
decorations and a 31x increase for Christmas lists. Brands can tap
into these trends through creator partnerships, shoppable content, and
timely products that reflect what consumers are already searching for
and discussing.

 HOW MANY PRODUCTS DOES AN ECOMMERCE CATEGORY PAGE NEED? (7 MINUTE
READ) [12]

 Across 25,000+ Shopify category pages, most of the gain in Google
visibility came from the first 16 products, with much smaller gains
after that. The cutoff moved with search demand, since niche
categories stopped improving at 9-16 products while mid-volume ones
kept climbing until 33-64. Homepage links mattered more as categories
grew, since linked ones ranked 15-30 points higher at every size and
kept climbing after unlinked ones stalled at 9-16 products. Empty
categories are better merged or redirected, since nearly half still
appeared in search with nothing on the page.

 HOW BLACK FRIDAY CHANGES AI SHOPPING ANSWERS (21 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Black Friday changes which pages AI shopping answers cite, not which
brands they name. Publisher best-of lists grew to 65-94% of Black
Friday citations in toys and superstores, with most of the gain in the
final 10 days. Brand websites gained none of that share, and most of
it went to pages published that season rather than older ones
refreshed with the new year. On ChatGPT's product tiles, about 4 in 10
products got cheaper that week, but the most-reviewed product came
first more often than the cheapest.
