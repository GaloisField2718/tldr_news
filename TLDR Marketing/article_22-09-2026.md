NEWS & TRENDS

 60-70% OF INFLUENCER BRIEFS NOW FAVOR MID-TIER CREATORS AS BRANDS
QUESTION COST OF MEGA INFLUENCERS (3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Brands are favoring mid-tier creators as engagement and measurable
performance matter more than follower count. Creators with 50K–500K
followers can deliver lower CPVs, while historical views, watch time,
and audience fit increasingly shape pricing decisions. Mega creators
still command premiums when they offer mass reach or cultural
relevance, but brands are pushing for fees that better reflect
expected campaign performance.

 WHY I AM BULLISH ON CHATGPT ADS (4 MINUTE READ) [8]

 OpenAI can build an effective ad model if it keeps ads separate from
answers and clearly labels them, similar to early Google AdWords.
ChatGPT Ads reached $1B in annualized revenue in under 200 days,
suggesting strong advertiser demand before the format has fully
matured. Running shoes, travel, and price comparisons are especially
promising because users now ask ChatGPT to research these commercial
decisions instead of using Google.

 10 UNCONVENTIONAL WAYS TO BRAG ABOUT YOUR CUSTOMERS (4 MINUTE READ)
[9]

 Customer proof lands harder as a story or a stunt than as a logo row.
Deel lists Snoop Dogg as a customer, Hinge commissioned novels from
real couples' stories, and Shopify lit up the Las Vegas Sphere with
every merchant's first sale. Smaller versions work too, like turning a
"too advanced" one-star review into an ad or showing "customer since"
dates under logos.

 GREAT PRODUCTS NEED GREAT STORIES (5 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Great products still need strong stories that help people imagine how
they fit into everyday life. Lead with the customer experience rather
than technical capabilities, and show the human outcome the product
enables. VisiCalc gave people a clear reason to buy the Apple II,
while Tesla could frame FSD around making long-distance travel less
exhausting rather than simply highlighting autonomous driving. Turn
complex technology into a relatable vision of what life could look
like.

 5 SPICY AI MARKETING TAKES (4 MINUTE READ) [14]

 AI is changing marketing without necessarily reducing demand for
marketers. Leaner teams may be offset by the growing number of
businesses needing distribution. Chat ads could become a major ad
format as LLM usage grows. Shared repositories can preserve
institutional knowledge and give AI better context. Employee-generated
content can extend brand reach through trusted voices. As AI handles
more digital interactions, IRL events may become more valuable for
creating human connection and closing deals.

 CHATGPT NOW KNOWS WHAT YOU DO ON OTHER WEBSITES VIA AD COLLECTOR (4
MINUTE READ) [15]

 OpenAI's ad pixel uses a one-year __obi cookie tied to ChatGPT
accounts and sends data to OpenAI when an advertiser's tag loads. It
also collects hashed emails and phone numbers, unencrypted city and
postal codes, and page paths containing medical and debt topics. The
pixel works for logged-out users but appears limited to Chrome on
Android, as iOS browsers block it.

 ‘NOT ENOUGH USAGE': YOUR BIGGEST CHURN REASON IS A BROKEN HABIT
LOOP (13 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Usage churn drives 26-40% of subscription app cancellations, often
because users fail to build a lasting habit rather than because of
price. Instead of defaulting to discounts, analyze engagement before
cancellation to find where the habit loop breaks. Weak triggers may
call for timely re-engagement, while poor onboarding or unclear value
requires helping users reach meaningful outcomes sooner. Retention
improves when the fix addresses why usage dropped rather than treating
“not enough usage” as the root cause.
