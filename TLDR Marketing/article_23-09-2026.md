 IT'S TIME TO RETHINK REDDIT'S ROLE IN THE MEDIA PLAN (3 MINUTE READ)
[5]

 Reddit can now play a broader role in media plans because its users
already show strong purchase intent across categories, not just gaming
or IT. New bidding options, lead generation, and age targeting give
advertisers more control, and early tests of the new bidding
structures showed a 15% reduction in CPA. Brands can treat Reddit as a
performance channel when they have a strong measurement framework,
rather than limiting it to experimental or upper funnel spend. Reddit
still needs to preserve the authenticity that creates its value, since
making ads too intrusive could undermine the user behavior that
generates purchase intent in the first place.

 GOOGLE'S OWN NOTEBOOKLM BECOMES A TARGET FOR PARASITE SEO (2 MINUTE
READ) [6]

 A public Google NotebookLM report was cited as an AI Overview source
within 24 hours of being published. Public notebooks promoting e-bike
and peptide coupon codes, crypto refunds, backlink tools, and
Instagram-engagement services are now showing up indexed on Google's
own domain, borrowing ranking trust that a standalone site wouldn't
have. Blocking crawler access and adding noindex work differently,
too, and applying either broadly would also remove the legitimate
study guides and business manuals the feature was built for.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 ADD TARGET KEYWORDS IN THE FIRST LINES OF LINKEDIN POSTS (1 MINUTE
READ) [7]

 Put target keywords in the first 1-2 lines of LinkedIn posts because
LinkedIn uses the opening text in the post URL slug. This can create a
public connection between your name and the terms you want to rank
for, helping Google surface your LinkedIn content for relevant
searches.

 YOUR CONTENT ISN'T BAD, BUT NOBODY KNOWS WHY THEY SHOULD CARE (3
MINUTE READ) [8]

 The clearest diagnostic is whether someone could describe what a
brand talks about in one sentence when recommending them to a friend,
and a vague answer usually means the positioning, not the writing, is
the problem. People don't follow accounts they vaguely find
interesting - they follow accounts that feel predictable in a good
way, recognizable rather than random. A content pillar, a specific
territory like social strategy or industry trends rather than an
occasional topic, gives someone a consistent answer to that test
instead of a different subject every day.

 BLOGGING STATISTICS 2026 (10 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Reported blog performance just hit an all-time low, with only 13.9%
of respondents calling results strong, and the cause traces back to
abandoned tactics rather than AI. Influencer collaboration, the single
strongest predictor of success in the dataset at 2.6x the benchmark,
has dropped from 25% of respondents in 2017 to 7% today, while
original research, keyword research, and formal human editing have all
declined too. AI use jumped from near zero to 92% in three years, yet
users and non-users report strong results at the same rate, with only
non-users skewing toward disappointing ones.

 HOW TO USE AI TO BE MORE CREATIVE (2 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Refining an idea with AI made it rated 30% more creative than not
using AI at all, though the type of AI feedback needs to match the
idea's maturity. Mature ideas refined through AI-written text came out
18% more creative than the same ideas refined through AI-generated
images. Vague ideas benefited more from AI images instead. Images make
an idea feel finished and discourage further refinement, while text
leaves gaps that force more imaginative thinking.

 THE BEST BRANDS MAKE THEMSELVES AT HOME (3 MINUTE READ) [13]

 Brands are optimising for visibility when they should be building
memory. As AI makes it easier to produce more content, physical brand
objects can create lasting connections that digital ads often lack.
The strongest objects are distinctive enough to identify without a
logo, useful or emotionally meaningful enough to keep, and rooted in
something the brand genuinely owns. Starting with what a brand can
make rather than what content a channel needs can lead to fewer, more
memorable ideas that generate organic digital content.

 THE TRUTH ABOUT BRAIN ROT: IT'S A CRY FOR HELP (6 MINUTE READ) [14]

 “Brain rot” may look like evidence of declining attention spans,
but Gen Z participants describe it as a response to an attention
economy that constantly demands engagement. Absurd, meaningless
content can feel like a break from polished, monetized media, even as
endless scrolling leaves people drained and guilty. The deeper problem
is the social media infrastructure that optimizes for attention rather
than users' well-being.
