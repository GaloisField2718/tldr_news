 WHAT'S WORKING IN B2B GTM IN 2026 (7 MINUTE READ) [8]

 Founder-led sales remains a leading GTM motion until companies reach
$10M ARR. Networking and introductions rank as the most effective
channel, while intimate events come second. AI search is now a core
channel for 39% of B2B companies, up 11 percentage points year over
year, and 41% are experimenting with it. Track whether AI tools

content engagement and website visits to prioritize outreach.

 CITIZENLAB DETAILS NEW SPYWARE TRIGGERED BY ONLINE ADVERTISING (2
MINUTE READ) [9]

 Discover-Tech, an Israeli firm presenting itself as an ad vendor,
displayed 442 million ads on more than 120,000 websites and apps,
including the BBC, The New York Times, and Duolingo. Some of those ads
carried zero-click spyware that infected phones and computers simply
by appearing on screen, with targets ranging from Al Jazeera
journalists to NATO and NVIDIA staff. It is the first confirmed case
of government-backed zero-click spyware delivered through online ads.
Google has since blocked Discover-Tech from its ad platforms.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 HOW TO PITCH LOCAL NEWSROOMS IN THE US (9 MINUTE READ) [10]

 Local news pitches are more effective when they target the right
journalist, demonstrate a clear community impact, and provide
everything needed to produce the story. Local newsrooms have declined
by 81% since 2002, making relevant, ready-to-use pitches especially
important. Start by reviewing journalists' recent coverage, then
contact assignment editors at TV stations or editors at print and
digital outlets when appropriate. Tailor pitches to the format: print
needs a clear story and supporting data, TV needs visuals and local
people to interview, and radio needs an available guest who can speak.
Include a timely local angle, relevant data, quotes, visuals, and
event details, and send the pitch before the newsroom's morning
planning meeting.

 WHY BRANDS NEED TO FUEL A LIFE, NOT FILL A FEED (4 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Pinterest reports that 40% of Gen Z wish social media had never been
invented and 63% intentionally unplug. Design campaigns that drive
real-world action rather than endless scrolling. Show how a product
fits into the audience's desired lifestyle instead of making the
product the hero. Turn inspiration into action with personalized
recommendations and specific next steps such as shopping lists or
linked outfits. Remove friction with tools that help people follow
through.

 THE ART OF BRAND MERCH (8 MINUTE READ) [12]

 People wear merch to say something about themselves, not to promote a
company. A logo on a T-shirt or water bottle asks customers to carry a
message without giving them a reason, and only works when the logo
already means something. Strong merch does one of three jobs. It
signals belonging through a recognizable color or shape, sells more
product by being designed around how that product gets used, or lets
in people who can't yet afford the core product.

 HOW LONG SHOULD A REEL BE? UNDER 3 MINUTES ON INSTAGRAM, LONGER ON
TIKTOK (12 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Video length barely matters on Instagram until the 3-minute mark,
where recommendations to new audiences stop. Reels from 10 seconds to
3 minutes all perform within 2% of each creator's median, while longer
ones fall to 0.91x. TikTok rewards length instead, with 3-to-10-minute
videos earning 1.22x and beating the same creator's 10-to-19-second
clips for 68% of creators.

 YOUR NEWSLETTER ISN'T THE UNSUBSCRIBE PROBLEM (3 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Google Postmaster Tools evaluates unsubscribe compliance across all
email systems sending from the same domain. If someone unsubscribes
from a newsletter but still receives CRM or sales emails, Google may
flag the domain. Sync unsubscribe lists across platforms and use
distinct From addresses and List-IDs for each mailing list. Verify
that every system supports one-click unsubscribes and removes
recipients within 48 hours. Compliance status can take 1-2 weeks to
update.

 A STANDING HOLDOUT IS THE ONLY AUDIT AN ALGORITHM CANNOT ARGUE WITH
(7 MINUTE READ) [18]

 Standing holdouts are a measurement method that reserves a portion of
markets or audiences from automated ad campaigns to measure whether
advertising generates incremental sales. Uber cut $100 million of its
$150 million annual rider acquisition budget without reducing app
installs, while Airbnb recovered to 95% of its 2019 traffic levels
before resuming marketing spend. A standing holdout reserves 5% to 10%
of markets and rotates them on a fixed schedule to measure advertising
performance over time. Each test defines its markets, measurement
period, success thresholds, and budget decisions in advance. If
holdout results conflict with the platform's reported returns beyond a
predefined margin, the holdout results determine budget decisions.

 AI AGENTS BUY THROUGH YOUR OWN CHECKOUT, AND DISPUTES STILL LAND ON
YOU (8 MINUTE READ) [19]

 In-chat AI checkouts are built for physical goods sold to US buyers
through big platforms. Google, Microsoft, and ChatGPT all exclude
subscriptions or say nothing about them. Independent sellers instead
meet agents like Amazon's Buy for Me and Meta's Muse on their own
checkout pages, where guest purchase and saved-wallet support decide
whether an order completes. Visa bars merchants from requiring dispute
waivers on agent purchases, so chargebacks still land on the seller.
