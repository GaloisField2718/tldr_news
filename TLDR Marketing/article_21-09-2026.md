NEWS & TRENDS

 A SMALL BUT GROWING NUMBER OF FOUNDERS ARE BETTING ON BRINGING PEOPLE
TOGETHER OFFLINE (5 MINUTE READ) [5]

 Founders are investing in businesses built around offline connection.
New ventures include social groups, hands-on craft schools, and shared
gaming experiences. People are wanting real-world interaction as
technology makes socializing more isolated. Some founders are also
targeting younger consumers seeking alternatives to traditional
education or more meaningful ways to spend time offline.

 CREATOR CONTENT PREFERRED IN AI RECOMMENDATIONS (3 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Creator content is particularly relevant to younger shoppers, with
65% of Gen Z and millennials preferring AI recommendations that cite
creators. However, only 29% say those citations increase their
likelihood of purchasing. Creators are most useful for discovering
brands and comparing products. Credibility matters more than audience
size when consumers assess their recommendations.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 5 LEVERS DRIVE CAC, SAYS AD PRO (3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 Lowering CAC on Meta and Google starts with giving platforms the
right signals and creative. Ads should lead with novel hooks tied to
customer pain points and use language that reflects the target
audience. Brands should optimize for new-customer purchases rather
than total purchases. Then, test creative across personas, angles, and
formats. 5 levers ultimately drive CAC: the offer, creative, account
structure, landing page, and optimization signals.

 WHY BUILDING A FREE AUDIENCE IS KEEPING YOU BROKE (5 MINUTE READ) [8]


 Free audiences are much harder to monetize than paying customers. A
campaign generated 3 paid subscribers from 7,500+ free readers versus
34 from roughly 350 existing customers. Instead of building an
audience or product in isolation, build a small product, share the
process publicly, and build relationships with potential customers.
This creates distribution and trust while validating demand before
scaling.

MISCELLANEOUS

 GOOGLE ‘AI CONTRIBUTION PILOT' TESTS PAYING WEBSITES WHEN THEY'RE
USED IN AI RESULTS (2 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Google is testing a model that pays publishers when their content
materially contributes to AI-generated search results. The AI
Contribution Pilot includes dozens of mostly small and midsized
publishers across various industries. It could create a new revenue
stream as AI Overviews reduce traffic from traditional search and
establish a model for compensating publishers based on their content's
contribution to AI answers.

 HOW TO READ A DESIGN BRIEF: TRUEROLL GOLF BRAND IDENTITY CASE STUDY
(5 MINUTE READ) [13]

 A design brief often reveals only what clients know how to
articulate. The real brief may emerge later through casual comments
about how the brand will actually be used. For TrueRoll Golf Club,
that meant designing for golf courses rather than travelers. The
identity used familiar green and gold and minimal golf imagery.
Designing for physical applications also shaped the system. Listen
beyond the form and design for the brand's real audience and use
cases.
