STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 CREATIVE GUIDANCE ON YOUTUBE (2 MINUTE READ) [9]

 Google says creative can drive up to 49% of campaign ROI, and small
creative changes can lift YouTube conversions. For Demand Gen ads,
adding a human voice lifts conversions 12%, text overlays 3%, and
showing the brand within the first five seconds 4%. Google recommends
designing for YouTube's native look, avoiding fake buttons or
oversized logos, and featuring people wherever possible.

 HOW WE USE AI FOR EVERY ARTICLE WITHOUT MAKING AI SLOP (8 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Ahrefs uses AI throughout its content workflow while keeping humans
responsible for judgment. Writers define the audience, angle,
evidence, and original insights before using AI for research,
drafting, and fact-checking. Human review gates catch weak ideas and
unsupported claims before publication. Time saved on production goes
toward original research and data analysis. Every article also has an
owner and second reviewer who can challenge the work before it goes
live.

 GROWING LAZYWEB TO 50K+ AGENTS (4 MINUTE READ) [11]

 Lazyweb reached 50K signups in a few months through distribution
tactics built around AI agents. Removing email requirements increased
activation by 50%+ and a one-line installation command added 20%.
Automatic MCP routing more than doubled DAUs. Free MCP access to its
blog and data-driven research also helped agents discover and use the
product. Generic SEO content and novelty products had little impact.
The approach focused on making Lazyweb easy for agents to use while
giving away useful tools and data to drive word of mouth.

RESOURCES & TOOLS

 HELPFUL CONTENT GRADER GPT (17 MINUTE READ) [12]

 This GPT evaluates a webpage against Google's 32-question helpful
content self-assessment. Paste in a page, upload the content, or
provide a URL to get started. It scores the content across quality,
expertise, people-first value, and search-engine-first risks. The
output includes an overall grade, section scores, evidence for each
verdict, and prioritized fixes. The score is a diagnostic, not a
ranking guarantee. Google's questions describe what helpful content
should look like, but the score doesn't directly influence search
rankings.

 THE SHARED AGENT BRIEF: A PRACTICAL OPERATING AGREEMENT FOR GTM TEAMS
(23 MINUTE READ) [13]

 AI GTM workflows need clear decision rights before teams connect more
tools. A Shared Agent Brief defines who contributes what, who makes
each decision, what AI can handle, and when it must stop. Start with
one recurring workflow and test both a normal case and a conflict case
before running a small manual pilot. AI can handle research and
scoring, but should pause when evidence conflicts or ownership is
unclear. Expand permissions gradually, keeping external actions behind
human approval.

MISCELLANEOUS

 WE DESIGNED FOR FACEBOOK, RIPPLE, AND MERCK. HERE'S WHAT WORKING WITH
ENTERPRISE CLIENTS LOOKS LIKE (4 MINUTE READ) [14]

 Enterprise marketing work often gets slowed down by everything around
the work, not the work itself. Large companies bring more
stakeholders, longer approval cycles, and conflicting feedback. The
key is knowing who actually makes decisions, giving internal champions
what they need to sell the work, and having one clear project owner.
Once teams trust you, that relationship can expand into work across
departments and years of projects.

 DON'T LET RETAIL MEDIA TELL YOU IT'S BRAND BUILDING (3 MINUTE READ)
[15]

 Retail media is not brand building, no matter how retailers frame it.
It's the same shelf-position and end-cap money brands have paid since
the 1980s, just wrapped in a modern dashboard. Retail media only
reaches shoppers who are already in the store and ready to buy, not
the much larger group that actually drives category growth. Treat it
as a bottom-of-funnel investment that protects distribution, not a
substitute for building the brand.
