 CHATBOTS PUT PR BACK ON THE BRAND AGENDA. SOCIAL LISTENING DECIDES
WHAT IT SHOULD SAY (3 MINUTE READ) [8]

 AI search is giving PR a larger role in marketing because chatbots
use third-party coverage to answer questions and recommend brands. One
forecast projects that PR and earned media budgets will double by 2027
as more consumers use AI chatbots instead of traditional search.
Social listening also gives brands real-time audience research,
helping teams identify which creative ideas resonate and which risk
backlash before investing further. As AI makes content cheaper to
produce, audience understanding and independent brand credibility
become more valuable.

 OZEMPIC IS CHANGING HOW AMERICANS SPEND MONEY, AND THE NUMBERS ARE
STARTLING (3 MINUTE READ) [9]

 GLP-1 weight-loss drugs are reshaping consumer spending across food,
fitness, and retail. 11% of American adults use these medications,
with 26% of users spending more on clothing and 36% more on jewelry.
Meanwhile, household spending at fast-food restaurants drops nearly 9%
after 6-8 months on the drugs. Users are also buying fewer sweets and
sugary drinks while spending more on fresh produce and protein.

 SPOTTING CTR DECAY EARLY: HOW TO CATCH AD CREATIVE FATIGUE (10 MINUTE
READ) [10]

 Creative fatigue shows up as one ad's CTR sliding week after week
below its own launch-week level while frequency climbs and the other
ads in the same ad set hold steady. A single early drop that then
flattens is normal settling, not fatigue. CPM rising on every ad at
once points to the auction or the calendar instead, and frequency
rising while reach plateaus points to an exhausted audience. Meta's
own “Creative fatigue” status only appears once cost per result
doubles, well after spend is lost.

 I ANALYZED THE LEAD MAGNET FUNNEL OF A 190,000 LINKEDIN CREATOR (8
MINUTE READ) [11]

 An audit of a creator's lead magnet funnel reveals ways to turn email
subscribers into clients. Keep landing pages focused with a clear
promise and visible signup form. Use the thank-you page to book calls
or collect information for personalized follow-ups instead of showing
a generic confirmation. In the welcome email, build credibility with
personal stories and testimonials while previewing upcoming lessons.
Encourage replies to improve deliverability and let eager subscribers
access the full course immediately.

 THE ANATOMY OF THE BEST-PERFORMING ONBOARDING PAYWALLS (6 MINUTE
READ) [16]

 Multi-page onboarding paywalls convert 47% better than single-page
ones, and conversion peaks at 3 screens. The top performers follow the
same sequence, a free-trial promise with no prices, then a trial-end
reminder, then the offer. The reminder screen is the strongest single
signal, lifting conversion from 8.7% to 14.8% by removing the fear of
forgetting to cancel. Leading with one yearly plan beats the common
yearly-plus-monthly picker by about 1.5x, and an abandon offer for
users who cancel checkout adds roughly 18% revenue per view.

 HOW WE BUILT AND LAUNCHED A VIRAL RESEARCH CAMPAIGN IN PARTNERSHIP
WITH LINKEDIN (4 MINUTE READ) [17]

 Partnering with a brand that has complementary data and a shared
interest can make original research stand out in a crowded market and
expand its reach. Semrush combined its AI citation data with
LinkedIn's content and engagement data to analyze around 89,000
LinkedIn URLs cited by AI search tools. The study found that 95% of
cited posts were original content rather than reshares, and most cited
posts averaged just 15 to 25 reactions. Semrush and LinkedIn
coordinated media outreach, employee advocacy, social promotion, and
launch timing to reach both audiences and generate strong media
coverage, tens of thousands of visits, and dozens of signups. The
collaboration also created new opportunities for guest content and
speaking engagements beyond the initial campaign.
