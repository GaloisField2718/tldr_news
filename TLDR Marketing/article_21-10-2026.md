 CONSUMERS RETHINK VALUE AS UNCERTAINTY AND INFLATION PERSIST (2
MINUTE READ) [5]

 Consumers are adopting 3 distinct spending behaviors depending on the
purchase. Some pay more for premium experiences and better
performance. Others balance quality and convenience against price to
get the most from every dollar. Those seeking to limit spending choose
cheaper alternatives and promotions while protecting budgets for
priorities. These behaviors aren't fixed consumer segments. The same
person may splurge in one category while trading down in another
depending on the occasion and perceived value.

 GOOGLE IS FEEDING RANK TRACKERS POISONED SERPS WITH SWAPPED RESULTS,
NOZZLE FINDS (3 MINUTE READ) [6]

 Google has been feeding rank trackers fake search results since
September 13. Nozzle found that 8.4% of SERPs were affected across 27M
results analyzed. The poisoned pages look normal but retain just 22%
of their usual sites. This causes tracked top-10 rankings to disappear
60.2% of the time. Social platforms and forums appear more often in
these results. SEO teams should cross-check ranking drops against
Google Search Console before assuming their rankings have changed.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 PR FOR GEO: 4 WAYS IT CAN INFLUENCE AI VISIBILITY (11 MINUTE READ)
[7]

 PR can improve AI visibility by earning citations for original
research, reinforcing how third-party publications describe a brand,
building backlinks, and increasing brand awareness. Research tied to
customer pain points is more likely to earn useful AI citations than
data that is merely related to the business. Consistently using the
same brand descriptor in pitches and press releases can also help AI
associate a company with its intended category. Measure one specific
goal by establishing a baseline and tracking the same prompts across
AI platforms over time. Media placements alone do not prove an impact
on AI visibility, so report coverage separately from changes in
citations, mentions, and recommendations.

 FIND JOBS BEYOND LINKEDIN WITH GOOGLE X-RAY SEARCHES (2 MINUTE READ)
[8]

 Job seekers can use Google X-ray searches to find openings beyond
LinkedIn by searching applicant tracking systems like Ashby or
Greenhouse directly. For example, “site:jobs.ashbyhq.com (business
development OR institutional sales) (crypto OR fintech) (remote OR
EMEA OR Europe)” finds relevant roles by title, industry, and
location. Swap in different keywords or job boards to fit your search.
Verify listings are current and remote roles accept applicants in your
country.

 CHECK IF GOOGLE CAN INDEX YOUR CONTENT WITH A SITE: SEARCH (1 MINUTE
READ) [9]

 To check whether Google can index specific content on a page, copy a
snippet of text and search site:[URL] [snippet of text] in Google.
Check whether the text appears in the result's meta description. If it
does, Google can access that content. If no results appear, test a few
other snippets to confirm there may be an indexing issue.

 THE DISABILITY INCLUSION EFFECT (3 MINUTE READ) [13]

 In tests with more than 4,500 participants, Ads featuring a model
with a disability made people 33% more likely to choose the product.
The lift holds even when people are told the brand cast a wheelchair
user only to meet regulations. However, it disappears when the ad
calls attention to the disability or portrays the person as
vulnerable, since the benefit comes from treating a stigmatized group
as ordinary customers.

 I ANALYZED 1,100+ MARKETING JOB POSTS. THEY'RE STUCK IN 2023 (26
MINUTE READ) [14]

 Companies are treating AI system-building as one specialist's job
rather than a baseline requirement for every marketing hire. Every GTM
engineer post asks for AI skills, compared with 47% of marketing posts
overall and just 25% of entry-level ones. Only 6% ask candidates to
contribute to the shared skills, context, and agents a team runs on,
and only 8% ask for proof of AI use.
