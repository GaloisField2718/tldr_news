NEWS & TRENDS

 ADVERTISING IS GOING AI. TV ISN'T (3 MINUTE READ) [7]

 By 2030, 27% of ad revenue could flow through AI-powered platforms.
TV is moving slower. North American TV ad revenue is expected to grow
just 3% in 2026, excluding political spending. Complex buying
workflows and fragmented inventory make automation harder. AI could
simplify TV buying, but it needs strong first-party data to work well.


 MORE LOWER-INCOME HOUSEHOLDS FEEL FINANCIALLY VULNERABLE (2 MINUTE
READ) [8]

 Financially vulnerable US households rose from 15% to 17% in 2026,
while fewer than 70% said they paid all bills on time over the past
year. Low-income consumers have shifted more than $10 billion from
discretionary purchases toward essentials since 2019, as prices for
that group have risen 35% since January 2018. Consumer spending has
still held steady in 2026, but many consumers are tapping into their
savings to keep spending. This may not be sustainable if inflation,
gas prices, and other costs continue to rise.

 CALIFORNIA WILL FINE CREATORS $5,000 A POST FOR HIDDEN POLITICAL ADS
(4 MINUTE READ) [9]

 California now allows fines of up to $5,000 per post for creators who
fail to disclose paid political content from campaigns. AB 1130 gives
the Fair Political Practices Commission enforcement powers that were
previously missing. The law applies when creators are paid by campaign
committees to support or oppose candidates or ballot measures. It
doesn't cover unpaid political opinions. The law follows growing use
of influencers in political campaigns and similar disclosure efforts
elsewhere.

 THE PERFECTLY OPTIMIZED PAGE FOR AEO (2 MINUTE READ) [10]

 A visual checklist showing the 6 key elements of a page optimized for
AEO. Allowing major AI crawlers and server-side rendering makes the
page accessible, while four to six direct sentences near the top give
AI a concise answer to extract. Tables, lists, clear heading
hierarchies, and FAQs make information easier to parse, and pages less
than three months old are reportedly 3x more likely to earn citations.


 I BUILT A GROWTH TEAM OF AGENTS TO RUN A 6 FIGURES BUSINESS (6 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 An agent growth team can take over drafting and routing but should
never take over sending. One solo operator pairs an orchestrator with
about ten channel agents, covering everything from SEO to paid media
to lifecycle email, each tied to a share of a 900-signup, 200-customer
monthly target. Every task goes on a Todoist board that separates
decisions from execution, and each card must say what the task is, why
it matters, and where to click. Finished cards trigger the next
agent's follow-up automatically, so the founder only approves and
sends.

 AI POLLING: HOW SYNTHETIC SURVEYS COULD PREDICT REAL BEHAVIOR (20
MINUTE READ) [15]

 People answer surveys as the person they want to be rather than the
one they are, and synthetic surveys try to close that gap by
simulating behavior instead of asking. AI personas built from
interviews and behavioral records can return results in hours at about
half the cost of human panels, and incumbents like Gallup and
Qualtrics are already testing them. The simulations tend to match
overall results but miss on subgroups and on questions outside their
training data, where they rarely admit uncertainty.

 WHAT WOULD A CHILD-FREE INTERNET LOOK LIKE? (4 MINUTE READ) [16]

 Australia's under-16 social media ban deactivated 4.7M accounts, yet
more than 80% of surveyed children still used age-restricted services.
A “child-free” internet could push young users toward gaming,
messaging, and smaller platforms, making their activity harder to
monitor. Children could also remain visible through adult-run family
influencer accounts. The shift could make young consumers' influence
less visible to brands while limiting children's access to online
communities and digital experiences.
