NEWS & TRENDS

 MARKETERS TO SPEND $116 BILLION ON CREATORS IN 2026 (2 MINUTE READ)
[8]

 Brand spending on creators is forecast to grow 15% in 2026 and reach
$178 billion by 2030, about twice the pace of advertising. Paid
amplification of creator content makes up 44% of spending, while
paying creators for content rather than reach is the fastest-growing
segment. Most of the growth comes from budgets shifted out of TV,
performance media, and creative production rather than new marketing
dollars.

 94% OF GLOBAL HITS WENT VIRAL ON TIKTOK FIRST, REVEALS 2026 MUSIC
IMPACT REPORT (4 MINUTE READ) [9]

 TikTok has become a major driver of music discovery and commercial
success. 94% of songs that entered the Billboard Global 200 in 2025
went viral on the platform first. Songs that go viral on TikTok spend
four times longer on the chart than non-viral tracks, and 45% of US
TikTok users discover new music on the platform. That discovery also
translates into purchases: TikTok music listeners are 78% more likely
to pay for a streaming subscription, and 61% purchased physical or
digital music in the past year.

STRATEGIES & TACTICS

 5 TIPS TO INCREASING LINKEDIN VIDEO REACH (2 MINUTE READ) [10]

 With LinkedIn reach declining, prioritize timely topics tied to
current conversations and use on-screen text hooks that create
curiosity rather than simply describe the video. Keep footage clear
and audio crisp without overcomplicating production. Focus on content
that offers value viewers can apply or save. Listicles and
self-improvement formats are gaining traction. Avoid excessive
transitions and visual effects that distract from the message.

 WIN REPORT: HOW CUTTING OVERWHELM GREW CONVERSIONS BY 13% (2 MINUTE
READ) [11]

 Reducing the effort required to compare options can increase
conversions, especially when a page overwhelms visitors with too much
information. Churnet Valley Railway replaced its long list of eight
dining experiences with categorized sections that put popular options
first and compact cards that made prices, departure times, and
durations easy to compare. It also replaced the “Book Now” CTA
with “Check Running Times” to give visitors a lower-commitment
next step into the booking process. The redesign increased conversions
by 13%, with an even stronger result among first-time visitors.

 5 WAYS TO PERSONALIZE YOUR EMAILS, FROM A STUDY OF 274 BILLION SENDS
(3 MINUTE READ) [12]

 Personalize emails by referencing shoppers' recent activity, such as
products they browsed, items in their cart, or their last purchase.
Use signup preferences to tailor welcome emails, and include
product-specific care tips, how-to content, or complementary product
recommendations in post-purchase emails. Show/hide logic can also
display different content to different segments in a single campaign,
such as VIP offers for loyalty members or location-specific
promotions. Klaviyo's analysis of 274 billion emails found that
messages that referenced shoppers' recent activity earned 45% higher
click rates in flows and 33% higher click rates in campaigns.

 THE AI AD CREATIVE MASTERCLASS: SKILLS, TOOLS, AND THE 21 FORMATS TOP

 24 of the 34 longest-running Meta ads from 48 top DTC brands were

 WHY YOU WANT MORE UNSUBSCRIBES (8 MINUTE READ) [16]

 A very low unsubscribe rate can mean email is landing in spam, since
nobody unsubscribes from a junk folder. Unsubscribing is also the
least damaging way to leave, since a spam report hurts placement and
silent non-readers drag it down over time. Splitting unsubscribes by
opt-in type separates intentional sign-ups, whose exits signal a
content problem, from consequential ones like gated downloads, whose
quick exits are expected. Making the exit easy, and occasionally
inviting it, keeps people who are done from reaching for the spam
button.

 AI PRODUCT RECOMMENDATIONS ARE MOSTLY A SUMMARY OF LISTICLES (7
MINUTE READ) [17]

 A study across 4 search engines found that 90% of recommended brands
appeared on at least one cited “best X” or “top X” list.
Brands featured on 3 or more cited lists were recommended 44% of the
time, compared with just 2.5% for brands absent from all lists. The
top 10 list publishers accounted for up to 90% of citations in some
categories. Updated comparisons also mattered, with 83% of Gemini's
cited pages featuring 2026 in the title or URL.

 3 THINGS I'M WATCHING IN MARKETING GOING INTO 2027 (2 MINUTE READ)
[18]

 Brands are replacing some agency work with in-house creators and
employee advocates to build trust through individual voices. Engaged
communities are becoming more valuable than large follower counts as
brands seek direct audience relationships beyond algorithm-dependent
platforms. Meanwhile, audiences are tuning out generic claims of
authenticity. Original perspectives and behind-the-scenes content are
becoming more effective ways to stand out.
